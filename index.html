Read more

The Federal Government has unveiled a new monthly cash transfer of ₦50,000 for vulnerable Nigerian households, as part of efforts to ease the pressure of high living costs.

What was announced

The Minister of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction, Dr Bernard Doro, announced the National Emergency Relief and Stabilisation Intervention (NERSI) in Abuja on Friday, 30 October 2026, describing it as a presidential initiative. He said the programme would reach millions of Nigerians, although the exact number of beneficiaries and how long the scheme will last were not given.
