Banky W returns to music on Victor Thompson's 'Super Power'

30 Oct 2026, 20:49 GMT

Banky W, the R&B singer turned pastor and former boss of Wizkid's old label, is making a return to music.

What he announced

On Friday, 30 October 2026, Banky W revealed on Instagram that he features on gospel singer Victor Thompson's upcoming single, 'Super Power', and shared a preview of the music video. The song is due to be released on Friday, 6 November. A clip of the two singers enjoying the song together was also shared online.

Why he returned

Banky W reportedly said he had accepted that his recording career had ended, and described the song as an act of obedience rather than a career move. He said he had prayed that if a return was meant to happen, the songs would come. About three weeks later, Thompson sent him a message saying they were meant to make music together.

Background

Banky W, whose real name is Olubankole Wellington, was one of Nigeria's leading R&B stars in the 2000s and early 2010s and founded the Empire Mates Entertainment label, which launched Wizkid's career. His last full project was the 2017 album 'Songs About U'. He has since focused on ministry and public life, and is married to actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington. Victor Thompson is known for the gospel hit 'This Year (Blessings)'.

How fans reacted

Fans welcomed the news with excitement, with many saying they had missed his voice. Some said they hoped the song would lead to a full gospel album.

  • 'Super Power' is due out on 6 November.
  • Banky W says the song is 'an act of obedience'.
  • His last full project was 'Songs About U' in 2017.

Eedris Abdulkareem releases new song asking Seyi Tinubu to plead with his father

3 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Veteran rapper Eedris Abdulkareem has reportedly released a new music video in which he addresses Seyi Tinubu, asking him to beg his father, President Tinubu, to ease Nigerians' hardship. He also criticised the new tax law and the electoral system.

Read more

Veteran rapper Eedris Abdulkareem has released a new song with a pointed political message for President Bola Tinubu, this time delivered through the President's son, Seyi Tinubu.

The song

In a music video posted on Instagram on Monday, 2 November 2026, Eedris reportedly addresses Seyi directly, asking him to plead with his father on behalf of Nigerians. The lyrics contrast the President's role as a good father to his own family with what the rapper describes as hardship for ordinary Nigerians. According to reports, the song also takes aim at the National Assembly over the newly passed tax law and criticises the electoral system.

Background

Eedris, best known for his 2004 hit 'Jaga Jaga', has used music to criticise Nigerian governments for more than two decades. In 2025 he released a song critical of the Tinubu government, which the broadcasting regulator restricted on air. On Monday he also denied claims that he had joined the APC, accusing Isaac Fayose of hypocrisy for saying so. Seyi Tinubu has become a visible figure in his father's re-election campaign, and the Presidency has defended the government's reforms, saying they are needed to fix the economy.

How people reacted

The video drew strong reactions online. Many fans praised the rapper for speaking up about the cost of living, while supporters of the President accused him of playing politics ahead of the 2027 elections.

What happens next

Neither Seyi Tinubu nor the Presidency had responded publicly at the time of reporting.

Odumodublvck claims his song made Declan Rice famous in Nigeria

2 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Rapper Odumodublvck has reportedly claimed that Nigerians did not know Arsenal midfielder Declan Rice until he released a song about him. Many fans mocked the claim, pointing out that Rice was already a well-known England player.

Read more

Rapper Odumodublvck has sparked amused reactions online after claiming he introduced England and Arsenal midfielder Declan Rice to Nigerians.

What he said

In comments shared on Monday, 2 November 2026, the rapper reportedly said that before he sang about Rice, nobody in Nigeria knew him, and that Rice blew up after the song, after which Arsenal discovered and signed him. He presented the claim as proof of how his music connects football and music fans.

The song

Odumodublvck released his hit 'Declan Rice' in 2023, and it became one of the most popular Nigerian rap songs of that year, helping to make him one of the country's best-known young rappers. Rice, who was then captain of West Ham United and already an England international, joined Arsenal in 2023 in a British record transfer.

How people reacted

Many fans mocked the claim, saying football followers in Nigeria knew Rice long before the song and that it was the rapper who benefited from the player's name, not the other way round. Others said he was joking or simply promoting himself, while some called it harmless banter.

Background

Odumodublvck is known for bold statements and a confident persona, which have helped keep him in the spotlight. Arsenal is one of the most widely supported English clubs in Nigeria.

Namibian Afrobeats singer Nally dies at 33 in crash that killed seven

2 Nov 2026, 14:56 GMT

Namibian singer Nally Simson has died at the age of 33 in a head-on collision that killed seven people in northern Namibia on Sunday. His last Instagram post, promoting his song 'Untouchable', has gone viral as fans mourn.

Read more

The Namibian music industry is in mourning after Afrobeats singer Nally Simson, widely known as Nally, died in a road accident on Sunday, 1 November 2026. He was 33.

What happened

Nally was one of seven people killed in a head-on collision between a pickup truck and an SUV at the Elim Curve on the Okahao-Oshakati main road in the north of the country. The regional police commander for Omusati, Commissioner Ismael Basson, said the crash happened at about 4am, and that six occupants of one vehicle and the driver of the SUV died at the scene. Police have not given further details about the cause.

Family statement

The singer's brother, Benjamin Simson, confirmed his death and asked for privacy as the family grieves.

His last post

A day before his death, on Saturday, 31 October, Nally shared a clip of his latest song, 'Untouchable', on Instagram. In the song he addresses critics, saying their words have no power over him. Fans who returned to the post after the news broke filled the comments with tributes, saying the lyrics now felt very emotional. A separate video in which a fan appeared to predict tragedy for him hours before the crash also spread online, drawing shocked reactions.

Background

Nally was one of Namibia's best-known Afrobeats artists and had built a following across southern Africa. Fellow musicians, fans and public figures in Namibia have paid tribute to him on social media.

What happens next

Funeral arrangements have not yet been announced.

Controversial relationship therapist Blessing CEO has shared an emotional message of regret, apologising to people she may have offended in the past. She described herself as broken and compared her journey to the biblical story of the prodigal son.

Controversial relationship therapist Blessing CEO has shared an emotional message of regret, apologising to people she may have offended through her words and actions in the past.

"I now understand the pain I once mocked"

In her statement, Blessing admitted that she once spoke carelessly about people's struggles without fully understanding what they were going through. Now, according to her, life has placed her in a position where she can finally relate to some of that pain.

She described herself as emotionally broken, saying her present struggles have left her feeling lost and surrounded by darkness.

Like the prodigal son

Blessing also compared her situation to the biblical story of the prodigal son, who returned home after going through difficult experiences.

Her words suggest a period of deep personal reflection, as she acknowledges past mistakes and expresses a desire to make peace with those she may have hurt.

A change in tone

For someone who built a reputation on speaking boldly about relationships, personal struggles and other people's choices, this public apology marks a noticeable change in tone.

Sometimes, life has a way of teaching people lessons they once thought they already understood.
