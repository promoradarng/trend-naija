Banky W returns to music on Victor Thompson's 'Super Power'

30 Oct 2026, 20:49 GMT

Banky W, the R&B singer turned pastor and former boss of Wizkid's old label, is making a return to music.

What he announced

On Friday, 30 October 2026, Banky W revealed on Instagram that he features on gospel singer Victor Thompson's upcoming single, 'Super Power', and shared a preview of the music video. The song is due to be released on Friday, 6 November. A clip of the two singers enjoying the song together was also shared online.

Why he returned

Banky W reportedly said he had accepted that his recording career had ended, and described the song as an act of obedience rather than a career move. He said he had prayed that if a return was meant to happen, the songs would come. About three weeks later, Thompson sent him a message saying they were meant to make music together.

Background

Banky W, whose real name is Olubankole Wellington, was one of Nigeria's leading R&B stars in the 2000s and early 2010s and founded the Empire Mates Entertainment label, which launched Wizkid's career. His last full project was the 2017 album 'Songs About U'. He has since focused on ministry and public life, and is married to actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington. Victor Thompson is known for the gospel hit 'This Year (Blessings)'.

How fans reacted

Fans welcomed the news with excitement, with many saying they had missed his voice. Some said they hoped the song would lead to a full gospel album.

  • 'Super Power' is due out on 6 November.
  • Banky W says the song is 'an act of obedience'.
  • His last full project was 'Songs About U' in 2017.

Davido says he would charge $500,000 a show for a joint tour with Wizkid

2 Nov 2026, 09:44 GMT

Davido has reportedly said a joint tour with Wizkid would sell out every venue and that he would charge $500,000 per show. He also explained why the two have not recorded a song together in about 10 years.

Read more

Davido has once again got fans talking about a possible joint tour with his long-time rival, Wizkid.

What he said

In an interview on the IONWANNATALK show, clips of which spread online on 1 and 2 November 2026, Davido reportedly said that if he and Wizkid toured together, he would ask for $500,000 per show because every venue would sell out. He was less sure that a joint song would be a hit, pointing to past collaborations between big stars that he said did not do as well as expected.

On the rivalry

Davido said the two had not recorded together in about 10 years, not because they could not work together, but because they were not in the same mental space. He acknowledged that people have different views on whether the rivalry helped or hurt their careers, and said he had once felt strongly about their differences but no longer did.

Background

Davido and Wizkid are two of the biggest names in Afrobeats. They collaborated in the early 2010s, but fans have long been divided into rival camps, and the two have occasionally traded words online. Davido has been in the news this week over his breakdown in relations with VeryDarkMan and his support for Zlatan's song 'Inferno'.

How fans reacted

Fans were excited by the idea of a joint tour, with many saying it would be the biggest event in African music. Wizkid had not responded at the time of the report.

Nathaniel Bassey becomes first African gospel singer to pass 1 billion YouTube views

2 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Gospel singer and pastor Nathaniel Bassey has reportedly become the first African gospel artist to pass one billion views on YouTube. The milestone came during this month's edition of his Hallelujah Challenge worship programme.

Read more

Nigerian gospel singer and pastor Nathaniel Bassey has reached a milestone no other African gospel artist has achieved, according to reports on Monday, 2 November 2026.

The milestone

His main YouTube channel had passed 1.014 billion views at the time of reporting, with about 5.24 million subscribers. The channel has been active since 2011. Among his most popular songs are 'Imela', 'Onise Iyanu', 'Olowogbogboro' and 'You Are God', which are sung in churches across Nigeria and the diaspora.

The Hallelujah Challenge

The milestone came during the October 2026 edition of the Hallelujah Challenge, the midnight prayer and worship programme Bassey started in 2017. It runs twice a year and draws huge online audiences, with many viewers joining live on Instagram and YouTube. Earlier this week, a plane crash survivor shared a testimony during the programme, and a Muslim woman's decision to join drew attention online.

A non-commercial approach

Some of Bassey's followers have suggested he should make money from the large audience. He has previously described the Hallelujah Challenge as a spiritual exercise focused on devotion and worship rather than revenue. He did not give a public statement about the milestone at the time of reporting.

Background

Bassey, who is also a trumpeter, has become one of the best-known voices in Nigerian worship music. His success shows the growing global reach of Nigerian gospel music, which has produced several international stars in recent years.

How fans reacted

Fans celebrated the news online, describing it as a proud moment for African gospel music.

Flamingos beat Venezuela to reach U-17 Women's World Cup semi-finals, face Brazil next

2 Nov 2026, 09:44 GMT

Nigeria's Flamingos beat Venezuela 1-0 in Morocco through a late penalty from captain Azeezat Oduntan to reach the semi-finals of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup. They are Africa's last team in the tournament and will play Brazil on Wednesday.

Read more

Nigeria's under-17 women's team, the Flamingos, are into the semi-finals of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The match

The Flamingos beat Venezuela 1-0 in the quarter-final on Sunday, 1 November 2026, at the Mohammed VI Football Academy in Salé. Captain Azeezat Oduntan scored the winner from the penalty spot in the 89th minute after Oluebube Umejiaku was fouled in the box, a decision confirmed by a VAR review.

What comes next

Nigeria will face Brazil in the semi-final on Wednesday, 4 November, at the Moulay El Hassan Stadium in Rabat. They are the only African side left in the competition after Ghana lost 1-0 to Japan in their quarter-final.

Reactions

The Confederation of African Football congratulated the team on social media, celebrating their place in the last four. Nigerian fans also shared videos of the players dancing and celebrating after earlier victories in the tournament.

Background

Nigeria has a strong record in women's youth football and has reached the later stages of age-group World Cups several times. The senior team, the Super Falcons, are the most successful side in African women's football and won another Women's Africa Cup of Nations title this year. The Flamingos' run has raised hopes of Nigeria's first-ever U-17 women's world title.

Dangote cuts petrol gantry price again, now ₦1,210 per litre

2 Nov 2026, 09:44 GMT

The Dangote Refinery has reportedly reduced its petrol gantry price by ₦55 to ₦1,210 per litre, citing a fall in European petrol benchmark prices. Depot prices in Lagos have also dropped, averaging about ₦1,259 per litre.

Read more

Petrol prices are falling again in Nigeria after the Dangote Refinery announced another cut.

The new price

According to reports on Monday, 2 November 2026, the refinery reduced its petrol gantry price by ₦55, from ₦1,265 to ₦1,210 per litre, a drop of about 4.35 per cent. The cut was announced through marketers linked to the refinery. Industry sources said it followed a fall in the European petrol benchmark that the refinery uses to set its prices, as well as better supply.

Depot prices

Other Lagos depots had already reduced their prices at the end of last week. Depot owners including African Terminal, Rainoil, Ardova and NIPCO were selling petrol at about ₦1,260 per litre, with Aiteo slightly lower at ₦1,255. Prices also fell in Port Harcourt and Calabar. Diesel prices have moved in the opposite direction after the refinery raised its diesel gantry price last week.

What it means for motorists

Gantry and depot prices are what marketers pay before selling at filling stations, so the cuts could lead to lower pump prices in the coming days. However, actual pump prices also depend on transport costs and how quickly retailers pass on savings.

Background

This is the refinery's second petrol price cut in less than a week. Since fuel subsidies were removed in 2023, prices have been set by the market, and the Dangote Refinery has become a major influence on what Nigerians pay at the pump.
