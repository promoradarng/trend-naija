Petrol prices fall at filling stations after Dangote cuts rate by ₦51

18 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Major marketers including AA Rano, NIPCO and AP Ardova have reportedly reduced pump prices to between ₦1,350 and ₦1,385 per litre. It follows a ₦51 per litre cut by Dangote Refinery.

Read more

Motorists are beginning to see some relief at filling stations as major marketers reduce petrol prices following a ₦51 per litre cut by the Dangote Petroleum Refinery.

The new prices

A market survey on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, reportedly showed that AA Rano, the Nigerian Independent Petroleum Company (NIPCO) and AP Ardova had lowered their petrol prices to between ₦1,350 and ₦1,385 per litre. Several filling stations are said to have cut prices by between ₦20 and ₦60 per litre, with NNPC and other marketers also adjusting their rates.

Why prices are falling

The reductions come as the cost of importing petrol has dropped, with the landing cost of imported fuel reportedly falling to about ₦1,067 per litre, below Dangote Refinery's own rate. This has put pressure on local prices.

The survey suggested more stations could follow with price cuts in the coming days.

Flamingos coach Busari nominated for CAF Women's Coach of the Year

18 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Akeem Busari, who led the Flamingos to the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup title, has been nominated for CAF Women's Coach of the Year. The awards take place on 2 December.

Read more

Flamingos head coach Akeem Busari has been nominated for the CAF Women's Coach of the Year award after leading Nigeria to victory at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The shortlist

Busari is one of 10 coaches on the shortlist. Others include Algeria's Farid Benstiti, Cameroon's Valentine Nguele, Ghana's Kim Lars Bjorkegren, Morocco's Jorge Vilda, South Africa's Desiree Ellis and Tanzania's Bakari Nyundo Shime, as well as Silveria Nedio of Cabo Verde, Lovemore Fazili of Malawi and Hicham Drissi of Moroccan club AS FAR.

His achievement

Busari guided the Flamingos to Nigeria's first U-17 Women's World Cup title, making them the first African team to win the competition after beating Japan 2-1 in the final.

When the winners are announced

The 2026 CAF Awards will take place on Wednesday, 2 December, at the Professor Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Arts.
