AY Makun responds to backlash after missing Cardiff show, promises free return

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Comedian AY Makun has defended himself after failing to appear at his AY Live show in Cardiff, which led to calls for him to be cancelled. He blamed repeated problems on the tour and promised a free return show, while saying ticket holders will still get refunds.

Read more

Comedian and filmmaker Ayo 'AY' Makun has spoken out after facing a wave of criticism for not turning up at his AY Live show in Cardiff, Wales.

What happened

Fans who bought tickets for the Cardiff show on Sunday were left angry when the comedian did not appear. Many criticised him on social media, and some called for him to be cancelled. AY had earlier apologised, saying fans' time, money and trust deserved respect, but the apology did not end the criticism.

His side of the story

In a long statement reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, AY said veterans in the industry had advised him to stay quiet, but he felt his audience deserved an explanation. He said he had spent years performing at events where payment promises were broken, sometimes paying fellow performers from his own pocket. He said a five-hour drive from Sheffield to Cardiff, after repeated bad experiences on the same tour, became his breaking point, but added that the fans who turned up had done nothing wrong. He also complained that negative stories about him get more attention than his achievements, and said calls for cancellation would not make him give up.

What he promised

AY said he would host a free return show in Cardiff, paid for by himself, once a venue and logistics are confirmed. He said this would not replace refunds owed to people who bought tickets for the original event.

Background

AY is one of Nigeria's best-known comedians and has run the AY Live brand for many years, as well as producing hit films. His account of the tour problems has not been confirmed by the organisers.

Agbara Nla: The Return earns over $170,000 abroad ahead of US release

30 Oct 2026, 06:44 GMT

Mike Bamiloye's faith film Agbara Nla: The Return has reportedly earned about $173,650 in the UK, Ireland and Canada and opens in US cinemas on Friday. It has made ₦408 million in Nigeria and topped the local box office for four weekends.

Read more

The faith-based Nollywood film Agbara Nla: The Return is reportedly breaking records abroad as it prepares to open in cinemas across the United States.

The figures

According to the film's distributor, the movie has earned about $173,650 across the UK, Ireland and Canada, including about £86,700 from the UK and Ireland and about CAD$82,200 from Canada. The distributor claims it recorded the biggest opening weekend ever for a Nollywood film in the UK, as well as record pre-sales. These figures come from the distributor and have not been independently verified. In Nigeria, the film has grossed about ₦408 million, making it the second-highest-grossing Nollywood film of 2026, and has held the number one spot for four weekends in a row.

US release

The film opens in American cinemas on Friday, 30 October 2026, with tickets available through major cinema chains, the distributor announced on Instagram this week.

Background

Agbara Nla: The Return is a modern retelling of the 1993 classic Agbara Nla by Mount Zion Faith Ministries founder Mike Bamiloye, who reprises his role as the herbalist Isawuru. The original film is one of the best-known Christian movies in Nigeria and was watched by millions on video. The new version has drawn church groups and families to cinemas in large numbers.

Why it matters

The film's success abroad shows the growing audience for Nigerian faith-based stories among the diaspora, and could encourage more Nollywood releases in foreign cinemas.

"My romantic side is off social media!" Jarvis reacts to online drama involving Peller

30 Oct 2026, 15:08 GMT

Streamer Jarvis says she will no longer show her romantic moments with Peller online, after people turned their playful behaviour into a story of disrespect. In a message on her WhatsApp channel, she said nobody has the right to tell her how to live or love.

Read more

Popular streamer Jarvis has finally spoken out after the recent controversy surrounding her relationship with Peller, and it appears she has had enough of people interfering in their private lives.

What Jarvis said

In a message shared on her WhatsApp channel, Jarvis made it clear that she will no longer display her romantic moments with Peller on social media.

According to her, the two have spent the past two to three years loving each other, playing, arguing and settling their differences like siblings. Now, she says, people have turned their usual playful behaviour into a story of disrespect, and she is clearly not happy about it.

"Nobody tells me how to live my life"

Jarvis questioned why people expect her relationship to look perfect all the time, and reminded critics that nobody has the right to dictate how she should live or love.

She also took a swipe at those who constantly judge other people's relationships while pretending their own marriages are perfect. "Mr and Mrs All Perfect, my foot! Nobody tells me how to live my life," she wrote.

Choosing privacy

For Jarvis, the decision is simple: she would rather enjoy her relationship privately than keep putting her happiness on display for strangers to analyse, criticise and argue about.

She ended her message by saying people could call her whatever names they liked, but their opinions would never break her.

Fans who enjoy watching Jarvis and Peller's romantic moments online may now have to get used to seeing much less of that side of their relationship.

But here is the question: when celebrities constantly share their relationships online, can they really expect people not to have opinions about what they see?

Sowore says armed thugs attacked and abducted him in Ado-Ekiti restaurant

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has alleged that armed thugs attacked him inside a restaurant in Ado-Ekiti and kidnapped him. The claim, posted on his social media accounts on Friday, had not been confirmed by the police at the time of reporting.

Read more

Omoyele Sowore, the presidential candidate of the African Action Congress (AAC) for 2027, has raised the alarm that he was attacked and abducted by armed thugs in Ado-Ekiti, the capital of Ekiti State.

What he said

In a Facebook post on Friday, 30 October 2026, Sowore wrote that thugs had attacked him and his team inside a restaurant in Ado-Ekiti. A further post said he had been kidnapped by armed men and that his location was unknown. His campaign repeated the alarm on his official X account. It was not immediately clear how the attackers got into the restaurant, how many people were involved, or whether anyone with him was hurt.

Official response

At the time of reporting, neither the police nor the Ekiti State government had issued a statement confirming the incident, and several news outlets said they could not independently verify the claims. Further updates on his whereabouts are expected.

Background

Sowore, the founder of the online news platform SaharaReporters, has run for president before and is seeking the office again in 2027. He has been one of the loudest critics of the government in recent weeks, condemning the remand of actress and AAC governorship candidate Doris Ogala and claiming that President Bola Tinubu's trip to Saudi Arabia was for medical care, a claim the Presidency rejected. He has also accused the police of using the Cybercrimes Act to protect powerful people. The alleged attack comes amid rising tension and reports of violence at political events as the January 2027 elections draw closer.

How people reacted

Supporters called on the police and the Department of State Services to find him quickly, while others urged caution until the facts are confirmed.
