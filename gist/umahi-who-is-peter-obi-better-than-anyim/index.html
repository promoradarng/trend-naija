"Who is Peter Obi better than? Is he better than me or Pius Anyim?": Minister Dave Umahi questions Obi's political superiority

3 Nov 2026, 22:36 GMT

Nigeria's political arguments have taken another interesting turn after the Minister of Works, David Umahi, questioned why former Anambra State Governor Peter Obi should be considered politically superior to other prominent leaders in the country.

What Umahi said

In remarks attributed to him, Umahi reportedly asked a direct question: "Who is Peter Obi better than? Is he better than me? Is he better than Anyim Pius Anyim?"

The comments have brought an old political argument back into focus, particularly in the South-East, where debates about leadership, political experience and who deserves national support continue to divide opinion.

The three men

Umahi is a former governor of Ebonyi State and now serves as Minister of Works. Anyim Pius Anyim is a former Senate President and former Secretary to the Government of the Federation. Both have held important positions in Nigeria.

Peter Obi, a former governor of Anambra State who contested for president under the Labour Party in 2023 and is now a member of the NDC, has built a strong following, particularly among young Nigerians who see him as an alternative to the traditional political establishment.

What makes one politician better than another?

Umahi's reported remarks raise a fair question. Is it the number of offices someone has held? The projects completed while in government? Or should Nigerians judge leaders by their integrity, performance, accountability and ability to improve ordinary people's lives?

All three men have political records that Nigerians can examine, and perhaps that is where the real argument should begin.

Rather than letting politicians decide who is superior through public arguments, Nigerians should be the ones examining their achievements and deciding who deserves their confidence.

"Grow up, the office is too big for jokes": Sule Lamido tells Shettima after VP claims he backs Tinubu

19 Nov 2026, 17:21 GMT

Former Jigawa Governor Sule Lamido has told Vice-President Kashim Shettima to be more careful with his public comments, after Shettima claimed Lamido supports President Tinubu and the APC. Lamido firmly distanced himself from the ruling party.

Read more

Former Jigawa State Governor Sule Lamido has told Vice-President Kashim Shettima to be more careful with his public comments, saying the office of the Vice-President is "too big for jokes".

How it started

Lamido was reacting to a claim by Shettima that he now supports President Bola Tinubu and the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Lamido rejected the claim outright and distanced himself from the ruling party.

"Ikon Allah! I didn't join the APC in 2014, only to join now in 2026?" he said, using a Hausa expression of astonishment. "Even though it was a Kanuri man who said it, I'm not surprised. But in your local dialect, the answer is 'Kattuwunum!'"

"Grow up"

Lamido described Shettima as intelligent and eloquent, but said the office of the Vice-President demands seriousness and restraint.

He also criticised some past remarks attributed to Shettima about former Vice-President Yemi Osinbajo, former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar and former Senate President Ahmad Lawan.

Political background

Lamido, a veteran politician and two-term governor of Jigawa State, has long been a member of the opposition. His sharp response makes clear that, whatever claims are made, he is not switching sides ahead of 2027.

Shettima has not publicly responded to Lamido's remarks.

Over to you

Was Lamido right to tell the Vice-President to "grow up", or was his response too harsh?

SDP's Adewole Adebayo vows to end Boko Haram in three months and cut petrol to ₦200

19 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

SDP presidential candidate Adewole Adebayo has reportedly promised to defeat Boko Haram within three months and bring petrol down to ₦200 a litre if elected. He spoke at a rally in Yola.

Read more

Social Democratic Party (SDP) presidential candidate Prince Adewole Adebayo has reportedly promised to end the Boko Haram insurgency within his first three months in office if he wins the 2027 election.

The rally

Adebayo made the pledge on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, at the SDP presidential campaign rally in Yola, Adamawa State, according to a statement from the party's campaign spokesman.

He blamed poor governance for the insecurity in the North-East, recalling the military campaigns to retake towns such as Mubi, Madagali, Michika, Gwoza and Bama, and argued that no government had properly dealt with the humanitarian fallout.

Other promises

His plans reportedly include bringing petrol down to ₦200 per litre, expanding the use of CNG, affordable food, free education and better healthcare.

The race

Adebayo is one of several candidates in the 16 January 2027 presidential election, alongside President Bola Tinubu (APC), Atiku Abubakar (ADC), Peter Obi (NDC) and others.

Pastor Paul Eneche's sermon on unwanted leaders goes viral, seen as a jab at Tinubu

19 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

A sermon by Pastor Paul Eneche saying leaders the people do not want should not contest has gone viral. Many on social media read it as aimed at President Tinubu ahead of 2027.

Read more

A sermon by Pastor Paul Eneche has gone viral, with many social media users interpreting it as a message aimed at President Bola Tinubu ahead of the 2027 election.

What he said

In the clip, posted on Thursday, 19 November 2026, the cleric said a leader who is not wanted by the people has no business seeking or holding power. He argued that leadership should come from the genuine wishes of the governed, not from money or manipulation.

He questioned why anyone would spend money to impose themselves on people who do not want them, and said such people should not contest at all.

Strong warning

He is also reported to have issued a warning to those he described as the architects of terrorism in Nigeria.

Reactions

The clip spread quickly online. While Eneche did not name anyone in the portion widely shared, many viewers linked his remarks to the President. No response from the Presidency was included in the reports.

Atiku asks Tinubu to release full Villa interview, raises questions over $4.5m lobbying deal

18 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has challenged President Tinubu to release the unedited recording of a recent Villa interview. His campaign questioned whether it was linked to a $4.5 million US lobbying contract.

Read more

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has challenged President Bola Tinubu to release the full, unedited recording of a recent interview at the Presidential Villa.

The claims

In a statement issued on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, through his campaign's director of strategic communication, Phrank Shaibu, Atiku raised questions about whether the interview was funded through a $4.5 million foreign lobbying contract paid on Nigeria's behalf.

He pointed to US filings that reportedly list a consultant working with the lobbying firm DCI Group on work for the Republic of Nigeria. The filings are said to record a $4.5 million retainer paid to the firm, but do not identify the interview as one of the services covered.

What Atiku wants

The former vice president called for the unedited recording to be made public and for the government to explain the lobbying arrangement.

The other side

Lobbyist deletes posts

US lobbyist Diana London has since deleted posts on X about her meeting with President Tinubu. She had shared photos on Tuesday showing her with the President at the Villa, with cameras and recording equipment visible, and praised the US-Nigeria security partnership. The posts were no longer available on Wednesday morning, and she had not publicly explained their removal.

No response from the Presidency or the APC campaign to Atiku's questions was included in the reports.
