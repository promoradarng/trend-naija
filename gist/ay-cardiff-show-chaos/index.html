The Benue entertainment industry is mourning popular dancer and entertainer Kelvin Ngutor, known as Benue Chris Brown, who was reportedly killed by suspected armed robbers in Makurdi, the state capital.

What happened

Ngutor was reportedly attacked on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, by suspected robbers who allegedly took his motorcycle. A former aide to Governor Hyacinth Alia, Terver Atu, who announced the incident on social media, alleged that the attackers had been waiting for him at the entrance of an estate.

Tributes

Colleagues and admirers have been remembering the young entertainer for his talent, passion and determination to build a career in entertainment.

Police

No statement from the police about the incident was included in the reports.

Former Big Brother Naija housemate Saskay has called out President Bola Tinubu after she said she missed her flight because of a road closure near the airport.

In a post on X, the reality TV star said security operatives blocked a section of the airport road because the President was attending a conference nearby. She said the restriction left many motorists stuck in traffic for hours, causing her and several other travellers to miss their flights.

Her complaint

Visibly frustrated, Saskay questioned why ordinary citizens going about their business should be inconvenienced in this way.

No response yet

There was no official response to her complaint in the reports.

Former senator Aishatu Binani announces death of her father

21 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Former Adamawa governorship candidate and ex-senator Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, known as Binani, has announced the death of her father after a brief illness. His funeral prayer was fixed for Saturday at the Central Mosque.

Read more

Former senator and Adamawa State governorship candidate Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, popularly known as Binani, has announced the death of her father.

The announcement

In a post on social media, Binani said her father died after a brief illness in Abuja. She opened her message with the Islamic expression of condolence and thanked God despite the loss.

Funeral arrangements

She said the funeral prayer, known as Jana'iza, would take place on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the Central Mosque after the afternoon Zuhr prayer.

Who she is

Binani is a former senator who contested the Adamawa governorship election in 2023. She is now a member of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).
