Tinubu kicks off 2027 campaign in Kaduna on Saturday

23 Oct 2026, 13:55 BST

The APC has lined up rallies for President Tinubu in 12 states, starting in Kaduna on 24 October and ending in Lagos on 9 January 2027. Port Harcourt, Jos, Ibadan, Maiduguri, Enugu, Bauchi, Sokoto, Asaba, Kano and Yola are also on the list. Kaduna's deputy governor says the state is ready to host the flag-off, and security operatives have been posted to guard campaign posters and billboards.
