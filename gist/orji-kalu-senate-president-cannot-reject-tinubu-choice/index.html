2027 Senate presidency: Orji Uzor Kalu says he cannot reject Tinubu's choice as political battle begins

10 Nov 2026, 01:13 GMT

Senator Orji Uzor Kalu has openly said he would be willing to become Nigeria's next Senate President, and would have no reason to turn down the position if President Bola Ahmed Tinubu backed him for it.

"I can't say no"

The former Abia State governor, who represents Abia North Senatorial District, made the statement in a television interview, as talk about the leadership of the next National Assembly begins to gather pace ahead of the 2027 general elections.

"If Tinubu chooses me as the next Senate President, I can't say no," Kalu said.

Brief as it was, the statement has added another twist to the political calculations already taking shape within the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

Zoning to the South-East?

For some time, there has been talk of zoning the next Senate presidency to the South-East. Nothing has been officially agreed, but that has not stopped political figures from being linked to the position.

One of the names being mentioned is Imo State Governor Hope Uzodimma, who served as senator for Imo West before becoming governor. His reported interest has sparked political conversation, given his influence within the APC and his relationship with the party's national leadership.

There have also been reports of reactions involving FCT Minister Nyesom Wike, although the extent of any disagreement over the position remains unclear.

Kalu versus Uzodimma

Both Kalu and Uzodimma are experienced politicians from the South-East. Both have served in the Senate, and both have strong relationships within the ruling party.

The big question is whether the APC will actually zone the Senate presidency to the South-East in 2027, and if so, who will win the party's backing. For now, there is no official confirmation that the position has been reserved for the region.

The senators decide

It is worth remembering that the President has no constitutional power to appoint the Senate President. Senators elect their presiding officer themselves when a new National Assembly is inaugurated.

Even so, presidential support and internal party negotiations often play a major role in deciding who eventually emerges.

Whether Kalu's remarks signal serious ambition or simply a willingness to serve if called upon remains to be seen. What is clear is that the race for the leadership of the next National Assembly is already attracting attention, even before Nigerians go to the polls.

At an APC rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, President Tinubu raised the hands of the party's governorship candidates for Oyo, Ogun and Lagos. He also guaranteed improved electricity and promised a factory in Ibadan.

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos States at a campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The endorsements

Telling the crowd he would show them who to vote for as governor, Tinubu raised the hands of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC candidate in Oyo; Senator Solomon Adeola, known as Yayi, the party's candidate in Ogun; and Lagos Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, the APC flagbearer in Lagos. Promise on power

During the visit, the President also assured Nigerians of better electricity supply, saying the Minister of Power and his team were working hard. He added that the government would build a factory in Ibadan.

The rally is part of the APC's campaign ahead of the 16 January 2027 elections.

Reactions from the opposition parties to the rally were not included in the reports.

Jimmy Odukoya sues sister and Fountain of Life trustees over his removal

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court in Lagos against his sister, Tolu Odukoya, and members of the Fountain of Life Church board over his removal as senior pastor.

Read more

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has gone to court over his removal as senior pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, filing a suit against his sister and several members of the church's board of trustees.

The suit

The suit was filed at the Federal High Court in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. Eight people are named as respondents, including his sister Tolu Odukoya, interim leader Pastor Rotimi Okpaise and other trustees.

The filing marks a significant escalation in the leadership dispute at the Ilupeju church founded by his late father, Taiwo Odukoya.

Background

Earlier this week, the trustees announced that Jimmy had been removed because his appointment did not reflect his father's succession wishes, and released a 2021 video in which the late founder reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his successor. Jimmy has insisted his removal is invalid. A Lagos State High Court had already granted the trustees an order barring him from acting as senior pastor while their own suit is heard.

What happens next

The court is expected to fix a date to hear the case. The trustees had not responded publicly to the new suit in the reports.

The Nigerian Education Loan Fund (NELFUND) is considering an increase to the ₦20,000 monthly upkeep allowance it pays to student beneficiaries.

What was said

NELFUND's Managing Director, Akin Sawyer, said in a television interview on Friday, 20 November 2026, that the allowance was being reviewed in light of the economic pressures facing students.

He said the matter is under constant review, but stressed that any increase would need careful consideration.

Background

NELFUND, set up under President Bola Tinubu's administration, gives loans to students in tertiary institutions and pays a monthly upkeep allowance to beneficiaries.
