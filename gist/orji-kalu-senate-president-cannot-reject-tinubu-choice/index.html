2027 Senate presidency: Orji Uzor Kalu says he cannot reject Tinubu's choice as political battle begins

10 Nov 2026, 01:13 GMT

Senator Orji Uzor Kalu has openly said he would be willing to become Nigeria's next Senate President, and would have no reason to turn down the position if President Bola Ahmed Tinubu backed him for it.

"I can't say no"

The former Abia State governor, who represents Abia North Senatorial District, made the statement in a television interview, as talk about the leadership of the next National Assembly begins to gather pace ahead of the 2027 general elections.

"If Tinubu chooses me as the next Senate President, I can't say no," Kalu said.

Brief as it was, the statement has added another twist to the political calculations already taking shape within the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

Zoning to the South-East?

For some time, there has been talk of zoning the next Senate presidency to the South-East. Nothing has been officially agreed, but that has not stopped political figures from being linked to the position.

One of the names being mentioned is Imo State Governor Hope Uzodimma, who served as senator for Imo West before becoming governor. His reported interest has sparked political conversation, given his influence within the APC and his relationship with the party's national leadership.

There have also been reports of reactions involving FCT Minister Nyesom Wike, although the extent of any disagreement over the position remains unclear.

Kalu versus Uzodimma

Both Kalu and Uzodimma are experienced politicians from the South-East. Both have served in the Senate, and both have strong relationships within the ruling party.

The big question is whether the APC will actually zone the Senate presidency to the South-East in 2027, and if so, who will win the party's backing. For now, there is no official confirmation that the position has been reserved for the region.

The senators decide

It is worth remembering that the President has no constitutional power to appoint the Senate President. Senators elect their presiding officer themselves when a new National Assembly is inaugurated.

Even so, presidential support and internal party negotiations often play a major role in deciding who eventually emerges.

Whether Kalu's remarks signal serious ambition or simply a willingness to serve if called upon remains to be seen. What is clear is that the race for the leadership of the next National Assembly is already attracting attention, even before Nigerians go to the polls.

Abia governor Alex Otti has reportedly arrived in Enugu to join other South-East governors in receiving President Tinubu for the APC's South-East campaign rally. His appearance comes days after Tinubu said Otti was with him.

Abia State Governor Alex Otti reportedly arrived in Enugu on Saturday, 28 November 2026, to join other South-East governors and political leaders in receiving President Bola Tinubu ahead of the APC's South-East presidential campaign rally.

The arrival

Otti, who came with his wife, was reportedly received at the Akanu Ibiam International Airport by Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. He was also said to be listed among the leaders present at the rally at Michael Okpara Square in Enugu.

'He's with me'

His appearance came days after President Tinubu, speaking at a meeting with Igbo leaders in Lagos, claimed that Otti was among the South-East governors backing his re-election, recalling that the two had worked together before Otti became governor.

Mixed reactions

Otti's presence has reportedly sparked debate on social media, as he was elected governor on the Labour Party platform. Some users questioned why he attended an APC campaign event, while others said what mattered most was his performance in Abia State.

Background

The rally is part of the APC's zonal campaign tour ahead of the 16 January 2027 presidential election, as the NDC, ADC and other opposition parties also step up their campaigns.

INEC holds nationwide mock accreditation in 17,656 polling units

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

INEC is conducting a mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise across 17,656 polling units in all 36 states and the FCT on Saturday. It is testing its upgraded BVAS machines and security arrangements.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) is conducting a nationwide mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise on Saturday, 28 November 2026.

The exercise

The test is taking place at 17,656 polling units across the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. It is designed to test INEC's upgraded Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS), the result transmission process and election security arrangements under simulated election-day conditions. Sample ballot papers are being used.

Why it matters

The exercise comes ahead of the presidential and National Assembly elections on 16 January 2027. INEC has faced criticism in past elections over delays in uploading results, and this week the commission named nine states as hotspots for campaign violence.

Lagos adjusts

Lagos State cancelled its monthly environmental sanitation exercise, which had been scheduled for the same morning, so that residents could take part.

First Lady Oluremi Tinubu has challenged the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) to investigate the funds she uses for her empowerment programmes, insisting she does not use government money.

What she said

Speaking on Friday, 27 November 2026, at the South-South APC women's presidential campaign rally in Warri, Delta State, the First Lady said wealthy Nigerians willingly donate to her charitable work because they trust her not to misuse their money. She said she had been involved in charity for years, had served three terms as a senator, and did not need to beg anyone for money.

Peace message

She also urged APC supporters not to fight anyone during the campaign, saying the party was confident of victory.

Background

The rally took place in Delta State days after a deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Ughelli North, and amid growing concern about political violence ahead of the 2027 elections.
