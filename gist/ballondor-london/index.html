Messi booed as Inter Miami crash out of MLS Cup play-offs

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Inter Miami's MLS Cup defence has ended after a 1-0 loss to Columbus Crew, with Lionel Messi reportedly booed during the match. Columbus won the first-round series 2-0.

Read more

Inter Miami's defence of the MLS Cup is over after a 1-0 defeat to Columbus Crew on Friday, 27 November 2026, in the second game of their first-round play-off series. Lionel Messi was reportedly booed and jeered during the match.

How it happened

Columbus had already won game one 3-0 in Florida, so Inter Miami needed a win in Ohio to force a deciding third game. The match appeared to be heading for penalties until Santiago Rodriguez scored in the second minute of added time after being set up by Casemiro, completing a 2-0 series win for Columbus.

A tough night for Messi

Messi reportedly missed a clear early chance from close range and struggled to keep possession. He did deliver a free-kick that Casemiro headed in during the 76th minute, but the goal was ruled out for offside.

A poor season

The defeat capped a disappointing campaign for Inter Miami, who also went out of the CONCACAF Champions Cup in the last 16 and failed to get past the league phase of the Leagues Cup.

WWE star Bronson Reed dies at 38

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

WWE has announced the death of wrestler Bronson Reed, real name Jermaine Haley, at the age of 38 from medical complications. He had been seriously injured at a WWE event in Saudi Arabia three weeks earlier.

Read more

WWE has announced the death of wrestler Bronson Reed, whose real name was Jermaine Haley, at the age of 38.

The announcement

In a statement on its website on Friday, 27 November 2026, WWE said Reed had died earlier that day from medical complications, but did not give further details. The company described him as a larger-than-life figure, a devoted family man and a beloved colleague, and sent its condolences to his pregnant wife, Paige, and their daughter.

Earlier injury

His death came about three weeks after he was seriously injured at WWE's Crown Jewel event in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on 7 November. During a tag team title match, Nigerian-born wrestler Oba Femi threw him off the stage through a stack of tables. Reed reportedly suffered broken ribs and a lacerated kidney and spent more than a week in hospital in Saudi Arabia. WWE has not said whether his death was linked to the injury.

His career

Born in Adelaide, Australia, Reed joined WWE in 2019. He won the NXT North American Championship and the 2024 André the Giant Memorial Battle Royal. Wrestlers and fans around the world have paid tribute.

Reed began wrestling in 2007 on Australia's independent circuit under the name Jonah Rock, and had two spells with WWE, from 2019 to 2021 and from 2022 to 2026. He reportedly returned to the United States around 18 November to continue his recovery.

Fans defend Oba Femi

After his death, some social media users directed personal attacks at Oba Femi over the Crown Jewel segment. Many WWE fans pushed back, saying the stage spot was a planned, scripted performance and that the Nigerian wrestler was not to blame. No official cause of death has been given, and WWE has not linked the incident to his death.

Chelsea suffer third straight Premier League defeat with 3-2 loss at Forest

28 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Chelsea lost 3-2 to Nottingham Forest at the City Ground on Friday, their third Premier League defeat in a row. Emmanuel Emegha scored twice but could not prevent the loss.

Chelsea's poor run continued on Friday, 27 November 2026, as they lost 3-2 to Nottingham Forest at the City Ground, their third Premier League defeat in a row.

The match

Igor Jesus gave Forest the lead inside five minutes, and the home side led at half-time. Early in the second half, Morgan Gibbs-White scored from the penalty spot after Wesley Fofana fouled Liam Delap. Emmanuel Emegha pulled a goal back for Chelsea, but Gibbs-White restored the two-goal lead soon after. Emegha scored again late on, but Chelsea ran out of time.

Alonso's verdict

Manager Xabi Alonso said after the game that his side were struggling with confidence.

Poor form

Chelsea have now lost four of their last five league games, with their only win in that run coming against Manchester United. Last weekend they were beaten 4-1 at home by Leeds, after which Cole Palmer and Joao Pedro were seen exchanging words on the touchline.

Alex Otti joins South-East governors to receive Tinubu at Enugu APC rally

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Abia governor Alex Otti has reportedly arrived in Enugu to join other South-East governors in receiving President Tinubu for the APC's South-East campaign rally. His appearance comes days after Tinubu said Otti was with him.

Read more

Abia State Governor Alex Otti reportedly arrived in Enugu on Saturday, 28 November 2026, to join other South-East governors and political leaders in receiving President Bola Tinubu ahead of the APC's South-East presidential campaign rally.

The arrival

Otti, who came with his wife, was reportedly received at the Akanu Ibiam International Airport by Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. He was also said to be listed among the leaders present at the rally at Michael Okpara Square in Enugu.

'He's with me'

His appearance came days after President Tinubu, speaking at a meeting with Igbo leaders in Lagos, claimed that Otti was among the South-East governors backing his re-election, recalling that the two had worked together before Otti became governor.

Mixed reactions

Otti's presence has reportedly sparked debate on social media, as he was elected governor on the Labour Party platform. Some users questioned why he attended an APC campaign event, while others said what mattered most was his performance in Abia State.

Background

The rally is part of the APC's zonal campaign tour ahead of the 16 January 2027 presidential election, as the NDC, ADC and other opposition parties also step up their campaigns.
