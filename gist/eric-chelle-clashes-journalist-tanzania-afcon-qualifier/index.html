Eric Chelle reportedly snaps at journalist over tactics ahead of Tanzania return leg

16 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Super Eagles head coach Eric Chelle reportedly clashed with a journalist at a press conference in Tanzania on Monday, 16 November 2026, ahead of Nigeria's second-leg AFCON 2027 qualifier.

What happened

The session is said to have become tense when a reporter questioned Chelle's formation and player selection. The coach reportedly responded firmly, drawing a line between his job and that of the media, and said his decisions are shaped by the players he has available and the opponents, not outside opinion. A video of the exchange was shared on X.

It is not the first time Chelle has had a heated exchange with Nigerian journalists over his choices.

The match

Nigeria arrived in Tanzania on Sunday and held their final training session on Monday. The Super Eagles won the first leg 3-1 in Uyo, so a good result in the return leg in Zanzibar on Tuesday would put them firmly on course for the tournament.

Injury worries

The team will be without Ademola Lookman, who has been ruled out for several weeks with an injury, while Victor Osimhen is also absent injured, leaving the attack short of two key players.

  • Nigeria lead Tanzania 3-1 from the first leg.
  • The return leg is on Tuesday in Zanzibar.
  • Lookman and Osimhen are both out injured.

Raheem Sterling given suspended jail term for dangerous driving

25 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has been handed a 12-month prison sentence, suspended for 18 months, after pleading guilty to dangerous driving. The court heard he was inhaling from a balloon while driving his Lamborghini.

Read more

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has avoided an immediate prison term after a court handed him a 12-month sentence, suspended for 18 months, for dangerous driving.

The sentence

The sentence was handed down on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, at Basingstoke Magistrates' Court. The judge said the 31-year-old had shown a conscious disregard for the safety of other road users.

What happened

The court heard that Sterling drove his Lamborghini on the M25 and M3 motorways during the morning rush hour on 28 May. Witnesses told police they saw him weaving between lanes while inhaling from a balloon. Officers who attended his later crash found him in the passenger seat with two canisters of nitrous oxide.

Background

Sterling, who most recently played for Dutch club Feyenoord, pleaded guilty to dangerous driving in September.

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie settle long legal battle over French winery

26 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled their years-long dispute over Château Miraval out of court. The terms were not disclosed.

Read more

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled their long-running legal battle over the sale of their French winery, Château Miraval.

The settlement

In a joint statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the former couple said they had resolved the case out of court in the best interests of their family and would not comment further. They added that any comments attributed to anonymous sources did not come from either of them or anyone close to them. The terms of the settlement were not disclosed.

The dispute

Pitt, 62, and Jolie, 51, bought the estate in the south of France in 2008 and had been in dispute over it since they split in 2016. In 2022, Pitt sued Jolie, claiming they had agreed that neither would sell their share without the other's consent, and accusing her of selling her stake to a Russian businessman without his permission. Jolie disputed his account in her response.
