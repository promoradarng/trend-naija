VeryDarkMan brings out cash counting machine after claiming record ₦90 million sales in one day

1 Nov 2026, 16:09 GMT

Social media activist and businessman VeryDarkMan (VDM) has once again got people talking, this time after bringing out a cash counting machine to count money reportedly received from the sale of his RATEL products.

Photos show him and a colleague surrounded by bundles of naira notes, with an open suitcase full of cash, stacks of money on cartons of RATEL power stations and a counting machine beside them.

A record day

According to VDM, the business recorded about ₦90 million in sales in one day, the biggest single-day figure since the products were launched.

Ready to show proof

VDM knows how Nigerians think. Once you mention ₦90 million in one day, some people will immediately start asking questions, while others will simply refuse to believe it.

To clear the doubts, he has promised to go a step further and make his bank account statement public so people can see the transactions for themselves. He appeared confident about the figures and made it clear he was ready to back up his claims with evidence.

A fast-growing brand

What makes this development interesting is how quickly the RATEL business appears to be gaining attention. From importing products from China to demonstrating how they perform in public, VDM has continued to attract customers and supporters.

However, the ₦90 million figure has yet to be independently verified, and sales revenue should not be confused with profit.

Over to you

If VDM can truly generate ₦90 million in sales in a single day, imagine what this business could become in the next few years.

VeryDarkMan urges Obi, Atiku and Makinde to form one party to face Tinubu

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one political party ahead of the 2027 election.

What he said

In a post on Instagram, VDM said the three should sit down together and form one party to defeat President Bola Tinubu. He argued that they need to set aside their personal ambitions, and that a united front would make the contest against the ruling party more competitive.

Background

Obi of the NDC, Atiku of the ADC and Makinde of the APM are expected at an opposition summit in Abuja on Monday, 30 November, where parties are due to sign a cooperation pact. NAFDAC shuts Abuja's Zuba International Market over fake products worth ₦300m

27 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

NAFDAC has shut Zuba International Market in Abuja after a joint operation with police and the DSS reportedly uncovered counterfeit and unwholesome goods worth more than ₦300 million. Over 160 shops were inspected.

Read more

The National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) has shut down Zuba International Market in the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja.

The operation

The closure followed a joint enforcement operation with the Nigeria Police Force and the Department of State Services, which reportedly uncovered counterfeit and unwholesome products worth more than ₦300 million. The exercise covered more than 160 shops in the market.

Why now

NAFDAC said the closure was part of a wider crackdown on substandard and fake goods ahead of the festive season.
