Obi says election riggers will go to jail under him as Keyamo mocks his Nasarawa remarks

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has said anyone who rigs elections under his government will go to prison. He made the comments at a party town hall in Nasarawa State on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, during a campaign visit that has also drawn a sharp response from the ruling party.

What Obi said

Asked what his party would do about rigging in 2027, Obi said that under his presidency INEC officials or anyone else who manipulated elections would be jailed. He said strengthening political parties and the electoral process would make rigging impossible, and that the law would apply equally to everyone, from the President to the ordinary citizen. He also proposed that candidates for public office should prove they attended primary and secondary school, with records open to public checks, and said anyone found to have forged certificates would face prison.

Keyamo's reaction

In a radio interview during the same visit, Obi argued that Nigeria would be much richer if public money had been kept in banks earning interest instead of being mismanaged. He also said the results of government projects should speak for themselves without ceremonies or television coverage. Aviation Minister Festus Keyamo seized on those comments in a post on X on Thursday, describing them in strongly mocking terms and saying he did not know whether to laugh or cry. He claimed the remarks explained what he described as a fall in Obi's popularity.

Background

Obi, a former Anambra governor, came third in the 2023 presidential election as the Labour Party candidate and is now running on the NDC ticket with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. Exchanges between his camp and senior APC figures have grown more heated since campaigns began this month, with both sides trading personal attacks online.

What happens next

Obi is expected to continue his tour of the North Central, while the APC campaign has indicated it will keep challenging his record in office.

  • Obi says INEC officials who rig elections will be jailed if he wins.
  • He wants candidates' school records to be open to public checks.
  • Keyamo mocked his comments about keeping public money in banks.

What his campaign said

In a statement issued on Sunday, 22 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, the campaign is said to have stressed that no group presenting itself as Atiku's supporters had the authority to announce a withdrawal on his behalf.

The statement reportedly accused those behind the claim of borrowing tactics it associated with the ruling APC, such as presenting so-called supporters from a rival camp. SERAP sues INEC and data commission over suspected AI-generated photos in voter register

22 Nov 2026, 11:58 GMT

SERAP has filed a Federal High Court lawsuit asking for an independent investigation into suspected AI-generated photographs and other questionable records in INEC's preliminary voter register, and into whether voters' personal data was unlawfully accessed or altered.

Read more

The Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project (SERAP) has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court seeking an independent investigation into suspected artificial intelligence-generated photographs and other questionable records in INEC's preliminary Register of Voters.

The lawsuit

The suit, numbered FHC/ABJ/CS/2863/2026, names the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) as defendants.

SERAP is asking the court to compel the data protection commission to examine the relevant records and electronic data held by INEC.

It also wants investigators to establish whether voters' personal information was unlawfully accessed or altered, and whether the Nigeria Data Protection Act was breached.

What INEC has said

INEC has already acknowledged that suspected AI-generated photographs appeared in the preliminary register. The commission said its Automated Biometric Identification System flagged 268 records involving suspected AI images or other irregularities.

INEC insists the published register is not final, and says verification and data-cleaning will be completed before the register is certified on 15 December.

What the court has not decided

No court has found INEC responsible for manipulating the register, and the case has not yet been given a public hearing date.

Why it matters

The voter register is the foundation of any credible election. With the 2027 general elections approaching, questions about its accuracy, and about how voters' personal data is protected, go to the heart of public trust in the process.

Pochettino's Man United reign starts with 4-0 thrashing at Liverpool

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United manager ended in a 4-0 defeat at Anfield after Lenny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute. United are 19th in the Premier League.

Read more

Mauricio Pochettino's time in charge of Manchester United got off to a painful start on Sunday, 22 November 2026, as Liverpool beat his side 4-0 at Anfield.

How the game went

United's plans fell apart early when defender Lenny Yoro was shown a red card in the eighth minute, leaving them with ten men for more than 80 minutes. Liverpool quickly took advantage. Dominik Szoboszlai opened the scoring in the 10th minute, and Florian Wirtz doubled the lead three minutes later.

Liverpool kept their two-goal lead until half-time and added two more after the break through Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.

Where it leaves the clubs

The result left United in 19th place, inside the Premier League relegation zone, while Liverpool moved into the top four.

Pochettino's reaction

Speaking after the match, Pochettino reportedly admitted that the early red card shaped the game, but urged his players to turn their frustration into better performances in the coming matches.

Background

Pochettino took over at Old Trafford only recently. The club has been linked with several players ahead of the January window, including Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey.
