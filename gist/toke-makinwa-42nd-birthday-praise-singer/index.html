Toke Makinwa celebrates 42nd birthday with praise singer and thanksgiving

4 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Media personality Toke Makinwa has marked her 42nd birthday with a joyful thanksgiving celebration.

How she celebrated

On Tuesday, 3 November 2026, the former radio presenter shared a video of herself dancing as a praise singer performed songs of worship for her. In her caption, she said she owed God praise over a personal matter and had come to give it, adding that she was trusting God for another matter too. She also posted birthday photos in a white dress and revealed that she almost skipped the photo shoot.

Background

Toke Makinwa is one of Nigeria's best-known media personalities, a former presenter on Rhythm FM and a television host. She is also an author, businesswoman and vlogger, and has spoken openly about her personal life, including her divorce. She is a mother to a young daughter, and recently shared a light-hearted video of herself teasing her during feeding time.

How fans reacted

Fans and fellow celebrities flooded her page with birthday wishes and prayers, with many saying her thanksgiving was inspiring and praying that her other request would also be granted.

What he shared

Denrele said his father, who was born on 17 November 1941, died on Monday, 12 October 2026, after a brief illness, just weeks before the family had planned to celebrate his birthday in a big way.

Tribute

In a moving tribute, Denrele described his father as a man whose faith was part of his everyday life, not just his Sunday routine. He was a church elder, a warden and a bass singer in the choir of the Methodist church.

Nollywood actress Iheme Nancy has opened up about returning to church after four years away, crediting reality TV star Mercy Eke for bringing her back.

What she shared

In a post shared on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Nancy said a painful experience within the church in 2022, which she described as a betrayal by fellow Christians, made her walk away from her faith completely after the death of her mother.

Petrol prices fall at filling stations after Dangote cuts rate by ₦51

18 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Major marketers including AA Rano, NIPCO and AP Ardova have reportedly reduced pump prices to between ₦1,350 and ₦1,385 per litre. It follows a ₦51 per litre cut by Dangote Refinery.

Read more

Motorists are beginning to see some relief at filling stations as major marketers reduce petrol prices following a ₦51 per litre cut by the Dangote Petroleum Refinery.

The new prices

A market survey on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, reportedly showed that AA Rano, the Nigerian Independent Petroleum Company (NIPCO) and AP Ardova had lowered their petrol prices to between ₦1,350 and ₦1,385 per litre. Several filling stations are said to have cut prices by between ₦20 and ₦60 per litre, with NNPC and other marketers also adjusting their rates.

Why prices are falling

The reductions come as the cost of importing petrol has dropped, with the landing cost of imported fuel reportedly falling to about ₦1,067 per litre, below Dangote Refinery's own rate. This has put pressure on local prices.

The survey suggested more stations could follow with price cuts in the coming days.
