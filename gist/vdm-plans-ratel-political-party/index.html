VeryDarkMan says he will register his own Ratel party and fund it himself

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has announced plans to register his own political party, the Ratel Political Party, before 2032.

Paying his own way

In a video, VDM said he would fund the project with his own earnings from his businesses, rather than relying on supporters. He said he had heard the registration fee was over ₦50 million and that he would raise it. INEC's party registration guidelines set a non-refundable administrative fee of ₦50 million, and payment does not guarantee approval.

Grassroots first

VDM said the Ratel movement would begin by focusing on grassroots positions, such as local government chairmen and councillors, rather than seats in the National Assembly.

Background

'Ratel' is the name VDM uses for his followers. This week he also urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one party against President Tinubu.

  • VDM wants to register the party before 2032.
  • He says he will pay the ₦50m fee himself.
  • He plans to start with local government seats.

VeryDarkMan urges Obi, Atiku and Makinde to form one party to face Tinubu

Activist VeryDarkMan has called on Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to put their ambitions aside and unite under one party against President Tinubu. The three are due at an opposition summit on Monday.

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one political party ahead of the 2027 election.

What he said

In a post on Instagram, VDM said the three should sit down together and form one party to defeat President Bola Tinubu. He argued that they need to set aside their personal ambitions, and that a united front would make the contest against the ruling party more competitive.

Background

Obi of the NDC, Atiku of the ADC and Makinde of the APM are expected at an opposition summit in Abuja on Monday, 30 November, where parties are due to sign a cooperation pact. The organisers have said it will not be a formal merger, and Makinde's camp has denied reports that he plans to step down for Atiku.

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has called out the chairman of Ogoja Local Government Area in Cross River State, Christopher Agbeh, over a community's lack of clean water.

What he said

In a video message, VDM said it was unfortunate that in 2026 a whole community was still crying out for something as basic as water. He described the situation as disappointing and told the chairman he had two weeks to make himself useful to his people.

Lagos West senator and former deputy governor Idiat Adebule dies at 56

28 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Senator Idiat Adebule, who represented Lagos West and was formerly deputy governor of Lagos State, has reportedly died at 56. Her death reportedly came less than a day after her birthday.

Read more

Senator Idiat Adebule, who represented the Lagos West Senatorial District in the Nigerian Senate, has reportedly died at the age of 56.

What happened

Adebule was reportedly taken to a private hospital in the GRA area of Ikeja after she fell ill, where she was later pronounced dead. Her death is said to have come less than a day after she marked her 56th birthday. No official cause of death has been announced.

Her career

Adebule served as deputy governor of Lagos State before she was elected to the Senate on the platform of the All Progressives Congress (APC). At the time of her death, she was reportedly seeking re-election to a second term in the Senate in the 2027 elections.

What happens next

Her family, the Lagos State government and the Senate are expected to make formal statements and announce burial arrangements. Tributes are expected from across the political divide.

Shettima mocks Atiku and Obi at Enugu rally, suggests Obi for South-East commission job

28 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Vice President Kashim Shettima told the APC's South-East rally in Enugu that Atiku and Obi are not in the race to win. He suggested Tinubu appoint Obi to chair the South-East Development Commission, a remark that drew criticism online.

Read more

Vice President Kashim Shettima took aim at opposition candidates Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi on Saturday, 28 November 2026, during the APC's South-East presidential campaign rally in Enugu.

What he said

Shettima told supporters that the two men were not in the 2027 race to win, but for money and to cause trouble after losing. He said the APC would 'retire' Atiku to Yola, his home in Adamawa State.

Turning to Obi, the vice president said the NDC candidate had visited 17 states, including the five South-East states, most of the South-South, Kaduna and Lagos, and argued that a presidential election cannot be won with support from only a few regions. He then suggested that President Tinubu appoint Obi as chairman of the South-East Development Commission, saying it was about the only job he was fit for.

How people reacted

The remarks drew criticism from many Nigerians on X, with some accusing the vice president of attacking Obi instead of highlighting the Tinubu administration's achievements. Shettima has made similar jabs at both men at earlier rallies.

The opposition

Atiku's camp has repeatedly said he is not stepping down for anyone. Opposition parties, including Atiku's ADC, Obi's NDC and Makinde's APM, are due to meet at a summit in Abuja on Monday, 30 November.
