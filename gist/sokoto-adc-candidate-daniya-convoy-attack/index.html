Sokoto ADC governorship candidate says his convoy was attacked near airport

31 Oct 2026, 06:42 GMT

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) candidate for governor of Sokoto State, Manir Muhammad Dan'iya, has alleged that his convoy was attacked near the Sultan Abubakar III International Airport in Sokoto on Friday night, 30 October 2026.

What he says happened

According to his media aide, Aminu Abdullahi, the incident happened at about 9pm, shortly after Dan'iya had seen off former governor Aminu Waziri Tambuwal at the airport. He alleged that members of the Sokoto State Marshal and Community Guard blocked the convoy, saying they were acting on orders to keep ADC members out of the state. He said efforts to calm the situation failed before shots were fired. Dan'iya reportedly escaped unhurt, but his bulletproof vehicles were damaged, and he said two Department of State Services officers attached to his security team were taken away. He also said he had noticed unusual deployments at several entry points into the state earlier in the day.

Official response

The Sokoto State Police Command spokesperson, DSP Ahmad Rufae, said the command would take necessary action and that anyone found responsible would face the law. The Sokoto State Government and the Marshal and Community Guard had not publicly responded at the time of the report, and the allegations have not been independently verified.

What he is asking for

Dan'iya reported the matter to the state Commissioner of Police and asked the Inspector-General of Police and the head of the DSS to investigate and secure the release of the two officers. He also called on the state government to explain the alleged orders.

Background

The incident is the latest in a string of reported attacks on opposition figures as campaigning for the January 2027 elections intensifies. On the same day, AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore said he was attacked in Ado-Ekiti, and an APC rally in Katsina was disrupted earlier this week.

  • Dan'iya says his convoy was blocked and shot at; he was not hurt.
  • He blames state security personnel; the state government has not responded.
  • Police say anyone found responsible will face the law.

Adamu Garba denies endorsing Peter Obi for 2027, says he has quit partisan politics

19 Nov 2026, 17:04 GMT

Former APC presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has dismissed reports that he endorsed Peter Obi for the 2027 presidential election, saying he has withdrawn from partisan politics to focus on his businesses and Africa's digital economy.

Read more

Former All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has denied reports that he endorsed Peter Obi ahead of Nigeria's 2027 presidential election.

"The claim is false"

In a public statement dated 18 November 2026, Garba described the claim circulating online as false.

He said he has withdrawn from partisan politics and is now focusing on his business ventures and on Africa's digital economy.

His new focus

Garba named Crowwe, Tablars and Justitia SI among the projects currently receiving his attention.

He also urged the public to rely only on his official channels for accurate information about his activities.

Where did the story come from?

The origin of the endorsement claim remains unclear. No post, interview or public statement showing Garba endorsing Obi has been found.

What is clear is that Garba has publicly disputed the claim.

A familiar pattern

With the 2027 elections approaching, false claims about endorsements are spreading quickly on social media. It is the second time this week that a story about backing for Obi has been corrected, after Obi himself clarified that his Washington visit was not an endorsement from the US Congress.

Tinubu's praise singer Rarara mobbed at Abuja National Mosque

21 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Hausa praise singer Dauda Kahutu Rarara, a well-known supporter of President Tinubu, was attacked by an angry crowd at the Abuja National Mosque during Friday prayers. Security operatives struggled to hold the crowd back.

Read more

Popular Hausa praise singer Dauda Kahutu, widely known as Rarara, was attacked by an angry crowd at the Abuja National Mosque during Friday prayers on 20 November 2026.

What happened

Rarara, a prominent supporter of President Bola Tinubu, was entering the mosque with plain-clothed and uniformed security personnel when a group of people rushed at him. Security operatives tried to hold the crowd back but could not fully contain them. At one point, the singer removed his cap, which carried Tinubu's insignia.

A video of the incident has spread widely online.

Context

The incident came a week after President Tinubu's convoy was pelted with stones after Friday prayers at a mosque in Garki, Abuja, an attack for which police arrested 78 people before the Presidency directed their release.

Reactions

Paul Ibe, media adviser to ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar, condemned the violence on X while also making a political point about the incident.

Yahaya Bello says he will beat Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan 'overwhelmingly' in Kogi Central

21 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Former Kogi Governor Yahaya Bello, who plans to contest the Kogi Central senatorial seat in 2027, has claimed his political reach far exceeds that of the sitting senator, Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan.

Read more

Former Kogi State Governor Yahaya Bello has said he will win the Kogi Central senatorial seat in the 2027 elections overwhelmingly, describing the incumbent, Senator Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan, as politically junior to him.

What he said

In an interview published on Friday, 20 November 2026, Bello reportedly said his political influence in Kogi State is far greater than that of any other politician. Asked whether nearly four years out of elective office had reduced his standing, he said the break had actually added to his popularity.

He also claimed he would not need to spend his own money on the campaign, saying residents had started contributing to it.

The contest

Akpoti-Uduaghan currently represents Kogi Central in the Senate. Her response to his remarks was not included in the reports.

Peter Obi says Nigerians will 'feel the difference from day one' if he and Kwankwaso win

20 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi, speaking in Anambra alongside former Kano governor Rabiu Kwankwaso, has promised to fight corruption and use public money for the public good.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has told supporters in Anambra State that Nigerians will feel a difference from the very first day if he and Rabiu Kwankwaso win the 2027 election.

What he said

Speaking in Anambra alongside the former Kano governor, Obi reportedly said their administration would prioritise accountability, transparency and the responsible management of public funds.

He said the two of them would fight corruption and make sure public money is used for the public good, adding that Nigerians would know from day one that a new team was in charge.

Defend your votes

At an NDC town hall and rally at All Saints Anglican Cathedral in Onitsha on Friday, Obi also urged Nigerians to turn out in large numbers on 16 January 2027, stay at polling units until results are counted and transmitted, and guard against rigging. He said Nigerians should accept the result of any genuinely free and fair contest.

The context

Obi's comments came a day after Anambra Governor Charles Soludo hosted an APC rally in Awka for President Bola Tinubu and said the election was already decided. ADC chieftain Dele Momodu responded that Obi would win Anambra comfortably.

The race

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027, with Tinubu (APC), Atiku Abubakar (ADC), Obi (NDC) and other candidates in the race.
