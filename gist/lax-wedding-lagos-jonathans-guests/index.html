Goodluck and Patience Jonathan among guests at singer L.A.X's Lagos wedding

2 Nov 2026, 09:44 GMT

Singer L.A.X's wedding celebration in Lagos turned into a gathering of political, royal and entertainment heavyweights.

The wedding

The singer, whose real name is Damilola Afolabi, held his wedding reception on Victoria Island on Sunday, 1 November 2026. It was hosted by his father, Taiwo Afolabi, chairman of the SIFAX Group. The couple had earlier held a civil ceremony in Malta in September and an Islamic wedding ceremony on 29 October.

The guests

Former President Goodluck Jonathan and his wife, Patience Jonathan, were among the guests, along with the Emir of Kano, Muhammadu Sanusi II, several traditional rulers and stars from the entertainment industry. Lagos monarch Oba Saheed Elegushi and his wife were also pictured at the event.

How people reacted

Photos and videos of the former First Lady drew warm and nostalgic reactions online. Many users recalled her time in office with affection, while others remembered the viral moments from her public appearances. Fans also congratulated L.A.X on his marriage and remarked on the high-profile guest list.

Background

L.A.X rose to fame in the mid-2010s with hits such as 'Caro' with Wizkid. His father, Taiwo Afolabi, is a prominent businessman in the maritime sector and a well-known figure in Nigerian society.

  • The reception was hosted by SIFAX chairman Taiwo Afolabi.
  • The Jonathans and the Emir of Kano attended.
  • The couple had earlier wed in Malta and held a Nikkah.

IK Ogbonna and friends remember Alexx Ekubo six months after his death

12 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Actor IK Ogbonna, businessman Vintage Luxury and the late actor's sister have shared emotional tributes to Alexx Ekubo, six months after he died of kidney cancer at the age of 40.

Read more

Friends and family of the late Nollywood actor Alexx Ekubo have shared emotional tributes to mark six months since his death.

The tributes

On Thursday, 12 November 2026, actor IK Ogbonna, widely regarded as Ekubo's closest friend, posted a throwback video of the two of them on Instagram with a simple heart emoji, saying in a separate post that six months still felt like yesterday. Businessman Vintage Luxury also shared a tribute describing his late friend as rare, while Ekubo's sister posted a message saying she missed him more than life.

His death

Alexx Ekubo died on Monday, 11 May 2026, at a hospital in Lagos after complications from advanced kidney cancer. He was 40. His death shocked Nollywood, and tributes poured in from colleagues, fans and public figures. He was buried on 18 June 2026 in Arochukwu, Abia State, in a ceremony attended by many figures from the entertainment industry.

Background

Ekubo was one of Nollywood's best-known leading men and was known for his close friendship with IK Ogbonna.

Wumi Toriola confronts content creator who kept filming as she fixed her outfit

12 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Actress Wumi Toriola reportedly confronted a content creator who kept recording while she adjusted her skirt on the red carpet at a Lagos film premiere. The creator was heard apologising, and the video has sparked debate about camera etiquette.

Read more

Nollywood actress Wumi Toriola has sparked conversation online after confronting a content creator at a film premiere in Lagos.

What happened

The incident took place on Sunday, 8 November 2026, at the premiere of the film 'Five Figurines'. While posing for photos on the red carpet, the actress noticed that her skirt had shifted and stopped to adjust it, apparently expecting those filming to pause. A content creator reportedly kept recording, and Toriola responded with a sarcastic remark about where the camera was pointed. The content creator could be heard apologising.

How people reacted

The video went viral this week. Many users backed the actress, saying the person filming should have stopped, while others joked about her well-known outspokenness. The clip has prompted wider debate about privacy and the limits content creators should respect when filming celebrities at public events.
