Goodluck and Patience Jonathan among guests at singer L.A.X's Lagos wedding

2 Nov 2026, 09:44 GMT

Singer L.A.X's wedding celebration in Lagos turned into a gathering of political, royal and entertainment heavyweights.

The wedding

The singer, whose real name is Damilola Afolabi, held his wedding reception on Victoria Island on Sunday, 1 November 2026. It was hosted by his father, Taiwo Afolabi, chairman of the SIFAX Group. The couple had earlier held a civil ceremony in Malta in September and an Islamic wedding ceremony on 29 October.

The guests

Former President Goodluck Jonathan and his wife, Patience Jonathan, were among the guests, along with the Emir of Kano, Muhammadu Sanusi II, several traditional rulers and stars from the entertainment industry. Lagos monarch Oba Saheed Elegushi and his wife were also pictured at the event.

How people reacted

Photos and videos of the former First Lady drew warm and nostalgic reactions online. Many users recalled her time in office with affection, while others remembered the viral moments from her public appearances. Fans also congratulated L.A.X on his marriage and remarked on the high-profile guest list.

Background

L.A.X rose to fame in the mid-2010s with hits such as 'Caro' with Wizkid. His father, Taiwo Afolabi, is a prominent businessman in the maritime sector and a well-known figure in Nigerian society.

  • The Jonathans and the Emir of Kano attended.
  • The couple had earlier wed in Malta and held a Nikkah.

Mary Njoku questions NAFDAC chief over unsafe food products

4 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Actress and filmmaker Mary Njoku has asked NAFDAC Director-General Mojisola Adeyeye whether Nigerians are now safe from fake and unsafe products, criticising what she called a cycle of outrage with no results.

Read more

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Mary Njoku has publicly questioned the head of the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC), Professor Mojisola Adeyeye, over continuing concerns about fake and unsafe products in Nigeria.

What she said

In a social media post on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, Njoku recalled the NAFDAC chief's laughter during a recent food safety controversy, which she said she would never forget, and asked whether Nigerians are actually safe now. She criticised what she described as a familiar Nigerian cycle in which people raise an alarm, nothing is resolved, and attention moves to the next controversy. She also asked whether ordinary people now need special expertise to know whether their food is safe.

Background

Food safety has been a major public concern in Nigeria this year, with reports of fake and expired products, altered expiry dates and contaminated foods. This week, police in Kano arrested traders accused of changing expiry dates on milk, and a processor in Ondo was arrested over unhygienic food handling. NAFDAC has carried out raids and destroyed large quantities of unsafe products, but critics say enforcement remains weak.

Who is Mary Njoku

Mary Njoku is an actress, producer and the founder of the ROK Studios film company, which she sold to the French broadcaster Canal+ in 2019. She remains one of Nollywood's most influential figures.

How people reacted

Many Nigerians agreed with her, sharing their own fears about unsafe products, while others urged consumers to report suspicious goods to the agency. NAFDAC had not responded publicly at the time of reporting.

Lege Miami weeps at Tinubu rally as Eniola Badmus appears to snub him

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Comic actor Lege Miami reportedly broke down in tears while thanking MC Oluomo and others at a rally he organised for President Tinubu's re-election. A separate clip from the same day appeared to show actress Eniola Badmus ignoring him.

Nollywood actor Adams Kehinde, popularly known as Lege Miami, became emotional at a campaign event for President Bola Tinubu's re-election on Tuesday, 3 November 2026.

What happened

According to reports, the actor organised a rally attended by transport union leader Musiliu Akinsanya, known as MC Oluomo, and other personalities. In a video shared online, he broke down in tears while thanking those who had supported the event financially and taken time out of their busy schedules to attend.

The Eniola Badmus moment

In another clip that went viral the same day, actress Eniola Badmus, who also backs the President, appeared to ignore Lege Miami at a pro-Tinubu gathering. She reportedly took a seat across the room after walking in, and kept her distance even after he made a light-hearted attempt to ease the tension. The pair have had a public disagreement since 2024, linked to a dispute over matchmaking comments made online.

Background

Several Nollywood stars and entertainers have openly declared support for candidates ahead of the January 2027 elections. Eniola Badmus has been a visible supporter of the President. Supporters of other candidates, including Peter Obi and Atiku Abubakar, have also held events with entertainers in recent weeks.

How people reacted

Fans were divided over the tears, with some saying they were genuine and others mocking the display. Many joked about the frosty moment with Eniola Badmus, asking why the two always seem to be at odds.
