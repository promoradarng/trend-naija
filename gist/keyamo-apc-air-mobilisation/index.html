Tinubu names Festus Keyamo to head air mobilisation for his 2027 campaign

26 Oct 2026, 09:34 GMT

President Tinubu has appointed Aviation Minister Festus Keyamo to lead the Directorate of Air Mobilisation in the APC presidential campaign council, according to a statement from his media adviser. The FAAN managing director, Olubunmi Kuku, is named a deputy director. The directorate will handle air travel and logistics across the six geopolitical zones.

  • Engr. Farouk Ahmed Umar is the second deputy director.
  • The APC flagged off its re-election campaign in Kaduna over the weekend.
  • The NDC has also named its own campaign council.

NCDC warns 2027 elections could be postponed if Ebola spreads widely

27 Oct 2026, 08:35 GMT

The Nigeria Centre for Disease Control has reportedly said the 2027 general elections could be postponed if Ebola spreads widely across the country. The warning comes as Nigeria has no active confirmed case but has not yet been declared Ebola-free. The health minister earlier said the country could declare itself Ebola-free from 7 December.
