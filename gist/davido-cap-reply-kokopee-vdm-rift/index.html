Davido appears to dismiss Kokopee's account of his rift with VeryDarkMan

30 Oct 2026, 17:42 GMT

Davido has given a short but pointed response to claims by content creator Kokopee about the singer's fallout with social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM).

What happened

On Friday, 30 October 2026, Davido reportedly replied with a single 'cap' emoji, internet slang for a lie or exaggeration, two days after a video by Kokopee spread widely on X. Many fans took the post as a denial of Kokopee's account, although the singer did not explain it further.

Kokopee's claims

In his video, Kokopee, a close friend of VDM, said he had tried to save the friendship between the two men by passing messages between them privately. He said VDM felt betrayed by Davido, while Davido rejected that view. His most talked-about claim was that Davido personally reached out to him, and he presented what he described as evidence of their conversation.

How people reacted

Reactions were mixed. Some fans said the emoji showed Kokopee had exaggerated, while others challenged both sides to share full evidence. Some urged people to stay out of the dispute. Neither Davido nor VDM had made any further statement at the time of the report.

Background

Davido and VDM were once close allies, but their friendship broke down earlier this year. This week Davido also congratulated rapper Zlatan Ibile on his chart-topping song 'Inferno', which fans see as a jab at VDM, adding to the tension.

2Baba says election riggers deserve severe punishment amid 'rig and die' debate

27 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Singer 2Baba has said he supports tough punishment for anyone who manipulates election results, as debate continues over the 'rig and die' slogan. He said the slogan reflects Nigerians' frustration.

Read more

Music star Innocent Idibia, popularly known as 2Baba, has weighed in on the controversial 'rig and die' slogan ahead of the 2027 elections.

What he said

Speaking in a radio interview on Thursday, 26 November 2026, the African Queen singer said he supported severe punishment for anyone who tries to manipulate election results, arguing that serious consequences would discourage others from electoral malpractice.

He described the national mood as tense, and said the slogan reflected the desperation and frustration many Nigerians feel. He also said he did not see anything wrong with the slogan itself.

Background

The phrase 'rig and die' has spread widely online and at rallies. Earlier this week, INEC warned that it was dangerous, saying a slogan linking an electoral offence to death could incite mob attacks at polling units and put voters and election staff at risk. A civic group has also recorded 124 cases of electoral violence since July.

Trump calls for probe after US growth figure is revised upward

27 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

President Donald Trump has demanded an investigation after official figures revised US third-quarter growth up to 4.3%, claiming without evidence that the earlier figure was deliberately understated before the midterms.

Read more

US President Donald Trump has called for an immediate investigation into an upward revision of US economic growth figures.

The revision

On Wednesday, the US Commerce Department's Bureau of Economic Analysis revised annualised growth for the third quarter to 4.3%, up from its earlier estimate of 3.4% released on 29 October. Such revisions are a standard part of the process as more complete data becomes available.

Trump's claim

In a post on Truth Social on Thursday, 26 November 2026, Trump branded the earlier, pre-election figure fake and asked why the correct number was not released before the 3 November midterm elections. He claimed, without evidence, that the earlier figure had been deliberately understated.

Background

Trump's Republican Party suffered heavy losses in the midterms, with Democrats winning control of both the House and the Senate.

Makinde camp denies claim he will step down for Atiku

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde's campaign has dismissed as fake a claim that he plans to withdraw his presidential bid in favour of Atiku Abubakar on 30 November. The claim was made by a political commentator.

Read more

The campaign of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has dismissed as fake news a claim that he plans to drop his 2027 presidential bid in favour of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar.

The claim

Political commentator Lere Olayinka had written that Makinde, the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) candidate, would step down for Atiku on 30 November, the date of the opposition parties' summit in Abuja.

The denial

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, the verified X account linked to Makinde's campaign rejected the claim and accused the ruling APC of spreading falsehoods. The post drew both support and criticism from social media users.

Background

Makinde said this week that he remains open to working with Atiku and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu, but ruled out a formal merger of parties. Opposition parties are due to hold their second summit in Abuja on Monday. No response from the APC to the accusation was included in the reports.
