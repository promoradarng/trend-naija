Russia's ambassador to the US, Alexander Darchiev, dies at 66

11 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Alexander Darchiev, Russia's ambassador to the United States, has died at the age of 66.

What happened

The Russian Foreign Ministry announced his death on Wednesday, 11 November 2026. Ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova said he died in Moscow from an unspecified chronic illness. The ministry praised him as an outstanding diplomat and an expert on the United States, and said details of a memorial service would be released later.

His career

Darchiev became ambassador in Washington in early 2025, succeeding Anatoly Antonov. Before that, he headed the Foreign Ministry's North America department, served as Russia's ambassador to Canada and worked as a senior diplomat at the embassy in Washington. In February 2025, he led Russia's delegation at talks with the United States in Istanbul.

Background

In 2017, Russia's UN envoy Vitaly Churkin also died suddenly in New York.

MTN reopens talks to buy South Africa's Telkom

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

MTN Group has confirmed early talks to buy rival Telkom, four years after an earlier attempt collapsed. Telkom shares jumped 18% on the news.

Read more

MTN Group, Africa's largest mobile operator by subscribers, has reportedly reopened talks to acquire South African rival Telkom.

What happened

The two companies confirmed preliminary discussions on Monday, 16 November 2026, four years after an earlier round of negotiations ended without a deal. MTN said it was considering a cash offer, possibly with an option for Telkom shareholders to take MTN shares instead, but stressed the talks were at an early stage and might not lead to an agreement.

Market reaction

Telkom's shares rose about 18% to their highest level in more than seven years, while MTN's shares fell 1.7%. Telkom's enterprise value was put at about 38 billion rand, or around $2.2 billion, while MTN's market value stood at about 432 billion rand.

Why it matters

A deal would add Telkom's mobile customers to MTN's South African business, which could push MTN ahead of Vodacom as the biggest mobile operator in South Africa by subscribers.

Pope Leo hit in the eye by rosary thrown from crowd in Peru

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Pope Leo XIV was reportedly struck in the eye by a rosary thrown from the crowd as he rode through Lima in the Popemobile. He was on the last day of a 12-day South America tour.

Read more

Pope Leo XIV was reportedly hit in the eye by a rosary thrown by a worshipper as he toured Lima, Peru, on Monday, 16 November 2026.

What happened

The Pope was riding through the city in the Popemobile when the rosary, thrown from the crowd, struck him. Video of the moment shows him holding and rubbing his eye. There was no report of serious injury, and he continued to a farewell Mass at Las Palmas air base, which was said to have drawn around one million people.

A special visit

The trip marked the final day of a 12-day tour of South America, six days of which he spent in Peru. The 71-year-old has deep ties to the country. Born in Chicago, he arrived in Peru in 1985 as a 29-year-old Augustinian missionary and spent many years there, including a decade in the northern city of Chiclayo, where he was known as Padre Roberto.

He is the first American pope and the first to hold Peruvian citizenship. Tens of thousands of Peruvians lined the streets on Monday, many carrying banners in his honour.

Robert Pattinson reportedly named the new James Bond

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Robert Pattinson has reportedly been confirmed as the next James Bond, ending years of speculation over who would follow Daniel Craig. He is said to star in the upcoming 007 film directed by Denis Villeneuve.

Read more

British actor Robert Pattinson has reportedly been named the next James Bond, ending years of guessing over who would take over the role of the famous secret agent.

The announcement

The franchise is said to have confirmed the casting on Monday, 16 November 2026, with a short video showing the 40-year-old actor in a black tuxedo, pointing a gun. Pattinson is best known for the Twilight films and for currently playing Bruce Wayne in DC's Batman films.

The race for the role

Several actors, including Callum Turner, Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor, were reportedly screen-tested before the casting was announced for the new film, which will be directed by Denis Villeneuve. In a recent magazine interview, Villeneuve spoke about the pressure of taking on the franchise and said casting was still in progress at the time.

Background

Daniel Craig stepped down as 007 after five films over 15 years, ending with No Time to Die in October 2021. He was the sixth actor to play the character, after Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton and Pierce Brosnan. If confirmed, Pattinson would become the seventh actor to play Bond in the official film series.

What happens next

No release date for the new Bond film was given in the reports.

Sowore calls for legal cannabis, says Nigeria could earn up to ₦2.86 trillion a year

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has urged the federal government to legalise, regulate and tax cannabis. He pointed to revenues in Canada and US states and projected Nigeria could earn between ₦715 billion and ₦2.86 trillion yearly.

Read more

African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has called on the federal government to legalise cannabis, arguing that Nigeria is missing out on billions of naira in revenue by keeping it illegal.

What he said

In a post on X on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, Sowore said the country should build a regulated cannabis industry instead of spending resources arresting people and destroying plantations. He said he has backed legalisation since 2018.

The figures

He pointed to regulated markets abroad, citing about CA$2.2 billion in government revenue from recreational cannabis in Canada in 2023-2024, around $1.067 billion in cannabis tax in California in 2024, and roughly $255.4 million in Colorado.

Using those examples, he projected that Nigeria could earn between $550 million and $2.2 billion a year, or about ₦715 billion to ₦2.86 trillion at an exchange rate of ₦1,300 to the dollar. He acknowledged that actual earnings would depend on how any such market is designed and run.

He also argued that legalisation could create jobs in farming, pharmaceutical production and industrial hemp, and support research and exports.

Background

Cannabis remains illegal in Nigeria, and the NDLEA regularly carries out raids on farms and arrests dealers. Sowore, a long-time activist, is running for president in 2027 on the AAC platform.
