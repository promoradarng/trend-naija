No Nigerian on CAF Women's Player of the Year shortlist for the first time

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Nigeria has no player on the shortlist for the 2026 CAF Women's Player of the Year award, the first time the country has been left out since the prize was introduced in 2001, according to reports.

The nominees

The Confederation of African Football released the nominations this week. The list includes Malawian sisters Tabitha and Temwa Chawinga, Cameroon's Marie Ngah Manga, Michaely Bihina and Monique Ngock, Zambia's Barbra Banda, South Africa's Thembi Kgatlana, Morocco's Hanane Ait El Haj, Algeria's Marine Dafeur and Ghana's Portia Boakye.

Nigerians on other lists

Nigeria still features in other categories. The Super Falcons were shortlisted for Women's National Team of the Year, Janet Akekoromowei and Oluwakemi Adegbuyi were nominated for the young talent award, and Edo Queens goalkeeper Christiana Uzoma is among the contenders for Goalkeeper of the Year.

Background

Nigerian players have dominated the award over the years, with Asisat Oshoala winning it a record six times. The omission follows a disappointing 2026 Women's Africa Cup of Nations for the Super Falcons and their failure to qualify for the Women's World Cup for the first time. The team only this week received the cash rewards promised for winning the previous WAFCON.

What happens next

The 2026 CAF Awards will be held on 7 December at the Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Creative Arts in Lagos.

  • It is the first time since 2001 that no Nigerian made the list.
  • The Super Falcons are nominated for Team of the Year.
  • The awards hold on 7 December.

Ex-Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami survives attack at his Abeokuta home

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Former Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami says he fought off three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta in the early hours of Friday. Police say he has been treated and discharged from hospital.

Read more

Former Super Eagles captain and football administrator Segun Odegbami has survived an attack by three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta, Ogun State, in the early hours of Friday, 20 November 2026.

What happened

The attackers reportedly overpowered his domestic staff and driver before forcing their way into his bedroom at about 2:30am. The 74-year-old was injured in the attack.

Odegbami described the incident as an attempt on his life. He said he fought back with help from his driver and house help, and the attackers eventually fled. He said it was the closest he had ever come to death.

Police response

The Ogun State Police Command confirmed the attack and said the former international had been treated in hospital and discharged.

Who he is

Odegbami is a former captain of the Super Eagles and one of Nigeria's best-known football figures.

Manhunt ordered

Ogun State Commissioner of Police Bode Ojajuni visited Odegbami at his home and ordered an immediate manhunt for the attackers, promising they would be prosecuted once caught.

FIFA President Infantino arrives in Warri for stadium commissioning

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has arrived in Warri, Delta State, for the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium. He was received by Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former NFF president Amaju Pinnick.

Read more

FIFA President Gianni Infantino arrived in Warri, Delta State, on Friday, 20 November 2026, ahead of the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium.

The arrival

Infantino landed at Osubi Airport on a private jet and was received by Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former Nigeria Football Federation president Amaju Pinnick. He said the warm welcome showed the unifying power of football.

Third visit

It is Infantino's third visit to Nigeria. He first came in 2016 shortly after taking office, and returned in 2023 for the Aisha Buhari Cup.

Background

The visit comes days after FIFA appointed a normalisation committee to run the NFF, and as Infantino prepares to face challengers in the FIFA presidential election in March 2027.

Tinubu endorses APC governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos at Ibadan rally, promises better power

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

At an APC rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, President Tinubu raised the hands of the party's governorship candidates for Oyo, Ogun and Lagos. He also guaranteed improved electricity and promised a factory in Ibadan.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos States at a campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The endorsements

Telling the crowd he would show them who to vote for as governor, Tinubu raised the hands of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC candidate in Oyo; Senator Solomon Adeola, known as Yayi, the party's candidate in Ogun; and Lagos Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, the APC flagbearer in Lagos. He also raised the hand of Remi Oseni, the party's senatorial candidate for Oyo South.

Promise on power

During the visit, the President also assured Nigerians of better electricity supply, saying the Minister of Power and his team were working hard. He added that the government would build a factory in Ibadan.

Context

The rally is part of the APC's campaign ahead of the 16 January 2027 elections.

The other side

Reactions from the opposition parties to the rally were not included in the reports.

Jimmy Odukoya sues sister and Fountain of Life trustees over his removal

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court in Lagos against his sister, Tolu Odukoya, and members of the Fountain of Life Church board over his removal as senior pastor.

Read more

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has gone to court over his removal as senior pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, filing a suit against his sister and several members of the church's board of trustees.

The suit

The suit was filed at the Federal High Court in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. Eight people are named as respondents, including his sister Tolu Odukoya, interim leader Pastor Rotimi Okpaise and other trustees.

The filing marks a significant escalation in the leadership dispute at the Ilupeju church founded by his late father, Taiwo Odukoya.

Background

Earlier this week, the trustees announced that Jimmy had been removed because his appointment did not reflect his father's succession wishes, and released a 2021 video in which the late founder reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his successor. Jimmy has insisted his removal is invalid. A Lagos State High Court had already granted the trustees an order barring him from acting as senior pastor while their own suit is heard.

What happens next

The court is expected to fix a date to hear the case. The trustees had not responded publicly to the new suit in the reports.
