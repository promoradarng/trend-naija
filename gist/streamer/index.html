GehGeh withdraws support for Delta Governor Oborevwori, cites mother's death

23 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Social commentator GehGeh has reportedly withdrawn his endorsement of Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori. He blamed poor healthcare in his community for his mother's death and said Peter Obi remains his presidential choice.

Read more

Social commentator and content creator GehGeh has reportedly withdrawn his earlier support for Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori's re-election bid.

His reasons

GehGeh said he lost his mother on 8 December 2024, and blamed the lack of adequate healthcare facilities in his community and neighbouring areas as a major cause of her death. He said he was ashamed to have previously endorsed the governor and asked his followers to forgive him.

He also questioned what the state government was doing to tackle youth unemployment.

His presidential choice

GehGeh added that Peter Obi of the NDC remains his preferred presidential candidate for 2027.

The other side

No response from the Delta State government or the governor's campaign was included in the reports.

APC says letter asking INEC to guarantee Tinubu's 2027 win is fake

23 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The APC has dismissed a letter circulating online that claimed its national chairman asked INEC to ensure President Tinubu is declared winner in 2027. The party called it fake and malicious.

Read more

The All Progressives Congress (APC) has rejected a letter circulating on social media that claimed to be from its National Chairman, Professor Nentawe Yilwatda, asking the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) to guarantee President Bola Tinubu's victory in the 2027 election.

What the letter claimed

The document, dated 22 November 2026 and addressed to INEC Chairman Professor Joash Amupitan, purported to ask the commission to use its administrative and operational machinery to make sure Tinubu was declared winner of the next presidential election.

The party's response

In a statement on Monday, the chairman's special adviser on media described the letter as fake and malicious, and a calculated attempt to damage the reputation of the party's leadership and weaken trust in democratic institutions. The party said the letter did not come from Yilwatda or his office, and pointed to features missing from the document that would appear on genuine correspondence.

Background

The row comes as campaigns for the January 2027 elections intensify.

Tinubu asks Sunday Igboho to deploy his security network in the South-West

23 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu reportedly asked activist Sunday Igboho to use his Iru Ekun Security Network to help protect lives and property in the South-West. The two met briefly in the president's car after the Ibadan rally.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has reportedly asked Yoruba nation activist Sunday Adeyemo, popularly known as Sunday Igboho, to deploy his Iru Ekun Security Network to help protect lives and property across South-West Nigeria.

The meeting

According to a statement released on Monday, 23 November 2026, by Igboho's media office in Ibadan, the two met briefly inside the president's official car after the APC presidential campaign rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, on Saturday.

What was discussed

The meeting is said to have focused on the work of Iru Ekun, a community security outfit founded by Igboho, and how its members could help reduce insecurity in rural and urban areas of the region.

Background

Igboho is a well-known campaigner for Yoruba self-determination. The meeting took place on the day of Tinubu's campaign rally in Ibadan, where the president also met traditional rulers.

Fani-Kayode denies being recalled as Nigeria's envoy to South Africa

23 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, has rejected social media claims that he was recalled or reprimanded. He said he was in Abuja for routine consultations.

Read more

Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, has dismissed reports on social media that the federal government had recalled or reprimanded him, describing them as completely unfounded.

What he said

Fani-Kayode made the clarification during a visit to Abuja, where he said he had been meeting officials at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs as part of routine diplomatic consultations. He said the talks focused on recent developments in South Africa and the direction of Nigeria's foreign policy on the continent.

He insisted that his presence in the capital had nothing to do with any disciplinary action.

Background

Fani-Kayode, a former aviation minister, is a vocal supporter of President Bola Tinubu.
