VeryDarkMan puts his Ratel Power Station to the test, shows off battery power as Blord rivalry continues

29 Oct 2026, 13:55 GMT

Popular Nigerian social media activist and businessman Martins Vincent Otse, better known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has once again got Nigerians talking after demonstrating the capabilities of his newly imported Ratel Power Station.

VeryDarkMan, who recently received a 40-foot container loaded with power stations and other gadgets from China, has begun testing the products to show potential customers what they can do.

One particular demonstration has caught the attention of many people.

In the test, the smallest model, rated at 1,200 watts, powered a television, a standing fan and a refrigerator for 5 hours 17 minutes. The TV drew 74W, the fan 50W and the fridge 150W, a total of 274W when all three were running.

And remember, this is just the smallest model in the range!

For a country like Nigeria, where unreliable electricity continues to affect homes, students, artisans and small businesses, a portable power station with such capabilities could be a useful alternative during power outages.

VDM has previously explained that his target customers include students, barbers, makeup artists and other Nigerians whose daily activities depend on electricity.

However, the demonstration has also added another chapter to the ongoing social media rivalry between VeryDarkMan and businessman Blord.

While Blord has continued promoting his own gadgets through videos and online publicity, supporters of VeryDarkMan are challenging him to demonstrate the actual performance of his products in a similar way.

And this is where the argument becomes interesting. Making promotional videos is one thing, but showing customers what a product can actually do is another.

VDM appears determined to let the performance of his products speak for itself, and his supporters are already celebrating his approach.

The box shows a battery capacity of 932Wh. A runtime of 5 hours 17 minutes fits with that, because a fridge's compressor switches on and off rather than running all the time, so the average load stays below the full 274W. Actual runtime will still vary with the appliances used and how they are used.

Nevertheless, the excitement surrounding the Ratel Power Station shows how much attention VeryDarkMan's latest business venture is attracting.

From activism and philanthropy to importing products from China, VDM is gradually expanding into commercial ventures. And as his supporters would say, VeryDarkMan is whoever he says he is!

Now, the big question is this: between VeryDarkMan's Ratel Power Station and Blord's gadgets, who is actually giving Nigerians better value for their money?

Let the products do the talking!

His reasons

GehGeh said he lost his mother on 8 December 2024, and blamed the lack of adequate healthcare facilities in his community and neighbouring areas as a major cause of her death. He said he was ashamed to have previously endorsed the governor and asked his followers to forgive him.

Abure-led Labour Party faction endorses Tinubu for 2027

23 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Labour Party faction led by Julius Abure has reportedly endorsed President Tinubu for re-election. Abure said the faction would mobilise the votes the party won in 2023 behind the president.

Read more

A faction of the Labour Party led by Julius Abure has reportedly endorsed President Bola Tinubu for re-election in the 2027 general election.

The endorsement

Abure announced the decision on Monday, 23 November 2026, after a meeting of the faction's National Executive Council in Abuja. The faction is said to have described Tinubu as the most suitable candidate to lead Nigeria beyond 2027, pointing to his political experience and his time as governor of Lagos State.

Abure also reportedly pledged to mobilise the six million votes the Labour Party claims it won in the 2023 presidential election in support of the president.

Background

Earlier this month, Abure said he would not support Peter Obi, the party's 2023 presidential candidate, who is now running on the NDC platform. He had said he was open to working with either Tinubu or Atiku Abubakar, depending on how the party's leadership dispute was resolved. The Labour Party has been split by a long-running leadership tussle.

How people reacted

The endorsement drew criticism and sarcastic comments on social media, with many users questioning the faction's motives.

Infantino says Nigeria is not ready to host a World Cup yet

23 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has reportedly said Nigeria cannot yet host the World Cup because of gaps in stadiums, airports and transport. He added that the country has the building blocks to get there.

Read more

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has reportedly said Nigeria is not currently ready to host the FIFA World Cup.

What he said

Speaking during his visit to Nigeria, Infantino was asked whether the country could stage a tournament of that size. He is said to have pointed to serious shortfalls in stadiums, airports and transport networks as the main obstacles, and explained the kind of infrastructure any host nation must have in place.

Hope for the future

However, he reportedly did not rule out Nigeria's long-term chances, saying the country has the building blocks needed to eventually meet the standard. He also described the national training facilities he visited in Abuja and Warri as impressive.

Background

South Africa remains the only sub-Saharan African country to have hosted the World Cup, in 2010, while Morocco will co-host the 2030 edition with Spain and Portugal.

How fans reacted

His comments drew mixed reactions from Nigerian football fans online.

Former world heavyweight boxing champion Tyson Fury has reportedly revealed that he was diagnosed with schizophrenia and bipolar disorder after a severe mental health crisis.

The documentary

The 38-year-old British boxer opens up about his struggles in an upcoming Netflix documentary titled Fury, which looks at his life, career and personal battles. It features interviews with Fury, his wife Paris and his father John.

Fury and his wife recalled how, after he was hospitalised with a suspected heart attack in June 2016, he began hearing threatening voices. Background
