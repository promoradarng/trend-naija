VeryDarkMan puts his Ratel Power Station to the test, shows off battery power as Blord rivalry continues

29 Oct 2026, 13:55 GMT

Popular Nigerian social media activist and businessman Martins Vincent Otse, better known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has once again got Nigerians talking after demonstrating the capabilities of his newly imported Ratel Power Station.

VeryDarkMan, who recently received a 40-foot container loaded with power stations and other gadgets from China, has begun testing the products to show potential customers what they can do.

One particular demonstration has caught the attention of many people.

In the test, the smallest model, rated at 1,200 watts, powered a television, a standing fan and a refrigerator for 5 hours 17 minutes. The TV drew 74W, the fan 50W and the fridge 150W, a total of 274W when all three were running.

And remember, this is just the smallest model in the range!

For a country like Nigeria, where unreliable electricity continues to affect homes, students, artisans and small businesses, a portable power station with such capabilities could be a useful alternative during power outages.

VDM has previously explained that his target customers include students, barbers, makeup artists and other Nigerians whose daily activities depend on electricity.

However, the demonstration has also added another chapter to the ongoing social media rivalry between VeryDarkMan and businessman Blord.

While Blord has continued promoting his own gadgets through videos and online publicity, supporters of VeryDarkMan are challenging him to demonstrate the actual performance of his products in a similar way.

And this is where the argument becomes interesting. Making promotional videos is one thing, but showing customers what a product can actually do is another.

VDM appears determined to let the performance of his products speak for itself, and his supporters are already celebrating his approach.

The box shows a battery capacity of 932Wh. A runtime of 5 hours 17 minutes fits with that, because a fridge's compressor switches on and off rather than running all the time, so the average load stays below the full 274W. Actual runtime will still vary with the appliances used and how they are used.

Nevertheless, the excitement surrounding the Ratel Power Station shows how much attention VeryDarkMan's latest business venture is attracting.

From activism and philanthropy to importing products from China, VDM is gradually expanding into commercial ventures. And as his supporters would say, VeryDarkMan is whoever he says he is!

Now, the big question is this: between VeryDarkMan's Ratel Power Station and Blord's gadgets, who is actually giving Nigerians better value for their money?

Let the products do the talking!

VeryDarkMan warns Tinubu over deadly attack on NDC campaign in Delta

24 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has issued a strong warning to President Tinubu after gunmen killed four people, including NDC youth leader Efemena Umukoro, at Omo-Agege's campaign event in Delta. He said political violence could spiral out of control.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has issued a fierce public warning to President Bola Tinubu following the deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North, Delta State.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram, VDM told the president that he would burn Nigeria down, arguing that targeting opposition figures with gunfire is a dangerous political strategy that could spiral far beyond anyone's control. He warned that even people close to the president would not be safe if the cycle of violence continued, and drew attention to the fact that the attack happened in Delta State.

The attack

Gunmen opened fire on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege's campaign team on Monday, 23 November 2026, while he was addressing supporters. Four people were killed, according to his campaign, including Efemena Umukoro, an Urhobo youth leader popularly known as General Akima. Omo-Agege was unhurt.

Other reactions

Other social media commentators also condemned the killings, and Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori called for everyone involved to be arrested regardless of their party. The Inspector-General of Police has ordered an investigation.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to VDM's remarks was included in the reports.

Nollywood legend Pete Edochie dies at 79

25 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Veteran actor Pete Edochie, famous for playing Okonkwo in Things Fall Apart, has died at the age of 79. His family says he died in Enugu early on Wednesday after a long battle with prostate cancer.

Read more

Veteran Nollywood actor Pete Edochie has died at the age of 79, his family has announced.

The announcement

In a statement shared on Instagram by his son, Leo Pete Edochie, on behalf of the family, the family said the actor died at 12:30am on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, in Enugu, after a prolonged battle with prostate cancer. The statement, titled 'An Iroko Has Fallen', described him as a beloved husband, father, brother and kinsman.

He is survived by his wife, Josephine Edochie, his children, including actors Yul and Linc Edochie, his siblings and other relatives. The family said funeral arrangements would be announced in due course.

His life and career

Pete Edochie hailed from Akamnato, Ifite Nteje, in Anambra State. He began his career in broadcasting in 1967 before becoming one of Nigeria's best-known actors. His major breakthrough came in 1987, when he played Okonkwo in the Nigerian Television Authority's adaptation of Chinua Achebe's Things Fall Apart, a role that brought him recognition far beyond Nigeria.

Known for his commanding voice and powerful screen presence, he became famous for portraying traditional patriarchs and kings in countless Nollywood films. He was honoured with the national award of Member of the Order of the Niger (MON).

His final weeks

In October, during a visit to Ghana, he spoke openly about his declining health, saying walking had become difficult, and prayed that he would not become a burden to those around him. Just this week, actress Toyin Abraham and her crew knelt to welcome him on her film set, and he had spoken publicly about his son Yul days earlier.

Tributes

News of his death has drawn an outpouring of grief from fans, many of whom described it as the end of an era for Nigerian film.

Judy Austin tells court she is not a 'husband snatcher'

Actress Judy Austin has denied being a husband snatcher while giving evidence in her defamation cases against Rita Edochie at an Abuja High Court. She declined to answer questions about her marriages.

Read more

Nollywood actress Judy Austin has rejected the 'husband snatcher' label that has followed her relationship with actor Yul Edochie, while giving evidence at an Abuja High Court.

In court

Austin appeared on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, in proceedings in her defamation cases against veteran actress Rita Edochie, Yul's aunt. Under cross-examination by Rita's lawyer, she denied snatching Yul from anyone and said she did not belong to any group of husband snatchers.

She refused to answer questions about her former marriage to Emmanuel Obasi or her current marriage to Yul, insisting that her marital affairs are private.

Background

Austin is suing Rita Edochie over comments she says were defamatory. Yul Edochie, who took Austin as his second wife, accompanied her to court. A video also showed Rita Edochie confronting people she believed were filming her at the court premises.

Sad timing

The court appearance came a day before the family of Yul's father, Nollywood legend Pete Edochie, announced his death at the age of 79.

Obi defends Kwankwasiyya supporters who carry sticks to rallies

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has reportedly defended Kwankwasiyya members who bring sticks to political events, saying the items have never been used to hurt anyone. He spoke in Jalingo, Taraba State.

Peter Obi, the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) presidential candidate, has reportedly defended members of the Kwankwasiyya movement who carry sticks to political gatherings.

What he said

Speaking in a television interview in Jalingo, Taraba State, on Tuesday evening, 24 November 2026, after a town hall meeting, Obi is said to have explained that the movement, led by his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso, has a long tradition of supporters attending political events with sticks. He said there was no record of the sticks being used to harm anyone.

He also said that calls against electoral malpractice were being misread as opposition to campaigning.

Background

Political violence has become a major concern ahead of the January 2027 elections, following Monday's deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Delta State. At the Taraba town hall, Obi led a minute of silence for the party members killed.
