VeryDarkMan puts his Ratel Power Station to the test, shows off battery power as Blord rivalry continues

29 Oct 2026, 13:55 GMT

Popular Nigerian social media activist and businessman Martins Vincent Otse, better known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has once again got Nigerians talking after demonstrating the capabilities of his newly imported Ratel Power Station.

VeryDarkMan, who recently received a 40-foot container loaded with power stations and other gadgets from China, has begun testing the products to show potential customers what they can do.

One particular demonstration has caught the attention of many people.

In the test, the smallest model, rated at 1,200 watts, powered a television, a standing fan and a refrigerator for 5 hours 17 minutes. The TV drew 74W, the fan 50W and the fridge 150W, a total of 274W when all three were running.

And remember, this is just the smallest model in the range!

For a country like Nigeria, where unreliable electricity continues to affect homes, students, artisans and small businesses, a portable power station with such capabilities could be a useful alternative during power outages.

VDM has previously explained that his target customers include students, barbers, makeup artists and other Nigerians whose daily activities depend on electricity.

However, the demonstration has also added another chapter to the ongoing social media rivalry between VeryDarkMan and businessman Blord.

While Blord has continued promoting his own gadgets through videos and online publicity, supporters of VeryDarkMan are challenging him to demonstrate the actual performance of his products in a similar way.

And this is where the argument becomes interesting. Making promotional videos is one thing, but showing customers what a product can actually do is another.

VDM appears determined to let the performance of his products speak for itself, and his supporters are already celebrating his approach.

The box shows a battery capacity of 932Wh. A runtime of 5 hours 17 minutes fits with that, because a fridge's compressor switches on and off rather than running all the time, so the average load stays below the full 274W. Actual runtime will still vary with the appliances used and how they are used.

Nevertheless, the excitement surrounding the Ratel Power Station shows how much attention VeryDarkMan's latest business venture is attracting.

From activism and philanthropy to importing products from China, VDM is gradually expanding into commercial ventures. And as his supporters would say, VeryDarkMan is whoever he says he is!

Now, the big question is this: between VeryDarkMan's Ratel Power Station and Blord's gadgets, who is actually giving Nigerians better value for their money?

Let the products do the talking!

VeryDarkMan, Opeyemi Famakin and Peller defend Enioluwa after youth comments backlash

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have spoken in defence of influencer Enioluwa, who apologised after his comments about young Nigerians drew criticism.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

His mother speaks

Enioluwa's mother, Bola Adeoluwa, a university lecturer, has also addressed the backlash after some students on TikTok turned their criticism on her, alleging she was a harsh grader. She responded publicly to the claims and the criticism of her son.

Tinubu endorses APC governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos at Ibadan rally, promises better power

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

At an APC rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, President Tinubu raised the hands of the party's governorship candidates for Oyo, Ogun and Lagos. He also guaranteed improved electricity and promised a factory in Ibadan.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos States at a campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The endorsements

Telling the crowd he would show them who to vote for as governor, Tinubu raised the hands of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC candidate in Oyo; Senator Solomon Adeola, known as Yayi, the party's candidate in Ogun; and Lagos Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, the APC flagbearer in Lagos. He also raised the hand of Remi Oseni, the party's senatorial candidate for Oyo South.

Promise on power

During the visit, the President also assured Nigerians of better electricity supply, saying the Minister of Power and his team were working hard. He added that the government would build a factory in Ibadan.

Context

The rally is part of the APC's campaign ahead of the 16 January 2027 elections.

The other side

Reactions from the opposition parties to the rally were not included in the reports.

Jimmy Odukoya sues sister and Fountain of Life trustees over his removal

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court in Lagos against his sister, Tolu Odukoya, and members of the Fountain of Life Church board over his removal as senior pastor.

Read more

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has gone to court over his removal as senior pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, filing a suit against his sister and several members of the church's board of trustees.

The suit

The suit was filed at the Federal High Court in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. Eight people are named as respondents, including his sister Tolu Odukoya, interim leader Pastor Rotimi Okpaise and other trustees.

The filing marks a significant escalation in the leadership dispute at the Ilupeju church founded by his late father, Taiwo Odukoya.

Background

Earlier this week, the trustees announced that Jimmy had been removed because his appointment did not reflect his father's succession wishes, and released a 2021 video in which the late founder reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his successor. Jimmy has insisted his removal is invalid. A Lagos State High Court had already granted the trustees an order barring him from acting as senior pastor while their own suit is heard.

What happens next

The court is expected to fix a date to hear the case. The trustees had not responded publicly to the new suit in the reports.
