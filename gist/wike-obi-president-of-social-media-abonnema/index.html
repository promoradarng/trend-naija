Wike mocks Peter Obi: 'Vote for him as president of social media, not Nigeria'

6 Nov 2026, 09:45 GMT

FCT Minister Nyesom Wike has taken another swipe at Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

What Wike said

Speaking at a town hall meeting in Abonnema, Akuku-Toru Local Government Area of Rivers State, on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Wike reportedly said that people who wanted a president of social media should vote for Obi, but that he should not be voted for as president of Nigeria. He also mocked Obi's well-known habit of carrying his own bag, saying it did not show that he had the capacity to rule the country.

Background

Wike, a former Rivers governor, leads the Rainbow Coalition, a group of PDP and other politicians backing President Bola Tinubu's re-election in the presidential election scheduled for 16 January 2027. He has repeatedly criticised Obi in recent weeks. Obi, a former Anambra governor, has a large following among young voters, especially online, and is running with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. Wike made the comments during a busy week in which he also spoke about Governor Siminalayi Fubara's political future and denied any role in the court ruling on the Imo West Senate ticket.

How people reacted

Obi's supporters responded angrily online, with many saying Wike could not campaign without mentioning Obi and describing the former governor's simple lifestyle as a strength. Supporters of the minister said Obi's online popularity had not translated into votes in Rivers State in the past. Obi's campaign had not publicly responded to the latest remarks at the time of writing.

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari, widow of the late President Muhammadu Buhari, wearing clothing printed with President Bola Tinubu's campaign logo has stirred political debate ahead of the 2027 elections.

The video

The clip was posted on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by former presidential aide Bashir Ahmad, with a caption describing it as a message from Daura, Buhari's hometown.

Why it matters

The video came days after Solomon Dalung, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), claimed in a television interview that Aisha Buhari had visited ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar and told him that the late President's large voter base would be backing him in 2027.

Many social media users took the new video as a response to that claim, suggesting the former First Lady is supporting Tinubu.

No statement

Aisha Buhari herself had not made a public statement on her political preference at the time of the reports.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. The campaign

Moniepoint opens North Central regional headquarters in Abuja

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened a regional headquarters in Abuja where customers can get in-person help, in line with CBN rules requiring physical locations for national microfinance banks.

Read more

Digital lender Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened its North Central regional headquarters in Abuja, giving customers a place to get face-to-face support.

Why now

The move is in line with a Central Bank of Nigeria requirement for nationally licensed microfinance banks to keep physical locations alongside their digital services.

Wider presence

Moniepoint already runs offices and service centres in several states, including Borno, Bauchi, Kaduna, Kano and Sokoto, and its point-of-sale network serves businesses in all 774 local government areas.
