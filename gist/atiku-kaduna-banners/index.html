The Federal Government is reportedly considering legislation that would protect key economic reforms introduced under President Bola Ahmed Tinubu from being easily reversed by future administrations.

The proposed law could cover major economic policies, including taxation, tariffs and subsidy reforms, with the aim of providing long-term policy stability regardless of who occupies Aso Rock.

If introduced and passed by the National Assembly, such legislation could make it more difficult for future governments to change these policies without going through the necessary legislative process.

However, one important question remains: should one administration be allowed to make economic policies that future elected governments would find difficult to reverse, especially when those policies directly affect the cost of living?

A future National Assembly would ordinarily retain the power to amend or repeal such laws.

What do you think? Is this about protecting Nigeria's economic future, or limiting the choices of future presidents and the Nigerian people?

Drama as crowd allegedly chants "Tinubu must go" during President's speech, sparking reactions

27 Oct 2026, 10:15 GMT

Footage circulating online reportedly shows voices in a crowd chanting "Tinubu must go!" while President Bola Ahmed Tinubu was giving a speech about a brighter future for Nigeria. Social media users have pointed to the reactions of Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu and other dignitaries on stage. Where and when the clip was recorded has not been independently verified.

Read more

A dramatic moment reportedly unfolded during a public gathering as President Bola Ahmed Tinubu was delivering a speech about Nigeria's future, with voices in the background allegedly chanting, "Tinubu must go!"

The President was addressing the audience with a message of hope and optimism, assuring Nigerians that a brighter future lies ahead. However, what was supposed to be an encouraging moment appeared to take an unexpected turn when some people in the crowd reportedly began shouting slogans calling for his departure from office.

In footage circulating online, the chants could reportedly be heard while Tinubu continued speaking.

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu and other dignitaries present were also seen reacting to the situation. Some social media commentators interpreted their expressions and body language as signs of discomfort or concern.

Where and when the footage was recorded, and whether the audio has been edited, have not been independently verified.
