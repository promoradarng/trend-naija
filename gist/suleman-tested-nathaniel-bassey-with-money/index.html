Apostle Suleman says he tested Nathaniel Bassey with money, and was impressed

6 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Apostle Johnson Suleman, founder of Omega Fire Ministries, has shared a story about gospel singer Nathaniel Bassey that has drawn wide attention.

What he said

Speaking during a Sunday service, in comments shared this week, Suleman reportedly said he first met Bassey on a plane ahead of his church's convention. According to him, Bassey said he wanted to attend the convention not as a paid performer but to support his spiritual father, whom Suleman named as Pastor Deboye. Curious about his reasons, Suleman said he deliberately offered the singer money to see how he would respond, as a way of studying his character and values.

The response

Suleman said Bassey's response left him with a powerful lesson about mentorship and respect for spiritual leaders, though reports did not detail exactly what the singer said.

Background

Nathaniel Bassey is one of Africa's most popular gospel ministers and recently became the first African gospel artist to reach 1 billion views on YouTube. He is known for his worship sessions and the annual Hallelujah Challenge. Suleman is a prominent and sometimes controversial Pentecostal preacher based in Auchi, Edo State.

How people reacted

Many Christians praised Bassey's humility, while others debated whether it was right to test someone with money.

  • Suleman says he offered Bassey money to test him.
  • Bassey wanted to attend the convention to support his spiritual father.
  • Suleman says the response taught him a lesson.

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has introduced his wife to the public for the first time, sharing loved-up photos and videos of the couple on Instagram.

How they met

In the post, the actor said they met almost five years ago in an airport lounge before a flight to Houston. He recalled asking if the seat next to her was free, and said she firmly told him it was taken, but he sat down anyway. They ignored each other for an hour, then burst out laughing when they realised they were on the same flight.

His tribute

Jim Iyke described their relationship as something sacred rather than hidden, and said they have built a family together and travelled to eight countries. He called her his best friend, his prayer warrior and his wife, and said they have never stopped laughing together.

Femi Branch opens up on two years out of work after on-set accident

Nollywood actor Femi Branch has opened up about a difficult period in his life after a serious accident on a film set left him unable to work for two years.

The accident

In an interview that surfaced online on Friday, 20 November 2026, Branch said he was injured while shooting a stunt in Ikorodu, Lagos. He said he had to jump from a storey building while dressed as a security guard, and the impact when he landed was so severe it sounded like an explosion.

Hard times

He said the injury kept him out of work for two years, leaving him isolated and in financial difficulty. He revealed that he had to sell his mother's car during that period, and said some colleagues deserted him while he was recovering.
