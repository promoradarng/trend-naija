Don Jazzy says he blew his first big music money on 50 bottles of champagne

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

Music producer and Mavin Records founder Michael Collins Ajereh, better known as Don Jazzy, has admitted that he wasted his first major earnings from music, and says the lesson changed his life.

What he said

Speaking on an episode of the 'On The Grind' podcast, in comments that spread on Monday, 2 November 2026, Don Jazzy said that when he got his first big money from music, he bought 50 bottles of champagne and went clubbing. He said he quickly pulled himself together and began investing in signing new artistes, adding that, as a result, he no longer has to work.

His advice

The producer urged young people to put the money they earn in their youth into profitable businesses rather than spending it on luxuries. He said they did not necessarily need to start a record label, but should find something lucrative to invest in.

Background

Don Jazzy co-founded Mo' Hits Records with D'banj in 2004 and produced many of the label's biggest hits. After the label split in 2012, he set up Mavin Records, which has gone on to sign and develop stars such as Tiwa Savage, Rema, Ayra Starr, Johnny Drille and Crayon. The label attracted a major investment from Universal Music Group in 2024. He is also known for his generosity online and recently announced a technology scholarship for young Nigerians.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised his honesty and said the advice was useful for young people who start earning early. Others joked that 50 bottles of champagne would be a dream night out, while some pointed out that his success depended on spotting talent, which not every investor can do.

  • He urged young people to invest early.

Paul Okoye celebrates birthday without twin Peter for the first time

P-Square's Paul Okoye marked his birthday on 18 November alone for the first time, after his twin brother Peter adopted 30 November as his new official birthday. Fans reacted with sadness over the split.

Paul Okoye, the singer known as Rudeboy, celebrated his birthday on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, without his twin brother Peter for the first time.

The celebration

The P-Square star shared a video of himself sitting alone at a dining table filled with food, with a caption expressing gratitude and pride.

Why they are apart

The solo celebration follows Peter Okoye's announcement in April 2026 that he would no longer share their birth date. Peter, who performs as Mr P, said on X that 30 November would now be his official birthday, describing it as a personal decision and thanking fans for understanding.

How fans reacted

The birthday post drew many reactions, with numerous fans expressing sadness that the twins, who rose to fame together as P-Square, now mark their birthdays separately.

JMartins' appeal

Singer JMartins, a longtime friend of the twins, used the occasion to wish both brothers a happy birthday and urged them to let go of pride. He said life is short and asked them to reconcile, even if they never reunite as P-Square, so that their children do not grow up as enemies.

Kano court jails TikToker five months and orders 10 strokes for insulting Hisbah commander

19 Nov 2026, 06:41 GMT

A Shariah court in Kano has reportedly sentenced a 19-year-old TikTok user to five months in prison and 10 strokes of the cane after she pleaded guilty to insulting the Hisbah Board and its commander. Two other defendants pleaded not guilty.

Read more

A Shariah court in Kano State has reportedly sentenced a 19-year-old TikTok user, Zainab Abubakar, to five months in prison and 10 strokes of the cane for insulting the state Hisbah Board and its commander, Malam Aminu Ibrahim Daurawa.

The judgment

The court, sitting at the Hisbah Board headquarters in Sharada, delivered the judgment on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, after she pleaded guilty to the charge under Section 312 of the Penal Code. She was sentenced without the option of a fine.

The other defendants

She was arraigned alongside two young men who were also arrested by the Hisbah over allegations of using TikTok to insult the agency and its commander. Both pleaded not guilty to charges of conspiracy and insulting the board. The court remanded them in custody for two weeks while it considers their bail application and directed the prosecution to prepare its witnesses.

Background

The Kano Hisbah is the state's religious police, which enforces Islamic moral codes. Earlier this week, TikTokers were reported to have been arrested over videos said to have insulted its commander.

FG, states and councils share ₦2.44 trillion October revenue

19 Nov 2026, 06:41 GMT

The Federation Account Allocation Committee has shared ₦2.441 trillion from October 2026 revenue among the federal, state and local governments. Both oil-related revenue and VAT dipped slightly from September.

Read more

The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) has shared ₦2.441 trillion from October 2026 revenue among the federal government, states and local government councils.

The breakdown

According to a statement on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, from the Office of the Accountant-General of the Federation, the pool was made up of ₦1.688 trillion in statutory revenue and ₦752.488 billion from Value Added Tax (VAT).

The federal government received ₦856.065 billion, the states ₦809.909 billion and local government councils ₦568.701 billion. A further ₦206.384 billion made up the rest of the pool.

Revenue dips

Total gross revenue for October was ₦3.811 trillion. Gross statutory revenue fell by about ₦97 billion to ₦2.999 trillion, compared with September, while gross VAT revenue dipped slightly to ₦812.446 billion. Around ₦128.8 billion was deducted as cost of collection and ₦1.241 trillion for transfers, refunds and savings.
