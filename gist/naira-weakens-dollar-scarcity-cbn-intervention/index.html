Naira weakens in October as dollar demand outstrips CBN's $1.98bn intervention

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Nigeria's currency has come under fresh pressure as businesses scramble for dollars ahead of the festive season, according to market data reported on Tuesday, 3 November 2026.

The numbers

The naira closed October at ₦1,346.91 per dollar on the official market, compared with ₦1,329.50 at the end of September, a fall of about 1.31 per cent. It was the first monthly decline after three consecutive months of gains. The Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) reportedly injected about $1.98 billion into the market during the month, but demand was fully met in only two of its five interventions.

Why it is happening

Importers, manufacturers and traders typically need more foreign currency towards the end of the year to bring in goods for Christmas and New Year sales. Analysts say this seasonal demand, combined with limited dollar inflows, is putting pressure on the naira.

What it means

A weaker naira raises the cost of imported goods and raw materials, which can push up prices for consumers. The CBN says stronger dollar inflows from oil exports, foreign investment and remittances are key to keeping the currency stable.

Background

The naira has been more stable in recent months after sharp falls in 2023 and 2024 following the unification of exchange rates. Foreign reserves have risen to their highest level in years, and inflation has fallen. The government points to these gains as evidence that its reforms are working, while critics say ordinary Nigerians are still struggling with high prices.

  • The naira fell about 1.31% in October.
  • The CBN injected nearly $2 billion.
  • Festive-season demand is adding pressure.

Regulator says cooking gas prices will fall further as supply rises

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The petroleum regulator NMDPRA has reportedly said higher local production and better distribution will bring down cooking gas prices. It says prices have already dropped by ₦300–₦400 per kilogram.

Read more

The federal government has reportedly outlined fresh steps to bring down the price of cooking gas, as many households struggle with high energy costs.

What the regulator said

The chief executive of the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority (NMDPRA), Rabiu Umar, said increased domestic production, better distribution and the removal of logistics bottlenecks would help moderate the price of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG). He spoke during a visit to the Nigeria LNG plant and its Train 7 project site in Bonny on Saturday.

Umar is said to have explained that the government is prioritising gas availability and energy security, in line with plans to increase the use of gas within the country.

Prices already falling

According to the regulator, cooking gas prices have already fallen by between ₦300 and ₦400 per kilogram thanks to improved supply and regulatory action. He blamed earlier price increases on supply problems.

Petrol prices fall by up to ₦85 a litre as Dangote suspends diesel price cut

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Petrol pump prices have reportedly fallen by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre in a week as refiners and importers compete. Dangote Refinery has, however, withdrawn a planned ₦150 cut in diesel prices pending a review.

Read more

Motorists are seeing some relief at filling stations, with petrol prices reportedly falling by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre over the past seven days.

Petrol prices

The reductions follow fresh price cuts by Dangote Refinery, importers and major marketers, amid growing competition between locally refined and imported petrol. Dangote has cut its petrol price several times this month, most recently to ₦1,070 per litre on 19 November, its lowest quoted rate since July.

Diesel U-turn

However, Dangote Refinery has withdrawn an earlier announcement that it would reduce the gantry price of diesel from ₦1,720 to ₦1,570 per litre. In a new message to customers, the refinery said the proposed ₦150 cut, which was meant to take effect on Saturday, 21 November, was still under internal review.

Marketers and businesses that rely on diesel are now waiting for clarity on the new rate.

Depot prices

Depot prices of petrol and diesel fell between 16 and 20 November in Lagos, Warri, Port Harcourt and Calabar. Lagos saw the biggest cuts, with some depots reducing petrol by as much as ₦120 per litre.

Troops foil suspected kidnap attempt at Kaduna school

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Soldiers reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at a college in Zangon Kataf, Kaduna State. A headcount showed no student was missing.

Read more

Troops have reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at Tafawa Balewa Memorial Commercial College in Samaru, Zangon Kataf Local Government Area of Kaduna State.

What happened

The incident happened at about 11:56pm on 18 November 2026, after reports of suspicious movements by unidentified people around the school. Soldiers responded quickly and moved to the school, forcing the suspects to abandon their plan and flee.

All students safe

The troops searched the school and surrounding areas but did not make contact with the fleeing suspects. A headcount carried out with the school authorities showed that no student or other occupant was missing.

Security stepped up

The troops have continued patrols in the area to prevent further attacks.

Moniepoint opens North Central regional headquarters in Abuja

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened a regional headquarters in Abuja where customers can get in-person help, in line with CBN rules requiring physical locations for national microfinance banks.

Read more

Digital lender Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened its North Central regional headquarters in Abuja, giving customers a place to get face-to-face support.

Why now

The move is in line with a Central Bank of Nigeria requirement for nationally licensed microfinance banks to keep physical locations alongside their digital services.

Wider presence

Moniepoint already runs offices and service centres in several states, including Borno, Bauchi, Kaduna, Kano and Sokoto, and its point-of-sale network serves businesses in all 774 local government areas.
