JIBWIS and Christian group call for DSS probe of video allegedly threatening worshippers in Enugu and Jos

26 Oct 2026, 10:42 GMT

Update: JIBWIS national chairman Sheikh Abdullahi Bala Lau has urged the DSS, the Nigeria Police Force and other security agencies to identify the individual in the video. The Northern Christian Association has also called on security agencies to establish whether the footage is authentic and identify those featured.

There are conflicting claims online about when the video was recorded, with some users saying it dates back to 2020 and others saying it was posted only days ago. Social media users have also named a man they claim is the speaker, and there are unconfirmed reports that he has been suspended by the movement. None of these claims has been independently verified, and no official has named anyone.

Of particular concern is the apparent presence of children and young people during the chant. Critics fear that exposing minors to messages that appear to encourage religious violence could contribute to radicalisation and deepen religious tensions.

A disturbing video reportedly filmed somewhere in Northern Nigeria has raised serious concerns over alleged threats of religious violence, and is now being reported by several Nigerian news outlets.

The footage shows young men in brown uniforms and blue berets marching and chanting in Hausa during what appears to be a drill. News reports have linked the uniforms to the first-aid volunteer wing of the Islamic movement JIBWIS, whose members help coordinate Friday Jumu'ah prayers.

According to a Hausa-to-English translation circulating with the video, the drill leader allegedly calls for AK-47 rifles so the group can travel to Enugu and attack Christians during Sunday worship. He reportedly refers to the worshippers as "infidels", imitates the sound of gunfire, and also mentions Jos as another target.

Lere Olayinka, media aide to the FCT Minister, has called on the DSS and the police to investigate the video.

However, the location, date, identity of the speaker, the group's actual affiliation and the accuracy of the translation have not been independently verified. No response from the group had been reported at the time of writing.

If the translation is accurate, this is an extremely serious matter. Nobody should be allowed to use religion as an excuse to threaten innocent people, whether they are Christians or Muslims.

If someone openly calls for weapons to attack people during worship, shouldn't that be enough to trigger an immediate security investigation? Nigeria has suffered enough religious violence, and credible threats against worshippers cannot be treated as ordinary speech.

Petrol prices fall by up to ₦85 a litre as Dangote suspends diesel price cut

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Petrol pump prices have reportedly fallen by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre in a week as refiners and importers compete. Dangote Refinery has, however, withdrawn a planned ₦150 cut in diesel prices pending a review.

Read more

Motorists are seeing some relief at filling stations, with petrol prices reportedly falling by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre over the past seven days.

Petrol prices

The reductions follow fresh price cuts by Dangote Refinery, importers and major marketers, amid growing competition between locally refined and imported petrol. Dangote has cut its petrol price several times this month, most recently to ₦1,105 per litre at its depot.

Diesel U-turn

However, Dangote Refinery has withdrawn an earlier announcement that it would reduce the gantry price of diesel from ₦1,720 to ₦1,570 per litre. In a new message to customers, the refinery said the proposed ₦150 cut, which was meant to take effect on Saturday, 21 November, was still under internal review.

Marketers and businesses that rely on diesel are now waiting for clarity on the new rate.

Troops foil suspected kidnap attempt at Kaduna school

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Soldiers reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at a college in Zangon Kataf, Kaduna State. A headcount showed no student was missing.

Read more

Troops have reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at Tafawa Balewa Memorial Commercial College in Samaru, Zangon Kataf Local Government Area of Kaduna State.

What happened

The incident happened at about 11:56pm on 18 November 2026, after reports of suspicious movements by unidentified people around the school. Soldiers responded quickly and moved to the school, forcing the suspects to abandon their plan and flee.

All students safe

The troops searched the school and surrounding areas but did not make contact with the fleeing suspects. A headcount carried out with the school authorities showed that no student or other occupant was missing.

Security stepped up

The troops have continued patrols in the area to prevent further attacks.

Moniepoint opens North Central regional headquarters in Abuja

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened a regional headquarters in Abuja where customers can get in-person help, in line with CBN rules requiring physical locations for national microfinance banks.

Read more

Digital lender Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened its North Central regional headquarters in Abuja, giving customers a place to get face-to-face support.

Why now

The move is in line with a Central Bank of Nigeria requirement for nationally licensed microfinance banks to keep physical locations alongside their digital services.

Wider presence

Moniepoint already runs offices and service centres in several states, including Borno, Bauchi, Kaduna, Kano and Sokoto, and its point-of-sale network serves businesses in all 774 local government areas.
