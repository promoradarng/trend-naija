Raye leads 2027 Grammy nominations with 11, Rosalía next with nine

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

British singer Raye leads the 2027 Grammy nominations with 11, including Album, Song and Record of the Year. Rosalía has nine, and the ceremony holds in Los Angeles on 7 February.

Read more

British singer-songwriter Raye has led the nominations for the 2027 Grammy Awards with 11 nods, while Spain's Rosalía is second with nine.

The leaders

Raye is nominated in three of the top categories: Album of the Year, Song of the Year and Record of the Year, for her album This Music May Contain Hope, which mixes pop and R&B with jazz. She also earned a Songwriter of the Year nod for her work with other artists. Rosalía's nominations follow a strong showing at last week's Latin Grammys with her album Lux.

Others on the list

Country singers Ella Langley, a first-time nominee, and Miranda Lambert have seven each, as do Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers. Leon Thomas, Bruno Mars and Olivia Dean have six each, while Taylor Swift has five. Rock band Geese also has five in its first appearance on the ballot. Women claimed many of the top spots this year, at a ceremony where they have historically been underrepresented.

The Nigerian angle

Nigerian stars are also in the mix, with Tems, Davido and Ayra Starr among those nominated, and Beyoncé reaching a record 100 career nominations.

When and where

The 69th Grammy Awards will be held in Los Angeles on 7 February 2027, with 100 competitive categories.

Beyoncé reportedly becomes first artist with 100 Grammy nominations

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Beyoncé's nomination for Best R&B Solo Performance at the 2027 Grammys reportedly takes her career total to 100, the most in Grammy history. Tems is among the artists nominated in the same category.

Read more

Beyoncé has reportedly become the first artist in history to receive 100 Grammy nominations, after picking up a new nod in the nominations for the 69th Annual Grammy Awards announced this week.

The nomination

The singer is nominated for Best R&B Solo Performance for Morning Dew (Donk), a song she released as a surprise on 4 July 2026. A remix featuring her husband, Jay-Z, followed a month later. She faces Kehlani, Victoria Monét, Jill Scott and Nigeria's Tems in the category.

A record career

Beyoncé was already the most-decorated artist in Grammy history, with 35 wins. Jay-Z is said to be close behind her on the all-time nominations list, now with 90.

The Nigerian angle

Tems' place in the same category means Nigerian fans will be watching this race closely. She is one of several Nigerian stars recognised in this year's nominations, alongside Davido and Ayra Starr, who are nominated for Best Global Music Album.

What happens next

Winners will be announced at the 2027 Grammy Awards ceremony early next year.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

More nominations

Tems also picked up nominations for Best African Music Performance for 'Big Daddy', and Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song for 'What You Need'. Ayra Starr is nominated for Best African Music Performance for 'Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)', featuring Angélique Kidjo.

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.

Fountain of Life Church removes Jimmy Odukoya as senior pastor

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The board of trustees of The Fountain of Life Church in Lagos has reportedly removed Jimmy Odukoya as senior pastor with immediate effect. It said his appointment did not reflect the succession wishes of his late father and founder, Taiwo Odukoya.

Read more

The Fountain of Life Church in Ilupeju, Lagos, has reportedly removed Jimmy Odukoya as its senior pastor, with the board of trustees saying his appointment conflicted with the succession plans left by the church's late founder, Taiwo Odukoya.

What the board said

In a statement on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the board is said to have explained that it reviewed the church's constitution alongside recordings of the founder's wishes made before his death in 2023. It concluded that Jimmy's tenure did not reflect those wishes and said he was no longer recognised as senior pastor, effective immediately.

The board reportedly stressed that the decision was not a personal judgement against him but a governance matter, describing it as necessary to uphold the church's constitution and preserve the succession arrangements set up by the founder. It said the decision was not taken lightly.

Background

Jimmy Odukoya, an actor and pastor, took over leadership of the church after his father's death. Earlier this month, on 1 November, he remarried and presented his new wife to the congregation, in a ceremony at which Pastor Tunde Bakare officiated the blessing.

How people reacted

The news quickly became a talking point online, with many people revisiting a cryptic post he shared days before the announcement, and a video of the late founder that is said to show the role he intended for his son.

What happens next

There was no public response from Jimmy Odukoya in the reports, and the board did not say who would lead the church.
