Blord spotted in Kwankwaso-linked 'Nigeria Must Be OK' cap in Kano

10 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Businessman and social media personality Linus Williams, popularly known as Blord, has drawn attention after being seen in a cap linked to Senator Rabiu Kwankwaso.

What happened

During a visit to Kano State, reported on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Blord was seen wearing a cap with the words 'Nigeria Must Be OK', a slogan associated with Kwankwaso, the NDC's vice-presidential candidate on Peter Obi's ticket. Many social media users took it as a possible sign of support for the Obi–Kwankwaso campaign.

'Nigeria Must Be OK' caption

Blord later shared a photo of himself in the cap with a caption praising Kano and ending with the phrase 'Nigeria Must Be OK', associated with the Obi–Kwankwaso campaign, further fuelling speculation that he has shifted his support from Sowore, who is running for president on a different platform.

Background

The appearance drew attention because Blord had earlier declared support for activist Omoyele Sowore after Sowore was released from Kuje Prison in April 2026. At the time, Blord praised Sowore for intervening in his own legal case, described himself as a member of the Revolution Now movement and was presented with a branded beret. Blord has not publicly announced any change in his political allegiance.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says he nearly died

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Social media critic VeryDarkMan says he has been admitted to hospital in China after being diagnosed with what he called life-threatening malaria. He said he nearly lost his life on Wednesday night before being rushed to hospital.

Read more

Social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has been hospitalised in China after being diagnosed with what he described as life-threatening malaria.

What he said

VDM, who is in China on a business trip, shared the health update in a video on Instagram on Thursday, 19 November 2026. He said his condition got worse despite his attempts to treat the illness himself.

According to him, things became so serious that he nearly lost his life on Wednesday night, before he was rushed to hospital the next morning.

First hospital stay

The 32-year-old said he had never spent a night in hospital before, but that his condition would now keep him under medical care for several days.

Background

VDM is one of Nigeria's most influential social media critics. Earlier this week, Oyo corps members credited him after their delayed allowances were paid following his intervention.

Kano court jails TikToker five months and orders 10 strokes for insulting Hisbah commander

19 Nov 2026, 06:41 GMT

A Shariah court in Kano has reportedly sentenced a 19-year-old TikTok user to five months in prison and 10 strokes of the cane after she pleaded guilty to insulting the Hisbah Board and its commander. Two other defendants pleaded not guilty.

Read more

A Shariah court in Kano State has reportedly sentenced a 19-year-old TikTok user, Zainab Abubakar, to five months in prison and 10 strokes of the cane for insulting the state Hisbah Board and its commander, Malam Aminu Ibrahim Daurawa.

The judgment

The court, sitting at the Hisbah Board headquarters in Sharada, delivered the judgment on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, after she pleaded guilty to the charge under Section 312 of the Penal Code. She was sentenced without the option of a fine.

The other defendants

She was arraigned alongside two young men who were also arrested by the Hisbah over allegations of using TikTok to insult the agency and its commander. Both pleaded not guilty to charges of conspiracy and insulting the board. The court remanded them in custody for two weeks while it considers their bail application and directed the prosecution to prepare its witnesses.

Background

The Kano Hisbah is the state's religious police, which enforces Islamic moral codes. Earlier this week, TikTokers were reported to have been arrested over videos said to have insulted its commander.
