IGP Disu warns officers: 'Do not be used' by politicians in 2027 elections

12 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

The Inspector-General of Police, Tunji Disu, has warned police officers against allowing politicians to use them to undermine the 2027 elections.

What he said

Speaking on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, at a monthly meeting with senior officers in Abuja, Disu said the police would remain neutral and give equal protection to all candidates, parties, supporters and voters. He said he would not tolerate any officer using police authority to advance the interests of a party, candidate or sponsor, and that officers found colluding with politicians, aiding thuggery or interfering with voters' rights would be disciplined. He told officers that the force is not a tool for any politician and urged them not to allow themselves to be used.

Officers to stay at posts

The IGP directed commissioners of police and other senior officers to remain in their commands throughout the electoral process.

Background

The warning comes amid concern over political violence ahead of the elections and a recent controversy over a video showing police officers escorting businessman Blord, which the police said was unlawful. On the same day, the police said they had raided so-called phone farms used to spread election misinformation. The European Union has also raised concerns about violence and hate speech before the polls.

  • Disu says the police will stay neutral.
  • Officers who collude with politicians face discipline.
  • Senior officers must stay in their commands.

Abure-led Labour Party faction endorses Tinubu for 2027

23 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Labour Party faction led by Julius Abure has reportedly endorsed President Tinubu for re-election. Abure said the faction would mobilise the votes the party won in 2023 behind the president.

A faction of the Labour Party led by Julius Abure has reportedly endorsed President Bola Tinubu for re-election in the 2027 general election.

The endorsement

Abure announced the decision on Monday, 23 November 2026, after a meeting of the faction's National Executive Council in Abuja. The faction is said to have described Tinubu as the most suitable candidate to lead Nigeria beyond 2027, pointing to his political experience and his time as governor of Lagos State.

Abure also reportedly pledged to mobilise the six million votes the Labour Party claims it won in the 2023 presidential election in support of the president.

Background

Earlier this month, Abure said he would not support Peter Obi, the party's 2023 presidential candidate, who is now running on the NDC platform. He had said he was open to working with either Tinubu or Atiku Abubakar, depending on how the party's leadership dispute was resolved. The Labour Party has been split by a long-running leadership tussle.

How people reacted

The endorsement drew criticism and sarcastic comments on social media, with many users questioning the faction's motives.

Makinde says Tinubu has only a 30% chance of winning in 2027

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly put President Tinubu's chances of re-election at 30 per cent, citing economic hardship and insecurity. The figure is his own estimate, not from a poll.

Read more

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly said President Bola Tinubu has only a 30 per cent chance of winning the 2027 presidential election.

His argument

Makinde is said to have argued that poor governance has worn away the goodwill the ruling APC once enjoyed. He pointed to food insecurity, the rising cost of living and insecurity as the main reasons he believes many Nigerians have turned against the party.

He also reportedly accused the APC leadership of dismissing citizens' concerns instead of addressing them.

Not a poll

The 30 per cent figure is Makinde's own assessment and was not taken from any published opinion poll or independent survey.

Background

Makinde is himself among the challengers in the race. His comments come days after Tinubu's APC held a campaign rally in Ibadan, the Oyo State capital.

Defence Ministry deletes post promoting Tinubu's Ibadan rally after backlash

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Ministry of Defence has reportedly removed a post from its official X account that publicised the APC presidential rally in Ibadan. Critics said it breached rules on the use of state resources in campaigns.

Read more

Nigeria's Ministry of Defence has reportedly taken down a post from its official social media accounts that promoted President Bola Tinubu's APC campaign rally in Ibadan, after a public outcry over the neutrality of state institutions.

The post

The post, published on Saturday on the ministry's verified X account, announced the APC's re-election rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, and included photos showing campaign banners of Tinubu and Vice President Kashim Shettima. It said Defence Minister Christopher Musa had inspected the venue to check security and crowd-control arrangements ahead of the president's arrival.

The criticism

Civic groups and opposition figures are said to have condemned the post, arguing that it went against the Electoral Act's ban on using state resources and public media to favour or disadvantage any party, as well as guidelines that require public institutions to remain neutral.

Background

Tinubu held the rally in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026, where APC candidates in the South-West were endorsed. He later said he kept the event low-key out of respect for victims of the Bayelsa boat accident. The ministry was not reported to have commented on why the post was removed.
