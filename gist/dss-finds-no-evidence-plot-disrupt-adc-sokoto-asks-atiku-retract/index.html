DSS says it found no evidence it planned to disrupt ADC campaign in Sokoto, asks Atiku to retract or provide proof

18 Nov 2026, 07:57 GMT

The Department of State Services (DSS) says its investigation found no evidence that it planned to recruit protesters to disrupt the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential campaign in Sokoto.

How it started

Earlier this month, former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar accused the leadership of the DSS of recruiting protesters to disrupt the ADC's presidential campaign flag-off rally in Sokoto.

The allegation drew wide attention, and the DSS responded by ordering an investigation into the claim.

The DSS findings

The agency now says its investigation found nothing to support the allegation that it planned to recruit protesters or disrupt the ADC campaign.

It has asked Atiku to either retract or clarify his claim, or to provide evidence that could support further investigation.

Not the final word

It is important to note that this is the DSS's own preliminary finding. It was not an independent investigation, which means the agency effectively investigated an allegation made against itself.

Atiku has not issued a fresh public response to the DSS findings.

Why it matters

With the 2027 elections approaching, allegations that security agencies are being used against opposition parties are likely to keep coming up. Public trust depends on such claims being properly examined, whether they turn out to be true or not.

APC says letter asking INEC to guarantee Tinubu's 2027 win is fake

23 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The APC has dismissed a letter circulating online that claimed its national chairman asked INEC to ensure President Tinubu is declared winner in 2027. The party called it fake and malicious.

Read more

The All Progressives Congress (APC) has rejected a letter circulating on social media that claimed to be from its National Chairman, Professor Nentawe Yilwatda, asking the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) to guarantee President Bola Tinubu's victory in the 2027 election.

What the letter claimed

The document, dated 22 November 2026 and addressed to INEC Chairman Professor Joash Amupitan, purported to ask the commission to use its administrative and operational machinery to make sure Tinubu was declared winner of the next presidential election.

The party's response

In a statement on Monday, the chairman's special adviser on media described the letter as fake and malicious, and a calculated attempt to damage the reputation of the party's leadership and weaken trust in democratic institutions. The party said the letter did not come from Yilwatda or his office, and pointed to features missing from the document that would appear on genuine correspondence.

Background

The row comes as campaigns for the January 2027 elections intensify.

Abure-led Labour Party faction endorses Tinubu for 2027

23 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Labour Party faction led by Julius Abure has reportedly endorsed President Tinubu for re-election. Abure said the faction would mobilise the votes the party won in 2023 behind the president.

Read more

A faction of the Labour Party led by Julius Abure has reportedly endorsed President Bola Tinubu for re-election in the 2027 general election.

The endorsement

Abure announced the decision on Monday, 23 November 2026, after a meeting of the faction's National Executive Council in Abuja. The faction is said to have described Tinubu as the most suitable candidate to lead Nigeria beyond 2027, pointing to his political experience and his time as governor of Lagos State.

Abure also reportedly pledged to mobilise the six million votes the Labour Party claims it won in the 2023 presidential election in support of the president.

Background

Earlier this month, Abure said he would not support Peter Obi, the party's 2023 presidential candidate, who is now running on the NDC platform. He had said he was open to working with either Tinubu or Atiku Abubakar, depending on how the party's leadership dispute was resolved. The Labour Party has been split by a long-running leadership tussle.

How people reacted

The endorsement drew criticism and sarcastic comments on social media, with many users questioning the faction's motives.

Senate President Godswill Akpabio has reportedly said Akwa Ibom will give President Tinubu two million votes in 2027. At the same event in Uyo, a pro-Tinubu group backed by Tompolo received 31 campaign vehicles.

Read more

Senate President Godswill Akpabio has reportedly said Akwa Ibom State will give overwhelming support to President Bola Tinubu in the 2027 elections, setting a target of two million votes.

The event

Akpabio spoke at Ibom Hall Ground in Uyo, where the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement inaugurated its Akwa Ibom executives. The movement's grand patron, former militant leader Government Ekpemupolo, known as Tompolo, donated 31 campaign vehicles and other campaign materials. Akpabio is said to have praised the movement's growing grassroots presence. Its national coordinator described the Akwa Ibom inauguration as another milestone in its nationwide expansion.

Makinde says Tinubu has only a 30% chance of winning in 2027

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly put President Tinubu's chances of re-election at 30 per cent, citing economic hardship and insecurity. The figure is his own estimate, not from a poll.

Read more

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly said President Bola Tinubu has only a 30 per cent chance of winning the 2027 presidential election.

His argument

Makinde is said to have argued that poor governance has worn away the goodwill the ruling APC once enjoyed. He pointed to food insecurity, the rising cost of living and insecurity as the main reasons he believes many Nigerians have turned against the party.

He also reportedly accused the APC leadership of dismissing citizens' concerns instead of addressing them.

Not a poll

The 30 per cent figure is Makinde's own assessment and was not taken from any published opinion poll or independent survey.

Background

Makinde is himself among the challengers in the race. His comments come days after Tinubu's APC held a campaign rally in Ibadan, the Oyo State capital.
