Omoni Oboli and husband Nnamdi celebrate 26 years of marriage

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

Nollywood actress, director and producer Omoni Oboli and her husband, Nnamdi Oboli, are celebrating 26 years of marriage.

What they shared

On Thursday, 29 October 2026, Nnamdi posted a throwback photo of the couple and thanked God for another year together, describing their marriage as the Lord's doing and writing that love lives in their home. Omoni responded with her own tribute, calling him the wind beneath her wings and promising to love him always and forever.

Background

Omoni Oboli is one of Nollywood's best-known filmmakers. She rose to fame as an actress in the 1990s and has since directed and produced hit films including 'Wives on Strike' and 'Okafor's Law'. She has often spoken about her husband's support for her career and about the importance of family. The couple have three sons.

How fans reacted

Fans and colleagues congratulated the couple, with many describing them as an example of a lasting marriage in the entertainment industry.

Issakaba Returns sets opening weekend record for a Nollywood action film

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Lancelot Oduwa Imasuen's Issakaba Returns has reportedly grossed ₦47.8 million in its opening weekend, the highest-ever debut for an action thriller at the Nigerian box office.

Nollywood action thriller Issakaba Returns has reportedly set a new box office record, earning ₦47.8 million in its opening weekend.

The record

The figure makes it the highest-grossing debut ever for an action thriller at the Nigerian box office. The film premiered at a cinema in Victoria Island, Lagos, before its general cinema release.

The filmmaker

Director Lancelot Oduwa Imasuen, popularly known as D'Guvnor, thanked everyone who bought tickets and supported the film, describing the achievement as more than just numbers.

Femi Branch opens up on two years out of work after on-set accident

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Actor Femi Branch says a stunt that went wrong on a film set in Ikorodu left him unable to work for two years, forcing him to sell his late mother's car. He said some colleagues abandoned him.

Read more

Nollywood actor Femi Branch has opened up about a difficult period in his life after a serious accident on a film set left him unable to work for two years.

The accident

In an interview that surfaced online on Friday, 20 November 2026, Branch said he was injured while shooting a stunt in Ikorodu, Lagos. He said he had to jump from a storey building while dressed as a security guard, and the impact when he landed was so severe it sounded like an explosion.

Hard times

He said the injury kept him out of work for two years, leaving him isolated and in financial difficulty. He revealed that he had to sell his mother's car during that period, and said some colleagues deserted him while he was recovering.

Leeds stun Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge

21 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Leeds United beat Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge in the Premier League. Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso admitted the loss was painful and that his side's confidence has taken a hit.

Read more

Leeds United inflicted a heavy 4-1 defeat on Chelsea at Stamford Bridge on matchday 11 of the Premier League on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The match

Emmanuel Emegha gave Chelsea an early lead on his first start for the club since joining in the summer. Leeds hit back quickly through Noah Okafor, and the sides went into half-time level at 1-1.

After the break, Leeds were awarded a penalty when Morgan Rogers was judged to have handled the ball in the area, a decision that sparked debate, and went on to complete a convincing win.

Alonso's reaction

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso said the result was painful and reflected a team struggling to keep up its earlier form. He admitted his players' confidence had taken a hit at a difficult time.
