Political violence: Jonathan sounds alarm, Senate asks INEC to disqualify violent candidates as APC and ADC trade blame

17 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Concern over violence ahead of the 2027 elections dominated political discussion on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, with a former president, the Senate and the main parties all weighing in.

Jonathan's warning

Speaking at a peace and security conference organised by his foundation in Abuja, former President Goodluck Jonathan warned that rising political intolerance and violence could threaten the elections. He said some political groups were running their campaign teams like cult groups, and that Nigerians at home and abroad had expressed fears about possible crises after the polls. He also condemned thuggery and the use of young people as tools of violence.

The Senate's resolution

The Senate, acting on a motion by Senator Abdul Ningi (ADC, Bauchi Central), condemned recent attacks on political figures. These included the stoning of President Bola Tinubu's motorcade after Friday prayers in Abuja, reported attacks on NDC candidate Peter Obi in Edo State, a disrupted NDC procession in Asaba on 7 November that left one person dead, and an attack on ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar's convoy in Sokoto.

Lawmakers asked the Inspector-General of Police to carry out impartial investigations and prosecute those responsible, and urged INEC to apply the law disqualifying candidates linked to electoral violence. The motion was seconded by Senator Ali Ndume (APC, Borno South).

APC and ADC trade accusations

The APC presidential campaign council, through its spokesperson Dele Alake, accused the ADC and NDC of sponsoring violence, deepfakes and hired protests to damage President Tinubu's campaign, pointing to the convoy incident in Abuja.

The ADC campaign council, through Phrank Shaibu, dismissed the claim as a distraction from the government's economic record. It challenged the APC to provide evidence or withdraw the allegation, and asked why no one had been prosecuted if the ruling party had proof. The NDC's response was not included in the reports.

The chants

The APC statement also blamed the opposition for youths chanting "Tinubu Must Go" and the Hausa phrase "Ba mayi", meaning roughly "we are not supporting you", at some of the ruling party's rallies.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

  • Jonathan says some campaigns are run like cult groups.
  • The Senate wants INEC to disqualify candidates linked to violence.
  • The ADC rejects APC claims that it sponsors attacks.

Tinubu arrives in Lagos for family prayer session after Ibadan rally

21 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

President Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday after his campaign rally in Ibadan, ahead of a special prayer session his family has organised for him on Sunday. He was received by Governors Sanwo-Olu and Abiodun.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday, 21 November 2026, after addressing an APC presidential campaign rally in Ibadan, Oyo State.

The arrival

His aircraft landed at the Presidential Wing of the Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja at about 3pm. He was received by Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, Ogun Governor Dapo Abiodun, his Chief of Staff Femi Gbajabiamila and former Ogun governor Segun Osoba, along with party leaders and supporters.

Family prayer

The visit is centred on a special prayer session organised in his honour by members of his family, scheduled for Sunday, 22 November. The President is expected to return to Abuja afterwards.

The campaign

The trip comes as the APC steps up its campaign across the South-West. At the Ibadan rally, Tinubu endorsed the party's governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos and promised improved electricity supply.

Tinubu endorses APC governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos at Ibadan rally, promises better power

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

At an APC rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, President Tinubu raised the hands of the party's governorship candidates for Oyo, Ogun and Lagos. He also guaranteed improved electricity and promised a factory in Ibadan.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos States at a campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The endorsements

Telling the crowd he would show them who to vote for as governor, Tinubu raised the hands of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC candidate in Oyo; Senator Solomon Adeola, known as Yayi, the party's candidate in Ogun; and Lagos Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, the APC flagbearer in Lagos. He also raised the hand of Remi Oseni, the party's senatorial candidate for Oyo South.

Promise on power

During the visit, the President also assured Nigerians of better electricity supply, saying the Minister of Power and his team were working hard. He added that the government would build a factory in Ibadan.

Context

The rally is part of the APC's campaign ahead of the 16 January 2027 elections.

The other side

Reactions from the opposition parties to the rally were not included in the reports.

Sowore makes surprise Onitsha market visit, says 'no rented crowds'

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore says he received a spontaneous welcome at Onitsha's Ogbo Ogwu market, a day after Peter Obi campaigned in the city. He also criticised the partition on the Niger Bridge.

Read more

African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has made a surprise visit to the Ogbo Ogwu medicine market in Onitsha, Anambra State, where he says traders gave him a warm welcome.

The visit

Sowore shared details of the visit on X on Saturday, 21 November 2026. He said he walked across the First Niger Bridge and arrived at the market without any prior announcement.

He described the reception as spontaneous and genuine, insisting that no crowds had been hired or organised for his arrival, and promised to return.

Bridge complaint

During the visit, Sowore criticised Works Minister David Umahi over a partition installed on the Niger Bridge, claiming it was disrupting traffic and causing hardship for commuters.

Context

His visit came a day after NDC candidate Peter Obi held a campaign event in Onitsha.

Former senator and Adamawa State governorship candidate Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, popularly known as Binani, has announced the death of her father.

The announcement

In a post on social media, Binani said her father died after a brief illness in Abuja. She opened her message with the Islamic expression of condolence and thanked God despite the loss.

Funeral arrangements

She said the funeral prayer, known as Jana'iza, would take place on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the Central Mosque after the afternoon Zuhr prayer.

Who she is

Binani is a former senator who contested the Adamawa governorship election in 2023. She is now a member of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).
