Enioluwa apologises after backlash over comments on young Nigerians and work

17 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa has apologised after a clip from a youth conference, in which he appeared to suggest young people don't like to work, drew wide criticism. BBNaija's Nelly Mbonu was among those who pushed back.

Read more

Influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa has issued a public apology after remarks he made about young Nigerians at a youth conference sparked a backlash online.

What happened

A clip from the Emergence of Stars Port Harcourt Youth Conference showed Enioluwa making remarks that many viewers felt painted young Nigerians as unwilling to work. The video spread quickly on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, drawing criticism from people who said he ignored unemployment, systemic barriers and the economic struggles young Nigerians face.

Many critics accused him of speaking from a position of privilege. BBNaija star Nelly Mbonu was among those who publicly challenged him, rejecting the idea that young people simply choose not to work hard.

The apology

Later the same day, Enioluwa posted a statement on X addressing the reaction, apologising for the remarks and acknowledging his privilege.

Aremu Afolayan reportedly clashes with Isbae U on Curiosity Made Me Ask

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actor Aremu Afolayan's appearance on Isbae U's show Curiosity Made Me Ask reportedly ended in a heated exchange. He took offence when the host compared their late fathers.

Read more

Nollywood actor Aremu Afolayan has become a talking point online after his appearance on comedian Isbae U's interview show, Curiosity Made Me Ask, reportedly ended in a heated exchange.

What happened

Isbae U, known for his sarcastic interview style, is said to have questioned whether Aremu was still active in Nollywood and made jokes about his body and how he earns a living given his few recent film roles.

Aremu reportedly explained that he has stayed away from film sets because of insecurity, saying travelling for shoots had become dangerous and he was not willing to risk his life for a role paying ₦2 million or ₦3 million.

Where it went wrong

The mood reportedly changed when Isbae U brought up his own late father, actor Sir K. Kamoru, and compared him with Aremu's late father, the veteran filmmaker Ade Love Afolayan. Aremu is said to have taken offence and warned the host against such comparisons, insisting his father's contribution to the film industry was not up for debate. He also reportedly questioned Isbae U's own achievements as an actor.

Popular streamer Peller has reportedly hit out at the Recording Academy after young singer Ice King Ochacho was left out of the 2027 Grammy nominations.

What Peller said

In a video that circulated online on Monday, 16 November 2026, Peller is said to have voiced his frustration at the snub and declared that he would unfollow the Grammys' account. He reportedly argued that the Academy should even have created a special category to recognise the young performer.

Who is Ice King Ochacho

Ice King Ochacho, a teenager, is the son of businessman King Ochacho. He released his debut album, The Future, in October 2026, featuring collaborations with established artists, and his single No Competition reportedly passed one million streams on Spotify.

Background

Peller has been one of the young singer's loudest supporters. In September, King Ochacho recalled that the streamer had earlier predicted his son would one day win a Grammy.

The 2027 Grammy nominations, announced this week, include Nigerian stars Tems, Davido and Ayra Starr.

Super Eagles held 0-0 by Tanzania, AFCON 2027 hopes take a hit

17 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigeria were held to a goalless draw by Tanzania in Zanzibar in their AFCON 2027 qualifier, missing the chance to move to nine points. Tolu Arokodare hit the post in the first half, and coach Eric Chelle admitted afterwards that the team is in trouble.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles were held to a 0-0 draw by Tanzania at the Amaan Stadium in Zanzibar on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, a result that reportedly dents their hopes in 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying Group L.

The match

Nigeria went into the game knowing a win would take them to nine points and put pressure on the group leaders, Guinea-Bissau. Despite having long spells of possession, they could not break down a well-organised Tanzanian side.

Their best chance came in the 33rd minute, when Moses Usor set up Tolu Arokodare, whose close-range shot hit the post. Calvin Bassey fired over five minutes later, while Tanzania also struck the woodwork in the 42nd minute. Coach Eric Chelle made changes just after the hour, but the breakthrough never came.

After the game

Chelle reportedly admitted after the match that the team is now in trouble in the qualifying race. Nigeria had won the first leg 3-1 in Uyo, but the draw means they missed the chance to move above Guinea-Bissau.

Background

The game came a day after Chelle clashed with a journalist over his tactics at the pre-match press conference. Nigeria were without injured forwards Ademola Lookman and Victor Osimhen.
