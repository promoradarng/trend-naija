Ex-Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami survives attack at his Abeokuta home

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Former Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami says he fought off three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta in the early hours of Friday. Police say he has been treated and discharged from hospital.

Read more

Former Super Eagles captain and football administrator Segun Odegbami has survived an attack by three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta, Ogun State, in the early hours of Friday, 20 November 2026.

What happened

The attackers reportedly overpowered his domestic staff and driver before forcing their way into his bedroom at about 2:30am. The 74-year-old was injured in the attack.

Odegbami described the incident as an attempt on his life. He said he fought back with help from his driver and house help, and the attackers eventually fled. He said it was the closest he had ever come to death.

Police response

The Ogun State Police Command confirmed the attack and said the former international had been treated in hospital and discharged.

Who he is

Odegbami is a former captain of the Super Eagles and one of Nigeria's best-known football figures.

Manhunt ordered

Ogun State Commissioner of Police Bode Ojajuni visited Odegbami at his home and ordered an immediate manhunt for the attackers, promising they would be prosecuted once caught.

FIFA President Infantino arrives in Warri for stadium commissioning

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has arrived in Warri, Delta State, for the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium. He was received by Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former NFF president Amaju Pinnick.

Read more

FIFA President Gianni Infantino arrived in Warri, Delta State, on Friday, 20 November 2026, ahead of the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium.

The arrival

Infantino landed at Osubi Airport on a private jet and was received by Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former Nigeria Football Federation president Amaju Pinnick. He said the warm welcome showed the unifying power of football.

Third visit

It is Infantino's third visit to Nigeria. He first came in 2016 shortly after taking office, and returned in 2023 for the Aisha Buhari Cup.

Background

The visit comes days after FIFA appointed a normalisation committee to run the NFF, and as Infantino prepares to face challengers in the FIFA presidential election in March 2027.

Tinubu's praise singer Rarara mobbed at Abuja National Mosque

21 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Hausa praise singer Dauda Kahutu Rarara, a well-known supporter of President Tinubu, was attacked by an angry crowd at the Abuja National Mosque during Friday prayers. Security operatives struggled to hold the crowd back.

Read more

Popular Hausa praise singer Dauda Kahutu, widely known as Rarara, was attacked by an angry crowd at the Abuja National Mosque during Friday prayers on 20 November 2026.

What happened

Rarara, a prominent supporter of President Bola Tinubu, was entering the mosque with plain-clothed and uniformed security personnel when a group of people rushed at him. Security operatives tried to hold the crowd back but could not fully contain them. At one point, the singer removed his cap, which carried Tinubu's insignia.

A video of the incident has spread widely online.

Context

The incident came a week after President Tinubu's convoy was pelted with stones after Friday prayers at a mosque in Garki, Abuja, an attack for which police arrested 78 people before the Presidency directed their release.

Reactions

Paul Ibe, media adviser to ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar, condemned the violence on X while also making a political point about the incident.

Yahaya Bello says he will beat Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan 'overwhelmingly' in Kogi Central

21 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Former Kogi Governor Yahaya Bello, who plans to contest the Kogi Central senatorial seat in 2027, has claimed his political reach far exceeds that of the sitting senator, Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan.

Read more

Former Kogi State Governor Yahaya Bello has said he will win the Kogi Central senatorial seat in the 2027 elections overwhelmingly, describing the incumbent, Senator Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan, as politically junior to him.

What he said

In an interview published on Friday, 20 November 2026, Bello reportedly said his political influence in Kogi State is far greater than that of any other politician. Asked whether nearly four years out of elective office had reduced his standing, he said the break had actually added to his popularity.

He also claimed he would not need to spend his own money on the campaign, saying residents had started contributing to it.

The contest

Akpoti-Uduaghan currently represents Kogi Central in the Senate. Her response to his remarks was not included in the reports.
