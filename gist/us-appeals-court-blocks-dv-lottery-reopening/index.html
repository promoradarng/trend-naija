US appeals court puts hold on order to reopen 2027 visa lottery

1 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Hopes of an imminent opening of the US Diversity Visa lottery for 2027 have been put on hold after a federal appeals court intervened, according to reports on Sunday, 1 November 2026.

What happened

The US Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit reportedly granted an emergency stay on 29 October, pausing an order issued by District Judge Jinsook Ohta on 14 October. That order, made in a case brought by immigration law firm Red Eagle Law against Secretary of State Marco Rubio, had required the State Department to announce registration dates, open applications and notify selected entrants by 3 December. The lower court had set 29 October for publishing registration details and 1 November for opening the application window.

Why it was paused

The appeals judges reportedly found that the law firm had not shown that its financial interest in representing lottery winners was closely enough linked to the programme's goal of increasing immigration from under-represented countries. The court said its decision was not an endorsement of the State Department's failure to open the programme.

Who is affected

The lottery offers up to 55,000 US immigrant visas each year to people from countries with low rates of immigration to the United States, and African applicants have long made up a large share of winners. Tens of thousands of hopefuls across the continent now have no confirmed registration date. Nigeria has been excluded from the lottery for several years because of its high number of immigrants to the US, but many Nigerians follow the programme closely and some qualify through a spouse's or parent's country of birth.

What happens next

The registration window will remain closed until the appeal is decided or the State Department announces its own dates.

  • The Ninth Circuit paused an order to open the 2027 lottery.
  • The lower court had set 1 November for registration to open.
  • Applicants must wait for the appeal to be decided.

What happened

Democrats win biggest US House majority in more than three decades

23 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

US Democrats now hold 258 of 435 House seats after a recount in North Carolina, their largest majority since 1992. They also won the Senate in this month's midterm elections.

Read more

The Democratic Party has secured its largest majority in the US House of Representatives in more than 30 years, according to media projections.

The deciding race

US networks projected on Sunday, 22 November 2026, that Democrat Chuck Hubbard had defeated Republican incumbent Virginia Foxx in North Carolina after a recount requested by Foxx. The win gives Democrats 258 seats in the 435-member chamber, matching the number they won in 1992, the year Bill Clinton was elected president. Hubbard said Foxx had conceded and thanked her for her decades of service.

House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries celebrated on X, saying the party now held the largest majority of any party in Congress this century.

Senate too

Democrats also won control of the Senate in this month's midterm elections, a major setback for President Donald Trump and the Republican Party.

What it means

Control of both chambers gives Democrats more power to block Trump's legislative agenda and launch investigations during the final two years of his presidency. The results were widely seen as reflecting voter frustration over living costs, tariffs and the conflict with Iran.

Thousands evacuated from Don Toliver concert in Manchester after unknown substance released

23 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Police declared a major incident at Manchester's Co-op Live arena after an unknown substance made concertgoers unwell during a Don Toliver show. More than 50 people were assessed and one was taken to hospital.

Read more

Thousands of fans were evacuated from Manchester's Co-op Live arena on Sunday night after an unknown substance reportedly made several concertgoers unwell.

What happened

About 12,000 people were at a performance by American rapper Don Toliver, part of the UK leg of his Octane World Tour, when emergency services were called. Greater Manchester Police declared a major incident after reports of people falling ill in the crowd.

The response

The ambulance service said it assessed more than 50 people, some of whom reported eye and breathing symptoms, and decontamination was carried out at the venue. Police said on Monday that several people were treated at the scene and one was taken to hospital.

Investigation

Officers said they were working to find out what the substance was. The arena, which describes itself as the UK's largest indoor entertainment venue, said it was cooperating with the police.
