US appeals court puts hold on order to reopen 2027 visa lottery

1 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Hopes of an imminent opening of the US Diversity Visa lottery for 2027 have been put on hold after a federal appeals court intervened, according to reports on Sunday, 1 November 2026.

What happened

The US Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit reportedly granted an emergency stay on 29 October, pausing an order issued by District Judge Jinsook Ohta on 14 October. That order, made in a case brought by immigration law firm Red Eagle Law against Secretary of State Marco Rubio, had required the State Department to announce registration dates, open applications and notify selected entrants by 3 December. The lower court had set 29 October for publishing registration details and 1 November for opening the application window.

Why it was paused

The appeals judges reportedly found that the law firm had not shown that its financial interest in representing lottery winners was closely enough linked to the programme's goal of increasing immigration from under-represented countries. The court said its decision was not an endorsement of the State Department's failure to open the programme.

Who is affected

The lottery offers up to 55,000 US immigrant visas each year to people from countries with low rates of immigration to the United States, and African applicants have long made up a large share of winners. Tens of thousands of hopefuls across the continent now have no confirmed registration date. Nigeria has been excluded from the lottery for several years because of its high number of immigrants to the US, but many Nigerians follow the programme closely and some qualify through a spouse's or parent's country of birth.

What happens next

The registration window will remain closed until the appeal is decided or the State Department announces its own dates.

  • The Ninth Circuit paused an order to open the 2027 lottery.
  • The lower court had set 1 November for registration to open.
  • Applicants must wait for the appeal to be decided.

UK says Putin will not be invited to 2027 G20 summit, breaking with Trump

6 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

British Prime Minister Andy Burnham has reportedly said Russian President Vladimir Putin will not attend the G20 summit the UK hosts in Manchester in November 2027, because of sanctions. President Trump has invited Putin to this year's G20 in Miami.

Read more

The United Kingdom says it will not invite Russian President Vladimir Putin to the G20 summit it is hosting in 2027, setting it apart from the United States.

What was said

In an interview reported on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Prime Minister Andy Burnham was reportedly asked whether Putin would be invited to the summit in Manchester in November 2027, and replied that he would not. He said the guest list was handled by the G20, but that Putin would not be coming because of an outstanding sanction.

A contrast with Washington

President Donald Trump has invited Putin to this year's G20 summit in Miami in December. The Kremlin has not confirmed whether the Russian leader will attend.

Background

Britain has avoided direct engagement with Russian ministers since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, and has imposed wide sanctions on Russian officials. Tensions over Russia's role in the G20 were on display at a meeting of foreign ministers in October, when a planned group photo was cancelled after European ministers refused to pose with Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov. The G20 includes the world's largest economies, and the African Union became a full member in 2023.

Why it matters

The disagreement highlights divisions among Western allies over how to deal with Russia as the war in Ukraine continues.

Nicki Minaj tells critics 'Trump is still your President' after Democrats' midterm win

Rapper Nicki Minaj has reportedly posted a list of messages backing President Trump after the Democrats won control of Congress, praising JD Vance and Elon Musk and urging Republicans to wake up.

American rapper Nicki Minaj has doubled down on her support for President Donald Trump after his party's heavy losses in the US midterm elections.

What she posted

In a series of posts on X on Wednesday night, 4 November 2026, Minaj reportedly shared a numbered list of messages, declaring that Trump is still the president and, in a reference to herself, that 'the Queen' is still people's obsession. She also praised Vice President JD Vance, congratulated Republican winners including Florida's governor-elect Byron Donalds, described Elon Musk as a trillionaire and urged Republicans to wake up. She thanked her fans, known as the Barbz, and shared a video responding to criticism of her support for Trump.

Background

The Democrats are projected to have won control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate in the 3 November midterms, in a result widely seen as a rebuke of Trump's second-term agenda. Trump said he was not on the ballot and later admitted the Iran war had contributed to the losses. Minaj, who was born in Trinidad and Tobago, has in recent years become an outspoken supporter of Trump, which has divided her fan base.

How people reacted

Her posts drew mixed reactions, with some fans applauding her and others criticising her political views.

Paraguay's President Santiago Peña resigns over bank collapse scandal

5 Nov 2026, 20:43 GMT

Paraguay's President Santiago Peña has resigned after the lower house voted to begin impeachment proceedings over the collapse of a bank he was once linked to as a shareholder. He apologised to Paraguayans.

Read more

Paraguay's President Santiago Peña has resigned amid a political crisis caused by the collapse of a bank he was previously linked to.

What happened

In a video message posted on X, Peña said he was stepping down to protect the country's stability and democratic institutions, a day after opposition lawmakers requested his impeachment and the lower house of Congress voted to begin proceedings, according to reports on Thursday, 5 November 2026. He apologised to the people if he had failed them at any point, saying it was never his intention to betray their trust.

The bank collapse

The crisis began when Ueno Bank collapsed and froze its operations, reportedly leaving large numbers of customers unable to access their accounts. Lawmakers accused Peña of conflicts of interest, pointing to his past shareholding in the company that owned the bank.

Background

Peña, an economist and former finance minister, took office in August 2023 as a member of the long-dominant Colorado Party. Under Paraguay's constitution, the vice-president takes over when a president leaves office before the end of the term.

What happens next

Attention now turns to who will lead the country for the rest of the term, and to investigations into the bank's collapse and whether depositors will recover their money.

Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

Well-wishers have been visiting Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu with gifts, money and encouragement after a viral video showed her co-wife's children refusing to spray money on her at her husband's Ozo title ceremony. Her story has reopened a conversation about how childless women are treated in some Nigerian families.

Read more

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.
