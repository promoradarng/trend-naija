Hackers send fake nuclear attack alerts to millions in Russia and Ukraine

2 Nov 2026, 14:56 GMT

Emergency alert services in both Russia and Ukraine were reportedly hit by cyber attacks that sent false warnings of nuclear, chemical and radiation threats to civilians, according to reports on Monday, 2 November 2026.

In Russia

Fake nuclear attack warnings appeared on channels linked to Russia's Emergency Situations Ministry in Moscow, St Petersburg and several other regions at about 2.45am, reportedly telling residents to gather three weeks' supply of food and take shelter in cellars. Similar messages were reported on the ministry's mobile app, by text message and on the state-backed messaging platform MAX. The ministry described it as a massive cyber attack, but said its sirens, loudspeakers, television and radio warning systems had not been affected. It has not been established who carried out the attack.

In Ukraine

Hours earlier, Ukraine's Air Alert service sent a series of false notifications claiming radiation and chemical threats in Kyiv, a nuclear alert across the country and street fighting in the capital, and telling residents to stay indoors. Ukrainian authorities confirmed the service had been hacked and said it was working normally again after midnight. The app normally warns people of incoming Russian missile and drone attacks, often giving them only minutes to find shelter. Hours after the false alerts, real warnings sounded during a Russian attack on Odesa that reportedly injured four people.

Background

The incidents came amid continued strikes by both sides and talk of possible negotiations. A Kremlin adviser said he believed President Vladimir Putin would take a phone call from President Volodymyr Zelensky, although Putin has refused his calls in the past.

Why it matters

Experts warn that attacks on alert systems are dangerous because they can cause panic and make people less likely to trust genuine warnings.

  • False alerts warned of nuclear, chemical and radiation threats.
  • Russia called it a massive cyber attack.
  • Both countries said their systems were restored.

At least 30 killed as fuel tanker overturns and bursts into flames in Cairo

26 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

At least 30 people died and 15 were injured when a fuel tanker overturned and caught fire on the Greater Cairo Ring Road in Egypt on Wednesday. The blaze engulfed 27 vehicles.

Read more

At least 30 people have died and 15 others were injured after a fuel tanker overturned and burst into flames on a busy stretch of the Greater Cairo Ring Road in Egypt on Wednesday, 25 November 2026.

What happened

According to Egypt's Ministry of Health and Population, the fire broke out above Tunnel 10 in the densely populated Al-Warraq area of Imbaba, a suburb of Greater Cairo. Preliminary findings suggest the privately operated tanker swerved, hit a concrete road divider and toppled over, causing an explosion that spread to 27 nearby vehicles.

The response

Authorities sent 39 ambulances to the scene, taking survivors to three hospitals in the area. Firefighters brought the blaze under control, and heavy machinery was brought in to clear the burnt-out vehicles.

Condolences

Egyptian President Abdel-Fattah el-Sisi and Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly sent condolences to the families of the victims.

Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico has won Miss Universe 2026, becoming the first mother and first married woman to take the crown. The final was held in San Juan.

Puerto Rico's Jennifer Barreto has made history as the first mother to win the Miss Universe crown.

The final

The 27-year-old was crowned Miss Universe 2026 on Tuesday, 24 November, at the grand finale of the pageant's 75th edition, held at the José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum in San Juan, Puerto Rico. She beat contestants from 115 other countries.

A historic win

Barreto is married and has a seven-year-old daughter, making her the first mother and the first married woman to win the title. Her victory also gives Puerto Rico its sixth Miss Universe crown, ending a 20-year wait.

The winning answer

In the final question round, she was asked what human value should never be lost as artificial intelligence grows. She stressed using AI responsibly and keeping human values alive through consistent actions.

Canada resumes Express Entry draws, invites 1,999 for permanent residence

24 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Canada has resumed Express Entry draws, inviting 1,999 provincial nominees to apply for permanent residence on 23 November. It was the first draw under new ranking rules that reward Canadian work experience and high-wage job offers.

Read more

Canada has resumed its Express Entry draws after a short pause, issuing 1,999 Invitations to Apply (ITAs) for permanent residence to provincial nominees on 23 November 2026.

The draw

According to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada, candidates needed a minimum Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score of 673 to be invited, and must have created their profile before 31 July 2026.

New rules

It was the first draw since major changes to the CRS took effect on 3 November 2026. Candidates can now earn extra points through a new Labour Market Integration Factor, which rewards Canadian work experience or a qualifying job offer in a high-wage occupation.

Why it matters

Express Entry is one of Canada's main routes to permanent residence for skilled workers.
