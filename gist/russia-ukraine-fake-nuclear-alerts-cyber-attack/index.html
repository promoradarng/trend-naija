Hackers send fake nuclear attack alerts to millions in Russia and Ukraine

2 Nov 2026, 14:56 GMT

Emergency alert services in both Russia and Ukraine were reportedly hit by cyber attacks that sent false warnings of nuclear, chemical and radiation threats to civilians, according to reports on Monday, 2 November 2026.

In Russia

Fake nuclear attack warnings appeared on channels linked to Russia's Emergency Situations Ministry in Moscow, St Petersburg and several other regions at about 2.45am, reportedly telling residents to gather three weeks' supply of food and take shelter in cellars. Similar messages were reported on the ministry's mobile app, by text message and on the state-backed messaging platform MAX. The ministry described it as a massive cyber attack, but said its sirens, loudspeakers, television and radio warning systems had not been affected. It has not been established who carried out the attack.

In Ukraine

Hours earlier, Ukraine's Air Alert service sent a series of false notifications claiming radiation and chemical threats in Kyiv, a nuclear alert across the country and street fighting in the capital, and telling residents to stay indoors. Ukrainian authorities confirmed the service had been hacked and said it was working normally again after midnight. The app normally warns people of incoming Russian missile and drone attacks, often giving them only minutes to find shelter. Hours after the false alerts, real warnings sounded during a Russian attack on Odesa that reportedly injured four people.

Background

The incidents came amid continued strikes by both sides and talk of possible negotiations. A Kremlin adviser said he believed President Vladimir Putin would take a phone call from President Volodymyr Zelensky, although Putin has refused his calls in the past.

Why it matters

Experts warn that attacks on alert systems are dangerous because they can cause panic and make people less likely to trust genuine warnings.

  • False alerts warned of nuclear, chemical and radiation threats.
  • Russia called it a massive cyber attack.
  • Both countries said their systems were restored.

Paolo Bulgari, jeweller to royals and film stars, dies at 89

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Paolo Bulgari, the longtime chairman of the Bvlgari luxury group, has died in Rome at the age of 89. His family called him the greatest jeweller of our times.

Read more

Italian jeweller Paolo Bulgari, who helped turn his family's Roman workshop into a global luxury brand, has died in Rome at the age of 89.

The announcement

His family announced his death on Saturday, 21 November 2026, describing him as an extraordinary entrepreneur who promoted Italian craftsmanship around the world. The cause of death was not disclosed. The Bvlgari brand also paid tribute to its former chairman.

His life and work

Born in Rome in 1937, Bulgari joined the family firm in 1960 and became known as one of the leading experts on precious gemstones. He became chairman in 1984. The house was founded in 1884 by his grandfather, a Greek silversmith who settled in Rome.

Under Paolo and his brothers, the brand became known for bold designs, colourful gems and yellow gold, opened shops in Paris, New York and Geneva, and expanded into watches, perfumes, leather goods and hotels.

Famous clients

During Rome's glamorous era of the 1950s and 1960s, the brand's flagship store became a favourite of royalty and film stars, including Sophia Loren and Audrey Hepburn.

Pochettino's Man United reign starts with 4-0 thrashing at Liverpool

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United manager ended in a 4-0 defeat at Anfield after Lenny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute. United are 19th in the Premier League.

Read more

Mauricio Pochettino's time in charge of Manchester United got off to a painful start on Sunday, 22 November 2026, as Liverpool beat his side 4-0 at Anfield.

How the game went

United's plans fell apart early when defender Lenny Yoro was shown a red card in the eighth minute, leaving them with ten men for more than 80 minutes. Liverpool quickly took advantage. Dominik Szoboszlai opened the scoring in the 10th minute, and Florian Wirtz doubled the lead three minutes later.

Liverpool kept their two-goal lead until half-time and added two more after the break through Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.

Where it leaves the clubs

The result left United in 19th place, inside the Premier League relegation zone, while Liverpool moved into the top four.

Pochettino's reaction

Speaking after the match, Pochettino reportedly admitted that the early red card shaped the game, but urged his players to turn their frustration into better performances in the coming matches.

Background

Pochettino took over at Old Trafford only recently. The club has been linked with several players ahead of the January window, including Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey.
