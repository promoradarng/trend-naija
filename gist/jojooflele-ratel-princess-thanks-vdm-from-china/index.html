"If not for VeryDarkMan, I wouldn't be where I am today": Ratel Princess Jojooflele expresses gratitude from China

5 Nov 2026, 21:44 GMT

Popular social media personality Jojooflele, widely known as the Ratel Princess, has openly expressed her appreciation for VeryDarkMan (VDM), acknowledging the role he has played in changing her life.

"If not for VDM"

Speaking from China, Jojooflele reflected on how far she has come and how her association with VDM opened doors she probably never imagined would open so soon.

"If not for VDM, I wouldn't be where I am today," she said.

According to her, things took a completely different turn after she met VeryDarkMan. Her social media pages began attracting more attention, her followers increased, and opportunities started coming her way.

How it all started

What makes her story even more interesting is how it began. Many will remember the controversy involving Nedu, when Jojooflele found herself at the centre of public attention, with people discussing her personal life and questioning her actions.

For someone else, that kind of controversy could have been enough to damage their confidence or push them off social media completely.

Instead, Jojooflele turned the attention into something positive. Rather than letting the controversy define her future, she kept building her presence online and eventually became a familiar face among supporters of the Ratel Movement.

A lesson in gratitude

Today, she is in China, reflecting on her journey and publicly appreciating someone she believes contributed greatly to her success.

Jojooflele deserves credit for how she has handled her growing popularity. The right connections can open doors, but what you do with those opportunities matters just as much.

Her appreciation for VDM is also worth acknowledging. In a society where people sometimes forget those who supported them once they start to succeed, publicly recognising someone's contribution says a lot about gratitude.

Setbacks are not the end

Perhaps the biggest lesson here is never to be too quick to conclude that your present difficulties will decide your future. The situation people expect to destroy you can end up introducing you to opportunities you never knew existed.

What looks like a setback today may become part of the story you proudly tell tomorrow.

The photo

What VDM said

He also said that his past claims always end up being proved right, dismissing what he called propaganda against him.

Background

Davido had not responded to VDM's comments at the time of the reports.

Isaac Fayose defends Davido

Footage also emerged of Davido and his wife Chioma sharing a meal with Seyi Tinubu at an amala restaurant in Abuja, which many took as a sign he was moving towards the Tinubu camp. Isaac Fayose, brother of former Ekiti governor Ayo Fayose, came to the singer's defence in a video, insisting Davido would still vote for Peter Obi. Davido reportedly reacted to Fayose's video.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.

Six presidential candidates have reportedly been invited to a policy debate in Abuja on 10 January 2027, six days before the presidential election.

The announcement

The convener of the organising group, Lai Omotola, announced the plan at a press conference in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. He said the debate is designed to push the candidates beyond campaign slogans and into detailed discussions of their policies.

Who is invited

The six candidates invited are President Bola Tinubu of the APC, Atiku Abubakar of the ADC, Peter Obi of the NDC, Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde, former Cross River governor Donald Duke and the SDP's Adewole Adebayo.

Will they attend?

It was not clear from the reports whether all the candidates have confirmed they will take part.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
