"If not for VeryDarkMan, I wouldn't be where I am today": Ratel Princess Jojooflele expresses gratitude from China

5 Nov 2026, 21:44 GMT

Popular social media personality Jojooflele, widely known as the Ratel Princess, has openly expressed her appreciation for VeryDarkMan (VDM), acknowledging the role he has played in changing her life.

"If not for VDM"

Speaking from China, Jojooflele reflected on how far she has come and how her association with VDM opened doors she probably never imagined would open so soon.

"If not for VDM, I wouldn't be where I am today," she said.

According to her, things took a completely different turn after she met VeryDarkMan. Her social media pages began attracting more attention, her followers increased, and opportunities started coming her way.

How it all started

What makes her story even more interesting is how it began. Many will remember the controversy involving Nedu, when Jojooflele found herself at the centre of public attention, with people discussing her personal life and questioning her actions.

For someone else, that kind of controversy could have been enough to damage their confidence or push them off social media completely.

Instead, Jojooflele turned the attention into something positive. Rather than letting the controversy define her future, she kept building her presence online and eventually became a familiar face among supporters of the Ratel Movement.

A lesson in gratitude

Today, she is in China, reflecting on her journey and publicly appreciating someone she believes contributed greatly to her success.

Jojooflele deserves credit for how she has handled her growing popularity. The right connections can open doors, but what you do with those opportunities matters just as much.

Her appreciation for VDM is also worth acknowledging. In a society where people sometimes forget those who supported them once they start to succeed, publicly recognising someone's contribution says a lot about gratitude.

Setbacks are not the end

Perhaps the biggest lesson here is never to be too quick to conclude that your present difficulties will decide your future. The situation people expect to destroy you can end up introducing you to opportunities you never knew existed.

What looks like a setback today may become part of the story you proudly tell tomorrow.

President Bola Tinubu has announced a support package of ₦100 million and a three-bedroom flat for each family that lost a loved one in the crash of the Nigerian Air Force aircraft NAF 931 in Ondo State.

The pledge

The president made the announcement at a memorial and wreath-laying ceremony in Abuja held in honour of the victims. The package covers the families of both the military personnel and the civilians who died in the crash.

Military support

Tinubu also assured the armed forces that the government would provide better training and equipment to improve the performance and readiness of personnel.

The Coalition for Democracy has confirmed that the second summit of Nigeria's opposition political parties will hold on Monday, 30 November 2026, at the NAF Conference Centre in Abuja.

The details

Proceedings will begin at 10am, with accreditation opening at 9am. The confirmation followed the seventh meeting of the coalition's central leadership committee on Wednesday, 26 November. The statement was signed on behalf of the coalition parties by Salihu Lukman.

What to expect

The summit is expected to adopt three key documents and deliberate on democratic renewal, party unity and electoral integrity ahead of the 2027 elections.

Benin photographer Sh0tbySav reportedly dies after 'one-chance' attack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A photographer known as Sh0tbySav has reportedly died in Benin City after being robbed and pushed out of a moving vehicle by suspected 'one-chance' criminals. Fellow photographers are mourning her.

Read more

The photography community in Benin City, Edo State, is mourning after reports that a photographer known as Sh0tbySav died following a suspected 'one-chance' robbery.

What reportedly happened

According to accounts shared on social media, she was returning home after a photoshoot for a client when she boarded a commercial vehicle believed to have been operated by criminals. She was reportedly robbed of her belongings and pushed out of the moving vehicle.

Details of the incident remain limited, and no police statement was included in the reports.

A warning

Her death has drawn fresh attention to the dangers posed by 'one-chance' gangs, criminals who pose as commercial drivers and passengers to rob unsuspecting commuters, a recurring problem in several Nigerian cities.

Tributes

Fellow photographers have shared tributes online.
