Tinubu's 2027 re-election campaign flags off in Kaduna today

24 Oct 2026, 03:32 BST

The APC is launching President Tinubu's second-term campaign in Kaduna on Saturday, the first stop on a tour of selected states. Rival camps, including Atiku's ADC and the Obi-Kwankwaso NDC, have also been mobilising their own campaign councils.

  • Campaign DG Abdulaziz Yari says Tinubu has a broader national base than his challengers.
  • The presidential election is reportedly set for 16 January 2027.
  • Supporters of other APC figures have also been reacting to the flag-off.
