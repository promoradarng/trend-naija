VeryDarkMan, Opeyemi Famakin and Peller defend Enioluwa after youth comments backlash

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

His mother speaks

Enioluwa's mother, Bola Adeoluwa, a university lecturer, has also addressed the backlash after some students on TikTok turned their criticism on her, alleging she was a harsh grader. She responded publicly to the claims and the criticism of her son.

Okanlawon weighs in

Actor Deyemi Okanlawon also criticised social media users who attacked Enioluwa in harsh terms. Enioluwa had told a conference that many young people expect success to come easily and asked those in their twenties who say they are tired of life whether they had really experienced life.

GehGeh withdraws support for Delta Governor Oborevwori, cites mother's death

23 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Social commentator GehGeh has reportedly withdrawn his endorsement of Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori. He blamed poor healthcare in his community for his mother's death and said Peter Obi remains his presidential choice.

Social commentator and content creator GehGeh has reportedly withdrawn his earlier support for Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori's re-election bid.

His reasons

GehGeh said he lost his mother on 8 December 2024, and blamed the lack of adequate healthcare facilities in his community and neighbouring areas as a major cause of her death. He said he was ashamed to have previously endorsed the governor and asked his followers to forgive him.

He also questioned what the state government was doing to tackle youth unemployment.

His presidential choice

GehGeh added that Peter Obi of the NDC remains his preferred presidential candidate for 2027.

The other side

No response from the Delta State government or the governor's campaign was included in the reports.

Four killed as gunmen attack Omo-Agege's NDC campaign in Delta

23 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Gunmen opened fire on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege and his campaign team in Uduere, Ughelli North, Delta State, on Monday. His campaign says four people were killed and four others critically injured, and that the senator escaped unhurt.

Read more

Four people were killed and several others injured when gunmen attacked a Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North Local Government Area of Delta State, on Monday, 23 November 2026.

What happened

According to his campaign organisation, armed men opened fire at close range on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege, the NDC's Delta Central senatorial candidate, and his entourage while he was addressing a crowd that had gathered to welcome him. The campaign said the senator was not hit, although bullets passed close to him.

The victims

The Omo-Agege Campaign Organisation said four people died and four others were critically injured and receiving treatment. It named two of the dead as aides of the campaign.

'Assassination attempt'

In a statement by its media adviser, the campaign described the incident as a targeted attempt on the senator's life rather than a political clash, citing eyewitnesses who said the attackers made clear he was their target.

What happens next

There was no word in the reports on arrests or a police statement. The attack comes as campaigns for the 2027 elections gather pace.

Tinubu tells Igbo leaders in Lagos he was upset over 2023 votes but they have settled

President Tinubu reportedly told Igbo stakeholders in Lagos that he was angry that some of them did not vote for him in 2023, but said the matter had been settled. He urged them to collect their PVCs and vote for him on 16 January.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has reportedly told Igbo leaders and stakeholders in Lagos that he expects their votes in the coming presidential election.

What he said

Speaking in an informal tone at a meeting on Monday, 23 November 2026, the president acknowledged that some of those present did not vote for him in 2023. He said he had been upset about it at the time, but that both sides had since settled their differences.

He urged them to register, collect their Permanent Voter Cards and vote on 16 January, reminding them of what he described as an agreement between them, and stressing that he is the APC's candidate.

Background

Lagos has a large Igbo population. The NDC's Peter Obi is among Tinubu's main challengers for 2027.

Democrats win biggest US House majority in more than three decades

23 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

US Democrats now hold 258 of 435 House seats after a recount in North Carolina, their largest majority since 1992. They also won the Senate in this month's midterm elections.

Read more

The Democratic Party has secured its largest majority in the US House of Representatives in more than 30 years, according to media projections.

The deciding race

US networks projected on Sunday, 22 November 2026, that Democrat Chuck Hubbard had defeated Republican incumbent Virginia Foxx in North Carolina after a recount requested by Foxx. The win gives Democrats 258 seats in the 435-member chamber, matching the number they won in 1992, the year Bill Clinton was elected president. Hubbard said Foxx had conceded and thanked her for her decades of service.

House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries celebrated on X, saying the party now held the largest majority of any party in Congress this century.

Senate too

Democrats also won control of the Senate in this month's midterm elections, a major setback for President Donald Trump and the Republican Party.

What it means

Control of both chambers gives Democrats more power to block Trump's legislative agenda and launch investigations during the final two years of his presidency. The results were widely seen as reflecting voter frustration over living costs, tariffs and the conflict with Iran.
