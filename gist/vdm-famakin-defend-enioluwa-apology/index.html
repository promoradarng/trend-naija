VeryDarkMan, Opeyemi Famakin and Peller defend Enioluwa after youth comments backlash

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

His mother speaks

Enioluwa's mother, Bola Adeoluwa, a university lecturer, has also addressed the backlash after some students on TikTok turned their criticism on her, alleging she was a harsh grader. She responded publicly to the claims and the criticism of her son.

Okanlawon weighs in

Actor Deyemi Okanlawon also criticised social media users who attacked Enioluwa in harsh terms. Enioluwa had told a conference that many young people expect success to come easily and asked those in their twenties who say they are tired of life whether they had really experienced life.

  • Peller says he was wrong but deserves a second chance.

Petrol prices fall by up to ₦85 a litre as Dangote suspends diesel price cut

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Petrol pump prices have reportedly fallen by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre in a week as refiners and importers compete. Dangote Refinery has, however, withdrawn a planned ₦150 cut in diesel prices pending a review.

Read more

Motorists are seeing some relief at filling stations, with petrol prices reportedly falling by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre over the past seven days.

Petrol prices

The reductions follow fresh price cuts by Dangote Refinery, importers and major marketers, amid growing competition between locally refined and imported petrol. Dangote has cut its petrol price several times this month, most recently to ₦1,105 per litre at its depot.

Diesel U-turn

However, Dangote Refinery has withdrawn an earlier announcement that it would reduce the gantry price of diesel from ₦1,720 to ₦1,570 per litre. In a new message to customers, the refinery said the proposed ₦150 cut, which was meant to take effect on Saturday, 21 November, was still under internal review.

Marketers and businesses that rely on diesel are now waiting for clarity on the new rate.

Troops foil suspected kidnap attempt at Kaduna school

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Soldiers reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at a college in Zangon Kataf, Kaduna State. A headcount showed no student was missing.

Read more

Troops have reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at Tafawa Balewa Memorial Commercial College in Samaru, Zangon Kataf Local Government Area of Kaduna State.

What happened

The incident happened at about 11:56pm on 18 November 2026, after reports of suspicious movements by unidentified people around the school. Soldiers responded quickly and moved to the school, forcing the suspects to abandon their plan and flee.

All students safe

The troops searched the school and surrounding areas but did not make contact with the fleeing suspects. A headcount carried out with the school authorities showed that no student or other occupant was missing.

Security stepped up

The troops have continued patrols in the area to prevent further attacks.

Aisha Buhari seen in Tinubu-branded outfit, days after claim she backed Atiku

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari wearing attire printed with President Tinubu's campaign logo has sparked debate. It came days after ADC chieftain Solomon Dalung claimed she had told Atiku that Buhari's supporters would back him.

Read more

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari, widow of the late President Muhammadu Buhari, wearing clothing printed with President Bola Tinubu's campaign logo has stirred political debate ahead of the 2027 elections.

The video

The clip was posted on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by former presidential aide Bashir Ahmad, with a caption describing it as a message from Daura, Buhari's hometown.

Why it matters

The video came days after Solomon Dalung, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), claimed in a television interview that Aisha Buhari had visited ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar and told him that the late President's large voter base would be backing him in 2027.

Many social media users took the new video as a response to that claim, suggesting the former First Lady is supporting Tinubu.

No statement

Aisha Buhari herself had not made a public statement on her political preference at the time of the reports.

KWAM 1 backs Tinubu's fuel subsidy removal at APC campaign event

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Fuji star Wasiu Ayinde, KWAM 1, has defended President Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy, saying it mainly benefited a small group rather than ordinary Nigerians.

Read more

Veteran Fuji musician Wasiu Ayinde, popularly known as KWAM 1, has thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. He questioned who had really benefited from the subsidy, saying a small group of people had gained from it while ordinary Nigerians saw little benefit.

Background

The petrol subsidy was removed when Tinubu took office in 2023, leading to a sharp rise in fuel prices.

The campaign

His comments came during the APC's campaign tour of the South-West, which included a presidential rally in Ibadan.
