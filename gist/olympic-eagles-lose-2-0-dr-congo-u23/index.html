Olympic Eagles lose 2-0 to DR Congo in U-23 AFCON qualifier first leg

14 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Nigeria's Olympic Eagles suffered a 2-0 defeat to the Democratic Republic of Congo in the first leg of their second-round qualifier for the 2027 Under-23 Africa Cup of Nations.

The match

In Lubumbashi on Friday, 13 November 2026, Nigeria started brightly, with midfielder Victor Samuel shooting narrowly wide early on and missing a one-on-one chance soon after. The hosts took the lead inside 20 minutes with a goal that Nigeria felt should have been ruled out for a challenge on goalkeeper Samuel James. Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna was then forced off injured on the half-hour, and DR Congo doubled their lead five minutes before half-time.

Who was in charge

The team was led by Ikorodu City coach Bright Osehai Ozebagbe and former U-20 assistant coach Deniran Ortega, while Eric Chelle was with the Super Eagles, who beat Tanzania 3-1 on the same day.

What happens next

Nigeria will need to overturn the two-goal deficit in the return leg at home to reach the tournament.

Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna, who was making his first start for the U-23 side, has been ruled out of the return leg with the injury he picked up in Lubumbashi.

Juan Mata retires from football at 38

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former Chelsea and Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement at the age of 38. He won the World Cup and Euro 2012 with Spain and the Champions League with Chelsea.

Read more

Former Spain, Chelsea and Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement from professional football at the age of 38.

The announcement

Mata confirmed the decision on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, with a farewell video on X in which he symbolically hung up his boots. He thanked the game, saying that sometimes loving something means letting it go.

A decorated career

Mata was part of the Spain squads that won the 2010 World Cup and Euro 2012. After coming through Real Madrid's youth system and breaking through at Valencia, he joined Chelsea in 2011, where he won the Champions League and FA Cup in 2012 and the Europa League in 2013.

He moved to Manchester United in 2014 and made 285 appearances over eight seasons, winning the FA Cup in 2016 and the League Cup and Europa League in 2017.

Later years

After leaving United in 2022, he won domestic titles with Galatasaray in Turkey and Vissel Kobe in Japan, before finishing his career in Australia with Western Sydney Wanderers and Melbourne Victory, where he also took an ownership stake in the club earlier this year.

Osimhen's agent denies Man City want him to replace Haaland

18 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Victor Osimhen's representative has reportedly denied claims that Manchester City are eyeing the Super Eagles striker as a replacement for Erling Haaland. The report had suggested City would pay €75 million.

Victor Osimhen's agent has reportedly denied claims that Manchester City are lining up the Nigerian striker as a possible replacement for Erling Haaland.

The claim

A Spanish report had claimed City would be prepared to pay €75 million for the Super Eagles forward if Haaland left the club. The figure matches what Galatasaray paid Napoli to sign Osimhen permanently in 2025.

The denial

Turkish media later carried a firm rebuttal from the player's representative, dismissing the reports.

Background

The speculation came as Manchester City face possible punishment from the Premier League over alleged breaches of financial rules, with sanctions reportedly including relegation. Some reports have suggested this could lead to the departure of top players such as Haaland.

Osimhen, who plays for Galatasaray, missed Nigeria's recent AFCON qualifiers through injury.

Flamingos coach Busari nominated for CAF Women's Coach of the Year

18 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Akeem Busari, who led the Flamingos to the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup title, has been nominated for CAF Women's Coach of the Year. The awards take place on 2 December.

Read more

Flamingos head coach Akeem Busari has been nominated for the CAF Women's Coach of the Year award after leading Nigeria to victory at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The shortlist

Busari is one of 10 coaches on the shortlist. Others include Algeria's Farid Benstiti, Cameroon's Valentine Nguele, Ghana's Kim Lars Bjorkegren, Morocco's Jorge Vilda, South Africa's Desiree Ellis and Tanzania's Bakari Nyundo Shime, as well as Silveria Nedio of Cabo Verde, Lovemore Fazili of Malawi and Hicham Drissi of Moroccan club AS FAR.

His achievement

Busari guided the Flamingos to Nigeria's first U-17 Women's World Cup title, making them the first African team to win the competition after beating Japan 2-1 in the final.

When the winners are announced

The 2026 CAF Awards will take place on Wednesday, 2 December, at the Professor Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Arts.

Tiger Woods to make competitive return at Hero World Challenge

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Tiger Woods will play the Hero World Challenge in the Bahamas next month, his first outdoor tournament since the 2024 Open. The 15-time major champion has been held back by injuries.

Read more

Tiger Woods has announced that he will play at next month's Hero World Challenge in the Bahamas, his first competitive outdoor appearance since the 2024 Open Championship.

The tournament

The 50-year-old will be part of a 20-player field for the event, which runs from 3 to 6 December. Scottie Scheffler, Justin Thomas, Xander Schauffele and Rickie Fowler are also due to play.

Long road back

Woods, a 15-time major champion, missed the cut at the 2024 Open while dealing with serious injuries, and further problems kept him away from outdoor competition for more than two years. He returned to competition at an indoor TGL match in March.

Days later, he was involved in a car crash in Florida. He later reached a plea deal, pleading guilty to reckless driving and refusing to take a test, and lost his driving licence for five years.

What happens next

It is not yet clear whether Woods plans to play a wider schedule of tournaments after the event.
