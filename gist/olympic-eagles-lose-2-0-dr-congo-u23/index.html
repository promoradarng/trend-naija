Olympic Eagles lose 2-0 to DR Congo in U-23 AFCON qualifier first leg

14 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Nigeria's Olympic Eagles suffered a 2-0 defeat to the Democratic Republic of Congo in the first leg of their second-round qualifier for the 2027 Under-23 Africa Cup of Nations.

The match

In Lubumbashi on Friday, 13 November 2026, Nigeria started brightly, with midfielder Victor Samuel shooting narrowly wide early on and missing a one-on-one chance soon after. The hosts took the lead inside 20 minutes with a goal that Nigeria felt should have been ruled out for a challenge on goalkeeper Samuel James. Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna was then forced off injured on the half-hour, and DR Congo doubled their lead five minutes before half-time.

Who was in charge

The team was led by Ikorodu City coach Bright Osehai Ozebagbe and former U-20 assistant coach Deniran Ortega, while Eric Chelle was with the Super Eagles, who beat Tanzania 3-1 on the same day.

What happens next

Nigeria will need to overturn the two-goal deficit in the return leg at home to reach the tournament.

Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna, who was making his first start for the U-23 side, has been ruled out of the return leg with the injury he picked up in Lubumbashi.

Raheem Sterling given suspended jail term for dangerous driving

25 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has been handed a 12-month prison sentence, suspended for 18 months, after pleading guilty to dangerous driving. The court heard he was inhaling from a balloon while driving his Lamborghini.

Read more

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has avoided an immediate prison term after a court handed him a 12-month sentence, suspended for 18 months, for dangerous driving.

The sentence

The sentence was handed down on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, at Basingstoke Magistrates' Court. The judge said the 31-year-old had shown a conscious disregard for the safety of other road users.

What happened

The court heard that Sterling drove his Lamborghini on the M25 and M3 motorways during the morning rush hour on 28 May. Witnesses told police they saw him weaving between lanes while inhaling from a balloon. Officers who attended his later crash found him in the passenger seat with two canisters of nitrous oxide.

Background

Sterling, who most recently played for Dutch club Feyenoord, pleaded guilty to dangerous driving in September.

Report records 124 cases of electoral violence and 22 deaths in four months

26 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

A civic group says it has documented 124 incidents of election-related violence and 22 deaths across Nigeria since July. It warned the trend could worsen as the January 2027 elections approach.

Read more

A non-partisan civic organisation has warned of rising political violence in Nigeria, saying it recorded 124 cases of electoral violence and 22 deaths in the past four months.

The figures

In a statement in Abuja on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the Kimpact Development Initiative (KDI) said the incidents were documented between 1 July and 23 November 2026 through its election violence monitoring project. Incidents rose from three in July to 48 in October, with another 40 recorded in the first half of November alone.

The warning

The group warned that the trend could worsen as the 16 January 2027 elections draw closer unless political parties, security agencies and other stakeholders take urgent action. It pointed to growing political intolerance and inflammatory rhetoric among politicians and their supporters as key triggers.

KDI stressed that the problem cuts across parties and should not be blamed on any single group, and urged candidates to restrain their supporters.

Background

The report comes days after deadly attacks on NDC campaign events in Delta and Edo states, and after INEC warned that the popular 'rig and die' slogan could endanger polling staff and voters. The House of Representatives has also warned that a state of emergency may be needed in Delta if violence continues.

Makinde says he is open to working with Atiku and Obi to unseat Tinubu

26 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

Oyo Governor and APM presidential candidate Seyi Makinde has reportedly said he remains open to an arrangement with Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi to stop President Tinubu winning a second term. He ruled out a formal merger of parties.

Read more

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde, the presidential candidate of the Allied Peoples Movement (APM), has reportedly spoken about a possible political arrangement involving himself, the ADC's Atiku Abubakar and the NDC's Peter Obi, aimed at stopping President Bola Tinubu from winning a second term in 2027.

No merger

Makinde is said to have made clear that what he has in mind would not be a formal coalition or merger of political parties, but he said he remains open to working with the two opposition candidates.

Background

Earlier this week, Makinde said he believed Tinubu had only a 30% chance of re-election, citing economic hardship and insecurity.

The other side

No response from the APC, Atiku or Obi to Makinde's comments was included in the reports.
