Pochettino's Man United reign starts with 4-0 thrashing at Liverpool

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United manager ended in a 4-0 defeat at Anfield after Lenny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute. United are 19th in the Premier League.

Read more

Mauricio Pochettino's time in charge of Manchester United got off to a painful start on Sunday, 22 November 2026, as Liverpool beat his side 4-0 at Anfield.

How the game went

United's plans fell apart early when defender Lenny Yoro was shown a red card in the eighth minute, leaving them with ten men for more than 80 minutes. Liverpool quickly took advantage. Dominik Szoboszlai opened the scoring in the 10th minute, and Florian Wirtz doubled the lead three minutes later.

Liverpool kept their two-goal lead until half-time and added two more after the break through Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.

Where it leaves the clubs

The result left United in 19th place, inside the Premier League relegation zone, while Liverpool moved into the top four.

Pochettino's reaction

Speaking after the match, Pochettino reportedly admitted that the early red card shaped the game, but urged his players to turn their frustration into better performances in the coming matches.

Background

Pochettino took over at Old Trafford only recently. The club has been linked with several players ahead of the January window, including Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey.

Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey has reportedly confirmed that he is in talks with Fulham over a new long-term contract.

What he said

Speaking after Fulham's 1-1 draw with Manchester City at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday, the 26-year-old said negotiations were ongoing and that he would like a deal done quickly. He described Fulham as an amazing place to be and said the manager had shown great faith in him.

Transfer interest

His comments come amid reports linking him with a January move to Manchester United under new head coach Mauricio Pochettino, as well as interest from Italian side Inter Milan.

His contract

Bassey's current deal runs until next summer, although Fulham hold an option to extend it by a year. He captains the club under manager Álvaro Arbeloa.

The result

Saturday's draw ended Fulham's run of 17 straight defeats against Manchester City.

Argentina World Cup winner Leandro Paredes collapses during live TV interview

22 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Boca Juniors midfielder Leandro Paredes reportedly collapsed while giving a live interview minutes after his club won the Copa Sudamericana. The final was played in intense heat and humidity in Colombia.

Read more

Argentine midfielder Leandro Paredes, who won the 2022 FIFA World Cup with Argentina, reportedly collapsed on live television on Saturday, 21 November 2026, shortly after Boca Juniors won the Copa Sudamericana.

What happened

The 32-year-old was giving a post-match interview when he lost consciousness and fell. The journalist beside him reportedly moved quickly to stop his fall before medical staff rushed to help him.

The heat

The final, at the Estadio Metropolitano in Barranquilla, Colombia, was played in punishing conditions, with temperatures of around 32°C and humidity of about 80 per cent forecast for the day. Paredes is said to have shown signs of distress late in the first half, and his coach later confirmed it. He was substituted in the 57th minute.

The match

Boca were already 2-0 up by then, and goals from Santiago Ascacibar and Miguel Merentiel gave them victory over Brazilian side Atletico Mineiro. The win ended Boca's 18-year wait for international silverware.

Tom Brady buys minority stake in Chelsea

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

NFL legend Tom Brady has reportedly bought a minority stake in Chelsea Football Club. The size of the stake and price were not disclosed.

Read more

American football great Tom Brady has reportedly bought a minority stake in Premier League club Chelsea.

The deal

The size of the seven-time Super Bowl winner's stake and the amount he paid have not been disclosed. Chelsea is valued at about $4.2 billion by one leading business publication.

Leaving Birmingham

The move comes weeks after Brady sold his 3.3% stake in Birmingham City, who play in England's second tier. The reports cited English Football League rules on holding stakes in more than one club as the reason.

Growing sports portfolio

Since retiring from the NFL, Brady has built up a range of sports investments. He holds a 5% stake in the Las Vegas Raiders and part-owns the Las Vegas Aces basketball team, an E1 boat-racing team and a pickleball team.

Chelsea's season

The news came on the same day Chelsea suffered a heavy 4-1 home defeat to Leeds United.
