Video of Catholic priest leading campaign song for Obi and Kwankwaso goes viral

3 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A video of a Catholic priest leading worshippers in a song backing NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso has spread widely on social media.

What the video shows

In the clip, reportedly filmed during a Sunday service, the priest leads the congregation in an Igbo song saying the party of Obi and Kwankwaso is better, and the worshippers sing along. The priest's name and the location of the church have not been confirmed. The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), which ratified Obi as its presidential candidate in May, shared the video.

Religious leaders and politics

The video comes as religious leaders across Nigeria increasingly declare support for or against candidates ahead of the 2027 elections. According to reports, a separate bishops' conference has endorsed President Bola Tinubu for a second term, while an Islamic cleric was reported to have urged Muslims to vote along religious lines. Last week, a Catholic bishop warned politicians against trying to manipulate the electoral system, and a priest urged Nigerians to collect their voter cards instead of complaining.

How people reacted

Obi's supporters celebrated the video, while critics said churches should not be used for partisan campaigns and warned that such displays could deepen religious and ethnic divisions. Others said priests, like all citizens, are entitled to their political views.

Background

Electoral rules prohibit the use of places of worship for political campaigns, although enforcement has historically been rare. The Catholic Church has not publicly commented on the video.

  • A separate bishops' conference has endorsed Tinubu.

Former All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has denied reports that he endorsed Peter Obi ahead of Nigeria's 2027 presidential election.

"The claim is false"

In a public statement dated 18 November 2026, Garba described the claim circulating online as false.

He said he has withdrawn from partisan politics and is now focusing on his business ventures and on Africa's digital economy.

His new focus

Garba named Crowwe, Tablars and Justitia SI among the projects currently receiving his attention.

He also urged the public to rely only on his official channels for accurate information about his activities.

Where did the story come from?

The origin of the endorsement claim remains unclear. No post, interview or public statement showing Garba endorsing Obi has been found.

What is clear is that Garba has publicly disputed the claim.

A familiar pattern

With the 2027 elections approaching, false claims about endorsements are spreading quickly on social media. It is the second time this week that a story about backing for Obi has been corrected, after Obi himself clarified that his Washington visit was not an endorsement from the US Congress.

Suspected insurgents bomb power lines linking Kainji Dam to Jebba

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Suspected Boko Haram insurgents reportedly blew up two electricity transmission towers connecting the Kainji Dam power plant to Jebba in Niger State. No lives were lost, but the attack raised fears over the safety of power infrastructure.

Read more

Suspected Boko Haram insurgents have reportedly bombed two electricity transmission towers connecting the Kainji Dam power station to Jebba in Niger State.

What happened

The explosions reportedly happened at about 2pm on 17 November 2026, between Kanti community in Mashegu Local Government Area and Kanya community in Mokwa Local Government Area. The attackers are said to have targeted the transmission infrastructure, raising concerns that the Kainji Dam power plant itself could be at risk.

No lives were lost, according to the reports, but the blasts caused panic in nearby communities. Security monitoring has continued in the area.

Why it matters

Kainji and Jebba are hydropower stations that feed the national grid.

Background

The incident follows a series of attacks on and vandalism of electricity infrastructure across the country. This month, the House of Representatives resolved to investigate the destruction of 10 transmission towers within five months.

Nigeria begins building $70m passport factory, local production from May 2027

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

The federal government has started building a $70 million passport production plant in Abuja. Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo says it will start producing passport booklets locally from May 2027.

Read more

Nigeria has started building a $70 million passport production plant in Abuja, as the federal government moves to end its reliance on foreign suppliers for passport booklets.

The announcement

Minister of Interior Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo announced the project at a groundbreaking ceremony at the headquarters of the Nigeria Immigration Service. He described it as a major step towards a self-sufficient passport system and said local production should begin by May 2027.

The plant

The facility is being built in partnership with IRIS Smart Technologies and will initially produce six million electronic passport booklets a year, with capacity that could later rise to 10 million.

Why it matters

The plant is meant to end Nigeria's reliance on foreign suppliers for passport booklets.

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore says he received a spontaneous welcome at Onitsha's Ogbo Ogwu market, a day after Peter Obi campaigned in the city. He also criticised the partition on the Niger Bridge.

African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has made a surprise visit to the Ogbo Ogwu medicine market in Onitsha, Anambra State, where he says traders gave him a warm welcome.

The visit

Sowore shared details of the visit on X on Saturday, 21 November 2026. He said he walked across the First Niger Bridge and arrived at the market without any prior announcement.

He described the reception as spontaneous and genuine, insisting that no crowds had been hired or organised for his arrival, and promised to return.

Bridge complaint

During the visit, Sowore criticised Works Minister David Umahi over a partition installed on the Niger Bridge, claiming it was disrupting traffic and causing hardship for commuters.

Context

His visit came a day after NDC candidate Peter Obi held a campaign event in Onitsha.
