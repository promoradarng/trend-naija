"VeryDarkMan, you have gone too far. Respect Davido's marriage and leave Chioma out of this": Content creator Roskey blasts VDM

4 Nov 2026, 22:57 GMT

Content creator Roskey has called out social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) over his decision to resurface a video involving Nigerian music superstar Davido, popularly known as OBO.

According to Roskey, some things should be left alone, especially when a married man and his family could be dragged into unnecessary controversy.

"You went too far"

Roskey questioned why VDM would bring back a video of Davido enjoying Zlatan's song, knowing fully well that the singer is now married to Chioma.

What appears to have upset him even more is that VDM attended Davido's wedding. In Roskey's view, that alone should have been reason enough for VDM to show some restraint.

"VeryDarkMan, you went too far by posting that video of Davido. You know OBO is married now, and his wife, Chioma, won't be happy seeing that video circulating again," Roskey said.

He argued that marriage comes with responsibilities, and that people who have access to celebrities should know when to draw the line.

"He barely danced"

Roskey also questioned what Davido actually did in the video to deserve so much attention. According to him, Davido barely danced in the footage. He simply raised his hand while Zlatan's song was playing.

So why make such a big issue out of something that looked so harmless?

The Carter Efe question

Roskey went further and questioned VDM's priorities, bringing up his clash with fellow entertainer Carter Efe, who recently made insulting remarks about VDM's mother in a video.

"Why haven't you gone after Carter Efe, who insulted your mother? Why are you targeting Davido? Or is there something you're not telling us?" he asked.

That last question has added another layer to the controversy. Roskey appears to believe there may be more behind VDM's decision to revisit the Davido video, although he presented no evidence of any hidden motive.

What about Davido's marriage?

Roskey's bigger concern is the possible effect of these online fights on Davido's marriage. Celebrities are constantly under public scrutiny, but he believes there should be some consideration for their spouses and families, particularly when old videos are brought back into circulation.

Whether the video poses any real threat to Davido's marriage remains a matter of speculation, and there is no sign that Chioma has publicly expressed displeasure over the footage.

For now, Roskey's message to VDM is clear: whatever point he is trying to make, Davido's marriage should not become collateral damage.

VeryDarkMan questions INEC chairman's photo with APC UK chapter chairman

14 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has questioned why INEC chairman Joash Amupitan was photographed with the APC's UK chapter chairman during his London trip, asking whether other parties' UK leaders were given similar access.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has raised questions about a photograph showing the chairman of the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) with a leader of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) in the UK.

What he said

In a post reported on Saturday, 14 November 2026, VDM clarified that the man wearing an APC-branded cap in the photo was not INEC chairman Professor Joash Amupitan, but Tunde Doherty, chairman of the APC's UK chapter. He asked what the APC official was doing with the INEC chairman and whether the UK leaders of other parties, including the NDC, Labour Party, PDP and ADC, had been given the same opportunity to meet him. He went on to claim that INEC was compromised.

Background

Amupitan was in London this week to speak at Chatham House about preparations for the 2027 elections. Protesters confronted him outside the venue, accusing the commission of bias, and he pledged to reject any political appointment after leaving office to show his independence. INEC has also denied reports that he plans to resign. INEC had not responded to VDM's post at the time of writing, and the circumstances of the photo are not clear.

APC UK chairman responds

Tunde Doherty, chairman of the APC in the UK, later rejected claims that he was part of the INEC chairman's entourage at Chatham House. He said opposition figures had deliberately misrepresented a photo of him taken outside the venue, that he had never held any position or role in INEC and had no affiliation with it, and that as a party leader he had a right to attend public policy discussions there.

NNPC cuts petrol prices in Lagos, Abuja, Kaduna and Sokoto

15 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NNPC has reportedly cut pump prices at its outlets in Lagos, Abuja, Kaduna and Sokoto by between ₦52 and ₦66 per litre, with Lagos now at ₦1,289, the lowest of the four.

Read more

The Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPC) has reduced petrol pump prices at its retail stations in four cities, according to its latest price sheet reported on Sunday, 15 November 2026.

The new prices

In Lagos, the price fell by ₦61, from ₦1,350 to ₦1,289 per litre, the lowest of the four locations. In Abuja it dropped by ₦55, from ₦1,380 to ₦1,325. Kaduna motorists will now pay ₦1,329, down ₦52 from ₦1,381, while Sokoto saw the biggest cut, ₦66, with the price falling from ₦1,378 to ₦1,312.

Why prices are falling

The cuts follow a series of reductions in wholesale depot prices and Dangote Refinery's switch to pricing linked to international petrol prices, which reportedly brought its loading price down to ₦1,250 per litre. MRS stations and depot owners have also lowered their prices in recent days.

Background

A recent comparison listed Nigeria among the countries with the cheapest petrol in West Africa.

Bandits kill Zamfara governor's aide during dawn attack in Kaura Namoda

15 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Bandits have killed Nasiru Sani Kaura, a senior special assistant to Zamfara governor Dauda Lawal, during an attack on worshippers gathered for early morning prayers in Kaura Namoda. Several others were reportedly abducted.

Read more

Suspected bandits have killed a senior aide to Zamfara State governor Dauda Lawal during an early morning attack in Kaura Namoda.

What happened

According to reports, the attack happened on Sunday, 15 November 2026, in the Low Cost area of Kaura Namoda town, as residents gathered for the dawn prayer. The gunmen killed Nasiru Sani Kaura, the governor's senior special assistant, and reportedly abducted several worshippers. Residents said the attackers fired indiscriminately and drove people from their homes.

Who he was

Kaura was described as a grassroots politician and community leader, and his death has left the town in mourning. He was buried according to Islamic rites.

Background

Zamfara is one of the states worst hit by banditry and kidnapping in the North-West. The attack came days after the DSS intercepted a large consignment of weapons allegedly being smuggled from Niger Republic to bandits in neighbouring Sokoto State. The police had not issued a statement at the time of writing, and it is not clear how many people were abducted.
