"VeryDarkMan, you have gone too far. Respect Davido's marriage and leave Chioma out of this": Content creator Roskey blasts VDM

4 Nov 2026, 22:57 GMT

Content creator Roskey has called out social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) over his decision to resurface a video involving Nigerian music superstar Davido, popularly known as OBO.

According to Roskey, some things should be left alone, especially when a married man and his family could be dragged into unnecessary controversy.

"You went too far"

Roskey questioned why VDM would bring back a video of Davido enjoying Zlatan's song, knowing fully well that the singer is now married to Chioma.

What appears to have upset him even more is that VDM attended Davido's wedding. In Roskey's view, that alone should have been reason enough for VDM to show some restraint.

"VeryDarkMan, you went too far by posting that video of Davido. You know OBO is married now, and his wife, Chioma, won't be happy seeing that video circulating again," Roskey said.

He argued that marriage comes with responsibilities, and that people who have access to celebrities should know when to draw the line.

"He barely danced"

Roskey also questioned what Davido actually did in the video to deserve so much attention. According to him, Davido barely danced in the footage. He simply raised his hand while Zlatan's song was playing.

So why make such a big issue out of something that looked so harmless?

The Carter Efe question

Roskey went further and questioned VDM's priorities, bringing up his clash with fellow entertainer Carter Efe, who recently made insulting remarks about VDM's mother in a video.

"Why haven't you gone after Carter Efe, who insulted your mother? Why are you targeting Davido? Or is there something you're not telling us?" he asked.

That last question has added another layer to the controversy. Roskey appears to believe there may be more behind VDM's decision to revisit the Davido video, although he presented no evidence of any hidden motive.

What about Davido's marriage?

Roskey's bigger concern is the possible effect of these online fights on Davido's marriage. Celebrities are constantly under public scrutiny, but he believes there should be some consideration for their spouses and families, particularly when old videos are brought back into circulation.

Whether the video poses any real threat to Davido's marriage remains a matter of speculation, and there is no sign that Chioma has publicly expressed displeasure over the footage.

For now, Roskey's message to VDM is clear: whatever point he is trying to make, Davido's marriage should not become collateral damage.

"You're becoming very noisy, 'money na water'!": Umahi fires back at Cubana Chief Priest, questions his wealth and contributions to society

8 Nov 2026, 17:03 GMT

Works Minister David Umahi has hit back at Cubana Chief Priest, weeks after the celebrity businessman called him the second-worst minister from the South-East. Umahi mocked his famous "money na water" catchphrase and asked how many people he has given scholarships.

Read more

"You're becoming very noisy. Money na water. How much do you have? Have you ever seen Dangote saying, 'I have money,' or Otedola saying that he's rich? Money speaks for itself. It's not by saying money na water. What have you done? How many people have you given scholarships?"

Those were the strong words of Nigeria's Minister of Works, David Umahi, as he fired back at Cubana Chief Priest, weeks after the celebrity businessman described him as the second-worst minister from the South-East.

"Money speaks for itself"

The criticism clearly did not go down well with the former Ebonyi State governor, who has now turned the conversation towards wealth, public conduct and what rich people are doing to improve the lives of others.

Umahi questioned the constant public display of wealth, particularly the expression "money na water" (meaning money flows like water), which Cubana Chief Priest is widely known for.

He pointed to two of Nigeria's richest businessmen, Aliko Dangote and Femi Otedola, and asked whether Nigerians regularly hear them announcing how much money they have. For Umahi, genuine wealth should speak through achievements, investments and contributions to society, not constant declarations on social media.

The scholarship question

The line that stands out most is his question: "How many people have you given scholarships?"

That question takes the argument beyond who has more money. It raises a different issue entirely: what impact are wealthy Nigerians making in the lives of ordinary people?

Two different conversations

Cubana Chief Priest has every right to criticise a public officeholder, and Umahi, as a serving minister, is equally entitled to respond. But criticism of a minister's performance is ultimately best answered with evidence of work done, projects completed and results delivered.

That is what makes this exchange interesting. Umahi is questioning Cubana Chief Priest's display of wealth, while the original criticism was about Umahi's performance as Minister of Works. These are two different conversations, and Nigerians will have their opinions on both.

Bandits reportedly kill 12 members of one family in Zamfara village attack

8 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Twelve members of the same family were reportedly killed when bandits set fire to the building where they had taken refuge during an attack on Gidan Taba village in Maradun LGA, Zamfara State. Other residents were said to have been kidnapped.

Read more

Twelve members of one family have reportedly been killed in an attack by bandits on a village in Zamfara State.

What happened

The attack took place late on Saturday, 7 November 2026, in Gidan Taba village, Dosara Birnin Kaya ward, in Maradun Local Government Area, according to residents. A resident said the head of the family had gathered his relatives into a single room to try to stop them from being kidnapped. When the attackers found the family and they refused to come out, the bandits reportedly set the building on fire, and all 12 people inside died. An unknown number of other residents were reportedly abducted during the raid. The victims were buried according to Islamic rites on Sunday morning.

Official response

The police and the Zamfara State Government had not publicly commented on the attack at the time of writing.

Background

Zamfara is one of the states worst affected by banditry in Nigeria's North-West, with armed groups carrying out killings, mass kidnappings and attacks on farming communities. Last week, bandits ambushed a bus carrying police officers on the Sokoto–Gusau road, reportedly killing and abducting some of them. Security forces have carried out repeated operations in the state's forests, but attacks on villages continue.

Amaechi warns court ruling against ADC could trigger unrest if mishandled

8 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

ADC vice-presidential candidate Rotimi Amaechi has warned that the court judgment removing the party's leadership and voiding Atiku Abubakar's nomination could lead to serious unrest, even civil war, if the dispute is not properly managed.

Read more

Rotimi Amaechi, the vice-presidential candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), has warned that the court ruling against the party's leadership could cause serious unrest if it is not handled carefully.

What he said

In comments reported on Sunday, 8 November 2026, Amaechi said the judgment that nullified the party's leadership and the process that produced Atiku Abubakar as presidential candidate could lead to civil war if the dispute is not properly managed. He did not set out in detail how he believed the crisis should be resolved.

The ruling

On Thursday, Justice Peter Lifu of the Federal High Court in Abuja ruled that the national working committee led by former Senate President David Mark was invalid. The judgment also nullified the emergence of former Interior Minister Rauf Aregbesola as national secretary, and decisions taken by the Mark-led leadership, including the process that produced Atiku as candidate. The court directed INEC not to recognise decisions taken at the party's national executive committee meeting of 27 July 2025. The suit was filed by Nafiu Gombe, a former deputy national chairman of the party.

Background

The ADC has appealed the judgment and asked INEC not to act on it until the appeal is heard. Atiku has vowed to stay in the race and accused the government of using the courts against the opposition. Amaechi, a former Rivers governor and transport minister, this week also said former President Muhammadu Buhari had promised in writing to back him in 2022.

What happens next

Attention is now on the Court of Appeal and on how INEC responds before the presidential election on 16 January 2027.

Gift Orban overtakes injured Osimhen in Turkish league scoring race

8 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Super Eagles striker Gift Orban scored his 10th Turkish Süper Lig goal of the season for Amedspor, moving one ahead of the injured Victor Osimhen in the race for the Golden Boot, though Amedspor lost 4-3 to Galatasaray.

Read more

Nigerian striker Gift Orban has moved ahead of Victor Osimhen in the race to be top scorer in Turkey's Süper Lig.

The goal

Orban scored his 10th league goal of the season for Amedspor in their 4-3 defeat away to Galatasaray, reported on Sunday, 8 November 2026. After a quiet first half, the former Lyon striker headed home from close range from a cross by substitute Furkan Soyalp. Another Nigerian, Yira Sor, also scored for Amedspor after coming off the bench, but Galatasaray held on to win.

The scoring race

The goal took Orban to 10 goals and five assists in 11 league appearances, one more goal than Osimhen, who has nine goals and three assists in eight league games. Osimhen missed the match after having knee surgery on Saturday to remove a loose body from his left knee, and is expected to be out for at least three weeks.

Background

Orban joined Amedspor on loan in the summer transfer window. He made his name at Belgian club Gent, where he scored freely, before moving to Lyon. Osimhen, the 2023 African Player of the Year, has been out of action since the Champions League win over Stuttgart. Both are Super Eagles strikers, and with Osimhen ruled out of the AFCON qualifiers against Tanzania this month, Orban's form will interest Nigeria's coaching staff.
