"VeryDarkMan, you have gone too far. Respect Davido's marriage and leave Chioma out of this": Content creator Roskey blasts VDM

4 Nov 2026, 22:57 GMT

Content creator Roskey has called out social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) over his decision to resurface a video involving Nigerian music superstar Davido, popularly known as OBO.

According to Roskey, some things should be left alone, especially when a married man and his family could be dragged into unnecessary controversy.

"You went too far"

Roskey questioned why VDM would bring back a video of Davido enjoying Zlatan's song, knowing fully well that the singer is now married to Chioma.

What appears to have upset him even more is that VDM attended Davido's wedding. In Roskey's view, that alone should have been reason enough for VDM to show some restraint.

"VeryDarkMan, you went too far by posting that video of Davido. You know OBO is married now, and his wife, Chioma, won't be happy seeing that video circulating again," Roskey said.

He argued that marriage comes with responsibilities, and that people who have access to celebrities should know when to draw the line.

"He barely danced"

Roskey also questioned what Davido actually did in the video to deserve so much attention. According to him, Davido barely danced in the footage. He simply raised his hand while Zlatan's song was playing.

So why make such a big issue out of something that looked so harmless?

The Carter Efe question

Roskey went further and questioned VDM's priorities, bringing up his clash with fellow entertainer Carter Efe, who recently made insulting remarks about VDM's mother in a video.

"Why haven't you gone after Carter Efe, who insulted your mother? Why are you targeting Davido? Or is there something you're not telling us?" he asked.

That last question has added another layer to the controversy. Roskey appears to believe there may be more behind VDM's decision to revisit the Davido video, although he presented no evidence of any hidden motive.

What about Davido's marriage?

Roskey's bigger concern is the possible effect of these online fights on Davido's marriage. Celebrities are constantly under public scrutiny, but he believes there should be some consideration for their spouses and families, particularly when old videos are brought back into circulation.

Whether the video poses any real threat to Davido's marriage remains a matter of speculation, and there is no sign that Chioma has publicly expressed displeasure over the footage.

For now, Roskey's message to VDM is clear: whatever point he is trying to make, Davido's marriage should not become collateral damage.

VeryDarkMan, Opeyemi Famakin and Peller defend Enioluwa after youth comments backlash

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have spoken in defence of influencer Enioluwa, who apologised after his comments about young Nigerians drew criticism.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

His mother speaks

Enioluwa's mother, Bola Adeoluwa, a university lecturer, has also addressed the backlash after some students on TikTok turned their criticism on her, alleging she was a harsh grader. She responded publicly to the claims and the criticism of her son.

Okanlawon weighs in

Actor Deyemi Okanlawon also criticised social media users who attacked Enioluwa in harsh terms. Enioluwa had told a conference that many young people expect success to come easily and asked those in their twenties who say they are tired of life whether they had really experienced life.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. Moniepoint opens North Central regional headquarters in Abuja

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened a regional headquarters in Abuja where customers can get in-person help, in line with CBN rules requiring physical locations for national microfinance banks.

Read more

Digital lender Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened its North Central regional headquarters in Abuja, giving customers a place to get face-to-face support.

Why now

The move is in line with a Central Bank of Nigeria requirement for nationally licensed microfinance banks to keep physical locations alongside their digital services.

Wider presence

Moniepoint already runs offices and service centres in several states, including Borno, Bauchi, Kaduna, Kano and Sokoto, and its point-of-sale network serves businesses in all 774 local government areas.
