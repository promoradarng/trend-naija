"Buhari promised me APC presidential ticket in 2022, I regret listening to him": Rotimi Amaechi

8 Nov 2026, 00:05 GMT

Former Rivers State Governor and ex-Minister of Transportation Rotimi Amaechi has reportedly expressed regret over his decision to trust former President Muhammadu Buhari during the race for the All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential ticket in 2022.

The promise

According to the statement attributed to Amaechi, Buhari had promised him the party's presidential ticket, a promise he now regrets believing.

The claim raises fresh questions about the political arrangements and behind-the-scenes discussions that took place before the APC primary that eventually produced Bola Ahmed Tinubu as the party's candidate.

Buhari died in July 2025, so he is no longer able to respond to Amaechi's account.

How the 2022 race played out

Amaechi was among the prominent politicians who contested the ticket in 2022. Despite being a serving minister in Buhari's government and a close associate of the President, he lost the primary to Tinubu.

As the sitting President and leader of the ruling party at the time, Buhari's influence was considerable, although the final decision rested with the party's delegates.

Loyalty without reward

For Amaechi, the disappointment appears to go beyond losing an election. His comments suggest he believed he had received an assurance from someone whose word carried enormous political weight.

That points to one of the oldest lessons in Nigerian politics: loyalty does not always guarantee reward.

Amaechi served under Buhari for nearly seven years as Minister of Transportation, and he was one of the key figures who helped build the coalition that brought the APC to power in 2015. Yet when his chance came to seek the presidency, things did not turn out as he had apparently expected.

The questions that remain

Did Buhari actually make such a commitment, and if he did, what happened between that promise and the primary?

Was Amaechi politically outplayed, or did he place too much confidence in a promise that was never guaranteed?

Whatever the answer, his regret offers another glimpse into the complicated world of Nigerian party politics, where today's allies can have very different interests when power is at stake.

Former Kaduna State Governor Nasir El-Rufai has reportedly told a Federal High Court in Abuja that he never intercepted the telephone conversations of National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu, and never asked anyone to do so for him.

In court

El-Rufai opened his defence on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, before Justice Joyce Abdulmalik, after his no-case submission was dismissed in October. Led in evidence by his lawyer, he told the court that he had never owned equipment capable of intercepting calls and had no technical knowledge of how such interception works.

He also denied having any access to the NSA's communication networks or personal devices.

How the case began

The case arises from remarks El-Rufai made in a television interview on 13 February 2026. He denies the charges.

What happens next

The trial continues as El-Rufai presents his defence.

'Political rhetoric'

El-Rufai told the court that his 'tapped' remark was political rhetoric. The court later adjourned the trial to February to allow his defence to continue.

Court freezes $100m linked to Governors' Forum in Paris Club fees dispute

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

An Abuja High Court has ordered the preservation of up to $100 million allegedly held by the Nigeria Governors' Forum, pending a dispute over legal and consultancy fees tied to Paris Club refunds. The order does not decide who owns the money.

Read more

A High Court of the Federal Capital Territory in Abuja has ordered the preservation of up to $100 million allegedly held by the Nigeria Governors' Forum (NGF), in a dispute over unpaid legal and consultancy fees connected to the Paris Club refunds.

The order

Justice O.C. Agbaza granted the interim freezing order on 19 November 2026, following an application by a lawyer against the NGF's incorporated trustees and 29 commercial banks. The order covers a single total sum of $100 million, about ₦135.6 billion, across the affected accounts.

What it means

The order preserves the disputed money until the court hears an application for an injunction or makes a further order. It does not decide who owns the funds.

The claim

The lawyer is seeking payment under an indemnity he says the NGF issued in December 2018, after the federal government paid $100 million to the forum to hold pending a decision on liability for professional fees. No response from the NGF was included in the reports.

Reps urge Tinubu to consider state of emergency in Delta if violence worsens

25 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The House of Representatives has asked President Tinubu to closely monitor security in Delta State, warning that a state of emergency may become necessary if political violence continues. It also told the police to set up a special investigative team.

Read more

The House of Representatives has called on President Bola Tinubu to closely watch the security situation in Delta State, warning that a state of emergency may be needed if conditions keep getting worse.

The motion

The resolution on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, followed a motion by Representative Benedict Etanabene, who pointed to a series of politically motivated attacks in the state. Lawmakers expressed concern that the violence was spreading fear and threatening peace among communities.

Directive to the police

The House also directed the Inspector-General of Police to identify flashpoints across the state and set up a special team to investigate the attacks.

Background

The resolution comes two days after gunmen attacked an NDC campaign event led by former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege in Uduere, Ughelli North, killing several people. Police have invited Omo-Agege and others for questioning, and the Inspector-General has ordered an investigation. Delta Governor Sheriff Oborevwori has called for everyone involved to be arrested, whatever their party.

President Bola Tinubu has congratulated Atiku Abubakar, the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate and former Vice President, on his 80th birthday.

The message

In a statement released on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by his Special Adviser on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, the president said the milestone deserved recognition. He noted that despite their different political paths, he wanted to acknowledge the history they share and Atiku's contributions to Nigeria.

Tinubu also recalled supporting Atiku's presidential bid in 2007.

Other well-wishers

Businessman Tein Jack-Rich was among others who sent birthday messages to the former vice president, praising his leadership and resilience.

Background

The two men are now rivals in the January 2027 presidential election, and their campaigns have traded sharp words in recent weeks.
