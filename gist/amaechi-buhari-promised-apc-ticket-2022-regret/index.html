"Buhari promised me APC presidential ticket in 2022, I regret listening to him": Rotimi Amaechi

8 Nov 2026, 00:05 GMT

Former Rivers State Governor and ex-Minister of Transportation Rotimi Amaechi has reportedly expressed regret over his decision to trust former President Muhammadu Buhari during the race for the All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential ticket in 2022.

The promise

According to the statement attributed to Amaechi, Buhari had promised him the party's presidential ticket, a promise he now regrets believing.

The claim raises fresh questions about the political arrangements and behind-the-scenes discussions that took place before the APC primary that eventually produced Bola Ahmed Tinubu as the party's candidate.

Buhari died in July 2025, so he is no longer able to respond to Amaechi's account.

How the 2022 race played out

Amaechi was among the prominent politicians who contested the ticket in 2022. Despite being a serving minister in Buhari's government and a close associate of the President, he lost the primary to Tinubu.

As the sitting President and leader of the ruling party at the time, Buhari's influence was considerable, although the final decision rested with the party's delegates.

Loyalty without reward

For Amaechi, the disappointment appears to go beyond losing an election. His comments suggest he believed he had received an assurance from someone whose word carried enormous political weight.

That points to one of the oldest lessons in Nigerian politics: loyalty does not always guarantee reward.

Amaechi served under Buhari for nearly seven years as Minister of Transportation, and he was one of the key figures who helped build the coalition that brought the APC to power in 2015. Yet when his chance came to seek the presidency, things did not turn out as he had apparently expected.

The questions that remain

Did Buhari actually make such a commitment, and if he did, what happened between that promise and the primary?

Was Amaechi politically outplayed, or did he place too much confidence in a promise that was never guaranteed?

Whatever the answer, his regret offers another glimpse into the complicated world of Nigerian party politics, where today's allies can have very different interests when power is at stake.

Seyi Tinubu turbaned Dan Zaki Gwandu; President congratulates son

12 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

President Tinubu has congratulated his son Seyi after the Emir of Gwandu gave him the title Dan Zaki Gwandu in Birnin Kebbi, a title the Emir said was the first of its kind in the emirate's history.

Read more

Seyi Tinubu, son of President Bola Tinubu, has been given a traditional title by the Emir of Gwandu in Kebbi State.

The title

The Emir of Gwandu, Alhaji Muhammadu Iliyasu Bashar, turbaned Seyi as Dan Zaki Gwandu, which translates as 'Son of the Lion of Gwandu', at the palace in Birnin Kebbi on Wednesday, 11 November 2026. The Emir said the conferment was the first of its kind in the history of the emirate.

The President's message

In a statement issued on Thursday by his special adviser on information and strategy, Bayo Onanuga, the President thanked the Emir and the emirate council, saying the honour reflected friendship, mutual respect and unity among Nigeria's peoples. He praised Seyi's work on youth engagement and community development, including through the City Boy Movement, and urged him to deepen his commitment to young people, peaceful coexistence and service to the country.

Background

Seyi Tinubu has become increasingly visible in his father's re-election campaign, and recently received a Middle Belt youth group that pledged support for the President.

Oyo Assembly suspends four council chairmen, a day after three defected to APC

11 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

The Oyo State House of Assembly has suspended four local government chairmen over alleged corruption and mismanagement of council funds. Three of them had defected from the APM to the APC a day earlier.

Read more

The Oyo State House of Assembly has suspended four local government chairmen over alleged corruption and mismanagement of council funds.

What happened

The assembly announced the suspensions on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, citing corruption and financial misconduct, but did not immediately release details of the allegations against each chairman. The allegations have not been tested and the chairmen had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

The defections

The timing has drawn attention because three of the four chairmen reportedly defected from the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) to the All Progressives Congress (APC) the day before. The fourth, the chairman of Iwajowa Local Government Area, Muritala Abiodun Oyewumi, was said to have remained in the APM.

Background

Oyo State has 33 local government areas, and lawmakers have recently called for greater accountability in how council funds are managed.

Armed thugs storm NDC town hall in Lagos; supporters fight back

12 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Suspected thugs armed with cutlasses, cudgels and stones stormed a Nigeria Democratic Congress town hall meeting at Oregun, Lagos, on Thursday morning, scattering attendees before party supporters regrouped and drove them out.

Read more

A town hall meeting of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) in Lagos was disrupted on Thursday morning, 12 November 2026, when suspected thugs stormed the venue.

What happened

The attackers, armed with cutlasses, cudgels and stones, arrived at an event centre in Oregun, Ikeja, where supporters of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso had gathered. The atmosphere had been calm, with supporters singing and dancing before the meeting began. According to a journalist at the scene, the group was heard saying the NDC supporters were few and could be overpowered, and some members were heard asking for clearance to enter with guns.

Supporters fight back

Many people inside the hall fled when the attackers forced their way in, while motorists on Kudirat Abiola Way turned back to avoid the area. NDC supporters then regrouped, reportedly chased the attackers out of the hall and caught one of them in the car park. It is not clear how many people were injured, who sent the attackers, or whether anyone has been arrested. The police had not publicly commented at the time of writing.

Background

Obi won Lagos in the 2023 presidential election as the Labour Party candidate, with more than 580,000 votes, in a state seen as a stronghold of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC). Campaign-related violence has been reported against several parties in recent weeks, and on Wednesday the Inspector-General of Police warned officers not to aid thuggery, while the European Union raised concerns about violence ahead of the 16 January 2027 election.

IK Ogbonna calls out Governor Okpebholo over 'dangerous' Edo roads

12 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Actor IK Ogbonna has criticised Edo State governor Monday Okpebholo over the poor state of roads he saw on a road trip through the state, saying Edo people deserve better.

Read more

Nollywood actor IK Ogbonna has publicly criticised Edo State governor Monday Okpebholo over the condition of roads in the state.

What he said

In posts on his Instagram Stories on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the actor said he had just travelled by road through Edo and was shocked by what he saw. He described the roads as dangerously bad and a real safety risk for travellers, and questioned how a state with such a proud cultural and historical identity could still have crumbling roads. He tagged the governor and said the people of Edo deserve better.

Background

On the same day, comedian Sabinus criticised the Rivers State government over the state of roads in Rivers. Okpebholo, of the All Progressives Congress (APC), became Edo governor in November 2024. His government had not publicly responded to the actor's comments at the time of writing.
