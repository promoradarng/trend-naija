Saidi Balogun hits back at critics over his campaigning for Tinubu

8 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Nollywood actor Saidi Balogun has responded to criticism over his open support for President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid.

What happened

The actor had shared videos on social media showing him hosting campaign rallies for the President in different parts of the country, which drew strong criticism from some social media users. In a response reported on Sunday, 8 November 2026, Balogun refused to back down, urging critics to allow people to hold different political views without being attacked.

Background

Balogun is a veteran actor, producer and director in the Yoruba film industry. He is one of several entertainers who have appeared at Tinubu's campaign events in recent weeks, alongside others such as actress Eniola Badmus, who also posted a video this week explaining why she believes the President deserves a second term.

The wider debate

The involvement of entertainers in the 2027 campaigns has divided opinion online. Some argue that public figures have every right to back the candidates they choose, while others say supporting the government amounts to endorsing economic hardship. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Nollywood veterans Patience Ozokwo and Ebele Okaro have reportedly visited their colleague Joke Silva at home to offer condolences over the death of her husband, the legendary actor Olu Jacobs.

The visit

Ozokwo shared a video of the visit on social media on Sunday, 22 November 2026. It showed her signing the condolence register before Joke Silva walked in to welcome the two actresses. Nollywood actress Anike Sinleke dies after battle with ovarian cancer

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Nollywood actress Adenike Abike Babajide, known as Anike Sinleke, has reportedly died after battling ovarian cancer. Her death was announced on Sunday by an Instagram show host she was due to appear with that day.

Read more

Nollywood is mourning the death of actress Adenike Abike Babajide, popularly known as Anike Sinleke, who has reportedly died after a battle with ovarian cancer.

How the news broke

The news emerged on Sunday, 22 November 2026, in a post by Bukola Fagbuyi, host of the Instagram live show Rubbin Minds With Bukky. Fagbuyi said the actress had been scheduled to appear as a guest on the programme that same day.

Her career

Anike Sinleke learned her craft under the mentorship of veteran actor Remmy Shitta and worked on film sets with filmmaker Deji Adenuga. At one point she stepped away from acting to complete her Higher National Diploma.

Beyond the screen, she was known in Lagos entertainment and cultural circles for her role with the Sisi Eko beauty pageant, which started around 2011.

Tributes

Fans and colleagues have taken to social media to mourn her, with many expressing shock at the news.
