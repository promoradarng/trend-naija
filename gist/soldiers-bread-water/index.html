Video of Nigerian soldiers dipping bread in river water sparks outrage

24 Oct 2026, 20:05 BST

A video circulating online appears to show Nigerian soldiers in the field crouching at a muddy riverbank and dipping bread into the water before eating it. The footage has drawn an emotional response from Nigerians, many of whom say it shows the tough conditions troops face while fighting insecurity. The Nigerian Army has not commented on the video, and it is not yet clear when or where it was recorded.

  • Many users have contrasted the scene with the large sums being spent on campaigns ahead of the 2027 elections.
  • Others are calling for better welfare, rations and supplies for troops on the front line.
  • Trend Naija has not independently confirmed the details of the video.
