Eight Zamfara lawmakers reportedly return to the APC after meeting Tinubu

24 Oct 2026, 20:33 BST

Eight Zamfara Assembly members who quit the APC earlier this month are reported to have rejoined the party. Minister of State for Defence Bello Matawalle is said to have led them to the President, and a planned move to the ADC was reportedly shelved. The lawmakers had complained about unpaid salaries and insecurity.

  • Matawalle is being hailed by party supporters over the return.
  • Vehicles were reportedly presented to the lawmakers in Gusau on 21 October.
  • The ADC has not yet responded to the reported reversal.
