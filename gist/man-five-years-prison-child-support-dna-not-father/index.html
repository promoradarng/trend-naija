Man reportedly spends five years in prison over child support, only to discover through DNA test that he is not the father

5 Nov 2026, 11:01 GMT

Imagine spending five years behind bars for failing to pay child support, only to come out and discover that the child you were punished for not supporting is not even yours.

That is the heartbreaking story being told online about a man identified as Mr Mensar.

Five years behind bars

According to the account circulating online, Mr Mensar was imprisoned for failing to meet his child support obligations. For five years, he reportedly lived with the consequences of a responsibility he believed was his.

The mother's admission

What makes the story even more disturbing is that the child's mother knew all along that Mr Mensar was not the biological father of her son.

She allowed him to face imprisonment without revealing the truth, and the account claims she remained in contact with the child's real biological father throughout the ordeal. When questioned by the judge, she accepted responsibility.

The DNA test

After serving his sentence, Mr Mensar reportedly demanded a DNA test to establish whether the child was biologically his. According to the account, the result confirmed his worst fear: he was not the father.

Five years gone. Five years he can never get back, all over a paternity question that could have been settled much earlier.

Questions that need answers

Serious questions must now be asked about the mother's conduct and about how the child support case was handled.

How does a man end up spending five years in prison over a child who is not biologically his? What happens to the years he lost? And if someone deliberately concealed the child's paternity, should there be legal consequences?

The full court records and DNA findings have not been made public.

Still, the story raises an important issue about paternity disputes, child support enforcement and fairness in the justice system.

Over to you

If a woman knowingly allows a man to go to prison over child support while hiding the fact that he is not the father, should she face criminal charges and be made to compensate him for the years he lost?

Many feared dead as boat carrying over 300 passengers capsizes in Bayelsa

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A boat reportedly carrying more than 300 passengers to a burial ceremony has capsized in Southern Ijaw, Bayelsa State. Police say marine units have been deployed for rescue, but casualty figures are not yet known.

Read more

A boat said to be carrying more than 300 passengers has capsized in Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, with many people feared dead or missing.

What happened

The boat was travelling from Ayama community to Ukubie for a burial ceremony when it capsized at about noon on Friday, 20 November 2026, shortly after passing Oweikorogha community.

The exact number of passengers on board and the number of casualties had not been established at the time of the reports, but residents fear several people may have lost their lives.

Rescue efforts

The Bayelsa State Police Command spokesperson, DSP Musa Muhammad, confirmed the incident and said the Commissioner of Police had deployed the Marine Police Unit and the local divisional police officer for rescue operations. He said further details on casualties and missing persons would be released.

A former state chairman of the Maritime Workers Union also confirmed the incident.

What happens next

Rescue and recovery efforts are continuing, and official casualty figures are expected once they are confirmed.

Former Super Eagles captain and football administrator Segun Odegbami has survived an attack by three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta, Ogun State, in the early hours of Friday, 20 November 2026.

What happened

The attackers reportedly overpowered his domestic staff and driver before forcing their way into his bedroom at about 2:30am. The 74-year-old was injured in the attack.

Odegbami described the incident as an attempt on his life. He said he fought back with help from his driver and house help, and the attackers eventually fled. He said it was the closest he had ever come to death.

Police response

The Ogun State Police Command confirmed the attack and said the former international had been treated in hospital and discharged.

Who he is
