Man reportedly spends five years in prison over child support, only to discover through DNA test that he is not the father

5 Nov 2026, 11:01 GMT

Imagine spending five years behind bars for failing to pay child support, only to come out and discover that the child you were punished for not supporting is not even yours.

That is the heartbreaking story being told online about a man identified as Mr Mensar.

Five years behind bars

According to the account circulating online, Mr Mensar was imprisoned for failing to meet his child support obligations. For five years, he reportedly lived with the consequences of a responsibility he believed was his.

The mother's admission

What makes the story even more disturbing is that the child's mother knew all along that Mr Mensar was not the biological father of her son.

She allowed him to face imprisonment without revealing the truth, and the account claims she remained in contact with the child's real biological father throughout the ordeal. When questioned by the judge, she accepted responsibility.

The DNA test

After serving his sentence, Mr Mensar reportedly demanded a DNA test to establish whether the child was biologically his. According to the account, the result confirmed his worst fear: he was not the father.

Five years gone. Five years he can never get back, all over a paternity question that could have been settled much earlier.

Questions that need answers

Serious questions must now be asked about the mother's conduct and about how the child support case was handled.

How does a man end up spending five years in prison over a child who is not biologically his? What happens to the years he lost? And if someone deliberately concealed the child's paternity, should there be legal consequences?

The full court records and DNA findings have not been made public.

Still, the story raises an important issue about paternity disputes, child support enforcement and fairness in the justice system.

Over to you

If a woman knowingly allows a man to go to prison over child support while hiding the fact that he is not the father, should she face criminal charges and be made to compensate him for the years he lost?

APC-branded ₦200 recharge cards reportedly handed out at Tinubu's Rivers rally spark debate

12 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Photos of ₦200 recharge cards bearing APC branding, reportedly distributed at President Tinubu's campaign rally in Rivers State, have drawn mockery and debate online about campaign giveaways.

Read more

Images of recharge cards bearing the logo of the All Progressives Congress (APC), reportedly handed out at President Bola Tinubu's campaign rally in Rivers State, have sparked debate on social media.

What happened

A man who came across pictures of the ₦200 airtime cards questioned online what anyone could achieve with such a small amount, and his comment, shared on Thursday, 12 November 2026, quickly drew reactions. The cards were said to have circulated among people who attended the rally.

How people reacted

Some users mocked the giveaway and said the money would be better spent on fixing the country, while others joked about how the airtime might be used. Some warned that small gifts like this could still influence some voters.

Background

Campaign giveaways have become a talking point ahead of the 2027 elections. The African Democratic Congress (ADC) recently accused the APC of handing out cash and gifts at campaign events and called on the anti-graft agency and INEC to investigate. The APC had not publicly responded to the recharge card images at the time of writing. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Four UNN students die after off-campus party in Nsukka, six in hospital

12 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The University of Nigeria, Nsukka has confirmed that four students died after a party held to celebrate a friend's final exams, after allegedly consuming a mix of alcohol and drugs. Six others are in hospital, and the university has declared three days of mourning.

Read more

The University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN) has confirmed that four of its students have died after an off-campus party in Nsukka, Enugu State.

What happened

Speaking to journalists on Thursday, 12 November 2026, Vice-Chancellor Professor Simon Ortuanya said 10 students were involved in the incident at the party on Wednesday, 11 November, which had been organised to celebrate a friend finishing his final-year examinations. He said the four who died allegedly consumed substances believed to be a mixture of alcohol and drugs. The other six are receiving treatment in different hospitals. Earlier reports had put the death toll as high as seven.

University responds

The Vice-Chancellor described the deaths as one too many and declared three days of mourning in honour of the students. He also corrected reports that all the students were from the Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences, saying they came from different departments. The exact substances involved have not been officially identified, and it is not clear whether the police have opened an investigation.

Background

Drug and alcohol misuse among young people has become a growing public health concern in Nigeria.

Makinde gives four reasons he is challenging Tinubu, whom he backed in 2023

12 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Oyo governor Seyi Makinde, the APM presidential candidate, says he is running against President Tinubu because economic gains have not reached ordinary Nigerians, the government keeps blaming inherited problems, alliances serve politics rather than the nation, and reforms lacked cushions.

Read more

Oyo State governor Seyi Makinde has explained why he is running against President Bola Tinubu in the 2027 presidential election, three years after supporting him.

His reasons

In a newsletter published on Thursday, 12 November 2026, Makinde, the presidential candidate of the Allied Peoples Movement (APM), said his decision was not about personal rivalry or a desire to live in Aso Rock. He said he backed Tinubu in 2023 because he believed the President's experience and relationships could unite the country. He listed four concerns: that improved economic figures have not translated into better lives for ordinary Nigerians; that the government keeps blaming problems it inherited; that political alliances appear to serve political rather than national interests, while opposition parties have been weakened; and that fuel subsidy removal and currency reforms came without enough protection for citizens.

What he would have done

Makinde said he would have used the transition period to coordinate with governors through the National Economic Council and prepare support measures before removing the subsidy. He pointed to Oyo's response, saying his government deployed extra buses, froze fares and introduced half-price travel for students and the elderly within 11 days, and later delivered food relief to 200,000 households. He also noted that Tinubu had opposed an abrupt subsidy removal under President Goodluck Jonathan in 2012.

Background

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027. The President has defended his reforms, saying ending the subsidy freed money for infrastructure and other investments. His campaign had not responded to the newsletter at the time of writing.

Afrobeats star Asake has announced a major end-of-year concert in Lagos.

The show

In a short video on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the singer, whose real name is Ahmed Ololade, announced 'M$NEY in Lagos', which will take place on 20 December at Oasis Ground on Coastal Road, Victoria Island. Ticket prices reportedly start at ₦100,000 and go up to ₦20 million for the most expensive package, making it one of the priciest concerts of the festive season.

Background

Asake rose to fame in 2020 with his hit 'Lady' and joined Olamide's YBNL label in 2022. He has released four studio albums: 'Mr Money With The Vibes' (2022), 'Work of Art' (2023), 'Lungu Boy' (2024) and 'M$NEY' (2026). In February 2025 he launched his own label, Giran Republik. He has earned two Grammy nominations, for 'Amapiano' with Olamide and 'MMS' with Wizkid. The Lagos show comes during a busy December, with Olamide also announcing a YBNL reunion concert in the city on 18 December.
