Fulham reportedly want £50m for Calvin Bassey as Manchester United show interest

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Fulham are reportedly ready to demand around £50 million for Nigerian international defender Calvin Bassey in the January transfer window, as Manchester United weigh a move.

The interest

Manchester United are said to be genuinely interested in the 26-year-old, who plays mainly at centre-back but can also play at left-back, as they look to strengthen their defence for the second half of the season.

United reportedly believe a bid above £40 million would be needed to start talks, but Fulham are said to be holding out for closer to £50 million. That would beat the club's record sale, Aleksandar Mitrović's move to Al-Hilal in 2023.

Contract situation

Bassey's contract runs until summer 2027, with Fulham holding an option to extend it by a year. Talks on a new long-term deal have reportedly moved slowly, which could leave the club open to a big offer if nothing is agreed before January.

Background

Bassey, a former Ajax and Rangers defender, is a regular for the Super Eagles. He is reportedly open to a move to Old Trafford.

Raheem Sterling given suspended jail term for dangerous driving

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has been handed a 12-month prison sentence, suspended for 18 months, after pleading guilty to dangerous driving. The court heard he was inhaling from a balloon while driving his Lamborghini.

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has avoided an immediate prison term after a court handed him a 12-month sentence, suspended for 18 months, for dangerous driving.

The sentence

The sentence was handed down on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, at Basingstoke Magistrates' Court. The judge said the 31-year-old had shown a conscious disregard for the safety of other road users.

What happened

The court heard that Sterling drove his Lamborghini on the M25 and M3 motorways during the morning rush hour on 28 May. Witnesses told police they saw him weaving between lanes while inhaling from a balloon. Officers who attended his later crash found him in the passenger seat with two canisters of nitrous oxide.

Background

Sterling, who most recently played for Dutch club Feyenoord, pleaded guilty to dangerous driving in September.

CAS restores Senegal as AFCON 2025 champions, overturning CAF ruling

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Court of Arbitration for Sport has confirmed Senegal as winners of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations. It overturned in full the CAF Appeal Board decision that had handed the title to Morocco.

Read more

Senegal are once again Africa's champions after the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) overturned the decision that had stripped them of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations title.

The verdict

CAS delivered its ruling on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, reversing in full the decision of CAF's Appeal Board, which had awarded the title to hosts Morocco. With the verdict, Senegal are officially recognised as the 2025 AFCON champions.

How it started

Senegal beat Morocco 1-0 after extra time in the final at the Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium in Rabat on 18 January 2026, with Pape Gueye scoring the only goal. During the match, Senegal's players walked off the pitch for about 15 minutes in protest at a late penalty awarded to Morocco, which was later saved.

The Royal Moroccan Football Federation then took legal action, and on 17 March CAF's Appeal Board ruled that Senegal had forfeited the match, awarding Morocco a 3-0 win and the trophy. The Senegalese Football Federation refused to accept the decision and appealed to CAS.

What it means

The ruling ends months of legal uncertainty over the continent's top football title. Before the verdict, CAF President Patrice Motsepe had said the body would accept whatever CAS decided.

Morocco to fight on

The Royal Moroccan Football Federation said it rejected the ruling and would challenge it through every legal channel available, citing what it described as procedural failures. Senegal star Sadio Mane welcomed the decision.

CAF responds

In a short statement, CAF acknowledged the ruling and pledged to act on it. The trophy, medals and prize money were never taken back from Senegal during the dispute and will remain with the team.

NDC says supporters were attacked in Benin as Obi arrived for campaign

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Nigeria Democratic Congress has alleged that gunmen attacked its supporters and campaign vehicles on Airport Road, Benin City, as Peter Obi arrived in Edo State on Wednesday. It said one supporter was critically wounded.

Read more

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has alleged that its supporters were attacked in Benin City, Edo State, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, as its presidential candidate Peter Obi arrived for a town hall meeting and other campaign events.

What the party says

According to the party, attackers who arrived in a pickup van opened fire on its campaign vehicles parked along Airport Road while supporters waited for Obi. A party spokesperson posted videos online showing damaged vehicles and bloodstains, and said one supporter was critically wounded and receiving hospital treatment.

In a statement, the NDC's National Publicity Secretary said the attackers also assaulted people in the crowd and destroyed buses, and described the attack as unprovoked. The party blamed thugs it said were linked to the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

The other side

No response from the APC or the police to the allegation was included in the reports.

Background

The incident comes two days after gunmen attacked an NDC campaign event in Delta State, killing several people, and on the same day the House of Representatives warned that a state of emergency might be needed in Delta if violence continues. The NDC has also boycotted a security meeting called by the police chief.

Tinubu holds rally in Maiduguri, orders fresh push against insurgents

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu visited Maiduguri on Wednesday, where he commissioned projects, held a campaign rally and directed security agencies to step up operations against insurgents. He promised to turn security gains in the North-East into economic recovery.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu visited Maiduguri, the Borno State capital, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, where he held a campaign rally ahead of the January 2027 election.

Projects commissioned

On arrival, the president commissioned two projects: a female lodge for National Youth Service Corps members and the Borno International Conference Centre.

Security push

Addressing the crowd, Tinubu directed security agencies to intensify operations against retreating insurgents and promised support for the families of troops. He pledged that the peace gains recorded in the North-East would be turned into full economic recovery.

Background

Borno has been at the centre of the insurgency in the North-East for more than a decade. Earlier this week, state officials revealed that eight soldiers and 13 construction workers were killed during the rebuilding of the Mayanti community, which Governor Babagana Zulum has now reopened.
