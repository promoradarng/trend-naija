Protesters heckle INEC chairman Amupitan in London; he denies running pro-APC X account

13 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Protesters confronted the chairman of the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), Professor Joash Amupitan, in London on Thursday, 12 November 2026.

What happened

Amupitan was in London to speak at a panel on Nigeria's preparations for the 2027 elections, hosted by Chatham House and the Kimpact Development Initiative. Nigerian diaspora activists who had mobilised on social media gathered outside the venue and reportedly surrounded his vehicle, accusing the commission of favouring President Bola Tinubu and the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) and demanding his resignation. They cited duplicate entries and AI-generated images in the preliminary voters' register and alleged fake polling units.

Amupitan's response

At the panel, Amupitan pledged to turn down any political or diplomatic appointment after leaving INEC, saying he wanted to demonstrate his impartiality, and said the commission was introducing technical safeguards. INEC has said it removed 1.6 million invalid records from the register, including entries with manipulated images, and flagged officials in 10 states. Amupitan also again denied operating an X account that had allegedly posted support for the APC during the 2023 elections, saying he had never had an X account and that it was never linked to his email. INEC said in April that the claims were fabricated and that fake accounts had been created in his name.

Background

At the same event, Amupitan said INEC would obey the final court ruling on Atiku Abubakar's candidacy. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

  • Protesters demanded Amupitan's resignation.
  • He pledged to refuse post-tenure appointments.
  • He denies running a pro-APC X account.

INEC names nine states as hotspots for campaign violence

27 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

INEC Chairman Joash Amupitan has listed Lagos, Edo, Ekiti, Enugu, Ebonyi, Delta, Cross River, Oyo and Benue as states hit by campaign violence and political intolerance. He warned that the unrest could threaten preparations for the 2027 elections.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has named nine states as hotspots for campaign violence and political intolerance ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The states

INEC Chairman Professor Joash Amupitan listed Lagos, Edo, Ekiti, Enugu, Ebonyi, Delta, Cross River, Oyo and Benue. He spoke on Wednesday in Abuja at a strategic election security conference organised with the Office of the National Security Adviser and attended by the heads of security agencies.

What he said

Amupitan said campaign convoys, town hall meetings, party offices and supporters had all come under attack in the listed states. He warned that the growing unrest could jeopardise preparations for the elections, and also raised concern about harassment, intimidation and assault of INEC officials and staff.

Background

The warning follows a series of violent incidents this month, including deadly attacks on NDC campaign events in Delta and Edo states. A civic group this week recorded 124 cases of electoral violence and 22 deaths since July, and INEC has also cautioned against the 'rig and die' slogan.

Shettima says Obi failed to transform his own hometown as governor

Vice President Kashim Shettima has reportedly mocked Peter Obi, saying he governed Anambra for eight years without transforming his hometown, Agulu. He praised Governor Chukwuma Soludo for dualising the road there.

Vice President Kashim Shettima has reportedly taken a swipe at NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi, questioning why his hometown, Agulu, was not transformed during his eight years as governor of Anambra State.

What he said

Shettima made the remarks at a town hall meeting with traditional rulers in Isuofia, Anambra State, where he praised Governor Chukwuma Soludo for infrastructure projects. He credited Soludo, who is from Isuofia, with dualising the road linking Agulu to Ekwulobia, saying it took another governor to transform the road to Obi's hometown.

Background

Shettima has been campaigning in Anambra for President Tinubu's re-election. He recently said he would keep firing back at Atiku Abubakar as long as he attacks the president.

The other side

No response from Obi or the NDC was included in the reports.

The campaign of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has dismissed as fake news a claim that he plans to drop his 2027 presidential bid in favour of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar.

The claim

Political commentator Lere Olayinka had written that Makinde, the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) candidate, would step down for Atiku on 30 November, the date of the opposition parties' summit in Abuja.

The denial

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, the verified X account linked to Makinde's campaign rejected the claim and accused the ruling APC of spreading falsehoods. The post drew both support and criticism from social media users.

Background

Makinde said this week that he remains open to working with Atiku and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu, but ruled out a formal merger of parties. Opposition parties are due to hold their second summit in Abuja on Monday. No response from the APC to the accusation was included in the reports.

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) deputy governorship candidate in Ebonyi State, Ambassador Uka Chris Ndubuisi, has reportedly left the party and joined the All Progressives Congress (APC).

The announcement

Ndubuisi made the announcement on Thursday, 26 November 2026, at an APC governorship campaign rally in the state. He pledged his loyalty to President Bola Tinubu and Governor Francis Nwifuru, and said the decision was entirely his own.

The welcome

Governor Nwifuru, Minister of Works David Umahi, who is a former governor of the state, and APC supporters were present to welcome him into the party.

The other side

No response from the NDC to the defection was included in the reports. Defections between parties have been common as the 2027 campaigns gather pace.
