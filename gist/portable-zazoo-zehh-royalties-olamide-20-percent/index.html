Portable reveals how 'Zazoo Zehh' royalties were shared with Olamide and Poco Lee

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Singer Portable has revealed how royalties from his breakthrough hit 'Zazoo Zehh' were shared.

What he said

In a video reported on Monday, 9 November 2026, Portable said rapper Olamide, who featured on the song, initially asked for 16 per cent because he contributed 16 bars, while the producer received 20 per cent. He said he later raised Olamide's share to 20 per cent because he felt 16 per cent was too small for an artiste of Olamide's status, and credited Olamide with helping him secure his royalties when the song came out. He said dancer Poco Lee received 30 per cent, and he kept 30 per cent for himself.

Background

'Zazoo Zehh', released in late 2021, became a massive street anthem and turned Portable, whose real name is Habeeb Okikiola, into one of Nigeria's most talked-about artistes. The remix featured Olamide and Poco Lee. Portable has since been involved in several controversies and public disputes, but remains popular with fans of street-pop music.

  • Poco Lee received 30%.
  • Portable kept 30% for himself.

Nathaniel Bassey builds apartments for his staff and musicians

Gospel singer Nathaniel Bassey has unveiled a housing project, Love Song Apartments, built for his staff and musicians. He said he hopes they will move in with their families before Christmas.

Gospel singer Nathaniel Bassey has drawn praise after revealing that he built apartments to help members of his team become homeowners.

The project

In an Instagram post, Bassey said the housing project, named Love Song Apartments, was created for his staff and musicians. He said he was particularly excited at the prospect of the beneficiaries moving into their new homes with their families before Christmas.

Brymo urges Tinubu to name National Theatre after Pete Edochie

25 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Singer Brymo has called on President Tinubu to rename the National Theatre in Lagos after the late Pete Edochie, while saying Wole Soyinka does not deserve the honour. His remarks have sparked heated debate.

Read more

Singer Brymo has sparked a heated online debate after calling on President Bola Tinubu to rename the National Theatre in Lagos in honour of the late Nollywood legend Pete Edochie.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, Brymo addressed the president directly, urging him to immortalise Edochie by attaching his name to the iconic landmark.

He also argued that Nobel laureate Wole Soyinka, whose name has been linked with the National Theatre, should not receive the honour, and spoke critically of the writer.

Background

Pete Edochie died in Enugu early on Wednesday at the age of 79, after a long battle with prostate cancer. Tributes have poured in from President Tinubu, state governors and colleagues across the film industry.

How people reacted

Brymo's comments have sparked heated debate online.

Makinde camp denies claim he will step down for Atiku

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde's campaign has dismissed as fake a claim that he plans to withdraw his presidential bid in favour of Atiku Abubakar on 30 November. The claim was made by a political commentator.

Read more

The campaign of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has dismissed as fake news a claim that he plans to drop his 2027 presidential bid in favour of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar.

The claim

Political commentator Lere Olayinka had written that Makinde, the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) candidate, would step down for Atiku on 30 November, the date of the opposition parties' summit in Abuja.

The denial

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, the verified X account linked to Makinde's campaign rejected the claim and accused the ruling APC of spreading falsehoods. The post drew both support and criticism from social media users.

Background

Makinde said this week that he remains open to working with Atiku and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu, but ruled out a formal merger of parties. Opposition parties are due to hold their second summit in Abuja on Monday. No response from the APC to the accusation was included in the reports.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu sent a delegation to Minna, Niger State, on Thursday, 26 November 2026, to visit former military Head of State General Ibrahim Babangida and former Head of State General Abdulsalami Abubakar.

The visit

The delegation was led by the Chief of Staff to the President, Femi Gbajabiamila, who announced the visit on X. He said it was made at the president's directive and that the delegation conveyed Tinubu's best wishes to both leaders, who he said looked well and in high spirits.

Babangida's message

Background
