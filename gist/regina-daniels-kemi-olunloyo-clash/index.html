Regina Daniels and Kemi Olunloyo trade words online

5 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actress Regina Daniels and journalist Kemi Olunloyo have exchanged sharp words on social media.

How it started

The exchange began on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, when Olunloyo posted a message on Instagram directed at Regina Daniels's fans, a day after the actress's estranged husband, Senator Ned Nwoko, asked the public to stop spreading false stories about her health and threatened legal action against those responsible.

The exchange

Regina Daniels reportedly responded by telling Olunloyo not to feel accomplished and questioning whether her comments were motivated by money. Olunloyo hit back, objecting to an insult directed at her and questioning who was running the actress's social media page.

Background

Regina Daniels has recently shared videos online, including one of herself swimming, after a period of low activity that led to speculation about her health. Nwoko said the actress has been dealing with health challenges and needs space to recover. Olunloyo is known for outspoken commentary on public figures, which has often drawn controversy.

How people reacted

Fans were divided, with many defending Regina Daniels and urging people to leave her alone, while others said both women should end the argument.

IK Ogbonna calls out Governor Okpebholo over 'dangerous' Edo roads

Nollywood actor IK Ogbonna has publicly criticised Edo State governor Monday Okpebholo over the condition of roads in the state.

What he said

In posts on his Instagram Stories on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the actor said he had just travelled by road through Edo and was shocked by what he saw. He described the roads as dangerously bad and a real safety risk for travellers, and questioned how a state with such a proud cultural and historical identity could still have crumbling roads. He tagged the governor and said the people of Edo deserve better.

Background

On the same day, comedian Sabinus criticised the Rivers State government over the state of roads in Rivers. Okpebholo, of the All Progressives Congress (APC), became Edo governor in November 2024. His government had not publicly responded to the actor's comments at the time of writing.
