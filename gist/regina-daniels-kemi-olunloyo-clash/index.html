Regina Daniels and Kemi Olunloyo trade words online

5 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actress Regina Daniels and journalist Kemi Olunloyo have exchanged sharp words on social media.

How it started

The exchange began on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, when Olunloyo posted a message on Instagram directed at Regina Daniels's fans, a day after the actress's estranged husband, Senator Ned Nwoko, asked the public to stop spreading false stories about her health and threatened legal action against those responsible.

The exchange

Regina Daniels reportedly responded by telling Olunloyo not to feel accomplished and questioning whether her comments were motivated by money. Olunloyo hit back, objecting to an insult directed at her and questioning who was running the actress's social media page.

Background

Regina Daniels has recently shared videos online, including one of herself swimming, after a period of low activity that led to speculation about her health. Nwoko said the actress has been dealing with health challenges and needs space to recover. Olunloyo is known for outspoken commentary on public figures, which has often drawn controversy.

How people reacted

Fans were divided, with many defending Regina Daniels and urging people to leave her alone, while others said both women should end the argument.

Nollywood actress Anike Sinleke dies after battle with ovarian cancer

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Nollywood actress Adenike Abike Babajide, known as Anike Sinleke, has reportedly died after battling ovarian cancer. Her death was announced on Sunday by an Instagram show host she was due to appear with that day.

Read more

Nollywood is mourning the death of actress Adenike Abike Babajide, popularly known as Anike Sinleke, who has reportedly died after a battle with ovarian cancer.

How the news broke

The news emerged on Sunday, 22 November 2026, in a post by Bukola Fagbuyi, host of the Instagram live show Rubbin Minds With Bukky. Fagbuyi said the actress had been scheduled to appear as a guest on the programme that same day.

Her career

Anike Sinleke learned her craft under the mentorship of veteran actor Remmy Shitta and worked on film sets with filmmaker Deji Adenuga. At one point she stepped away from acting to complete her Higher National Diploma.

Beyond the screen, she was known in Lagos entertainment and cultural circles for her role with the Sisi Eko beauty pageant, which started around 2011.

Tributes

Fans and colleagues have taken to social media to mourn her, with many expressing shock at the news.
