Kano drug task force seals pharmaceutical company after major pregabalin seizure

14 Nov 2026, 15:45 GMT

The Kano State Multi-Agency Task Force on Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking has sealed a pharmaceutical company following the earlier recovery of about 3.18 million capsules suspected to be pregabalin.

A ₦900 million haul

The task force estimates the value of the seized stock at more than ₦900 million. Photos of the seizure show cartons packed with boxes and blister packs of red-and-white capsules.

Pregabalin is a prescription medicine used to treat nerve pain and some other conditions. It is widely misused in parts of northern Nigeria, where it is taken in large doses for its sedating effects, and is a growing concern for health and security authorities.

Questions for the regulators

The task force has also raised allegations of possible regulatory failures that may have allowed such a large quantity of the drug to circulate.

The Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN) and the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) had not responded to the allegations at the time of reporting.

What is confirmed

The sealing of the company is confirmed. The allegations against the company and against regulatory officials remain under investigation and should not be treated as established facts.

Fountain of Life Church removes Jimmy Odukoya as senior pastor

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The board of trustees of The Fountain of Life Church in Lagos has reportedly removed Jimmy Odukoya as senior pastor with immediate effect. It said his appointment did not reflect the succession wishes of his late father and founder, Taiwo Odukoya.

Read more

The Fountain of Life Church in Ilupeju, Lagos, has reportedly removed Jimmy Odukoya as its senior pastor, with the board of trustees saying his appointment conflicted with the succession plans left by the church's late founder, Taiwo Odukoya.

What the board said

In a statement on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the board is said to have explained that it reviewed the church's constitution alongside recordings of the founder's wishes made before his death in 2023. It concluded that Jimmy's tenure did not reflect those wishes and said he was no longer recognised as senior pastor, effective immediately.

The board reportedly stressed that the decision was not a personal judgement against him but a governance matter, describing it as necessary to uphold the church's constitution and preserve the succession arrangements set up by the founder. It said the decision was not taken lightly.

Background

Jimmy Odukoya, an actor and pastor, took over leadership of the church after his father's death. Earlier this month, on 1 November, he remarried and presented his new wife to the congregation, in a ceremony at which Pastor Tunde Bakare officiated the blessing.

How people reacted

The news quickly became a talking point online, with many people revisiting a cryptic post he shared days before the announcement, and a video of the late founder that is said to show the role he intended for his son.

What happens next

There was no public response from Jimmy Odukoya in the reports, and the board did not say who would lead the church.

No NIN, no flight: FG sets 1 January deadline for domestic air passengers

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Passengers without a valid National Identification Number will be refused boarding on domestic flights from 1 January 2027. Aviation Minister Festus Keyamo announced the rule while unveiling a new passenger verification system.

Read more

The federal government has announced that passengers without a valid National Identification Number (NIN) will not be allowed to board domestic flights from 1 January 2027.

The new system

Minister of Aviation and Aerospace Development Festus Keyamo announced the policy while unveiling the Verified Passenger Authentication System (VPASS), a digital platform that links passengers' identities directly to their NIN records.

Keyamo said the system will help authorities confirm genuine passengers, prevent unauthorised access to restricted airport areas and make sure airline passenger lists are accurate.

Why it is being introduced

The minister pointed to irregularities in ticket sales, including bulk buying and reselling of tickets under different identities, as problems the government wants to stop. He said tying bookings to verified identity records would make it harder for anyone to travel using false details.

What airlines must do

Keyamo directed the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA) to ensure that domestic airlines connect to the platform before 31 December 2026, ahead of the January deadline.

What it means for travellers

Anyone planning to fly within Nigeria in the new year will need to make sure their NIN is valid and matches the details they use to book tickets.

FG orders ministries and agencies to freeze staff numbers for 2027

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The federal government has reportedly told ministries, departments and agencies to keep their workforce at current levels in 2027. Agencies that ignore the new reporting rules risk losing budget provisions.

Read more

The federal government has reportedly directed all ministries, departments and agencies (MDAs) to keep their workforce at current levels for the 2027 fiscal year.

The circular

The directive is contained in a circular dated 9 November 2026 from the Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation, titled the 2027 Workforce Budget Call Circular. It was addressed to ministers, permanent secretaries, service chiefs and heads of federal institutions.

The new rules

Under the circular, MDAs may not create new departments, units or other structures without clearance from the Head of Service. Every workforce budget proposal must include the institution's approved organisational structure, and no vacancies may be created in structures that have not been officially approved.

The government is said to have singled out posts on Grade Levels 15 to 17, the senior ranks of the federal civil service, warning agencies against creating unauthorised vacancies at those levels. Agencies that can show a genuine staffing need must back their requests with evidence.

Penalties

Institutions that fail to comply with the new reporting requirements reportedly risk having their budget provisions cut off.

Gas explosion at Lagos filling station spreads fire to neighbouring school, no casualties

16 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

A gas explosion at an NNPC filling station in Ejigbo, Lagos, on Sunday reportedly started a fire that spread to a school next door. Firefighters contained it and no one was hurt, as the school was closed for the weekend.

Read more

A gas explosion at an NNPC filling station at Savannah Bus Stop, Command, in the Ejigbo area of Lagos State reportedly caused a fire that spread to a neighbouring private school on Sunday, 15 November 2026.

What happened

The explosion is said to have happened at about 1pm. The Controller-General of the Lagos State Fire and Rescue Service, Margaret Adeseye, confirmed the incident and said the force of the blast caused the fire to spread quickly to Saint Lawrence Greater Height School, which shares a boundary with the station, affecting parts of the premises.

The response

Adeseye said the service received a distress call at 12:50pm and reached the scene at 1:13pm. Firefighters from the Ejigbo, Ikeja and Bolade fire stations were deployed, and they stopped the fire from spreading to other parts of the school.

No casualties

No one was injured or killed, as the school was closed for the weekend. The fire service said preliminary investigations into the cause of the explosion were under way.
