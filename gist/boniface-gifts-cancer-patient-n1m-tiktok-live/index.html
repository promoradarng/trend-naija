Victor Boniface sends ₦1m to cancer patient during TikTok livestream

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Super Eagles striker Victor Boniface has won praise online after donating ₦1 million to a woman with cancer during a TikTok livestream.

What happened

In a clip that spread widely on Friday, 13 November 2026, the woman joined Boniface's livestream and told him she needed about ₦4 million for her treatment. After listening to her, the Bayer Leverkusen forward asked for her account details and sent the money immediately, leaving her visibly emotional. Many Nigerians commended him for his generosity.

His career

Away from the gesture, Boniface is going through a difficult spell. Injuries have held back his progress at Leverkusen, and a loan spell at Werder Bremen last season did not revive his form. He has played only a few minutes of Bundesliga football this season and is reportedly keen to leave in the January transfer window. Leverkusen, where he is under contract until 2028, are said to be open to a sale or a loan.

Flamingos coach Busari nominated for CAF Women's Coach of the Year

18 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Akeem Busari, who led the Flamingos to the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup title, has been nominated for CAF Women's Coach of the Year. The awards take place on 2 December.

Read more

Flamingos head coach Akeem Busari has been nominated for the CAF Women's Coach of the Year award after leading Nigeria to victory at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The shortlist

Busari is one of 10 coaches on the shortlist. Others include Algeria's Farid Benstiti, Cameroon's Valentine Nguele, Ghana's Kim Lars Bjorkegren, Morocco's Jorge Vilda, South Africa's Desiree Ellis and Tanzania's Bakari Nyundo Shime, as well as Silveria Nedio of Cabo Verde, Lovemore Fazili of Malawi and Hicham Drissi of Moroccan club AS FAR.

His achievement

Busari guided the Flamingos to Nigeria's first U-17 Women's World Cup title, making them the first African team to win the competition after beating Japan 2-1 in the final.

When the winners are announced

The 2026 CAF Awards will take place on Wednesday, 2 December, at the Professor Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Arts.

Tiger Woods to make competitive return at Hero World Challenge

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Tiger Woods will play the Hero World Challenge in the Bahamas next month, his first outdoor tournament since the 2024 Open. The 15-time major champion has been held back by injuries.

Read more

Tiger Woods has announced that he will play at next month's Hero World Challenge in the Bahamas, his first competitive outdoor appearance since the 2024 Open Championship.

The tournament

The 50-year-old will be part of a 20-player field for the event, which runs from 3 to 6 December. Scottie Scheffler, Justin Thomas, Xander Schauffele and Rickie Fowler are also due to play.

Long road back

Woods, a 15-time major champion, missed the cut at the 2024 Open while dealing with serious injuries, and further problems kept him away from outdoor competition for more than two years. He returned to competition at an indoor TGL match in March.

Days later, he was involved in a car crash in Florida. He later reached a plea deal, pleading guilty to reckless driving and refusing to take a test, and lost his driving licence for five years.

What happens next

It is not yet clear whether Woods plans to play a wider schedule of tournaments after the event.

Olympic Eagles beat DR Congo 4-0 to overturn first-leg deficit

17 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Nigeria's U23 team beat DR Congo 4-0 in Ikenne to overturn a 2-0 first-leg loss and reach the final round of U23 qualifying. They will now face Zambia.

Read more

Nigeria's under-23 team, the Olympic Eagles, beat the Democratic Republic of Congo 4-0 at the Remo Stars Stadium in Ikenne to reach the final round of qualifying for the U23 Africa Cup of Nations.

The comeback

The win was all the more impressive because Nigeria had lost the first leg 2-0 in Lubumbashi four days earlier. Alex Oweilayefa opened the scoring in the eighth minute after the DR Congo goalkeeper spilled a powerful shot from Umar Abubakar.

The home side kept up the pressure, with Saleh Sadiq denied by a goal-line clearance before half-time and Abubakar missing a close-range chance, leaving the tie level on aggregate at the break. Nigeria pulled away in the second half to win 4-0 on the day.

What happens next

The Olympic Eagles will face Zambia in the final round of qualifying.

Bright spot

The result came on the same day the Super Eagles were held to a goalless draw by Tanzania in their AFCON 2027 qualifier.

Super Eagles held 0-0 by Tanzania, AFCON 2027 hopes take a hit

17 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigeria were held to a goalless draw by Tanzania in Zanzibar in their AFCON 2027 qualifier, missing the chance to move to nine points. Tolu Arokodare hit the post in the first half, and coach Eric Chelle admitted afterwards that the team is in trouble.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles were held to a 0-0 draw by Tanzania at the Amaan Stadium in Zanzibar on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, a result that reportedly dents their hopes in 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying Group L.

The match

Nigeria went into the game knowing a win would take them to nine points and put pressure on the group leaders, Guinea-Bissau. Despite having long spells of possession, they could not break down a well-organised Tanzanian side.

Their best chance came in the 33rd minute, when Moses Usor set up Tolu Arokodare, whose close-range shot hit the post. Calvin Bassey fired over five minutes later, while Tanzania also struck the woodwork in the 42nd minute. Coach Eric Chelle made changes just after the hour, but the breakthrough never came.

After the game

Chelle reportedly admitted after the match that the team is now in trouble in the qualifying race. Nigeria had won the first leg 3-1 in Uyo, but the draw means they missed the chance to move above Guinea-Bissau.

Background

The game came a day after Chelle clashed with a journalist over his tactics at the pre-match press conference. Nigeria were without injured forwards Ademola Lookman and Victor Osimhen.
