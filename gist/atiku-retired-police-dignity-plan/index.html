Atiku criticises Tinubu over retired police officers, promises pension reforms

2 Nov 2026, 18:01 GMT

Atiku Abubakar, presidential candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), has criticised President Bola Tinubu over the treatment of retired police officers and unveiled what he called a Police Dignity Guarantee Plan.

What he said

In comments reported on Monday, 2 November 2026, Atiku condemned what he described as the government's failure to protect retired officers who have been protesting at the National Pension Commission in Abuja over their pensions. He reportedly faulted the administration for not acting on a police exit bill which he said has been awaiting the President's signature since March 2026.

His promises

Atiku said that if elected, he would restore gratuity for retired officers and implement a Police Pension Board Bill within 30 days of taking office. He said police officers who served the country deserve to retire with dignity.

Background

Retired police officers have held repeated protests in Abuja over what they say are poor pensions under the contributory pension scheme, and have called for police to be removed from the scheme. The federal government has said it is working on reforms to improve welfare for serving and retired officers. Atiku has recently launched several attacks on the government's record, including accusing Tinubu of failing to keep his promises on electricity. The APC has dismissed such criticism, saying the government's reforms are beginning to show results.

What happens next

The Presidency had not responded to Atiku's latest comments at the time of reporting.

  • Atiku says a police exit bill has been unsigned since March.
  • He promises to restore gratuity for retired officers.
  • He would act on a pension board bill within 30 days.

Petrol prices fall by up to ₦85 a litre as Dangote suspends diesel price cut

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Petrol pump prices have reportedly fallen by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre in a week as refiners and importers compete. Dangote Refinery has, however, withdrawn a planned ₦150 cut in diesel prices pending a review.

Read more

Motorists are seeing some relief at filling stations, with petrol prices reportedly falling by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre over the past seven days.

Petrol prices

The reductions follow fresh price cuts by Dangote Refinery, importers and major marketers, amid growing competition between locally refined and imported petrol. Dangote has cut its petrol price several times this month, most recently to ₦1,105 per litre at its depot.

Diesel U-turn

However, Dangote Refinery has withdrawn an earlier announcement that it would reduce the gantry price of diesel from ₦1,720 to ₦1,570 per litre. In a new message to customers, the refinery said the proposed ₦150 cut, which was meant to take effect on Saturday, 21 November, was still under internal review.

Marketers and businesses that rely on diesel are now waiting for clarity on the new rate.

Troops foil suspected kidnap attempt at Kaduna school

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Soldiers reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at a college in Zangon Kataf, Kaduna State. A headcount showed no student was missing.

Read more

Troops have reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at Tafawa Balewa Memorial Commercial College in Samaru, Zangon Kataf Local Government Area of Kaduna State.

What happened

The incident happened at about 11:56pm on 18 November 2026, after reports of suspicious movements by unidentified people around the school. Soldiers responded quickly and moved to the school, forcing the suspects to abandon their plan and flee.

All students safe

The troops searched the school and surrounding areas but did not make contact with the fleeing suspects. A headcount carried out with the school authorities showed that no student or other occupant was missing.

Security stepped up

The troops have continued patrols in the area to prevent further attacks.

The video

Why it matters

KWAM 1 backs Tinubu's fuel subsidy removal at APC campaign event

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Fuji star Wasiu Ayinde, KWAM 1, has defended President Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy, saying it mainly benefited a small group rather than ordinary Nigerians.

Read more

Veteran Fuji musician Wasiu Ayinde, popularly known as KWAM 1, has thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. He questioned who had really benefited from the subsidy, saying a small group of people had gained from it while ordinary Nigerians saw little benefit.

Background

The petrol subsidy was removed when Tinubu took office in 2023, leading to a sharp rise in fuel prices.

The campaign

His comments came during the APC's campaign tour of the South-West, which included a presidential rally in Ibadan.
