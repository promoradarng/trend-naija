Car dealer Moses Auto says fire destroyed vehicles worth over ₦700 million

2 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Car dealer Moses Auto Car Care says a fire at his business destroyed his own cars worth more than ₦500 million and customers' cars worth over ₦200 million. Many people online sympathised and urged businesses to take insurance and fire safety seriously.

Read more

A Nigerian car dealer known online as Moses Auto Car Care has described the heavy losses he suffered after a fire swept through his car lot.

What he said

In comments shared on Monday, 2 November 2026, the dealer reportedly said that cars worth more than ₦500 million belonging to him were destroyed in the fire. He added that customers' vehicles worth over ₦200 million were also burnt, including cars that buyers had already paid for but had not yet collected. He did not say what caused the fire or when exactly it happened.

How people reacted

Many social media users expressed sympathy and prayed for him to recover from the loss, with some praising him for staying strong. Others said the incident showed the importance of insurance, which many Nigerian businesses do not take, and of basic fire safety equipment such as alarms and sprinklers. Some asked how the customers who had paid for cars would be compensated.

Background

Fires at markets, shops and car lots cause large losses in Nigeria every year, often linked to electrical faults or poor safety measures. Fire services frequently urge business owners to install extinguishers and alarms and to insure their goods.

What happens next

It is not yet clear whether the business was insured or how the dealer plans to settle with affected customers.

Atiku accuses DSS of plotting to disrupt ADC rally as Sokoto seals party office

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has alleged that the DSS is recruiting protesters to disrupt his party's rally in Sokoto. Separately, the Sokoto government sealed an ADC campaign office, saying the building's ownership was unclear and citing a disturbance.

Read more

Tension is rising in Sokoto State ahead of a planned campaign rally by the African Democratic Congress (ADC), with presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar accusing the Department of State Services (DSS) of plotting to disrupt it.

Atiku's allegation

In a statement on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, Atiku alleged that the leadership of the DSS was recruiting people to stage protests in Sokoto in order to create disorder. He claimed hired protesters were being gathered at an office on Koko Road and sent out in trucks to key locations in the city. He also said public facilities had been blocked and logistical support withheld to frustrate the ADC's campaign, and warned that security officials who help suppress political freedom would be held to account under the law. He said the rally would go ahead. The DSS had not responded publicly to the claims at the time of reporting.

Campaign office sealed

Separately, the Sokoto State Government sealed an ADC campaign office on Ahmadu Bello Way on Monday, 2 November. Commissioner for Land and Housing Nasiru Dantsoho said records showed the building, once used by the defunct Savannah Bank and previously by the PDP, still belonged to the bank, and that the state had written to the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation to clarify its ownership. He said the premises were sealed after a disturbance involving party supporters and others, to prevent further trouble. A media aide to the ADC governorship candidate confirmed the closure.

Background

Sokoto is governed by the APC. Opposition parties have complained of attacks and obstacles to their campaigns in several states, and an ADC candidate's convoy was attacked in Sokoto in recent weeks. The APC and state governments have generally denied using state agencies against opponents.

What happens next

Security agencies are expected to be on alert as the ADC presses ahead with its rally.

Fans raise concern over Doris Ogala's health in detention after medical report surfaces

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

A medical report shared online says actress and governorship aspirant Doris Ogala was treated for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition earlier this year, raising concern among supporters about her health in detention. Her bail ruling is due on 6 November.

Read more

Supporters of actress and governorship aspirant Doris Ogala have raised concerns about her health while she remains in detention, after a medical report about her condition was shared online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026.

The medical report

According to reports, the document from the Federal Medical Centre in Umuahia, dated 5 June 2026, shows that she was treated for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition, following an earlier surgical procedure in 2025. The report reportedly says she was admitted, treated and discharged after significant improvement, and was advised to avoid strenuous activity for six months and attend regular follow-up appointments. Reports claim she has been experiencing renewed bleeding while in detention, but this has not been independently confirmed.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters took to social media to express worry and call for her release on health grounds. Some urged the authorities to ensure she receives proper medical care while her case continues.

Background

Doris Ogala, 30, is a Nollywood actress and a governorship candidate of the African Action Congress. She was remanded in custody last month in a case linked to her dispute with Pastor Chris Okafor, which arose from social media posts. The AAC's presidential candidate, Omoyele Sowore, and other supporters have condemned her detention. A court is due to rule on her bail application on 6 November.

What happens next

Her lawyers are expected to raise her health when the court considers her bail application.

Rufai Oseni challenges Umahi to win six million votes before comparing himself to Obi

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

TV presenter Rufai Oseni has reportedly hit back at Works Minister Dave Umahi, who called Peter Obi a 'nobody' and described himself as the Lion of the South East. Oseni said Umahi should first run for president and win at least six million votes.

Read more

Television presenter Rufai Oseni has responded sharply to Minister of Works Dave Umahi's recent comments about NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi.

What Umahi said

At an APC town hall in Abakaliki, Ebonyi State, on Monday, 2 November 2026, Umahi reportedly questioned Obi's political relevance, described him as a 'nobody' and called himself the 'Lion of the South East'. He has also asked what makes Obi better than past South East leaders such as Sam Egwu and Anyim Pius Anyim, and has argued that the region should back President Bola Tinubu so that an Igbo candidate can later get eight years rather than four.

Oseni's response

Speaking on his breakfast television programme on Tuesday, 3 November, Oseni reportedly said self-given titles mean little without results. He said that before Umahi can compare himself with Obi, he should obtain a presidential nomination form, contest a general election and win at least six million votes, roughly the number Obi received in the 2023 presidential election.

Background

Umahi, a former two-term governor of Ebonyi State, is a leading APC figure in the South East and has campaigned hard for Tinubu's re-election. Obi, a former Anambra governor, came third in the 2023 presidential election as the Labour Party candidate and is now running on the NDC ticket with Rabiu Kwankwaso. The South East is seen as an important battleground in 2027, and four governors from the zone recently declared support for Tinubu.

How people reacted

Obi's supporters cheered Oseni's comments, while APC supporters said Umahi's record as governor and minister speaks for itself.
