Super Eagles held 0-0 by Tanzania, AFCON 2027 hopes take a hit

17 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigeria's Super Eagles were held to a 0-0 draw by Tanzania at the Amaan Stadium in Zanzibar on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, a result that reportedly dents their hopes in 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying Group L.

The match

Nigeria went into the game knowing a win would take them to nine points and put pressure on the group leaders, Guinea-Bissau. Despite having long spells of possession, they could not break down a well-organised Tanzanian side.

Their best chance came in the 33rd minute, when Moses Usor set up Tolu Arokodare, whose close-range shot hit the post. Calvin Bassey fired over five minutes later, while Tanzania also struck the woodwork in the 42nd minute. Coach Eric Chelle made changes just after the hour, but the breakthrough never came.

After the game

Chelle reportedly admitted after the match that the team is now in trouble in the qualifying race. Nigeria had won the first leg 3-1 in Uyo, but the draw means they missed the chance to move above Guinea-Bissau.

Background

The game came a day after Chelle clashed with a journalist over his tactics at the pre-match press conference. Nigeria were without injured forwards Ademola Lookman and Victor Osimhen.

Olympic Eagles beat DR Congo 4-0 to overturn first-leg deficit

17 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Nigeria's U23 team beat DR Congo 4-0 in Ikenne to overturn a 2-0 first-leg loss and reach the final round of U23 qualifying. They will now face Zambia.

Read more

Nigeria's under-23 team, the Olympic Eagles, beat the Democratic Republic of Congo 4-0 at the Remo Stars Stadium in Ikenne to reach the final round of qualifying for the U23 Africa Cup of Nations.

The comeback

The win was all the more impressive because Nigeria had lost the first leg 2-0 in Lubumbashi four days earlier. Alex Oweilayefa opened the scoring in the eighth minute after the DR Congo goalkeeper spilled a powerful shot from Umar Abubakar.

The home side kept up the pressure, with Saleh Sadiq denied by a goal-line clearance before half-time and Abubakar missing a close-range chance, leaving the tie level on aggregate at the break. Nigeria pulled away in the second half to win 4-0 on the day.

What happens next

The Olympic Eagles will face Zambia in the final round of qualifying.

Bright spot

The result came on the same day the Super Eagles were held to a goalless draw by Tanzania in their AFCON 2027 qualifier.

17 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Arsenal midfielder Declan Rice has reportedly agreed a new contract running until 2031. It would put him alongside Bukayo Saka as the club's highest-paid player.

Read more

Arsenal midfielder Declan Rice has reportedly agreed a new five-year contract that will keep him at the club until the summer of 2031.

The deal

The England international is expected to sign soon. The new contract is said to put him level with Bukayo Saka as Arsenal's top earner, with Saka reportedly earning more than £350,000 a week.

Rice hinted at the deal while on international duty with England, saying a new contract was hopefully close and praising manager Mikel Arteta and his teammates for the trust they have shown in him.

His Arsenal story

Rice joined Arsenal from West Ham in 2023 for £105 million and has become the centre of their midfield. He played 55 games last season, helping the club win the Premier League title and reach their first Champions League final since 2006, which they lost to Paris Saint-Germain.

What happens next

Talks on the deal are said to have been going on since at least March and have now been finalised, with only the signing left.

Crystal Palace appoint Thomas Frank after sacking Pierre Sage

Crystal Palace have named former Brentford and Tottenham boss Thomas Frank as their new manager on a three-year deal. He replaces Pierre Sage, who was sacked after four months with the club bottom of the Premier League.

Crystal Palace have appointed Thomas Frank as their new manager, following the sacking of Pierre Sage.

The change

Sage joined the club in the summer but was dismissed after only four months, with Palace bottom of the Premier League after 10 matches. Frank, who previously managed Brentford and Tottenham, has signed a three-year contract.

What they said

Palace chairman Steve Parish welcomed the Dane, saying he had a proven record of building winning teams and improving players, and that his Premier League experience would help the club in the rest of the season.

Frank said it was an honour to take charge of what he called an iconic club, and that he was looking forward to meeting the players and staff.

What happens next

His first game in charge is this weekend, when Palace travel to Coventry hoping to climb off the bottom of the table.

Fulham reportedly want £50m for Calvin Bassey as Manchester United show interest

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Fulham are reportedly asking for about £50 million for Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey amid interest from Manchester United. A sale at that price would be a club record for Fulham.

Read more

Fulham are reportedly ready to demand around £50 million for Nigerian international defender Calvin Bassey in the January transfer window, as Manchester United weigh a move.

The interest

Manchester United are said to be genuinely interested in the 26-year-old, who plays mainly at centre-back but can also play at left-back, as they look to strengthen their defence for the second half of the season.

United reportedly believe a bid above £40 million would be needed to start talks, but Fulham are said to be holding out for closer to £50 million. That would beat the club's record sale, Aleksandar Mitrović's move to Al-Hilal in 2023.

Contract situation

Bassey's contract runs until summer 2027, with Fulham holding an option to extend it by a year. Talks on a new long-term deal have reportedly moved slowly, which could leave the club open to a big offer if nothing is agreed before January.

Background

Bassey, a former Ajax and Rangers defender, is a regular for the Super Eagles. He is reportedly open to a move to Old Trafford.
