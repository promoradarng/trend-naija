Chinese national and police escort killed in ambush in Bauchi

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Suspected terrorists have killed a Chinese national and a police inspector in an attack in Bauchi State.

What happened

According to security sources, the attack happened on Saturday, 14 November 2026, at Ruga village in Alkaleri Local Government Area. The Chinese national, who was reportedly working on core drilling in the area, was travelling in a company vehicle when it was ambushed. A police inspector from a mobile police unit, who was deployed as his escort, was also killed. A local source said the attackers killed another man in the village.

Response

Troops of the Nigerian Army's 3 Division were informed of the attack late on Saturday night, and security sources said efforts were under way to track down the attackers and establish the circumstances of the ambush.

Background

Atiku Abubakar's campaign was in Bauchi State over the weekend, and insecurity remains a key issue ahead of the 2027 elections.

IGP tells doctors: no police report needed to treat gunshot victims

20 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Inspector-General of Police Olatunji Disu has reminded doctors and hospitals that the law does not require a police report before treating gunshot victims. He urged health workers to put saving lives first.

Read more

The Inspector-General of Police, Olatunji Disu, has urged doctors and other health workers to treat gunshot victims immediately, without first demanding a police report.

What the IGP said

In a message posted on the Nigeria Police Force's official X account on Thursday, 19 November 2026, Disu stressed that the law does not require hospitals to obtain a police report before attending to people with gunshot wounds.

He appealed to doctors and health workers to prioritise saving lives instead of waiting for police documents before starting emergency treatment.

Help for the public

The IGP also encouraged members of the public not to hesitate to help injured people get to hospital.

Background

Nigeria has a law requiring hospitals to treat gunshot victims without first demanding a police report.
