Osimhen and partner Stefanie welcome second daughter

30 Oct 2026, 09:49 GMT

Super Eagles striker Victor Osimhen is a father again after his partner, Stefanie Ladewig, gave birth to a baby girl.

What happened

His club, Galatasaray, announced the birth on social media on Thursday, 29 October 2026, congratulating the striker and Stefanie on the arrival of their daughter. The baby has reportedly been named Liora Audrey. The exact date of birth was not given.

Background

It is the couple's second child. Their first daughter, Hailey, was born in October 2022. The good news comes during a strong spell for Osimhen on the pitch. He scored twice in Galatasaray's derby win over Fenerbahçe on Monday and is closing in on the club's record for goals by a foreign player, with 68 goals so far.

How fans reacted

Nigerian and Turkish fans flooded social media with congratulations, with many joking that the new baby had brought him luck in front of goal.

Kimi Antonelli, 20, becomes youngest Formula 1 world champion

2 Nov 2026, 14:56 GMT

Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli has reportedly clinched the Formula 1 world title at the age of 20 after finishing third in Mexico City, with four races still to go. He breaks Sebastian Vettel's record and is Italy's first champion since 1953.

Read more

Italian driver Kimi Antonelli has become the youngest world champion in the history of Formula 1, according to reports, after sealing the title at the Mexico City Grand Prix on Sunday, 1 November 2026.

What happened

Antonelli, who drives for Mercedes, needed only a ninth-place finish to make sure of the championship. He finished third, behind race winner Max Verstappen of Red Bull and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, which put the title out of reach of his rivals with four races of the season still left. Hamilton and his Mercedes team-mate George Russell had been his closest challengers.

Records broken

At 20 years and 68 days, Antonelli breaks the record set by Sebastian Vettel, who was 23 years and 134 days old when he won his first title with Red Bull in 2010. He is also the first Italian to win the drivers' championship since Alberto Ascari in 1953, ending a wait of 73 years for the country. Remarkably, he is only in his second season in Formula 1.

What he said

Celebrating with his family and the Mercedes team, Antonelli reportedly thanked both, saying he would not be there without them. Verstappen was among the first to congratulate him and said he was very proud of the young driver, adding that it must be an emotional day for his family.

Background

Antonelli was picked by Mercedes as a teenager to replace Hamilton, who moved to Ferrari in 2025. Many questioned whether he was ready for such a big seat, but he has now delivered the team's first drivers' title since Hamilton's last championship in 2020.

What happens next

The remaining four races will decide the other places in the drivers' standings and the constructors' championship.

Flamingos beat Venezuela to reach U-17 Women's World Cup semi-finals, face Brazil next

2 Nov 2026, 09:44 GMT

Nigeria's Flamingos beat Venezuela 1-0 in Morocco through a late penalty from captain Azeezat Oduntan to reach the semi-finals of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup. They are Africa's last team in the tournament and will play Brazil on Wednesday.

Read more

Nigeria's under-17 women's team, the Flamingos, are into the semi-finals of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The match

The Flamingos beat Venezuela 1-0 in the quarter-final on Sunday, 1 November 2026, at the Mohammed VI Football Academy in Salé. Captain Azeezat Oduntan scored the winner from the penalty spot in the 89th minute after Oluebube Umejiaku was fouled in the box, a decision confirmed by a VAR review.

What comes next

Nigeria will face Brazil in the semi-final on Wednesday, 4 November, at the Moulay El Hassan Stadium in Rabat. They are the only African side left in the competition after Ghana lost 1-0 to Japan in their quarter-final.

Reactions

The Confederation of African Football congratulated the team on social media, celebrating their place in the last four. Nigerian fans also shared videos of the players dancing and celebrating after earlier victories in the tournament.

Background

Nigeria has a strong record in women's youth football and has reached the later stages of age-group World Cups several times. The senior team, the Super Falcons, are the most successful side in African women's football and won another Women's Africa Cup of Nations title this year. The Flamingos' run has raised hopes of Nigeria's first-ever U-17 women's world title.

Court orders arrest of Lagos NURTW chairman Sego over alleged contempt

2 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

The National Industrial Court in Lagos has ordered the arrest of Lagos NURTW chairman Mustapha Adekunle, known as Sego, and the union's secretary, after they failed to appear to explain why they should not be jailed for contempt. The case arises from a dispute with tricycle operators.

Read more

The National Industrial Court in Lagos has ordered the arrest and detention of the Lagos State chairman of the National Union of Road Transport Workers (NURTW), Mustapha Adekunle, popularly known as Sego, and the union's secretary, Usman Teslim.

What the court said

Justice Isaac Essien gave the order on Monday, 2 November 2026, after the two officials failed to appear in court to show why they should not be sent to prison for contempt. According to reports, the judge described their conduct as brazen disobedience of court orders and warned that the authority of the courts must not be undermined. He directed the Lagos Commissioner of Police, the state commandant of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps and other security agencies to arrest the two men and hand them over to the officer in charge of the Kirikiri Correctional Centre.

Bailiff obstruction

The court also raised concern over an affidavit filed by its bailiff, who said he was prevented from serving court papers on 5 October by a security officer linked to one of the parties in the case. Justice Essien reportedly described the alleged obstruction as a dangerous development.

Background

The case stems from a long-running dispute between commercial tricycle operators and the NURTW in Lagos over control of operations and levies. The union has been at the centre of several leadership and legal battles in the state in recent years. Sego and the union have not publicly responded to the order.

What happens next

The officials can still appear before the court or challenge the order on appeal. It is not yet clear whether security agencies have acted on the directive.

Iran says it could reopen Strait of Hormuz within two days if US meets its demands

2 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Iran has reportedly said it could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within 48 hours if Washington accepts its conditions, including lifting oil sanctions and ending a naval blockade. Talks in Doha ended without a breakthrough.

Read more

Iran says it could reopen the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most important oil shipping routes, within 48 hours if the United States accepts its conditions for ending the war between the two countries.

What Iran said

Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said on Monday, 2 November 2026, that Tehran had shortened an earlier seven-day proposal to two days, and reportedly described this as a sign of good faith. He said reopening the waterway and returning to negotiations depended on Washington agreeing to Iran's demands, and that messages were still being exchanged through mediators.

Iran's conditions

According to reports, Iran wants an end to the war across the Middle East, the release of its frozen assets abroad, the lifting of sanctions on its oil sector and an end to the US naval blockade of its ports. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has also called for a new transit arrangement for ships passing through the strait, agreed between Iran and Oman, and fewer US troops in the region.

Background

The two countries have been in conflict for about eight months. Iranian and Qatari negotiators held three days of talks in Doha aimed at reaching a deal, but they ended without a breakthrough. On Sunday, President Donald Trump told a rally that the US might eventually have to give Iran 'the hammer', and Iran's army said it was ready for a long war.

Why it matters

A large share of the world's oil passes through the Strait of Hormuz. Disruption there affects global oil prices, which matters for Nigeria's earnings from crude oil and for fuel costs around the world.
