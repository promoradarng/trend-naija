Jigawa declares holiday as Tinubu visits for rally and project launches

10 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Jigawa State has declared Tuesday, 10 November 2026, a public holiday for a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

Governor Umar Namadi told journalists in Dutse on Monday that the President would hold an All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign rally and inaugurate four projects. He said it would be Tinubu's first official visit to the state since he took office in May 2023. The projects include a technical services centre, a skills acquisition centre named after the President, an agricultural mechanisation programme and a dualised road in Dutse.

The farm equipment

The agricultural package includes 400 tractors, 60 combine harvesters, 150 boom sprayers and 150 seeders, along with other equipment intended to boost farm output in the state.

Power pledge at the rally

At the APC rally in Jigawa, Tinubu promised to lower the cost of living and provide reliable electricity, saying the old privatisation model was not working. During his 2023 campaign he said Nigerians should not re-elect him if he failed to provide stable power in his first term.

Background

The Jigawa visit continues the President's campaign tour, which in the past week has taken him to Akwa Ibom, Rivers and Ebonyi states. Namadi, elected on the APC platform in 2023, is a strong supporter of the President's re-election. The North-West, which includes Jigawa, has one of the largest voting populations in Nigeria.

Davido and cousins turn heads at Governor Adeleke's second-term inauguration

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Osun Governor Ademola Adeleke was sworn in for a second term on Friday in Osogbo. His nephew Davido and other family members drew attention with a coordinated appearance at the ceremony.

Read more

Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke was inaugurated for a second term on Friday, 27 November 2026, at the Osogbo Township Stadium.

Family affair

Afrobeats star Davido, the governor's nephew, drew almost as much attention as the ceremony itself. He arrived with his brother Adewale Adeleke and cousins Shina Adeleke, known as Shina Rambo, and Adebayo Adeleke, known as B-Red. The four men wore matching white traditional outfits, and photos of them spread quickly online.

Davido's father, businessman Adedeji Adeleke, also drew attention for showing respect to the Ooni of Ife at the event.

Background

Adeleke, a chieftain of the Accord Party, dissolved his executive council earlier this week ahead of the start of his second term.

OK Movement withdraws support from Obi and the NDC

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

The OK Movement, named after Peter Obi and Rabiu Kwankwaso, has reportedly pulled its campaign support from the NDC. Its director-general said the group had been told Obi and the party had chosen to cut ties with it.

Read more

The OK Movement, a support group named after the initials of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso, has reportedly withdrawn its campaign and mobilisation support from the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

The announcement

In a statement on Friday, 27 November 2026, the movement's national director-general, John Ughulu, said the group had been informed that Obi and the NDC had decided to cut ties with it. He directed the movement's ward, local government and state structures to stop all involvement in NDC activities immediately.

What next

Ughulu said the group had begun discussions with other political parties and presidential aspirants.

Background

The development comes days before Obi is due to attend an opposition summit in Abuja with Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde.

The other side

No response from Obi or the NDC was included in the reports.

VeryDarkMan urges Obi, Atiku and Makinde to form one party to face Tinubu

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has called on Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to put their ambitions aside and unite under one party against President Tinubu. The three are due at an opposition summit on Monday.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one political party ahead of the 2027 election.

What he said

In a post on Instagram, VDM said the three should sit down together and form one party to defeat President Bola Tinubu. He argued that they need to set aside their personal ambitions, and that a united front would make the contest against the ruling party more competitive.

Background

Obi of the NDC, Atiku of the ADC and Makinde of the APM are expected at an opposition summit in Abuja on Monday, 30 November, where parties are due to sign a cooperation pact. The organisers have said it will not be a formal merger, and Makinde's camp has denied reports that he plans to step down for Atiku.
