Ronaldo scores hat-trick for Al Nassr, now 15 goals short of 1,000

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Cristiano Ronaldo is closing in on one of football's most remarkable milestones after scoring three goals in Al Nassr's latest win.

What happened

The Portuguese forward reportedly scored in the 66th, 72nd and 81st minutes as Al Nassr beat Al Kholood 5-3 at their home ground in Riyadh, according to reports on Friday, 30 October 2026. The hat-trick took his career tally for club and country to 985 goals, leaving him 15 short of 1,000.

Background

Ronaldo, 41, joined Al Nassr in 2023 and has continued to score regularly in the Saudi Pro League. He has said publicly that reaching 1,000 goals is one of his remaining goals before retirement. His career total includes goals for Sporting Lisbon, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al Nassr and Portugal, for whom he is the all-time leading international scorer.

A big week

The hat-trick came in the same week that fans voted his 2007-08 season at Manchester United the greatest Ballon d'Or-winning season of all time in a poll run by France Football.

What happens next

At his current scoring rate, Ronaldo could reach the 1,000-goal mark before the end of the season, and fans around the world will be counting down.

  • Ronaldo scored three times in a 5-3 win over Al Kholood.
  • He now has 985 career goals.
  • He needs 15 more to reach 1,000.

Cristiano Ronaldo retires from international football at 41

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Cristiano Ronaldo has announced his retirement from international football, ending a 23-year career with Portugal in which he became the all-time leading scorer in men's international football. He announced it in a letter read in an Instagram video.

Read more

Cristiano Ronaldo has announced his retirement from international football, bringing to an end one of the most remarkable careers in the history of the game.

The announcement

The 41-year-old shared the news on Thursday, 5 November 2026, in an Instagram video in which he read a letter to the Portuguese people. He said the day had come to hand back the national team shirt and captain's armband, which he had never felt belonged only to him, and that the decision was made with the heart as well as the head. He described a journey that began in August 2003 and said that when he looks back he sees not only goals and trophies but the people who supported him, including Portuguese emigrants around the world.

A record career

Ronaldo made his Portugal debut in 2003 and went on to become the most-capped player and all-time top scorer in men's international football. He led Portugal to victory at Euro 2016, their first major title, and also won the UEFA Nations League twice. He played at six World Cups.

Club career continues

Ronaldo will continue playing for Al Nassr in Saudi Arabia. Last week he scored a hat-trick for the club, taking his career total to 985 goals, and he has said he wants to reach 1,000.

How people reacted

Fans, players and clubs around the world paid tribute, with many calling him one of the greatest players of all time.

Asisat Oshoala gifts her teenage coach a new Toyota Highlander

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Super Falcons star Asisat Oshoala has reportedly given a brand new Toyota Highlander to Emmanuel Osahon, chairman and head coach of FC Robo International, the Lagos club where her career began. The club thanked her publicly.

Read more

Super Falcons forward Asisat Oshoala has surprised her former youth coach with a new car.

What happened

According to reports on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Oshoala presented a brand new Toyota Highlander to Coach Emmanuel Osahon, the chairman and head coach of FC Robo International in Lagos, which nurtured her talent from 2009 when she was a teenager. The club released a statement thanking her, describing the gift as a beautiful demonstration of gratitude and a sign that she has not forgotten the people who helped her on her journey.

A history of giving back

The gesture recalled an earlier donation Oshoala made to the club during her first years in Europe.

Background

Oshoala is one of Africa's most decorated footballers, having won the CAF Women's Player of the Year award a record six times. She has played for clubs including Liverpool, Arsenal and Barcelona, where she won the UEFA Women's Champions League. She has also helped the Super Falcons win several Africa Women Cup of Nations titles. The gift comes in the same week that the Super Falcons received the cash rewards promised for their 2024 WAFCON victory.

How people reacted

Fans praised Oshoala for honouring her coach and said more successful athletes should support the people and clubs that helped them.

Flamingos beat Brazil 3-1 to reach first U-17 Women's World Cup final

5 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nigeria's Flamingos have reached the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup for the first time after beating Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final. Awawu Bashiru scored twice and Queen Joseph once. They face Japan in the final.

Read more

Nigeria's under-17 women's team, the Flamingos, have made history by reaching the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup for the first time.

The match

The Flamingos beat Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final on Wednesday night, 4 November 2026, according to reports. Queen Joseph opened the scoring in the 56th minute, and Awawu Bashiru doubled the lead in the 71st minute before adding her second goal in the 80th minute to put the game beyond Brazil's reach.

A record run

The win beats the team's previous best performance, a bronze medal at the 2022 edition of the tournament. On the way to the semi-final, the Flamingos beat Venezuela in the quarter-finals, a result that earned praise from the Confederation of African Football.

The final

The Flamingos will now face Japan in the final as they chase Nigeria's first FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup title. Japan, one of the strongest sides in women's youth football, won the tournament in 2014.

Background

The result comes at a difficult time for Nigerian women's football at senior level. The Super Falcons failed to qualify for the Women's World Cup for the first time, and no Nigerian player was shortlisted for this year's CAF Women's Player of the Year award. The Flamingos' success has been welcomed as a sign that a new generation of players is coming through.

FIFA's message

FIFA congratulated the Flamingos on qualifying for the final, a first for Nigeria at this level of the women's game.

How people reacted

Nigerians celebrated the win across social media, praising the young players and their coaches and urging the government and the football federation to support them ahead of the final.

Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

Well-wishers have been visiting Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu with gifts, money and encouragement after a viral video showed her co-wife's children refusing to spray money on her at her husband's Ozo title ceremony. Her story has reopened a conversation about how childless women are treated in some Nigerian families.

Read more

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.
