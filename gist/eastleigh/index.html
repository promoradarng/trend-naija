Moment multi-storey building under construction collapses in Nairobi's Eastleigh

23 Oct 2026, 19:21 BST

A multi-storey building still under construction came crashing down in the Eastleigh area of Nairobi, Kenya, early on Friday afternoon, sparking a frantic rescue operation amid fears that several people were trapped under the rubble. The structure stood along Yusuf Haji Avenue, next to Day to Day Supermarket on 9th Street, in one of the city's busiest commercial hubs. Reports of its height vary, with some outlets putting it at 18 floors and Nairobi police commander Issa Mohamed saying it was about 10. Videos from the scene show slabs of concrete falling and a thick cloud of dust rolling down the street as people ran for safety. Kenya Red Cross teams, Nairobi County emergency crews and police joined the search, and the county said excavators had been brought in to clear debris and reach anyone trapped. Police fired tear gas to push back crowds of onlookers and stop the looting of nearby shops. Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja reportedly confirmed that one person had been pulled out alive, and the search continued into the night. The cause of the collapse is not yet known. It comes less than a week after another building collapse on Moi Avenue in Nairobi's central business district.

  • Several people feared trapped as search goes on into the night
  • Police fire tear gas to clear onlookers and stop looting
  • Second Nairobi building collapse in under a week

Farage pledges to scrap UK graduate visa used by many Nigerian students

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Reform UK leader Nigel Farage has said his party would abolish the UK's post-study Graduate visa if it wins power. The route lets international graduates stay for two years after their degree.

Read more

Reform UK leader Nigel Farage has pledged to abolish the UK's post-study Graduate visa route if his party wins power, a move that would affect many Nigerian students who hope to work in Britain after their degrees.

What he said

In a post on X, Farage argued that the route makes it harder for young Britons to find jobs, because it allows international graduates to stay for two years without a job offer and compete for entry-level roles. He said the Conservatives created the route and Labour kept it, and that Reform would end it.

About the visa

The Graduate visa, introduced in July 2021, lets international students who complete an eligible UK degree stay to work or look for work without employer sponsorship or minimum salary requirements.

Netflix removes Tyson Fury schizophrenia claim from documentary

27 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Netflix has removed references to schizophrenia from its Tyson Fury documentary after it emerged the boxer has not been medically diagnosed with the condition. The claim had come from his wife, Paris.

Read more

Netflix has removed references to schizophrenia from its upcoming documentary about boxer Tyson Fury, after it emerged that he has not been medically diagnosed with the condition.

What changed

In the original version of the 90-minute film, Fury's wife, Paris, said doctors had regarded him as a mild schizophrenic in the year after he beat Wladimir Klitschko in November 2015. Those references have now been taken out. The documentary premieres on Netflix on Friday, 27 November 2026.

Boxing board concerns

The British Boxing Board of Control, which is sanctioning Fury's fight with Anthony Joshua on 11 December, had raised concerns about his mental health after the original statements. Its rules bar boxers from fighting if they have certain psychological disorders.

Background

Fury struggled with depression, drug and alcohol problems after the Klitschko win and did not return to the ring for nearly three years. He has spoken openly about his mental health, including bipolar disorder. An earlier story on this site said he had been diagnosed with schizophrenia; that report has been corrected.

Cleared to fight

The British Boxing Board of Control has since cleared Fury to face Anthony Joshua, saying he passed all up-to-date medical checks. His wife, Paris, has retracted her claim, saying he was never diagnosed by any doctor.

Trump calls for probe after US growth figure is revised upward

27 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

President Donald Trump has demanded an investigation after official figures revised US third-quarter growth up to 4.3%, claiming without evidence that the earlier figure was deliberately understated before the midterms.

Read more

US President Donald Trump has called for an immediate investigation into an upward revision of US economic growth figures.

The revision

On Wednesday, the US Commerce Department's Bureau of Economic Analysis revised annualised growth for the third quarter to 4.3%, up from its earlier estimate of 3.4% released on 29 October. Such revisions are a standard part of the process as more complete data becomes available.

Trump's claim

In a post on Truth Social on Thursday, 26 November 2026, Trump branded the earlier, pre-election figure fake and asked why the correct number was not released before the 3 November midterm elections. He claimed, without evidence, that the earlier figure had been deliberately understated.

Background

Trump's Republican Party suffered heavy losses in the midterms, with Democrats winning control of both the House and the Senate.

Elon Musk promises two SpaceX shares to every child in America

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Elon Musk has said every child in the United States will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from him personally. He did not explain how the plan would work.

Read more

Elon Musk has said every child in the United States will receive two shares in SpaceX, doubling a pledge he made last month.

The pledge

In a post on X on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, Musk said one share would come from the company and one from him personally. He said the company-funded share would be transferred next month, with his personal contribution following in June next year.

Questions remain

Musk did not explain how children would qualify, how the shares would be distributed or who would manage them on their behalf. He advised recipients to hold on to their shares, claiming SpaceX could one day be worth vastly more than today's global economy if it achieves its long-term goals.

Background

In October, Musk promised every American child one SpaceX share, describing it at the time as the first of many.
