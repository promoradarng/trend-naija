Moment multi-storey building under construction collapses in Nairobi's Eastleigh

23 Oct 2026, 19:21 BST

A multi-storey building still under construction came crashing down in the Eastleigh area of Nairobi, Kenya, early on Friday afternoon, sparking a frantic rescue operation amid fears that several people were trapped under the rubble. The structure stood along Yusuf Haji Avenue, next to Day to Day Supermarket on 9th Street, in one of the city's busiest commercial hubs. Reports of its height vary, with some outlets putting it at 18 floors and Nairobi police commander Issa Mohamed saying it was about 10. Videos from the scene show slabs of concrete falling and a thick cloud of dust rolling down the street as people ran for safety. Kenya Red Cross teams, Nairobi County emergency crews and police joined the search, and the county said excavators had been brought in to clear debris and reach anyone trapped. Police fired tear gas to push back crowds of onlookers and stop the looting of nearby shops. Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja reportedly confirmed that one person had been pulled out alive, and the search continued into the night. The cause of the collapse is not yet known. It comes less than a week after another building collapse on Moi Avenue in Nairobi's central business district.

  • Police fire tear gas to clear onlookers and stop looting
  • Second Nairobi building collapse in under a week

LeBron James sues AI betting app over alleged fake endorsement

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NBA star LeBron James has filed a lawsuit against AI sports betting app Bron AI, accusing it of using his identity to market a product he never endorsed.

NBA star LeBron James has sued the AI-powered sports betting app Bron AI, accusing the company of using his identity to promote a product he never endorsed.

The lawsuit

The suit, filed on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, in a New York federal court by James and his trademark company, targets PlayVision Inc., the app's parent company, and its two co-founders. It came 12 days after James sent the company a cease-and-desist letter over what he called the unauthorised use of his name, image and likeness.

The suit argues that his endorsement is uniquely valuable and accuses the company of deliberately exploiting it.

The app

Bron AI describes itself as a tool that gathers data and odds from sportsbooks and prediction markets to help users find bets and place them on other apps. A promotional video, since removed, reportedly featured a character resembling James wearing a jersey with his nickname, Bron, and his number 23.

What happens next

The app had removed the video after the cease-and-desist letter, but had not changed its name as of Tuesday evening. The company's response to the lawsuit was not included in the reports.

Ukraine strikes Crimean bridge, part of crossing collapses

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Ukraine has struck the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea, collapsing part of the road-and-rail crossing and halting road traffic. Russia also hit two major bridges in Kyiv.

Read more

Ukraine has struck the bridge linking Russia to the annexed Crimean peninsula, destroying part of the road-and-rail crossing and halting road traffic, according to reports on Tuesday, 17 November 2026.

The strike

Ukraine's SBU security service said one of the bridge's metal arches and the road span beneath it had been completely destroyed. The defence ministry said the central arch and spans had collapsed and the rail section had suffered critical damage. Images released by the SBU showed rubble and a gap several dozen metres wide between sections of the bridge.

Zelensky's message

President Volodymyr Zelensky described the strike as unprecedented and said it was a response to Russian attacks on Ukraine's infrastructure. He said Ukraine had repeatedly offered steps towards de-escalation but had been rebuffed.

Russia's attacks

Russia also attacked two major bridges in Kyiv, disrupting travel and damaging infrastructure in the Ukrainian capital. Road traffic on the Crimean bridge was suspended, and Russian-installed officials in Crimea urged residents not to panic.

Lagos-bound British Airways plane veers off taxiway at Heathrow

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

A British Airways Boeing 787 bound for Lagos reportedly veered off a taxiway at Heathrow, leaving its nose wheel stuck in the grass. The airline apologised to passengers for the disruption.

Read more

A British Airways Boeing 787 Dreamliner heading to Lagos reportedly veered off a taxiway at London's Heathrow Airport on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, leaving its nose wheel stuck in the grass.

What happened

The incident happened at about 10:10am as the aircraft was preparing for departure. Photos from the scene showed the plane's front wheel lodged in the grass beside the paved surface. No injuries were reported.

The airline's response

The incident disrupted the flight, and the airline had to make alternative arrangements for passengers. A British Airways spokesperson apologised to customers and said its teams were working to get them on their way as quickly as possible and were in touch with them.

What happens next

It was not immediately clear what caused the aircraft to leave the taxiway.
