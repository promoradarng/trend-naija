Naira Marley announces Marlians Fest comeback for 12 December

9 Nov 2026, 06:44 GMT

Singer Naira Marley has announced that his Marlians Fest concert is returning this December.

What he announced

In a long post on his social media pages on Sunday, 8 November 2026, the Marlian Records founder said the show will hold on 12 December at the Eagles Club Field in Surulere, Lagos, as part of the 'Detty December' season. He spoke about a difficult period in his life without going into detail, saying rumours and negative stories had circulated about him and that he had deliberately stepped back to find peace and heal. He described the concert as an event about growth, healing and moving forward, and dedicated it partly to everyone 'we have lost along the way'. He said friends would join him on stage and thanked supporters who stood by him.

Background

Naira Marley became one of Nigeria's most popular street-pop stars with songs such as 'Soapy' and 'Issa Goal'. He faced intense public criticism after the death of his former signee, Mohbad, in September 2023, when questions were raised about Mohbad's earlier complaints against the label. Naira Marley was arrested and questioned by police over the death and was later released; he has denied any wrongdoing, and no charges against him have been reported in connection with Mohbad's death. A coroner's inquest was held into the circumstances. This week, VeryDarkMan has also revived discussion of the Mohbad case during his feud with Davido, while Davido's aide denied any link between the singer and Naira Marley's troubles.

How people reacted

Davido was among those who liked the announcement on Instagram. Fans were divided, with some welcoming his return and others saying the wounds from Mohbad's death were still fresh.

Portable says he received ₦100 million after campaigning for APC

Singer Portable has claimed he woke up to ₦100 million after taking part in campaigning for the APC, without saying who sent it. The claim has drawn criticism online amid economic hardship.

Read more

Street-pop singer Portable has stirred debate online after claiming he received ₦100 million following his involvement in campaigns for the All Progressives Congress (APC).

What he said

In a post shared on Friday, 13 November 2026, the singer, whose real name is Habeeb Okikiola, said he simply woke up and found ₦100 million had been paid to him. He did not say who sent the money or what it was for. Earlier reports had linked the payment to an APC governorship candidate in Ogun State, but this has not been confirmed.

How people reacted

Many social media users criticised the claim, contrasting it with the economic hardship facing ordinary Nigerians and questioning whether celebrity endorsements actually influence voters.

Background

Portable has openly supported President Bola Tinubu and the APC. The APC has not commented on his claim.

Tems to lead soundtrack for Hollywood film Children of Blood and Bone

13 Nov 2026, 15:50 GMT

Tems is executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood adaptation of Tomi Adeyemi's novel 'Children of Blood and Bone', featuring Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Fireboy DML, BNXN and others. The album is out on 20 November.

Grammy-winning singer Tems has been named executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone'.

The soundtrack

The announcement was made on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026. The album brings together several of Africa's biggest stars, including Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Fireboy DML, BNXN and Kold AF. Tems described the project as a profound honour and said she was excited for fans to hear what she had put together. The soundtrack is due for release on 20 November, ahead of the film's cinema debut.

The film

'Children of Blood and Bone' is directed by Gina Prince-Bythewood and adapted from the bestselling fantasy novel by Nigerian-American author Tomi Adeyemi.

Background

Tems has become one of the most successful Nigerian artists internationally, winning Grammy Awards and earning an Oscar nomination for co-writing 'Lift Me Up' for 'Black Panther: Wakanda Forever'.

After 20 years of marriage, Kwankwaso's daughter Zainab files for divorce from Kano Governor Abba Yusuf, offers to refund dowry

14 Nov 2026, 17:44 GMT

Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, daughter of Senator Rabiu Kwankwaso, has reportedly filed for divorce from her husband, Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf, at an Upper Area Court in Abuja, and offered to refund her dowry. The governor has been summoned to appear on 17 November.

Read more

Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, daughter of former Kano governor and senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, has reportedly filed for divorce from her husband, Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf, after 20 years of marriage.

The court case

The case was filed at the Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja. A civil summons directs Governor Yusuf to appear in court on Tuesday, 17 November 2026.

Zainab is reported to be seeking khul'i, a process under Islamic law that allows a wife to end her marriage, and has offered to refund the dowry Yusuf paid when they married.

The summons is not a divorce order, and the court has yet to rule on the matter.

Her reasons

According to the filing, Zainab cited a long absence of mutual care, love and emotional connection in the marriage, and said reconciliation efforts had failed.

The couple married on 29 July 2006, when Zainab was working as an aide to her father, who was then governor of Kano State. They have six children.

The political backdrop

The marriage once symbolised the close bond between Kwankwaso and Yusuf, his long-time political protégé. That relationship has since broken down.

In January 2026, Governor Yusuf left Kwankwaso's party, the New Nigeria Peoples Party (NNPP), for the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC), a move Kwankwaso's camp described as a betrayal.

Some reports have linked the political split to tension in the marriage, but the reasons given in the court filing focus on the breakdown of the relationship itself.

No comment yet

As of Saturday, neither Governor Yusuf, Zainab nor Senator Kwankwaso had made a public statement on the divorce, and the Kano State Government had not commented.
