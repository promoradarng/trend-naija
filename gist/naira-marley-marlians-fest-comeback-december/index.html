Naira Marley announces Marlians Fest comeback for 12 December

9 Nov 2026, 06:44 GMT

Singer Naira Marley has announced that his Marlians Fest concert is returning this December.

What he announced

In a long post on his social media pages on Sunday, 8 November 2026, the Marlian Records founder said the show will hold on 12 December at the Eagles Club Field in Surulere, Lagos, as part of the 'Detty December' season. He spoke about a difficult period in his life without going into detail, saying rumours and negative stories had circulated about him and that he had deliberately stepped back to find peace and heal. He described the concert as an event about growth, healing and moving forward, and dedicated it partly to everyone 'we have lost along the way'. He said friends would join him on stage and thanked supporters who stood by him.

Background

Naira Marley became one of Nigeria's most popular street-pop stars with songs such as 'Soapy' and 'Issa Goal'. He faced intense public criticism after the death of his former signee, Mohbad, in September 2023, when questions were raised about Mohbad's earlier complaints against the label. Naira Marley was arrested and questioned by police over the death and was later released; he has denied any wrongdoing, and no charges against him have been reported in connection with Mohbad's death. A coroner's inquest was held into the circumstances. This week, VeryDarkMan has also revived discussion of the Mohbad case during his feud with Davido, while Davido's aide denied any link between the singer and Naira Marley's troubles.

How people reacted

Davido was among those who liked the announcement on Instagram. Fans were divided, with some welcoming his return and others saying the wounds from Mohbad's death were still fresh.

  • Marlians Fest returns on 12 December in Surulere.
  • Naira Marley says he stepped back to heal.
  • Davido liked the announcement.

Former Kaduna State Governor Nasir El-Rufai has reportedly told a Federal High Court in Abuja that he never intercepted the telephone conversations of National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu, and never asked anyone to do so for him.

In court

El-Rufai opened his defence on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, before Justice Joyce Abdulmalik, after his no-case submission was dismissed in October. He also denied having any access to the NSA's communication networks or personal devices.

How the case began

The case arises from remarks El-Rufai made in a television interview on 13 February 2026. He denies the charges.

What happens next

The trial continues as El-Rufai presents his defence.

Seyi Law admits his finances were better before Tinubu, but backs reforms

25 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Comedian Seyi Law has reportedly admitted that living standards have dropped under President Tinubu and that his own finances were better before 2023. He still defended the government's reforms, saying good policies take years to stabilise.

Read more

Comedian Seyi Law, a known supporter of President Bola Tinubu, has reportedly admitted that living standards have fallen sharply under the current administration.

What he said

Speaking on a podcast hosted by Nedu, Seyi Law is said to have acknowledged that his personal finances were much better before Tinubu took office in 2023, and that it would be hypocritical to say Nigerians are not suffering.

Still backing the reforms

He nevertheless defended the government's economic reforms, arguing that Nigerians would benefit in the long run. Comparing Nigeria with China, he said major national transformations often come with heavy early sacrifices. When the host asked whether that meant Nigerians must suffer greatly first, he said people must feel the pain of a policy before reaping its benefits, adding that good policies usually stabilise after 10 to 12 years.

How people reacted

His remarks have sparked debate online.
