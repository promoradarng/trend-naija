Doris Ogala reportedly taken to hospital after losing consciousness in detention

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Nollywood actress Doris Ogala, the African Action Congress (AAC) governorship candidate in Abia State, has reportedly been taken to hospital from detention.

What was said

On Thursday, 5 November 2026, a person close to the actress shared a video online saying he had spoken to her by phone while she was receiving treatment in hospital. According to him, she said she had lost consciousness, was bleeding and could not remember how she had been taken to the hospital. In the same video, Ogala could reportedly be heard speaking about her condition and raising concerns about bleeding linked to an earlier surgical procedure. The authorities have not publicly commented on her health.

Background

Ogala was remanded in custody in Lagos in late October after being arraigned before a Federal High Court on three counts linked to a complaint by the cleric Pastor Chris Okafor. The charges relate to the alleged publication of photographs, alleged false electronic messages and alleged threats. She has not been convicted of anything, and has described the case as an attempt to stop her governorship bid. At a later sitting she apologised to the court for her conduct at an earlier hearing, and the judge fixed Friday, 6 November, for a ruling on her bail application. Earlier this week, a medical report shared online said she had been treated in June for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters have expressed concern and called for her release on health grounds, urging the authorities to make sure she receives proper medical care while the case continues.

What happens next

Attention is now on the court's decision on her bail application.

  • Ogala reportedly blacked out and was bleeding.
  • She has been in custody since late October.
  • Her bail ruling was due on Friday.

Jim Iyke finally shows off his wife after almost five years together

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has shared photos and videos of his wife for the first time, revealing how they met on a flight to Houston almost five years ago.

Read more

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has introduced his wife to the public for the first time, sharing loved-up photos and videos of the couple on Instagram.

How they met

In the post, the actor said they met almost five years ago in an airport lounge before a flight to Houston. He recalled asking if the seat next to her was free, and said she firmly told him it was taken, but he sat down anyway. They ignored each other for an hour, then burst out laughing when they realised they were on the same flight.

His tribute

Jim Iyke described their relationship as something sacred rather than hidden, and said they have built a family together and travelled to eight countries. He called her his best friend, his prayer warrior and his wife, and said they have never stopped laughing together.

Femi Branch opens up on two years out of work after on-set accident

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Actor Femi Branch says a stunt that went wrong on a film set in Ikorodu left him unable to work for two years, forcing him to sell his late mother's car. He said some colleagues abandoned him.

Read more

Nollywood actor Femi Branch has opened up about a difficult period in his life after a serious accident on a film set left him unable to work for two years.

The accident

In an interview that surfaced online on Friday, 20 November 2026, Branch said he was injured while shooting a stunt in Ikorodu, Lagos. He said he had to jump from a storey building while dressed as a security guard, and the impact when he landed was so severe it sounded like an explosion.

Hard times

He said the injury kept him out of work for two years, leaving him isolated and in financial difficulty. He revealed that he had to sell his mother's car during that period, and said some colleagues deserted him while he was recovering.
