Doris Ogala reportedly taken to hospital after losing consciousness in detention

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Nollywood actress Doris Ogala, the African Action Congress (AAC) governorship candidate in Abia State, has reportedly been taken to hospital from detention.

What was said

On Thursday, 5 November 2026, a person close to the actress shared a video online saying he had spoken to her by phone while she was receiving treatment in hospital. According to him, she said she had lost consciousness, was bleeding and could not remember how she had been taken to the hospital. In the same video, Ogala could reportedly be heard speaking about her condition and raising concerns about bleeding linked to an earlier surgical procedure. The authorities have not publicly commented on her health.

Background

Ogala was remanded in custody in Lagos in late October after being arraigned before a Federal High Court on three counts linked to a complaint by the cleric Pastor Chris Okafor. The charges relate to the alleged publication of photographs, alleged false electronic messages and alleged threats. She has not been convicted of anything, and has described the case as an attempt to stop her governorship bid. At a later sitting she apologised to the court for her conduct at an earlier hearing, and the judge fixed Friday, 6 November, for a ruling on her bail application. Earlier this week, a medical report shared online said she had been treated in June for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters have expressed concern and called for her release on health grounds, urging the authorities to make sure she receives proper medical care while the case continues.

What happens next

Attention is now on the court's decision on her bail application.

  • Ogala reportedly blacked out and was bleeding.
  • She has been in custody since late October.
  • Her bail ruling was due on Friday.

Media personality Denrele Edun has announced the death of his father, Edun Adekunle Alaba, sharing the news on what would have been the late patriarch's 85th birthday.

What he shared

Denrele said his father, who was born on 17 November 1941, died on Monday, 12 October 2026, after a brief illness, just weeks before the family had planned to celebrate his birthday in a big way.

Tribute

In a moving tribute, Denrele described his father as a man whose faith was part of his everyday life, not just his Sunday routine. He was a church elder, a warden and a bass singer in the choir of the Methodist church.

With his usual humour, Denrele recalled how his father insisted that he and his siblings join the church choir, even though their singing voices were limited.

Iheme Nancy returns to church after four years, credits Mercy Eke

Actress Iheme Nancy says she has gone back to church for the first time in four years, after a painful experience in 2022 made her walk away. She credited BBNaija star Mercy Eke for persuading her to attend.

Nollywood actress Iheme Nancy has opened up about returning to church after four years away, crediting reality TV star Mercy Eke for bringing her back.

What she shared

In a post shared on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Nancy said a painful experience within the church in 2022, which she described as a betrayal by fellow Christians, made her walk away from her faith completely after the death of her mother.

She said Mercy Eke contacted her on Saturday and insisted that she would be attending Harvesters Church with her the next morning, promising she would not regret it. Mercy then checked on her at around 9am on Sunday to make sure she was getting ready.

At the service

Nancy admitted she was reluctant and in a bad mood, but went anyway. She said she was flustered when Mercy walked straight to the front pews, close to the pulpit, at the church led by Pastor Bolaji Idowu.

VDM praised as Oyo corps members finally receive delayed allowance

Oyo State Batch 25C corps members have reportedly received their delayed final ₦77,000 allowance, two days after VeryDarkMan raised the alarm. He shared a video of a corps member celebrating the payment.

Corps members of the Oyo State Batch 25C have reportedly received their long-delayed final allowance, after social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), whose real name is Martins Vincent Otse, publicly called out the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).

What happened

On Wednesday, 18 November 2026, VDM shared a video on Instagram showing a corps member celebrating after receiving a credit alert and thanking him for taking up the matter.

How it started

VDM first raised the issue on 16 November, after receiving messages from more than 100 affected corps members. He said they had completed their service and attended their passing-out parade at the end of October, but had not been paid their final ₦77,000 allowance. He called on the NYSC Director-General to act.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised the activist for taking up the corps members' complaint.
