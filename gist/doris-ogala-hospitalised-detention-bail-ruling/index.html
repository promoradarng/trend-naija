Doris Ogala reportedly taken to hospital after losing consciousness in detention

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Nollywood actress Doris Ogala, the African Action Congress (AAC) governorship candidate in Abia State, has reportedly been taken to hospital from detention.

What was said

On Thursday, 5 November 2026, a person close to the actress shared a video online saying he had spoken to her by phone while she was receiving treatment in hospital. According to him, she said she had lost consciousness, was bleeding and could not remember how she had been taken to the hospital. In the same video, Ogala could reportedly be heard speaking about her condition and raising concerns about bleeding linked to an earlier surgical procedure. The authorities have not publicly commented on her health.

Background

Ogala was remanded in custody in Lagos in late October after being arraigned before a Federal High Court on three counts linked to a complaint by the cleric Pastor Chris Okafor. The charges relate to the alleged publication of photographs, alleged false electronic messages and alleged threats. She has not been convicted of anything, and has described the case as an attempt to stop her governorship bid. At a later sitting she apologised to the court for her conduct at an earlier hearing, and the judge fixed Friday, 6 November, for a ruling on her bail application. Earlier this week, a medical report shared online said she had been treated in June for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters have expressed concern and called for her release on health grounds, urging the authorities to make sure she receives proper medical care while the case continues.

What happens next

Attention is now on the court's decision on her bail application.

  • Ogala reportedly blacked out and was bleeding.
  • She has been in custody since late October.
  • Her bail ruling was due on Friday.

IK Ogbonna and friends remember Alexx Ekubo six months after his death

12 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Actor IK Ogbonna, businessman Vintage Luxury and the late actor's sister have shared emotional tributes to Alexx Ekubo, six months after he died of kidney cancer at the age of 40.

Read more

Friends and family of the late Nollywood actor Alexx Ekubo have shared emotional tributes to mark six months since his death.

The tributes

On Thursday, 12 November 2026, actor IK Ogbonna, widely regarded as Ekubo's closest friend, posted a throwback video of the two of them on Instagram with a simple heart emoji, saying in a separate post that six months still felt like yesterday. Businessman Vintage Luxury also shared a tribute describing his late friend as rare, while Ekubo's sister posted a message saying she missed him more than life.

His death

Alexx Ekubo died on Monday, 11 May 2026, at a hospital in Lagos after complications from advanced kidney cancer. He was 40. His death shocked Nollywood, and tributes poured in from colleagues, fans and public figures. He was buried on 18 June 2026 in Arochukwu, Abia State, in a ceremony attended by many figures from the entertainment industry.

Background

Ekubo was one of Nollywood's best-known leading men and was known for his close friendship with IK Ogbonna.

BBNaija's Faith builds filter game for Funke Akindele's new film The Four

11 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Reality star Dr Faith Adewale has created a Snapchat filter game inspired by Funke Akindele's upcoming film The Four, complete with a ticket-booking button. Funke Akindele reacted warmly to the fan-made promotion.

Read more

Big Brother Naija star Dr Faith Adewale has won praise online after creating a game to promote Funke Akindele's upcoming film, The Four.

The game

In a video shared on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, Faith showed how the Snapchat filter works. Players are paired with three of their real friends and must complete quick physical challenges, such as tapping the screen rapidly or raising their eyebrows, to 'rescue' each one. When all three are saved, the group of four is reunited, echoing the theme of the film. Faith set a time of 15.4 seconds and challenged her followers to beat it. The filter also includes a button that links to a cinema website where users can buy tickets.

Funke reacts

Funke Akindele, who directs and stars in the film, spotted the post and responded with delight, and the cinema chain also celebrated the idea online.

Background

The Four is due in Nigerian cinemas on 11 December 2026, in time for the festive season. Funke Akindele is one of Nollywood's most commercially successful filmmakers, with several of her previous December releases breaking box office records.
