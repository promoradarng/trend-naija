Doris Ogala reportedly taken to hospital after losing consciousness in detention

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Nollywood actress Doris Ogala, the African Action Congress (AAC) governorship candidate in Abia State, has reportedly been taken to hospital from detention.

What was said

On Thursday, 5 November 2026, a person close to the actress shared a video online saying he had spoken to her by phone while she was receiving treatment in hospital. According to him, she said she had lost consciousness, was bleeding and could not remember how she had been taken to the hospital. In the same video, Ogala could reportedly be heard speaking about her condition and raising concerns about bleeding linked to an earlier surgical procedure. The authorities have not publicly commented on her health.

Background

Ogala was remanded in custody in Lagos in late October after being arraigned before a Federal High Court on three counts linked to a complaint by the cleric Pastor Chris Okafor. The charges relate to the alleged publication of photographs, alleged false electronic messages and alleged threats. She has not been convicted of anything, and has described the case as an attempt to stop her governorship bid. At a later sitting she apologised to the court for her conduct at an earlier hearing, and the judge fixed Friday, 6 November, for a ruling on her bail application. Earlier this week, a medical report shared online said she had been treated in June for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters have expressed concern and called for her release on health grounds, urging the authorities to make sure she receives proper medical care while the case continues.

What happens next

Attention is now on the court's decision on her bail application.

  • Ogala reportedly blacked out and was bleeding.
  • She has been in custody since late October.
  • Her bail ruling was due on Friday.

Court strikes out ₦6bn money-laundering charges against Mompha, socialite says

Read more

Socialite Ismaila Mustapha, popularly known as Mompha, says a court has struck out the money-laundering charges brought against him by the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC).

What he said

In an Instagram post on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Mompha said the court struck out the ₦6 billion charges on the grounds that they amounted to an abuse of court process and forum shopping. He described the outcome as personal vindication after what he called years of harassment, called the prosecution a witch-hunt, praised the judge and thanked his supporters. He also mocked the anti-graft agency in the post.

Background

Mompha's legal battle with the EFCC began in 2019, when he was arrested after returning to Nigeria. He has always denied the allegations, saying he came home to invest. The case dragged on for about seven years. The EFCC had not publicly commented on the ruling at the time of writing, and it was not immediately clear whether the commission would appeal or file fresh charges.

What it means

Mompha says his legal ordeal is now over.

Bimbo Ademoye rejects apology from man whose copyright claim hit her YouTube film

10 Nov 2026, 17:47 GMT

Actress Bimbo Ademoye says she will take action against a man who uploaded the soundtrack of her film 'My 30th Wedding' to streaming platforms, triggering a copyright claim that demonetised the film on YouTube, despite his public apology.

Read more

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Bimbo Ademoye has rejected an apology from a man whose copyright claim affected her YouTube film.

What happened

According to reports on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, the man uploaded the soundtrack from Ademoye's film 'My 30th Wedding' to a music streaming platform, which triggered an automatic copyright claim on the film on her YouTube channel, cutting off its earnings. He later posted an apology on Instagram, accepting responsibility and saying he had taken the song down from all platforms and that the claim should clear within 24 to 48 hours.

Her response

Ademoye shared a screenshot of the apology and said she was not satisfied with his explanation, vowing to take action. She also questioned why he had chosen that particular song from the film rather than a more popular one, suggesting it was because the artist behind the other song would not have tolerated it.

Background

Ademoye is one of Nollywood's most popular actresses and has built a large audience for films released on YouTube. Automated copyright systems such as YouTube's Content ID have become a common source of disputes for Nigerian filmmakers, as false or mistaken claims can redirect earnings away from creators.
