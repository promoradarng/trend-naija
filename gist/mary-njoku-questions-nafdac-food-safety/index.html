Mary Njoku questions NAFDAC chief over unsafe food products

4 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Mary Njoku has publicly questioned the head of the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC), Professor Mojisola Adeyeye, over continuing concerns about fake and unsafe products in Nigeria.

What she said

In a social media post on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, Njoku recalled the NAFDAC chief's laughter during a recent food safety controversy, which she said she would never forget, and asked whether Nigerians are actually safe now. She criticised what she described as a familiar Nigerian cycle in which people raise an alarm, nothing is resolved, and attention moves to the next controversy. She also asked whether ordinary people now need special expertise to know whether their food is safe.

Background

Food safety has been a major public concern in Nigeria this year, with reports of fake and expired products, altered expiry dates and contaminated foods. This week, police in Kano arrested traders accused of changing expiry dates on milk, and a processor in Ondo was arrested over unhygienic food handling. NAFDAC has carried out raids and destroyed large quantities of unsafe products, but critics say enforcement remains weak.

Who is Mary Njoku

Mary Njoku is an actress, producer and the founder of the ROK Studios film company, which she sold to the French broadcaster Canal+ in 2019. She remains one of Nollywood's most influential figures.

How people reacted

Many Nigerians agreed with her, sharing their own fears about unsafe products, while others urged consumers to report suspicious goods to the agency. NAFDAC had not responded publicly at the time of reporting.

  • Njoku asked whether Nigerians are now safe from unsafe products.
  • She criticised a cycle of outrage with no results.
  • NAFDAC has not responded.

Osita Iheme and I Go Save tell politicians: show us results, not dance moves

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Actor Osita Iheme and comedian I Go Save have criticised politicians who dance and show off their fitness at campaign rallies, saying Nigerians want competence, manifestos and results instead.

Read more

Two well-known entertainers have hit out at politicians who turn campaign rallies into dance shows ahead of the 2027 elections.

Osita Iheme

In an Instagram post on Monday, 16 November 2026, Nollywood actor Osita Iheme, popularly known as Pawpaw, urged politicians to spend their energy demonstrating competence rather than choreography. He said Nigerians want results, vision and accountability from their leaders, not entertainment, and issued what read as a warning to politicians to take voters seriously.

I Go Save

Comedian I Go Save also questioned why candidates increasingly run, jump and dance at public events instead of addressing national issues. He wondered whether the displays were meant to prove physical fitness or had become a new form of competition between candidates, and said they should present credible manifestos and practical ideas instead.

Background

Other celebrities have recently used their platforms to criticise politicians over roads and governance. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Toyin Abraham steps in to stop her police escorts roughing up area boys

15 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

A video shows actress Toyin Abraham intervening on a Lagos street to stop her police escorts from manhandling a group of area boys, calming the situation.

Read more

Nollywood actress Toyin Abraham has drawn attention after a video showed her stepping in to stop her police escorts from roughing up a group of young men on a Lagos street.

What happened

In the footage, which spread on X after the incident on Saturday, 14 November 2026, the actress walks into a crowded scene involving the young men and onlookers and calmly but firmly tells her escorts to back off. The situation quickly calmed down after her intervention.

The context

The encounter reportedly happened during a film production. Groups of area boys, often called agberos, are known to approach film crews shooting in public places in Lagos and demand payment before allowing work to continue, which can lead to tense confrontations.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised the actress for preventing the situation from escalating, while others debated the use of police escorts by celebrities. The police recently opened an investigation after a video showed officers escorting businessman Blord.
