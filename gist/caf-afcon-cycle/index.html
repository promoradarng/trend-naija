Former England winger Raheem Sterling has avoided an immediate prison term after a court handed him a 12-month sentence, suspended for 18 months, for dangerous driving.

The sentence

The sentence was handed down on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, at Basingstoke Magistrates' Court. The judge said the 31-year-old had shown a conscious disregard for the safety of other road users.

What happened

The court heard that Sterling drove his Lamborghini on the M25 and M3 motorways during the morning rush hour on 28 May. Background

CAS restores Senegal as AFCON 2025 champions, overturning CAF ruling

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Court of Arbitration for Sport has confirmed Senegal as winners of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations. It overturned in full the CAF Appeal Board decision that had handed the title to Morocco.

Read more

Senegal are once again Africa's champions after the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) overturned the decision that had stripped them of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations title.

The verdict

CAS delivered its ruling on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, reversing in full the decision of CAF's Appeal Board, which had awarded the title to hosts Morocco. With the verdict, Senegal are officially recognised as the 2025 AFCON champions.

How it started

Senegal beat Morocco 1-0 after extra time in the final at the Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium in Rabat on 18 January 2026, with Pape Gueye scoring the only goal. During the match, Senegal's players walked off the pitch for about 15 minutes in protest at a late penalty awarded to Morocco, which was later saved.

The Royal Moroccan Football Federation then took legal action, and on 17 March CAF's Appeal Board ruled that Senegal had forfeited the match, awarding Morocco a 3-0 win and the trophy. The Senegalese Football Federation refused to accept the decision and appealed to CAS.

What it means

The ruling ends months of legal uncertainty over the continent's top football title. Before the verdict, CAF President Patrice Motsepe had said the body would accept whatever CAS decided.

Morocco to fight on

The Royal Moroccan Football Federation said it rejected the ruling and would challenge it through every legal channel available, citing what it described as procedural failures. Senegal star Sadio Mane welcomed the decision.

CAF responds

In a short statement, CAF acknowledged the ruling and pledged to act on it. The trophy, medals and prize money were never taken back from Senegal during the dispute and will remain with the team.

Reps urge Tinubu to consider state of emergency in Delta if violence worsens

The House of Representatives has asked President Tinubu to closely monitor security in Delta State, warning that a state of emergency may become necessary if political violence continues. It also told the police to set up a special investigative team.

The House of Representatives has called on President Bola Tinubu to closely watch the security situation in Delta State, warning that a state of emergency may be needed if conditions keep getting worse.

The motion

The resolution on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, followed a motion by Representative Benedict Etanabene, who pointed to a series of politically motivated attacks in the state. Lawmakers expressed concern that the violence was spreading fear and threatening peace among communities.

Directive to the police

The House also directed the Inspector-General of Police to identify flashpoints across the state and set up a special team to investigate the attacks.

Background

The resolution comes two days after gunmen attacked an NDC campaign event led by former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege in Uduere, Ughelli North, killing several people. Police have invited Omo-Agege and others for questioning, and the Inspector-General has ordered an investigation. Delta Governor Sheriff Oborevwori has called for everyone involved to be arrested, whatever their party.

President Bola Tinubu has congratulated Atiku Abubakar, the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate and former Vice President, on his 80th birthday.

The message

In a statement released on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by his Special Adviser on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, the president said the milestone deserved recognition. He noted that despite their different political paths, he wanted to acknowledge the history they share and Atiku's contributions to Nigeria.

Tinubu also recalled supporting Atiku's presidential bid in 2007.

Other well-wishers

Businessman Tein Jack-Rich was among others who sent birthday messages to the former vice president, praising his leadership and resilience.

Background

The two men are now rivals in the January 2027 presidential election, and their campaigns have traded sharp words in recent weeks.
