President Tinubu seen kissing the sacred Black Stone during Umrah in Makkah

2 Nov 2026, 20:39 GMT

President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has been seen kissing the sacred Black Stone, known as Al-Hajar Al-Aswad, while performing Umrah in the holy city of Makkah, Saudi Arabia.

Photos from the visit show the President, dressed in white, leaning into the silver frame that holds the stone as Saudi security officers stand by. Another photo shows him with members of his delegation in the courtyard of the Grand Mosque.

About the Black Stone

The Black Stone sits at the eastern corner of the Kaaba, inside the Grand Mosque, and holds deep religious significance for Muslims around the world.

Kissing the stone is a practice associated with Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), and many pilgrims seek the chance to do so whenever they visit the holy site.

A spiritual journey

For Muslims, Umrah is a spiritual journey marked by prayer, worship and personal reflection.

The moment has also drawn attention to the religious side of Nigeria's leader as he performs one of Islam's important acts of worship.

Tinubu endorses APC governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos at Ibadan rally, promises better power

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

At an APC rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, President Tinubu raised the hands of the party's governorship candidates for Oyo, Ogun and Lagos. He also guaranteed improved electricity and promised a factory in Ibadan.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos States at a campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The endorsements

Telling the crowd he would show them who to vote for as governor, Tinubu raised the hands of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC candidate in Oyo; Senator Solomon Adeola, known as Yayi, the party's candidate in Ogun; and Lagos Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, the APC flagbearer in Lagos. He also raised the hand of Remi Oseni, the party's senatorial candidate for Oyo South.

Promise on power

During the visit, the President also assured Nigerians of better electricity supply, saying the Minister of Power and his team were working hard. He added that the government would build a factory in Ibadan.

Context

The rally is part of the APC's campaign ahead of the 16 January 2027 elections.

The other side

Reactions from the opposition parties to the rally were not included in the reports.

Sowore makes surprise Onitsha market visit, says 'no rented crowds'

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore says he received a spontaneous welcome at Onitsha's Ogbo Ogwu market, a day after Peter Obi campaigned in the city. He also criticised the partition on the Niger Bridge.

Read more

African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has made a surprise visit to the Ogbo Ogwu medicine market in Onitsha, Anambra State, where he says traders gave him a warm welcome.

The visit

Sowore shared details of the visit on X on Saturday, 21 November 2026. He said he walked across the First Niger Bridge and arrived at the market without any prior announcement.

He described the reception as spontaneous and genuine, insisting that no crowds had been hired or organised for his arrival, and promised to return.

Bridge complaint

During the visit, Sowore criticised Works Minister David Umahi over a partition installed on the Niger Bridge, claiming it was disrupting traffic and causing hardship for commuters.

Context

His visit came a day after NDC candidate Peter Obi held a campaign event in Onitsha.

Former senator Aishatu Binani announces death of her father

Former Adamawa governorship candidate and ex-senator Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, known as Binani, has announced the death of her father after a brief illness. His funeral prayer was fixed for Saturday at the Central Mosque.

Read more

Former senator and Adamawa State governorship candidate Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, popularly known as Binani, has announced the death of her father.

The announcement

In a post on social media, Binani said her father died after a brief illness in Abuja. She opened her message with the Islamic expression of condolence and thanked God despite the loss.

Funeral arrangements

She said the funeral prayer, known as Jana'iza, would take place on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the Central Mosque after the afternoon Zuhr prayer.

Who she is

Binani is a former senator who contested the Adamawa governorship election in 2023. She is now a member of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

A political group has announced a presidential debate in Abuja on 10 January 2027, six days before the election, inviting Tinubu, Atiku, Peter Obi, Seyi Makinde, Donald Duke and Adewole Adebayo.

Six presidential candidates have reportedly been invited to a policy debate in Abuja on 10 January 2027, six days before the presidential election.

The announcement

The convener of the organising group, Lai Omotola, announced the plan at a press conference in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. He said the debate is designed to push the candidates beyond campaign slogans and into detailed discussions of their policies.

Who is invited

The six candidates invited are President Bola Tinubu of the APC, Atiku Abubakar of the ADC, Peter Obi of the NDC, Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde, former Cross River governor Donald Duke and the SDP's Adewole Adebayo.

Will they attend?

It was not clear from the reports whether all the candidates have confirmed they will take part.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
