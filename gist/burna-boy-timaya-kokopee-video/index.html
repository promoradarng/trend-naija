Video of Burna Boy chatting with Timaya while Kokopee stands aside sparks debate

6 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

A video of Burna Boy in conversation with veteran singer Timaya has gone viral, with fans focusing on VeryDarkMan's friend Kokopee, who was standing nearby.

What the video shows

The clip, which began circulating on Thursday, 5 November 2026, shows Burna Boy and Timaya running into each other at an event and chatting. Kokopee, widely known as the best friend of activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), is seen standing a short distance away with his arms folded, watching the exchange. Burna Boy appears not to acknowledge him. At one point Kokopee seemed to move towards Burna Boy, but a bouncer reportedly stepped in before he could get close.

Why it got attention

Burna Boy has reportedly supported VDM's charity projects, and the two have been seen as friendly, so many fans asked whether the singer had deliberately ignored Kokopee. The video comes during VDM's public feud with Davido, which has drawn in several other celebrities this week. Kokopee has also been mentioned in the feud.

How people reacted

Opinions were divided. Some fans said Burna Boy had clearly snubbed Kokopee, while others said the moment was being over-analysed and that the singer may simply not have noticed him in a busy event. Neither Burna Boy nor Kokopee had publicly commented at the time of writing.

  • Kokopee stood nearby and was reportedly stopped by a bouncer.

Ayra Starr confirmed for Coachella 2027 on SZA-headlined Sunday

11 Nov 2026, 17:46 GMT

Ayra Starr will perform at the 2027 Coachella festival in California, appearing on the Sunday bill of both weekends, which SZA headlines. The singer told fans: 'See you in the desert.'

Read more

Afrobeats star Ayra Starr has been announced as one of the performers at the 2027 Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival.

The line-up

The festival's organisers revealed the line-up on Instagram on Wednesday, 11 November 2026. Ayra Starr is on the Sunday bill for both weekends and is expected to perform on 11 and 18 April 2027. The Sunday shows are headlined by American R&B star SZA, with Don Toliver, Kygo, Jorja Smith, Victoria Monét and Tove Lo among the other acts. Dua Lipa is among the festival's other headliners. Coachella takes place at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, from 9 to 11 April and 16 to 18 April.

Her reaction

The singer shared the news on her own Instagram page with a short message telling fans she would see them in the desert.

Background

Ayra Starr, signed to Mavin Records, rose to international fame with hits such as 'Rush' and has become one of the most successful young Nigerian artists of her generation. Coachella is one of the world's biggest music festivals.

Usher and Chris Brown's Los Angeles concert reportedly cancelled amid feud

11 Nov 2026, 17:46 GMT

Usher and Chris Brown's 16 November show at SoFi Stadium has reportedly been marked as cancelled on Ticketmaster, with fans told they will be refunded. It is unclear whether it is linked to the singers' backstage row in Atlanta.

Read more

A Los Angeles concert by R&B stars Usher and Chris Brown has reportedly been cancelled.

What happened

According to reports on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, the pair's 16 November show at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, was marked as cancelled on Ticketmaster, and ticket holders were told they would receive refunds. No reason was given. Their 15 November show at the same venue, and their remaining Atlanta dates, were still listed as going ahead.

The feud

It is not clear whether the cancellation is linked to the reported argument between the two singers before their Atlanta concert last week. Usher reportedly wanted to start that show on time, while Brown wanted to wait for fans stuck in traffic, and the disagreement was said to have delayed the performance by almost three hours. Sources quoted in reports say the two have still not met to settle their differences and describe the relationship as frosty.

Background

The two stars are on a joint tour. Neither singer had publicly commented on the cancellation at the time of writing.

Nigerian-born lawyer Shirley Emehelu becomes New Jersey's lieutenant governor

11 Nov 2026, 17:46 GMT

Nigerian-born lawyer Shirley Emehelu has been appointed lieutenant governor of the US state of New Jersey, becoming the first immigrant to hold the post. She will also serve as Secretary of State.

Read more

Shirley U. Emehelu, a lawyer born in Nigeria, has been appointed lieutenant governor of New Jersey in the United States.

The appointment

New Jersey Governor Mikie Sherrill appointed Emehelu on Monday, 9 November 2026, making her the state's fifth lieutenant governor and the first immigrant to hold the office. She was sworn in at the Statehouse in Trenton by the state's Chief Justice, Stuart Rabner. The appointment also means that, for the first time in New Jersey's history, women hold the state's two highest executive offices at the same time. Emehelu will also serve as Secretary of State, a role that covers government administration and oversight of elections.

Why she was chosen

Sherrill described Emehelu as an experienced public servant and pointed to her record prosecuting financial crimes, protecting consumers, defending civil rights and tackling government waste. The governor also praised her leadership as acting Secretary of State, including overseeing the state's recent elections.

Her Nigerian roots

Emehelu, who moved from Nigeria to the United States as a child, said she was proud of what the appointment means for Black women, immigrants and people who share her Nigerian heritage, and expressed hope that it would inspire young people to serve. She said she would work with the governor on the cost of living and economic opportunity.
