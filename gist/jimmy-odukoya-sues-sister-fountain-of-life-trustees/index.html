Court orders arrest of Jimmy Odukoya in Fountain of Life church dispute

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has gone to court over his removal as senior pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, filing a suit against his sister and several members of the church's board of trustees.

The suit

The suit was filed at the Federal High Court in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. Eight people are named as respondents, including his sister Tolu Odukoya, interim leader Pastor Rotimi Okpaise and other trustees.

The filing marks a significant escalation in the leadership dispute at the Ilupeju church founded by his late father, Taiwo Odukoya.

Background

Earlier this week, the trustees announced that Jimmy had been removed because his appointment did not reflect his father's succession wishes, and released a 2021 video in which the late founder reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his successor. Jimmy has insisted his removal is invalid. A Lagos State High Court had already granted the trustees an order barring him from acting as senior pastor while their own suit is heard.

What happens next

The court is expected to fix a date to hear the case. The trustees had not responded publicly to the new suit in the reports.

Rousing welcome

At Sunday service on 22 November, Jimmy Odukoya received loud cheers and applause from the congregation as he arrived, despite the court order barring him from presenting himself as senior pastor. There was an unusually heavy security presence around the church.

'I have not been served'

Speaking to the congregation on Sunday, Jimmy said he had heard about the injunction against him but had not seen it or been formally served, so legally it had not reached him. He pledged to carry on with his ministry. The Lagos State High Court order was granted on 18 November, and the matter was adjourned to 1 December.

Bishop Mike Okonkwo visits

Bishop Mike Okonkwo, who helped found the church, also attended a service and told worshippers the church had been battered on social media in recent weeks. He said he could not be separated from the Fountain of Life Church.

Wife breaks silence

Jimmy's wife, Mo Jasmine Daniel, ended her silence on social media by resharing her husband's post after his Sunday welcome, a move widely seen as a show of support.

'Church is not a political party'

In a sermon clip posted on Sunday, Bishop Mike Okonkwo told worshippers not to take sides with Tolu, Jimmy or the board, reminding them that none of them died for them and that the church is not a political party.

Court summons

On Monday, 23 November, the Lagos State High Court ordered Jimmy to appear on Thursday, 26 November, to answer contempt proceedings brought by the trustees. The judge allowed the trustees to serve him by substituted means, including newspaper publication, pasting at his last known address and electronic delivery, after normal service proved difficult. The notice warns of the consequences of disobeying the court's 18 November order, but it is not a finding of guilt.

Bench warrant issued

On Thursday, 26 November, Justice O. Adewunmi-Oshin issued a bench warrant for Jimmy's arrest after he failed to appear in court for the contempt proceedings brought by the church's trustees. The judge also sent the case back to the administrative judge for reassignment. Jimmy had earlier said he had not been served with the court's order.

Church faults warrant

In a statement on Thursday, the Fountain of Life Church, speaking for Jimmy as senior pastor, challenged the bench warrant, saying due process was not followed. It raised five objections, including how court documents from the 18 November orders were served on him.

  • He failed to appear in the contempt case.
  • The case has been sent for reassignment.

Benin photographer Sh0tbySav reportedly dies after 'one-chance' attack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A photographer known as Sh0tbySav has reportedly died in Benin City after being robbed and pushed out of a moving vehicle by suspected 'one-chance' criminals. Fellow photographers are mourning her.

Read more

The photography community in Benin City, Edo State, is mourning after reports that a photographer known as Sh0tbySav died following a suspected 'one-chance' robbery.

What reportedly happened

According to accounts shared on social media, she was returning home after a photoshoot for a client when she boarded a commercial vehicle believed to have been operated by criminals. She was reportedly robbed of her belongings and pushed out of the moving vehicle.

Details of the incident remain limited, and no police statement was included in the reports.

A warning

Her death has drawn fresh attention to the dangers posed by 'one-chance' gangs, criminals who pose as commercial drivers and passengers to rob unsuspecting commuters, a recurring problem in several Nigerian cities.

Tributes

Fellow photographers have shared tributes online.

Several killed as commercial bus catches fire on Lagos-Badagry Expressway

26 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Several passengers died and more than a dozen were injured when a commercial bus burst into flames at Magbon on the Lagos-Badagry Expressway on Thursday morning. Officials gave death tolls of six and eight.

Read more

Several people were killed and others injured when a commercial bus caught fire on the Lagos-Badagry Expressway on Thursday morning, 26 November 2026.

What happened

The incident happened near the Checkpoint area, inward Magbon, between 8am and 9am. The bus, travelling from Mile 2 towards Badagry, reportedly suffered brake failure, and the driver lost control. The vehicle somersaulted and burst into flames, trapping passengers inside.

The toll

Officials gave different figures. The Lagos State Fire and Rescue Service put the death toll at six adults, two women and four men, with 12 others injured. The Lagos State Traffic Management Authority (LASTMA) said eight passengers died and 13 suffered severe burns. The injured were taken to Badagry General Hospital.

Driver fled

The LASTMA general manager said the driver fled the scene after the crash, leaving passengers trapped, and offered condolences to the families of the victims. Traffic and road safety officers were deployed to the scene.

At least 16 people, including women, young people and children, have reportedly been killed in an attack on a community in Bassa Local Government Area of Plateau State.

The attack

According to the Irigwe Development Association, gunmen armed with assault rifles stormed the Tafi Gana community near Miango at about 11:30pm on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, and opened fire. By dawn on Thursday, 16 people were dead and four others had been taken to hospital with injuries.

Background

The attack is the latest in a series of deadly incidents in Plateau State, including in Bokkos, where gunmen set a secondary school on fire earlier this week.

Response

No statement from the police or the state government on the attack was included in the initial reports.
