Soludo says Tinubu doesn't need to rig 2027; Dele Momodu says Obi will beat him in Anambra

20 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Anambra State Governor Charles Soludo and ADC chieftain Dele Momodu have clashed over the 2027 presidential race in Anambra, a day after Soludo's party, APGA, adopted President Bola Tinubu as its candidate.

Soludo's claim

Speaking at the APC presidential campaign rally at Alex Ekwueme Square in Awka on Thursday, 19 November 2026, Soludo said the election was already won and lost and that President Tinubu does not need to rig it to secure a second term.

He argued that opposition candidates had strong support only in some parts of the country, while the Tinubu-Shettima ticket had a presence in every state.

Momodu's response

Reacting the same evening on a television programme, Dele Momodu dismissed Soludo's prediction and said Peter Obi, the NDC candidate, would defeat Tinubu in Anambra and embarrass the governor. He pointed to Obi's 2023 performance, when he won both Anambra and Lagos States.

Background

Anambra is Obi's home state, where he served as governor. APGA, which controls the state, formally adopted Tinubu as its candidate on Thursday, with Soludo handing the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima.

Tinubu's message

At the same rally, Vice President Kashim Shettima delivered a message on the President's behalf, saying no part of Nigeria can match the enterprise of the South-East and that the region's economic drive is vital to the country's competitiveness.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

  • Soludo says the election is already decided.
  • Momodu says Obi will win Anambra.
  • Obi won Anambra and Lagos in 2023.

Sowore makes surprise Onitsha market visit, says 'no rented crowds'

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore says he received a spontaneous welcome at Onitsha's Ogbo Ogwu market, a day after Peter Obi campaigned in the city. He also criticised the partition on the Niger Bridge.

African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has made a surprise visit to the Ogbo Ogwu medicine market in Onitsha, Anambra State, where he says traders gave him a warm welcome.

The visit

Sowore shared details of the visit on X on Saturday, 21 November 2026. He said he walked across the First Niger Bridge and arrived at the market without any prior announcement.

He described the reception as spontaneous and genuine, insisting that no crowds had been hired or organised for his arrival, and promised to return.

Bridge complaint

During the visit, Sowore criticised Works Minister David Umahi over a partition installed on the Niger Bridge, claiming it was disrupting traffic and causing hardship for commuters.

Context

His visit came a day after NDC candidate Peter Obi held a campaign event in Onitsha.

Tinubu's praise singer Rarara mobbed at Abuja National Mosque

21 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Hausa praise singer Dauda Kahutu Rarara, a well-known supporter of President Tinubu, was attacked by an angry crowd at the Abuja National Mosque during Friday prayers. Security operatives struggled to hold the crowd back.

Read more

Popular Hausa praise singer Dauda Kahutu, widely known as Rarara, was attacked by an angry crowd at the Abuja National Mosque during Friday prayers on 20 November 2026.

What happened

Rarara, a prominent supporter of President Bola Tinubu, was entering the mosque with plain-clothed and uniformed security personnel when a group of people rushed at him. Security operatives tried to hold the crowd back but could not fully contain them. At one point, the singer removed his cap, which carried Tinubu's insignia.

A video of the incident has spread widely online.

Context

The incident came a week after President Tinubu's convoy was pelted with stones after Friday prayers at a mosque in Garki, Abuja, an attack for which police arrested 78 people before the Presidency directed their release.

Reactions

Paul Ibe, media adviser to ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar, condemned the violence on X while also making a political point about the incident.

APC reaction

Former Imo East APC senatorial aspirant Belusochukwu Enwere condemned the attack, describing it as part of a growing pattern of hostility towards supporters of the ruling party and a threat to democratic stability ahead of 2027.
