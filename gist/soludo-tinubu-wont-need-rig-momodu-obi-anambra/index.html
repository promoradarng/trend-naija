Soludo says Tinubu doesn't need to rig 2027; Dele Momodu says Obi will beat him in Anambra

20 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Anambra State Governor Charles Soludo and ADC chieftain Dele Momodu have clashed over the 2027 presidential race in Anambra, a day after Soludo's party, APGA, adopted President Bola Tinubu as its candidate.

Soludo's claim

Speaking at the APC presidential campaign rally at Alex Ekwueme Square in Awka on Thursday, 19 November 2026, Soludo said the election was already won and lost and that President Tinubu does not need to rig it to secure a second term.

He argued that opposition candidates had strong support only in some parts of the country, while the Tinubu-Shettima ticket had a presence in every state.

Momodu's response

Reacting the same evening on a television programme, Dele Momodu dismissed Soludo's prediction and said Peter Obi, the NDC candidate, would defeat Tinubu in Anambra and embarrass the governor. He pointed to Obi's 2023 performance, when he won both Anambra and Lagos States.

Background

Anambra is Obi's home state, where he served as governor. APGA, which controls the state, formally adopted Tinubu as its candidate on Thursday, with Soludo handing the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima.

Tinubu's message

At the same rally, Vice President Kashim Shettima delivered a message on the President's behalf, saying no part of Nigeria can match the enterprise of the South-East and that the region's economic drive is vital to the country's competitiveness.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

  • Soludo says the election is already decided.
  • Momodu says Obi will win Anambra.
  • Obi won Anambra and Lagos in 2023.

Tinubu asks Sunday Igboho to deploy his security network in the South-West

23 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu reportedly asked activist Sunday Igboho to use his Iru Ekun Security Network to help protect lives and property in the South-West. The two met briefly in the president's car after the Ibadan rally.

President Bola Tinubu has reportedly asked Yoruba nation activist Sunday Adeyemo, popularly known as Sunday Igboho, to deploy his Iru Ekun Security Network to help protect lives and property across South-West Nigeria.

The meeting

According to a statement released on Monday, 23 November 2026, by Igboho's media office in Ibadan, the two met briefly inside the president's official car after the APC presidential campaign rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, on Saturday.

What was discussed

The meeting is said to have focused on the work of Iru Ekun, a community security outfit founded by Igboho, and how its members could help reduce insecurity in rural and urban areas of the region.

Background

Igboho is a well-known campaigner for Yoruba self-determination. The meeting took place on the day of Tinubu's campaign rally in Ibadan, where the president also met traditional rulers.

Fani-Kayode denies being recalled as Nigeria's envoy to South Africa

Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, has rejected social media claims that he was recalled or reprimanded. He said he was in Abuja for routine consultations.

Read more

Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, has dismissed reports on social media that the federal government had recalled or reprimanded him, describing them as completely unfounded.

What he said

Fani-Kayode made the clarification during a visit to Abuja, where he said he had been meeting officials at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs as part of routine diplomatic consultations. He insisted that his presence in the capital had nothing to do with any disciplinary action.

Background

Abba Yusuf claims Kwankwaso once sent him to promise Tinubu they would join APC

23 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Kano Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly claimed that Rabiu Kwankwaso sent him to tell President Tinubu they would return to the APC if the Supreme Court upheld his 2023 victory. Kwankwaso has not responded to the claim in reports.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly claimed that his former political mentor, Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, sent him to President Bola Tinubu after the 2023 governorship election to promise that they would return to the All Progressives Congress (APC).

The claim

Speaking in a Hausa-language interview on Sunday, 22 November 2026, Yusuf is said to have explained the background to his defection from the NNPP to the APC in January 2026. He claimed Kwankwaso asked him to tell Tinubu that they would join the ruling party if the Supreme Court upheld his victory over the APC's Nasir Yusuf Gawuna, and was even willing for him to swear on the Qur'an to show the promise was serious.

Yusuf also reportedly alleged that Kwankwaso held several meetings with Tinubu and sent delegations to discuss the arrangement.

Background

Yusuf and Kwankwaso, once close allies, have since split. Kwankwaso is now the running mate to Peter Obi on the NDC presidential ticket, while Yusuf is in the APC. In the same interview, the governor spoke about the end of his marriage to Kwankwaso's niece, Zainab.

The other side

No response from Kwankwaso or the NDC to the claim was included in the reports.

The timeline

According to the governor, the visit to Tinubu took place while his 2023 victory was being challenged in court by the APC's candidate, and the aim was to assure the president that they would join the APC if he let the case run its course without interference. Yusuf defected to the APC on 26 January 2026.

Tinubu says water supply went off in the Villa while he was shaving

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

President Tinubu has reportedly said the Presidential Villa also suffers water cuts, recalling that supply stopped while he was shaving last week. He promised to fix the country's infrastructure problems.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has reportedly said that the Presidential Villa in Abuja is not spared from the kind of utility problems many Nigerians face every day.

What he said

Speaking in Lagos on Sunday, 22 November 2026, the president said the water supply at the Villa was cut off while he was shaving last Wednesday, and joked about the inconvenience it caused.

He is said to have promised that his administration would fix the country's infrastructure problems, saying there can be no development without power and water. He insisted that Nigeria has the capacity to overcome its challenges and that the government had only just started.

Background

Poor water supply and unreliable electricity remain major complaints across Nigeria. The president's comments come as campaigns for the January 2027 elections get under way.

The setting

He spoke at a prayer session organised by his family at the Yoruba Tennis Club in Onikan, Lagos, a day after the APC rally in Ibadan, and said the water went off on Wednesday, 18 November. He also urged party supporters in Lagos to step up voter mobilisation and to reconcile.

Reactions

His comments drew mixed reactions online, with many critics mocking the promise to fix the problems, noting that the government has already been in office for several years.
