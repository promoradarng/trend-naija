Soludo says Tinubu doesn't need to rig 2027; Dele Momodu says Obi will beat him in Anambra

20 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Anambra State Governor Charles Soludo and ADC chieftain Dele Momodu have clashed over the 2027 presidential race in Anambra, a day after Soludo's party, APGA, adopted President Bola Tinubu as its candidate.

Soludo's claim

Speaking at the APC presidential campaign rally at Alex Ekwueme Square in Awka on Thursday, 19 November 2026, Soludo said the election was already won and lost and that President Tinubu does not need to rig it to secure a second term.

He argued that opposition candidates had strong support only in some parts of the country, while the Tinubu-Shettima ticket had a presence in every state.

Momodu's response

Reacting the same evening on a television programme, Dele Momodu dismissed Soludo's prediction and said Peter Obi, the NDC candidate, would defeat Tinubu in Anambra and embarrass the governor. He pointed to Obi's 2023 performance, when he won both Anambra and Lagos States.

Background

Anambra is Obi's home state, where he served as governor. APGA, which controls the state, formally adopted Tinubu as its candidate on Thursday, with Soludo handing the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima.

Tinubu's message

At the same rally, Vice President Kashim Shettima delivered a message on the President's behalf, saying no part of Nigeria can match the enterprise of the South-East and that the region's economic drive is vital to the country's competitiveness.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

  • Soludo says the election is already decided.
  • Momodu says Obi will win Anambra.
  • Obi won Anambra and Lagos in 2023.

Court freezes $100m linked to Governors' Forum in Paris Club fees dispute

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

An Abuja High Court has ordered the preservation of up to $100 million allegedly held by the Nigeria Governors' Forum, pending a dispute over legal and consultancy fees tied to Paris Club refunds. The order does not decide who owns the money.

Read more

A High Court of the Federal Capital Territory in Abuja has ordered the preservation of up to $100 million allegedly held by the Nigeria Governors' Forum (NGF), in a dispute over unpaid legal and consultancy fees connected to the Paris Club refunds.

The order

Justice O.C. Agbaza granted the interim freezing order on 19 November 2026, following an application by a lawyer against the NGF's incorporated trustees and 29 commercial banks. The order covers a single total sum of $100 million, about ₦135.6 billion, across the affected accounts.

What it means

The order preserves the disputed money until the court hears an application for an injunction or makes a further order. It does not decide who owns the funds.

The claim

The lawyer is seeking payment under an indemnity he says the NGF issued in December 2018, after the federal government paid $100 million to the forum to hold pending a decision on liability for professional fees. No response from the NGF was included in the reports.

ADC tells Tinubu to resign or face impeachment over Kano governor's interview

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The African Democratic Congress has reportedly demanded that President Tinubu resign or face impeachment, citing Kano Governor Abba Yusuf's recent interview about the 2023 Supreme Court ruling. The ADC says it points to possible gross misconduct.

Read more

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) has reportedly issued an ultimatum to President Bola Tinubu, demanding that he either resign or face impeachment proceedings in the National Assembly.

Why the ADC made the demand

The demand follows Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf's recent Hausa-language interview, in which he spoke about a promise to rejoin the APC if the president did not interfere in his Supreme Court case over the 2023 governorship election. The ADC argued that the governor's remarks suggested a link between the court case and a political deal, and that this could amount to gross misconduct, a constitutional ground for removing a president.

Background

Yusuf defected from the NNPP to the APC in January 2026. In his interview, he claimed that his former mentor, Rabiu Kwankwaso, sent him to make the promise to Tinubu. The Kwankwasiyya Movement has rejected the claim as false and said the governor offered no evidence.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to the ADC's demand was included in the reports.

NNPC says $2.04bn in crude oil proceeds is not missing

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

NNPC has dismissed reports that $2.04 billion in crude oil proceeds linked to its former trading arm Duke Oil is missing. It says the figure is the gross value of 29 cargoes and that all transactions were properly accounted for.

Read more

The Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPC) has dismissed reports that $2.04 billion in crude oil proceeds involving its trading subsidiary, formerly known as Duke Oil, is missing.

What NNPC said

In a statement on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, NNPC's chief corporate communications officer said the $2.04 billion represented the gross value of 29 crude oil cargoes, not missing or unpaid revenue. The statement was titled 'Duke Oil cargoes are fully accounted for'.

The company maintained that all transactions under the Direct Sale Direct Purchase (DSDP) programme were properly documented. It said the apparent gaps in dollar receipts were due to the way the programme's settlements were structured.

Background

Reports suggesting the money could not be traced had sparked concern online.

Air Peace to start London–Owerri flights on 15 December

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Air Peace says it will begin direct flights between London and Owerri on 15 December, from both Heathrow and Gatwick. The route is expected to ease travel for South-East Nigerians coming home for Christmas.

Read more

Air Peace has announced that it will begin flights between London and Owerri on 15 December 2026, in a move expected to make it easier for many Nigerians, especially those from the South-East, to travel home.

The route

The airline said in a statement on Monday that the new service would operate from both London Heathrow and London Gatwick airports, giving passengers more flexibility when planning their journeys.

Why it matters

The launch comes ahead of Christmas and the New Year, when many Nigerians living abroad return home to spend time with their families. A direct route to Owerri, the Imo State capital, is expected to spare travellers the long road journeys from Lagos or Abuja to the South-East.

Background

The announcement comes as domestic fares have surged ahead of the festive season, with some return tickets within Nigeria reported to have doubled in recent weeks.
