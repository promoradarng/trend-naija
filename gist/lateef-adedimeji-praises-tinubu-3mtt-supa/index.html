Lateef Adedimeji's praise for Tinubu's skills programmes divides fans

10 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Nollywood actor and filmmaker Lateef Adedimeji has sparked debate after speaking favourably about two of President Bola Tinubu's skills programmes.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Monday, 9 November 2026, Adedimeji spoke about the 3 Million Technical Talent (3MTT) programme and the Skills Up Artisans (SUPA) initiative, describing them as meaningful steps towards transforming Nigeria's tech and creative sectors. He encouraged his followers to position themselves to benefit from them.

The reaction

Many followers focused on the timing of the video, with the 2027 elections approaching, and saw it as politically motivated. Some long-time fans expressed disappointment, while others said they still admired him and that he was entitled to share information about government programmes.

Background

Adedimeji is one of the most popular actors in the Yoruba film industry and is married to actress Mo Bimpe. Several entertainers have openly backed the President's re-election, including Saidi Balogun and Eniola Badmus, and have also faced backlash online.

  • Adedimeji praised the 3MTT and SUPA programmes.
  • Some fans saw the video as political.
  • Others defended his right to share it.

The post

Ujah Idibia, brother of music star 2Baba, shared photos of the singer and Annie side by side on Instagram with a heartfelt caption.

IK Ogbonna calls out Governor Okpebholo over 'dangerous' Edo roads

12 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Actor IK Ogbonna has criticised Edo State governor Monday Okpebholo over the poor state of roads he saw on a road trip through the state, saying Edo people deserve better.

Read more

Nollywood actor IK Ogbonna has publicly criticised Edo State governor Monday Okpebholo over the condition of roads in the state.

What he said

In posts on his Instagram Stories on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the actor said he had just travelled by road through Edo and was shocked by what he saw. He described the roads as dangerously bad and a real safety risk for travellers, and questioned how a state with such a proud cultural and historical identity could still have crumbling roads. He tagged the governor and said the people of Edo deserve better.

Background

On the same day, comedian Sabinus criticised the Rivers State government over the state of roads in Rivers. Okpebholo, of the All Progressives Congress (APC), became Edo governor in November 2024. His government had not publicly responded to the actor's comments at the time of writing.

Presidency denies Tinubu's convoy was attacked after Friday prayers in Abuja

14 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

The Presidency has dismissed videos shared by ADC chieftain Dino Melaye that purportedly showed youths throwing stones at President Tinubu's convoy after Friday prayers in Abuja, calling them misleading and releasing its own footage.

Read more

The Presidency has denied claims that President Bola Tinubu's convoy was attacked by stone-throwing youths after he attended Friday prayers in Abuja.

The claim

Dino Melaye, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), shared a video on Friday, 13 November 2026, which he said showed young men throwing stones at a convoy belonging to the President after prayers at the Shagari Central Mosque in Area 1. Voices in the video can be heard chanting a Hausa phrase meaning 'we don't want you'.

The Presidency's response

O'tega Ogra, the President's senior special assistant on digital engagement and new media, disputed the account on X, describing the videos as misleading and linking them to opposition figures. He released a separate video showing Tinubu leaving the mosque in white attire and a black cap, with supporters waving and taking photographs as he walked to his vehicle. It has not been independently established where or when Melaye's video was recorded.

Background

The dispute comes amid rising concern about political violence ahead of the 2027 elections. On the same day, the President issued a statement condemning violence at political gatherings, directing security agencies to act firmly against thuggery and insisting that every party must be free to campaign anywhere. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
