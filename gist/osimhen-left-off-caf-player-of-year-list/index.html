Osimhen left off CAF Men's Player of the Year shortlist

4 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Super Eagles striker Victor Osimhen has been left out of the shortlist for the 2026 CAF Men's Player of the Year award, according to nominations released by the Confederation of African Football on Wednesday, 4 November.

The snub

The 27-year-old had what many considered a strong year. He helped Nigeria finish third at the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations in Morocco and was a key figure as Galatasaray won the Turkish Super Lig title in 2026. Osimhen won the award in 2023, becoming the first Nigerian to do so since Nwankwo Kanu in 1999.

Who made the list

The reigning African Player of the Year, Morocco and Paris Saint-Germain defender Achraf Hakimi, heads the 10-man shortlist, which reportedly also includes Senegal's Sadio Mané, Ghana's Antoine Semenyo and Ivory Coast's Amad Diallo.

Background

The omission follows news this week that no Nigerian player made the shortlist for the women's award for the first time since it began in 2001. Nigeria still has nominations in other categories, including the Super Falcons for Women's National Team of the Year. The CAF Awards ceremony will be held on 7 December at the Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Creative Arts in Lagos.

How people reacted

Nigerian fans reacted angrily, with many saying Osimhen's achievements deserved recognition and questioning how the shortlist was chosen. Others said the competition was strong this year.

Aubameyang crashes Ferrari on way to training in Spain, no serious injuries

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Former Arsenal striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang was involved in a crash on his way to training with Deportivo La Coruña. His Ferrari collided with another car, but no one was seriously hurt.

Former Arsenal and Chelsea striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang was involved in a road accident on his way to training with his Spanish club, Deportivo La Coruña, on Thursday, 26 November 2026.

The crash

The 37-year-old Gabon international was driving a Ferrari, reportedly worth around €380,000, when it collided with a BMW on the AP-9 motorway in north-west Spain at about 9:30am local time. The BMW overturned in the impact and had to be towed away.

No serious injuries

Emergency services, including the Civil Guard, firefighters and paramedics, attended the scene. Despite the force of the collision, no one was seriously injured.

FIFA says hackers stole personal and business information in cyberattack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

FIFA has confirmed it was the victim of a cyberattack in which criminals gained access to a limited set of personal and business information. It is working with law enforcement.

Read more

World football's governing body FIFA has confirmed that it was hit by a cyberattack in which hackers gained unauthorised access to personal and business information.

What FIFA said

In a statement on Thursday, 26 November 2026, FIFA said it had been the victim of a criminal cyberattack that gave access to a limited set of personal and business information. It said it was working with the relevant authorities, including law enforcement, to investigate.

The organisation strongly condemned the unlawful taking and publication of the information, particularly personal data, describing it as a serious violation of privacy that could have significant consequences for the people affected.

Unanswered questions

FIFA did not say when the attack happened, what kind of information was accessed or how many people were affected.

Kwankwaso accuses Kano Governor Abba Yusuf of using EFCC to target him

27 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

NDC vice-presidential candidate Rabiu Kwankwaso has accused his former protégé, Kano Governor Abba Yusuf, of trying to bring him down through anti-corruption agencies ahead of 2027. He claimed the governor asked the EFCC to act against him.

Read more

Rabiu Kwankwaso, the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) vice-presidential candidate and former governor of Kano State, has publicly accused Kano Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf of plotting against him through anti-corruption agencies ahead of the 2027 elections.

The accusation

In a statement on his X account on Friday, 27 November 2026, Kwankwaso claimed that the governor, who served under him for decades as an aide and commissioner, had formally asked the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) to act against him. He alleged that when that effort failed, the governor turned to a little-known group to stage protests at the EFCC's office to put pressure on the agency.

Kwankwaso said the governor wanted to damage his reputation.

Background

The two men, once close allies, have fallen out since Yusuf defected from the NNPP to the APC in January 2026. In recent days the governor claimed that Kwankwaso once sent him to promise President Tinubu they would rejoin the APC, a claim the Kwankwasiyya Movement has rejected. Yusuf has also spoken publicly about the end of his marriage to Kwankwaso's niece.

The other side

No response from Governor Yusuf or the EFCC to Kwankwaso's claims was included in the reports.
