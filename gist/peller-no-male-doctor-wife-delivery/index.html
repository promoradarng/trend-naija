Peller says no male doctor will attend his wife's delivery, sparking debate

30 Oct 2026, 20:49 GMT

Popular streamer and content creator Peller has got social media talking after saying he will not allow a male doctor to attend to his wife during childbirth.

What he said

In a viral Instagram video posted on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Peller reportedly said that only a woman would help his wife during labour and that he would go into the delivery room himself if he saw a man there. He said he did not trust men in such settings and challenged a person sitting beside him to say whether they would be comfortable with a male doctor delivering their own wife's baby. The moment was met with laughter.

How people reacted

Reactions online ranged from amusement to concern. Many users laughed at his tone, while others warned that labour is unpredictable and that a male doctor may be needed in an emergency, such as a caesarean section. Several commenters noted that the couple reportedly live in Canada, where hospitals typically assign whichever doctor or midwife is on duty, and questioned how his demand would work in practice.

Background

Peller is one of Nigeria's best-known streamers, with a large following on TikTok, and is often in the news for his livestreams and personal life. The comments came as he and his wife prepare to welcome their baby. He was also the subject of romance rumours with fellow streamer Jarvis this week.

What happens next

Fans are now waiting for news of the baby's arrival, with many joking that they hope the hospital is warned in advance.

  • Fans warned that emergencies may require any available doctor.

FG seeks another $500 million World Bank loan to expand renewable energy programme

16 Nov 2026, 16:20 GMT

The Federal Government is seeking an additional $500 million loan from the World Bank for its renewable energy programme, which would raise the World Bank's total financing for the project to $1.25 billion. Many Nigerians are asking where the results of earlier spending can be seen.

Read more

The Federal Government is seeking an additional $500 million loan from the World Bank to expand its renewable energy programme.

What the money is for

The programme is meant to expand access to electricity through renewable sources such as solar mini-grids and stand-alone solar systems, particularly for homes, businesses, schools and health centres in areas the national grid does not reach.

In 2023, the World Bank approved $750 million for Nigeria's Distributed Access through Renewable Energy Scale-up (DARES) project, which aims to bring clean electricity to millions of Nigerians. The new $500 million would raise the World Bank's total financing for the project to $1.25 billion.

"Where is the renewable energy?"

The news has been met with frustration by many Nigerians, who say they are yet to see the impact of the money already borrowed.

Millions of households and small businesses still depend on generators, while frequent grid collapses and rising electricity tariffs continue to bite. For many people, the question is simple: where is the renewable energy?

The debt question

The request also comes amid growing concern about Nigeria's rising debt. Critics argue that new loans should only be taken when there is clear evidence that earlier funds have been well spent and are delivering results.

Supporters of the programme argue that renewable energy is essential for closing Nigeria's huge electricity gap, and that such projects take time to deliver.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.
