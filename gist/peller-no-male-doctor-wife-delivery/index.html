Peller says no male doctor will attend his wife's delivery, sparking debate

30 Oct 2026, 20:49 GMT

Popular streamer and content creator Peller has got social media talking after saying he will not allow a male doctor to attend to his wife during childbirth.

What he said

In a viral Instagram video posted on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Peller reportedly said that only a woman would help his wife during labour and that he would go into the delivery room himself if he saw a man there. He said he did not trust men in such settings and challenged a person sitting beside him to say whether they would be comfortable with a male doctor delivering their own wife's baby. The moment was met with laughter.

How people reacted

Reactions online ranged from amusement to concern. Many users laughed at his tone, while others warned that labour is unpredictable and that a male doctor may be needed in an emergency, such as a caesarean section. Several commenters noted that the couple reportedly live in Canada, where hospitals typically assign whichever doctor or midwife is on duty, and questioned how his demand would work in practice.

Background

Peller is one of Nigeria's best-known streamers, with a large following on TikTok, and is often in the news for his livestreams and personal life. The comments came as he and his wife prepare to welcome their baby. He was also the subject of romance rumours with fellow streamer Jarvis this week.

What happens next

Fans are now waiting for news of the baby's arrival, with many joking that they hope the hospital is warned in advance.

  • Peller says only female staff should attend his wife's delivery.
  • Fans warned that emergencies may require any available doctor.
  • The couple reportedly live in Canada.

Corps members of the Oyo State Batch 25C have reportedly received their long-delayed final allowance, after social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), whose real name is Martins Vincent Otse, publicly called out the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).

What happened

On Wednesday, 18 November 2026, VDM shared a video on Instagram showing a corps member celebrating after receiving a credit alert and thanking him for taking up the matter.

Influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa has issued a public apology after remarks he made about young Nigerians at a youth conference sparked a backlash online.

What happened

A clip from the Emergence of Stars Port Harcourt Youth Conference showed Enioluwa making remarks that many viewers felt painted young Nigerians as unwilling to work. The video spread quickly on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, drawing criticism from people who said he ignored unemployment, systemic barriers and the economic struggles young Nigerians face.

The apology

Later the same day, Enioluwa posted a statement on X addressing the reaction, apologising for the remarks and acknowledging his privilege.

Peter Obi says his US trip was not to seek American endorsement

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has dismissed reports that he went to Washington to seek the backing of US lawmakers for 2027. He said his meetings focused on Nigeria's democracy and development.

Read more

Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) presidential candidate Peter Obi has dismissed reports that his recent trip to Washington, DC, was aimed at winning the backing of United States lawmakers for his 2027 bid.

What Obi said

In a statement on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, the former Anambra governor said media interpretations of his meetings with US lawmakers and policy figures had been inaccurate. He said he did not ask for an endorsement and did not present the meetings as formal support from the US Congress or any individual legislator.

He explained that the purpose of the visit was to exchange views with congressional leaders and other American stakeholders on Nigeria's development, the welfare of its citizens and, above all, the strengthening of its democracy. He said the talks focused on democratic institutions, accountability and making sure elections are free, fair and credible.

His advice to others

Obi encouraged other Nigerian politicians to hold similar international discussions on governance, and urged that public debate on Nigeria's democratic future should not be reduced to claims of foreign endorsement.

Background

Claims that the US Congress had endorsed Obi circulated online after the trip, and a US lobbyist also commented on the matter.

Juan Mata retires from football at 38

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former Chelsea and Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement at the age of 38. He won the World Cup and Euro 2012 with Spain and the Champions League with Chelsea.

Read more

Former Spain, Chelsea and Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement from professional football at the age of 38.

The announcement

Mata confirmed the decision on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, with a farewell video on X in which he symbolically hung up his boots. He thanked the game, saying that sometimes loving something means letting it go.

A decorated career

Mata was part of the Spain squads that won the 2010 World Cup and Euro 2012. After coming through Real Madrid's youth system and breaking through at Valencia, he joined Chelsea in 2011, where he won the Champions League and FA Cup in 2012 and the Europa League in 2013.

He moved to Manchester United in 2014 and made 285 appearances over eight seasons, winning the FA Cup in 2016 and the League Cup and Europa League in 2017.

Later years

After leaving United in 2022, he won domestic titles with Galatasaray in Turkey and Vissel Kobe in Japan, before finishing his career in Australia with Western Sydney Wanderers and Melbourne Victory, where he also took an ownership stake in the club earlier this year.
