Putin tells Europe Russia is 'ready for war' but warns there would be no winners

30 Oct 2026, 06:44 GMT

Russian President Vladimir Putin has issued a fresh warning to European countries, reportedly saying Russia is ready for war with Europe even though it does not want one.

What he said

Speaking at a Russia–Africa summit in Moscow, reported on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Putin is said to have told any European country seeking a conflict to prepare for it, and warned that if things escalated there would be no winners at all. He argued that Western efforts to defeat Russia strategically had failed. He also said Moscow did not want the Baltic Sea to face disruption like that seen in the Strait of Hormuz during the recent Iran war, which pushed up fuel prices around the world.

The summit

The meeting was attended by 48 countries, including 22 heads of state, according to reports. African countries have deepened trade and security ties with Russia in recent years, and the summit was seen as part of Moscow's effort to keep its influence on the continent.

Background

Tensions between Russia and NATO countries have risen in recent months. Several European governments have accused Russia of sabotage, surveillance of undersea cables and drone incursions into their airspace, including over Latvia and Estonia. Germany raised its assessment of the risk of war with Russia earlier this month. Meanwhile, the war in Ukraine continues, and a Russian strike on a railway station was reported to have killed 14 people this week, an attack Ukraine's president condemned.

What happens next

European leaders are expected to respond to the remarks, and NATO is likely to keep up its patrols in the Baltic region.

  • Putin says Russia does not want war but is ready for it.
  • He warned there would be 'no winners' if tensions escalate.
  • He spoke at a Russia–Africa summit attended by 48 countries.

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie settle long legal battle over French winery

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled their years-long dispute over Château Miraval out of court. The terms were not disclosed.

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled their long-running legal battle over the sale of their French winery, Château Miraval.

The settlement

In a joint statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the former couple said they had resolved the case out of court in the best interests of their family and would not comment further. They added that any comments attributed to anonymous sources did not come from either of them or anyone close to them. The terms of the settlement were not disclosed.

The dispute

Pitt, 62, and Jolie, 51, bought the estate in the south of France in 2008 and had been in dispute over it since they split in 2016. In 2022, Pitt sued Jolie, claiming they had agreed that neither would sell their share without the other's consent, and accusing her of selling her stake to a Russian businessman without his permission. Jolie disputed his account in her response.

At least 30 killed as fuel tanker overturns and bursts into flames in Cairo

26 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

At least 30 people died and 15 were injured when a fuel tanker overturned and caught fire on the Greater Cairo Ring Road in Egypt on Wednesday. The blaze engulfed 27 vehicles.

Read more

At least 30 people have died and 15 others were injured after a fuel tanker overturned and burst into flames on a busy stretch of the Greater Cairo Ring Road in Egypt on Wednesday, 25 November 2026.

What happened

According to Egypt's Ministry of Health and Population, the fire broke out above Tunnel 10 in the densely populated Al-Warraq area of Imbaba, a suburb of Greater Cairo. Preliminary findings suggest the privately operated tanker swerved, hit a concrete road divider and toppled over, causing an explosion that spread to 27 nearby vehicles.

The response

Authorities sent 39 ambulances to the scene, taking survivors to three hospitals in the area. Firefighters brought the blaze under control, and heavy machinery was brought in to clear the burnt-out vehicles.

Condolences

Egyptian President Abdel-Fattah el-Sisi and Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly sent condolences to the families of the victims.

Puerto Rico's Jennifer Barreto becomes first mother to win Miss Universe

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico has won Miss Universe 2026, becoming the first mother and first married woman to take the crown. The final was held in San Juan.

Read more

Puerto Rico's Jennifer Barreto has made history as the first mother to win the Miss Universe crown.

The final

The 27-year-old was crowned Miss Universe 2026 on Tuesday, 24 November, at the grand finale of the pageant's 75th edition, held at the José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum in San Juan, Puerto Rico. She beat contestants from 115 other countries.

A historic win

Barreto is married and has a seven-year-old daughter, making her the first mother and the first married woman to win the title. Her victory also gives Puerto Rico its sixth Miss Universe crown, ending a 20-year wait.

The winning answer

In the final question round, she was asked what human value should never be lost as artificial intelligence grows. She stressed using AI responsibly and keeping human values alive through consistent actions.
