VeryDarkMan questions INEC chairman's photo with APC UK chapter chairman

14 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has raised questions about a photograph showing the chairman of the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) with a leader of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) in the UK.

What he said

In a post reported on Saturday, 14 November 2026, VDM clarified that the man wearing an APC-branded cap in the photo was not INEC chairman Professor Joash Amupitan, but Tunde Doherty, chairman of the APC's UK chapter. He asked what the APC official was doing with the INEC chairman and whether the UK leaders of other parties, including the NDC, Labour Party, PDP and ADC, had been given the same opportunity to meet him. He went on to claim that INEC was compromised.

Background

Amupitan was in London this week to speak at Chatham House about preparations for the 2027 elections. Protesters confronted him outside the venue, accusing the commission of bias, and he pledged to reject any political appointment after leaving office to show his independence. INEC has also denied reports that he plans to resign. INEC had not responded to VDM's post at the time of writing, and the circumstances of the photo are not clear.

APC UK chairman responds

Tunde Doherty, chairman of the APC in the UK, later rejected claims that he was part of the INEC chairman's entourage at Chatham House. He said opposition figures had deliberately misrepresented a photo of him taken outside the venue, that he had never held any position or role in INEC and had no affiliation with it, and that as a party leader he had a right to attend public policy discussions there.

  • He asked if other parties had similar access.
  • INEC has not responded.

In a video message, VDM said it was unfortunate that in 2026 a whole community was still crying out for something as basic as water. He described the situation as disappointing and told the chairman he had two weeks to make himself useful to his people.

Actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington has revealed she spent much of 2026 going through medical procedures away from the public eye. She shared a video of her family celebrating test results, and some reports say she has been declared cancer-free.

Nollywood actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington has opened up about one of the hardest periods of her life, revealing that she spent much of 2026 dealing with health challenges that she kept largely private.

The announcement

In a video posted on Instagram on Friday, 27 November 2026, the actress, who is married to singer Banky W, was seen on a video call with a group of women, believed to be family members or close friends, as she shared test results from her doctor. The women burst into celebration on hearing the news.

Her message

In her post, Adesua said she had been quieter than usual this year because she was going through some of the most difficult months of her life, describing one procedure after another, tests, scans and hospital visits. Some reports said she had been declared cancer-free, though she did not give details of her diagnosis in the post.

How fans reacted

Fashola says Tinubu stands out because he has kept his allies for 16 years

27 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former Lagos Governor Babatunde Fashola has argued that President Tinubu differs from Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi because he has kept the loyalty of his political allies for about 16 years. He said even former opponents now work with him.

Read more

Former Lagos State Governor Babatunde Fashola has made a case for President Bola Tinubu's re-election, arguing that he stands apart from his main rivals by keeping his political allies for about 16 years.

The comparison

Speaking in a television interview on Friday, 27 November 2026, Fashola noted that Tinubu, Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi all began their political rise as state governors, although Atiku was elected governor but did not serve. He framed his argument as a job reference question, saying that Tinubu's long-time allies have stayed with him.

He also said that even some of the president's fiercest former opponents now work with him.

Background

Fashola, who succeeded Tinubu as Lagos governor and later served as a federal minister, also condemned the 'rig and die' slogan in the same interview. Atiku and Obi are expected to sign an opposition cooperation pact at a summit in Abuja on Monday.

The other side

No response from the Atiku or Obi campaigns was included in the reports.
