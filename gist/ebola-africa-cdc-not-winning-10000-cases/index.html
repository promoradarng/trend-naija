Africa CDC warns continent is 'not winning' as Ebola cases pass 10,000

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

The head of the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention (Africa CDC), Jean Kaseya, has warned that the continent is not winning the battle against the current Ebola outbreak, as confirmed cases passed 10,000.

The numbers

According to World Health Organization figures reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, there have been 10,172 confirmed cases and 4,932 deaths across five countries. The Democratic Republic of Congo, where the outbreak was declared in May, accounts for almost all of them, with more than 10,100 cases across eight provinces. Uganda has recorded 20 confirmed cases and two deaths, Kenya nine cases and two deaths, while France and Nigeria have each recorded one imported case with no deaths.

Nigeria's case

Nigeria's case involved a person who had recently travelled to Uganda and tested positive on 20 October. The patient was evacuated to Switzerland for specialist care, and the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention is monitoring 54 contacts.

What was agreed

Speaking at an emergency meeting of African health ministers, Kaseya reportedly said that Ebola anywhere in Africa demands preparedness everywhere in Africa. Ministers agreed to improve readiness for imported cases, coordinate surveillance across borders and keep trade routes open while tightening health checks. The outbreak is caused by the Bundibugyo strain of the virus.

Background

Kaseya recalled the 2014–2016 West Africa epidemic, which disrupted the education of millions of children, left about 23,000 children without a parent and cost close to $3 billion in lost economic output. Nigeria contained an outbreak in 2014 through rapid contact tracing, which is again being used now.

What it means

Health officials are urging Nigerians to report symptoms such as fever, weakness and bleeding promptly, and to follow advice on hand-washing and safe care of the sick.

  • There are 10,172 confirmed cases and 4,932 deaths, mostly in the DR Congo.
  • Nigeria has one imported case; 54 contacts are being monitored.
  • Ministers agreed to step up border surveillance.

NNPC cuts petrol prices in Lagos, Abuja, Kaduna and Sokoto

15 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NNPC has reportedly cut pump prices at its outlets in Lagos, Abuja, Kaduna and Sokoto by between ₦52 and ₦66 per litre, with Lagos now at ₦1,289, the lowest of the four.

Read more

The Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPC) has reduced petrol pump prices at its retail stations in four cities, according to its latest price sheet reported on Sunday, 15 November 2026.

The new prices

In Lagos, the price fell by ₦61, from ₦1,350 to ₦1,289 per litre, the lowest of the four locations. In Abuja it dropped by ₦55, from ₦1,380 to ₦1,325. Kaduna motorists will now pay ₦1,329, down ₦52 from ₦1,381, while Sokoto saw the biggest cut, ₦66, with the price falling from ₦1,378 to ₦1,312.

Why prices are falling

The cuts follow a series of reductions in wholesale depot prices and Dangote Refinery's switch to pricing linked to international petrol prices, which reportedly brought its loading price down to ₦1,250 per litre. MRS stations and depot owners have also lowered their prices in recent days.

Background

A recent comparison listed Nigeria among the countries with the cheapest petrol in West Africa.

Bandits kill Zamfara governor's aide during dawn attack in Kaura Namoda

15 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Bandits have killed Nasiru Sani Kaura, a senior special assistant to Zamfara governor Dauda Lawal, during an attack on worshippers gathered for early morning prayers in Kaura Namoda. Several others were reportedly abducted.

Read more

Suspected bandits have killed a senior aide to Zamfara State governor Dauda Lawal during an early morning attack in Kaura Namoda.

What happened

According to reports, the attack happened on Sunday, 15 November 2026, in the Low Cost area of Kaura Namoda town, as residents gathered for the dawn prayer. The gunmen killed Nasiru Sani Kaura, the governor's senior special assistant, and reportedly abducted several worshippers. Residents said the attackers fired indiscriminately and drove people from their homes.

Who he was

Kaura was described as a grassroots politician and community leader, and his death has left the town in mourning. He was buried according to Islamic rites.

Background

Zamfara is one of the states worst hit by banditry and kidnapping in the North-West. The attack came days after the DSS intercepted a large consignment of weapons allegedly being smuggled from Niger Republic to bandits in neighbouring Sokoto State. The police had not issued a statement at the time of writing, and it is not clear how many people were abducted.

Lagos says construction of Epe International Airport will start in 2027

15 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Lagos State says work on the proposed Epe International Airport will begin in 2027, with Governor Sanwo-Olu announcing the timeline at an APC campaign event in Epe and his deputy saying preliminary work is well advanced.

Read more

The Lagos State Government plans to begin construction of the proposed Epe International Airport in 2027.

The announcement

According to a statement by the Lagos chapter of the All Progressives Congress (APC), reported on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu announced the timeline at an APC governorship campaign event in Epe. His deputy, Dr Obafemi Hamzat, said significant progress had been made on the documentation and groundwork needed for the project.

What it could mean

Lagos APC spokesman Seye Oladejo described the airport as a major infrastructure investment that could turn Epe into an important aviation, industrial and commercial hub, and said it would create opportunities for residents and businesses along the Lekki–Epe corridor.

Background

Lagos is currently served by Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, which handles most of Nigeria's international air traffic. The Lekki–Epe corridor has seen rapid development in recent years, including the Dangote Refinery and the Lekki Deep Sea Port. No cost or funding details were given in the latest announcement.
