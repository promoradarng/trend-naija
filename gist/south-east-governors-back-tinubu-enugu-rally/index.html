South-East governors urge region to back Tinubu at APC women's rally in Enugu

1 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

Four South-East state governments have thrown their weight behind President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid, in a show of support in a region widely seen as Peter Obi's stronghold.

What happened

At an APC Women Mega Rally and Empowerment Programme for the South-East at Okpara Square, Enugu, on Saturday, 31 October 2026, the governors reportedly urged residents to back the President and APC candidates at all levels in the January 2027 elections.

What the governors said

Enugu Governor Peter Mbah said the region should build on the government's economic reforms rather than change course, comparing it to a sailor who does not abandon his ship mid-sea. He also described an empowerment programme for 5,000 women across the five South-East states. Imo Governor Hope Uzodimma said Igbo people were now seeing real inclusion and development under Tinubu, and that improved federal allocations had helped his state pay salaries and pensions regularly. Ebonyi Governor Francis Nwifuru praised the APC's skills programmes and denied links to any coalition outside the party. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo was represented by his deputy, Gilbert Ibezim, who said the region should repay the administration with votes. Deputy Speaker Benjamin Kalu also spoke, noting that no Igbo person had been president in six decades and praising the South East Development Commission.

Background

Peter Obi, the NDC presidential candidate and a former Anambra governor, won most of the South East in the 2023 presidential election. He has said he would serve only one term if elected, while some APC figures argue the region should aim for a longer stay in power through alliances. Obi had not responded to the rally at the time of the report.

What happens next

Both the APC and the NDC are expected to intensify their campaigns in the South East, which is likely to be one of the most closely watched regions in the election.

  • Governors Mbah, Uzodimma and Nwifuru spoke at the rally; Soludo was represented.
  • They urged the region to back Tinubu in January 2027.
  • The South East is seen as Peter Obi's stronghold.

In the post, the actor said they met almost five years ago in an airport lounge before a flight to Houston. Peter Obi says insecurity stops him walking freely in Nigeria, unlike in the UK

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi says security warnings prevent him from taking simple walks in Nigeria, recalling how he walked and took trains freely while living in London.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has spoken about worsening insecurity in Nigeria, saying safety warnings now stop him from taking simple walks or jogging in his own neighbourhood.

What he said

Addressing the Global Peter Obi Supporters Summit in Abuja on Saturday, 21 November 2026, the former Anambra governor recalled living in the UK, on the same street as the Mayor of London. He said they would walk the streets and take the train without security escorts.

He contrasted that with Nigeria today, recalling that he used to walk freely to church in Lagos in the 1980s and 1990s, but said security concerns now prevent him from doing so.

The campaign

Insecurity is one of the major issues in the 2027 campaign, ahead of the 16 January election.

Tom Brady buys minority stake in Chelsea

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

NFL legend Tom Brady has reportedly bought a minority stake in Chelsea Football Club. The size of the stake and price were not disclosed.

Read more

American football great Tom Brady has reportedly bought a minority stake in Premier League club Chelsea.

The deal

The size of the seven-time Super Bowl winner's stake and the amount he paid have not been disclosed. Chelsea is valued at about $4.2 billion by one leading business publication.

Leaving Birmingham

The move comes weeks after Brady sold his 3.3% stake in Birmingham City, who play in England's second tier. The reports cited English Football League rules on holding stakes in more than one club as the reason.

Growing sports portfolio

Since retiring from the NFL, Brady has built up a range of sports investments. He holds a 5% stake in the Las Vegas Raiders and part-owns the Las Vegas Aces basketball team, an E1 boat-racing team and a pickleball team.

Chelsea's season

The news came on the same day Chelsea suffered a heavy 4-1 home defeat to Leeds United.

Background
