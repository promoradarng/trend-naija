South-East governors urge region to back Tinubu at APC women's rally in Enugu

1 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

Four South-East state governments have thrown their weight behind President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid, in a show of support in a region widely seen as Peter Obi's stronghold.

What happened

At an APC Women Mega Rally and Empowerment Programme for the South-East at Okpara Square, Enugu, on Saturday, 31 October 2026, the governors reportedly urged residents to back the President and APC candidates at all levels in the January 2027 elections.

What the governors said

Enugu Governor Peter Mbah said the region should build on the government's economic reforms rather than change course, comparing it to a sailor who does not abandon his ship mid-sea. He also described an empowerment programme for 5,000 women across the five South-East states. Imo Governor Hope Uzodimma said Igbo people were now seeing real inclusion and development under Tinubu, and that improved federal allocations had helped his state pay salaries and pensions regularly. Ebonyi Governor Francis Nwifuru praised the APC's skills programmes and denied links to any coalition outside the party. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo was represented by his deputy, Gilbert Ibezim, who said the region should repay the administration with votes. Deputy Speaker Benjamin Kalu also spoke, noting that no Igbo person had been president in six decades and praising the South East Development Commission.

Background

Peter Obi, the NDC presidential candidate and a former Anambra governor, won most of the South East in the 2023 presidential election. He has said he would serve only one term if elected, while some APC figures argue the region should aim for a longer stay in power through alliances. Obi had not responded to the rally at the time of the report.

What happens next

Both the APC and the NDC are expected to intensify their campaigns in the South East, which is likely to be one of the most closely watched regions in the election.

  • They urged the region to back Tinubu in January 2027.
  • The South East is seen as Peter Obi's stronghold.

DSS says it found no evidence it planned to disrupt ADC campaign in Sokoto, asks Atiku to retract or provide proof

18 Nov 2026, 07:57 GMT

The Department of State Services says its internal investigation found no evidence that it planned to recruit protesters to disrupt the ADC's presidential campaign flag-off in Sokoto. It has asked Atiku Abubakar to retract or clarify his allegation, or provide evidence.

Read more

The Department of State Services (DSS) says its investigation found no evidence that it planned to recruit protesters to disrupt the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential campaign in Sokoto.

How it started

Earlier this month, former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar accused the leadership of the DSS of recruiting protesters to disrupt the ADC's presidential campaign flag-off rally in Sokoto.

The allegation drew wide attention, and the DSS responded by ordering an investigation into the claim.

The DSS findings

The agency now says its investigation found nothing to support the allegation that it planned to recruit protesters or disrupt the ADC campaign.

It has asked Atiku to either retract or clarify his claim, or to provide evidence that could support further investigation.

Not the final word

It is important to note that this is the DSS's own preliminary finding. It was not an independent investigation, which means the agency effectively investigated an allegation made against itself.

Atiku has not issued a fresh public response to the DSS findings.

Why it matters

With the 2027 elections approaching, allegations that security agencies are being used against opposition parties are likely to keep coming up. Public trust depends on such claims being properly examined, whether they turn out to be true or not.

Thousands march in Haiti to praise El Salvador's President Bukele as his troops take on gangs

18 Nov 2026, 14:49 GMT

Thousands of Haitians took to the streets of Port-au-Prince and other cities on Tuesday to praise El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele and his soldiers, who are leading a tough new offensive against the gangs that have terrorised Haiti for years.

Read more

Thousands of people took to the streets of Port-au-Prince and other parts of Haiti on Tuesday, praising El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele and his troops as they take on the country's powerful gangs.

"Viv Bukele"

Photos from the marches show huge crowds filling the streets, waving Haitian flags and holding up posters of the Salvadoran president. Many carried banners reading "Viv Bukele", Haitian Creole for "Long live Bukele".

Why Haitians are cheering

For years, armed gangs have controlled large parts of Port-au-Prince and beyond, killing, kidnapping and forcing hundreds of thousands of people from their homes.

Salvadoran troops have been in Haiti since early 2025 as part of an international mission, and recently launched their first major offensive against the gangs, with reports of heavy fighting and deaths.

Many marchers said Bukele is doing what the United Nations and the United States failed to do for years, while ordinary Haitians continued to suffer.

The Bukele model

Bukele became popular at home by cracking down hard on El Salvador's gangs, sharply reducing violence in a country once known as one of the most dangerous in the world. Many Haitians hope his forces can bring the same results.

His methods have also faced criticism from human rights groups, who say the crackdown in El Salvador involved mass arrests and abuses. But for Haitians who have lived in fear for years, many say any hope of relief is welcome.

Peter Obi says his US trip was not to seek American endorsement

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has dismissed reports that he went to Washington to seek the backing of US lawmakers for 2027. He said his meetings focused on Nigeria's democracy and development.

Read more

Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) presidential candidate Peter Obi has dismissed reports that his recent trip to Washington, DC, was aimed at winning the backing of United States lawmakers for his 2027 bid.

What Obi said

In a statement on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, the former Anambra governor said media interpretations of his meetings with US lawmakers and policy figures had been inaccurate. He said he did not ask for an endorsement and did not present the meetings as formal support from the US Congress or any individual legislator.

He explained that the purpose of the visit was to exchange views with congressional leaders and other American stakeholders on Nigeria's development, the welfare of its citizens and, above all, the strengthening of its democracy. He said the talks focused on democratic institutions, accountability and making sure elections are free, fair and credible.

His advice to others

Obi encouraged other Nigerian politicians to hold similar international discussions on governance, and urged that public debate on Nigeria's democratic future should not be reduced to claims of foreign endorsement.

Background

Claims that the US Congress had endorsed Obi circulated online after the trip, and a US lobbyist also commented on the matter.

Juan Mata retires from football at 38

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former Chelsea and Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement at the age of 38. He won the World Cup and Euro 2012 with Spain and the Champions League with Chelsea.

Read more

Former Spain, Chelsea and Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement from professional football at the age of 38.

The announcement

Mata confirmed the decision on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, with a farewell video on X in which he symbolically hung up his boots. He thanked the game, saying that sometimes loving something means letting it go.

A decorated career

Mata was part of the Spain squads that won the 2010 World Cup and Euro 2012. After coming through Real Madrid's youth system and breaking through at Valencia, he joined Chelsea in 2011, where he won the Champions League and FA Cup in 2012 and the Europa League in 2013.

He moved to Manchester United in 2014 and made 285 appearances over eight seasons, winning the FA Cup in 2016 and the League Cup and Europa League in 2017.

Later years

After leaving United in 2022, he won domestic titles with Galatasaray in Turkey and Vissel Kobe in Japan, before finishing his career in Australia with Western Sydney Wanderers and Melbourne Victory, where he also took an ownership stake in the club earlier this year.
