South-East governors urge region to back Tinubu at APC women's rally in Enugu

1 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

Four South-East state governments have thrown their weight behind President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid, in a show of support in a region widely seen as Peter Obi's stronghold.

What happened

At an APC Women Mega Rally and Empowerment Programme for the South-East at Okpara Square, Enugu, on Saturday, 31 October 2026, the governors reportedly urged residents to back the President and APC candidates at all levels in the January 2027 elections.

What the governors said

Enugu Governor Peter Mbah said the region should build on the government's economic reforms rather than change course, comparing it to a sailor who does not abandon his ship mid-sea. He also described an empowerment programme for 5,000 women across the five South-East states. Imo Governor Hope Uzodimma said Igbo people were now seeing real inclusion and development under Tinubu, and that improved federal allocations had helped his state pay salaries and pensions regularly. Ebonyi Governor Francis Nwifuru praised the APC's skills programmes and denied links to any coalition outside the party. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo was represented by his deputy, Gilbert Ibezim, who said the region should repay the administration with votes. Deputy Speaker Benjamin Kalu also spoke, noting that no Igbo person had been president in six decades and praising the South East Development Commission.

Background

Peter Obi, the NDC presidential candidate and a former Anambra governor, won most of the South East in the 2023 presidential election. He has said he would serve only one term if elected, while some APC figures argue the region should aim for a longer stay in power through alliances. Obi had not responded to the rally at the time of the report.

What happens next

Both the APC and the NDC are expected to intensify their campaigns in the South East, which is likely to be one of the most closely watched regions in the election.

  • Governors Mbah, Uzodimma and Nwifuru spoke at the rally; Soludo was represented.
  • They urged the region to back Tinubu in January 2027.
  • The South East is seen as Peter Obi's stronghold.

Edo State lawmaker Natasha Irobosa Osawaru has confirmed her political plans for 2027, sharing her certificate of return as the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) candidate for Egor constituency.

The certificate

In a social media post reported on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Osawaru displayed the certificate, dated 26 October 2026, which names her as the NDC's flag bearer for the Edo State House of Assembly seat representing Egor in the 2027 general election. The post confirmed her defection from the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) to the NDC, the party of presidential candidate Peter Obi.

Background

Osawaru represents Egor in the Edo State House of Assembly. She is also widely known as the partner of singer 2Baba, with whom she has a son who recently turned one. Her relationship with the singer has drawn public attention since 2Baba's separation from his former wife, Annie. The NDC has attracted several defectors from the PDP and other parties ahead of the elections.

How people reacted

Supporters congratulated her on securing the ticket, while some social media users debated her decision to switch parties.

Peter Obi condemns court ruling nullifying ADC chairman, says ruling party is engineering instability

5 Nov 2026, 20:38 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has condemned a Federal High Court ruling in Abuja that nullified the emergence of the ADC national chairman and all his actions. Obi warned the ruling could create serious uncertainty for opposition parties ahead of 2027, and accused the ruling party of engineering it.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has condemned the Federal High Court ruling that nullified the emergence of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) national chairman and the actions taken under his leadership.

"Do not undermine democracy"

In a statement on X titled "Do not Undermine Democracy", Obi said he received the news of the order by the Federal High Court in Abuja "with shock", describing it as "unsettling and surprising".

He was careful to say he was not challenging the court itself. "I do not challenge the ruling of the court. The court of law is constituted to determine disputes according to the law," he wrote.

Uncertainty before 2027

Obi's concern, he explained, is the timing. "My concern is that three months before a general election, it is in the public interest to have certainty. It is not good to have too many uncertainties about candidates and parties a few weeks before a presidential election."

Accusation against the ruling party

He went further, alleging that the instability was "obviously engineered by the ruling party, just as they have done against me in the Labour Party", and that the same approach was being used "to close down civic space for a robust campaign".

Obi also linked it to what he described as violence against opposition campaigns, saying it came "from the same rulebook that manufactures violence against us in our campaigns."

These are Obi's allegations, and the ruling party has not been quoted responding to them in his statement.

A call to the judiciary

Obi called on the judiciary to rise up and protect democracy by making sure opposition parties and their candidates are allowed to take part freely in the electoral process.

NDC takes Lagos by storm as large crowd turns out for campaign

5 Nov 2026, 20:05 GMT

The National Democratic Coalition (NDC) has taken its campaign to Lagos, drawing a large crowd of supporters. The turnout in one of Nigeria's biggest political battlegrounds has raised the question of whether the party can turn enthusiasm into votes in 2027.

Read more

The National Democratic Coalition (NDC) has taken its political campaign to Lagos, and judging by the crowd that turned out, the party appears determined to make its presence felt ahead of the 2027 general elections.

A charged atmosphere

The atmosphere was charged with excitement as supporters gathered to welcome the campaign team. Photos from the event show party leaders addressing the crowd, and streets packed with supporters, many in red caps, cheering and holding up their phones.

For a party seeking to establish itself as a serious political force, such a turnout could be an encouraging sign.

Why Lagos matters

This is Lagos, a state widely regarded as one of Nigeria's most important political battlegrounds and a stronghold of President Bola Tinubu's political influence.

Taking a campaign into Lagos and drawing a large crowd is bound to generate political discussion, especially as opposition parties begin positioning themselves for 2027.

Crowds versus votes

The real question goes beyond the number of people who attended. Can the NDC turn the enthusiasm at its campaign into actual votes when Nigerians return to the polling units?

Nigeria has seen massive political rallies before, only for the election results to tell a different story.

Still, one thing is becoming clear. The political atmosphere ahead of 2027 is gradually heating up, and Lagos will remain a major battleground.

Anambra NDC sets target of 98% of votes for Peter Obi

5 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Nigeria Democratic Congress in Anambra has inaugurated a campaign council for Peter Obi and set a target of 98% of the state's votes, or about 1.5 million, up from about 600,000 votes he won there in 2023. A homecoming rally is planned.

Read more

The Anambra State chapter of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has set an ambitious target for its presidential candidate, Peter Obi, in the 2027 election.

The target

At the inauguration of the state campaign council for the Obi–Kwankwaso ticket in Awka on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, state party chairman Ikem Uzoezie reportedly said the party aims to win 98 per cent of the votes cast in Anambra, about 1.5 million votes. That would be well above the roughly 600,000 votes Obi received in the state in 2023, when he ran on the Labour Party platform.

Campaign team

Former federal lawmaker Ifeanyi Ibezi was named state campaign coordinator. He promised a major homecoming rally for Obi in the coming weeks.

Background

Obi governed Anambra from 2006 to 2014 and remains popular in the state, which he won by a wide margin in 2023. Anambra's current governor, Charles Soludo of APGA, has said he backs President Bola Tinubu, while describing Obi as a close family friend. The APC is also campaigning hard in the South East, where several governors have declared support for the President. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

How people reacted

Obi's supporters welcomed the target as a sign of confidence, while rivals said the figure was unrealistic and that voters would decide.
