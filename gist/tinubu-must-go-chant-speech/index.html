Drama as crowd allegedly chants "Tinubu must go" during President's speech, sparking reactions

27 Oct 2026, 10:15 GMT

A dramatic moment reportedly unfolded during a public gathering as President Bola Ahmed Tinubu was delivering a speech about Nigeria's future, with voices in the background allegedly chanting, "Tinubu must go!"

The President was addressing the audience with a message of hope and optimism, assuring Nigerians that a brighter future lies ahead. However, what was supposed to be an encouraging moment appeared to take an unexpected turn when some people in the crowd reportedly began shouting slogans calling for his departure from office.

In footage circulating online, the chants could reportedly be heard while Tinubu continued speaking.

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu and other dignitaries present were also seen reacting to the situation. Some social media commentators interpreted their expressions and body language as signs of discomfort or concern.

Where and when the footage was recorded, and whether the audio has been edited, have not been independently verified.

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf confirms divorce from Kwankwaso's daughter, says he will marry again

16 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso, daughter of his former political mentor Rabiu Kwankwaso, has ended. Speaking with humour at a pensioners' event, he said the dissolution papers had been issued and that he still intends to take another wife.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, the daughter of former Kano governor and NDC leader Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, has ended.

What the governor said

Yusuf is said to have spoken about the matter on Monday, 16 November 2026, while addressing pensioners at an event at the Government House in Kano. Speaking in Hausa and in a light-hearted tone, he compared going from two wives to one to a demotion in military rank, from corporal to constable.

He reportedly confirmed that he had agreed to the divorce and that the papers for the dissolution had been issued. He also told the gathering that he still plans to take another wife, and asked residents to pray that he finds a suitable partner. He said he was at peace with the decision.

Background

The confirmation came two days after reports that Zainab Kwankwaso had filed a divorce petition at an Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja. She was said to be the governor's second wife.

The separation has drawn wide attention because of the long political relationship between Yusuf and Kwankwaso, who was his political mentor.

Wike tells FCT campaign council not to let ADC or NDC reach 25% in Abuja

16 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

FCT Minister Nyesom Wike has reportedly told the Tinubu campaign council and his Rainbow Coalition in Abuja that no opposition party should get 25 per cent of the vote in the territory in 2027. He said members would be held accountable for the result.

Read more

Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike has reportedly directed President Bola Tinubu's campaign team in Abuja to make sure no opposition party wins up to 25 per cent of the votes in the territory at the 2027 elections.

What happened

Wike gave the directive on Monday, 16 November 2026, while inaugurating a 466-member APC Presidential Campaign Council and Rainbow Coalition in Abuja. The coalition is said to also include members of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) who back the president.

He named the African Democratic Congress (ADC) and the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) as the main opposition parties the council should work against, saying they should not be allowed to reach 25 per cent. He reportedly said simply winning was not enough and that members would be held personally responsible for the outcome in the FCT.

Why 25 per cent matters

Under the Constitution, a presidential candidate must win at least 25 per cent of votes in two-thirds of the states and the FCT to be declared winner on the first ballot, which makes the territory an important battleground.

Jabs at opponents

Wike is also said to have dismissed claims that the ADC has strong support in the North or a real chance in the FCT, and challenged former Rivers governor Rotimi Amaechi to test his party's strength in Rivers State.

The other side

No response from the ADC or NDC was included in the reports. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

NDC vice-chairman and ward leaders in Kano's Bichi reportedly defect to APC

16 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

The NDC vice-chairman in Bichi Local Government, Kano State, and executives of five wards have reportedly joined the APC. They cited the work of their federal lawmaker and complaints about how the NDC is run in Kano.

Read more

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) in Bichi Local Government Area of Kano State has reportedly lost several officials to the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Who moved

The group was said to be led by the NDC vice-chairman in Bichi, Haladu Sa'adu, and included the party's local treasurer, the chairmen of five wards with their secretaries, and women and youth leaders, as well as some leaders of the Kwankwasiyya movement. They announced the move on Monday, 16 November 2026, during a visit to the office of Abubakar Kabir Abubakar Bichi, the APC lawmaker representing Bichi Federal Constituency and chairman of the House Committee on Appropriations.

Their reasons

Speaking for the group, the Danzabuwa ward chairman reportedly praised the lawmaker's projects in the area, saying his scholarship programme had paid for his own postgraduate studies. He also complained of poor coordination and a lack of respect for ordinary members within the NDC in Kano.

The APC welcome

The lawmaker welcomed the defectors and said their arrival would strengthen the APC at the grassroots in Bichi and Kano North.

The other side

No response from the NDC leadership in Kano was included in the reports. Bichi is the same area where Rabiu Kwankwaso's convoy was reportedly attacked last week.

Atiku accuses Tinubu's camp of using AI to fake rally crowds, says Potiskum crowd was real

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Atiku Abubakar has reportedly accused the presidency of using artificial intelligence to create images of large crowds at its events. He said the crowd that welcomed him in Potiskum, Yobe State, was genuine.

Read more

Former Vice President and ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly accused President Bola Tinubu's administration of using artificial intelligence to produce images of crowds at political events, while insisting the turnout at his own rally in Potiskum, Yobe State, was real.

What Atiku said

Atiku is said to have contrasted the welcome he received in Potiskum with what he described as doctored images released by the presidency, and mocked the quality of those images. He described the reception from the time his team entered Potiskum from Azare in Bauchi State as breathtaking, pointing to the size of the crowd and the chants from residents.

He said his campaign, which he calls the Affordable Life campaign, did not need artificial crowds, and that chants of "Tinubu Must Go" at his rallies reflected a real demand for change.

Praise for Yobe governor

Atiku reportedly thanked the Yobe State governor for allowing a fair environment for his campaign, saying open political competition strengthens democracy.

His promise

He pledged to act quickly on the cost of living if elected at the presidential election scheduled for 16 January 2027.

The other side

The reports did not include a response from the presidency or the APC campaign to the AI claim.
