Remi Tinubu and Tompolo share a cheerful moment at an APC rally

24 Oct 2026, 14:30 BST

First Lady Senator Oluremi Tinubu and Government Ekpemupolo, popularly known as Tompolo, were seen laughing and exchanging warm smiles at an APC rally. Tompolo, a former Niger Delta militant leader, now holds a major federal pipeline security contract and has become an influential figure with ties to the ruling party. The relaxed moment has sparked conversation online about how political relationships evolve in Nigeria.

  • Tompolo wore a cap and vest bearing the name of a pro-Tinubu support group.
  • His firm's pipeline surveillance contract has been credited by the government with helping to curb oil theft in the Niger Delta.
  • The rally is part of the APC's 2027 re-election campaign.
