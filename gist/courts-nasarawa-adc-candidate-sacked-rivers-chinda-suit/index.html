Court sacks Nasarawa ADC governorship candidate; Rivers APC candidate's case adjourned

4 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Two court cases involving governorship candidates for the 2027 elections made news on Wednesday, 4 November 2026.

Nasarawa ADC candidate sacked

A Federal High Court in Abuja reportedly nullified the nomination of retired Major General Nuhu Angbazo as the governorship candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) in Nasarawa State. Justice Mohammed Umar ruled that the primary election that produced him was inconclusive, and ordered the party to hold a fresh primary in Karu Local Government Area within 14 days. The court also barred INEC from recognising Angbazo as the party's candidate. The case was filed by former Minister of Environment Mohammed Abdullahi, who took part in the primary along with Angbazo and Senator Mohammed Onawo. Angbazo can appeal the judgment.

Rivers APC case adjourned

In Port Harcourt, a Federal High Court fixed 10 December for hearing in a suit seeking to disqualify Kingsley Chinda, the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidate in Rivers State. The plaintiff, Samuel Amatonjie, argues that Chinda did not properly leave the PDP for the APC under the Constitution, and has asked the court to compel INEC, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the Clerk of the National Assembly to produce documents on his defection. Chinda has not commented publicly on the case.

Background

The 2027 party primaries have produced many disputes, and courts have already ordered changes to several candidacies, including the Imo West senatorial ticket. INEC recently removed some candidates from its list, citing a Supreme Court ruling on party membership registers.

  • The court ordered a fresh ADC primary in Karu within 14 days.
  • INEC was barred from recognising Angbazo.
  • The Chinda case resumes on 10 December.

"Fubara, you will not regret what you have done as long as all of us are alive": Tinubu assures Rivers governor

7 Nov 2026, 16:46 GMT

At his presidential campaign rally in Rivers State, President Bola Tinubu gave Governor Siminalayi Fubara a strong personal assurance that he will not regret his decision. The remark has set people talking about what the governor did and what the President may have promised in return.

Read more

President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has given Rivers State Governor Siminalayi Fubara a strong personal assurance, telling him he will not regret the decision he has taken.

What Tinubu said

"Fubara, you will not regret what you have done as long as all of us are alive," the President said during his presidential campaign rally in the state.

Photos from the rally show Fubara smiling beside FCT Minister Nyesom Wike, both wearing red caps bearing Tinubu's image, a striking picture given the long-running feud between the two men.

Why the words matter

The statement is attracting attention because of the political tension that has surrounded Rivers State and the long-running power struggle between Governor Fubara and his political opponents.

For anyone who has followed Rivers politics closely, those words carry real weight. Fubara has faced some of the toughest political battles of his administration, with fights over political loyalty, control of the state legislature and the influence of powerful figures shaping events in the state.

A sacrifice, a deal, or a new understanding?

With the President offering such a personal assurance, questions are being raised. The President did not spell out exactly what decision he was referring to, or what he intends to do for the governor in return.

Was Tinubu acknowledging a major political sacrifice by Fubara? Was he reassuring him over a difficult decision? Or could this signal a new understanding within the political leadership of Rivers State ahead of 2027?

Middle Belt youth group switches from Obi to Tinubu, pledges 10 million votes

7 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A Middle Belt youth movement led by aviation businessman Isaac Balami, which mobilised for Peter Obi in 2023, has declared support for President Tinubu's re-election and pledged 10 million votes from the region.

Read more

A youth group from Nigeria's Middle Belt has declared support for President Bola Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid, after backing Peter Obi in 2023.

What was announced

Aviation businessman Isaac Balami, who convenes the National Rebirth Movement and is also a national director in the pro-Tinubu City Boy Movement, said in remarks reported on Saturday, 7 November 2026, that the initiative was inspired by a recent meeting between the President and a delegation of Middle Belt elders led by former defence minister General Theophilus Danjuma. He said the youths had listened to their elders and decided to take responsibility for the region's political future. Balami said he had earlier led the group to meet the President's son, Seyi Tinubu, where they pledged 10 million votes from the Middle Belt.

A change of side

In 2023, Balami and the same group had reportedly been asked by some of the same elders to mobilise support for Obi, then the Labour Party candidate, who performed strongly in Plateau and Nasarawa states and drew large youth support across the North Central zone. Balami said a sense of abandonment after that election led to the change. The group says it will work with the City Boy Movement and the APC campaign council.

Background

Obi is now the candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. Both the APC and the opposition parties are campaigning hard in the North Central, which is seen as a key battleground. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Reactions

Obi's campaign had not publicly responded at the time of writing. The 10 million figure is a pledge and cannot be independently verified.

ADP suspends national chairman Yabagi Sani, accuses him of backing Atiku

7 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

The Action Democratic Party's national working committee has suspended its chairman, Yabagi Yusuf Sani, accusing him of forgery, financial misconduct and openly endorsing ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar. He has not publicly responded.

Read more

The national working committee (NWC) of the Action Democratic Party (ADP) has placed its national chairman, Yabagi Yusuf Sani, on interim suspension.

The allegations

In a notice to the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), signed by national secretary Victor Fingesi and reported on Friday, 6 November 2026, the party accused Sani of forging party documents, gaining unauthorised access to and manipulating its candidate nomination portal, and behaving in ways that could damage the party's image. The party said it had already complained to INEC on 19 October about suspected irregularities in the uploading of its governorship candidates for Ogun, Enugu and Niger states. It also alleged that the management of the party's accounts had not been transparent, including money from the sale of nomination forms. None of the allegations has been tested in court.

Endorsing Atiku

The party also pointed to a television appearance on 30 August 2026 in which, it said, Sani described the ADP's own presidential candidate, Aliyu Ibn Abbas, as unserious and openly endorsed Atiku Abubakar of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), saying Atiku was the only candidate who provided money for campaign activities.

How it happened

The decision was reportedly taken at an NWC meeting in Abuja. The motion was moved by the party's national legal adviser and passed by a majority of members present. Sani is barred from acting as chairman pending a full investigation and a review by the party's national executive committee.

Background

Sani has led the ADP for several years. He had not publicly responded to the suspension at the time of writing. The move comes as the ADC itself faces a court ruling that removed its national leadership and voided Atiku's nomination, which the party has appealed.

APGA endorses Tinubu for 2027 as Soludo targets half of Anambra votes

6 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

The All Progressives Grand Alliance has formally adopted President Tinubu as its preferred presidential candidate for 2027 and will not field its own. Governor Charles Soludo pledged to deliver half of Anambra's votes to the President.

Read more

The All Progressives Grand Alliance (APGA) has formally endorsed President Bola Tinubu of the All Progressives Congress (APC) as its preferred candidate for the 2027 presidential election.

What was announced

Anambra State governor Charles Soludo, the party's national leader, announced the decision in Awka on Friday, 6 November 2026, at the inauguration of APGA's national campaign for the 2027 elections, according to reports. He said the party would not field its own presidential candidate. Soludo argued that Tinubu had shown genuine care for the Igbo people, particularly people from Anambra, pointing out that the President appointed several Anambra indigenes to key positions in Lagos when he was governor of that state.

Target of 50%

Soludo acknowledged that Tinubu received only about 5,000 votes in Anambra in the 2023 presidential election, but said the President had still moved to address problems in the state and the wider South-East. He pledged to deliver half of Anambra's votes to Tinubu in 2027, describing the endorsement as a strategic move to strengthen the state's bargaining power with the federal government. He also said APGA had backed the ruling party's presidential candidate in past elections, so the decision continued that pattern.

Background

APGA is the governing party in Anambra, which is also the home state of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi, a former APGA governor who won the state by a wide margin in 2023. Soludo has previously described Obi as a close family friend while declaring support for Tinubu, and he has been named a state coordinator in the President's expanded campaign council. This week the NDC's Anambra chapter set a target of 98 per cent of the state's votes for Obi.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027, and APGA is expected to campaign for the President alongside its own candidates for other offices.
