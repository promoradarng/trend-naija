Ebonyi women parade man in wheelbarrow over alleged repeated beating of his wife

7 Nov 2026, 18:58 GMT

Women in Ishiagu community, Ivo Local Government Area of Ebonyi State, have reportedly taken an unusual step to show their anger over domestic violence, publicly parading a man accused of repeatedly beating his wife.

Pushed around in a wheelbarrow

According to reports circulating online, the women placed the man in a wheelbarrow and pushed him around the community, apparently determined to make an example of him over his alleged treatment of his wife.

Photos show a large group of women surrounding the man, who sits in the wheelbarrow in a yellow T-shirt as several women push and guide it along a dirt path, with one carrying a calabash.

"Enough is enough"

The incident has drawn attention because of the unusual punishment the women chose. They appeared to have reached a point where they could no longer tolerate the alleged abuse.

In many communities, domestic violence is still treated as a private family matter, and victims are sometimes encouraged to endure abuse for the sake of their marriage. The women of Ishiagu reportedly decided that enough was enough.

Justice or humiliation?

Their action raises an important question about how communities should respond when a woman is repeatedly subjected to violence in her own home.

Many people may applaud the women for standing up against domestic abuse. Others may question whether publicly humiliating an accused person, who has not been tried, is the right approach.

The full circumstances of the incident, including the man's side of the story and whether the authorities have stepped in, remain unclear.

Soludo says Tinubu doesn't need to rig 2027; Dele Momodu says Obi will beat him in Anambra

20 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Anambra Governor Charles Soludo told an APC rally in Awka that the 2027 election is already decided and Tinubu does not need to rig it. ADC chieftain Dele Momodu fired back that Peter Obi will win Anambra and embarrass the governor.

Read more

Anambra State Governor Charles Soludo and ADC chieftain Dele Momodu have clashed over the 2027 presidential race in Anambra, a day after Soludo's party, APGA, adopted President Bola Tinubu as its candidate.

Soludo's claim

Speaking at the APC presidential campaign rally at Alex Ekwueme Square in Awka on Thursday, 19 November 2026, Soludo said the election was already won and lost and that President Tinubu does not need to rig it to secure a second term.

He argued that opposition candidates had strong support only in some parts of the country, while the Tinubu-Shettima ticket had a presence in every state.

Momodu's response

Reacting the same evening on a television programme, Dele Momodu dismissed Soludo's prediction and said Peter Obi, the NDC candidate, would defeat Tinubu in Anambra and embarrass the governor. He pointed to Obi's 2023 performance, when he won both Anambra and Lagos States.

Background

Anambra is Obi's home state, where he served as governor. APGA, which controls the state, formally adopted Tinubu as its candidate on Thursday, with Soludo handing the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Fidelity Bank Plc has confirmed Stanley Chiedoziem Amuchie as its new managing director and chief executive officer, following approval from the Central Bank of Nigeria.

The change

Amuchie, currently an executive director and the bank's chief operations and information officer, will take over on 1 January 2027. He succeeds Nneka Onyeali-Ikpe, whose term as MD/CEO ends on 31 December 2026.

Board's backing

The bank's chairman, Amaka Onwughalu, said the board was confident in Amuchie's ability to lead, pointing to his deep knowledge of the bank's operations and wider industry experience.

Google offers Nigerian students free AI subscription worth ₦92,400

Google is offering eligible university students in Nigeria and 26 other African countries 12 months of free access to its Google AI Plus plan, normally ₦7,700 a month.

Read more

Google is offering eligible higher-education students in Nigeria 12 months of free access to its AI subscription, a package worth about ₦92,400.

The offer

The promotion covers students in 27 African countries, including Nigeria. Google AI Plus normally costs ₦7,700 a month in Nigeria.

The package includes enhanced access to the Gemini assistant and other AI tools, including research and live conversation features, as well as 400GB of cloud storage.

Who can apply

The offer is aimed at eligible students in higher education institutions, who must verify their student status to claim it.
