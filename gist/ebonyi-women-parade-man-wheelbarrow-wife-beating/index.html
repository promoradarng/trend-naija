Ebonyi women parade man in wheelbarrow over alleged repeated beating of his wife

7 Nov 2026, 18:58 GMT

Women in Ishiagu community, Ivo Local Government Area of Ebonyi State, have reportedly taken an unusual step to show their anger over domestic violence, publicly parading a man accused of repeatedly beating his wife.

Pushed around in a wheelbarrow

According to reports circulating online, the women placed the man in a wheelbarrow and pushed him around the community, apparently determined to make an example of him over his alleged treatment of his wife.

Photos show a large group of women surrounding the man, who sits in the wheelbarrow in a yellow T-shirt as several women push and guide it along a dirt path, with one carrying a calabash.

"Enough is enough"

The incident has drawn attention because of the unusual punishment the women chose. They appeared to have reached a point where they could no longer tolerate the alleged abuse.

In many communities, domestic violence is still treated as a private family matter, and victims are sometimes encouraged to endure abuse for the sake of their marriage. The women of Ishiagu reportedly decided that enough was enough.

Justice or humiliation?

Their action raises an important question about how communities should respond when a woman is repeatedly subjected to violence in her own home.

Many people may applaud the women for standing up against domestic abuse. Others may question whether publicly humiliating an accused person, who has not been tried, is the right approach.

The full circumstances of the incident, including the man's side of the story and whether the authorities have stepped in, remain unclear.

CAS to rule on Tuesday on who won AFCON 2025, Senegal or Morocco

23 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Court of Arbitration for Sport will announce its decision on the disputed 2025 Africa Cup of Nations final on Tuesday, 24 November. Senegal won the match on the pitch, but CAF later handed the title to Morocco.

Read more

The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) will deliver its long-awaited ruling on the disputed 2025 Africa Cup of Nations final between Senegal and Morocco on Tuesday, 24 November 2026.

The announcement

CAS confirmed on Monday that all the parties had been notified of the decision date. The ruling is expected on Tuesday afternoon and will settle which country is officially recognised as champion. The court reminded the public that only information released through its own channels should be treated as official.

How the dispute started

The final was played in Rabat on 18 January 2026. Senegal beat hosts Morocco 1-0 after extra time, with Pape Gueye scoring the winner. But the match was marred by chaotic scenes when Senegal's players briefly walked off the pitch in protest at a late penalty awarded to Morocco. When play resumed after about 15 minutes, Brahim Diaz's attempted Panenka penalty was saved by Senegal goalkeeper Edouard Mendy.

Title stripped

On 17 March, CAF's Appeal Board overturned the result, ruling that Senegal had forfeited the match by leaving the pitch, and awarded the title to Morocco. The Senegalese Football Federation rejected the decision and took the case to CAS, naming CAF and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation as respondents.

What happens next

Tuesday's decision is final at the sporting level and will either restore Senegal's title or confirm Morocco as champions.

Zelensky says Ukrainian sea drones sank a Russian warship in Libya

23 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Ukraine says its naval drones sank a Russian corvette docked in Benghazi, Libya, and hit a Russian cargo ship off North Africa. President Zelensky released video of the operation on Monday.

Read more

Ukrainian naval drones have reportedly sunk a modern Russian warship docked in the eastern Libyan port of Benghazi and struck a Russian cargo ship off the North African coast, President Volodymyr Zelensky said on Monday, 23 November 2026.

The operation

In a statement on Telegram, released alongside video footage, Zelensky said Ukrainian forces used seven domestically made unmanned surface vessels to attack the corvette Merkury. The ship, commissioned in May 2023 and built with radar-evading features, caught fire and sank, and a combat helicopter on board was destroyed. Satellite images later reportedly showed the vessel capsized and mostly underwater beside the pier.

Zelensky described it as an exemplary operation in response to Russia's war and thanked Ukraine's Security Service and defence forces, saying the targets set had been achieved.

Why it matters

The strike marks a major expansion of Ukraine's attacks at sea, far beyond the Black Sea, where its drones have previously targeted Russian ships.

Russia's response

No response from Moscow was included in the initial reports.

23 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Kano Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly claimed that Rabiu Kwankwaso sent him to tell President Tinubu they would return to the APC if the Supreme Court upheld his 2023 victory. Kwankwaso has not responded to the claim in reports.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly claimed that his former political mentor, Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, sent him to President Bola Tinubu after the 2023 governorship election to promise that they would return to the All Progressives Congress (APC).

The claim

Speaking in a Hausa-language interview on Sunday, 22 November 2026, Yusuf is said to have explained the background to his defection from the NNPP to the APC in January 2026. He claimed Kwankwaso asked him to tell Tinubu that they would join the ruling party if the Supreme Court upheld his victory over the APC's Nasir Yusuf Gawuna, and was even willing for him to swear on the Qur'an to show the promise was serious.

Yusuf also reportedly alleged that Kwankwaso held several meetings with Tinubu and sent delegations to discuss the arrangement.

Background

Yusuf and Kwankwaso, once close allies, have since split. Kwankwaso is now the running mate to Peter Obi on the NDC presidential ticket, while Yusuf is in the APC. In the same interview, the governor spoke about the end of his marriage to Kwankwaso's niece, Zainab.

The other side

No response from Kwankwaso or the NDC to the claim was included in the reports.
