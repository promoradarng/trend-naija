Flamingos beat Japan 2-1 to win Nigeria's first U-17 Women's World Cup

7 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Nigeria's under-17 women's team, the Flamingos, are world champions after beating Japan 2-1 in the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Rabat, Morocco, on Saturday, 7 November 2026.

The match

Awawu Bashiru gave Nigeria the lead in the 39th minute at the Mohammed VI Football Complex, and Oluebube Umejiaku doubled the advantage two minutes after half-time, in the 47th minute. Japan pulled a goal back through Fuki Ito in the 80th minute, but the Flamingos held on to claim the trophy.

History made

The victory makes Nigeria the first African country to win the competition, and gives the country its first FIFA world title in women's football at any level. Before this tournament, the Flamingos' best finish was third place in 2022. They reached the final by beating Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final, a match in which Bashiru also scored twice, while Japan beat North Korea on penalties in their semi-final.

Background

Japan came into the final as one of the strongest teams in youth women's football, having won the tournament in 2014 and finished as runners-up in 2010 and 2016. CAF president Patrice Motsepe had travelled to Rabat to watch the final after praising the Flamingos for making Africa proud. Before the match, captain Harmony Chidi said the team was determined to bring the trophy home.

What happens next

The win comes as the senior Super Eagles prepare for their AFCON qualifiers against Tanzania next week.

Olympic Eagles lose 2-0 to DR Congo in U-23 AFCON qualifier first leg

Nigeria's U-23 team lost 2-0 to DR Congo in Lubumbashi in the first leg of their U-23 AFCON qualifier, with a controversial opening goal and an injury to Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna.

Nigeria's Olympic Eagles suffered a 2-0 defeat to the Democratic Republic of Congo in the first leg of their second-round qualifier for the 2027 Under-23 Africa Cup of Nations.

The match

In Lubumbashi on Friday, 13 November 2026, Nigeria started brightly, with midfielder Victor Samuel shooting narrowly wide early on and missing a one-on-one chance soon after. The hosts took the lead inside 20 minutes with a goal that Nigeria felt should have been ruled out for a challenge on goalkeeper Samuel James. Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna was then forced off injured on the half-hour, and DR Congo doubled their lead five minutes before half-time.

Who was in charge

The team was led by Ikorodu City coach Bright Osehai Ozebagbe and former U-20 assistant coach Deniran Ortega, while Eric Chelle was with the Super Eagles, who beat Tanzania 3-1 on the same day.

What happens next

Nigeria will need to overturn the two-goal deficit in the return leg at home to reach the tournament.

Arokodare scores twice as Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 in AFCON qualifier

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Substitute Tolu Arokodare scored twice as Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 in Uyo on matchday three of the 2027 AFCON qualifiers, moving the Super Eagles to second in Group L, a point behind Guinea-Bissau.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 at the Godswill Akpabio Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November 2026, in a crucial 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier.

How the match went

Alhassan Yusuf gave Nigeria a dream start with a goal in the second minute, but Tanzania equalised through Alphonce Masanga. Ademola Lookman was forced off injured late in the first half and replaced by Moses Simon. Coach Eric Chelle then brought on Tolu Arokodare and Moses Usor in the second half, and the change paid off immediately: Arokodare scored in the 52nd minute, and the two substitutes combined for Arokodare's second goal and Nigeria's third in the 60th minute.

Table

The win moved Nigeria to second in Group L, one point behind Guinea-Bissau, who were held 1-1 by Madagascar. Nigeria play Tanzania again in their next match, and a win there would take them to nine points, putting them in a strong position to qualify with two games left.

Arokodare delivers

Arokodare, who has been in strong form on loan at Ajax in the Netherlands, had promised to carry his club form into the national team in the absence of the injured Victor Osimhen. Chelle later sent him a message after his brace.

Lookman a doubt

After the match, Chelle said Ademola Lookman was a doubt for the return fixture against Tanzania. He said the Atletico Madrid forward had felt discomfort in his knee before a knock to the same area made it worse, and the coaching staff are not expected to rush him back. Tanzania are one of the co-hosts of AFCON 2027, along with Kenya and Uganda.

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has reportedly rejected a second contract offer from the club.

The talks

According to reports on Friday, 13 November 2026, United first offered Fernandes a new deal in the summer, which he turned down, and talks between his representatives and the club have continued since then without agreement. His current contract expires at the end of the season.

His record

The Portugal international had an outstanding 2025/26 season, helping United finish third and return to the Champions League after two years away. He set a new Premier League record of 21 assists in a season, breaking the mark previously shared by Thierry Henry and Kevin De Bruyne, and won both the FWA Footballer of the Year and PFA Player of the Year awards.

Background

The news adds to a difficult period at Old Trafford. The club sacked head coach Michael Carrick this week after one league win in 10 games left them in the relegation zone, and Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly agreed to take over.

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has denied claims that its chairman, Professor Joash Amupitan, is planning to resign.

Reports dismissed

The commission dismissed the reports, which had been circulating ahead of the 2027 general elections, and maintained that the chairman is not stepping down.

Why it matters

Any change at the top of the electoral body this close to a general election would raise serious concerns about preparations for the polls. INEC has faced intense scrutiny in recent weeks, including controversy over AI-generated images in the voter register and growing tension around the "Rig and Die" slogan.
