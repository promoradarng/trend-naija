Broda Mike says Ghe Ghe's sister's online attack was planned, replying to VeryDarkMan

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

Skit maker Broda Mike has made a surprising claim in his reply to social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), saying a recent online family drama involving internet personality Ghe Ghe was planned.

What he said

VDM had reportedly claimed that he helped Ghe Ghe when Ghe Ghe's sister publicly criticised him online. Responding in a video shared on Monday, 2 November 2026, Broda Mike said that he and Ghe Ghe had planned for the sister to drag Ghe Ghe on social media. He did not explain why, or whether the sister knew it was staged.

How people reacted

The claim drew mixed reactions. Many users said it confirmed their suspicion that much online drama is staged for content, and some said they felt deceived. One user noted that Ghe Ghe's sister had gone further than any plan by publicly claiming she had not received ₦5 million. Others mocked everyone involved. Ghe Ghe and his sister have not publicly responded to Broda Mike's claim.

Background

VDM has been in several online disputes in recent weeks, including with Davido's camp, Zlatan Ibile, former BBNaija winner Phyna and businessman Blord. He has also been promoting his Ratel brand of power products, which he says sold ₦90 million worth in one day. Content creators have increasingly been accused of staging arguments and family disputes to attract views, and the latest claim is likely to add to that debate.

What happens next

It is not yet clear whether VDM will respond, but fans expect the exchange to continue online.

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has introduced his wife to the public for the first time, sharing loved-up photos and videos of the couple on Instagram.

How they met

In the post, the actor said they met almost five years ago in an airport lounge before a flight to Houston. They ignored each other for an hour, then burst out laughing when they realised they were on the same flight.

His tribute

Jim Iyke described their relationship as something sacred rather than hidden, and said they have built a family together and travelled to eight countries. He called her his best friend, his prayer warrior and his wife, and said they have never stopped laughing together.

Femi Branch opens up on two years out of work after on-set accident

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Actor Femi Branch says a stunt that went wrong on a film set in Ikorodu left him unable to work for two years, forcing him to sell his late mother's car. He said some colleagues abandoned him.

Nollywood actor Femi Branch has opened up about a difficult period in his life after a serious accident on a film set left him unable to work for two years.

The accident

In an interview that surfaced online on Friday, 20 November 2026, Branch said he was injured while shooting a stunt in Ikorodu, Lagos. He said he had to jump from a storey building while dressed as a security guard, and the impact when he landed was so severe it sounded like an explosion.

Hard times

He said the injury kept him out of work for two years, leaving him isolated and in financial difficulty. He revealed that he had to sell his mother's car during that period, and said some colleagues deserted him while he was recovering.
