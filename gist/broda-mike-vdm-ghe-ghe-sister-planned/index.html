Broda Mike says Ghe Ghe's sister's online attack was planned, replying to VeryDarkMan

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

Skit maker Broda Mike has made a surprising claim in his reply to social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), saying a recent online family drama involving internet personality Ghe Ghe was planned.

What he said

VDM had reportedly claimed that he helped Ghe Ghe when Ghe Ghe's sister publicly criticised him online. Responding in a video shared on Monday, 2 November 2026, Broda Mike said that he and Ghe Ghe had planned for the sister to drag Ghe Ghe on social media. He did not explain why, or whether the sister knew it was staged.

How people reacted

The claim drew mixed reactions. Many users said it confirmed their suspicion that much online drama is staged for content, and some said they felt deceived. One user noted that Ghe Ghe's sister had gone further than any plan by publicly claiming she had not received ₦5 million. Others mocked everyone involved. Ghe Ghe and his sister have not publicly responded to Broda Mike's claim.

Background

VDM has been in several online disputes in recent weeks, including with Davido's camp, Zlatan Ibile, former BBNaija winner Phyna and businessman Blord. He has also been promoting his Ratel brand of power products, which he says sold ₦90 million worth in one day. Content creators have increasingly been accused of staging arguments and family disputes to attract views, and the latest claim is likely to add to that debate.

What happens next

It is not yet clear whether VDM will respond, but fans expect the exchange to continue online.

Late banker Herbert Wigwe's daughter Tochi marries Wilson Aikhomu

5 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Tochi Wigwe, the eldest daughter of the late Access Holdings CEO Herbert Wigwe, has held her traditional wedding to investment executive Wilson Aikhomu in Lagos. The groom was a close friend of her late brother, Chizi.

Read more

Otutochi 'Tochi' Wigwe, the eldest daughter of the late banker Herbert Wigwe, has married her partner, Wilson Omogbale Aikhomu, in a traditional ceremony in Lagos.

The wedding

The private ceremony, reported on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, brought both families together for traditional rites, including payment of the bride price, signing of family documents and the formal handing over of the bride to her husband's family. A video from the event spread widely online.

The couple

Tochi, 28, now leads the HOW Foundation set up by her father. Wilson Aikhomu is a Lagos-based investment professional and managing partner of an investment firm. He is the son of the late Rear Admiral Joseph Aikhomu and a nephew of the late Admiral Augustus Aikhomu, who served as Chief of General Staff under the military government of Ibrahim Babangida. He was a close friend of Tochi's late brother, Chizi.

Background

Herbert Wigwe, then chief executive of Access Holdings, died alongside his wife, Chizoba, and their son, Chizi, in a helicopter crash in California on 9 February 2024. Their deaths shocked Nigeria's business community and led to tributes across Africa. Tochi has since taken on a public role in continuing her father's philanthropic work.

How people reacted

Many people congratulated the couple and said the wedding brought joy to a family that had suffered a great loss, with some expressing emotion that her parents and brother could not be there.

The exchange began on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, when Olunloyo posted a message on Instagram directed at Regina Daniels's fans, a day after the actress's estranged husband, Senator Ned Nwoko, asked the public to stop spreading false stories about her health and threatened legal action against those responsible.

The exchange

Regina Daniels reportedly responded by telling Olunloyo not to feel accomplished and questioning whether her comments were motivated by money. Olunloyo hit back, objecting to an insult directed at her and questioning who was running the actress's social media page.

Background

Regina Daniels has recently shared videos online, including one of herself swimming, after a period of low activity that led to speculation about her health. Fans were divided, with many defending Regina Daniels and urging people to leave her alone, while others said both women should end the argument.
