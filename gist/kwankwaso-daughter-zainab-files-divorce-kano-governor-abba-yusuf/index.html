After 20 years of marriage, Kwankwaso's daughter Zainab files for divorce from Kano Governor Abba Yusuf, offers to refund dowry

14 Nov 2026, 17:44 GMT

Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, daughter of former Kano governor and senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, has reportedly filed for divorce from her husband, Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf, after 20 years of marriage.

The court case

The case was filed at the Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja. A civil summons directs Governor Yusuf to appear in court on Tuesday, 17 November 2026.

Zainab is reported to be seeking khul'i, a process under Islamic law that allows a wife to end her marriage, and has offered to refund the dowry Yusuf paid when they married.

The summons is not a divorce order, and the court has yet to rule on the matter.

Her reasons

According to the filing, Zainab cited a long absence of mutual care, love and emotional connection in the marriage, and said reconciliation efforts had failed.

The couple married on 29 July 2006, when Zainab was working as an aide to her father, who was then governor of Kano State. They have six children.

The political backdrop

The marriage once symbolised the close bond between Kwankwaso and Yusuf, his long-time political protégé. That relationship has since broken down.

In January 2026, Governor Yusuf left Kwankwaso's party, the New Nigeria Peoples Party (NNPP), for the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC), a move Kwankwaso's camp described as a betrayal.

Some reports have linked the political split to tension in the marriage, but the reasons given in the court filing focus on the breakdown of the relationship itself.

No comment yet

As of Saturday, neither Governor Yusuf, Zainab nor Senator Kwankwaso had made a public statement on the divorce, and the Kano State Government had not commented.

INEC says Atiku stays on candidates' list until courts settle ADC dispute

19 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

INEC has explained that Atiku Abubakar's name will remain on its list as the ADC presidential candidate until the courts resolve the party's leadership dispute. The ADC has appealed the judgment that voided his nomination.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has explained why Atiku Abubakar's name still appears on its website as the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate for the 2027 election.

INEC's position

A senior INEC official is reported to have said the commission will not make any changes until the courts settle the leadership dispute within the party.

The court case

On 5 November 2026, Justice Peter Lifu of the Federal High Court delivered a judgment that affected the David Mark-led leadership of the ADC and voided the process through which Atiku became the party's candidate. The party has filed an appeal raising 17 grounds and asked the court to suspend the judgment's implementation until the matter is decided.

What it means

For now, Atiku and his running mate, former Rivers governor Rotimi Amaechi, remain on INEC's list ahead of the 16 January 2027 election. The outcome of the appeal will determine whether that changes.

APGA formally adopts Tinubu as its 2027 presidential candidate

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The All Progressives Grand Alliance has formally adopted President Tinubu as its candidate for 2027. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo handed the party flag to Vice President Shettima.

Read more

The All Progressives Grand Alliance (APGA) has formally adopted President Bola Tinubu as its presidential candidate for the 2027 general elections.

The ceremony

Anambra State Governor and APGA leader Charles Soludo presided over a ceremony on Thursday, 19 November 2026, at which he presented the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima, who received it on the President's behalf.

What it means

Tinubu is already the presidential candidate of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC). APGA's adoption means the party will back him rather than field its own candidate, marking a significant alliance between the party and the federal government.

Background

APGA controls Anambra State, the home state of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi.

The other side

No reaction from the NDC or the ADC to the endorsement was included in the reports.

Anambra government says Peter Obi billboard was removed over unpaid signage fee

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Anambra State Government has confirmed it ordered the removal of a billboard featuring NDC candidate Peter Obi and Senator Victor Umeh. It said Umeh paid the fee but Obi did not.

Read more

The Anambra State Government has confirmed that it ordered the removal of a billboard showing Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

The government's explanation

The state government, led by Governor Charles Soludo, said the billboard was taken down because the required signage fee had not been paid for Obi's image.

The billboard belonged to Victor Umeh, the senator representing Anambra Central and the NDC candidate for the district, and featured photographs of both Umeh and Obi. According to the government, Umeh had paid the fee for his own image, but no fee had been paid for Obi's.

Context

The government's explanation came on the same day Governor Soludo's party, APGA, formally adopted President Bola Tinubu as its candidate for 2027. Obi is a former governor of Anambra State.

The other side

Reactions from Obi or the NDC to the government's explanation were not included in the reports.

Atiku asks INEC to make public details of AI system planned for 2027 elections

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has written to INEC asking it to disclose full details, including source code, of the artificial intelligence tools it plans to use in the 2027 elections.

Read more

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has asked the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) to make public all details of the artificial intelligence system it plans to use in the 2027 general elections.

The letter

According to his campaign's director of strategic communication, Phrank Shaibu, Atiku wrote directly to the INEC Chairman on behalf of the ADC and allied stakeholders. The statement was issued on Thursday, 19 November 2026.

He warned that Nigerians cannot be asked to trust a process they are not allowed to examine, and called for transparency about the technology, including its source code.

Background

The request follows INEC's announced plan to integrate AI and other intelligent systems into its election processes. AI has also become a flashpoint in the campaign, with the main parties accusing each other of using AI-generated images.

INEC's position

No response from INEC to the letter was included in the reports.
