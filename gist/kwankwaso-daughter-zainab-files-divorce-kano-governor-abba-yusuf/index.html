After 20 years of marriage, Kwankwaso's daughter Zainab files for divorce from Kano Governor Abba Yusuf, offers to refund dowry

14 Nov 2026, 17:44 GMT

Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, daughter of former Kano governor and senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, has reportedly filed for divorce from her husband, Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf, after 20 years of marriage.

The court case

The case was filed at the Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja. A civil summons directs Governor Yusuf to appear in court on Tuesday, 17 November 2026.

Zainab is reported to be seeking khul'i, a process under Islamic law that allows a wife to end her marriage, and has offered to refund the dowry Yusuf paid when they married.

The summons is not a divorce order, and the court has yet to rule on the matter.

Her reasons

According to the filing, Zainab cited a long absence of mutual care, love and emotional connection in the marriage, and said reconciliation efforts had failed.

The couple married on 29 July 2006, when Zainab was working as an aide to her father, who was then governor of Kano State. They have six children.

The political backdrop

The marriage once symbolised the close bond between Kwankwaso and Yusuf, his long-time political protégé. That relationship has since broken down.

In January 2026, Governor Yusuf left Kwankwaso's party, the New Nigeria Peoples Party (NNPP), for the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC), a move Kwankwaso's camp described as a betrayal.

Some reports have linked the political split to tension in the marriage, but the reasons given in the court filing focus on the breakdown of the relationship itself.

No comment yet

As of Saturday, neither Governor Yusuf, Zainab nor Senator Kwankwaso had made a public statement on the divorce, and the Kano State Government had not commented.

Sowore calls for legal cannabis, says Nigeria could earn up to ₦2.86 trillion a year

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has urged the federal government to legalise, regulate and tax cannabis. He pointed to revenues in Canada and US states and projected Nigeria could earn between ₦715 billion and ₦2.86 trillion yearly.

Read more

African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has called on the federal government to legalise cannabis, arguing that Nigeria is missing out on billions of naira in revenue by keeping it illegal.

What he said

In a post on X on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, Sowore said the country should build a regulated cannabis industry instead of spending resources arresting people and destroying plantations. He said he has backed legalisation since 2018.

The figures

He pointed to regulated markets abroad, citing about CA$2.2 billion in government revenue from recreational cannabis in Canada in 2023-2024, around $1.067 billion in cannabis tax in California in 2024, and roughly $255.4 million in Colorado.

Using those examples, he projected that Nigeria could earn between $550 million and $2.2 billion a year, or about ₦715 billion to ₦2.86 trillion at an exchange rate of ₦1,300 to the dollar. He acknowledged that actual earnings would depend on how any such market is designed and run.

He also argued that legalisation could create jobs in farming, pharmaceutical production and industrial hemp, and support research and exports.

Background

Cannabis remains illegal in Nigeria, and the NDLEA regularly carries out raids on farms and arrests dealers. Sowore, a long-time activist, is running for president in 2027 on the AAC platform.

Labour Party factional candidate Kennedy Ahanotu quits presidential race, backs Tinubu

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Kennedy Ahanotu, presidential candidate of the Abure-led Labour Party faction, has reportedly withdrawn from the 2027 race and endorsed President Tinubu. He blamed the party's long-running internal crisis.

Read more

Kennedy Ahanotu, who was chosen as presidential candidate by the Julius Abure-led faction of the Labour Party, has reportedly withdrawn from the 2027 presidential race and thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu.

What happened

Ahanotu is said to have announced the decision at a press briefing in Abuja on Monday, 16 November 2026. He said he would work with the City Boy Movement, a pro-Tinubu group, to mobilise support for the president's re-election.

The 42-year-old former national youth leader of the Labour Party, described as an ally of the party's 2023 candidate Peter Obi, reportedly said the move followed consultations with youth leaders from across the country, who concluded that the party's continuing internal crisis made a new direction necessary.

Background

Ahanotu was adopted as the factional candidate at primaries held in Abuja in May 2026, when the Labour Party was divided between the Abure-led group and the leadership headed by Senator Nenadi Usman. Peter Obi has since moved on to the NDC, where he is running with Rabiu Kwankwaso.

Betrayal claims

Responding to suggestions that he had betrayed the opposition, Ahanotu reportedly argued that it was the party that had failed its long-standing members, noting that he had joined it in 2016.

No response from either Labour Party faction was included in the reports.

Makinde warns NURTW against taking over Oyo motor parks after seven-day ultimatum

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly warned the NURTW not to forcibly take over motor parks. The union had given his government until 23 November to withdraw its Park Management System, citing court rulings.

Read more

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly warned the National Union of Road Transport Workers (NURTW) not to take over public motor parks by force, after the union issued an ultimatum to his government.

The ultimatum

On Sunday, the Oyo NURTW reportedly gave the government until 23 November to withdraw its Park Management System from parks across the state's 33 local government areas. The union said it would otherwise begin enforcing court judgments it says recognise its right to run the parks.

Its leaders, Abideen Olajide and Lekan Alesinloye, accused the administration of ignoring several rulings in the union's favour and said members had been excluded for seven and a half years. They said they had informed the federal government, the army, the DSS, the police and the Nigeria Labour Congress of their plans.

The governor's response

In a statement issued on Monday through his special adviser on media, Dr Sulaimon Olanrewaju, Makinde said his government remained committed to the rule of law and urged residents to stay calm. He insisted the union would not be allowed to take over the parks.

Background

The Makinde administration previously banned the NURTW in Oyo State and set up the Park Management System to run motor parks, a move that has been the subject of legal and political disputes since.

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf confirms divorce from Kwankwaso's daughter, says he will marry again

16 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso, daughter of his former political mentor Rabiu Kwankwaso, has ended. Speaking with humour at a pensioners' event, he said the dissolution papers had been issued and that he still intends to take another wife.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, the daughter of former Kano governor and NDC leader Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, has ended.

What the governor said

Yusuf is said to have spoken about the matter on Monday, 16 November 2026, while addressing pensioners at an event at the Government House in Kano. Speaking in Hausa and in a light-hearted tone, he compared going from two wives to one to a demotion in military rank, from corporal to constable.

He reportedly confirmed that he had agreed to the divorce and that the papers for the dissolution had been issued. He also told the gathering that he still plans to take another wife, and asked residents to pray that he finds a suitable partner. He said he was at peace with the decision.

Background

The confirmation came two days after reports that Zainab Kwankwaso had filed a divorce petition at an Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja. She was said to be the governor's second wife.

The separation has drawn wide attention because of the long political relationship between Yusuf and Kwankwaso, who was his political mentor.

Dalung's criticism

Former sports minister Solomon Dalung has criticised the governor over his public remarks on the divorce. He said a man does not need to humiliate the mother of his children to show that a marriage is over.

Reports say the governor told a gathering that the papers had been granted and joked that an advert would be placed for anyone wishing to fill the vacant position.

The political backdrop

Yusuf won the Kano governorship on the NNPP platform with Kwankwaso's backing, but later defected to the APC. The political fallout between the two men, which began about a year ago, is said to have strained the marriage.
