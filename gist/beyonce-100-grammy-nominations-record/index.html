Beyoncé reportedly becomes first artist with 100 Grammy nominations

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Beyoncé has reportedly become the first artist in history to receive 100 Grammy nominations, after picking up a new nod in the nominations for the 69th Annual Grammy Awards announced this week.

The nomination

The singer is nominated for Best R&B Solo Performance for Morning Dew (Donk), a song she released as a surprise on 4 July 2026. A remix featuring her husband, Jay-Z, followed a month later. She faces Kehlani, Victoria Monét, Jill Scott and Nigeria's Tems in the category.

A record career

Beyoncé was already the most-decorated artist in Grammy history, with 35 wins. Jay-Z is said to be close behind her on the all-time nominations list, now with 90.

The Nigerian angle

Tems' place in the same category means Nigerian fans will be watching this race closely. She is one of several Nigerian stars recognised in this year's nominations, alongside Davido and Ayra Starr, who are nominated for Best Global Music Album.

What happens next

Winners will be announced at the 2027 Grammy Awards ceremony early next year.

  • Her nomination is for Morning Dew (Donk).
  • Tems is nominated in the same category.
  • Jay-Z is reported to be second with 90 nominations.

Afrobeats star Davido has kicked off his birthday celebrations early, with a surprise party on board his father's private jet and a new initiative to support young Nigerian entrepreneurs.

Party in the sky

On Friday, 20 November 2026, his team reportedly surprised him with a celebration on the jet. Videos shared online showed Davido seated beside his wife, Chioma, as members of his 30BG crew sang Happy Birthday. The singer, in a playful mood, licked icing off the cake before cutting it, as champagne and finger food were served.

Startup funding

In a post on Instagram the same day, Davido announced that his foundation would expand its usual birthday charity work by providing startup funds to aspiring entrepreneurs, giving them a chance to turn their ideas into businesses, ahead of his birthday on Saturday, 21 November.

Background

His birthday this year comes amid debate over his recent photo with Seyi Tinubu, which VeryDarkMan criticised.

Turning 34

On Saturday, 21 November, Davido turned 34. His wife Chioma marked the day by sharing a childhood photo of him on her Instagram story with a short, affectionate caption, which the singer reposted.

Chioma's tribute

Later on his birthday, Chioma posted a longer tribute on Instagram calling Davido her forever home, alongside never-before-seen photos from her pregnancy and birthday wishes from their twins.

Jim Iyke finally shows off his wife after almost five years together

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has shared photos and videos of his wife for the first time, revealing how they met on a flight to Houston almost five years ago.

Read more

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has introduced his wife to the public for the first time, sharing loved-up photos and videos of the couple on Instagram.

How they met

In the post, the actor said they met almost five years ago in an airport lounge before a flight to Houston. He recalled asking if the seat next to her was free, and said she firmly told him it was taken, but he sat down anyway. They ignored each other for an hour, then burst out laughing when they realised they were on the same flight.

His tribute

Jim Iyke described their relationship as something sacred rather than hidden, and said they have built a family together and travelled to eight countries. He called her his best friend, his prayer warrior and his wife, and said they have never stopped laughing together.
