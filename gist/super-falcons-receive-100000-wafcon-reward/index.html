Super Falcons finally receive $100,000 each for 2024 WAFCON win

3 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Members of Nigeria's Super Falcons have reportedly begun receiving the cash reward promised by President Bola Tinubu after the team won the 2024 Women's Africa Cup of Nations (WAFCON).

What happened

According to reports, two players and a member of the technical crew confirmed that their accounts were credited on Monday, 2 November 2026. Each of the 24 players is said to have received the naira equivalent of $100,000, about ₦140 million, while each of the 11 members of the technical crew received the naira equivalent of $50,000. One player reportedly said she was grateful that the promise had finally been kept.

Other rewards

Apart from the cash, the President also approved national honours and three-bedroom apartments for the players and officials after the victory.

Background

The Super Falcons won a record-extending 10th continental title in Morocco in July 2025, coming from behind to beat the hosts in the final. The tournament, originally scheduled for 2024, was held a year later but kept its 2024 name. The win was celebrated across Nigeria, and the team was received by the President at the State House, where the rewards were announced. Delays in paying promised bonuses to national teams have been a long-running complaint in Nigerian sport, and some fans had questioned whether this reward would ever be paid.

How people reacted

Fans welcomed the news, saying the players deserved the money and calling on the government to pay other national teams promptly in future. Others said the long delay showed the need for clear and timely reward systems.

  • Technical crew members got $50,000 each.
  • The Falcons won their 10th title in Morocco in July 2025.

FIFA appoints normalisation committee to run NFF, names Amokachi and Enakhena

19 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

FIFA has named lawyer Sampson Ebomhe to chair a normalisation committee for the Nigeria Football Federation ahead of its 2027 elections. Daniel Amokachi, Godwin Enakhena, Amina Yerima and Joe Abah are also members.

Read more

FIFA has set up a normalisation committee to run the Nigeria Football Federation (NFF), appointing lawyer Sampson Ebomhe as chairman with immediate effect.

The members

The world governing body named four other members: sports journalist and administrator Godwin Enakhena, former Super Eagles captain Daniel Amokachi, Amina Yerima and Dr Joe Abah, former Director-General of the Bureau of Public Service Reforms.

FIFA's Chief Member Associations Officer, Elkhan Mammadov, confirmed the appointments in a letter, noting that all members are still subject to an eligibility check by the FIFA Review Committee before they are formally confirmed.

Background

Amokachi, nicknamed The Bull, played at two World Cups for Nigeria and is one of the country's best-known football figures.

Super Eagles climb to 29th in FIFA rankings despite Tanzania draw

19 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Nigeria have moved up one place to 29th in the latest FIFA world rankings and are back in Africa's top five. The boost comes despite the goalless draw with Tanzania that hurt their AFCON hopes.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles have moved up one place to 29th in the latest FIFA world rankings, ending their 2026 international calendar with a small boost.

The ranking

The Super Eagles climbed from 30th to 29th in the world and moved back into Africa's top five, after slipping out of that group earlier in the year.

The results behind it

Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 in Uyo before playing out a goalless draw in the return leg in Zanzibar. The draw left them on seven points, three behind group leaders Guinea-Bissau, making their path to AFCON 2027 more complicated.

Anthony Joshua welcomes baby girl with childhood sweetheart

19 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Boxer Anthony Joshua has welcomed a baby daughter with his longtime partner Nicole Osbourne. He said surviving last year's car crash in Nigeria made him want to bring new life into the world.

Read more

British-Nigerian heavyweight boxer Anthony Joshua has welcomed a baby girl with his longtime partner and childhood sweetheart, Nicole Osbourne.

What he said

Joshua said the baby arrived about three weeks earlier than expected. He explained that nearly losing his life last Christmas pushed him to make a deliberate choice to bring new life into the world.

The 35-year-old, who already has an 11-year-old son, JJ, said the couple did not find out the baby's sex during the pregnancy, so having a daughter was a surprise. He spoke warmly about becoming a girl dad.

Background

Joshua survived a car crash on the Lagos-Ibadan expressway in Nigeria late last year that killed two of his friends and trainers, Latif Ayodele and Sina Ghami.

What's next

The birth comes ahead of his planned bout with Tyson Fury.

Lookman faces backlash over video with Burna Boy on night of Tanzania draw

18 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Fans have criticised Ademola Lookman after a video reportedly showed him with Burna Boy in Lagos on the evening the Super Eagles drew with Tanzania. He had left the squad with a knee injury.

Read more

Super Eagles forward Ademola Lookman has come under fire from Nigerian fans after a video reportedly showed him socialising with Burna Boy in Lagos on the same evening the national team drew 0-0 with Tanzania.

Why fans are angry

Lookman had been excused from the squad before the second leg in Zanzibar, with the team citing a knee injury he picked up in the first leg in Uyo on 13 November. He was said to have been sent back to his club, Atletico Madrid, so its medical staff could assess the problem.

Many fans questioned why he appeared to be in Lagos instead, and criticised him for being seen out while the team struggled.

The bigger picture

The draw left Nigeria on seven points, three behind Guinea-Bissau, putting their AFCON 2027 qualification hopes under serious pressure.

No response yet

Lookman, the 2024 CAF Men's Player of the Year, had not publicly responded to the criticism at the time of the reports.
