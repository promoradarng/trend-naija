Super Falcons finally receive $100,000 each for 2024 WAFCON win

3 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Members of Nigeria's Super Falcons have reportedly begun receiving the cash reward promised by President Bola Tinubu after the team won the 2024 Women's Africa Cup of Nations (WAFCON).

What happened

According to reports, two players and a member of the technical crew confirmed that their accounts were credited on Monday, 2 November 2026. Each of the 24 players is said to have received the naira equivalent of $100,000, about ₦140 million, while each of the 11 members of the technical crew received the naira equivalent of $50,000. One player reportedly said she was grateful that the promise had finally been kept.

Other rewards

Apart from the cash, the President also approved national honours and three-bedroom apartments for the players and officials after the victory.

Background

The Super Falcons won a record-extending 10th continental title in Morocco in July 2025, coming from behind to beat the hosts in the final. The tournament, originally scheduled for 2024, was held a year later but kept its 2024 name. The win was celebrated across Nigeria, and the team was received by the President at the State House, where the rewards were announced. Delays in paying promised bonuses to national teams have been a long-running complaint in Nigerian sport, and some fans had questioned whether this reward would ever be paid.

How people reacted

Fans welcomed the news, saying the players deserved the money and calling on the government to pay other national teams promptly in future. Others said the long delay showed the need for clear and timely reward systems.

  • Each of the 24 players reportedly received about ₦140 million.
  • Technical crew members got $50,000 each.
  • The Falcons won their 10th title in Morocco in July 2025.

Ex-Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami survives attack at his Abeokuta home

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Former Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami says he fought off three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta in the early hours of Friday. Police say he has been treated and discharged from hospital.

Read more

Former Super Eagles captain and football administrator Segun Odegbami has survived an attack by three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta, Ogun State, in the early hours of Friday, 20 November 2026.

What happened

The attackers reportedly overpowered his domestic staff and driver before forcing their way into his bedroom at about 2:30am. The 74-year-old was injured in the attack.

Odegbami described the incident as an attempt on his life. He said he fought back with help from his driver and house help, and the attackers eventually fled. He said it was the closest he had ever come to death.

Police response

The Ogun State Police Command confirmed the attack and said the former international had been treated in hospital and discharged.

Who he is

Odegbami is a former captain of the Super Eagles and one of Nigeria's best-known football figures.

Manhunt ordered

Ogun State Commissioner of Police Bode Ojajuni visited Odegbami at his home and ordered an immediate manhunt for the attackers, promising they would be prosecuted once caught.

FIFA President Infantino arrives in Warri for stadium commissioning

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has arrived in Warri, Delta State, for the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium. He was received by Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former NFF president Amaju Pinnick.

Read more

FIFA President Gianni Infantino arrived in Warri, Delta State, on Friday, 20 November 2026, ahead of the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium.

The arrival

Infantino landed at Osubi Airport on a private jet and was received by Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former Nigeria Football Federation president Amaju Pinnick. He said the warm welcome showed the unifying power of football.

Third visit

It is Infantino's third visit to Nigeria. He first came in 2016 shortly after taking office, and returned in 2023 for the Aisha Buhari Cup.

Background

The visit comes days after FIFA appointed a normalisation committee to run the NFF, and as Infantino prepares to face challengers in the FIFA presidential election in March 2027.

Former senator and Adamawa State governorship candidate Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, popularly known as Binani, has announced the death of her father.

The announcement

In a post on social media, Binani said her father died after a brief illness in Abuja. She opened her message with the Islamic expression of condolence and thanked God despite the loss.

Funeral arrangements

She said the funeral prayer, known as Jana'iza, would take place on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the Central Mosque after the afternoon Zuhr prayer.

Who she is

Tinubu, Atiku, Peter Obi and three others invited to presidential debate on 10 January

21 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A political group has announced a presidential debate in Abuja on 10 January 2027, six days before the election, inviting Tinubu, Atiku, Peter Obi, Seyi Makinde, Donald Duke and Adewole Adebayo.

Read more

Six presidential candidates have reportedly been invited to a policy debate in Abuja on 10 January 2027, six days before the presidential election.

The announcement

The convener of the organising group, Lai Omotola, announced the plan at a press conference in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. He said the debate is designed to push the candidates beyond campaign slogans and into detailed discussions of their policies.

Who is invited

The six candidates invited are President Bola Tinubu of the APC, Atiku Abubakar of the ADC, Peter Obi of the NDC, Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde, former Cross River governor Donald Duke and the SDP's Adewole Adebayo.

Will they attend?

It was not clear from the reports whether all the candidates have confirmed they will take part.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
