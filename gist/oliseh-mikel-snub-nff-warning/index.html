Oliseh says Mikel snubbed him as Super Eagles coach and NFF warned him about three stars

29 Oct 2026, 17:46 GMT

Former Super Eagles head coach Sunday Oliseh has reopened old wounds from his time in charge of the national team, reportedly describing a snub by John Mikel Obi as one of the biggest insults he suffered in the job.

What he said

Speaking on a podcast, in comments reported on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Oliseh is said to have explained that he paid for his own trip from Belgium to the United Kingdom to meet Mikel and Victor Moses at a bar in central London, together with his assistant coach. According to Oliseh, Moses came and gave honest feedback about problems in the squad, which he said he respected, but Mikel did not turn up and did not reply to his WhatsApp message.

The NFF warning

Oliseh also reportedly said that when he took the job, officials of the Nigeria Football Federation (NFF) told him to stop inviting Mikel, goalkeeper Vincent Enyeama and striker Emmanuel Emenike, describing the three as troublesome for the federation. He said he now regrets not following that advice. The NFF has not commented on his account, and Mikel has not publicly responded.

Background

Oliseh, a former Super Eagles captain, coached the national team from 2015 until he resigned in 2016. His spell was marked by public disputes with players and officials. Enyeama retired from international football during that period after a falling-out with the coach. Mikel, who captained Nigeria and won the 2013 Africa Cup of Nations, has since become a popular podcaster and pundit.

How fans reacted

Fans were divided, with some saying the former coach should let the past go and others saying his account helps explain the tension in the team at the time.

  • He claims the NFF warned him against inviting Mikel, Enyeama and Emenike.
  • Mikel has not publicly responded.

Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly taken over as Manchester United manager, following the club's decision to sack Michael Carrick.

His arrival

The Argentine flew into England on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, after his final game in charge of the United States men's national team.

A tough start

His first match is a daunting one: a trip to Anfield to face league leaders Liverpool just five days after arriving. United are currently in the Premier League relegation zone.

A statistical model reportedly gives Liverpool a 57.3% chance of winning the game, with United at 20.5% and a draw at 22.2%.

His first interview

In his first sit-down interview as head coach, the 54-year-old said changing the culture at the club is his top priority, stressing the need for a winning mentality and consistent daily habits. United are 18th with just eight points from their first 10 games, their worst top-flight start since 1984.

Transfer plans

Reports have also linked Pochettino with transfer targets for January, with Nigeria defender Calvin Bassey said to be on his shopping list. Fulham are reportedly asking for around £50 million for the Super Eagles star.

Background

Pochettino previously managed Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea before taking the United States job.

Marathon world champion Peres Jepchirchir banned for three years for doping

19 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Kenya's Peres Jepchirchir, the reigning marathon world champion and Tokyo Olympic gold medallist, has been banned for three years after testing positive for EPO. Two other Kenyan runners were also banned.

Read more

Reigning marathon world champion Peres Jepchirchir of Kenya has been banned for three years for doping, the Athletics Integrity Unit (AIU) announced on Thursday, 19 November 2026.

The ban

The 33-year-old, who also won Olympic gold at the Tokyo Games in 2021, was suspended after an out-of-competition sample showed traces of the banned blood booster EPO. Her ban runs from 20 October.

Others banned

Two other Kenyan runners were also sanctioned. Veronica Loleo, a half marathon silver medallist at this year's World Road Running Championships, received three years for the same offence, while Ronald Korir, fourth at the 2023 Berlin Marathon, was banned for six years over abnormalities in his biological passport.

A wider problem

They join more than 140 Kenyan athletes, including women's marathon world record-holder Ruth Chepngetich, who have been suspended or banned by the AIU since 2017. The AIU acknowledged that Kenyan authorities have made progress against doping but said these cases show there is still a long way to go.

Opposition coalition backing Atiku, Peter Obi and Makinde's parties moves summit to 30 November

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Coalition for Democracy, which includes opposition parties linked to Atiku Abubakar, Peter Obi and Seyi Makinde, has moved its second summit from 23 to 30 November. It said candidates' campaign schedules made the original date impossible.

Read more

The Coalition for Democracy, an alliance of opposition parties ahead of the 2027 elections, has reportedly postponed its second summit by one week.

The new date

The coalition's Central Leadership Committee announced on Friday, 20 November 2026, that the summit would now hold on Monday, 30 November, instead of Monday, 23 November.

Why it was moved

The committee said the request came from member parties whose presidential candidates and campaign teams are busy with intensive campaigns across the country. It said it was more important for every party, presidential candidate and national chairman to attend than to keep the original date.

Who is involved

The coalition brings together parties linked to ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar, NDC candidate Peter Obi and Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde. Member parties are expected to pause their nationwide campaigns to attend.

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.
