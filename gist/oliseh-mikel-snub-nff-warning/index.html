Oliseh says Mikel snubbed him as Super Eagles coach and NFF warned him about three stars

29 Oct 2026, 17:46 GMT

Former Super Eagles head coach Sunday Oliseh has reopened old wounds from his time in charge of the national team, reportedly describing a snub by John Mikel Obi as one of the biggest insults he suffered in the job.

What he said

Speaking on a podcast, in comments reported on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Oliseh is said to have explained that he paid for his own trip from Belgium to the United Kingdom to meet Mikel and Victor Moses at a bar in central London, together with his assistant coach. According to Oliseh, Moses came and gave honest feedback about problems in the squad, which he said he respected, but Mikel did not turn up and did not reply to his WhatsApp message.

The NFF warning

Oliseh also reportedly said that when he took the job, officials of the Nigeria Football Federation (NFF) told him to stop inviting Mikel, goalkeeper Vincent Enyeama and striker Emmanuel Emenike, describing the three as troublesome for the federation. He said he now regrets not following that advice. The NFF has not commented on his account, and Mikel has not publicly responded.

Background

Oliseh, a former Super Eagles captain, coached the national team from 2015 until he resigned in 2016. His spell was marked by public disputes with players and officials. Enyeama retired from international football during that period after a falling-out with the coach. Mikel, who captained Nigeria and won the 2013 Africa Cup of Nations, has since become a popular podcaster and pundit.

How fans reacted

Fans were divided, with some saying the former coach should let the past go and others saying his account helps explain the tension in the team at the time.

  • Oliseh says Mikel ignored a meeting he travelled to London for.
  • He claims the NFF warned him against inviting Mikel, Enyeama and Emenike.
  • Mikel has not publicly responded.

FIFA President Gianni Infantino arrived in Warri, Delta State, on Friday, 20 November 2026, ahead of the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium.

The arrival

Infantino landed at Osubi Airport on a private jet and was received by Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former Nigeria Football Federation president Amaju Pinnick. He said the warm welcome showed the unifying power of football.

Third visit

Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly taken over as Manchester United manager, following the club's decision to sack Michael Carrick.

His arrival

The Argentine flew into England on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, after his final game in charge of the United States men's national team.

A tough start

His first match is a daunting one: a trip to Anfield to face league leaders Liverpool just five days after arriving. United are currently in the Premier League relegation zone.

A statistical model reportedly gives Liverpool a 57.3% chance of winning the game, with United at 20.5% and a draw at 22.2%.

In his first sit-down interview as head coach, the 54-year-old said changing the culture at the club is his top priority, stressing the need for a winning mentality and consistent daily habits. United are 18th with just eight points from their first 10 games, their worst top-flight start since 1984.

Reports have also linked Pochettino with transfer targets for January, with Nigeria defender Calvin Bassey said to be on his shopping list. Fulham are reportedly asking for around £50 million for the Super Eagles star.

Court rejects Atiku's bid to add new evidence to suit against Tinubu's eligibility

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

A Federal High Court in Abuja has dismissed an application by Atiku Abubakar to introduce fresh evidence into his suit challenging President Tinubu's eligibility for 2027. The judge said the constitutional 14-day window had passed.

Read more

A Federal High Court in Abuja has thrown out an application by ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar to bring new evidence into his suit challenging President Bola Tinubu's eligibility to contest the 2027 election.

The ruling

Justice Inyang Ekwo held that Section 285(9) of the Constitution allows only 14 days for evidence to be filed in a pre-election case. He found that Atiku's request to amend his originating summons would effectively bring in fresh evidence after that period had expired, which would breach the Constitution.

What it means

The ruling is a setback for Atiku's legal challenge, although the main suit itself was not decided in the reports. Atiku's camp had not responded publicly to the ruling in the reports.

Background

Atiku is also involved in a separate legal battle over his ADC candidacy, after a court on 5 November voided the process that produced him as the party's candidate. INEC has said he will remain on its list until that dispute is settled.
