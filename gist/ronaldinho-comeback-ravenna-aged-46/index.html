Ronaldinho set for competitive comeback at 46 with Italian club Ravenna

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Former Barcelona, PSG and AC Milan star Ronaldinho is set to return to competitive football at the age of 46.

The comeback

Ravenna manager Andrea Mandorlini confirmed on Friday, 13 November 2026, that the Brazilian will start for the Italian Serie C club in a local derby against Forli on Sunday, 15 November, as one of three forwards. Mandorlini described him as one of the greatest players of the modern era, adding that the coaching staff would decide together how long he plays.

A long wait

Ronaldinho joined Ravenna as an investor in August. His last competitive appearance was for Brazilian club Fluminense in September 2015, making Sunday's match his first in more than ten years.

Background

Ronaldinho won the Ballon d'Or in 2005 and the World Cup with Brazil in 2002, and is remembered as one of the most gifted and entertaining players of his generation.

  • Ronaldinho will start against Forli on Sunday.
  • It is his first competitive match since 2015.
  • He joined Ravenna as an investor in August.

American boxing legend Floyd Mayweather has been spotted training in Abuja during a visit to Nigeria.

Why he is in Nigeria

Mayweather arrived in the capital on Thursday, 13 November 2026, ahead of a press conference the next day to launch the TMT Frontrow Fight Series, a programme designed to give young Nigerian boxers access to professional coaching, international exposure and competitive fights. Portugal retires Ronaldo's number 7 shirt design after end of his international career

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Portugal's football federation has permanently changed the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt in honour of Cristiano Ronaldo, whose international career has ended, and players will all wear 7 during their walk-out against Denmark.

Read more

Portugal's football federation has honoured Cristiano Ronaldo by permanently changing the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt.

The tribute

The Portuguese Football Federation announced on X that the style of number 7 worn by Ronaldo will not be used by any other player, and a new version will appear on future shirts. According to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026, every Portugal player will wear a number 7 shirt during the walk-out before their Nations League match against Denmark as a collective farewell.

How it ended

Ronaldo's international career reportedly came to an end after a public disagreement with Jorge Jesus over a substitution during a match in September.

Background

Ronaldo is Portugal's all-time record goalscorer and most-capped player and led the country to the Euro 2016 title. The gesture mirrors the honour Argentina gave Lionel Messi after his international retirement.

Lagos says construction of Epe International Airport will start in 2027

15 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Lagos State says work on the proposed Epe International Airport will begin in 2027, with Governor Sanwo-Olu announcing the timeline at an APC campaign event in Epe and his deputy saying preliminary work is well advanced.

Read more

The Lagos State Government plans to begin construction of the proposed Epe International Airport in 2027.

The announcement

According to a statement by the Lagos chapter of the All Progressives Congress (APC), reported on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu announced the timeline at an APC governorship campaign event in Epe. His deputy, Dr Obafemi Hamzat, said significant progress had been made on the documentation and groundwork needed for the project.

What it could mean

Lagos APC spokesman Seye Oladejo described the airport as a major infrastructure investment that could turn Epe into an important aviation, industrial and commercial hub, and said it would create opportunities for residents and businesses along the Lekki–Epe corridor.

Background

Lagos is currently served by Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, which handles most of Nigeria's international air traffic. The Lekki–Epe corridor has seen rapid development in recent years, including the Dangote Refinery and the Lekki Deep Sea Port. No cost or funding details were given in the latest announcement.

Otutochi 'Tochi' Wigwe, the eldest daughter of the late banker Herbert Wigwe, has married her partner, Wilson Omogbale Aikhomu, in a traditional ceremony in Lagos.

The ceremony

The wedding on Saturday, 14 November 2026, drew prominent guests including former Vice-President Yemi Osinbajo, Lagos governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, businessman Aliko Dangote, the Emir of Kano Muhammadu Sanusi II, banker Aigboje Aig-Imoukhuede and Seyi Tinubu. The couple

The groom is a Lagos-based investment professional and managing partner of an investment firm. He is the son of the late Rear Admiral Joseph Aikhomu and a nephew of former Chief of General Staff Admiral Augustus Aikhomu, and was a close friend of Tochi's late brother, Chizi. Tochi, 28, leads her late father's non-profit organisation.

Background

Herbert Wigwe, chief executive of Access Holdings, died in a helicopter crash in California in February 2024 alongside his wife, Chizoba, and son, Chizi.
