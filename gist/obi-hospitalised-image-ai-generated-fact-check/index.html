Viral image of Peter Obi in hospital after gym injury is AI-generated, checks show

9 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

A viral image claiming that Peter Obi, presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), was in hospital after a gym injury has been found to be fake.

The claim

The image was posted on X on Sunday, 1 November 2026, with a caption claiming Obi had injured his neck on a treadmill and been admitted to hospital. By Monday, 9 November, the post had been viewed more than 67,000 times, and similar posts had spread on other platforms.

The fact-check

According to a fact-check published on Monday, a reverse image search linked the picture to similar claims on other platforms, suggesting a wider effort to spread it. Several AI-detection tools found a high probability that the image was AI-generated, and a further check indicated it had been created with an AI image tool.

Background

Obi's campaign had earlier denied reports that he broke his neck in a gym accident, and the former Anambra governor has continued to campaign publicly, including at airports and rallies in recent days. Opposition parties have raised concerns about alleged AI-generated images in the voter register.

Why it matters

Fake images and videos of candidates are expected to be a growing problem in the 2027 campaign.

  • The image claimed Obi was hospitalised after a gym injury.
  • Detection tools found it was AI-generated.
  • Obi's camp had earlier denied the report.

NDC says supporters were attacked in Benin as Obi arrived for campaign

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Nigeria Democratic Congress has alleged that gunmen attacked its supporters and campaign vehicles on Airport Road, Benin City, as Peter Obi arrived in Edo State on Wednesday. It said one supporter was critically wounded.

Read more

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has alleged that its supporters were attacked in Benin City, Edo State, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, as its presidential candidate Peter Obi arrived for a town hall meeting and other campaign events.

What the party says

According to the party, attackers who arrived in a pickup van opened fire on its campaign vehicles parked along Airport Road while supporters waited for Obi. A party spokesperson posted videos online showing damaged vehicles and bloodstains, and said one supporter was critically wounded and receiving hospital treatment.

In a statement, the NDC's National Publicity Secretary said the attackers also assaulted people in the crowd and destroyed buses, and described the attack as unprovoked. The party blamed thugs it said were linked to the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

The other side

No response from the APC or the police to the allegation was included in the reports.

Background

The incident comes two days after gunmen attacked an NDC campaign event in Delta State, killing several people, and on the same day the House of Representatives warned that a state of emergency might be needed in Delta if violence continues. The NDC has also boycotted a security meeting called by the police chief.

Supreme Court affirms Ogundipe as ADC governorship candidate in Ogun

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Supreme Court has recognised Dr Biodun Collins Ogundipe as the ADC's governorship candidate in Ogun State, overturning lower court rulings in favour of Jimi Lawal. It held that the party's own primary guidelines decide the outcome.

Read more

The Supreme Court has affirmed Dr Biodun Collins Ogundipe as the African Democratic Congress (ADC) governorship candidate in Ogun State for the 2027 elections.

The ruling

The apex court set aside earlier judgments of the Federal High Court and the Court of Appeal, which had recognised Jimi Lawal as the party's rightful candidate. A key issue was which body had the authority to collate and declare results in the ADC's primary. The court upheld the party's three-tier primary guidelines, set by its National Working Committee, as the binding framework.

Reaction

Ogundipe welcomed the judgment with humility and gratitude, saying it was consistent with the Supreme Court's long-held position that political parties have the power to run their own primaries without outside interference.

Background

This week, lower courts also voided the candidacies of the NDC's Ovie Omo-Agege in Delta Central and the APC's candidate for Bayelsa West.

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

FCT Minister Nyesom Wike has reportedly asked President Tinubu and campaign DG Abdul'aziz Yari to put him in charge of the APC's campaign in Imo and Kwara states. He said he had already secured Rivers and the FCT.

FCT Minister Nyesom Wike has reportedly asked President Bola Tinubu and the Director-General of the APC presidential campaign council, Abdul'aziz Yari, to assign him to lead the party's re-election campaign in Imo and Kwara states.

What he said

Wike made the request at a town hall meeting with residents and members of his Rainbow Coalition in Gokana Local Government Area of Rivers State. He is said to have argued that his work in Rivers State and the Federal Capital Territory was complete, and that he was ready to step in where the APC was struggling.

He reportedly said he had received reports of difficulties for the party in Imo and of divisions within APC ranks in Kwara.

Background

Wike, a former PDP governor of Rivers State, leads a coalition of PDP members backing Tinubu. No response from the APC campaign was included in the reports.
