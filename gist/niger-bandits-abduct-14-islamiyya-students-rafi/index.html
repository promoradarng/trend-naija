Gunmen abduct 14 Islamiyya students and their teacher on Niger farm

3 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Suspected bandits have abducted 14 students of an Islamic school and their teacher in Rafi Local Government Area of Niger State, police have confirmed.

What happened

The attack happened at about 12.30pm on Saturday, 31 October 2026, in Tungan-Bako village near Pandogari. According to the police, the students were helping their teacher harvest maize on his farm when armed men stormed the area and took them away.

Police statement

The spokesman for the Niger State Police Command, Wasiu Abiodun, confirmed the incident on Monday, 2 November. He said the number of students abducted was 14, correcting earlier reports that about 50 pupils had been seized. He said a joint team of security forces had been deployed to rescue the victims and that further updates would be given.

Background

Rafi and nearby local government areas in Niger State have suffered repeated attacks by armed groups in recent years, including mass abductions from schools and villages. Kidnapping for ransom remains a serious problem across north-west and north-central Nigeria, and farmers are often targeted during the harvest season. On the same day the abduction was confirmed, bandits ambushed a bus carrying police officers in neighbouring Zamfara State.

What happens next

Families and community leaders are calling for the quick and safe release of the students.

  • The students were harvesting maize on their teacher's farm.
  • Police say 14 students were taken, not 50.
  • A joint rescue operation is under way.

Tinubu promises ₦100m and a house to each family of NAF plane crash victims

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu has pledged ₦100 million and a three-bedroom flat to every family that lost a loved one in the NAF 931 aircraft crash in Ondo. He spoke at a memorial ceremony in Abuja.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has announced a support package of ₦100 million and a three-bedroom flat for each family that lost a loved one in the crash of the Nigerian Air Force aircraft NAF 931 in Ondo State.

The pledge

The president made the announcement at a memorial and wreath-laying ceremony in Abuja held in honour of the victims. The package covers the families of both the military personnel and the civilians who died in the crash.

Military support

Tinubu also assured the armed forces that the government would provide better training and equipment to improve the performance and readiness of personnel.

Benin photographer Sh0tbySav reportedly dies after 'one-chance' attack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A photographer known as Sh0tbySav has reportedly died in Benin City after being robbed and pushed out of a moving vehicle by suspected 'one-chance' criminals. Fellow photographers are mourning her.

Read more

The photography community in Benin City, Edo State, is mourning after reports that a photographer known as Sh0tbySav died following a suspected 'one-chance' robbery.

What reportedly happened

According to accounts shared on social media, she was returning home after a photoshoot for a client when she boarded a commercial vehicle believed to have been operated by criminals. She was reportedly robbed of her belongings and pushed out of the moving vehicle.

Details of the incident remain limited, and no police statement was included in the reports.

A warning

Her death has drawn fresh attention to the dangers posed by 'one-chance' gangs, criminals who pose as commercial drivers and passengers to rob unsuspecting commuters, a recurring problem in several Nigerian cities.

Tributes

Fellow photographers have shared tributes online.

Several killed as commercial bus catches fire on Lagos-Badagry Expressway

26 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Several passengers died and more than a dozen were injured when a commercial bus burst into flames at Magbon on the Lagos-Badagry Expressway on Thursday morning. Officials gave death tolls of six and eight.

Read more

Several people were killed and others injured when a commercial bus caught fire on the Lagos-Badagry Expressway on Thursday morning, 26 November 2026.

What happened

The incident happened near the Checkpoint area, inward Magbon, between 8am and 9am. The bus, travelling from Mile 2 towards Badagry, reportedly suffered brake failure, and the driver lost control. The vehicle somersaulted and burst into flames, trapping passengers inside.

The toll

Officials gave different figures. The Lagos State Fire and Rescue Service put the death toll at six adults, two women and four men, with 12 others injured. The Lagos State Traffic Management Authority (LASTMA) said eight passengers died and 13 suffered severe burns. The injured were taken to Badagry General Hospital.

Driver fled

The LASTMA general manager said the driver fled the scene after the crash, leaving passengers trapped, and offered condolences to the families of the victims. Traffic and road safety officers were deployed to the scene.

At least 16 killed in gunmen attack on Plateau community

26 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Gunmen have killed at least 16 people, including women and children, in an attack on a community in Bassa Local Government Area of Plateau State, according to a local association. Four others were injured.

Read more

At least 16 people, including women, young people and children, have reportedly been killed in an attack on a community in Bassa Local Government Area of Plateau State.

The attack

According to the Irigwe Development Association, gunmen armed with assault rifles stormed the Tafi Gana community near Miango at about 11:30pm on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, and opened fire. By dawn on Thursday, 16 people were dead and four others had been taken to hospital with injuries.

Background

The attack is the latest in a series of deadly incidents in Plateau State, including in Bokkos, where gunmen set a secondary school on fire earlier this week.

Response

No statement from the police or the state government on the attack was included in the initial reports.
