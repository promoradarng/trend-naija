Sola Sobowale opens care home for older people in Ibadan, Makinde commissions it

3 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Veteran actress Sola Sobowale has opened Joseph and Esther Homes, a residential care facility for older adults in Akobo, Ibadan. Governor Seyi Makinde commissioned the home, with Nollywood stars in attendance.

Read more

Veteran Nollywood actress Sola Sobowale has opened a residential care home for older people in Ibadan, Oyo State.

The opening

Oyo governor Seyi Makinde formally commissioned the facility, named Joseph and Esther Homes, on Tuesday, 3 November 2026. Located in the Akobo area of Ibadan, it is designed to give older adults a safe and supportive place where they can live independently while receiving professional care. The governor toured the indoor and outdoor areas and cut the ribbon.

The idea behind it

The home operates under the motto 'Live Well, Age Well, Thrive Together'. Its organisers say it is meant to complement family care rather than replace it, helping families look after their elderly relatives better. Formal care homes are still uncommon in Nigeria, where older people have traditionally been cared for at home by relatives.

Who attended

Sobowale welcomed guests alongside veteran actor Adebayo Salami. Other Nollywood figures at the event included Kunle Afolayan, Femi Adebayo and Ibrahim Chatta. The launch was streamed live on the actress's Instagram page.

Background

Sola Sobowale is one of Nollywood's most respected actresses, known for her role in the 'King of Boys' films and decades of work in Yoruba and English-language cinema.

How people reacted

Fans praised the project, saying it addresses a growing need as more young Nigerians move to cities or abroad, leaving ageing parents behind.

Supporters of actress and governorship aspirant Doris Ogala have raised concerns about her health while she remains in detention, after a medical report about her condition was shared online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026.

The medical report

According to reports, the document from the Federal Medical Centre in Umuahia, dated 5 June 2026, shows that she was treated for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition, following an earlier surgical procedure in 2025. The report reportedly says she was admitted, treated and discharged after significant improvement, and was advised to avoid strenuous activity for six months and attend regular follow-up appointments. Reports claim she has been experiencing renewed bleeding while in detention, but this has not been independently confirmed.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters took to social media to express worry and call for her release on health grounds. Some urged the authorities to ensure she receives proper medical care while her case continues.

Background

Doris Ogala, 30, is a Nollywood actress and a governorship candidate of the African Action Congress. She was remanded in custody last month in a case linked to her dispute with Pastor Chris Okafor, which arose from social media posts. The AAC's presidential candidate, Omoyele Sowore, and other supporters have condemned her detention. A court is due to rule on her bail application on 6 November.

What happens next

Her lawyers are expected to raise her health when the court considers her bail application.
