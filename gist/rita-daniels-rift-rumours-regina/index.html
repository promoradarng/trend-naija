Toyin Abraham's throwback TikTok post sparks talk of rivalry with Funke Akindele

31 Oct 2026, 09:42 GMT

Toyin Abraham has shared an old TikTok collaboration from her film 'OverSabi Aunty' and said she looks forward to more ahead of her December release 'Bestie'. Fans linked the post to Funke Akindele's new Facebook partnership for 'The Four', reviving talk of a rivalry.

Read more

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Toyin Abraham has once again found herself at the centre of talk about a rivalry with Funke Akindele, after a social media post that many fans read as a response to her colleague.

What she posted

On Friday, 30 October 2026, Toyin Abraham shared on Instagram a throwback clip of a TikTok collaboration she did while promoting her earlier film, 'OverSabi Aunty'. In the caption, she said she was looking forward to more collaborations ahead of her new film, 'Bestie', which is due in cinemas in December 2026.

Why fans linked it to Funke

The post came just days after Funke Akindele announced a partnership with Facebook for her new film, 'The Four'. Many fans interpreted Toyin's post as a subtle reminder that she had worked with a major platform first, although she did not mention Funke, and her intention has not been confirmed.

Background

The two actresses are among the most successful filmmakers in Nollywood, and fans have long compared their box office results. Toyin has also faced accusations of copying Funke's ideas, which she has denied. In the past she has spoken warmly about Funke, congratulating her and calling her the winner of their rivalry after one of Funke's films crossed ₦1 billion at the box office.

How people reacted

Reactions were divided. Critics questioned the timing of the post and accused her of seeking attention, while her supporters said she had every right to talk about her own past work. Funke Akindele has not commented.

AY Makun responds to backlash after missing Cardiff show, promises free return

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Comedian AY Makun has defended himself after failing to appear at his AY Live show in Cardiff, which led to calls for him to be cancelled. He blamed repeated problems on the tour and promised a free return show, while saying ticket holders will still get refunds.

Read more

Comedian and filmmaker Ayo 'AY' Makun has spoken out after facing a wave of criticism for not turning up at his AY Live show in Cardiff, Wales.

What happened

Fans who bought tickets for the Cardiff show on Sunday were left angry when the comedian did not appear. Many criticised him on social media, and some called for him to be cancelled. AY had earlier apologised, saying fans' time, money and trust deserved respect, but the apology did not end the criticism.

His side of the story

In a long statement reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, AY said veterans in the industry had advised him to stay quiet, but he felt his audience deserved an explanation. He said he had spent years performing at events where payment promises were broken, sometimes paying fellow performers from his own pocket. He said a five-hour drive from Sheffield to Cardiff, after repeated bad experiences on the same tour, became his breaking point, but added that the fans who turned up had done nothing wrong. He also complained that negative stories about him get more attention than his achievements, and said calls for cancellation would not make him give up.

What he promised

AY said he would host a free return show in Cardiff, paid for by himself, once a venue and logistics are confirmed. He said this would not replace refunds owed to people who bought tickets for the original event.

Background

AY is one of Nigeria's best-known comedians and has run the AY Live brand for many years, as well as producing hit films. His account of the tour problems has not been confirmed by the organisers.

Tinubu's daughter warns voters against Atiku and Obi at Abuja launch

31 Oct 2026, 09:42 GMT

Iyaloja-General Folasade Tinubu-Ojo, the President's daughter, has reportedly urged Nigerians not to back Atiku Abubakar or Peter Obi in 2027, saying either would reverse the government's progress. She spoke at the launch of a pro-Tinubu market women's movement in Abuja.

Read more

Mujidat Folasade Tinubu-Ojo, the daughter of President Bola Tinubu and Iyaloja-General of Nigeria, has waded into the 2027 presidential race with a warning to voters about two of her father's main rivals.

What she said

Speaking at the launch of the Iyaloja General Movement for Renewed Hope Initiatives at Eagle Square in Abuja on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Tinubu-Ojo reportedly urged Nigerians not to support Atiku Abubakar of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) or Peter Obi of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC). She argued that if either of them won, they would undo the progress made by the current government and set the country back. The remarks were reported on Saturday, 31 October.

The movement

The new group is meant to mobilise market women and traders across the country to support President Tinubu's re-election. As Iyaloja-General, Tinubu-Ojo is the ceremonial head of market women's associations nationwide, a role her father's supporters see as a valuable link to grassroots voters.

Background

Several pro-Tinubu groups have been launched in recent weeks, including a two-million-man march announced by Atiku's son, Abba, and the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement backed by Tompolo. Atiku and Obi are both campaigning hard, and the debate over the economy, rising prices and the government's borrowing has dominated the race so far. Neither the ADC nor the NDC had responded to her comments at the time of the report.

How people reacted

APC supporters welcomed the launch, while opposition supporters accused the President's family of turning a traditional market leadership role into a campaign platform.

Adamawa APC inaugurates 3,000-member campaign council, receives ADC and Labour defectors

31 Oct 2026, 09:42 GMT

Adamawa Governor Ahmadu Fintiri has reportedly inaugurated a campaign council of more than 3,000 members for the APC ahead of 2027, with retired General Buba Marwa as co-chairman. Defectors from the ADC and Labour Party were also received at the event in Yola.

Read more

The All Progressives Congress (APC) in Adamawa State has inaugurated a campaign council of more than 3,000 members for the 2027 general elections, according to reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026.

What happened

Governor Ahmadu Umaru Fintiri reportedly inaugurated the council at the Banquet Hall of Government House in Yola on Thursday, 29 October. Retired General Buba Marwa was named co-chairman, and the council includes representatives from the state's three senatorial zones and its communities. At the same event, a group of defectors from the African Democratic Congress (ADC) and the Labour Party was received into the APC and presented to the state party chairman, Hamza Bello Madagali, by the party's North-East national vice chairman, Idris Shuaibu.

What was said

Speakers reportedly said the party would leave nothing to chance in its bid to win the state, and Fintiri warned that anti-party behaviour would not be tolerated. Madagali acknowledged that some stakeholders had been left off the initial list and appealed for patience.

Background

Adamawa is the home state of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar, the ADC presidential candidate, which makes the contest there especially significant. Buba Marwa is a former military governor of Lagos State and has headed the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency. Party switching has been common across the country in the run-up to the elections, with governors and lawmakers moving between parties.

What happens next

The council is expected to begin campaigning across the state's local government areas in the coming weeks.
