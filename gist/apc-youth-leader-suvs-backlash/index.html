A DJ at a Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) rally in Onitsha, Anambra State, reportedly got an unexpected reaction from the crowd after switching to Flavour's hit song Baby Oku.

What happened

The rally, held on 21 November 2026 in support of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso, was dominated by a Hausa-language Obi-Kwankwaso campaign song, which supporters were dancing and chanting to.

When the DJ suddenly changed to the highlife track by Flavour, whose real name is Chinedu Okoli, the crowd is said to have shut it down immediately and kept up the pressure until the DJ went back to the campaign song.

How people reacted

A video of the moment spread quickly on social media, drawing a wide range of comments as campaigns for the 2027 elections heat up.

Abure-led Labour Party faction endorses Tinubu for 2027

23 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Labour Party faction led by Julius Abure has reportedly endorsed President Tinubu for re-election. Abure said the faction would mobilise the votes the party won in 2023 behind the president.

Read more

A faction of the Labour Party led by Julius Abure has reportedly endorsed President Bola Tinubu for re-election in the 2027 general election.

The endorsement

Abure announced the decision on Monday, 23 November 2026, after a meeting of the faction's National Executive Council in Abuja. The faction is said to have described Tinubu as the most suitable candidate to lead Nigeria beyond 2027, pointing to his political experience and his time as governor of Lagos State.

Abure also reportedly pledged to mobilise the six million votes the Labour Party claims it won in the 2023 presidential election in support of the president.

Background

Earlier this month, Abure said he would not support Peter Obi, the party's 2023 presidential candidate, who is now running on the NDC platform. He had said he was open to working with either Tinubu or Atiku Abubakar, depending on how the party's leadership dispute was resolved. The Labour Party has been split by a long-running leadership tussle.

How people reacted

The endorsement drew criticism and sarcastic comments on social media, with many users questioning the faction's motives.

Infantino says Nigeria is not ready to host a World Cup yet

23 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has reportedly said Nigeria cannot yet host the World Cup because of gaps in stadiums, airports and transport. He added that the country has the building blocks to get there.

Read more

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has reportedly said Nigeria is not currently ready to host the FIFA World Cup.

What he said

Speaking during his visit to Nigeria, Infantino was asked whether the country could stage a tournament of that size. He is said to have pointed to serious shortfalls in stadiums, airports and transport networks as the main obstacles, and explained the kind of infrastructure any host nation must have in place.

Hope for the future

However, he reportedly did not rule out Nigeria's long-term chances, saying the country has the building blocks needed to eventually meet the standard. He also described the national training facilities he visited in Abuja and Warri as impressive.

Background

South Africa remains the only sub-Saharan African country to have hosted the World Cup, in 2010, while Morocco will co-host the 2030 edition with Spain and Portugal.

How fans reacted

His comments drew mixed reactions from Nigerian football fans online.

Former world heavyweight boxing champion Tyson Fury has reportedly revealed that he was diagnosed with schizophrenia and bipolar disorder after a severe mental health crisis.

The documentary

The 38-year-old British boxer opens up about his struggles in an upcoming Netflix documentary titled Fury, which looks at his life, career and personal battles. It features interviews with Fury, his wife Paris and his father John.

His crisis

Fury and his wife recalled how, after he was hospitalised with a suspected heart attack in June 2016, he began hearing threatening voices. At the time he had sunk into depression and was struggling with drugs and alcohol. He described that period as an indescribable darkness and said he feared he would end up locked away.

Background

Fury has previously spoken publicly about his battle with bipolar disorder.
