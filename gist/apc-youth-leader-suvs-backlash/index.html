A campaign film featuring Burna Boy and several of Africa's best-known footballers has picked up a string of international awards.

What happened

'For Everybody', made by Sporty Group's production arm Sporty Studios together with RadicalMedia, reportedly won 15 awards at the 2026 w3 Awards, according to reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026. It won a gold award in the creator and brand collaboration category, as well as 14 silver awards in categories including music video, cinematography, brand storytelling, branded content, and sports and entertainment.

About the film

The film was directed by Dave Meyers, an American director best known for his music videos with major global stars, and was released in January 2026. It brings together music and sport to celebrate African talent and culture. Alongside Burna Boy, it features Nigerian football stars Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, as well as Spain's Nico Williams, France's Eduardo Camavinga, Ghana legend Michael Essien, Kenya's Victor Wanyama and South African rugby star Cheslin Kolbe.

Background

The w3 Awards recognise creative work in digital media, including websites, videos and online campaigns. Burna Boy, a Grammy winner, has increasingly worked on projects linking music and sport, and recently presented the Socrates Award at this year's Ballon d'Or ceremony. Oshoala is one of Africa's most decorated female footballers, having won the African Women's Footballer of the Year award a record number of times.

How fans reacted

Fans celebrated the recognition online, saying the film showed how African music and sport can work together to reach global audiences.

Tinubu's daughter warns voters against Atiku and Obi at Abuja launch

31 Oct 2026, 09:42 GMT

Iyaloja-General Folasade Tinubu-Ojo, the President's daughter, has reportedly urged Nigerians not to back Atiku Abubakar or Peter Obi in 2027, saying either would reverse the government's progress. She spoke at the launch of a pro-Tinubu market women's movement in Abuja.

Read more

Mujidat Folasade Tinubu-Ojo, the daughter of President Bola Tinubu and Iyaloja-General of Nigeria, has waded into the 2027 presidential race with a warning to voters about two of her father's main rivals.

What she said

Speaking at the launch of the Iyaloja General Movement for Renewed Hope Initiatives at Eagle Square in Abuja on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Tinubu-Ojo reportedly urged Nigerians not to support Atiku Abubakar of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) or Peter Obi of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC). She argued that if either of them won, they would undo the progress made by the current government and set the country back. The remarks were reported on Saturday, 31 October.

The movement

The new group is meant to mobilise market women and traders across the country to support President Tinubu's re-election. As Iyaloja-General, Tinubu-Ojo is the ceremonial head of market women's associations nationwide, a role her father's supporters see as a valuable link to grassroots voters.

Background

Several pro-Tinubu groups have been launched in recent weeks, including a two-million-man march announced by Atiku's son, Abba, and the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement backed by Tompolo. Atiku and Obi are both campaigning hard, and the debate over the economy, rising prices and the government's borrowing has dominated the race so far. Neither the ADC nor the NDC had responded to her comments at the time of the report.

How people reacted

APC supporters welcomed the launch, while opposition supporters accused the President's family of turning a traditional market leadership role into a campaign platform.
