Kano court orders TikToker to clean traffic agency offices for two weeks over defamation

4 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

A Kano magistrate court has reportedly ordered a TikTok user to clean the headquarters of the state traffic agency, KAROTA, for two weeks and write an apology, after finding that his videos defamed the agency's officials.

Read more

A magistrate court in Kano has handed an unusual sentence to a TikTok user over videos that ridiculed officials of the state's traffic agency.

The sentence

According to the Kano State Road Traffic Agency (KAROTA), the court found that the TikTok user published videos containing statements that damaged the image of its personnel and exposed them to public ridicule. Magistrate Halima Wali ordered him to clean the agency's headquarters on Club Road, Kano, for two weeks and to carry out any other task the agency considers appropriate. He was also ordered to write a formal letter of apology to KAROTA's management and promise not to repeat the conduct. The agency's spokesman, Abubakar Ibrahim Sharada, confirmed the sentence.

Background

Non-custodial sentences such as community service are increasingly used by Nigerian courts for minor offences, partly to reduce overcrowding in prisons. Content creators have faced a rising number of cases over online posts in recent years, often under defamation or cybercrime laws. Rights groups have warned that such laws can be used to silence critics, while authorities say people must be held responsible for false or damaging content.

How people reacted

Many social media users found the sentence amusing and said it was a fair alternative to jail, while others said it showed the risks content creators face when they criticise officials.

Former Osun governor Oyinlola says his endorsement of Tinubu 'has expired'

5 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Former Osun governor Olagunsoye Oyinlola has reportedly withdrawn his 2025 endorsement of President Tinubu for 2027, saying it was a political move to resolve Osun's local government crisis that did not work.

Read more

Former Osun State governor Olagunsoye Oyinlola has said his endorsement of President Bola Tinubu for the 2027 election is no longer valid.

What he said

Asked in a television interview, reported on Thursday, 5 November 2026, whether he still stood by the endorsement, Oyinlola reportedly replied that it had expired. He explained that he and other Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) stakeholders in Osun endorsed the President on 20 July 2025 as a strategic move, because of problems in the state that they believed only the President could solve, including missing local government funds and a dispute over who should occupy the local council offices. He said the endorsement was meant to help resolve those issues, but the solution had not come.

Background

Oyinlola governed Osun State from 2003 to 2010. Osun has been at the centre of a long-running dispute over control of its local governments, involving the PDP-led state government of Governor Ademola Adeleke and APC-backed council officials, with court rulings and disagreements over the release of council funds. The PDP itself is divided, with some members backing Tinubu, others joining opposition coalitions, and a court battle over its national leadership.

Why it matters

The withdrawal is a setback for the President's campaign in Osun, a swing state in the South West. The APC says it remains confident of winning the state, while opposition parties hope to benefit from discontent there.

What happens next

Oyinlola did not say which candidate, if any, he would now support.

In recent weeks Blessing CEO has been mourning the death of her nine-year-old son, who died while she was in custody, and this week she issued a tearful public apology to people she had hurt with her words. How people reacted

Many fans welcomed the news and wished her peace and healing, while others said she should focus on her children and her recovery.
