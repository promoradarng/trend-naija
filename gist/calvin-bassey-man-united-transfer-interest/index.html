Manchester United reportedly eyeing Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey is reportedly attracting interest from Manchester United, according to transfer reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026.

What is being reported

United are said to be considering Bassey as a possible replacement for Argentine defender Lisandro Martinez, whose contract is reported to expire at the end of the season. Martinez is said to be open to leaving Old Trafford, with Barcelona reportedly making enquiries. Luke Shaw and Harry Maguire are also said to be out of contract next summer, which could leave United needing several defenders.

Bassey's situation

Bassey has been one of Fulham's most consistent players this season. His contract at Craven Cottage reportedly runs out at the end of the season, although Fulham are said to have an option to extend it by a year and would prefer to agree a new deal. Because his contract is running down, he could be available for a relatively low fee. Newcastle United, Everton, Inter Milan, Borussia Dortmund and RB Leipzig are also reportedly monitoring him.

Background

Bassey, who can play at centre-back or left-back, joined Fulham from Ajax in 2023 after earlier spells at Rangers. He has become a regular for the Super Eagles and is valued for his pace and versatility. Manchester United, managed by Michael Carrick, have made a slow start to the season.

What happens next

No formal offer has been reported, and any move is unlikely before the January transfer window at the earliest.

  • United reportedly see Bassey as a possible Martinez replacement.
  • His Fulham contract reportedly expires at the end of the season.
  • Newcastle, Inter and Dortmund are also said to be interested.

Cristiano Ronaldo retires from international football at 41

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Cristiano Ronaldo has announced his retirement from international football, ending a 23-year career with Portugal in which he became the all-time leading scorer in men's international football. He announced it in a letter read in an Instagram video.

Read more

Cristiano Ronaldo has announced his retirement from international football, bringing to an end one of the most remarkable careers in the history of the game.

The announcement

The 41-year-old shared the news on Thursday, 5 November 2026, in an Instagram video in which he read a letter to the Portuguese people. He said the day had come to hand back the national team shirt and captain's armband, which he had never felt belonged only to him, and that the decision was made with the heart as well as the head. He described a journey that began in August 2003 and said that when he looks back he sees not only goals and trophies but the people who supported him, including Portuguese emigrants around the world.

A record career

Ronaldo made his Portugal debut in 2003 and went on to become the most-capped player and all-time top scorer in men's international football. He led Portugal to victory at Euro 2016, their first major title, and also won the UEFA Nations League twice. He played at six World Cups.

Club career continues

Ronaldo will continue playing for Al Nassr in Saudi Arabia. Last week he scored a hat-trick for the club, taking his career total to 985 goals, and he has said he wants to reach 1,000.

How people reacted

Fans, players and clubs around the world paid tribute, with many calling him one of the greatest players of all time.

Asisat Oshoala gifts her teenage coach a new Toyota Highlander

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Super Falcons star Asisat Oshoala has reportedly given a brand new Toyota Highlander to Emmanuel Osahon, chairman and head coach of FC Robo International, the Lagos club where her career began. The club thanked her publicly.

Read more

Super Falcons forward Asisat Oshoala has surprised her former youth coach with a new car.

What happened

According to reports on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Oshoala presented a brand new Toyota Highlander to Coach Emmanuel Osahon, the chairman and head coach of FC Robo International in Lagos, which nurtured her talent from 2009 when she was a teenager. The club released a statement thanking her, describing the gift as a beautiful demonstration of gratitude and a sign that she has not forgotten the people who helped her on her journey.

A history of giving back

The gesture recalled an earlier donation Oshoala made to the club during her first years in Europe.

Background

Oshoala is one of Africa's most decorated footballers, having won the CAF Women's Player of the Year award a record six times. She has played for clubs including Liverpool, Arsenal and Barcelona, where she won the UEFA Women's Champions League. She has also helped the Super Falcons win several Africa Women Cup of Nations titles. The gift comes in the same week that the Super Falcons received the cash rewards promised for their 2024 WAFCON victory.

How people reacted

Fans praised Oshoala for honouring her coach and said more successful athletes should support the people and clubs that helped them.

Flamingos beat Brazil 3-1 to reach first U-17 Women's World Cup final

5 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nigeria's Flamingos have reached the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup for the first time after beating Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final. Awawu Bashiru scored twice and Queen Joseph once. They face Japan in the final.

Read more

Nigeria's under-17 women's team, the Flamingos, have made history by reaching the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup for the first time.

The match

The Flamingos beat Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final on Wednesday night, 4 November 2026, according to reports. Queen Joseph opened the scoring in the 56th minute, and Awawu Bashiru doubled the lead in the 71st minute before adding her second goal in the 80th minute to put the game beyond Brazil's reach.

A record run

The win beats the team's previous best performance, a bronze medal at the 2022 edition of the tournament. On the way to the semi-final, the Flamingos beat Venezuela in the quarter-finals, a result that earned praise from the Confederation of African Football.

The final

The Flamingos will now face Japan in the final as they chase Nigeria's first FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup title. Japan, one of the strongest sides in women's youth football, won the tournament in 2014.

Background

The result comes at a difficult time for Nigerian women's football at senior level. The Super Falcons failed to qualify for the Women's World Cup for the first time, and no Nigerian player was shortlisted for this year's CAF Women's Player of the Year award. The Flamingos' success has been welcomed as a sign that a new generation of players is coming through.

FIFA's message

FIFA congratulated the Flamingos on qualifying for the final, a first for Nigeria at this level of the women's game.

How people reacted

Nigerians celebrated the win across social media, praising the young players and their coaches and urging the government and the football federation to support them ahead of the final.

Tension erupts as angry crowd throws stones towards stage while Nasarawa governor addresses presidential campaign rally

6 Nov 2026, 05:30 GMT

A presidential campaign rally was disrupted when some angry youths in the crowd reportedly threw stones towards the stage as Nasarawa State Governor Abdullahi Sule addressed supporters. Photos show stones lying on the stage beside the governor and his team.

Read more

What was supposed to be a peaceful political campaign reportedly took a dramatic turn when some angry youths in the crowd began throwing stones towards the stage while Nasarawa State Governor Abdullahi Sule was addressing supporters.

What happened

The incident happened during a presidential campaign rally on Thursday, 5 November 2026, and created tension at the venue as the gathering was disrupted.

Photos from the event show large stones lying on the red carpet of the stage, close to the governor's feet. In one image, a man in a suit bends to pick up one of the stones as the governor, microphone in hand, points towards the crowd, with security officers standing beside him.

Why the anger?

The exact reason for the crowd's anger remains unclear. The incident does, however, raise questions about growing frustration among sections of the population and how political leaders are being received at public gatherings.

For many Nigerians struggling with the rising cost of living, unemployment and insecurity, political promises are becoming harder to accept without visible results.

Even so, it would be premature to blame those grievances for the disturbance without hearing from the people involved.

Violence is never the answer

Whatever the reason, throwing stones at a political gathering is dangerous and should never be encouraged. People have every right to express their dissatisfaction with their leaders, but political disagreement should never turn violent.
