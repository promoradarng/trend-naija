Manchester United reportedly eyeing Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey is reportedly attracting interest from Manchester United, according to transfer reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026.

What is being reported

United are said to be considering Bassey as a possible replacement for Argentine defender Lisandro Martinez, whose contract is reported to expire at the end of the season. Martinez is said to be open to leaving Old Trafford, with Barcelona reportedly making enquiries. Luke Shaw and Harry Maguire are also said to be out of contract next summer, which could leave United needing several defenders.

Bassey's situation

Bassey has been one of Fulham's most consistent players this season. His contract at Craven Cottage reportedly runs out at the end of the season, although Fulham are said to have an option to extend it by a year and would prefer to agree a new deal. Because his contract is running down, he could be available for a relatively low fee. Newcastle United, Everton, Inter Milan, Borussia Dortmund and RB Leipzig are also reportedly monitoring him.

Background

Bassey, who can play at centre-back or left-back, joined Fulham from Ajax in 2023 after earlier spells at Rangers. He has become a regular for the Super Eagles and is valued for his pace and versatility. Manchester United, managed by Michael Carrick, have made a slow start to the season.

What happens next

No formal offer has been reported, and any move is unlikely before the January transfer window at the earliest.

  • His Fulham contract reportedly expires at the end of the season.
  • Newcastle, Inter and Dortmund are also said to be interested.

Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly taken over as Manchester United manager, following the club's decision to sack Michael Carrick.

His arrival

The Argentine flew into England on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, after his final game in charge of the United States men's national team.

A tough start

His first match is a daunting one: a trip to Anfield to face league leaders Liverpool just five days after arriving. United are currently in the Premier League relegation zone.

A statistical model reportedly gives Liverpool a 57.3% chance of winning the game, with United at 20.5% and a draw at 22.2%.

His first interview

In his first sit-down interview as head coach, the 54-year-old said changing the culture at the club is his top priority, stressing the need for a winning mentality and consistent daily habits. United are 18th with just eight points from their first 10 games, their worst top-flight start since 1984.

Transfer plans

Reports have also linked Pochettino with transfer targets for January, with Nigeria defender Calvin Bassey said to be on his shopping list. Fulham are reportedly asking for around £50 million for the Super Eagles star.

Background

Pochettino previously managed Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea before taking the United States job.

Marathon world champion Peres Jepchirchir banned for three years for doping

19 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Kenya's Peres Jepchirchir, the reigning marathon world champion and Tokyo Olympic gold medallist, has been banned for three years after testing positive for EPO. Two other Kenyan runners were also banned.

Read more

Reigning marathon world champion Peres Jepchirchir of Kenya has been banned for three years for doping, the Athletics Integrity Unit (AIU) announced on Thursday, 19 November 2026.

The ban

The 33-year-old, who also won Olympic gold at the Tokyo Games in 2021, was suspended after an out-of-competition sample showed traces of the banned blood booster EPO. Her ban runs from 20 October.

Others banned

Two other Kenyan runners were also sanctioned. Veronica Loleo, a half marathon silver medallist at this year's World Road Running Championships, received three years for the same offence, while Ronald Korir, fourth at the 2023 Berlin Marathon, was banned for six years over abnormalities in his biological passport.

A wider problem

They join more than 140 Kenyan athletes, including women's marathon world record-holder Ruth Chepngetich, who have been suspended or banned by the AIU since 2017. The AIU acknowledged that Kenyan authorities have made progress against doping but said these cases show there is still a long way to go.

FIFA appoints normalisation committee to run NFF, names Amokachi and Enakhena

19 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

FIFA has named lawyer Sampson Ebomhe to chair a normalisation committee for the Nigeria Football Federation ahead of its 2027 elections. Daniel Amokachi, Godwin Enakhena, Amina Yerima and Joe Abah are also members.

Read more

FIFA has set up a normalisation committee to run the Nigeria Football Federation (NFF), appointing lawyer Sampson Ebomhe as chairman with immediate effect.

The members

The world governing body named four other members: sports journalist and administrator Godwin Enakhena, former Super Eagles captain Daniel Amokachi, Amina Yerima and Dr Joe Abah, former Director-General of the Bureau of Public Service Reforms.

FIFA's Chief Member Associations Officer, Elkhan Mammadov, confirmed the appointments in a letter, noting that all members are still subject to an eligibility check by the FIFA Review Committee before they are formally confirmed.

Background

Amokachi, nicknamed The Bull, played at two World Cups for Nigeria and is one of the country's best-known football figures.

Super Eagles climb to 29th in FIFA rankings despite Tanzania draw

19 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Nigeria have moved up one place to 29th in the latest FIFA world rankings and are back in Africa's top five. The boost comes despite the goalless draw with Tanzania that hurt their AFCON hopes.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles have moved up one place to 29th in the latest FIFA world rankings, ending their 2026 international calendar with a small boost.

The ranking

The Super Eagles climbed from 30th to 29th in the world and moved back into Africa's top five, after slipping out of that group earlier in the year.

The results behind it

Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 in Uyo before playing out a goalless draw in the return leg in Zanzibar. The draw left them on seven points, three behind group leaders Guinea-Bissau, making their path to AFCON 2027 more complicated.
