CAF scraps four-year plan, AFCON returns to every two years

30 Oct 2026, 09:49 GMT

The Africa Cup of Nations will once again be played every two years after the Confederation of African Football (CAF) reversed its earlier decision to move the tournament to a four-year cycle.

What happened

CAF's Executive Committee reportedly approved the change unanimously on Thursday, 29 October 2026, after months of discussion. CAF President Patrice Motsepe announced the decision in a statement on the body's website.

Why CAF changed course

Motsepe said the decision was taken to advance and protect the interests of African football, its 54 member associations and African players at clubs abroad. CAF had planned to introduce an Africa Nations League to fill the gap between tournaments, but it was reportedly concerned about how long the new competition would take to deliver the expected money and development benefits. Returning to a two-year AFCON was seen as the safer option in the short term.

What comes next

AFCON 2027 will be co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda. Motsepe said he was confident the three countries would deliver a successful competition. He described AFCON as more than a football tournament, calling it a celebration of African cultures that brings together people on the continent and in the diaspora.

Background

AFCON has been held every two years for most of its history, which has sometimes caused friction with European clubs that lose key players in the middle of their seasons. The move to a four-year cycle had been welcomed by some clubs but criticised by many fans and smaller federations, who rely on tournament income.

How fans reacted

Many African football fans, including Nigerians, welcomed the decision, saying more frequent AFCONs give the Super Eagles and other national teams more chances to compete for the title.

Argentina World Cup winner Leandro Paredes collapses during live TV interview

22 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Boca Juniors midfielder Leandro Paredes reportedly collapsed while giving a live interview minutes after his club won the Copa Sudamericana. The final was played in intense heat and humidity in Colombia.

Read more

Argentine midfielder Leandro Paredes, who won the 2022 FIFA World Cup with Argentina, reportedly collapsed on live television on Saturday, 21 November 2026, shortly after Boca Juniors won the Copa Sudamericana.

What happened

The 32-year-old was giving a post-match interview when he lost consciousness and fell. The journalist beside him reportedly moved quickly to stop his fall before medical staff rushed to help him.

The heat

The final, at the Estadio Metropolitano in Barranquilla, Colombia, was played in punishing conditions, with temperatures of around 32°C and humidity of about 80 per cent forecast for the day. Paredes is said to have shown signs of distress late in the first half, and his coach later confirmed it. He was substituted in the 57th minute.

The match

Boca were already 2-0 up by then, and goals from Santiago Ascacibar and Miguel Merentiel gave them victory over Brazilian side Atletico Mineiro. The win ended Boca's 18-year wait for international silverware.

American football great Tom Brady has reportedly bought a minority stake in Premier League club Chelsea.

The deal

The size of the seven-time Super Bowl winner's stake and the amount he paid have not been disclosed. Chelsea is valued at about $4.2 billion by one leading business publication.

Leaving Birmingham

The move comes weeks after Brady sold his 3.3% stake in Birmingham City, who play in England's second tier. The reports cited English Football League rules on holding stakes in more than one club as the reason.

Growing sports portfolio

Since retiring from the NFL, Brady has built up a range of sports investments. He holds a 5% stake in the Las Vegas Raiders and part-owns the Las Vegas Aces basketball team, an E1 boat-racing team and a pickleball team.

Chelsea's season

The news came on the same day Chelsea suffered a heavy 4-1 home defeat to Leeds United.

Leeds stun Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge

21 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Leeds United beat Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge in the Premier League. Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso admitted the loss was painful and that his side's confidence has taken a hit.

Read more

Leeds United inflicted a heavy 4-1 defeat on Chelsea at Stamford Bridge on matchday 11 of the Premier League on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The match

Emmanuel Emegha gave Chelsea an early lead on his first start for the club since joining in the summer. Leeds hit back quickly through Noah Okafor, and the sides went into half-time level at 1-1.

After the break, Leeds were awarded a penalty when Morgan Rogers was judged to have handled the ball in the area, a decision that sparked debate, and went on to complete a convincing win.

Alonso's reaction

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso said the result was painful and reflected a team struggling to keep up its earlier form. He admitted his players' confidence had taken a hit at a difficult time.

Palmer and Pedro row

After the match, a video appeared to show Cole Palmer and the injured Joao Pedro exchanging words on the touchline before Palmer walked straight down the tunnel. Alonso described it as a fragile moment for the club and urged players and fans to stay united. The defeat was Chelsea's third in four league games and dropped them to eighth.

Atiku denies quitting 2027 race, says those spreading the claim are working for Tinubu

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly dismissed claims that he has withdrawn from the 2027 election. His campaign said no group calling itself his supporters can speak for him, and accused rivals of tactics that help the ruling party.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly rejected claims that he has pulled out of the 2027 presidential race, describing them as a deliberate falsehood.

What his campaign said

In a statement issued on Sunday, 22 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, the campaign is said to have stressed that no group presenting itself as Atiku's supporters had the authority to announce a withdrawal on his behalf.

The statement reportedly accused those behind the claim of borrowing tactics it associated with the ruling APC, such as presenting so-called supporters from a rival camp. It said any opposition party spreading such stories was effectively helping President Bola Tinubu, and urged parties that want to defeat the APC to make their case directly to voters.

Still campaigning

Atiku's campaign said his Affordable Life campaign was still active and focused on the election.

Background

The denial comes as campaigns for the January 2027 presidential election gather pace, with Tinubu of the APC, Atiku of the ADC and Peter Obi of the NDC among the leading candidates. Speculation about possible withdrawals and alliances has been circulating online in recent days.
