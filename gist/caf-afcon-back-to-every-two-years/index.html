CAF scraps four-year plan, AFCON returns to every two years

30 Oct 2026, 09:49 GMT

The Africa Cup of Nations will once again be played every two years after the Confederation of African Football (CAF) reversed its earlier decision to move the tournament to a four-year cycle.

What happened

CAF's Executive Committee reportedly approved the change unanimously on Thursday, 29 October 2026, after months of discussion. CAF President Patrice Motsepe announced the decision in a statement on the body's website.

Why CAF changed course

Motsepe said the decision was taken to advance and protect the interests of African football, its 54 member associations and African players at clubs abroad. CAF had planned to introduce an Africa Nations League to fill the gap between tournaments, but it was reportedly concerned about how long the new competition would take to deliver the expected money and development benefits. Returning to a two-year AFCON was seen as the safer option in the short term.

What comes next

AFCON 2027 will be co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda. Motsepe said he was confident the three countries would deliver a successful competition. He described AFCON as more than a football tournament, calling it a celebration of African cultures that brings together people on the continent and in the diaspora.

Background

AFCON has been held every two years for most of its history, which has sometimes caused friction with European clubs that lose key players in the middle of their seasons. The move to a four-year cycle had been welcomed by some clubs but criticised by many fans and smaller federations, who rely on tournament income.

How fans reacted

Many African football fans, including Nigerians, welcomed the decision, saying more frequent AFCONs give the Super Eagles and other national teams more chances to compete for the title.

CAS to rule on Tuesday on who won AFCON 2025, Senegal or Morocco

23 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Court of Arbitration for Sport will announce its decision on the disputed 2025 Africa Cup of Nations final on Tuesday, 24 November. Senegal won the match on the pitch, but CAF later handed the title to Morocco.

Read more

The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) will deliver its long-awaited ruling on the disputed 2025 Africa Cup of Nations final between Senegal and Morocco on Tuesday, 24 November 2026.

The announcement

CAS confirmed on Monday that all the parties had been notified of the decision date. The ruling is expected on Tuesday afternoon and will settle which country is officially recognised as champion. The court reminded the public that only information released through its own channels should be treated as official.

How the dispute started

The final was played in Rabat on 18 January 2026. Senegal beat hosts Morocco 1-0 after extra time, with Pape Gueye scoring the winner. But the match was marred by chaotic scenes when Senegal's players briefly walked off the pitch in protest at a late penalty awarded to Morocco. When play resumed after about 15 minutes, Brahim Diaz's attempted Panenka penalty was saved by Senegal goalkeeper Edouard Mendy.

Title stripped

On 17 March, CAF's Appeal Board overturned the result, ruling that Senegal had forfeited the match by leaving the pitch, and awarded the title to Morocco. The Senegalese Football Federation rejected the decision and took the case to CAS, naming CAF and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation as respondents.

What happens next

Tuesday's decision is final at the sporting level and will either restore Senegal's title or confirm Morocco as champions.

Pochettino's Man United reign starts with 4-0 thrashing at Liverpool

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United manager ended in a 4-0 defeat at Anfield after Lenny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute. United are 19th in the Premier League.

Mauricio Pochettino's time in charge of Manchester United got off to a painful start on Sunday, 22 November 2026, as Liverpool beat his side 4-0 at Anfield.

How the game went

United's plans fell apart early when defender Lenny Yoro was shown a red card in the eighth minute, leaving them with ten men for more than 80 minutes. Liverpool quickly took advantage. Dominik Szoboszlai opened the scoring in the 10th minute, and Florian Wirtz doubled the lead three minutes later.

Liverpool kept their two-goal lead until half-time and added two more after the break through Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.

Where it leaves the clubs

The result left United in 19th place, inside the Premier League relegation zone, while Liverpool moved into the top four.

Pochettino's reaction

Speaking after the match, Pochettino reportedly admitted that the early red card shaped the game, but urged his players to turn their frustration into better performances in the coming matches.

Background

Pochettino took over at Old Trafford only recently. The club has been linked with several players ahead of the January window, including Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey.

Speaking after Fulham's 1-1 draw with Manchester City at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday, the 26-year-old said negotiations were ongoing and that he would like a deal done quickly. He described Fulham as an amazing place to be and said the manager had shown great faith in him.

Argentina World Cup winner Leandro Paredes collapses during live TV interview

22 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Boca Juniors midfielder Leandro Paredes reportedly collapsed while giving a live interview minutes after his club won the Copa Sudamericana. The final was played in intense heat and humidity in Colombia.

Read more

Argentine midfielder Leandro Paredes, who won the 2022 FIFA World Cup with Argentina, reportedly collapsed on live television on Saturday, 21 November 2026, shortly after Boca Juniors won the Copa Sudamericana.

What happened

The 32-year-old was giving a post-match interview when he lost consciousness and fell. The journalist beside him reportedly moved quickly to stop his fall before medical staff rushed to help him.

The heat

The final, at the Estadio Metropolitano in Barranquilla, Colombia, was played in punishing conditions, with temperatures of around 32°C and humidity of about 80 per cent forecast for the day. Paredes is said to have shown signs of distress late in the first half, and his coach later confirmed it. He was substituted in the 57th minute.

The match

Boca were already 2-0 up by then, and goals from Santiago Ascacibar and Miguel Merentiel gave them victory over Brazilian side Atletico Mineiro. The win ended Boca's 18-year wait for international silverware.
