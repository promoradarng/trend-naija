Chelsea beat Manchester United 2-0 as Carrick insists he will stay in charge

1 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Manchester United lost 2-0 at Chelsea on Saturday, leaving them 17th in the Premier League, one point above the relegation zone. Manager Michael Carrick said he expects to remain in charge for Tuesday's Champions League game against Roma.

Read more

Pressure on Manchester United manager Michael Carrick has grown after another defeat in the Premier League.

The match

Chelsea beat United 2-0 on Saturday, 31 October 2026, with both goals coming in the second half from Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer, according to reports. It was United's fourth league defeat of the season and leaves them 17th in the table, just one point above the relegation zone.

What Carrick said

Speaking to TNT Sports after the game, Carrick was asked directly whether he expected to still be in charge for United's next match, the Champions League tie against Roma at Old Trafford on Tuesday. He reportedly answered simply that he did.

Background

Carrick, a former United midfielder, has faced growing criticism after a poor start to the season. Reports earlier this week said the club's board had decided against sacking him for now. The club is also facing decisions over several senior defenders whose contracts expire at the end of the season, with Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey among the players reportedly being considered as a replacement.

What happens next

Tuesday's Champions League match against Roma is now seen as an important test for the manager. Fans are divided, with some calling for patience and others for an immediate change.

Manchester United reportedly eyeing Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

Manchester United are reportedly interested in Fulham and Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey as a possible replacement for Lisandro Martinez. Bassey's contract is said to run out at the end of the season, and several other European clubs are also said to be watching him.

Read more

Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey is reportedly attracting interest from Manchester United, according to transfer reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026.

What is being reported

United are said to be considering Bassey as a possible replacement for Argentine defender Lisandro Martinez, whose contract is reported to expire at the end of the season. Martinez is said to be open to leaving Old Trafford, with Barcelona reportedly making enquiries. Luke Shaw and Harry Maguire are also said to be out of contract next summer, which could leave United needing several defenders.

Bassey's situation

Bassey has been one of Fulham's most consistent players this season. His contract at Craven Cottage reportedly runs out at the end of the season, although Fulham are said to have an option to extend it by a year and would prefer to agree a new deal. Because his contract is running down, he could be available for a relatively low fee. Newcastle United, Everton, Inter Milan, Borussia Dortmund and RB Leipzig are also reportedly monitoring him.

Background

Bassey, who can play at centre-back or left-back, joined Fulham from Ajax in 2023 after earlier spells at Rangers. He has become a regular for the Super Eagles and is valued for his pace and versatility. Manchester United, managed by Michael Carrick, have made a slow start to the season.

What happens next

No formal offer has been reported, and any move is unlikely before the January transfer window at the earliest.

South-East governors urge region to back Tinubu at APC women's rally in Enugu

1 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

The governors of Enugu, Imo and Ebonyi, and a representative of Anambra's governor, have reportedly urged South-East voters to support President Tinubu's re-election at an APC women's rally in Enugu. The call sets up a direct contest with Peter Obi in his home region.

Read more

Four South-East state governments have thrown their weight behind President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid, in a show of support in a region widely seen as Peter Obi's stronghold.

What happened

At an APC Women Mega Rally and Empowerment Programme for the South-East at Okpara Square, Enugu, on Saturday, 31 October 2026, the governors reportedly urged residents to back the President and APC candidates at all levels in the January 2027 elections.

What the governors said

Enugu Governor Peter Mbah said the region should build on the government's economic reforms rather than change course, comparing it to a sailor who does not abandon his ship mid-sea. He also described an empowerment programme for 5,000 women across the five South-East states. Imo Governor Hope Uzodimma said Igbo people were now seeing real inclusion and development under Tinubu, and that improved federal allocations had helped his state pay salaries and pensions regularly. Ebonyi Governor Francis Nwifuru praised the APC's skills programmes and denied links to any coalition outside the party. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo was represented by his deputy, Gilbert Ibezim, who said the region should repay the administration with votes. Deputy Speaker Benjamin Kalu also spoke, noting that no Igbo person had been president in six decades and praising the South East Development Commission.

Background

Peter Obi, the NDC presidential candidate and a former Anambra governor, won most of the South East in the 2023 presidential election. He has said he would serve only one term if elected, while some APC figures argue the region should aim for a longer stay in power through alliances. Obi had not responded to the rally at the time of the report.

What happens next

Both the APC and the NDC are expected to intensify their campaigns in the South East, which is likely to be one of the most closely watched regions in the election.

The rift between Davido and social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has moved to a new stage, with the two reportedly unfollowing each other on Instagram.

What happened

Checks on Saturday, 31 October 2026, reportedly showed that Davido, who has more than 31 million followers, had removed VDM from his following list, and that VDM, who has more than 3 million followers, had done the same. Neither appears to have blocked the other, and neither has publicly explained the move.

The build-up

The two were once close allies, but their friendship broke down earlier this year. This week, VDM's friend Kokopee said he had tried to bring them back together and that VDM felt betrayed by the singer. Davido responded with a 'cap' emoji, suggesting he disputed the account. He also congratulated rapper Zlatan Ibile on 'Inferno', a chart-topping song widely seen as a jab at VDM.

VDM's court case

Separately, VDM announced that a criminal case brought against him by the police had been struck out, and he shared his excitement about the ruling.

How people reacted

Fans were divided. Some praised Davido for cutting ties, while others criticised him and urged him to support VDM's charity work instead. Many said the unfollow confirmed that a reconciliation was unlikely for now.
