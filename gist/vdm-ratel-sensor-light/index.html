VeryDarkMan unveils RATEL Sensor Light, an affordable motion-activated light for Nigerian homes

31 Oct 2026, 14:32 GMT

VeryDarkMan (VDM) has unveiled another product under his growing RATEL brand, and this time it is something many Nigerian households may find useful, especially with the country's unpredictable electricity supply.

The new product is the RATEL Sensor Light, a motion-activated light that comes on automatically when it detects movement.

How much it costs

According to VDM, the sensor light comes in two sizes: the small size costs ₦4,200, while the big size costs ₦9,000.

Why it could be useful

Anyone living in Nigeria knows how frustrating the electricity situation can be. Imagine NEPA suddenly takes light at midnight and everywhere goes dark. You need to leave your bedroom, walk downstairs, check on your children or find your way outside to switch on the generator. Before you even find your torchlight, you have already knocked your leg against a chair.

This is where a sensor light could come in handy. It can be placed along staircases, corridors, bedroom entrances, wardrobes, storerooms and other dark corners of the house. Once it detects movement within its range, it switches on automatically, with no need to search for a wall switch in the dark.

A possible security benefit

The light could also help around entrances, gates and backyards. If somebody quietly approaches that area at night, the space around the sensor suddenly becomes bright.

Of course, a sensor light cannot replace proper home security, but it may help discourage unwanted visitors and make movement around a compound more noticeable.

VDM also explained that the product comes with different lighting colour options, including multicolour settings.

What buyers should check

At ₦4,200 for the smaller version, some households may find it affordable enough to install several units: one on the staircase, another in the corridor and perhaps two around the entrance. However, buyers should check the battery life, charging method, weather resistance and detection range before deciding where to install it.

A growing RATEL range

What stands out about this latest RATEL product is the idea behind it. VDM appears to be paying attention to the everyday inconveniences Nigerians face, especially those caused by poor electricity supply. From power stations to smaller household gadgets, he is gradually expanding his product range.

There is something worth appreciating about businesses that identify ordinary problems and offer affordable solutions. You don't always need to spend hundreds of thousands of naira to make your home more comfortable, and sometimes even a small gadget can save you the stress of walking around in darkness.

Imagine having four of these lights around your house. NEPA takes light, but as you move around, the lights come on automatically. Small enjoyment!

Mauricio Pochettino's time in charge of Manchester United got off to a painful start on Sunday, 22 November 2026, as Liverpool beat his side 4-0 at Anfield.

How the game went

United's plans fell apart early when defender Lenny Yoro was shown a red card in the eighth minute, leaving them with ten men for more than 80 minutes. Liverpool quickly took advantage. Dominik Szoboszlai opened the scoring in the 10th minute, and Florian Wirtz doubled the lead three minutes later.

Liverpool kept their two-goal lead until half-time and added two more after the break through Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.

Where it leaves the clubs

The result left United in 19th place, inside the Premier League relegation zone, while Liverpool moved into the top four.

Pochettino's reaction

Speaking after the match, Pochettino reportedly admitted that the early red card shaped the game, but urged his players to turn their frustration into better performances in the coming matches.

Background

Pochettino took over at Old Trafford only recently. The club has been linked with several players ahead of the January window, including Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey.

Nollywood is mourning the death of actress Adenike Abike Babajide, popularly known as Anike Sinleke, who has reportedly died after a battle with ovarian cancer.

How the news broke

The news emerged on Sunday, 22 November 2026, in a post by Bukola Fagbuyi, host of the Instagram live show Rubbin Minds With Bukky. Fagbuyi said the actress had been scheduled to appear as a guest on the programme that same day.

Her career

Anike Sinleke learned her craft under the mentorship of veteran actor Remmy Shitta and worked on film sets with filmmaker Deji Adenuga. At one point she stepped away from acting to complete her Higher National Diploma.

Beyond the screen, she was known in Lagos entertainment and cultural circles for her role with the Sisi Eko beauty pageant, which started around 2011.

Tributes

Fans and colleagues have taken to social media to mourn her, with many expressing shock at the news.

Goodluck Jonathan says dishonest leaders have made Nigeria a laughing stock

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Former President Goodluck Jonathan has reportedly said broken promises by politicians have damaged Nigeria's global image. He called for credible elections and a political culture that rewards competence over money.

Read more

Former President Goodluck Jonathan has reportedly criticised Nigeria's political class, saying dishonest leadership and unkept campaign promises have hurt the country's standing in the world.

Where he spoke

Jonathan gave the keynote address on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the third Paul Alaje Colloquium, themed The Brand: Nigeria, at the NAF Conference Centre in Kado, Abuja.

What he said

He is said to have lamented that Nigeria has lost much of the respect it once enjoyed internationally, and accused office holders of misleading citizens and acting against their public promises. He warned that continued dishonesty in public life would hold back the country's progress.

Jonathan reportedly called for reforms to how leaders are chosen, credible elections and real democracy within parties. He said the country needs a political culture that rewards ideas, character and competence rather than money, patronage and sectional interests.

Background

Jonathan was president from 2010 to 2015. His remarks come as campaigns for the January 2027 elections get under way.
