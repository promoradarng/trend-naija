VeryDarkMan unveils RATEL Sensor Light, an affordable motion-activated light for Nigerian homes

31 Oct 2026, 14:32 GMT

VeryDarkMan (VDM) has unveiled another product under his growing RATEL brand, and this time it is something many Nigerian households may find useful, especially with the country's unpredictable electricity supply.

The new product is the RATEL Sensor Light, a motion-activated light that comes on automatically when it detects movement.

How much it costs

According to VDM, the sensor light comes in two sizes: the small size costs ₦4,200, while the big size costs ₦9,000.

Why it could be useful

Anyone living in Nigeria knows how frustrating the electricity situation can be. Imagine NEPA suddenly takes light at midnight and everywhere goes dark. You need to leave your bedroom, walk downstairs, check on your children or find your way outside to switch on the generator. Before you even find your torchlight, you have already knocked your leg against a chair.

This is where a sensor light could come in handy. It can be placed along staircases, corridors, bedroom entrances, wardrobes, storerooms and other dark corners of the house. Once it detects movement within its range, it switches on automatically, with no need to search for a wall switch in the dark.

A possible security benefit

The light could also help around entrances, gates and backyards. If somebody quietly approaches that area at night, the space around the sensor suddenly becomes bright.

Of course, a sensor light cannot replace proper home security, but it may help discourage unwanted visitors and make movement around a compound more noticeable.

VDM also explained that the product comes with different lighting colour options, including multicolour settings.

What buyers should check

At ₦4,200 for the smaller version, some households may find it affordable enough to install several units: one on the staircase, another in the corridor and perhaps two around the entrance. However, buyers should check the battery life, charging method, weather resistance and detection range before deciding where to install it.

A growing RATEL range

What stands out about this latest RATEL product is the idea behind it. VDM appears to be paying attention to the everyday inconveniences Nigerians face, especially those caused by poor electricity supply. From power stations to smaller household gadgets, he is gradually expanding his product range.

There is something worth appreciating about businesses that identify ordinary problems and offer affordable solutions. You don't always need to spend hundreds of thousands of naira to make your home more comfortable, and sometimes even a small gadget can save you the stress of walking around in darkness.

Imagine having four of these lights around your house. NEPA takes light, but as you move around, the lights come on automatically. Small enjoyment!

Cute Abiola says he sold one of his cars as he appeals to Tinubu over power and hardship

6 Nov 2026, 09:45 GMT

Comedian and skit maker The Cute Abiola has posted videos appealing to President Tinubu over poor electricity and the cost of living, saying his solar system had run down and that he recently sold one of his cars to settle debts.

Read more

Comedian and skit maker The Cute Abiola has joined Nigerians complaining about poor electricity supply and economic hardship, in videos addressed directly to President Bola Tinubu.

What he said

In one video shared online on Friday, 6 November 2026, the content creator said his solar power system had gone off because of heavy use during the long power outages in his area. He appealed to the President to help Nigerians, saying everywhere was hot, there was no light and the suffering was too much. In another video, he said he had just sold one of his cars to ease his financial situation, explaining that he owed people money and still needed funds to shoot new content. He added that he hoped things would not get to the point where he had to sell his house.

Background

Abiola, whose real name is Abdulgafar Ahmed, became popular for his comedy skits and is a former member of the Nigerian Navy. His complaints come as consumers in Lagos, Abuja, Ogun, Kwara and Oyo states report renewed power outages, which the federal government has blamed on gas shortages, maintenance problems and reduced hydropower generation. The government has also started paying a ₦50,000 monthly relief grant to selected households.

How people reacted

Many fans sympathised and said his videos reflected what millions of Nigerians are going through. Some critics said he had not praised the government when things were going well for him and was now blaming the President, while others said public figures should be free to speak about their struggles.

Obi asks Tinubu to account for subsidy savings and over $100bn in loans

8 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

NDC candidate Peter Obi has challenged President Tinubu to explain what his government has done with the ₦15.8 trillion reportedly saved from fuel subsidy removal and more than $100 billion in borrowing, a day after Tinubu criticised Obi's record in Anambra.

Read more

Peter Obi, presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has called on President Bola Tinubu to account for money saved from the removal of the fuel subsidy and from government borrowing.

What Obi said

In a statement on his X account on Saturday, 7 November 2026, titled 'Good Day, Mr President: Nigerians Deserve Accountability', Obi said the large sums flowing into government coffers, including higher tax revenues, should be producing clear improvements in Nigerians' daily lives. He urged the President to visit the remaining 33 states he has not yet toured, spend time with the people and explain what his administration had done with the ₦15.8 trillion he said had been saved from subsidy removal, the more than $100 billion he said had been borrowed, and increased revenue from taxes.

The background to the exchange

Obi's statement came a day after Tinubu, speaking in Akwa Ibom, criticised the former Anambra governor's record on erosion in the state. At a reception in Uyo the same day, Tinubu said the naira would have become worthless without his reforms and that Nigeria would never go back to the subsidy. Vice-President Kashim Shettima also told an APC rally in Uyo that Obi had run himself out of the race.

The figures

Obi did not give a breakdown of how he arrived at the figures. The World Bank reported this week that Nigeria's total external debt, including private-sector debt, rose to about $145 billion in 2025. The government says its borrowing is needed to fund infrastructure and that its reforms have saved the economy from collapse.

What happens next

The Presidency had not publicly responded to Obi's statement at the time of writing. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Ghanaian actor Van Vicker has dismissed viral images appearing to show him with Nollywood actress Regina Daniels, saying they were generated using artificial intelligence.

The photos

According to reports on Sunday, 8 November 2026, one of the images showed Van Vicker carrying a much younger Regina Daniels, while another placed him beside her during what looked like her recent birthday photoshoot. The pictures spread widely online and caused a stir among fans of both actors.

What he said

Van Vicker said neither image was real, and that he and Regina Daniels had not been in contact for well over a decade. He spoke out quickly to stop the rumours from spreading further.

Background

Van Vicker is one of the best-known Ghanaian actors in Nollywood and has starred in many Nigerian films since the mid-2000s. Regina Daniels, who began acting as a child, has been in the news recently, including a public clash with journalist Kemi Olunloyo, and new videos from the mansion of her husband, Senator Ned Nwoko, have also drawn attention online. The case is the latest example of AI-generated images of celebrities spreading on social media and being mistaken for real photos.

Petrol prices at Lagos depots rise by up to ₦60 a litre as diesel falls

8 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Depot prices of petrol in Lagos rose by as much as ₦60 per litre this week, with several depots moving from ₦1,220 to ₦1,280, while diesel prices fell. The rise came despite a drop in global crude oil prices.

Read more

Petrol prices at major fuel depots in Lagos rose by as much as ₦60 per litre this week, while diesel prices fell, according to market data reported on Sunday, 8 November 2026.

The figures

Between Monday and Friday, the average petrol price across seven comparable depots in Lagos rose by about ₦41 per litre, while diesel fell by about ₦33. Some of the biggest increases were recorded at African Terminal, Integrated and A.A. Rano, which each raised their prices from ₦1,220 to ₦1,280 per litre. Aiteo raised its price by ₦56 to ₦1,277, Rain Oil by ₦55 to ₦1,280 and Nipco by ₦29 to ₦1,279, while MRS Tincan cut its price by ₦30 to ₦1,280. Similar movements were seen at depots in Warri and Port Harcourt.

Crude prices fell

The increases came even though international crude oil prices fell during the week, with Brent crude reportedly dropping by almost 5 per cent. Domestic supply conditions and marketers' costs also affect local prices.

Background

Depot prices are what marketers pay before selling to motorists, so changes usually feed through to filling-station prices within days. Petrol prices are now set by the market after the removal of the fuel subsidy in 2023, and have fluctuated with competition between the Dangote refinery, NNPC and importers. President Bola Tinubu said in Uyo this week that the government will never return to the subsidy. Cooking gas prices have also reportedly risen to about ₦1,900 per kilogram.

What it means

Motorists may see higher pump prices in the coming days, while businesses that rely on diesel could get some relief.
