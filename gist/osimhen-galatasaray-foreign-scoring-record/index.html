Osimhen closes in on Galatasaray's record for goals by a foreign player

29 Oct 2026, 17:46 GMT

Super Eagles striker Victor Osimhen is moving quickly up Galatasaray's list of all-time foreign goalscorers, and the club record is now within reach.

Where he stands

Osimhen's two goals in Monday's 2-1 derby win over Fenerbahçe took his tally for the Istanbul club to 68, according to reports on Thursday, 29 October 2026. That puts him third among foreign players in the club's history. Romanian great Gheorghe Hagi is second with 72 goals, while Argentine striker Mauro Icardi holds the record with 77, just nine more than Osimhen.

His form this season

The 27-year-old has reportedly scored nine goals and provided three assists in nine appearances in all competitions this season under coach Okan Buruk. His derby double was celebrated widely by Galatasaray fans and by Nigerians on social media.

Background

Osimhen first joined Galatasaray on loan from Napoli and became a fan favourite after a prolific first season that helped the club win the Turkish league title. He later completed a permanent move. The former African Footballer of the Year remains Nigeria's main attacking threat, and his form will be closely watched by Super Eagles fans.

What happens next

At his current rate, Osimhen could pass Hagi within weeks, and could challenge Icardi's record before the end of the season.

  • Osimhen has 68 goals for Galatasaray.
  • Mauro Icardi holds the foreign-player record with 77.
  • He scored twice in the derby win over Fenerbahçe.

Manchester United reportedly eyeing Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

Manchester United are reportedly interested in Fulham and Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey as a possible replacement for Lisandro Martinez. Bassey's contract is said to run out at the end of the season, and several other European clubs are also said to be watching him.

Read more

Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey is reportedly attracting interest from Manchester United, according to transfer reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026.

What is being reported

United are said to be considering Bassey as a possible replacement for Argentine defender Lisandro Martinez, whose contract is reported to expire at the end of the season. Martinez is said to be open to leaving Old Trafford, with Barcelona reportedly making enquiries. Luke Shaw and Harry Maguire are also said to be out of contract next summer, which could leave United needing several defenders.

Bassey's situation

Bassey has been one of Fulham's most consistent players this season. His contract at Craven Cottage reportedly runs out at the end of the season, although Fulham are said to have an option to extend it by a year and would prefer to agree a new deal. Because his contract is running down, he could be available for a relatively low fee. Newcastle United, Everton, Inter Milan, Borussia Dortmund and RB Leipzig are also reportedly monitoring him.

Background

Bassey, who can play at centre-back or left-back, joined Fulham from Ajax in 2023 after earlier spells at Rangers. He has become a regular for the Super Eagles and is valued for his pace and versatility. Manchester United, managed by Michael Carrick, have made a slow start to the season.

What happens next

No formal offer has been reported, and any move is unlikely before the January transfer window at the earliest.

Samson Adamu becomes first Nigerian to be confirmed as CAF General Secretary

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

CAF has reportedly confirmed Samson Adamu, 40, as its substantive General Secretary, making him the first Nigerian to hold the post since the body was founded in 1957. He had served in an interim role since March.

Read more

Nigerian football administrator Samson Adamu has been confirmed as General Secretary of the Confederation of African Football (CAF), the first Nigerian to hold the position.

What happened

CAF's executive committee reportedly approved his appointment as substantive General Secretary, according to reports on Friday, 30 October 2026. Adamu had held the role on an interim basis since March 2026, after his predecessor, Veron Mosengo-Omba, resigned. The same executive committee meeting also voted to return the Africa Cup of Nations to a two-year cycle.

Who is Samson Adamu

The 40-year-old previously served as CAF's Director of Competitions, where he was involved in organising the Africa Cup of Nations and the CAF Champions League. He holds a FIFA Master's degree in sports management, law and humanities. He began his career in football administration by founding the Copa Lagos beach soccer tournament in the early 2010s.

What the job involves

As General Secretary, Adamu will run CAF's day-to-day administration, oversee legal matters and carry out decisions taken by the executive committee. His first big task will be preparations for AFCON 2027, which will be co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.

How people reacted

Nigerian football fans and officials celebrated the appointment as a proud moment for the country, which has produced many top players but rarely held top administrative jobs in African football.

US court rejects bid to seek evidence from Tinubu in FOIA case, Presidency says

31 Oct 2026, 17:44 GMT

A federal court in Washington has reportedly refused a request by US researcher Aaron Greenspan to seek evidence from President Bola Tinubu and a lobbying firm in his records lawsuit against the FBI and DEA. The Presidency welcomed the ruling, while the underlying case continues.

Read more

A United States federal court has turned down an attempt to obtain evidence from President Bola Tinubu as part of a long-running records lawsuit, according to a statement from the Presidency on Saturday, 31 October 2026.

What the court decided

The case, before the US District Court for the District of Columbia, is a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit filed by American researcher Aaron Greenspan against the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA). On 30 October, the court reportedly denied Greenspan's request for limited discovery from President Tinubu and the lobbying firm DCI Group, which he had linked to alleged cyberattacks on his website. The court found the request was not relevant enough to the main records dispute. It also rejected a request by a third party to file a friend-of-the-court brief, refused a related request for judicial notice, and denied Greenspan's motion for summary judgment, meaning the main case continues.

The Presidency's reaction

The President's adviser on information and strategy, Bayo Onanuga, announced the development. Lawyers quoted in the statement said the ruling kept the case focused on the issues properly before the court and set clear limits on what it would consider.

Background

Greenspan has for several years sought the release of US government records he says relate to investigations involving the President in the 1990s. The President's lawyers have consistently opposed the release of the records, and the Presidency has dismissed the claims linked to them. The case has drawn wide attention in Nigeria, particularly from opposition supporters.

What happens next

The FOIA case itself will continue, and further rulings are expected. Greenspan had not publicly responded to the decision at the time of the report.

Content creator De Peters questions how VeryDarkMan presents his power station business

31 Oct 2026, 17:44 GMT

Content creator De Peters has criticised VeryDarkMan for presenting his power station venture as a favour to Nigerians when he is selling the units for profit. He also accused VDM of criticising other influencers for promotions while advertising his own products.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) is facing fresh criticism over his power station business, this time from content creator De Peters.

What De Peters said

In comments reported on Saturday, 31 October 2026, De Peters reportedly asked why VDM was acting as though he was giving Nigerians free power stations, when he was in fact selling them to make money. He argued that VDM often criticises other influencers for promoting cryptocurrency, betting platforms and other products, yet was now promoting his own goods. He also said other businesses already sell power stations in Nigeria, some with similar capacity and specifications at lower prices.

Background

VDM has spent recent weeks promoting his Ratel power stations, imported from China, with videos from a warehouse and demonstrations of the units. He has said buyers will be limited to two units each and has clashed with businessman Blord, whom he accuses of copying the idea. Other commentators have praised VDM for bringing more affordable solar options to Nigerians, with one claiming that prices of power stations have fallen since the Ratel brand launched. The debate comes as federal agencies warn celebrities they will be held responsible for promoting fraudulent products, although there is no suggestion that VDM's products are fraudulent.

How people reacted

The comments sparked a wider conversation about influencer marketing. Some users agreed that VDM should not present a business as charity, while his supporters said he was offering a useful product at a fair price. VDM had not publicly responded at the time of the report.
