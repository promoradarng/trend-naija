First Muslim senator and first Black woman governor among US midterm history-makers

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The 2026 US midterm elections produced several historic firsts, according to results and projections reported on Wednesday, 4 November 2026.

First Muslim senator

Democrat Abdul El-Sayed reportedly defeated Republican Mike Rogers in Michigan's Senate race, becoming the first Muslim member of the US Senate. A former Detroit health director, he ran on a progressive platform including Medicare for All and higher taxes on billionaires, and won the seat left by retiring Democrat Gary Peters. His campaign survived a bitter contest in which a Democratic rival he beat in the primary endorsed his Republican opponent.

First Black woman governor

In Georgia, former Atlanta mayor Keisha Lance Bottoms, 56, defeated Republican businessman Rick Jackson, becoming the first Black woman elected governor of any US state and Georgia's first female governor. Her victory reportedly ends 24 years of Republican control of the office. Atlanta-born Nigerian singer Davido shared the news on his Instagram Story.

Texas turns blue

In Texas, Democrat Gina Hinojosa reportedly defeated Republican governor Greg Abbott, who was seeking a fourth term, by about 53.5 per cent to 46.5 per cent. When she takes office in January 2027, the 52-year-old will be the state's first Latina governor and its first Democratic governor since Ann Richards left office in 1995.

More firsts

In New Hampshire, Democratic congressman Chris Pappas, 46, defeated former Republican senator John E. Sununu to become the first openly gay man elected to the US Senate, keeping the seat of retiring Democrat Jeanne Shaheen. In New Mexico, Democrat Deb Haaland, a member of Laguna Pueblo and a former US interior secretary, won the governorship, becoming the first Native American woman elected governor of a US state.

Kansas breaks a long streak

In Kansas, Democrat Adam Hamilton, a pastor who leads the largest United Methodist church in the country, reportedly defeated Republican senator Roger Marshall, becoming the first Democrat elected to the US Senate from the state since 1932. He ran as a moderate and won endorsements from some prominent Kansas Republicans.

Background

The results came as Democrats were projected to win control of both the House and the Senate, in what many analysts described as a rebuke of President Donald Trump's second-term agenda amid concern over the economy, tariffs and the conflict with Iran.

  • Abdul El-Sayed is the first Muslim US senator.
  • Keisha Lance Bottoms is the first Black woman elected governor of a US state.
  • Chris Pappas and Deb Haaland also made history.

Putin, Trump and Xi meet at APEC reception ahead of three-way talks

Vladimir Putin greeted Donald Trump at an APEC welcome reception in Shenzhen hosted by Xi Jinping. The three leaders are expected to hold a rare trilateral meeting.

Russian President Vladimir Putin joined US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping at a welcome reception for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in Shenzhen, China, on Wednesday, 18 November 2026.

The meeting

Footage from the reception showed Putin walking up to Trump and shaking hands as Xi looked on during the welcome banquet and group photo. It was the first in-person meeting between Trump and Putin since their August 2025 summit in Alaska.

Three-way talks

The leaders are expected to hold a trilateral meeting, widely seen as the highlight of the gathering. It would be the first time the three have held joint face-to-face talks.

The summit

The two-day APEC leaders' meeting, themed around building a prosperous Asia-Pacific community, is focusing on regional economic integration, trade, investment, AI governance and supply chains.

Thousands march in Haiti to praise El Salvador's President Bukele as his troops take on gangs

18 Nov 2026, 14:49 GMT

Thousands of Haitians took to the streets of Port-au-Prince and other cities on Tuesday to praise El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele and his soldiers, who are leading a tough new offensive against the gangs that have terrorised Haiti for years.

Read more

Thousands of people took to the streets of Port-au-Prince and other parts of Haiti on Tuesday, praising El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele and his troops as they take on the country's powerful gangs.

"Viv Bukele"

Photos from the marches show huge crowds filling the streets, waving Haitian flags and holding up posters of the Salvadoran president. Many carried banners reading "Viv Bukele", Haitian Creole for "Long live Bukele".

Why Haitians are cheering

For years, armed gangs have controlled large parts of Port-au-Prince and beyond, killing, kidnapping and forcing hundreds of thousands of people from their homes.

Salvadoran troops have been in Haiti since early 2025 as part of an international mission, and recently launched their first major offensive against the gangs, with reports of heavy fighting and deaths.

Many marchers said Bukele is doing what the United Nations and the United States failed to do for years, while ordinary Haitians continued to suffer.

The Bukele model

Bukele became popular at home by cracking down hard on El Salvador's gangs, sharply reducing violence in a country once known as one of the most dangerous in the world. Many Haitians hope his forces can bring the same results.

His methods have also faced criticism from human rights groups, who say the crackdown in El Salvador involved mass arrests and abuses. But for Haitians who have lived in fear for years, many say any hope of relief is welcome.

LeBron James sues AI betting app over alleged fake endorsement

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NBA star LeBron James has filed a lawsuit against AI sports betting app Bron AI, accusing it of using his identity to market a product he never endorsed.

Read more

NBA star LeBron James has sued the AI-powered sports betting app Bron AI, accusing the company of using his identity to promote a product he never endorsed.

The lawsuit

The suit, filed on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, in a New York federal court by James and his trademark company, targets PlayVision Inc., the app's parent company, and its two co-founders. It came 12 days after James sent the company a cease-and-desist letter over what he called the unauthorised use of his name, image and likeness.

The suit argues that his endorsement is uniquely valuable and accuses the company of deliberately exploiting it.

The app

Bron AI describes itself as a tool that gathers data and odds from sportsbooks and prediction markets to help users find bets and place them on other apps. A promotional video, since removed, reportedly featured a character resembling James wearing a jersey with his nickname, Bron, and his number 23.

What happens next

The app had removed the video after the cease-and-desist letter, but had not changed its name as of Tuesday evening. The company's response to the lawsuit was not included in the reports.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China, where he sources products for his RATEL brand, and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.
