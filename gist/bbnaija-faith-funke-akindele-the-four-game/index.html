BBNaija's Faith builds filter game for Funke Akindele's new film The Four

11 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Big Brother Naija star Dr Faith Adewale has won praise online after creating a game to promote Funke Akindele's upcoming film, The Four.

The game

In a video shared on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, Faith showed how the Snapchat filter works. Players are paired with three of their real friends and must complete quick physical challenges, such as tapping the screen rapidly or raising their eyebrows, to 'rescue' each one. When all three are saved, the group of four is reunited, echoing the theme of the film. Faith set a time of 15.4 seconds and challenged her followers to beat it. The filter also includes a button that links to a cinema website where users can buy tickets.

Funke reacts

Funke Akindele, who directs and stars in the film, spotted the post and responded with delight, and the cinema chain also celebrated the idea online.

Background

The Four is due in Nigerian cinemas on 11 December 2026, in time for the festive season. Funke Akindele is one of Nollywood's most commercially successful filmmakers, with several of her previous December releases breaking box office records.

Makinde camp denies claim he will step down for Atiku

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde's campaign has dismissed as fake a claim that he plans to withdraw his presidential bid in favour of Atiku Abubakar on 30 November. The claim was made by a political commentator.

Read more

The campaign of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has dismissed as fake news a claim that he plans to drop his 2027 presidential bid in favour of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar.

The claim

Political commentator Lere Olayinka had written that Makinde, the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) candidate, would step down for Atiku on 30 November, the date of the opposition parties' summit in Abuja.

The denial

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, the verified X account linked to Makinde's campaign rejected the claim and accused the ruling APC of spreading falsehoods. The post drew both support and criticism from social media users.

Background

Makinde said this week that he remains open to working with Atiku and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu, but ruled out a formal merger of parties. Opposition parties are due to hold their second summit in Abuja on Monday. No response from the APC to the accusation was included in the reports.
