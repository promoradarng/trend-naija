Victor Umeh: Any Igbo person against Peter Obi is an enemy of the Igbo

9 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Senator Victor Umeh, who represents Anambra Central, has warned that any Igbo person working against Peter Obi's presidential ambition is an enemy of the Igbo people.

What he said

In a video that circulated on Monday, 9 November 2026, Umeh, a member of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), described Obi as the conscience of the Igbo people in the 2027 election and urged South-East residents to unite behind his bid. He cautioned political leaders in the region, particularly the Anambra State Government, against actions that could undermine Obi's campaign.

Background

Obi, a former Anambra governor, is the NDC's presidential candidate, with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. While he remains popular in the South-East, several of the region's governors have declared support for President Bola Tinubu. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo's APGA last week adopted Tinubu as its candidate and promised him half of the state's votes, while Ebonyi Governor Francis Nwifuru on Monday promised Tinubu 80 per cent of Ebonyi's votes. The NDC in Anambra has set a target of 98 per cent of the state's votes for Obi.

Reactions

The Anambra government had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

Yul Edochie responds

Actor Yul Edochie, a supporter of President Tinubu, challenged Umeh in a video on 10 November, saying he respected the senator but that such remarks could make Igbo people see one another as enemies. He said political choice is a personal right, and noted that Tinubu and Seyi Makinde are both from the South-West, yet no Yoruba leader has called supporters of either man enemies of the region.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Opposition parties to sign unity pact in Abuja on 23 November

16 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Nigeria's main opposition parties will sign a joint commitment to democratic renewal at a summit in Abuja on 23 November, adopting a national covenant, a framework for opposition unity and a reform programme.

Read more

Nigeria's main opposition parties are set to sign a joint pact at a summit in Abuja on Monday, 23 November 2026.

The plan

In a statement on Monday, 16 November 2026, signed by Salihu Lukman, the Coalition for Democracy said the Second Summit of Nigeria's Opposition Political Parties would take place at the NAF Conference Centre. Participating parties are expected to adopt three documents prepared by working groups over several months: a National Covenant for Democratic Renewal, a Framework for Opposition Unity and a National Reform Programme. Party leaders will then sign them. The coalition described the summit as a turning point in how the opposition coordinates ahead of the 2027 elections.

Background

The event follows the first opposition leaders' summit, held at the Yar'Adua Centre in Abuja on 31 August 2026, convened by the G100 group with the House of Representatives minority caucus. Efforts to field a single opposition candidate against President Bola Tinubu have so far failed, with Atiku Abubakar, Peter Obi and Seyi Makinde all running, though the Atiku and Makinde camps are reportedly in talks. It is not clear whether the pact will include any agreement on candidates. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Atiku says Tinubu only came to Plateau when he needed votes

16 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has criticised President Tinubu's visit to Plateau State, saying residents needed their president during killings and kidnappings, not at an election rally.

Read more

Former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar has criticised President Bola Tinubu over his recent campaign visit to Plateau State.

What he said

In a statement issued from Potiskum, Yobe State, on Monday, 16 November 2026, by his campaign's director of strategic communication, Phrank Shaibu, Atiku said no campaign promise or appeal for forgiveness could make up for the President's absence when communities in Plateau were under attack. He said residents had needed the President at the height of killings and kidnappings, and accused him of using people's grief as a political tool by returning to seek votes.

Atiku's campaign

Background

Tinubu's campaign says the government has launched a five-year defence plan and approved the recruitment of 28,000 soldiers and 50,000 police officers. Former Plateau governors Simon Lalong and Joshua Dariye recently endorsed the President. The Presidency had not responded to Atiku's statement at the time of writing.

Kwara APC governorship rally in Offa reportedly turns violent over unpaid mobilisation funds

16 Nov 2026, 11:21 GMT

An APC governorship rally in Offa, Kwara State, reportedly descended into chaos after claims that some participants were not paid the mobilisation allowances they had been promised. The incident has revived debate about paying crowds to attend political events.

Read more

A governorship campaign rally organised by the All Progressives Congress (APC) in Offa, Kwara State, has reportedly descended into chaos following claims that some participants were not paid the mobilisation allowances they had been promised.

What reportedly happened

According to reports, the rally drew a large crowd of supporters to the streets of Offa. But tension rose when some of those who had turned up complained that they had not received the money they were allegedly promised for attending.

The situation reportedly turned violent as angry participants confronted organisers, disrupting the event.

The full details, including whether anyone was injured and how much money was involved, have not been independently confirmed, and the party has not publicly commented on the incident.

The business of crowds

The incident shines a light on a practice many Nigerians already know about: paying people, often called "mobilisation", to attend political rallies.

Across parties, campaign organisers frequently provide transport, food, branded caps and T-shirts, and sometimes cash, to fill venues and create the impression of massive support. When those promises are not kept, frustration can quickly boil over.

It also raises a deeper question. If people have to be paid to attend, how much do the crowds at political rallies really tell us about genuine support?

Should people be paid to attend political rallies, or should campaigns rely only on supporters who come out of conviction?

Sowore calls for legal cannabis, says Nigeria could earn up to ₦2.86 trillion a year

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has urged the federal government to legalise, regulate and tax cannabis. He pointed to revenues in Canada and US states and projected Nigeria could earn between ₦715 billion and ₦2.86 trillion yearly.

Read more

African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has called on the federal government to legalise cannabis, arguing that Nigeria is missing out on billions of naira in revenue by keeping it illegal.

What he said

In a post on X on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, Sowore said the country should build a regulated cannabis industry instead of spending resources arresting people and destroying plantations. He said he has backed legalisation since 2018.

The figures

He pointed to regulated markets abroad, citing about CA$2.2 billion in government revenue from recreational cannabis in Canada in 2023-2024, around $1.067 billion in cannabis tax in California in 2024, and roughly $255.4 million in Colorado.

Using those examples, he projected that Nigeria could earn between $550 million and $2.2 billion a year, or about ₦715 billion to ₦2.86 trillion at an exchange rate of ₦1,300 to the dollar. He acknowledged that actual earnings would depend on how any such market is designed and run.

He also argued that legalisation could create jobs in farming, pharmaceutical production and industrial hemp, and support research and exports.

Background

Cannabis remains illegal in Nigeria, and the NDLEA regularly carries out raids on farms and arrests dealers. Sowore, a long-time activist, is running for president in 2027 on the AAC platform.
