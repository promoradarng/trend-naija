Toyin Abraham's throwback TikTok post sparks talk of rivalry with Funke Akindele

31 Oct 2026, 09:42 GMT

Toyin Abraham has shared an old TikTok collaboration from her film 'OverSabi Aunty' and said she looks forward to more ahead of her December release 'Bestie'. Fans linked the post to Funke Akindele's new Facebook partnership for 'The Four', reviving talk of a rivalry.

Read more

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Toyin Abraham has once again found herself at the centre of talk about a rivalry with Funke Akindele, after a social media post that many fans read as a response to her colleague.

What she posted

On Friday, 30 October 2026, Toyin Abraham shared on Instagram a throwback clip of a TikTok collaboration she did while promoting her earlier film, 'OverSabi Aunty'. In the caption, she said she was looking forward to more collaborations ahead of her new film, 'Bestie', which is due in cinemas in December 2026.

Why fans linked it to Funke

The post came just days after Funke Akindele announced a partnership with Facebook for her new film, 'The Four'. Many fans interpreted Toyin's post as a subtle reminder that she had worked with a major platform first, although she did not mention Funke, and her intention has not been confirmed.

Background

The two actresses are among the most successful filmmakers in Nollywood, and fans have long compared their box office results. Toyin has also faced accusations of copying Funke's ideas, which she has denied. In the past she has spoken warmly about Funke, congratulating her and calling her the winner of their rivalry after one of Funke's films crossed ₦1 billion at the box office.

How people reacted

Reactions were divided. Critics questioned the timing of the post and accused her of seeking attention, while her supporters said she had every right to talk about her own past work. Funke Akindele has not commented.

AY Makun responds to backlash after missing Cardiff show, promises free return

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Comedian AY Makun has defended himself after failing to appear at his AY Live show in Cardiff, which led to calls for him to be cancelled. He blamed repeated problems on the tour and promised a free return show, while saying ticket holders will still get refunds.

Read more

Comedian and filmmaker Ayo 'AY' Makun has spoken out after facing a wave of criticism for not turning up at his AY Live show in Cardiff, Wales.

What happened

Fans who bought tickets for the Cardiff show on Sunday were left angry when the comedian did not appear. Many criticised him on social media, and some called for him to be cancelled. AY had earlier apologised, saying fans' time, money and trust deserved respect, but the apology did not end the criticism.

His side of the story

In a long statement reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, AY said veterans in the industry had advised him to stay quiet, but he felt his audience deserved an explanation. He said he had spent years performing at events where payment promises were broken, sometimes paying fellow performers from his own pocket. He said a five-hour drive from Sheffield to Cardiff, after repeated bad experiences on the same tour, became his breaking point, but added that the fans who turned up had done nothing wrong. He also complained that negative stories about him get more attention than his achievements, and said calls for cancellation would not make him give up.

What he promised

AY said he would host a free return show in Cardiff, paid for by himself, once a venue and logistics are confirmed. He said this would not replace refunds owed to people who bought tickets for the original event.

Background

AY is one of Nigeria's best-known comedians and has run the AY Live brand for many years, as well as producing hit films. His account of the tour problems has not been confirmed by the organisers.

NCDC reports two suspected Ebola cases in North-East, patients isolated

31 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

The Nigeria Centre for Disease Control says two suspected Ebola cases have been identified in the North-East and both people have been isolated. Laboratory tests are under way and contacts are being traced; the cases have not been confirmed.

Read more

The Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention (NCDC) has announced two suspected cases of Ebola virus disease in the North-East of the country.

What the NCDC said

In a social media post on Saturday, 31 October 2026, the NCDC said the two cases were suspected only and had not been confirmed. Both people have been isolated, laboratory tests are being carried out, and health workers have begun identifying and listing everyone who came into contact with them. The agency did not say which states the cases are in or give details about the patients, and said it would provide updates as verified information becomes available.

Advice to the public

The NCDC urged Nigerians to wash their hands thoroughly and often, improve sanitation and follow guidance from health authorities. Health officials generally advise anyone with symptoms such as sudden fever, weakness, vomiting or unexplained bleeding, especially after contact with a sick person or recent travel to an affected country, to seek medical care quickly and avoid self-medication.

Background

Nigeria recorded one confirmed imported Ebola case this month in a traveller who had recently been to Uganda. That patient was evacuated to Switzerland for treatment, and dozens of contacts were monitored. The wider outbreak, caused by the Bundibugyo strain, began in the Democratic Republic of Congo in May and has now passed 10,000 confirmed cases, with nearly 5,000 deaths. Africa CDC warned this week that the continent is not winning the fight against the virus.

What happens next

Test results are expected in the coming days. If the cases are confirmed, they would be the first locally detected cases in Nigeria during this outbreak.

Atiku condemns attacks on Sowore and Sokoto ADC candidate, demands probe

31 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has condemned the recent attacks on Omoyele Sowore and Sokoto ADC governorship candidate Manir Dan'iya, blaming supporters of the ruling party. He demanded transparent investigations and a guarantee of a free and fair election.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has spoken out against a string of reported attacks on opposition politicians, accusing supporters of the ruling party of trying to intimidate rivals ahead of the 2027 elections.

What he said

In a statement on X on Saturday, 31 October 2026, Atiku, the presidential candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), condemned the assault on AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore in Ado-Ekiti and the attack on the convoy of Manir Muhammad Dan'iya, the ADC governorship candidate in Sokoto State. He also criticised what he said was harassment of residents in Sokoto for wearing ADC-branded clothing, and described the unexplained disappearance of some social media influencers as a warning sign.

Who he blamed

Atiku blamed loyalists and enforcers of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC), noting that the attacks happened while President Bola Tinubu was abroad on a religious pilgrimage. He described the violence as a sign of desperation and said the ADC would resist any attempt to wipe out political opposition.

His demands

He called on the federal government to order immediate and transparent investigations into the attacks on Sowore and Dan'iya, and to guarantee a free, fair and competitive environment for the 2027 elections.

Background

Sowore says he and his team were attacked at a restaurant in Ado-Ekiti on Friday; police deny he was kidnapped and have ordered an investigation. Dan'iya says his convoy was blocked and shot at near the Sokoto airport on Friday night. The APC and the Sokoto State Government had not responded to Atiku's statement at the time of the report.
