Mary Njoku questions NAFDAC chief over unsafe food products

4 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Actress and filmmaker Mary Njoku has asked NAFDAC Director-General Mojisola Adeyeye whether Nigerians are now safe from fake and unsafe products, criticising what she called a cycle of outrage with no results.

Read more

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Mary Njoku has publicly questioned the head of the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC), Professor Mojisola Adeyeye, over continuing concerns about fake and unsafe products in Nigeria.

What she said

In a social media post on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, Njoku recalled the NAFDAC chief's laughter during a recent food safety controversy, which she said she would never forget, and asked whether Nigerians are actually safe now. She criticised what she described as a familiar Nigerian cycle in which people raise an alarm, nothing is resolved, and attention moves to the next controversy. She also asked whether ordinary people now need special expertise to know whether their food is safe.

Background

Food safety has been a major public concern in Nigeria this year, with reports of fake and expired products, altered expiry dates and contaminated foods. This week, police in Kano arrested traders accused of changing expiry dates on milk, and a processor in Ondo was arrested over unhygienic food handling. NAFDAC has carried out raids and destroyed large quantities of unsafe products, but critics say enforcement remains weak.

Who is Mary Njoku

Mary Njoku is an actress, producer and the founder of the ROK Studios film company, which she sold to the French broadcaster Canal+ in 2019. She remains one of Nollywood's most influential figures.

How people reacted

Many Nigerians agreed with her, sharing their own fears about unsafe products, while others urged consumers to report suspicious goods to the agency. NAFDAC had not responded publicly at the time of reporting.

Actor Uche Odoputa to hold empty-casket funeral for father killed after 2022 kidnapping

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actor Uche Odoputa says his family will hold a funeral with an empty casket for his father, who was abducted in Imo State in 2022 and killed despite a ransom payment. He urged people carrying hidden grief to seek support.

Read more

Nollywood actor Uche Odoputa has announced that his family will hold a funeral for his late father without a body to bury, more than four years after his death.

What he said

In an emotional video posted on Instagram on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, the actor said the family would gather in a few weeks to honour his father, Mr Kenneth Chukwumezie Odoputa, whom those close to him called 'Honestman'. He said they have no body to lay to rest, but have his life to remember and his dignity to honour.

What happened to his father

According to reports, the actor's father, who was born on 10 August 1950, was kidnapped by gunmen in Imo State in May 2022 and was killed despite a ransom being paid. He died on 6 May 2022. His remains were never recovered.

A message on grief

Odoputa also used the video to encourage people who are carrying hidden grief to seek help from someone they trust, saying no one should carry such pain alone.

Background

Kidnapping for ransom has affected many families across Nigeria, including in the South East, where armed groups have attacked travellers and residents in recent years. Odoputa is known for his roles in Nollywood films. Ebonyi State's governor this week sent a bill to make kidnapping punishable by death.

How people reacted

Colleagues and fans sent condolences and prayers, praising the actor for speaking openly about his loss.
