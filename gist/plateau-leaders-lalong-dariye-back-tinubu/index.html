Lalong, Dariye and other Plateau leaders endorse Tinubu for 2027

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Prominent political figures from Plateau State have endorsed President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid for 2027.

The meeting

The endorsement was made by voice vote at a meeting in Jos organised by Arewa for Asiwaju (A4A), a pro-Tinubu group, according to a statement released on Friday, 13 November 2026. Those present included former governors, ministers, senators, members of the House of Representatives, commissioners and council chairmen, who also adopted other All Progressives Congress (APC) candidates in the state.

What the leaders said

Former governor Simon Lalong, who chaired the meeting as deputy chairman of A4A's board of trustees, said those present believed the President was committed to Nigeria's development and that keeping him in office would speed up economic growth. Former governor Joshua Dariye credited the Tinubu administration with meaningful contributions to Plateau and the country.

Background

Plateau is a key battleground in the North Central. Peter Obi won the state in the 2023 presidential election. A Middle Belt youth group that backed Obi in 2023 also recently switched its support to Tinubu. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Defence Ministry deletes post promoting Tinubu's Ibadan rally after backlash

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Ministry of Defence has reportedly removed a post from its official X account that publicised the APC presidential rally in Ibadan. Critics said it breached rules on the use of state resources in campaigns.

Read more

Nigeria's Ministry of Defence has reportedly taken down a post from its official social media accounts that promoted President Bola Tinubu's APC campaign rally in Ibadan, after a public outcry over the neutrality of state institutions.

The post

The post, published on Saturday on the ministry's verified X account, announced the APC's re-election rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, and included photos showing campaign banners of Tinubu and Vice President Kashim Shettima. It said Defence Minister Christopher Musa had inspected the venue to check security and crowd-control arrangements ahead of the president's arrival.

The criticism

Civic groups and opposition figures are said to have condemned the post, arguing that it went against the Electoral Act's ban on using state resources and public media to favour or disadvantage any party, as well as guidelines that require public institutions to remain neutral.

Background

Tinubu held the rally in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026, where APC candidates in the South-West were endorsed. He later said he kept the event low-key out of respect for victims of the Bayelsa boat accident. The ministry was not reported to have commented on why the post was removed.

Atiku denies quitting 2027 race, says those spreading the claim are working for Tinubu

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly dismissed claims that he has withdrawn from the 2027 election. His campaign said no group calling itself his supporters can speak for him, and accused rivals of tactics that help the ruling party.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly rejected claims that he has pulled out of the 2027 presidential race, describing them as a deliberate falsehood.

What his campaign said

In a statement issued on Sunday, 22 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, the campaign is said to have stressed that no group presenting itself as Atiku's supporters had the authority to announce a withdrawal on his behalf.

The statement reportedly accused those behind the claim of borrowing tactics it associated with the ruling APC, such as presenting so-called supporters from a rival camp. It said any opposition party spreading such stories was effectively helping President Bola Tinubu, and urged parties that want to defeat the APC to make their case directly to voters.

Still campaigning

Atiku's campaign said his Affordable Life campaign was still active and focused on the election.

Background

The denial comes as campaigns for the January 2027 presidential election gather pace, with Tinubu of the APC, Atiku of the ADC and Peter Obi of the NDC among the leading candidates. Speculation about possible withdrawals and alliances has been circulating online in recent days.

Wike says rejecting Tinubu in 2027 would leave reforms unfinished

FCT Minister Nyesom Wike has reportedly said voting President Tinubu out in 2027 would leave his economic reforms incomplete. He spoke at a church service in Rivers State that dedicated his Rainbow Coalition campaign council.

FCT Minister Nyesom Wike has reportedly warned that rejecting President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid in 2027 would leave the government's economic reforms unfinished and could undo gains already made.

Where he spoke

Wike made the remarks on Sunday, 22 November 2026, at a thanksgiving and dedication service at St Peter's Anglican Church in Rumueprikom, his hometown in Obio/Akpor Local Government Area of Rivers State. The service formally dedicated the Rainbow Coalition Campaign Council ahead of the elections, and Wike said campaigning would begin on 23 November.

His argument

He reportedly described Tinubu as a leader who took unpopular but necessary decisions to stop the country from sliding into crisis. As an example, he pointed to the university system, saying the repeated cycle of lecturers' strikes over unpaid entitlements had largely been broken.

Background

Wike, a former PDP governor of Rivers State, is one of Tinubu's most vocal backers and leads a coalition of PDP members supporting the president. Earlier this month, he told his FCT campaign team not to let opposition parties reach 25 per cent of the vote in Abuja.

SERAP sues INEC and data commission over suspected AI-generated photos in voter register

22 Nov 2026, 11:58 GMT

SERAP has filed a Federal High Court lawsuit asking for an independent investigation into suspected AI-generated photographs and other questionable records in INEC's preliminary voter register, and into whether voters' personal data was unlawfully accessed or altered.

Read more

The Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project (SERAP) has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court seeking an independent investigation into suspected artificial intelligence-generated photographs and other questionable records in INEC's preliminary Register of Voters.

The lawsuit

The suit, numbered FHC/ABJ/CS/2863/2026, names the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) as defendants.

SERAP is asking the court to compel the data protection commission to examine the relevant records and electronic data held by INEC.

It also wants investigators to establish whether voters' personal information was unlawfully accessed or altered, and whether the Nigeria Data Protection Act was breached.

What INEC has said

INEC has already acknowledged that suspected AI-generated photographs appeared in the preliminary register. The commission said its Automated Biometric Identification System flagged 268 records involving suspected AI images or other irregularities.

INEC insists the published register is not final, and says verification and data-cleaning will be completed before the register is certified on 15 December.

What the court has not decided

No court has found INEC responsible for manipulating the register, and the case has not yet been given a public hearing date.

Why it matters

The voter register is the foundation of any credible election. With the 2027 general elections approaching, questions about its accuracy, and about how voters' personal data is protected, go to the heart of public trust in the process.
