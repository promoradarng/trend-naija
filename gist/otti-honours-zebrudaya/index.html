Alex Otti praised for honouring Nollywood legend Zebrudaya with ₦10 million and a car as Anambra government faces criticism

27 Oct 2026, 07:40 GMT

Veteran Nollywood actor and entertainment personality Ambassador Tony Akposheri has praised Abia State Governor Alex Otti for reportedly honouring legendary Nigerian comedian and actor Chief Chika Okpala, popularly known as Zebrudaya, with a gift of ₦10 million and a car.

Akposheri expressed disappointment that Zebrudaya, who hails from Nnobi in Anambra State, has allegedly not received similar recognition from his home state despite his enormous contributions to Nigeria's entertainment industry.

According to the veteran actor, it is remarkable that a governor from another state has chosen to celebrate a man whose talent and creativity helped shape Nigerian television entertainment for generations.

Zebrudaya: a legend who entertained generations of Nigerians

Chief Chika Okpala became a household name through his unforgettable portrayal of Chief Zebrudaya Okoroigwe Nwogbo, alias 4.30, in the popular Nigerian television comedy series New Masquerade.

The programme was one of Nigeria's most celebrated television productions, entertaining millions of families across the country during the 1970s, 1980s and beyond.

With his distinctive command of English, humorous expressions, traditional attire and remarkable comic timing, Zebrudaya became one of the most recognisable characters in Nigerian television history.

For many Nigerians who grew up watching New Masquerade, Zebrudaya represents an important part of their childhood memories and the golden era of Nigerian television.

His contributions helped establish comedy as a respected form of entertainment long before the emergence of today's Nollywood industry and modern stand-up comedy scene.

Tony Akposheri questions Anambra State's recognition of its own legend

Reacting to Governor Otti's reported gesture, Akposheri questioned why the Anambra State Government had not adequately celebrated a personality of Zebrudaya's standing.

He argued that individuals who dedicated decades of their lives to entertaining Nigerians deserve recognition and appreciation while they are still alive.

Akposheri stated: "Zebrudaya is from Nnobi, Anambra State, but the Anambra State Government has not recognised or celebrated him for his contributions to the entertainment industry. Instead, it was Alex Otti, the governor of Abia State, who recently celebrated him with a gift of ₦10 million and a car.

"God bless Alex Otti! I wish other governors could replicate what Alex Otti is doing by honouring our legends who put smiles on our faces during our childhood."

His remarks have brought attention to the wider issue of how Nigerian society recognises veteran entertainers who contributed to the growth of the country's creative industry.

Otti's gesture sparks conversation about celebrating living legends

Governor Alex Otti's reported gesture has been described by Akposheri as an example of the importance of appreciating distinguished Nigerians regardless of their states of origin.

The recognition also raises an important question about the responsibility of state governments to preserve their cultural and entertainment heritage.

Across Nigeria, several veteran actors, musicians and comedians have spent decades building careers that influenced younger generations of entertainers. Yet concerns persist about whether these pioneers receive sufficient institutional recognition and support, particularly as they grow older.

For many observers, honouring entertainment veterans should go beyond financial gifts. It could include lifetime achievement awards, cultural preservation projects, healthcare support and programmes documenting their contributions to Nigerian history.

Should state governments do more for veteran entertainers?

Akposheri's comments have reopened discussions about the need for Nigerian governors to celebrate distinguished citizens who have made lasting contributions to entertainment, education, sports and other areas of national development.

While Zebrudaya's reported recognition by the Abia State Governor has attracted praise, Akposheri's criticism of the Anambra State Government remains his personal assessment. The state's full record of honours or recognition for the veteran actor has not been established in this report.

For generations of Nigerians, Chief Chika Okpala remains a treasured figure whose performances brought laughter into countless homes.

The question now is whether other state governors will follow Governor Otti's reported example by celebrating Nigeria's surviving entertainment pioneers while they are still alive to enjoy the recognition.

What is your opinion? Should the Anambra State Government do more to honour Zebrudaya, or should recognition of national legends go beyond their states of origin?

Joke Silva thanks supporters as 40-day mourning for Olu Jacobs ends

31 Oct 2026, 17:44 GMT

Veteran actress Joke Silva has thanked Nigerians for their prayers, visits and support as the family marks the end of the 40-day mourning period for her husband, the late screen legend Olu Jacobs.

Veteran Nollywood actress Joke Silva has issued a heartfelt message of thanks as her family marks the end of the 40-day mourning period for her late husband, Olu Jacobs.

What she wrote

In an Instagram post on Saturday, 31 October 2026, accompanied by a photo of herself and her husband, Joke Silva wrote on behalf of the Jacobs family to thank everyone who had supported them. She thanked those who sent prayers, messages and calls, those who visited in person, and those who gave gifts or contributed towards the funeral. She said the 40 days had come and gone, and that the family was humbled and deeply grateful. She signed the message as Ajoke Silva, on behalf of the Jacobs family.

Who was Olu Jacobs

Olu Jacobs was one of the most respected actors in Nigerian film history, with a career spanning stage, television and cinema in Nigeria and the United Kingdom. He appeared in many Nollywood classics and international productions and was widely regarded as a father figure in the industry. He and Joke Silva, herself an acclaimed actress and co-founder of the Lufodo Academy of Performing Arts, were one of Nollywood's most admired couples.

How fans reacted

Fans and colleagues responded with messages of love and prayers for the family, praising Joke Silva for her strength and dignity during the period of mourning.

Toyin Abraham has shared an old TikTok collaboration from her film 'OverSabi Aunty' and said she looks forward to more ahead of her December release 'Bestie'. Fans linked the post to Funke Akindele's new Facebook partnership for 'The Four', reviving talk of a rivalry.

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Toyin Abraham has once again found herself at the centre of talk about a rivalry with Funke Akindele, after a social media post that many fans read as a response to her colleague.

What she posted

On Friday, 30 October 2026, Toyin Abraham shared on Instagram a throwback clip of a TikTok collaboration she did while promoting her earlier film, 'OverSabi Aunty'. In the caption, she said she was looking forward to more collaborations ahead of her new film, 'Bestie', which is due in cinemas in December 2026.

Why fans linked it to Funke

The post came just days after Funke Akindele announced a partnership with Facebook for her new film, 'The Four'. Many fans interpreted Toyin's post as a subtle reminder that she had worked with a major platform first, although she did not mention Funke, and her intention has not been confirmed.

Background

The two actresses are among the most successful filmmakers in Nollywood, and fans have long compared their box office results. Toyin has also faced accusations of copying Funke's ideas, which she has denied. In the past she has spoken warmly about Funke, congratulating her and calling her the winner of their rivalry after one of Funke's films crossed ₦1 billion at the box office.

How people reacted

Reactions were divided. Critics questioned the timing of the post and accused her of seeking attention, while her supporters said she had every right to talk about her own past work. Funke Akindele has not commented.

AY Makun responds to backlash after missing Cardiff show, promises free return

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Comedian AY Makun has defended himself after failing to appear at his AY Live show in Cardiff, which led to calls for him to be cancelled. He blamed repeated problems on the tour and promised a free return show, while saying ticket holders will still get refunds.

Read more

Comedian and filmmaker Ayo 'AY' Makun has spoken out after facing a wave of criticism for not turning up at his AY Live show in Cardiff, Wales.

What happened

Fans who bought tickets for the Cardiff show on Sunday were left angry when the comedian did not appear. Many criticised him on social media, and some called for him to be cancelled. AY had earlier apologised, saying fans' time, money and trust deserved respect, but the apology did not end the criticism.

His side of the story

In a long statement reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, AY said veterans in the industry had advised him to stay quiet, but he felt his audience deserved an explanation. He said he had spent years performing at events where payment promises were broken, sometimes paying fellow performers from his own pocket. He said a five-hour drive from Sheffield to Cardiff, after repeated bad experiences on the same tour, became his breaking point, but added that the fans who turned up had done nothing wrong. He also complained that negative stories about him get more attention than his achievements, and said calls for cancellation would not make him give up.

What he promised

AY said he would host a free return show in Cardiff, paid for by himself, once a venue and logistics are confirmed. He said this would not replace refunds owed to people who bought tickets for the original event.

Background

AY is one of Nigeria's best-known comedians and has run the AY Live brand for many years, as well as producing hit films. His account of the tour problems has not been confirmed by the organisers.

US court rejects bid to seek evidence from Tinubu in FOIA case, Presidency says

31 Oct 2026, 17:44 GMT

A federal court in Washington has reportedly refused a request by US researcher Aaron Greenspan to seek evidence from President Bola Tinubu and a lobbying firm in his records lawsuit against the FBI and DEA. The Presidency welcomed the ruling, while the underlying case continues.

Read more

A United States federal court has turned down an attempt to obtain evidence from President Bola Tinubu as part of a long-running records lawsuit, according to a statement from the Presidency on Saturday, 31 October 2026.

What the court decided

The case, before the US District Court for the District of Columbia, is a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit filed by American researcher Aaron Greenspan against the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA). On 30 October, the court reportedly denied Greenspan's request for limited discovery from President Tinubu and the lobbying firm DCI Group, which he had linked to alleged cyberattacks on his website. The court found the request was not relevant enough to the main records dispute. It also rejected a request by a third party to file a friend-of-the-court brief, refused a related request for judicial notice, and denied Greenspan's motion for summary judgment, meaning the main case continues.

The Presidency's reaction

The President's adviser on information and strategy, Bayo Onanuga, announced the development. Lawyers quoted in the statement said the ruling kept the case focused on the issues properly before the court and set clear limits on what it would consider.

Background

Greenspan has for several years sought the release of US government records he says relate to investigations involving the President in the 1990s. The President's lawyers have consistently opposed the release of the records, and the Presidency has dismissed the claims linked to them. The case has drawn wide attention in Nigeria, particularly from opposition supporters.

What happens next

The FOIA case itself will continue, and further rulings are expected. Greenspan had not publicly responded to the decision at the time of the report.
