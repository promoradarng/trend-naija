Alex Otti praised for honouring Nollywood legend Zebrudaya with ₦10 million and a car as Anambra government faces criticism

27 Oct 2026, 07:40 GMT

Veteran Nollywood actor and entertainment personality Ambassador Tony Akposheri has praised Abia State Governor Alex Otti for reportedly honouring legendary Nigerian comedian and actor Chief Chika Okpala, popularly known as Zebrudaya, with a gift of ₦10 million and a car.

Akposheri expressed disappointment that Zebrudaya, who hails from Nnobi in Anambra State, has allegedly not received similar recognition from his home state despite his enormous contributions to Nigeria's entertainment industry.

According to the veteran actor, it is remarkable that a governor from another state has chosen to celebrate a man whose talent and creativity helped shape Nigerian television entertainment for generations.

Zebrudaya: a legend who entertained generations of Nigerians

Chief Chika Okpala became a household name through his unforgettable portrayal of Chief Zebrudaya Okoroigwe Nwogbo, alias 4.30, in the popular Nigerian television comedy series New Masquerade.

The programme was one of Nigeria's most celebrated television productions, entertaining millions of families across the country during the 1970s, 1980s and beyond.

With his distinctive command of English, humorous expressions, traditional attire and remarkable comic timing, Zebrudaya became one of the most recognisable characters in Nigerian television history.

For many Nigerians who grew up watching New Masquerade, Zebrudaya represents an important part of their childhood memories and the golden era of Nigerian television.

His contributions helped establish comedy as a respected form of entertainment long before the emergence of today's Nollywood industry and modern stand-up comedy scene.

Tony Akposheri questions Anambra State's recognition of its own legend

Reacting to Governor Otti's reported gesture, Akposheri questioned why the Anambra State Government had not adequately celebrated a personality of Zebrudaya's standing.

He argued that individuals who dedicated decades of their lives to entertaining Nigerians deserve recognition and appreciation while they are still alive.

Akposheri stated: "Zebrudaya is from Nnobi, Anambra State, but the Anambra State Government has not recognised or celebrated him for his contributions to the entertainment industry. Instead, it was Alex Otti, the governor of Abia State, who recently celebrated him with a gift of ₦10 million and a car.

"God bless Alex Otti! I wish other governors could replicate what Alex Otti is doing by honouring our legends who put smiles on our faces during our childhood."

His remarks have brought attention to the wider issue of how Nigerian society recognises veteran entertainers who contributed to the growth of the country's creative industry.

Otti's gesture sparks conversation about celebrating living legends

Governor Alex Otti's reported gesture has been described by Akposheri as an example of the importance of appreciating distinguished Nigerians regardless of their states of origin.

The recognition also raises an important question about the responsibility of state governments to preserve their cultural and entertainment heritage.

Across Nigeria, several veteran actors, musicians and comedians have spent decades building careers that influenced younger generations of entertainers. Yet concerns persist about whether these pioneers receive sufficient institutional recognition and support, particularly as they grow older.

For many observers, honouring entertainment veterans should go beyond financial gifts. It could include lifetime achievement awards, cultural preservation projects, healthcare support and programmes documenting their contributions to Nigerian history.

Should state governments do more for veteran entertainers?

Akposheri's comments have reopened discussions about the need for Nigerian governors to celebrate distinguished citizens who have made lasting contributions to entertainment, education, sports and other areas of national development.

While Zebrudaya's reported recognition by the Abia State Governor has attracted praise, Akposheri's criticism of the Anambra State Government remains his personal assessment. The state's full record of honours or recognition for the veteran actor has not been established in this report.

For generations of Nigerians, Chief Chika Okpala remains a treasured figure whose performances brought laughter into countless homes.

The question now is whether other state governors will follow Governor Otti's reported example by celebrating Nigeria's surviving entertainment pioneers while they are still alive to enjoy the recognition.

What is your opinion? Should the Anambra State Government do more to honour Zebrudaya, or should recognition of national legends go beyond their states of origin?

Actress and Abia governorship candidate Doris Ogala has reportedly been taken to hospital after blacking out and bleeding while in detention, according to a video shared by a person close to her. Her bail ruling was due on Friday.

Nollywood actress Doris Ogala, the African Action Congress (AAC) governorship candidate in Abia State, has reportedly been taken to hospital from detention.

What was said

On Thursday, 5 November 2026, a person close to the actress shared a video online saying he had spoken to her by phone while she was receiving treatment in hospital. According to him, she said she had lost consciousness, was bleeding and could not remember how she had been taken to the hospital. In the same video, Ogala could reportedly be heard speaking about her condition and raising concerns about bleeding linked to an earlier surgical procedure. The authorities have not publicly commented on her health.

Background

Ogala was remanded in custody in Lagos in late October after being arraigned before a Federal High Court on three counts linked to a complaint by the cleric Pastor Chris Okafor. The charges relate to the alleged publication of photographs, alleged false electronic messages and alleged threats. She has not been convicted of anything, and has described the case as an attempt to stop her governorship bid. At a later sitting she apologised to the court for her conduct at an earlier hearing, and the judge fixed Friday, 6 November, for a ruling on her bail application. Earlier this week, a medical report shared online said she had been treated in June for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters have expressed concern and called for her release on health grounds, urging the authorities to make sure she receives proper medical care while the case continues.

What happens next

Attention is now on the court's decision on her bail application.

Kate Henshaw slams power minister Joseph Tegbe over electricity crisis

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Actress Kate Henshaw has criticised Minister of Power Joseph Tegbe in an Instagram video, complaining that power in her area lasts only minutes and mocking a reported remark about Nigerians asking for less electricity.

Read more

Veteran Nollywood actress Kate Henshaw has joined the growing number of Nigerians complaining about poor electricity supply, in a video aimed at the Minister of Power, Joseph Tegbe.

What she said

In the video, posted on Instagram and reported on Friday, 6 November 2026, Henshaw said electricity in her area comes on for only a few minutes before going off again. She was particularly angered by a reported statement from the minister suggesting that some Nigerians had asked for power supply to be reduced because their freezers were freezing food. She accused officials of not telling the truth, demanded accountability for the billions of naira spent on the power sector, and mocked the way households now have to combine generators, inverters and solar panels to get through the day. She also criticised the minister's predecessor over repeated tariff increases. Henshaw said she had deliberately avoided commenting on national issues for most of the year, but had reached her limit.

Background

Consumers in Lagos, Abuja, Ogun, Kwara and Oyo states have reported renewed outages this week. The minister has blamed the drop in supply on gas shortages, maintenance problems and reduced generation from hydropower plants. Comedian The Cute Abiola also posted videos this week appealing to President Bola Tinubu over the outages and economic hardship.

How people reacted

The video drew thousands of comments from Nigerians who said they shared her frustration. The minister had not publicly responded to her comments at the time of writing.
