Paolo Bulgari, jeweller to royals and film stars, dies at 89

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Paolo Bulgari, the longtime chairman of the Bvlgari luxury group, has died in Rome at the age of 89. His family called him the greatest jeweller of our times.

Read more

Italian jeweller Paolo Bulgari, who helped turn his family's Roman workshop into a global luxury brand, has died in Rome at the age of 89.

The announcement

His family announced his death on Saturday, 21 November 2026, describing him as an extraordinary entrepreneur who promoted Italian craftsmanship around the world. The cause of death was not disclosed. The Bvlgari brand also paid tribute to its former chairman.

His life and work

Born in Rome in 1937, Bulgari joined the family firm in 1960 and became known as one of the leading experts on precious gemstones. He became chairman in 1984. The house was founded in 1884 by his grandfather, a Greek silversmith who settled in Rome.

Under Paolo and his brothers, the brand became known for bold designs, colourful gems and yellow gold, opened shops in Paris, New York and Geneva, and expanded into watches, perfumes, leather goods and hotels.

Famous clients

During Rome's glamorous era of the 1950s and 1960s, the brand's flagship store became a favourite of royalty and film stars, including Sophia Loren and Audrey Hepburn.

Troops rescue 18 of 22 children abducted in Kaduna, search on for four

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Soldiers have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kachia/Kajuru, Kaduna State, early on Sunday. The army says the search for the remaining four continues.

Read more

The Nigerian Army says its troops have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kaduna State early on Sunday, 22 November 2026, and are searching for four others.

What happened

According to the army, armed men intercepted 22 members of the Boys' and Girls' Brigade in the Mother-Cat community, in the Kachia/Kajuru area of the state, while they were on an early-morning group road walk, and took them away.

The rescue

The army's acting director of public relations, Colonel Appolonia Anele, said troops of 1 Division operating under Operation FANSAN YAMMA moved immediately after receiving a distress call and pursued the abductors. Troops advanced towards a riverside area, where some of the children were found after the fleeing attackers abandoned them. In all, 18 were rescued.

What happens next

The army said operations were continuing to rescue the remaining four children. The attack comes days after troops foiled a suspected kidnap attempt at a school in another part of Kaduna State.
