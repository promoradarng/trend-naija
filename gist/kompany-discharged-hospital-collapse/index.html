Bayern Munich coach Vincent Kompany discharged from hospital after collapse

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

Bayern Munich head coach Vincent Kompany was discharged from hospital on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, days after he collapsed at the club's training ground. The club said he will now rest at home before returning to work.

What happened

Kompany collapsed on Friday while watching a second-team training session. Bayern later said the cause was meningeal irritation, an inflammation of the membranes around the brain. Earlier that week he had skipped media duties before the Champions League match against Arsenal because of a viral infection, although he was in the dugout for the 2-2 draw.

What the club said

Sporting director Max Eberl described the discharge as the best possible news for everyone at the club. He said Kompany would rest and take medication and expressed confidence that the coach would return at full strength. Assistant coach Aaron Danks confirmed that Kompany would only return after receiving medical clearance and said there was no fixed timescale. In his absence, the coaching staff led the team to a 6-1 win over Magdeburg in the German Cup.

Background

Kompany, a former Manchester City captain and Belgium international, took charge of Bayern in 2024 after spells as manager of Anderlecht and Burnley. He led the club back to the Bundesliga title in his first season and remains popular with players and fans.

How fans reacted

Messages of support poured in from around football after news of the collapse broke, with former teammates and rival clubs wishing him a full recovery. Fans welcomed the update on Wednesday but urged the club not to rush him back.

  • The club says the cause was meningeal irritation.
  • There is no fixed date for his return.
  • Bayern beat Magdeburg 6-1 without him.

Floyd Mayweather trains in Abuja as he launches Nigerian boxing programme

15 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Unbeaten boxing legend Floyd Mayweather has been training in Abuja after arriving to launch the TMT Frontrow Fight Series, a programme to develop young Nigerian boxers, ahead of his exhibition fight in Bangkok in December.

American boxing legend Floyd Mayweather has been spotted training in Abuja during a visit to Nigeria.

Why he is in Nigeria

Mayweather arrived in the capital on Thursday, 13 November 2026, ahead of a press conference the next day to launch the TMT Frontrow Fight Series, a programme designed to give young Nigerian boxers access to professional coaching, international exposure and competitive fights. Organisers say boxing academies will be set up in Lagos, Abuja, Enugu, Calabar and other cities, and the first event in the series is planned for 2027.

Training for Bangkok

Videos of the 49-year-old training in Abuja delighted fans online. He is preparing for an exhibition bout against Australian light-heavyweight Matt Floyd in Bangkok, Thailand, on 19 December 2026.

Background

Mayweather retired from professional boxing in 2017 with an unbeaten record of 50 wins, 27 of them by knockout.

Ronaldinho makes winning comeback at 46 as Ravenna beat Forli

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Brazilian legend Ronaldinho is set to start for Serie C club Ravenna against Forli on Sunday, his first competitive match in more than a decade, the club's manager has confirmed.

Read more

Former Barcelona, PSG and AC Milan star Ronaldinho is set to return to competitive football at the age of 46.

The comeback

Ravenna manager Andrea Mandorlini confirmed on Friday, 13 November 2026, that the Brazilian will start for the Italian Serie C club in a local derby against Forli on Sunday, 15 November, as one of three forwards. Mandorlini described him as one of the greatest players of the modern era, adding that the coaching staff would decide together how long he plays.

A long wait

Ronaldinho joined Ravenna as an investor in August. His last competitive appearance was for Brazilian club Fluminense in September 2015, making Sunday's match his first in more than ten years.

Background

Ronaldinho won the Ballon d'Or in 2005 and the World Cup with Brazil in 2002, and is remembered as one of the most gifted and entertaining players of his generation.

The comeback

Ronaldinho played the first half as Ravenna beat Forli 2-0 on Sunday, 15 November, in front of a packed crowd at the Stadio Bruno Benelli, where the club had installed a temporary 1,800-seat stand for the occasion. He walked out to flares and music, and although he played at a relaxed pace and mostly made simple passes, he was involved in the move that led to one of the goals.

Portugal retires Ronaldo's number 7 shirt design after end of his international career

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Portugal's football federation has permanently changed the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt in honour of Cristiano Ronaldo, whose international career has ended, and players will all wear 7 during their walk-out against Denmark.

Read more

Portugal's football federation has honoured Cristiano Ronaldo by permanently changing the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt.

The tribute

The Portuguese Football Federation announced on X that the style of number 7 worn by Ronaldo will not be used by any other player, and a new version will appear on future shirts. According to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026, every Portugal player will wear a number 7 shirt during the walk-out before their Nations League match against Denmark as a collective farewell.

How it ended

Ronaldo's international career reportedly came to an end after a public disagreement with Jorge Jesus over a substitution during a match in September.

Background

Ronaldo is Portugal's all-time record goalscorer and most-capped player and led the country to the Euro 2016 title. The gesture mirrors the honour Argentina gave Lionel Messi after his international retirement.

Suspected thugs attack Kwankwaso's convoy in Kano, smash about seven vehicles

16 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Suspected thugs reportedly attacked the campaign convoy of NDC vice-presidential candidate Rabiu Kwankwaso and Kano governorship candidate Aminu Abdussalam Gwarzo in Bichi on Sunday evening, injuring team members and damaging about seven vehicles.

Read more

The campaign convoy of Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) vice-presidential candidate and former Kano governor, was reportedly attacked by suspected thugs in Kano State on Sunday evening, 15 November 2026.

What happened

According to Ibrahim Garba Shu'aibu, spokesperson for the NDC's Kano governorship candidate, Aminu Abdussalam Gwarzo, the convoy had passed peacefully through Tofa, Rimin Gado, Kabo, Gwarzo, Shanono and Bagwai local government areas before reaching Bichi. He said the team was warned that its route into Bichi had been blocked by people opposed to the rally, and that stones were thrown when the delegation arrived. The meeting went ahead, but as the convoy left, a group of armed youths near the local government secretariat reportedly attacked it, injuring some members of the team and smashing about seven vehicles. A media aide to Kwankwaso also confirmed the incident on social media.

Response

The police had not publicly commented on the incident at the time of writing, and it is not clear who was behind the attack.

Background

The attack is the latest in a series of incidents of political violence ahead of the 2027 elections. Suspected thugs stormed an NDC town hall in Lagos last week, and supporters of other parties, including the APC, have also been attacked at rallies in several states. President Bola Tinubu has ordered security agencies to deal firmly with thuggery, and the House of Representatives is investigating attacks on political events. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
