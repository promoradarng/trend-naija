Bayern Munich coach Vincent Kompany discharged from hospital after collapse

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

Bayern Munich head coach Vincent Kompany was discharged from hospital on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, days after he collapsed at the club's training ground. The club said he will now rest at home before returning to work.

What happened

Kompany collapsed on Friday while watching a second-team training session. Bayern later said the cause was meningeal irritation, an inflammation of the membranes around the brain. Earlier that week he had skipped media duties before the Champions League match against Arsenal because of a viral infection, although he was in the dugout for the 2-2 draw.

What the club said

Sporting director Max Eberl described the discharge as the best possible news for everyone at the club. He said Kompany would rest and take medication and expressed confidence that the coach would return at full strength. Assistant coach Aaron Danks confirmed that Kompany would only return after receiving medical clearance and said there was no fixed timescale. In his absence, the coaching staff led the team to a 6-1 win over Magdeburg in the German Cup.

Background

Kompany, a former Manchester City captain and Belgium international, took charge of Bayern in 2024 after spells as manager of Anderlecht and Burnley. He led the club back to the Bundesliga title in his first season and remains popular with players and fans.

How fans reacted

Messages of support poured in from around football after news of the collapse broke, with former teammates and rival clubs wishing him a full recovery. Fans welcomed the update on Wednesday but urged the club not to rush him back.

  • There is no fixed date for his return.
  • Bayern beat Magdeburg 6-1 without him.

Osimhen left off CAF Men's Player of the Year shortlist

4 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Victor Osimhen has reportedly been left out of CAF's 10-man shortlist for the 2026 Men's Player of the Year award, despite helping Nigeria win AFCON bronze and Galatasaray win the Turkish league. Reigning winner Achraf Hakimi heads the list.

Read more

Super Eagles striker Victor Osimhen has been left out of the shortlist for the 2026 CAF Men's Player of the Year award, according to nominations released by the Confederation of African Football on Wednesday, 4 November.

The snub

The 27-year-old had what many considered a strong year. He helped Nigeria finish third at the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations in Morocco and was a key figure as Galatasaray won the Turkish Super Lig title in 2026. Osimhen won the award in 2023, becoming the first Nigerian to do so since Nwankwo Kanu in 1999.

Who made the list

The reigning African Player of the Year, Morocco and Paris Saint-Germain defender Achraf Hakimi, heads the 10-man shortlist, which reportedly also includes Senegal's Sadio Mané, Ghana's Antoine Semenyo and Ivory Coast's Amad Diallo.

Background

The omission follows news this week that no Nigerian player made the shortlist for the women's award for the first time since it began in 2001. Nigeria still has nominations in other categories, including the Super Falcons for Women's National Team of the Year. The CAF Awards ceremony will be held on 7 December at the Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Creative Arts in Lagos.

How people reacted

Nigerian fans reacted angrily, with many saying Osimhen's achievements deserved recognition and questioning how the shortlist was chosen. Others said the competition was strong this year.

Thugs attack Peter Obi's convoy in Calabar, one arrested

4 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Suspected thugs attacked NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi's convoy in Calabar on Wednesday, throwing stones and bottles. Police say one suspect has been arrested. Obi blamed APC supporters, a claim the party has not answered.

Read more

The convoy of Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) presidential candidate Peter Obi was attacked by suspected political thugs in Calabar, Cross River State, on Wednesday, 4 November 2026.

What happened

According to reports, the attack happened around the Watt Market area as Obi's convoy moved through the city towards a hotel where the party had scheduled a presidential town hall meeting. Obi was in Calabar with his wife, Margaret, and his running mate, Rabiu Kwankwaso. Attackers threw stones, bottles and other objects at vehicles and supporters who had lined the route. Several supporters were reportedly injured and vehicles were damaged. An aide to Obi, Esther Umoh, said one supporter was hit by a stray bullet.

Police response

The Cross River State Police Command confirmed the incident and said a 27-year-old suspect had been arrested. Police said investigations were continuing to identify those behind the attack.

What Obi said

Speaking after the incident, Obi alleged that APC supporters threw stones at his convoy, forcing people to run for safety. He said such attacks happen because Nigerians tolerate them, and vowed that it would not be allowed in 2027. The APC had not responded to the claim at the time of reporting.

Background

Cross River is governed by the APC. Attacks on campaign events have increased as the January 2027 elections approach. Earlier this week, ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar accused the DSS of plotting to disrupt his Sokoto rally, and APC supporters were attacked ahead of a rally in Katsina. Civil society groups and religious leaders have urged all parties to restrain their supporters.

Tinubu departs Saudi Arabia for Abuja after Umrah

4 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

President Tinubu has left Jeddah for Abuja after completing his Umrah in Makkah, according to his spokesman. He was seen off by the Deputy Governor of the Makkah Region and Nigerian diplomats.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has left Saudi Arabia for Abuja after completing his Umrah, or lesser pilgrimage, in Makkah.

The departure

According to a statement by presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga, the President's aircraft took off from the Royal Terminal of King Abdulaziz International Airport in Jeddah at 5.13pm Saudi time on Wednesday, 4 November 2026. He was seen off by the Deputy Governor of the Makkah Region, Prince Saud bin Mishal bin Abdulaziz Al Saud, Nigeria's ambassador to Saudi Arabia, Yakubu Gambo, and the Consul-General, Muazam Ibrahim Nayaya. Before leaving, the President held a brief meeting with the deputy governor at the Royal Guest Palace in the airport.

Background

Tinubu left Abuja for Makkah on 29 October. During the trip he was granted the rare honour of entering the Kaaba with his delegation, where he said he prayed for peace, unity and prosperity for Nigeria. He also hosted governors and senior officials at a dinner in Makkah. The trip drew comment from opposition figures, with Atiku Abubakar's campaign saying it would not guarantee him a second term and activist Omoyele Sowore claiming it was for medical treatment, which the Presidency denied.

What happens next

The President is expected to resume official duties in Abuja. Among matters awaiting him is his request to the Senate for approval of $7.7 billion in new foreign borrowing.

Seyi Vibez gifts his father a second house in Lagos

4 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Singer Seyi Vibez has celebrated buying his father a second house, congratulating him in an Instagram post with a video of the property. Fans praised him for honouring his parent.

Read more

Street-pop singer Seyi Vibez has given his father a new house, his second, and shared the moment with fans.

What happened

In an Instagram post on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, the singer congratulated his father on the property, writing that he celebrated him and adding a word of thanks to God. He also shared a video of the house.

Background

Seyi Vibez, real name Oluwaseyi Balogun, rose to fame in recent years with a run of popular songs and albums and has become one of the leading voices in Nigeria's street-pop scene. He often speaks about his family and his humble beginnings in Lagos. Gifting parents houses and cars has become a popular way for Nigerian stars to show gratitude.

How fans reacted

Fans flooded the post with congratulations, praising the singer for taking care of his father and praying for more success. Many said it was a good example for young people who make money early.
