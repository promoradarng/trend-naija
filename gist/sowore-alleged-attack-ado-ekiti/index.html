Sowore treated in hospital after attack in Ado-Ekiti; police deny he was kidnapped

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

Omoyele Sowore, the presidential candidate of the African Action Congress (AAC) for 2027, has raised the alarm that he was attacked and abducted by armed thugs in Ado-Ekiti, the capital of Ekiti State.

What he said

In a Facebook post on Friday, 30 October 2026, Sowore wrote that thugs had attacked him and his team inside a restaurant in Ado-Ekiti. A further post said he had been kidnapped by armed men and that his location was unknown. His campaign repeated the alarm on his official X account. It was not immediately clear how the attackers got into the restaurant, how many people were involved, or whether anyone with him was hurt.

Hospital and police account

Later on Friday, Sowore shared photos of himself and an aide receiving treatment, and reports said his brother and some supporters were also hurt. The Ekiti State Police Command denied that he had been kidnapped. Its spokesperson, SP Sunday Abutu, said officers responded to a distress call after an altercation at a restaurant in the Dalimore area, rescued him and took him to the Police Hospital, where he was treated for a minor injury. The Commissioner of Police, Michael Falade, ordered an investigation.

Sowore's accusations

Sowore rejected the police account, saying there was no altercation and that he and his team were violently attacked. He accused the Department of State Services of trailing his team since they arrived in the city and giving their location to the attackers, and claimed the attackers came from Ekiti Government House. Neither the state government nor the DSS had publicly responded at the time of reporting. The attack reportedly happened shortly after he left a radio interview.

Background

Sowore, the founder of the online news platform SaharaReporters, has run for president before and is seeking the office again in 2027. He has been one of the loudest critics of the government in recent weeks, condemning the remand of actress and AAC governorship candidate Doris Ogala and claiming that President Bola Tinubu's trip to Saudi Arabia was for medical care, a claim the Presidency rejected. He has also accused the police of using the Cybercrimes Act to protect powerful people. The alleged attack comes amid rising tension and reports of violence at political events as the January 2027 elections draw closer.

How people reacted

Supporters called on the police and the Department of State Services to find him quickly, while others urged caution until the facts are confirmed.

Report records 124 cases of electoral violence and 22 deaths in four months

26 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

A civic group says it has documented 124 incidents of election-related violence and 22 deaths across Nigeria since July. It warned the trend could worsen as the January 2027 elections approach.

Read more

A non-partisan civic organisation has warned of rising political violence in Nigeria, saying it recorded 124 cases of electoral violence and 22 deaths in the past four months.

The figures

In a statement in Abuja on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the Kimpact Development Initiative (KDI) said the incidents were documented between 1 July and 23 November 2026 through its election violence monitoring project. Incidents rose from three in July to 48 in October, with another 40 recorded in the first half of November alone.

The warning

The group warned that the trend could worsen as the 16 January 2027 elections draw closer unless political parties, security agencies and other stakeholders take urgent action. It pointed to growing political intolerance and inflammatory rhetoric among politicians and their supporters as key triggers.

KDI stressed that the problem cuts across parties and should not be blamed on any single group, and urged candidates to restrain their supporters.

Background

The report comes days after deadly attacks on NDC campaign events in Delta and Edo states, and after INEC warned that the popular 'rig and die' slogan could endanger polling staff and voters. The House of Representatives has also warned that a state of emergency may be needed in Delta if violence continues.

Atiku challenges Tinubu to explain ₦1.44tn drop in oil revenue

25 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly asked President Tinubu to explain a ₦1.44 trillion fall in oil revenue in the first half of 2026, citing Budget Office figures. His campaign says collections were far below target.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly challenged President Bola Tinubu to publicly explain a ₦1.44 trillion drop in Nigeria's gross oil revenue in the first half of 2026.

The figures

In a statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, Atiku's campaign cited figures from the government's own Budget Office. It said the government collected ₦7.88 trillion in oil revenue between January and June 2026, well below its half-year target of ₦18.65 trillion, leaving a shortfall of about ₦10.78 trillion.

The campaign argued that the drop came even as international crude prices rose in the second quarter of the year.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC campaign was included in the reports.

Background

Atiku, who turned 80 on Wednesday, is the ADC's candidate in the January 2027 election. President Tinubu sent him a birthday message despite their rivalry.

The NDC's Kano campaign council has alleged that four masked, armed men broke into the home of social media personality Maryam Golden on Tuesday night. It described the incident as an assassination attempt.

Read more

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) campaign council in Kano State has raised the alarm over what it described as an assassination attempt on Maryam Golden, a prominent social media personality who promotes the Kwankwasiyya movement online.

What the party says

In a statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the council's director-general said four masked men carrying dangerous weapons broke into Ms Golden's home in the Rimin Kebe area of Kano at about 9pm on Tuesday.

Background

The Kwankwasiyya movement is led by former Kano governor Rabiu Kwankwaso, who is now the NDC's vice-presidential candidate. The allegation comes amid growing concern about political violence ahead of the 2027 elections, following deadly attacks on NDC events in Delta and Edo states this week.

The other side

No statement from the police on the incident was included in the reports, and no arrests were reported.
