Sowore treated in hospital after attack in Ado-Ekiti; police deny he was kidnapped

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

Omoyele Sowore, the presidential candidate of the African Action Congress (AAC) for 2027, has raised the alarm that he was attacked and abducted by armed thugs in Ado-Ekiti, the capital of Ekiti State.

What he said

In a Facebook post on Friday, 30 October 2026, Sowore wrote that thugs had attacked him and his team inside a restaurant in Ado-Ekiti. A further post said he had been kidnapped by armed men and that his location was unknown. His campaign repeated the alarm on his official X account. It was not immediately clear how the attackers got into the restaurant, how many people were involved, or whether anyone with him was hurt.

Hospital and police account

Later on Friday, Sowore shared photos of himself and an aide receiving treatment, and reports said his brother and some supporters were also hurt. The Ekiti State Police Command denied that he had been kidnapped. Its spokesperson, SP Sunday Abutu, said officers responded to a distress call after an altercation at a restaurant in the Dalimore area, rescued him and took him to the Police Hospital, where he was treated for a minor injury. The Commissioner of Police, Michael Falade, ordered an investigation.

Sowore's accusations

Sowore rejected the police account, saying there was no altercation and that he and his team were violently attacked. He accused the Department of State Services of trailing his team since they arrived in the city and giving their location to the attackers, and claimed the attackers came from Ekiti Government House. Neither the state government nor the DSS had publicly responded at the time of reporting. The attack reportedly happened shortly after he left a radio interview.

Background

Sowore, the founder of the online news platform SaharaReporters, has run for president before and is seeking the office again in 2027. He has been one of the loudest critics of the government in recent weeks, condemning the remand of actress and AAC governorship candidate Doris Ogala and claiming that President Bola Tinubu's trip to Saudi Arabia was for medical care, a claim the Presidency rejected. He has also accused the police of using the Cybercrimes Act to protect powerful people. The alleged attack comes amid rising tension and reports of violence at political events as the January 2027 elections draw closer.

How people reacted

Supporters called on the police and the Department of State Services to find him quickly, while others urged caution until the facts are confirmed.

  • Police deny he was kidnapped and have ordered an investigation.
  • Sowore accuses the Ekiti government and the DSS; neither has responded.

Kwara APC governorship rally in Offa reportedly turns violent over unpaid mobilisation funds

16 Nov 2026, 11:21 GMT

An APC governorship rally in Offa, Kwara State, reportedly descended into chaos after claims that some participants were not paid the mobilisation allowances they had been promised. The incident has revived debate about paying crowds to attend political events.

Read more

A governorship campaign rally organised by the All Progressives Congress (APC) in Offa, Kwara State, has reportedly descended into chaos following claims that some participants were not paid the mobilisation allowances they had been promised.

What reportedly happened

According to reports, the rally drew a large crowd of supporters to the streets of Offa. But tension rose when some of those who had turned up complained that they had not received the money they were allegedly promised for attending.

The situation reportedly turned violent as angry participants confronted organisers, disrupting the event.

The full details, including whether anyone was injured and how much money was involved, have not been independently confirmed, and the party has not publicly commented on the incident.

The business of crowds

The incident shines a light on a practice many Nigerians already know about: paying people, often called "mobilisation", to attend political rallies.

Across parties, campaign organisers frequently provide transport, food, branded caps and T-shirts, and sometimes cash, to fill venues and create the impression of massive support. When those promises are not kept, frustration can quickly boil over.

It also raises a deeper question. If people have to be paid to attend, how much do the crowds at political rallies really tell us about genuine support?

Kwara suspends children's hospital chief after mothers and sick children were locked out

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Kwara State Government has suspended the chief medical director of the Children's Specialist Hospital in Ilorin, after a viral video showed mothers and sick children allegedly locked out for arriving by 7am. The board says a shortage of doctors caused a backlog.

Read more

The Kwara State Government has reportedly suspended the Chief Medical Director of the Children's Specialist Hospital, Centre Igboro, Ilorin, Dr Mosunmola Folorunsho, after mothers and their sick children were allegedly locked out of the hospital.

What happened

A video that went viral showed mothers with sick children who said they had been shut out for arriving by 7am, reportedly because they had not arrived by 5:30am. Following the outrage, the Executive Secretary of the Kwara State Hospitals Management Board, Dr Abdulraheem Malik, and members of the management team made an emergency visit to the hospital.

The government's action

In a statement on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the board said the CMD had been suspended and given 48 hours to explain her role in the incident. She was directed to hand over to the most senior doctor at the hospital pending further directives.

The board's explanation

The board said its findings showed the hospital had a temporary shortage of doctors, after one of the doctors scheduled for the morning shift was away at a malaria training programme. This created a backlog of patients waiting to be seen.

Ukraine strikes Crimean bridge, part of crossing collapses

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Ukraine has struck the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea, collapsing part of the road-and-rail crossing and halting road traffic. Russia also hit two major bridges in Kyiv.

Read more

Ukraine has struck the bridge linking Russia to the annexed Crimean peninsula, destroying part of the road-and-rail crossing and halting road traffic, according to reports on Tuesday, 17 November 2026.

The strike

Ukraine's SBU security service said one of the bridge's metal arches and the road span beneath it had been completely destroyed. The defence ministry said the central arch and spans had collapsed and the rail section had suffered critical damage. Images released by the SBU showed rubble and a gap several dozen metres wide between sections of the bridge.

Zelensky's message

President Volodymyr Zelensky described the strike as unprecedented and said it was a response to Russian attacks on Ukraine's infrastructure. He said Ukraine had repeatedly offered steps towards de-escalation but had been rebuffed.

Russia's attacks

Russia also attacked two major bridges in Kyiv, disrupting travel and damaging infrastructure in the Ukrainian capital. Road traffic on the Crimean bridge was suspended, and Russian-installed officials in Crimea urged residents not to panic.

TRCN gives all teachers until end of 2027 to get licensed

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria says every teacher must be certified and licensed by December 2027. Licences will have to be renewed every three years.

Read more

The Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria (TRCN) has given teachers across the country until December 2027 to obtain professional certification and practising licences.

What the council said

The council's Registrar, Dr Ronke Soyombo, announced the deadline while speaking to journalists in Abuja on Tuesday, 17 November 2026. She said the council will no longer tolerate people teaching without the required credentials and is strengthening registration, licensing and qualifying examinations.

How it works

Soyombo explained that professional certification is obtained once, while practising licences must be renewed every three years, with evidence that the teacher has taken part in continuous professional development programmes.

Education students are now required to sit the TRCN examination before completing their training, while serving teachers must also meet the requirements.

Help for non-education graduates

The council is introducing a faster route to professional status for teachers who do not hold education degrees, so they can get the training they need before the deadline.
