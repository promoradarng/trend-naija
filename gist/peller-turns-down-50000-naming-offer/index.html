Peller turns down $50,000 offer to let fan name his unborn child

5 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Popular streamer Peller has rejected a large cash offer in exchange for the right to name his unborn child.

What happened

During a livestream, reported on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, cryptocurrency trader Habby Forex offered Peller $50,000, about ₦70 million, to choose the name of the baby Peller is expecting with his wife, fellow content creator Jarvis. Peller initially seemed surprised when the amount was converted into naira and said he would have to discuss it with his wife. But after asking what name Habby Forex had in mind, he turned the offer down.

What he said

Peller reportedly said money is not everything and that he does not play with his family when it comes to money.

Background

Peller, whose real name is Habeeb Adelaja, is one of Nigeria's most-followed TikTok streamers. He and Jarvis held their white wedding in Lekki, Lagos, in August. Earlier this week he apologised after a livestream in which he called himself Jesus Christ, which drew widespread criticism. Large cash gifts and offers on livestreams have become common among Nigerian streamers and their wealthy fans.

How fans reacted

Many fans praised Peller for putting his family first, while others joked that they would have accepted the money. Some questioned whether the offer was genuine.

  • Habby Forex offered about ₦70 million to name the baby.
  • Peller said he would not use his family for money.
  • He and Jarvis married in August.

Aremu Afolayan reportedly clashes with Isbae U on Curiosity Made Me Ask

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actor Aremu Afolayan's appearance on Isbae U's show Curiosity Made Me Ask reportedly ended in a heated exchange. He took offence when the host compared their late fathers.

Read more

Nollywood actor Aremu Afolayan has become a talking point online after his appearance on comedian Isbae U's interview show, Curiosity Made Me Ask, reportedly ended in a heated exchange.

What happened

Isbae U, known for his sarcastic interview style, is said to have questioned whether Aremu was still active in Nollywood and made jokes about his body and how he earns a living given his few recent film roles.

Aremu reportedly explained that he has stayed away from film sets because of insecurity, saying travelling for shoots had become dangerous and he was not willing to risk his life for a role paying ₦2 million or ₦3 million.

Where it went wrong

The mood reportedly changed when Isbae U brought up his own late father, actor Sir K. Kamoru, and compared him with Aremu's late father, the veteran filmmaker Ade Love Afolayan. Aremu is said to have taken offence and warned the host against such comparisons, insisting his father's contribution to the film industry was not up for debate. He also reportedly questioned Isbae U's own achievements as an actor.

Peller vows to unfollow the Grammys after Ice King Ochacho misses out on nomination

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Streamer Peller has reportedly criticised the Recording Academy for leaving young singer Ice King Ochacho out of the 2027 Grammy nominations. He said he would unfollow the Grammys in protest.

Read more

Popular streamer Peller has reportedly hit out at the Recording Academy after young singer Ice King Ochacho was left out of the 2027 Grammy nominations.

What Peller said

In a video that circulated online on Monday, 16 November 2026, Peller is said to have voiced his frustration at the snub and declared that he would unfollow the Grammys' account. He reportedly argued that the Academy should even have created a special category to recognise the young performer.

Who is Ice King Ochacho

Ice King Ochacho, a teenager, is the son of businessman King Ochacho. He released his debut album, The Future, in October 2026, featuring collaborations with established artists, and his single No Competition reportedly passed one million streams on Spotify.

Background

Peller has been one of the young singer's loudest supporters. In September, King Ochacho recalled that the streamer had earlier predicted his son would one day win a Grammy.

The 2027 Grammy nominations, announced this week, include Nigerian stars Tems, Davido and Ayra Starr.

Tinubu announces home loans of up to ₦100m at 9.75% interest over 20 years

17 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

President Tinubu says eligible Nigerians can get home loans of up to ₦100 million at a fixed 9.75% interest rate, repayable over 20 years. Buyers must pay at least 10% of the property's value upfront.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has announced that eligible Nigerians can now access home loans of up to ₦100 million at a fixed interest rate of 9.75%, repayable over 20 years.

How it works

In a post on his X account on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the President reportedly said the loans are available through the ₦1 trillion MOFI Real Estate Investment Fund (MREIF). Applicants must pay a deposit of at least 10% of the property's value to qualify.

Using a ₦10 million home as an example, he said the buyer would pay ₦1 million upfront and then about ₦85,400 a month over the 20 years.

Extra options

He added that workers in the Contributory Pension Scheme can use up to 25% of their retirement savings towards the deposit, while married couples can apply jointly to qualify for bigger loans.

What happens next

Full eligibility rules and the application process were not detailed in the reports.

Defence minister says US is giving Nigeria satellite intelligence to hunt terrorist leaders

17 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Defence Minister Christopher Musa says the United States is providing satellite imagery, intelligence and training to help Nigeria's military track militant leaders before the 2027 elections. He said troops have killed several commanders in recent operations.

Read more

Nigeria's Minister of Defence, Christopher Musa, has reportedly said the United States is providing the Nigerian military with satellite imagery, intelligence and training to help locate militant leaders ahead of the 2027 general elections.

What the minister said

Speaking to journalists on Monday, 16 November 2026, Musa said troops had killed a number of bandit and terrorist commanders in overnight operations across the north-west and north-east. He described the operations as happening almost daily and said they were backed by much better intelligence.

He said the partnership with Washington had improved the military's ability to find and strike targets.

More equipment

The minister also said Nigeria is expecting more helicopters from the United States and Italy to support operations against insurgents in the north-east and armed bandits in the north-west.
