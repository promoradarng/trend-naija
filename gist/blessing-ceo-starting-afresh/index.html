"I have lost everything I worked for, I am starting afresh" – Blessing CEO breaks down in tears as she recounts losing her son and her time in custody

27 Oct 2026, 18:43 GMT

Controversial Nigerian relationship therapist and social media personality Blessing CEO has sparked emotional reactions after breaking down in tears while recounting some of the most painful experiences of her life, including the loss of her son and her time in custody.

In an emotional account, Blessing opened up about the personal struggles she has endured, revealing that her experiences have forced her to reflect deeply on her life, decisions and the direction she wants to take going forward.

According to her, she has lost virtually everything she spent years working to build and is now faced with the difficult task of starting all over again.

One of the most touching moments came when she spoke about losing her son, an experience that appeared to leave her overwhelmed with grief.

She also reflected on her time in detention and how it affected her emotionally and personally.

However, what caught the attention of many was her willingness to examine her past actions and acknowledge that there may be areas where she got things wrong.

Blessing expressed her readiness to identify her mistakes, make necessary corrections and take a different path in life.

For someone who has built a public image around controversial opinions, outspoken statements and heated social media exchanges, her emotional reflections have introduced another side of her personality to the public.

She now appears determined to rebuild her life, even if it means starting from nothing.

Her story raises important questions about personal growth, forgiveness and second chances. Can someone who has made mistakes in the past genuinely change and rebuild their life?

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.

Over to you

Join us in wishing VDM a quick recovery. Have you ever tried to treat malaria yourself before going to hospital?

Social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has been hospitalised in China after being diagnosed with what he described as life-threatening malaria.

What he said

VDM, who is in China on a business trip, shared the health update in a video on Instagram on Thursday, 19 November 2026. He said his condition got worse despite his attempts to treat the illness himself.

According to him, things became so serious that he nearly lost his life on Wednesday night, before he was rushed to hospital the next morning.

First hospital stay

The 32-year-old said he had never spent a night in hospital before, but that his condition would now keep him under medical care for several days.

Background

VDM is one of Nigeria's most influential social media critics. Earlier this week, Oyo corps members credited him after their delayed allowances were paid following his intervention.
