Enioluwa apologises after backlash over comments on young Nigerians and work

17 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa has issued a public apology after remarks he made about young Nigerians at a youth conference sparked a backlash online.

What happened

A clip from the Emergence of Stars Port Harcourt Youth Conference showed Enioluwa making remarks that many viewers felt painted young Nigerians as unwilling to work. The video spread quickly on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, drawing criticism from people who said he ignored unemployment, systemic barriers and the economic struggles young Nigerians face.

Many critics accused him of speaking from a position of privilege. BBNaija star Nelly Mbonu was among those who publicly challenged him, rejecting the idea that young people simply choose not to work hard.

The apology

Later the same day, Enioluwa posted a statement on X addressing the reaction, apologising for the remarks and acknowledging his privilege.

  • The clip came from a youth conference in Port Harcourt.
  • Nelly Mbonu was among his critics.
  • He has acknowledged his privilege and apologised.

VeryDarkMan accuses Davido of hypocrisy over photo with Seyi Tinubu

19 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan has called out Davido over a photo with Seyi Tinubu in which both appeared to raise their hands in a gesture he read as the APC's "4+4" slogan. He said it contradicts the singer's past stance against the APC.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has reportedly called out Afrobeats star Davido after a photo of the singer with Seyi Tinubu, son of President Bola Tinubu, circulated online.

The photo

The picture showed Davido and Seyi Tinubu raising their hands in a gesture VDM interpreted as the APC's "4+4" slogan, which supporters use to call for a second term for President Tinubu in 2027.

What VDM said

VDM said the photo was significant given Davido's status and his previous statements that he would never support the All Progressives Congress (APC). He accused the singer of contradicting himself and questioned why he would pose in a picture that could be read as a political statement.

He also said that his past claims always end up being proved right, dismissing what he called propaganda against him.

Background

Davido had not responded to VDM's comments at the time of the reports.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.

Over to you

Join us in wishing VDM a quick recovery. Have you ever tried to treat malaria yourself before going to hospital?
