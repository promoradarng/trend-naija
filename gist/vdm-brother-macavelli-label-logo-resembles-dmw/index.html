VDM's brother unveils record label with logo likened to Davido's DMW

10 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

VeryDarkMan's elder brother, known as Macavelli, has unveiled a new home for artists signed to his record label, Velli Music Worldwide.

The video

In a video reported on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Macavelli gave a tour of a four-bedroom property he described as the label's new home, with rooms for artists, a kitchen and space for a music studio, and said some work was still being done. He recalled that he had earlier complained about being unable to secure an apartment because of a problem with a landlord, and said the label had eventually got a larger property for the same amount.

The logo

The label's logo caught the attention of social media users because of its resemblance to the branding of Davido Music Worldwide (DMW), Davido's record label.

Background

The unveiling comes during a bitter public feud between VeryDarkMan and Davido, with the activist releasing a series of videos and recordings aimed at the singer and his associates. Some fans accused the label of using Davido's brand for attention.

VeryDarkMan, Opeyemi Famakin and Peller defend Enioluwa after youth comments backlash

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have spoken in defence of influencer Enioluwa, who apologised after his comments about young Nigerians drew criticism.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

His mother speaks

Enioluwa's mother, Bola Adeoluwa, a university lecturer, has also addressed the backlash after some students on TikTok turned their criticism on her, alleging she was a harsh grader. She responded publicly to the claims and the criticism of her son.

Okanlawon weighs in

Actor Deyemi Okanlawon also criticised social media users who attacked Enioluwa in harsh terms. Enioluwa had told a conference that many young people expect success to come easily and asked those in their twenties who say they are tired of life whether they had really experienced life.

Jim Iyke finally shows off his wife after almost five years together

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has shared photos and videos of his wife for the first time, revealing how they met on a flight to Houston almost five years ago.

Read more

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has introduced his wife to the public for the first time, sharing loved-up photos and videos of the couple on Instagram.

How they met

In the post, the actor said they met almost five years ago in an airport lounge before a flight to Houston. He recalled asking if the seat next to her was free, and said she firmly told him it was taken, but he sat down anyway. They ignored each other for an hour, then burst out laughing when they realised they were on the same flight.

His tribute

Jim Iyke described their relationship as something sacred rather than hidden, and said they have built a family together and travelled to eight countries. He called her his best friend, his prayer warrior and his wife, and said they have never stopped laughing together.

Peter Obi says insecurity stops him walking freely in Nigeria, unlike in the UK

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi says security warnings prevent him from taking simple walks in Nigeria, recalling how he walked and took trains freely while living in London.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has spoken about worsening insecurity in Nigeria, saying safety warnings now stop him from taking simple walks or jogging in his own neighbourhood.

What he said

Addressing the Global Peter Obi Supporters Summit in Abuja on Saturday, 21 November 2026, the former Anambra governor recalled living in the UK, on the same street as the Mayor of London. He said they would walk the streets and take the train without security escorts.

He contrasted that with Nigeria today, recalling that he used to walk freely to church in Lagos in the 1980s and 1990s, but said security concerns now prevent him from doing so.

The campaign

Insecurity is one of the major issues in the 2027 campaign, ahead of the 16 January election.

Tom Brady buys minority stake in Chelsea

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

NFL legend Tom Brady has reportedly bought a minority stake in Chelsea Football Club. The size of the stake and price were not disclosed.

Read more

American football great Tom Brady has reportedly bought a minority stake in Premier League club Chelsea.

The deal

The size of the seven-time Super Bowl winner's stake and the amount he paid have not been disclosed. Chelsea is valued at about $4.2 billion by one leading business publication.

Leaving Birmingham

The move comes weeks after Brady sold his 3.3% stake in Birmingham City, who play in England's second tier. The reports cited English Football League rules on holding stakes in more than one club as the reason.

Growing sports portfolio

Since retiring from the NFL, Brady has built up a range of sports investments. He holds a 5% stake in the Las Vegas Raiders and part-owns the Las Vegas Aces basketball team, an E1 boat-racing team and a pickleball team.

Chelsea's season

The news came on the same day Chelsea suffered a heavy 4-1 home defeat to Leeds United.
