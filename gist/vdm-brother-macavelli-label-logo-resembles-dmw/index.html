VDM's brother unveils record label with logo likened to Davido's DMW

10 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

VeryDarkMan's elder brother, known as Macavelli, has unveiled a new home for artists signed to his record label, Velli Music Worldwide.

The video

In a video reported on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Macavelli gave a tour of a four-bedroom property he described as the label's new home, with rooms for artists, a kitchen and space for a music studio, and said some work was still being done. He recalled that he had earlier complained about being unable to secure an apartment because of a problem with a landlord, and said the label had eventually got a larger property for the same amount.

The logo

The label's logo caught the attention of social media users because of its resemblance to the branding of Davido Music Worldwide (DMW), Davido's record label.

Background

The unveiling comes during a bitter public feud between VeryDarkMan and Davido, with the activist releasing a series of videos and recordings aimed at the singer and his associates. Some fans accused the label of using Davido's brand for attention.

Carter Efe and Peller miss out as India's Ashswag wins international streamer award

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Nigerian streamers Carter Efe and Peller lost out on the Best International Streamer prize at the 2026 Streamer Awards, which went to Indian streamer Ashswag.

Read more

Nigerian content creators Carter Efe and Peller have missed out on an international award at the 2026 Streamer Awards.

The result

The two were nominated for Best International Streamer after the organisers released the shortlist in October. At the ceremony, reported on Friday, 13 November 2026, the prize went to Indian streamer Ashswag, who beat the Nigerian pair and other nominees from around the world.

Why it mattered

The nominations put the Nigerian duo among creators from around the world competing for the category.

Peller and Phyna react

In a post on X on Thursday night, Peller thanked his fans, saying their loyalty meant more to him than any trophy and that the nomination itself was a blessing. He complained, however, that the result of his category was announced on the red carpet before the main ceremony began, which he called unfair. Reality star Phyna went further in a livestream, accusing the organisers of using the Nigerian nominees to attract Nigerian viewers and engagement with no real intention of giving them the award.

Background

Peller rose to prominence as one of Nigeria's biggest TikTok live streamers, while Carter Efe, a skit maker and musician, has also built a large streaming following.

Social media personality and relationship coach Blessing CEO has admitted that she did not have breast cancer, months after telling the public she was battling the disease.

What she said

In an interview with activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), reported on Thursday, 12 November 2026, she said a medical check-up had found a lump in her breast, but that it was not cancerous. She admitted that she used the discovery to tell people it was cancer because she wanted financial support. She said she first gave the impression that she was thinking of selling her car and house, before fans encouraged her to share her account details so they could donate.

How the donations ended

She said money started coming in from members of the public until VeryDarkMan questioned the fundraising and demanded proof of her diagnosis, after which the donations stopped.

VeryDarkMan criticised

VeryDarkMan later asked Nigerians to forgive her, drawing criticism from some social media users. Commentator Japhman, in a video on 12 November, accused him of a double standard, saying the activist's 'Ratel' movement is built on demanding accountability from others.

Copyright Commission takes legal action against NetNaija, MovieBox over piracy

14 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Nigerian Copyright Commission says it has begun legal action against operators of platforms including NetNaija and MovieBox over alleged copyright violations, warning that sites distributing protected content without permission could be shut down.

Read more

The Nigerian Copyright Commission (NCC) says it has started legal action against the operators of several online platforms, including NetNaija and MovieBox, over alleged copyright infringement.

What the commission said

Speaking on Thursday, 12 November 2026, at a copyright sensitisation programme at the University of Ibadan, Barrister Kunle Olatunji, deputy director of the commission's Regulatory and Compliance Department, said the action was part of efforts to protect authors, publishers, filmmakers and other creators whose work is used without permission. He said the Copyright Act 2022 had widened the commission's powers to tackle online piracy, allowing it to seek the closure of entire websites rather than just the removal of individual pirated items.

Why it matters

Olatunji said the law protects the rights of creative professionals whose works are exploited without their consent.

Background

The allegations against the platforms have not been tested in court, and their operators had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

Nollywood actress Nkechi Blessing has shared a frightening experience she had while in the United Kingdom.

What happened

In a video posted on Instagram on Friday, 13 November 2026, the actress said she had slipped in her bathtub earlier that day while getting ready for a photoshoot. Her message

In the caption, she thanked God for protecting her and asked her followers to help her give thanks.

How people reacted

Colleagues and fans flooded her comments with messages of relief and prayers. The actress has been in the news this week after publicly addressing speculation about her love life.
