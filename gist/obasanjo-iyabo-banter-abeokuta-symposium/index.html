Obasanjo and daughter Iyabo trade jokes on stage at Abeokuta symposium

31 Oct 2026, 20:42 GMT

Former President Olusegun Obasanjo and his daughter, former senator Iyabo Obasanjo, provided a light-hearted moment at a public event in Abeokuta, Ogun State, with a playful exchange that quickly spread online.

What happened

At the Ajibosin Platform symposium, held at the Olusegun Obasanjo Presidential Library on Friday, 30 October 2026, Iyabo reportedly greeted the dignitaries in her opening address and acknowledged her father last, joking that he was someone she did not know. When the former President took the microphone, he responded that the abuse of artificial intelligence had already started, since his own daughter now claimed not to know her father. The audience laughed at both remarks.

About the event

The symposium, part of the Odun Omo Olowu festival, focused on using artificial intelligence to solve grassroots problems and speed up national development. It was at the same event that Obasanjo criticised President Bola Tinubu's attitude to borrowing, saying debt destroys generations, and warned against relying too heavily on advice from foreign-trained economists.

Background

Iyabo Obasanjo is a public health specialist who served as Ogun State Commissioner for Health and later as a senator representing Ogun Central. She is currently the PDP candidate for Ogun Central in the 2027 elections. Father and daughter have had a well-publicised strained relationship in the past, which made their friendly exchange all the more talked about.

How people reacted

Many social media users enjoyed the moment, saying it showed a warmer side of the former President, while others joked about the role of AI in family relationships.

  • Iyabo joked she did not know her father.
  • Obasanjo replied that it was 'abuse of AI'.
  • The symposium focused on AI for development.

Tunde Bakare says he turned down Tinubu's offer of a ministerial post

9 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Pastor Tunde Bakare says President Tinubu offered him a cabinet position, which he declined because he felt too old for the job, preferring to advise the government from the background. He says he is not part of the opposition.

Read more

Pastor Tunde Bakare, leader of the Citadel Global Community Church, says President Bola Tinubu offered him a ministerial position, which he turned down.

What he said

Speaking at an event in Leicester, United Kingdom, on Sunday, 8 November 2026, Bakare said he told the President he was too old for the demands of the office and would rather work quietly in the background. He said he offered instead to give advice and raise concerns wherever he believed government policy had fallen short. He insisted that not being in the cabinet did not make him part of the opposition, describing his approach as a 'propositional platform' focused on identifying problems and proposing solutions.

Background

Bakare was Muhammadu Buhari's running mate in the 2011 presidential election and sought the APC presidential ticket in 2022, which Tinubu won. He has been an outspoken commentator on national affairs for decades and has had a mixed relationship with successive governments.

"Pharaoh, let my people go!": Cubana Chief Priest replies Umahi, accuses him of giving Tinubu fake promises on South-East votes

9 Nov 2026, 11:14 GMT

Cubana Chief Priest has fired back at Works Minister David Umahi, nicknaming him "Pharaoh" and accusing him of making empty promises to President Tinubu about South-East votes. He also claimed Umahi is preparing his son to become Ebonyi governor, and mocked him with his own "money na water" catchphrase.

Read more

Socialite and businessman Cubana Chief Priest has replied Minister of Works David Umahi, a day after the minister mocked his famous "money na water" catchphrase and asked how many people he has given scholarships.

"Pharaoh, let my people go"

Borrowing from the Bible story of Moses and Pharaoh, Cubana Chief Priest gave the minister a new nickname, and accused him of making promises to President Bola Tinubu that he cannot deliver.

"Pharaoh, let my people go. Stop giving Tinubu fake promises on votes. What project are you using to convince South-East people to vote for him?" he said.

The governorship claim

He went further, claiming that Umahi is preparing his son to become governor of Ebonyi State after the current governor, Francis Nwifuru.

Speaking partly in Pidgin, he said, in effect: "You're busy preparing your son to be governor after Nwifuru. God won't let that happen. You want to use Ebonyi State to settle your child. Let the young man come and meet us on the street so we can teach him how to make 'money na water'."

This is Cubana Chief Priest's claim, and Umahi has not yet responded to this latest attack.

How it started

The exchange began weeks ago when Cubana Chief Priest described Umahi as the second-worst minister from the South-East. Umahi hit back by telling him he was "becoming very noisy", comparing him unfavourably with Dangote and Otedola, and asking, "How many people have you given scholarships?"

Now the socialite has turned the argument back to politics, questioning what Umahi has done to win South-East votes for the President.

Nigeria seeks $500m World Bank loan for infrastructure financing platform

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

The federal government has approached the World Bank for a $500 million loan to set up a platform to attract private investment into infrastructure, part of a wider $3.85 billion programme called INFRAGROW.

Read more

The federal government has asked the World Bank for a $500 million loan to set up a platform to attract private investment into Nigerian infrastructure.

The loan

According to a World Bank project document updated on 4 November 2026 and reported on Monday, 9 November, the facility is part of a broader $3.85 billion programme called the Nigeria Infrastructure Financing and De-Risking Platform for Growth and Jobs, or INFRAGROW. The Federal Ministry of Finance is listed as the borrower and the Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority (NSIA) as the implementing agency. The aim is to reduce the risks that discourage private investors from funding infrastructure projects.

Why the bank says it is needed

The World Bank estimates that Nigeria has an infrastructure financing gap of about $100 billion a year, which holds back growth, competitiveness and job creation. It says the problem is not only a lack of money but also weak project preparation and difficulty turning proposals into commercially viable investments.

Background

The request comes as President Bola Tinubu also seeks Senate approval for about $7.7 billion in new external borrowing, and as government borrowing becomes a major campaign issue. NDC candidate Peter Obi has challenged the President to account for loans, while the Presidency says claims that it has borrowed ₦120 trillion are distorted. The World Bank reported last week that Nigeria's total external debt rose to about $145 billion in 2025.

Seyi Tinubu tells Isaac Fayose to 'behave' after video criticising youths

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

President Tinubu's son Seyi has hit back at businessman Isaac Fayose, who posted a video calling Nigerian youths lazy and criticising the heavy security at a City Boy Movement walk in Port Harcourt that Seyi led.

Read more

Seyi Tinubu, son of President Bola Tinubu, has responded to businessman Isaac Fayose after a video in which Fayose criticised young Nigerians and a pro-Tinubu walk.

What Fayose said

In a video posted on Instagram on Saturday, 7 November 2026, Fayose used footage from a City Boy Movement walk in Port Harcourt, led by Seyi Tinubu, to question why the President's son was accompanied by a large security team of police, DSS and civil defence officers. He described Nigerian youths who turned out to support the President's re-election as lazy and distracted, pointing to insecurity in Benue, Plateau and Kogi states, deportations from the United States and hostility towards Nigerians in other African countries.

Seyi Tinubu's response

Seyi Tinubu hit back, telling Fayose, whom he described as a content creator, to behave himself, according to reports on Monday, 9 November.

Background

The City Boy Movement is a youth group backing Tinubu's re-election, and Seyi Tinubu has become increasingly visible in the campaign, leading walks and meeting youth groups. Isaac Fayose, a businessman and brother of former Ekiti governor Ayodele Fayose, is known for outspoken videos on social media and recently claimed that AI-generated voters could affect the 2027 elections.
