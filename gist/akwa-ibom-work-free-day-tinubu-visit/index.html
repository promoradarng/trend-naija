Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

Family affair

Alex Otti joins South-East governors to receive Tinubu at Enugu APC rally

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Abia governor Alex Otti has reportedly arrived in Enugu to join other South-East governors in receiving President Tinubu for the APC's South-East campaign rally. His appearance comes days after Tinubu said Otti was with him.

Read more

Abia State Governor Alex Otti reportedly arrived in Enugu on Saturday, 28 November 2026, to join other South-East governors and political leaders in receiving President Bola Tinubu ahead of the APC's South-East presidential campaign rally.

The arrival

Otti, who came with his wife, was reportedly received at the Akanu Ibiam International Airport by Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. He was also said to be listed among the leaders present at the rally at Michael Okpara Square in Enugu.

'He's with me'

His appearance came days after President Tinubu, speaking at a meeting with Igbo leaders in Lagos, claimed that Otti was among the South-East governors backing his re-election, recalling that the two had worked together before Otti became governor.

Mixed reactions

Otti's presence has reportedly sparked debate on social media, as he was elected governor on the Labour Party platform. Some users questioned why he attended an APC campaign event, while others said what mattered most was his performance in Abia State.

Background

The rally is part of the APC's zonal campaign tour ahead of the 16 January 2027 presidential election, as the NDC, ADC and other opposition parties also step up their campaigns.

Sola Sobowale and Nollywood stars visit Ngozi Nwosu, appeal for help before surgery

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Veteran actresses Sola Sobowale, Ebele Okaro, Chinyere Wilfred and Gloria Young have visited Ngozi Nwosu as she prepares for another surgery. They urged Nigerians to support her financially.

Read more

Veteran Nollywood actress Ngozi Nwosu has received a visit from some of her well-known colleagues as she prepares for another surgery.

The visit

A video circulating on social media shows actresses Sola Sobowale, Ebele Okaro, Chinyere Wilfred and Gloria Young spending time with Nwosu and encouraging her. During the visit, the group appealed to members of the public to help her financially ahead of her next medical procedure.

Earlier appeal

The visit comes months after Nwosu made an emotional appeal for help in July 2026. At the time, she said she had spent three weeks in hospital and urgently needed money to cover the cost of three surgeries.

How fans reacted

Many fans responded with prayers for her recovery. Several said Nollywood had lost too many people recently and urged Nigerians to step in so the industry would not mourn another veteran, coming in the same week as the death of Pete Edochie.
