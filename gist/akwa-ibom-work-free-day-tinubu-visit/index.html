Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

Obi and NDC supporters recite old national anthem instead of new one at Lagos town hall

13 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

A video from the NDC town hall in Lagos shows Peter Obi and Lagos governorship candidate Funso Doherty leading supporters in reciting the old national anthem, 'Arise O Compatriots', instead of the new one adopted in 2024.

Read more

Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has drawn attention after reportedly leading supporters in reciting Nigeria's former national anthem rather than the current one.

What happened

A video from the party's town hall meeting in Oregun, Lagos, on Thursday, 12 November 2026, which went viral afterwards, shows Obi and the NDC's Lagos governorship candidate, Funso Doherty, choosing not to recite the current anthem when attendees were asked to stand for it. Instead, they and the crowd recited the old anthem, 'Arise O Compatriots', together.

The anthem change

Nigeria replaced 'Arise O Compatriots', which had been in use since 1978, with its original post-independence anthem, 'Nigeria We Hail Thee', in May 2024. President Bola Tinubu signed the change into law after the bill passed quickly through the National Assembly. The speed of the change drew criticism at the time.

Response

Neither Obi's campaign nor the APC had commented on the video at the time of writing.

Background

The same town hall meeting was disrupted earlier in the day by suspected thugs, before it went ahead. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Petrol depot prices fall to about ₦1,210 per litre in Lagos, diesel also drops

13 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Average depot prices of petrol in Lagos have reportedly fallen by ₦29 to about ₦1,210 per litre, with diesel also cheaper, raising hopes of further cuts at filling stations.

Read more

Depot prices of petrol and diesel have fallen further in Lagos, according to market data.

The new prices

Figures published on Thursday, 12 November 2026, show that the average depot price of petrol dropped by ₦29 per litre, from ₦1,239 to ₦1,210, a decline of about 2.3 per cent. Some depots were reportedly selling at around ₦1,200. The average price of diesel also fell, to about ₦1,688 per litre.

Why it matters

Depot prices are what marketers pay before selling to motorists, so lower wholesale prices could lead to cheaper petrol at filling stations in the coming days. The cuts came despite a rebound in global crude oil prices linked to renewed tension between the United States and Iran.

Background

Petrol prices have been easing in recent days. Dangote Refinery reportedly cut its loading price to ₦1,250 per litre after switching to a pricing system linked to global petrol prices, and MRS filling stations in Abuja reduced their pump price to ₦1,399 per litre.
