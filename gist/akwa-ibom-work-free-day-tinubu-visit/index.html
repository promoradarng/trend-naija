Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

APGA formally adopts Tinubu as its 2027 presidential candidate

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The All Progressives Grand Alliance has formally adopted President Tinubu as its candidate for 2027. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo handed the party flag to Vice President Shettima.

The All Progressives Grand Alliance (APGA) has formally adopted President Bola Tinubu as its presidential candidate for the 2027 general elections.

The ceremony

Anambra State Governor and APGA leader Charles Soludo presided over a ceremony on Thursday, 19 November 2026, at which he presented the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima, who received it on the President's behalf.

What it means

Tinubu is already the presidential candidate of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC). APGA's adoption means the party will back him rather than field its own candidate, marking a significant alliance between the party and the federal government.

Background

APGA controls Anambra State, the home state of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi.

The other side

No reaction from the NDC or the ADC to the endorsement was included in the reports.

Anambra government says Peter Obi billboard was removed over unpaid signage fee

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Anambra State Government has confirmed it ordered the removal of a billboard featuring NDC candidate Peter Obi and Senator Victor Umeh. It said Umeh paid the fee but Obi did not.

Read more

The Anambra State Government has confirmed that it ordered the removal of a billboard showing Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

The government's explanation

The state government, led by Governor Charles Soludo, said the billboard was taken down because the required signage fee had not been paid for Obi's image.

The billboard belonged to Victor Umeh, the senator representing Anambra Central and the NDC candidate for the district, and featured photographs of both Umeh and Obi. According to the government, Umeh had paid the fee for his own image, but no fee had been paid for Obi's.

Context

The government's explanation came on the same day Governor Soludo's party, APGA, formally adopted President Bola Tinubu as its candidate for 2027. Obi is a former governor of Anambra State.

The other side

Reactions from Obi or the NDC to the government's explanation were not included in the reports.

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has asked the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) to make public all details of the artificial intelligence system it plans to use in the 2027 general elections.

The letter

According to his campaign's director of strategic communication, Phrank Shaibu, Atiku wrote directly to the INEC Chairman on behalf of the ADC and allied stakeholders. The statement was issued on Thursday, 19 November 2026.

He warned that Nigerians cannot be asked to trust a process they are not allowed to examine, and called for transparency about the technology, including its source code.

Background

The request follows INEC's announced plan to integrate AI and other intelligent systems into its election processes. AI has also become a flashpoint in the campaign, with the main parties accusing each other of using AI-generated images.

INEC's position

No response from INEC to the letter was included in the reports.

"Grow up, the office is too big for jokes": Sule Lamido tells Shettima after VP claims he backs Tinubu

19 Nov 2026, 17:21 GMT

Former Jigawa Governor Sule Lamido has told Vice-President Kashim Shettima to be more careful with his public comments, after Shettima claimed Lamido supports President Tinubu and the APC. Lamido firmly distanced himself from the ruling party.

Read more

Former Jigawa State Governor Sule Lamido has told Vice-President Kashim Shettima to be more careful with his public comments, saying the office of the Vice-President is "too big for jokes".

How it started

Lamido was reacting to a claim by Shettima that he now supports President Bola Tinubu and the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Lamido rejected the claim outright and distanced himself from the ruling party.

"Ikon Allah! I didn't join the APC in 2014, only to join now in 2026?" he said, using a Hausa expression of astonishment. "Even though it was a Kanuri man who said it, I'm not surprised. But in your local dialect, the answer is 'Kattuwunum!'"

"Grow up"

Lamido described Shettima as intelligent and eloquent, but said the office of the Vice-President demands seriousness and restraint.

He also criticised some past remarks attributed to Shettima about former Vice-President Yemi Osinbajo, former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar and former Senate President Ahmad Lawan.

Political background

Lamido, a veteran politician and two-term governor of Jigawa State, has long been a member of the opposition. His sharp response makes clear that, whatever claims are made, he is not switching sides ahead of 2027.
