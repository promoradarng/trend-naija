Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

El-Rufai tells court he never tapped Ribadu's phone

25 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Former Kaduna Governor Nasir El-Rufai has reportedly told a Federal High Court in Abuja that he never intercepted the phone calls of National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu. He opened his defence on Wednesday.

Former Kaduna State Governor Nasir El-Rufai has reportedly told a Federal High Court in Abuja that he never intercepted the telephone conversations of National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu, and never asked anyone to do so for him.

In court

El-Rufai opened his defence on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, before Justice Joyce Abdulmalik, after his no-case submission was dismissed in October. Led in evidence by his lawyer, he told the court that he had never owned equipment capable of intercepting calls and had no technical knowledge of how such interception works.

He also denied having any access to the NSA's communication networks or personal devices.

How the case began

The case arises from remarks El-Rufai made in a television interview on 13 February 2026. He denies the charges.

What happens next

The trial continues as El-Rufai presents his defence.

'Political rhetoric'

El-Rufai told the court that his 'tapped' remark was political rhetoric. The court later adjourned the trial to February to allow his defence to continue.

Court freezes $100m linked to Governors' Forum in Paris Club fees dispute

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

An Abuja High Court has ordered the preservation of up to $100 million allegedly held by the Nigeria Governors' Forum, pending a dispute over legal and consultancy fees tied to Paris Club refunds. The order does not decide who owns the money.

Read more

A High Court of the Federal Capital Territory in Abuja has ordered the preservation of up to $100 million allegedly held by the Nigeria Governors' Forum (NGF), in a dispute over unpaid legal and consultancy fees connected to the Paris Club refunds.

The order

Justice O.C. Agbaza granted the interim freezing order on 19 November 2026, following an application by a lawyer against the NGF's incorporated trustees and 29 commercial banks. The order covers a single total sum of $100 million, about ₦135.6 billion, across the affected accounts.

What it means

The order preserves the disputed money until the court hears an application for an injunction or makes a further order. It does not decide who owns the funds.

The claim

The lawyer is seeking payment under an indemnity he says the NGF issued in December 2018, after the federal government paid $100 million to the forum to hold pending a decision on liability for professional fees. No response from the NGF was included in the reports.

NDC says supporters were attacked in Benin as Obi arrived for campaign

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Nigeria Democratic Congress has alleged that gunmen attacked its supporters and campaign vehicles on Airport Road, Benin City, as Peter Obi arrived in Edo State on Wednesday. It said one supporter was critically wounded.

Read more

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has alleged that its supporters were attacked in Benin City, Edo State, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, as its presidential candidate Peter Obi arrived for a town hall meeting and other campaign events.

What the party says

According to the party, attackers who arrived in a pickup van opened fire on its campaign vehicles parked along Airport Road while supporters waited for Obi. A party spokesperson posted videos online showing damaged vehicles and bloodstains, and said one supporter was critically wounded and receiving hospital treatment.

In a statement, the NDC's National Publicity Secretary said the attackers also assaulted people in the crowd and destroyed buses, and described the attack as unprovoked. The party blamed thugs it said were linked to the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

The other side

No response from the APC or the police to the allegation was included in the reports.

Background

The incident comes two days after gunmen attacked an NDC campaign event in Delta State, killing several people, and on the same day the House of Representatives warned that a state of emergency might be needed in Delta if violence continues. The NDC has also boycotted a security meeting called by the police chief.

Tinubu holds rally in Maiduguri, orders fresh push against insurgents

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu visited Maiduguri on Wednesday, where he commissioned projects, held a campaign rally and directed security agencies to step up operations against insurgents. He promised to turn security gains in the North-East into economic recovery.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu visited Maiduguri, the Borno State capital, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, where he held a campaign rally ahead of the January 2027 election.

Projects commissioned

On arrival, the president commissioned two projects: a female lodge for National Youth Service Corps members and the Borno International Conference Centre.

Security push

Addressing the crowd, Tinubu directed security agencies to intensify operations against retreating insurgents and promised support for the families of troops. He pledged that the peace gains recorded in the North-East would be turned into full economic recovery.

Background

Borno has been at the centre of the insurgency in the North-East for more than a decade. Earlier this week, state officials revealed that eight soldiers and 13 construction workers were killed during the rebuilding of the Mayanti community, which Governor Babagana Zulum has now reopened.
