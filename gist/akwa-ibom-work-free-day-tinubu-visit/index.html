Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

Court rejects Atiku's bid to add new evidence to suit against Tinubu's eligibility

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

A Federal High Court in Abuja has dismissed an application by Atiku Abubakar to introduce fresh evidence into his suit challenging President Tinubu's eligibility for 2027. The judge said the constitutional 14-day window had passed.

Read more

A Federal High Court in Abuja has thrown out an application by ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar to bring new evidence into his suit challenging President Bola Tinubu's eligibility to contest the 2027 election.

The ruling

Justice Inyang Ekwo held that Section 285(9) of the Constitution allows only 14 days for evidence to be filed in a pre-election case. He found that Atiku's request to amend his originating summons would effectively bring in fresh evidence after that period had expired, which would breach the Constitution.

What it means

The ruling is a setback for Atiku's legal challenge, although the main suit itself was not decided in the reports. Atiku's camp had not responded publicly to the ruling in the reports.

Background

Atiku is also involved in a separate legal battle over his ADC candidacy, after a court on 5 November voided the process that produced him as the party's candidate. INEC has said he will remain on its list until that dispute is settled.

Opposition coalition backing Atiku, Peter Obi and Makinde's parties moves summit to 30 November

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Coalition for Democracy, which includes opposition parties linked to Atiku Abubakar, Peter Obi and Seyi Makinde, has moved its second summit from 23 to 30 November. It said candidates' campaign schedules made the original date impossible.

Read more

The Coalition for Democracy, an alliance of opposition parties ahead of the 2027 elections, has reportedly postponed its second summit by one week.

The new date

The coalition's Central Leadership Committee announced on Friday, 20 November 2026, that the summit would now hold on Monday, 30 November, instead of Monday, 23 November.

Why it was moved

The committee said the request came from member parties whose presidential candidates and campaign teams are busy with intensive campaigns across the country. It said it was more important for every party, presidential candidate and national chairman to attend than to keep the original date.

Who is involved

The coalition brings together parties linked to ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar, NDC candidate Peter Obi and Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde. Member parties are expected to pause their nationwide campaigns to attend.

Soludo says Tinubu doesn't need to rig 2027; Dele Momodu says Obi will beat him in Anambra

20 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Anambra Governor Charles Soludo told an APC rally in Awka that the 2027 election is already decided and Tinubu does not need to rig it. ADC chieftain Dele Momodu fired back that Peter Obi will win Anambra and embarrass the governor.

Read more

Anambra State Governor Charles Soludo and ADC chieftain Dele Momodu have clashed over the 2027 presidential race in Anambra, a day after Soludo's party, APGA, adopted President Bola Tinubu as its candidate.

Soludo's claim

Speaking at the APC presidential campaign rally at Alex Ekwueme Square in Awka on Thursday, 19 November 2026, Soludo said the election was already won and lost and that President Tinubu does not need to rig it to secure a second term.

He argued that opposition candidates had strong support only in some parts of the country, while the Tinubu-Shettima ticket had a presence in every state.

Momodu's response

Reacting the same evening on a television programme, Dele Momodu dismissed Soludo's prediction and said Peter Obi, the NDC candidate, would defeat Tinubu in Anambra and embarrass the governor. He pointed to Obi's 2023 performance, when he won both Anambra and Lagos States.

Background

Anambra is Obi's home state, where he served as governor. APGA, which controls the state, formally adopted Tinubu as its candidate on Thursday, with Soludo handing the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima.

Tinubu's message

At the same rally, Vice President Kashim Shettima delivered a message on the President's behalf, saying no part of Nigeria can match the enterprise of the South-East and that the region's economic drive is vital to the country's competitiveness.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
