Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

Oyo Assembly suspends four council chairmen, a day after three defected to APC

The Oyo State House of Assembly has suspended four local government chairmen over alleged corruption and mismanagement of council funds.

What happened

The assembly announced the suspensions on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, citing corruption and financial misconduct, but did not immediately release details of the allegations against each chairman. The allegations have not been tested and the chairmen had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

The defections

The timing has drawn attention because three of the four chairmen reportedly defected from the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) to the All Progressives Congress (APC) the day before. The fourth, the chairman of Iwajowa Local Government Area, Muritala Abiodun Oyewumi, was said to have remained in the APM.

Background

Oyo State has 33 local government areas, and lawmakers have recently called for greater accountability in how council funds are managed.

EU raises alarm over election violence and hate speech ahead of 2027 polls

12 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

The European Union's ambassador to Nigeria, Gautier Mignot, has warned that rising election-related violence, hate speech and intimidation threaten democratic progress and could discourage Nigerians from voting in 2027.

Read more

The European Union has expressed concern about rising election-related violence, hate speech and political intimidation in Nigeria ahead of the 2027 general elections.

What the EU said

Speaking at the opening of the 2026 Civil Society and Human Rights Defenders Forum in Abuja, reported on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the EU Ambassador to Nigeria and ECOWAS, Gautier Mignot, warned that such incidents erode democratic gains and discourage citizens from taking full part in elections. He said free civic and political space allows civil society groups and citizens to monitor how elections are run, track campaign promises and hold leaders to account, and helps prevent authorities from manipulating the rules.

Support for institutions

Mignot said open public debate, independent media, peaceful assembly and an active civil society are vital to credible elections. He said the EU would continue to support Nigerian institutions, including the National Human Rights Commission, to help create a free and fair electoral environment, while stressing that it would not interfere in domestic politics.

Background

The warning follows a string of violent incidents linked to campaigns in several states. Peter Obi's convoy was attacked in Calabar, NDC supporters' vehicles were destroyed at a rally in Delta, and APC supporters were attacked ahead of a rally in Katsina. The Inspector-General of Police has also warned officers against being used by politicians. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

IGP Disu warns officers: 'Do not be used' by politicians in 2027 elections

12 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Inspector-General of Police Tunji Disu has warned officers that anyone who colludes with politicians, facilitates thuggery or interferes with voters will be disciplined, and has ordered senior officers to stay in their commands until the elections are over.

Read more

The Inspector-General of Police, Tunji Disu, has warned police officers against allowing politicians to use them to undermine the 2027 elections.

What he said

Speaking on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, at a monthly meeting with senior officers in Abuja, Disu said the police would remain neutral and give equal protection to all candidates, parties, supporters and voters. He said he would not tolerate any officer using police authority to advance the interests of a party, candidate or sponsor, and that officers found colluding with politicians, aiding thuggery or interfering with voters' rights would be disciplined. He told officers that the force is not a tool for any politician and urged them not to allow themselves to be used.

Officers to stay at posts

The IGP directed commissioners of police and other senior officers to remain in their commands throughout the electoral process.

Background

The warning comes amid concern over political violence ahead of the elections and a recent controversy over a video showing police officers escorting businessman Blord, which the police said was unlawful. On the same day, the police said they had raided so-called phone farms used to spread election misinformation. The European Union has also raised concerns about violence and hate speech before the polls.

DSS intercepts weapons smuggled on camels from Niger Republic into Sokoto

12 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

The DSS has reportedly intercepted a consignment of weapons, including 21 AK-47 rifles, four machine guns and 825 magazines, allegedly being smuggled on camels from Niger Republic to bandits in north-west Nigeria.

Read more

The Department of State Services (DSS) has intercepted a large consignment of weapons and ammunition allegedly being smuggled from Niger Republic to armed bandits in north-west Nigeria.

What was seized

According to security sources quoted in reports on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the interception took place on Tuesday, 10 November, at Dawakawa in Goronyo Local Government Area of Sokoto State. Operatives reportedly recovered 21 AK-47 rifles, four PKT machine guns and 825 magazines. The suspected smugglers were said to have been using camels to move the weapons along remote routes to avoid detection.

Why it matters

Security analysts described the seizure as a significant blow to an alleged cross-border arms supply network that is suspected of arming bandit groups in the North-West. The use of camels shows how criminal networks are using unconventional methods to move weapons across the long, porous border with Niger. Some reports described the operation as foiling a major terror plot.

Arrests

It is not clear whether any suspects were arrested.
