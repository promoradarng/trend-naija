Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

"Your approach was inappropriate": South African presidency fires back at Fani-Kayode over livestream complaint

14 Nov 2026, 09:27 GMT

South Africa's presidency has accused Nigeria's High Commissioner, Femi Fani-Kayode, of misrepresenting what happened when his speech was cut off at the ceremony where he presented his credentials to President Cyril Ramaphosa, describing his public complaint as inappropriate.

Read more

The South African presidency has criticised Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, over his public complaint that his speech was cut off during the ceremony where he presented his letters of credence to President Cyril Ramaphosa.

Fani-Kayode's complaint

Fani-Kayode had raised concerns about the incident, suggesting that he was interrupted while addressing the gathering during the official diplomatic ceremony.

South Africa responds

The South African presidency has rejected his account. Responding to the controversy, President Ramaphosa's spokesperson, Vincent Magwenya, accused Fani-Kayode of misrepresenting the situation and of wrongly dragging the South African President into a matter that did not require his involvement.

Magwenya also described the Nigerian diplomat's handling of the issue as inappropriate.

A routine ceremony turned row

The disagreement has drawn attention to what should have been a routine diplomatic ceremony between two African countries.

Diplomatic engagements are usually guided by established protocols, and disagreements over procedure are generally expected to be handled through official channels rather than in public.

For a newly appointed high commissioner, the public exchange has raised questions about diplomatic conduct, and about how misunderstandings between representatives of friendly nations should be resolved.

Atiku says nearly 40% of bread makers have shut down under Tinubu

14 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Campaigning in Bauchi, ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar said the Premium Breadmakers Association of Nigeria has lost close to 40 per cent of its members in two years, blaming soaring energy and financing costs under President Tinubu.

Read more

Former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar has accused the Tinubu administration of pushing Nigeria's baking industry to the brink.

What he said

In a statement released in Bauchi on Saturday, 14 November 2026, by Phrank Shaibu, director of strategic communication for his campaign, Atiku said the Premium Breadmakers Association of Nigeria had lost close to 40 per cent of its members in two years. He was in the state as part of what his campaign calls its 'affordable life' tour.

Rising costs

According to the statement, energy accounts for roughly half of most bakeries' production costs. It said one operator's monthly diesel bill had risen from ₦9 million to ₦19 million, and that switching to alternative power could cost a small bakery ₦40 million to ₦50 million, money most cannot raise. The statement blamed high energy prices and expensive financing for the closures.

Background

Atiku, the presidential candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), has made the cost of living a central theme of his campaign. The government says its reforms, including the removal of fuel subsidy, were necessary to stabilise the economy and free up money for infrastructure. The Presidency had not responded to the statement at the time of writing. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

FG signs Port Harcourt airport concession deal, unions say no jobs will be lost

14 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

The federal government has signed a concession agreement for Port Harcourt International Airport with PH Airport Limited. Aviation unions backed the deal after assurances that no worker would lose their job.

Read more

The federal government has signed a concession agreement for the Port Harcourt International Airport with a private operator.

The deal

The agreement with PH Airport Limited was signed at the office of the Minister of Aviation and Aerospace Development, Festus Keyamo, according to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026. The concessionaire was represented by its group managing director, Eyo Asuquo. The heads of the Nigeria Civil Aviation Authority and the Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria, ministry officials and aviation union leaders attended the signing. The company reportedly plans to recruit more workers.

Unions back the deal

Ben Nnabue, president of the National Union of Air Transport Employees, said organised labour supported the agreement after receiving assurances that no aviation worker would lose their job because of the concession, adding that protecting jobs remained the unions' top priority.

Background

The government has been seeking private investors to run and upgrade major airports.

Hilda Dokubo challenges Umahi to show what he has delivered, sparks debate

14 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Actress Hilda Dokubo has challenged Works Minister Dave Umahi to point to what he has delivered for ordinary Nigerians instead of asking for votes for President Tinubu, but many social media users defended the minister's road projects.

Read more

Nollywood actress Hilda Dokubo has criticised Minister of Works Dave Umahi over his campaigning for President Bola Tinubu.

What she said

In a video posted on Instagram on Saturday, 14 November 2026, the actress is seen watching Umahi speak at an All Progressives Congress (APC) campaign event in Ebonyi State. She then addresses the minister directly, arguing that campaigns should focus on what officials have actually delivered for people rather than repeated appeals for votes.

How people reacted

The video spread widely on X. Many users pushed back, pointing to what they described as a substantial record of road projects under Umahi's ministry.

Background

Dokubo, a veteran actress, has often spoken out on political issues. Umahi, a former Ebonyi governor, is one of the most visible members of the Tinubu government and is campaigning for the President's re-election. He had not responded to the video at the time of writing.
