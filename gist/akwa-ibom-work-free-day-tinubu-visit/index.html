Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

Microsoft outage disrupts Outlook, Teams and Copilot in 74 countries

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A global Microsoft 365 outage that began on Thursday has disrupted Outlook, Teams and Copilot for users in 74 countries and territories, with nearly 140,000 problem reports logged at its peak. Microsoft says it is working to restore services.

Read more

Microsoft is working to restore its services after a global outage disrupted Outlook email, Teams and its Copilot AI assistant for users around the world.

What happened

The outage began on Thursday, 12 November 2026, and affected Microsoft 365 services in 74 countries and territories, according to reports on Friday. Many users were unable to load their inboxes or send and receive emails, while others had trouble with Teams calls and Copilot. Reports of Outlook problems on an outage-tracking website peaked at nearly 140,000 worldwide.

Microsoft's explanation

Microsoft initially blamed the problem on unusually high traffic across parts of its infrastructure, which caused connection errors. The company said it had paused some deployments while it worked on a fix.

Why it matters

Outlook and Teams are widely used by businesses and organisations around the world, including in Nigeria.

Victor Boniface sends ₦1m to cancer patient during TikTok livestream

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Super Eagles striker Victor Boniface has been praised after sending ₦1 million to a woman battling cancer who told him on his TikTok livestream that she needed about ₦4 million for treatment.

Read more

Super Eagles striker Victor Boniface has won praise online after donating ₦1 million to a woman with cancer during a TikTok livestream.

What happened

In a clip that spread widely on Friday, 13 November 2026, the woman joined Boniface's livestream and told him she needed about ₦4 million for her treatment. After listening to her, the Bayer Leverkusen forward asked for her account details and sent the money immediately, leaving her visibly emotional. Many Nigerians commended him for his generosity.

His career

Away from the gesture, Boniface is going through a difficult spell. Injuries have held back his progress at Leverkusen, and a loan spell at Werder Bremen last season did not revive his form. He has played only a few minutes of Bundesliga football this season and is reportedly keen to leave in the January transfer window. Leverkusen, where he is under contract until 2028, are said to be open to a sale or a loan.
