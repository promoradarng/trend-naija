Akwa Ibom declares Friday work-free day for Tinubu's visit

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Akwa Ibom State governor Umo Eno has declared Friday, 6 November 2026, a work-free day in the state ahead of a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

The governor made the announcement after inspecting projects lined up for commissioning during the visit, including the International Terminal Building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo, according to reports. He described the visit as timely and significant for the state's development and urged residents to come out and welcome the President. A rally in support of Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid is also planned.

Background

Eno was elected governor in 2023 on the PDP platform and later joined the APC. He is one of several governors who have defected to the ruling party and endorsed the President's re-election. The visit comes shortly after Tinubu returned from Saudi Arabia, where he performed Umrah. Uyo will also host the Super Eagles' AFCON qualifier against Tanzania on 13 November.

Reactions

Supporters welcomed the visit and the new projects.

Two-state trip

Presidential spokesman Bayo Onanuga said the President will leave Abuja on Friday for a two-day visit to Akwa Ibom and Rivers states. After commissioning projects in Uyo, he will attend an APC rally in Port Harcourt, his third campaign outing since the start of his re-election drive.

Security measures

Akwa Ibom police commissioner Baba Azare banned the use of drones, fireworks and other devices that could cause panic during the visit, and said access would be restricted on major roads including Airport Road, Iron Road and Ring Road III leading to the Godswill Akpabio Stadium. He urged other parties to reschedule events planned for Friday. Projects due to be inaugurated include the new terminal at Victor Attah International Airport, an Aviation Village, the Ring Road III extension and a resort park in Uyo.

The visit

On Friday, the President inaugurated several projects in Uyo, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport. He described the trip as a homecoming and praised Governor Eno, saying the airport's international status was a result of the governor's commitment. Vice-President Kashim Shettima then addressed an APC campaign rally in the city, where he criticised NDC candidate Peter Obi.

What happens next

Security is expected to be tight in Uyo during the visit.

  • Friday is a work-free day in Akwa Ibom.
  • Tinubu will commission projects including a new airport terminal.
  • A pro-Tinubu rally is planned.

WWE star Bronson Reed dies at 38

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

WWE has announced the death of wrestler Bronson Reed, real name Jermaine Haley, at the age of 38 from medical complications. He had been seriously injured at a WWE event in Saudi Arabia three weeks earlier.

Read more

WWE has announced the death of wrestler Bronson Reed, whose real name was Jermaine Haley, at the age of 38.

The announcement

In a statement on its website on Friday, 27 November 2026, WWE said Reed had died earlier that day from medical complications, but did not give further details. The company described him as a larger-than-life figure, a devoted family man and a beloved colleague, and sent its condolences to his pregnant wife, Paige, and their daughter.

Earlier injury

His death came about three weeks after he was seriously injured at WWE's Crown Jewel event in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on 7 November. During a tag team title match, Nigerian-born wrestler Oba Femi threw him off the stage through a stack of tables. Reed reportedly suffered broken ribs and a lacerated kidney and spent more than a week in hospital in Saudi Arabia. WWE has not said whether his death was linked to the injury.

His career

Born in Adelaide, Australia, Reed joined WWE in 2019. He won the NXT North American Championship and the 2024 André the Giant Memorial Battle Royal. Wrestlers and fans around the world have paid tribute.

INEC holds nationwide mock accreditation in 17,656 polling units

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

INEC is conducting a mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise across 17,656 polling units in all 36 states and the FCT on Saturday. It is testing its upgraded BVAS machines and security arrangements.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) is conducting a nationwide mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise on Saturday, 28 November 2026.

The exercise

The test is taking place at 17,656 polling units across the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. It is designed to test INEC's upgraded Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS), the result transmission process and election security arrangements under simulated election-day conditions. Sample ballot papers are being used.

Why it matters

The exercise comes ahead of the presidential and National Assembly elections on 16 January 2027. INEC has faced criticism in past elections over delays in uploading results, and this week the commission named nine states as hotspots for campaign violence.

Lagos adjusts

Lagos State cancelled its monthly environmental sanitation exercise, which had been scheduled for the same morning, so that residents could take part.
