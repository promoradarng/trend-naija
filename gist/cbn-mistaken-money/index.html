CBN warns that keeping money received by mistake could mean 5 to 10 years in prison

24 Oct 2026, 22:32 BST

The Central Bank of Nigeria has warned that anyone who keeps money mistakenly paid into their account could face a prison sentence of five to ten years. The warning comes as more Nigerians use transfers and mobile banking. Customers are advised to report wrongly received funds to their banks.

  • The CBN urged customers to alert their banks promptly.
  • The reminder follows several public cases of wrong transfers.

Nigeria confirms an Ebola case in Abuja as health officials trace 44 contacts

25 Oct 2026, 03:33 GMT

The Nigeria Centre for Disease Control says a woman who arrived from Uganda has tested positive for Ebola in Abuja, the country's first case in about a decade. Health Minister Muhammad Pate says 44 contacts, including health workers and caregivers, are being traced. A toll-free line, 6232, has been opened for suspected cases, while some Nigerians question how long detection took.
