Jerry Eze's protocol officer sparks outrage at Ibiyeomie's church dedication

7 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

A video from the dedication of Pastor David Ibiyeomie's 120,000-seat Hand of God Cathedral in Port Harcourt has sparked debate online, after a member of Pastor Jerry Eze's security team was seen reacting roughly to a worshipper.

What the video shows

The footage, shared on Facebook on Friday, 6 November 2026, shows the founder of Streams of Joy International arriving at the event. A man dropped to his knees as a sign of respect as the cleric walked past, and Pastor Eze briefly acknowledged him. However, one of his protocol officers then reportedly acted towards the man in a way many viewers felt was unnecessarily aggressive and disrespectful.

Background

The dedication of the Hand of God Cathedral, led by Bishop David Oyedepo, drew many of Nigeria's best-known pastors. Another widely shared video from the same event showed Jerry Eze kneeling to greet Oyedepo, Ibiyeomie and other senior ministers. Eze is known for his popular online prayer programme, 'New Season Prophetic Prayers and Declarations', which draws large audiences on YouTube and social media.

How people reacted

Many Nigerians criticised the protocol officer's behaviour, saying it was out of place at a celebratory religious event and that church leaders' security teams should treat members of the public with respect. Pastor Eze had not publicly commented at the time of writing.

  • A man knelt to greet Jerry Eze at the dedication.
  • His protocol officer's reaction drew criticism.
  • Eze has not commented.

A political group has announced a presidential debate in Abuja on 10 January 2027, six days before the election, inviting Tinubu, Atiku, Peter Obi, Seyi Makinde, Donald Duke and Adewole Adebayo.

Six presidential candidates have reportedly been invited to a policy debate in Abuja on 10 January 2027, six days before the presidential election.

The announcement

The convener of the organising group, Lai Omotola, announced the plan at a press conference in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. He said the debate is designed to push the candidates beyond campaign slogans and into detailed discussions of their policies.

Who is invited

The six candidates invited are President Bola Tinubu of the APC, Atiku Abubakar of the ADC, Peter Obi of the NDC, Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde, former Cross River governor Donald Duke and the SDP's Adewole Adebayo.

Will they attend?

It was not clear from the reports whether all the candidates have confirmed they will take part.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

The announcement

Soludo announced the bonus at the 2026 Civil Service Day celebration at the Jerome Udoji State Secretariat in Awka on Friday, 20 November. He also gave on-the-spot orders to improve working conditions at the secretariat.

Workers had raised concerns about how Anambra's pay compares with other states, and the governor reportedly responded to those comparisons during the event.

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has stayed out of one of Nigerian music's biggest rivalries, refusing to choose between Wizkid and Davido.
