Giant Tinubu campaign balloon spotted floating in Lagos lagoon

13 Nov 2026, 15:50 GMT

A large inflatable balloon promoting President Bola Tinubu's 2027 re-election campaign has been spotted floating loose in the Lagos lagoon.

The video

A short clip shared on X on Friday, 13 November 2026, shows the oversized balloon, bearing Tinubu's image, the All Progressives Congress (APC) colours and campaign branding, drifting in the water beside the Third Mainland Bridge. Comedian and content creator Carter Efe, who filmed it from his car, reacted with surprise, and his response quickly became the highlight of the clip for many viewers.

Background

Large inflatable balloons and outdoor displays have been part of the APC's early campaign push in Lagos and other cities ahead of the 2027 elections. It is not clear how the balloon ended up in the lagoon. The APC had not commented at the time of writing.

Supporters at an NDC rally in Onitsha reportedly rejected Flavour's hit Baby Oku when a DJ played it in place of the Obi-Kwankwaso campaign song. A video of the moment has gone viral.

A DJ at a Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) rally in Onitsha, Anambra State, reportedly got an unexpected reaction from the crowd after switching to Flavour's hit song Baby Oku.

What happened

The rally, held on 21 November 2026 in support of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso, was dominated by a Hausa-language Obi-Kwankwaso campaign song, which supporters were dancing and chanting to.

When the DJ suddenly changed to the highlife track by Flavour, whose real name is Chinedu Okoli, the crowd is said to have shut it down immediately and kept up the pressure until the DJ went back to the campaign song.

How people reacted

A video of the moment spread quickly on social media, drawing a wide range of comments as campaigns for the 2027 elections heat up.

VeryDarkMan warns Tinubu over deadly attack on NDC campaign in Delta

24 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has issued a strong warning to President Tinubu after gunmen killed four people, including NDC youth leader Efemena Umukoro, at Omo-Agege's campaign event in Delta. He said political violence could spiral out of control.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has issued a fierce public warning to President Bola Tinubu following the deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North, Delta State.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram, VDM told the president that he would burn Nigeria down, arguing that targeting opposition figures with gunfire is a dangerous political strategy that could spiral far beyond anyone's control. He warned that even people close to the president would not be safe if the cycle of violence continued, and drew attention to the fact that the attack happened in Delta State.

The attack

Gunmen opened fire on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege's campaign team on Monday, 23 November 2026, while he was addressing supporters. Four people were killed, according to his campaign, including Efemena Umukoro, an Urhobo youth leader popularly known as General Akima. Omo-Agege was unhurt.

Other reactions

Other social media commentators also condemned the killings, and Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori called for everyone involved to be arrested regardless of their party. The Inspector-General of Police has ordered an investigation.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to VDM's remarks was included in the reports.

Iran reportedly gives singer Parastoo Ahmadi 74 lashes for performing without hijab

24 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Iranian authorities have reportedly flogged singer Parastoo Ahmadi and four members of her team over an online concert she performed without a headscarf. Rights groups say they also received two-year bans on artistic work.

Read more

Iranian authorities have reportedly administered 74 lashes to the singer Parastoo Ahmadi and four members of her production team over a concert she performed without wearing the mandatory Islamic headscarf.

What happened

According to a human rights lawyer and monitoring groups, the punishment was carried out on Monday, 23 November 2026, at the prosecutor's office in the city of Qom.

The concert

The case stems from a virtual concert Ahmadi recorded in December 2024 at a historic caravanserai in Qom and streamed on YouTube. In the video, which was widely viewed, she performed traditional Persian music with male musicians while wearing a sleeveless dress with her hair uncovered, defying rules that ban women from singing solo in public and appearing without a hijab.

The sentence

A court found Ahmadi and four male musicians and crew members guilty of offending public decency by publishing what it called immoral content online. As well as the lashes, each of the five was reportedly given a two-year ban on artistic activity.

Christmas airfares double as return tickets hit ₦822,000

24 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Domestic airfares have reportedly risen by as much as 100% in three weeks, with some return tickets now above ₦800,000 ahead of Christmas. Travellers heading to the South-East and South-South are hit hardest.

Read more

Nigerians planning to fly home for Christmas are facing steep price rises, with domestic airfares reportedly up by as much as 100% in just three weeks.

The prices

Return tickets on some routes now cost more than ₦800,000, with fares as high as ₦822,000 recorded on some airline booking platforms. Airlines including Air Peace and United Nigeria are among those whose prices have gone up.

Airlines including Air Peace and United Nigeria are among those that have raised fares.

The increases are raising the cost of travel for millions of Nigerians, especially those heading to the South-East and South-South for the festive season.

Why prices are rising

Airlines are said to be struggling to meet the seasonal surge in demand with limited aircraft, while also dealing with higher operating costs. Security worries on major highways are also pushing more travellers towards flying.

Several carriers are reportedly adding extra flights on domestic and international routes between late November and mid-December to cope with the expected rush, but fares have continued to climb.
