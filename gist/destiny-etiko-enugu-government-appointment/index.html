Destiny Etiko reportedly appointed Senior Special Assistant to Enugu Governor Peter Mbah

9 Nov 2026, 06:44 GMT

Popular Nollywood actress Destiny Etiko has reportedly secured a political appointment in Enugu State, with reports saying Governor Peter Mbah has named her Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy.

If confirmed, the appointment would mark a new chapter in the actress's career, taking her beyond the movie industry and into public service.

A familiar face

Destiny Etiko has built a strong reputation in Nollywood over the years, becoming one of the industry's most recognisable faces. With millions of followers across social media, she has also become an influential personality, particularly among young Nigerians.

What the role involves

Her new role is expected to involve supporting youth engagement programmes, encouraging young talent and helping to develop Enugu State's growing creative economy.

There is plenty of work to be done. Enugu has a rich history in entertainment, arts and culture. With the right support, the state could create more opportunities for young filmmakers, musicians, content creators and others trying to earn a living from their creativity.

Celebrity appointment or real impact?

The real question is how much influence and responsibility the actress will have in shaping those opportunities.

Celebrity appointments often attract public attention, but what matters in the long run is the impact they make on ordinary people.

For Destiny Etiko, this could be a chance to use her popularity, industry experience and connections to help young people who are struggling to find their feet in entertainment.

What he shared

Denrele said his father, who was born on 17 November 1941, died on Monday, 12 October 2026, after a brief illness, just weeks before the family had planned to celebrate his birthday in a big way.

Tribute

In a moving tribute, Denrele described his father as a man whose faith was part of his everyday life, not just his Sunday routine. He was a church elder, a warden and a bass singer in the choir of the Methodist church.

With his usual humour, Denrele recalled how his father insisted that he and his siblings join the church choir, even though their singing voices were limited.

What she shared

In a post shared on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Nancy said a painful experience within the church in 2022, which she described as a betrayal by fellow Christians, made her walk away from her faith completely after the death of her mother.

She said Mercy Eke contacted her on Saturday and insisted that she would be attending Harvesters Church with her the next morning, promising she would not regret it. Mercy then checked on her at around 9am on Sunday to make sure she was getting ready.

At the service

Nollywood actor Yul Edochie has reportedly sparked a fresh online debate after criticising men who go down on one knee to propose marriage.

What happened

Yul was reacting to a viral video, said to have been edited, showing a man kneeling to propose to his partner at an event, only for her to turn him down.

In a post on X, he reportedly described kneeling to propose as a practice Africans copied from Western culture, arguing that it reduces the dignity of African men. He used strong language to dismiss men who do it.

How people reacted

His comments drew a wide range of responses. Some users backed his view on cultural identity, while many others pushed back, saying the way a man proposes has nothing to do with the quality of a marriage, and some criticised the actor directly.
