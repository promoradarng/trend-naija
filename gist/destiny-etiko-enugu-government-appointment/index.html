Destiny Etiko reportedly appointed Senior Special Assistant to Enugu Governor Peter Mbah

9 Nov 2026, 06:44 GMT

Popular Nollywood actress Destiny Etiko has reportedly secured a political appointment in Enugu State, with reports saying Governor Peter Mbah has named her Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy.

If confirmed, the appointment would mark a new chapter in the actress's career, taking her beyond the movie industry and into public service.

A familiar face

Destiny Etiko has built a strong reputation in Nollywood over the years, becoming one of the industry's most recognisable faces. With millions of followers across social media, she has also become an influential personality, particularly among young Nigerians.

What the role involves

Her new role is expected to involve supporting youth engagement programmes, encouraging young talent and helping to develop Enugu State's growing creative economy.

There is plenty of work to be done. Enugu has a rich history in entertainment, arts and culture. With the right support, the state could create more opportunities for young filmmakers, musicians, content creators and others trying to earn a living from their creativity.

Celebrity appointment or real impact?

The real question is how much influence and responsibility the actress will have in shaping those opportunities.

Celebrity appointments often attract public attention, but what matters in the long run is the impact they make on ordinary people.

For Destiny Etiko, this could be a chance to use her popularity, industry experience and connections to help young people who are struggling to find their feet in entertainment.

Makinde camp denies claim he will step down for Atiku

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde's campaign has dismissed as fake a claim that he plans to withdraw his presidential bid in favour of Atiku Abubakar on 30 November. The claim was made by a political commentator.

Read more

The campaign of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has dismissed as fake news a claim that he plans to drop his 2027 presidential bid in favour of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar.

The claim

Political commentator Lere Olayinka had written that Makinde, the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) candidate, would step down for Atiku on 30 November, the date of the opposition parties' summit in Abuja.

The denial

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, the verified X account linked to Makinde's campaign rejected the claim and accused the ruling APC of spreading falsehoods. The post drew both support and criticism from social media users.

Background

Makinde said this week that he remains open to working with Atiku and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu, but ruled out a formal merger of parties. Opposition parties are due to hold their second summit in Abuja on Monday. No response from the APC to the accusation was included in the reports.
