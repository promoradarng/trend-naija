Destiny Etiko reportedly appointed Senior Special Assistant to Enugu Governor Peter Mbah

9 Nov 2026, 06:44 GMT

Popular Nollywood actress Destiny Etiko has reportedly secured a political appointment in Enugu State, with reports saying Governor Peter Mbah has named her Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy.

If confirmed, the appointment would mark a new chapter in the actress's career, taking her beyond the movie industry and into public service.

A familiar face

Destiny Etiko has built a strong reputation in Nollywood over the years, becoming one of the industry's most recognisable faces. With millions of followers across social media, she has also become an influential personality, particularly among young Nigerians.

What the role involves

Her new role is expected to involve supporting youth engagement programmes, encouraging young talent and helping to develop Enugu State's growing creative economy.

There is plenty of work to be done. Enugu has a rich history in entertainment, arts and culture. With the right support, the state could create more opportunities for young filmmakers, musicians, content creators and others trying to earn a living from their creativity.

Celebrity appointment or real impact?

The real question is how much influence and responsibility the actress will have in shaping those opportunities.

Celebrity appointments often attract public attention, but what matters in the long run is the impact they make on ordinary people.

For Destiny Etiko, this could be a chance to use her popularity, industry experience and connections to help young people who are struggling to find their feet in entertainment.

Yul Edochie and Judy Austin announce fourth child as May Edochie releases gospel song

Nollywood actor Yul Edochie and his second wife, Judy Austin, have announced that they are expecting their fourth child together.

The announcement

The couple revealed the pregnancy in a new skit shared on Sunday, 15 November 2026, confirming rumours that had been circulating online. They already have three children together, and Judy has children from previous relationships. The news drew a flood of comments online, ranging from humour to disapproval.

May's new song

On the same day, Yul's first wife, May Edochie, released a gospel single titled 'Sorom', recorded with an artist called Nwando, on major streaming platforms. Announcing it on Instagram, she said there was a good reason for releasing it that day and described God as always being with her and never letting her enemies triumph over her. The lyrics draw on biblical imagery about escaping a snare and finding joy.

Background

Yul's public relationship with Judy Austin and his separation from May, with whom he has children, have been among the most talked-about celebrity stories in Nigeria in recent years. The couple's daughter was recently in the news for buying her first car.

Toyin Abraham steps in to stop her police escorts roughing up area boys

15 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

A video shows actress Toyin Abraham intervening on a Lagos street to stop her police escorts from manhandling a group of area boys, calming the situation.

Read more

Nollywood actress Toyin Abraham has drawn attention after a video showed her stepping in to stop her police escorts from roughing up a group of young men on a Lagos street.

What happened

In the footage, which spread on X after the incident on Saturday, 14 November 2026, the actress walks into a crowded scene involving the young men and onlookers and calmly but firmly tells her escorts to back off. The situation quickly calmed down after her intervention.

The context

The encounter reportedly happened during a film production. Groups of area boys, often called agberos, are known to approach film crews shooting in public places in Lagos and demand payment before allowing work to continue, which can lead to tense confrontations.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised the actress for preventing the situation from escalating, while others debated the use of police escorts by celebrities. The police recently opened an investigation after a video showed officers escorting businessman Blord.

Late banker Herbert Wigwe's daughter Tochi weds in star-studded Lagos ceremony

15 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Tochi Wigwe, eldest daughter of the late Access Holdings chief Herbert Wigwe, has married investment professional Wilson Aikhomu in a traditional ceremony in Lagos attended by Osinbajo, Sanwo-Olu, Dangote and the Emir of Kano, with performances by Davido and Flavour.

Read more

Otutochi 'Tochi' Wigwe, the eldest daughter of the late banker Herbert Wigwe, has married her partner, Wilson Omogbale Aikhomu, in a traditional ceremony in Lagos.

The ceremony

The wedding on Saturday, 14 November 2026, drew prominent guests including former Vice-President Yemi Osinbajo, Lagos governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, businessman Aliko Dangote, the Emir of Kano Muhammadu Sanusi II, banker Aigboje Aig-Imoukhuede and Seyi Tinubu. Davido and Flavour performed for guests.

The couple

The groom is a Lagos-based investment professional and managing partner of an investment firm. He is the son of the late Rear Admiral Joseph Aikhomu and a nephew of former Chief of General Staff Admiral Augustus Aikhomu, and was a close friend of Tochi's late brother, Chizi. Tochi, 28, leads her late father's non-profit organisation.

Background

Herbert Wigwe, chief executive of Access Holdings, died in a helicopter crash in California in February 2024 alongside his wife, Chizoba, and son, Chizi.
