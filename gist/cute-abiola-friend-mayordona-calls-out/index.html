Cute Abiola's old friend says the comedian drifted away after fame

6 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

A TikToker who says he was once a close friend of skit maker The Cute Abiola has gone public with complaints that the comedian abandoned him after becoming famous.

What he posted

The man, who goes by the handle Mayordona Nolan, posted a short clip on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, showing himself and Cute Abiola together in a car. He described the two of them and another friend as a close trio who supported each other through hard times, and said that two of them had since found success while he remained in a difficult situation, and the friendship had faded. He posted another throwback video of the three friends the next day.

'Not about money'

After some viewers accused him of feeling entitled, he posted a long clarification insisting that his posts had nothing to do with money or expectations of financial help.

Background

Cute Abiola, a former member of the Nigerian Navy, is one of Nigeria's best-known skit makers. The complaints came in the same week that the comedian posted videos appealing to President Bola Tinubu over power cuts and economic hardship, saying he had sold one of his cars to settle debts.

How people reacted

Reactions were divided. Some said friends who shared hard times should make an effort to stay in touch, while others said success does not create an obligation to keep every old relationship and accused the man of entitlement. Cute Abiola had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

  • Cute Abiola has not responded.

VDM praised as Oyo corps members finally receive delayed allowance

18 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Oyo State Batch 25C corps members have reportedly received their delayed final ₦77,000 allowance, two days after VeryDarkMan raised the alarm. He shared a video of a corps member celebrating the payment.

Read more

Corps members of the Oyo State Batch 25C have reportedly received their long-delayed final allowance, after social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), whose real name is Martins Vincent Otse, publicly called out the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).

What happened

On Wednesday, 18 November 2026, VDM shared a video on Instagram showing a corps member celebrating after receiving a credit alert and thanking him for taking up the matter.

How it started

VDM first raised the issue on 16 November, after receiving messages from more than 100 affected corps members. He said they had completed their service and attended their passing-out parade at the end of October, but had not been paid their final ₦77,000 allowance. He called on the NYSC Director-General to act.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised the activist for taking up the corps members' complaint.

Enioluwa apologises after backlash over comments on young Nigerians and work

17 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa has apologised after a clip from a youth conference, in which he appeared to suggest young people don't like to work, drew wide criticism. BBNaija's Nelly Mbonu was among those who pushed back.

Read more

Influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa has issued a public apology after remarks he made about young Nigerians at a youth conference sparked a backlash online.

What happened

A clip from the Emergence of Stars Port Harcourt Youth Conference showed Enioluwa making remarks that many viewers felt painted young Nigerians as unwilling to work. The video spread quickly on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, drawing criticism from people who said he ignored unemployment, systemic barriers and the economic struggles young Nigerians face.

Many critics accused him of speaking from a position of privilege. BBNaija star Nelly Mbonu was among those who publicly challenged him, rejecting the idea that young people simply choose not to work hard.

The apology

Later the same day, Enioluwa posted a statement on X addressing the reaction, apologising for the remarks and acknowledging his privilege.

Nigerian stock market loses ₦2.27 trillion in one day

18 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Investors lost about ₦2.27 trillion on the Nigerian Exchange on Tuesday as the All-Share Index fell 1.38%. Trading volume and deals also dropped.

Read more

The Nigerian stock market closed sharply lower on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, as sell-offs in major stocks wiped about ₦2.27 trillion off market value in a single trading session.

The numbers

Data from the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) showed the All-Share Index fell by 3,491.13 points, or 1.38%, to close at 249,318.65, down from 252,809.78 in the previous session. Total market capitalisation dropped by the same percentage, from ₦164.15 trillion to ₦161.88 trillion.

Trading activity

Activity also slowed. Investors traded 564.04 million shares, down 16.26% from 673.6 million in the previous session, while the number of deals fell by 18.91% to 30,992.

Bright spots

The losses were partly cushioned by buying interest in consumer goods and insurance stocks, with Cadbury Nigeria among the top gainers.

MTN launches MTN One TV streaming service with Nigerian original series

18 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

MTN has introduced MTN One TV, an ad-supported streaming platform to compete with Netflix and others. Its first original is a Nigerian series, 100 Ways to Stay Single in Lagos.

Read more

Telecommunications giant MTN has launched MTN One TV, an advertising-supported streaming service aimed at African audiences, as it takes on global platforms such as Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video and Apple TV.

The platform

The service is designed to give viewers access to local entertainment and original productions, and is expected to eventually include free-to-air television channels.

The first original

MTN has unveiled 100 Ways to Stay Single in Lagos, a Nigerian-produced series featuring actors from Nigeria, Ghana and South Africa. It is the platform's first original series.

Why it matters

The move signals MTN's ambition to become more than a mobile network provider, as competition for viewers in Africa's growing streaming market increases.
