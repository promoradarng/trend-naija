Cute Abiola's old friend says the comedian drifted away after fame

6 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

A TikToker who says he was once a close friend of skit maker The Cute Abiola has gone public with complaints that the comedian abandoned him after becoming famous.

What he posted

The man, who goes by the handle Mayordona Nolan, posted a short clip on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, showing himself and Cute Abiola together in a car. He described the two of them and another friend as a close trio who supported each other through hard times, and said that two of them had since found success while he remained in a difficult situation, and the friendship had faded. He posted another throwback video of the three friends the next day.

'Not about money'

After some viewers accused him of feeling entitled, he posted a long clarification insisting that his posts had nothing to do with money or expectations of financial help.

Background

Cute Abiola, a former member of the Nigerian Navy, is one of Nigeria's best-known skit makers. The complaints came in the same week that the comedian posted videos appealing to President Bola Tinubu over power cuts and economic hardship, saying he had sold one of his cars to settle debts.

How people reacted

Reactions were divided. Some said friends who shared hard times should make an effort to stay in touch, while others said success does not create an obligation to keep every old relationship and accused the man of entitlement. Cute Abiola had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

Carter Efe and Peller miss out as India's Ashswag wins international streamer award

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Nigerian streamers Carter Efe and Peller lost out on the Best International Streamer prize at the 2026 Streamer Awards, which went to Indian streamer Ashswag.

Read more

Nigerian content creators Carter Efe and Peller have missed out on an international award at the 2026 Streamer Awards.

The result

The two were nominated for Best International Streamer after the organisers released the shortlist in October. At the ceremony, reported on Friday, 13 November 2026, the prize went to Indian streamer Ashswag, who beat the Nigerian pair and other nominees from around the world.

Why it mattered

The nominations put the Nigerian duo among creators from around the world competing for the category.

Peller and Phyna react

In a post on X on Thursday night, Peller thanked his fans, saying their loyalty meant more to him than any trophy and that the nomination itself was a blessing. He complained, however, that the result of his category was announced on the red carpet before the main ceremony began, which he called unfair. Reality star Phyna went further in a livestream, accusing the organisers of using the Nigerian nominees to attract Nigerian viewers and engagement with no real intention of giving them the award.

Background

Peller rose to prominence as one of Nigeria's biggest TikTok live streamers, while Carter Efe, a skit maker and musician, has also built a large streaming following.

Blessing CEO admits she did not have breast cancer in interview with VeryDarkMan

Relationship coach Blessing CEO has admitted in an interview with VeryDarkMan that she did not have breast cancer, months after announcing that she was battling the illness and receiving donations from the public.

Social media personality and relationship coach Blessing CEO has admitted that she did not have breast cancer, months after telling the public she was battling the disease.

What she said

In an interview with activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), reported on Thursday, 12 November 2026, she said a medical check-up had found a lump in her breast, but that it was not cancerous. She admitted that she used the discovery to tell people it was cancer because she wanted financial support. She said she first gave the impression that she was thinking of selling her car and house, before fans encouraged her to share her account details so they could donate.

How the donations ended

She said money started coming in from members of the public until VeryDarkMan questioned the fundraising and demanded proof of her diagnosis, after which the donations stopped.

VeryDarkMan criticised

VeryDarkMan later asked Nigerians to forgive her, drawing criticism from some social media users. Commentator Japhman, in a video on 12 November, accused him of a double standard, saying the activist's 'Ratel' movement is built on demanding accountability from others.

Obi and NDC supporters recite old national anthem instead of new one at Lagos town hall

13 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

A video from the NDC town hall in Lagos shows Peter Obi and Lagos governorship candidate Funso Doherty leading supporters in reciting the old national anthem, 'Arise O Compatriots', instead of the new one adopted in 2024.

Read more

Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has drawn attention after reportedly leading supporters in reciting Nigeria's former national anthem rather than the current one.

What happened

A video from the party's town hall meeting in Oregun, Lagos, on Thursday, 12 November 2026, which went viral afterwards, shows Obi and the NDC's Lagos governorship candidate, Funso Doherty, choosing not to recite the current anthem when attendees were asked to stand for it. Instead, they and the crowd recited the old anthem, 'Arise O Compatriots', together.

The anthem change

Nigeria replaced 'Arise O Compatriots', which had been in use since 1978, with its original post-independence anthem, 'Nigeria We Hail Thee', in May 2024. President Bola Tinubu signed the change into law after the bill passed quickly through the National Assembly. The speed of the change drew criticism at the time.

Response

Neither Obi's campaign nor the APC had commented on the video at the time of writing.

Background

The same town hall meeting was disrupted earlier in the day by suspected thugs, before it went ahead. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Bruno Fernandes reportedly rejects second Man United contract offer

13 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has reportedly turned down a second contract offer, with his current deal running out at the end of the season, adding to the turmoil after Michael Carrick's sacking.

Read more

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has reportedly rejected a second contract offer from the club.

The talks

According to reports on Friday, 13 November 2026, United first offered Fernandes a new deal in the summer, which he turned down, and talks between his representatives and the club have continued since then without agreement. His current contract expires at the end of the season.

His record

The Portugal international had an outstanding 2025/26 season, helping United finish third and return to the Champions League after two years away. He set a new Premier League record of 21 assists in a season, breaking the mark previously shared by Thierry Henry and Kevin De Bruyne, and won both the FWA Footballer of the Year and PFA Player of the Year awards.

Background

The news adds to a difficult period at Old Trafford. The club sacked head coach Michael Carrick this week after one league win in 10 games left them in the relegation zone, and Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly agreed to take over.
