Usher and Chris Brown reportedly argued backstage before delayed Atlanta show

7 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Usher and Chris Brown reportedly argued backstage before their joint concert in Atlanta on Thursday, contributing to a delay of nearly three hours. The dispute was said to be over whether to start while fans were still arriving.

Read more

R&B stars Usher and Chris Brown reportedly had an argument backstage before their concert in Atlanta on Thursday night, 5 November 2026, which contributed to a delay of almost three hours.

What happened

According to reports citing people close to the tour, the disagreement was about whether to start the show while many fans were still stuck in heavy traffic around the venue. Usher reportedly wanted to go ahead, while Brown preferred to wait until more concertgoers had reached their seats. The two are said to have exchanged words, with one source describing them as highly competitive throughout their joint tour and blaming the clash on egos.

Tour to continue

The sources reportedly said the two had not settled their differences, but did not believe the row threatened the rest of the tour. The pair were scheduled to perform in Atlanta again on Friday night. Some fans complained online about the long wait before the show began.

Background

Usher and Chris Brown are two of the biggest names in R&B and have collaborated in the past. The two reportedly had a confrontation at Brown's birthday party in 2023, when Usher was said to have stepped in after Brown began shouting at singer Teyana Taylor. Neither singer had publicly commented on the latest reports at the time of writing.

Why it matters

Both artists have large fan bases in Nigeria, and Chris Brown has strong links with Nigerian stars, having worked with Davido and other Afrobeats acts.

Video of Burna Boy chatting with Timaya while Kokopee stands aside sparks debate

A video of Burna Boy in conversation with veteran singer Timaya has gone viral, with fans focusing on VeryDarkMan's friend Kokopee, who was standing nearby.

What the video shows

The clip, which began circulating on Thursday, 5 November 2026, shows Burna Boy and Timaya running into each other at an event and chatting. Kokopee, widely known as the best friend of activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), is seen standing a short distance away with his arms folded, watching the exchange. Burna Boy appears not to acknowledge him. At one point Kokopee seemed to move towards Burna Boy, but a bouncer reportedly stepped in before he could get close.

Why it got attention

Burna Boy has reportedly supported VDM's charity projects, and the two have been seen as friendly, so many fans asked whether the singer had deliberately ignored Kokopee. The video comes during VDM's public feud with Davido, which has drawn in several other celebrities this week. Kokopee has also been mentioned in the feud.

How people reacted

Opinions were divided. Some fans said Burna Boy had clearly snubbed Kokopee, while others said the moment was being over-analysed and that the singer may simply not have noticed him in a busy event. Neither Burna Boy nor Kokopee had publicly commented at the time of writing.

Obi asks Tinubu to account for subsidy savings and over $100bn in loans

8 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

NDC candidate Peter Obi has challenged President Tinubu to explain what his government has done with the ₦15.8 trillion reportedly saved from fuel subsidy removal and more than $100 billion in borrowing, a day after Tinubu criticised Obi's record in Anambra.

Read more

Peter Obi, presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has called on President Bola Tinubu to account for money saved from the removal of the fuel subsidy and from government borrowing.

What Obi said

In a statement on his X account on Saturday, 7 November 2026, titled 'Good Day, Mr President: Nigerians Deserve Accountability', Obi said the large sums flowing into government coffers, including higher tax revenues, should be producing clear improvements in Nigerians' daily lives. He urged the President to visit the remaining 33 states he has not yet toured, spend time with the people and explain what his administration had done with the ₦15.8 trillion he said had been saved from subsidy removal, the more than $100 billion he said had been borrowed, and increased revenue from taxes.

The background to the exchange

Obi's statement came a day after Tinubu, speaking in Akwa Ibom, criticised the former Anambra governor's record on erosion in the state. At a reception in Uyo the same day, Tinubu said the naira would have become worthless without his reforms and that Nigeria would never go back to the subsidy. Vice-President Kashim Shettima also told an APC rally in Uyo that Obi had run himself out of the race.

The figures

Obi did not give a breakdown of how he arrived at the figures. The World Bank reported this week that Nigeria's total external debt, including private-sector debt, rose to about $145 billion in 2025. The government says its borrowing is needed to fund infrastructure and that its reforms have saved the economy from collapse.

What happens next

The Presidency had not publicly responded to Obi's statement at the time of writing. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Van Vicker says viral photos of him with Regina Daniels are AI-generated

Ghanaian actor Van Vicker has dismissed viral images appearing to show him with Nollywood actress Regina Daniels, saying they were generated using artificial intelligence.

The photos

According to reports on Sunday, 8 November 2026, one of the images showed Van Vicker carrying a much younger Regina Daniels, while another placed him beside her during what looked like her recent birthday photoshoot. The pictures spread widely online and caused a stir among fans of both actors.

What he said

Van Vicker said neither image was real, and that he and Regina Daniels had not been in contact for well over a decade. He spoke out quickly to stop the rumours from spreading further.

Background

Van Vicker is one of the best-known Ghanaian actors in Nollywood and has starred in many Nigerian films since the mid-2000s. Regina Daniels, who began acting as a child, has been in the news recently, including a public clash with journalist Kemi Olunloyo, and new videos from the mansion of her husband, Senator Ned Nwoko, have also drawn attention online. The case is the latest example of AI-generated images of celebrities spreading on social media and being mistaken for real photos.
