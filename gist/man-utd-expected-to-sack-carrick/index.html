Manchester United expected to sack Michael Carrick with club in relegation zone

10 Nov 2026, 09:45 GMT

Manchester United are increasingly expected to sack head coach Michael Carrick after a poor start to the 2026/27 season.

What is reported

According to reports on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox are expected to recommend to minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe and the Glazer family that the 45-year-old be dismissed. No final decision has been made.

Poor results

United had decided against sacking Carrick after a 3-0 home defeat to Bournemouth on 25 October, giving him until the November international break to improve results. But the team is in the relegation zone after 10 Premier League matches. They drew 4-4 with Aston Villa at Old Trafford on Sunday, after losing 2-0 to Chelsea and beating Roma 3-2 in the Champions League.

Background

Carrick is a former United midfielder. The club has a huge following in Nigeria, where Premier League football is followed closely.

Nigeria wins both 100m golds at Youth Olympics as Rosemary Nwankwo and Gift Bright triumph

10 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Seventeen-year-old Rosemary Nwankwo won the girls' 100m at the Youth Olympic Games in Dakar in a wind-assisted 11.16 seconds, and Gift Bright won the boys' race, making Nigeria the first country to win both 100m titles at the same Games.

Read more

Nigeria has made history at the 2026 Youth Olympic Games in Dakar, Senegal, winning gold in both the girls' and boys' 100 metres.

The races

Rosemary Chigozie Nwankwo, a 17-year-old secondary school student, won the girls' 100m final in a wind-assisted 11.16 seconds, according to reports on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. Arwa Ali of the United Arab Emirates took silver in 11.41 seconds and Dana Salem of Qatar won bronze in 11.48. Nwankwo had reached the final by winning her semi-final in 11.53 seconds. Her compatriot Gift Bright then won the boys' 100m in a personal best of 10.28 seconds.

A first

The double makes Nigeria the first country to win both 100m titles at the same edition of the Youth Olympic Games. Nwankwo's win was also Nigeria's first gold medal of the Dakar Games, and she becomes the third Nigerian woman to win the event, after Josephine Omaka in Singapore in 2010 and Rosemary Chukwuma in Buenos Aires in 2018.

Background

The Dakar Games are the first Olympic event ever held in Africa. The sprint golds come days after Nigeria's Flamingos won the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco, adding to a remarkable run for Nigerian youth sport.

Flamingos get $50,000 reward, but no FIFA prize money for World Cup win

8 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigeria's U-17 world champions, the Flamingos, have received a $50,000 cash gift from a sponsor of the National Sports Commission at a reception in Rabat. FIFA does not pay prize money for the tournament because it is classed as a developmental competition.

Read more

Nigeria's Flamingos have received a $50,000 cash reward after winning the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup, but the team will not receive any official prize money from FIFA.

The reward

The gift was presented by Alhaji Saleh Ahmadu, a sponsor of the National Sports Commission (NSC), at a reception held in the team's honour in Rabat, Morocco, on Saturday night, 7 November 2026, according to reports. The event was hosted by NSC chairman Shehu Dikko and director-general Bukola Olopade, and was also attended by the Comptroller-General of the Nigeria Customs Service, Bashir Adewale Adeniyi. The sponsor said the gift recognised the players' outstanding performance, commitment and determination.

No FIFA prize money

FIFA classifies the U-17 Women's World Cup as a developmental tournament and does not attach official prize money to it, unlike the senior World Cups. That means the champions receive the trophy and medals but no payment from world football's governing body.

How the final was won

The Flamingos beat Japan 2-1 in the final on Saturday. Awawu Bashiru opened the scoring when her looping effort slipped through the hands of Japan's goalkeeper, and Oluebube Umejiaku doubled the lead with a lob after a through ball from Esther Stephen. Japan's Fuki Ito pulled one back with ten minutes left, but Nigeria held on to become the first African winners of the tournament.

Otti orders arrest of anyone attacking opposition campaigns in Abia, including his own party members

10 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Abia governor Alex Otti has told supporters that nobody has his authority to attack opposition parties and directed security agencies to arrest anyone who disrupts lawful political activities, including members of his own Labour Party.

Abia State governor Alex Otti has warned against political violence and intimidation ahead of the 2027 elections.

What he said

Speaking to supporters on Monday, 9 November 2026, Otti said no person or group had his mandate to attack opposition parties or stop their members from campaigning. He directed security agencies to arrest and deal decisively with anyone who tries to disrupt lawful political activities in the state, regardless of party, including members of his own Labour Party.

Background

Concerns about political violence have grown as campaigns intensify. In the past week, one person died and five were injured in a clash at an NDC rally in Asaba, Delta State, and supporters of different parties have been attacked in other states. INEC has also condemned inflammatory slogans and warned of plots against election workers. Otti, a former banker, won the Abia governorship on the Labour Party platform in 2023, when Peter Obi was the party's presidential candidate. Obi has since moved to the NDC.
