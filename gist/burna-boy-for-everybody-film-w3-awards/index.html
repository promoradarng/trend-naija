Burna Boy's 'For Everybody' campaign film wins 15 awards

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

A campaign film featuring Burna Boy and several of Africa's best-known footballers has picked up a string of international awards.

What happened

'For Everybody', made by Sporty Group's production arm Sporty Studios together with RadicalMedia, reportedly won 15 awards at the 2026 w3 Awards, according to reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026. It won a gold award in the creator and brand collaboration category, as well as 14 silver awards in categories including music video, cinematography, brand storytelling, branded content, and sports and entertainment.

About the film

The film was directed by Dave Meyers, an American director best known for his music videos with major global stars, and was released in January 2026. It brings together music and sport to celebrate African talent and culture. Alongside Burna Boy, it features Nigerian football stars Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, as well as Spain's Nico Williams, France's Eduardo Camavinga, Ghana legend Michael Essien, Kenya's Victor Wanyama and South African rugby star Cheslin Kolbe.

Background

The w3 Awards recognise creative work in digital media, including websites, videos and online campaigns. Burna Boy, a Grammy winner, has increasingly worked on projects linking music and sport, and recently presented the Socrates Award at this year's Ballon d'Or ceremony. Oshoala is one of Africa's most decorated female footballers, having won the African Women's Footballer of the Year award a record number of times.

How fans reacted

Fans celebrated the recognition online, saying the film showed how African music and sport can work together to reach global audiences.

  • The film won one gold and 14 silver awards.
  • It was directed by Dave Meyers.
  • It also features Nico Williams, Camavinga and Michael Essien.

Gospel singer Frank Edwards has reportedly opened up about a deal he rejected early in his career, which he believes could have made him wealthy enough to own three private jets.

The offer

Speaking during a concert ministration, in a clip that circulated online on 21 November 2026, Edwards is said to have explained that the offer came shortly after he travelled to South Africa to shoot the video for his song Beautiful.

Promoters reportedly offered to take his music to a global audience on one condition: that he remove the name of Jesus from his songs and replace it with God, arguing that this would make the music more widely acceptable.

His message

Edwards told his audience that the temptation came disguised as a genuine business opportunity, and warned that not every compromise looks obviously wrong at first. He said his mother's advice helped him turn the deal down.

APC says letter asking INEC to guarantee Tinubu's 2027 win is fake

23 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The APC has dismissed a letter circulating online that claimed its national chairman asked INEC to ensure President Tinubu is declared winner in 2027. The party called it fake and malicious.

Read more

The All Progressives Congress (APC) has rejected a letter circulating on social media that claimed to be from its National Chairman, Professor Nentawe Yilwatda, asking the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) to guarantee President Bola Tinubu's victory in the 2027 election.

What the letter claimed

The document, dated 22 November 2026 and addressed to INEC Chairman Professor Joash Amupitan, purported to ask the commission to use its administrative and operational machinery to make sure Tinubu was declared winner of the next presidential election.

The party's response

In a statement on Monday, the chairman's special adviser on media described the letter as fake and malicious, and a calculated attempt to damage the reputation of the party's leadership and weaken trust in democratic institutions. The party said the letter did not come from Yilwatda or his office, and pointed to features missing from the document that would appear on genuine correspondence.

Background

The row comes as campaigns for the January 2027 elections intensify.
