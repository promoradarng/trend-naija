Burna Boy's 'For Everybody' campaign film wins 15 awards

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

A campaign film featuring Burna Boy and several of Africa's best-known footballers has picked up a string of international awards.

What happened

'For Everybody', made by Sporty Group's production arm Sporty Studios together with RadicalMedia, reportedly won 15 awards at the 2026 w3 Awards, according to reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026. It won a gold award in the creator and brand collaboration category, as well as 14 silver awards in categories including music video, cinematography, brand storytelling, branded content, and sports and entertainment.

About the film

The film was directed by Dave Meyers, an American director best known for his music videos with major global stars, and was released in January 2026. It brings together music and sport to celebrate African talent and culture. Alongside Burna Boy, it features Nigerian football stars Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, as well as Spain's Nico Williams, France's Eduardo Camavinga, Ghana legend Michael Essien, Kenya's Victor Wanyama and South African rugby star Cheslin Kolbe.

Background

The w3 Awards recognise creative work in digital media, including websites, videos and online campaigns. Burna Boy, a Grammy winner, has increasingly worked on projects linking music and sport, and recently presented the Socrates Award at this year's Ballon d'Or ceremony. Oshoala is one of Africa's most decorated female footballers, having won the African Women's Footballer of the Year award a record number of times.

How fans reacted

Fans celebrated the recognition online, saying the film showed how African music and sport can work together to reach global audiences.

  • The film won one gold and 14 silver awards.
  • It was directed by Dave Meyers.
  • It also features Nico Williams, Camavinga and Michael Essien.

Paul Okoye celebrates birthday without twin Peter for the first time

P-Square's Paul Okoye marked his birthday on 18 November alone for the first time, after his twin brother Peter adopted 30 November as his new official birthday. Fans reacted with sadness over the split.

Paul Okoye, the singer known as Rudeboy, celebrated his birthday on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, without his twin brother Peter for the first time.

The celebration

The P-Square star shared a video of himself sitting alone at a dining table filled with food, with a caption expressing gratitude and pride.

Why they are apart

The solo celebration follows Peter Okoye's announcement in April 2026 that he would no longer share their birth date. Peter, who performs as Mr P, said on X that 30 November would now be his official birthday, describing it as a personal decision and thanking fans for understanding.

How fans reacted

The birthday post drew many reactions, with numerous fans expressing sadness that the twins, who rose to fame together as P-Square, now mark their birthdays separately.

JMartins' appeal

Singer JMartins, a longtime friend of the twins, used the occasion to wish both brothers a happy birthday and urged them to let go of pride. He said life is short and asked them to reconcile, even if they never reunite as P-Square, so that their children do not grow up as enemies.

FIFA appoints normalisation committee to run NFF, names Amokachi and Enakhena

19 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

FIFA has named lawyer Sampson Ebomhe to chair a normalisation committee for the Nigeria Football Federation ahead of its 2027 elections. Daniel Amokachi, Godwin Enakhena, Amina Yerima and Joe Abah are also members.

Read more

FIFA has set up a normalisation committee to run the Nigeria Football Federation (NFF), appointing lawyer Sampson Ebomhe as chairman with immediate effect.

The members

The world governing body named four other members: sports journalist and administrator Godwin Enakhena, former Super Eagles captain Daniel Amokachi, Amina Yerima and Dr Joe Abah, former Director-General of the Bureau of Public Service Reforms.

FIFA's Chief Member Associations Officer, Elkhan Mammadov, confirmed the appointments in a letter, noting that all members are still subject to an eligibility check by the FIFA Review Committee before they are formally confirmed.

Background

Amokachi, nicknamed The Bull, played at two World Cups for Nigeria and is one of the country's best-known football figures.

Super Eagles climb to 29th in FIFA rankings despite Tanzania draw

19 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Nigeria have moved up one place to 29th in the latest FIFA world rankings and are back in Africa's top five. The boost comes despite the goalless draw with Tanzania that hurt their AFCON hopes.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles have moved up one place to 29th in the latest FIFA world rankings, ending their 2026 international calendar with a small boost.

The ranking

The Super Eagles climbed from 30th to 29th in the world and moved back into Africa's top five, after slipping out of that group earlier in the year.

The results behind it

Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 in Uyo before playing out a goalless draw in the return leg in Zanzibar. The draw left them on seven points, three behind group leaders Guinea-Bissau, making their path to AFCON 2027 more complicated.
