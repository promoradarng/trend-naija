Bobrisky says leaving Nigeria was one of the best decisions of their life

Socialite Idris Okuneye, known as Bobrisky, says leaving Nigeria was one of the best decisions of their life.

What Bobrisky said

In an Instagram post on Saturday, 7 November 2026, Bobrisky described Nigeria as a toxic environment where a new drama, piece of gossip or fight springs up every day. The socialite said life since relocating had been peaceful and that they would not give up that peace.

Background

Bobrisky left Nigeria in November 2024 after a public row with the federal government over claims about the conditions under which a six-month jail sentence for abusing the naira had been served. The sentence was handed down in April 2024 after Bobrisky pleaded guilty to spraying and mutilating naira notes. Allegations that the socialite had served part of the sentence outside prison led to a government investigation and a dispute with activist VeryDarkMan, who first raised the claims. Bobrisky was stopped at the Seme border while trying to leave the country in October 2024 before eventually relocating.

DNA samples taken from Mohbad's remains and son Liam under court supervision

7 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

DNA samples have reportedly been collected from the remains of the late singer Mohbad and from his son, Liam, in a court-supervised exercise in Lagos, after his widow, Wunmi, applied for a test to settle questions about the boy's paternity.

Read more

DNA samples have been collected from the remains of the late singer Ilerioluwa Aloba, known as Mohbad, and from his young son, Liam, as the long-running dispute over the child's paternity moves towards a resolution.

What happened

According to reports on Saturday, 7 November 2026, the exercise took place on Saturday, 31 October, under the supervision of a Lagos court. The court had approved an application by Mohbad's widow, Omowunmi (Wunmi) Aloba, for a DNA test to establish Liam's paternity, and appointed a welfare officer to oversee the process. Samples were first taken from the singer's remains at the Mainland General Hospital in Yaba, then escorted under tight security to the Lagos State DNA and Forensic Centre, where Liam's samples were also collected.

Who was present

The exercise was reportedly carried out with heavy security because of concerns about possible crowds. Mohbad's mother, his younger brother and his widow were present. The results have not been made public.

Background

Mohbad died in Lagos in September 2023 at the age of 27. His death led to protests, a police investigation and a coroner's inquest. Since then, his father, Joseph Aloba, has repeatedly called for a DNA test on Liam, which led to a bitter public dispute with Wunmi and divided the singer's fans. The singer's death has also been at the centre of this week's public feud between VeryDarkMan and Davido.

What happens next

The results are expected once the forensic analysis is complete.

Kunle Afod laments 20-minute wait for help after accident on Ibadan–Sagamu road

7 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actor Kunle Afod says he witnessed a serious accident on the Ibadan–Sagamu Expressway and that no ambulance or emergency service arrived for about 20 minutes. He called on the authorities to improve emergency response on highways.

Read more

Nollywood actor Kunle Afod has called for better emergency services on Nigeria's highways after witnessing a serious accident on the Ibadan–Sagamu Expressway.

What he said

In a video shared on social media on Saturday, 7 November 2026, the actor said he and others saw a terrible crash on the busy road, and that for about 20 minutes no ambulance or emergency responders came to help the victims. He described the situation as very sad and urged the relevant authorities to take urgent action so that accident victims receive timely medical help. He did not give details of how many people were involved or their condition.

Background

The Lagos–Ibadan and Ibadan–Sagamu corridors are among the busiest roads in the country, and crashes are common. The Federal Road Safety Corps and state emergency agencies run ambulance services on some highways, but road users often complain that help arrives late, especially outside major cities. Afod has also been in the news this week after speaking publicly for the first time about his separation from his wife, Desola.

Group disputes his account

On Saturday, a group called Ogun Crime Watch asked the police to question the actor over the video, which it described as misleading. The group said the crash, involving an 18-seater bus that reportedly burst a tyre and hit a concrete barrier near Saapade, happened on Friday, and that Ogun State emergency responders arrived at 12.28pm, one minute before Afod reportedly posted his video. It said 13 passengers were injured and taken to hospital, and accused the video of trying to make the state government look bad. Afod had not responded to the group's claims at the time of writing.

Ogun government responds

The Ogun State Government also rejected the actor's account. In a statement on Saturday, the governor's special adviser on media, Kayode Akinmade, said a passer-by reported the crash at the Federal Road Safety Corps point in Ogere at 12.18pm, and that a state ambulance and FRSC officials reached the scene at 12.28pm, within 10 minutes. It said the state runs six ambulance points along the expressway, that its emergency call centre received no distress call about the crash, and that victims were taken to a hospital in Ogere, with the more seriously injured moved to Olabisi Onabanjo University Teaching Hospital in Sagamu. The statement accused the actor of framing the incident to damage the government.

How people reacted

Many social media users agreed with the actor, saying faster emergency response could save lives, while others criticised the government for spending heavily on election campaigns while basic emergency services remain poor.

Genevieve Nnaji wows fans with rare appearance at Lagos Fashion Week

7 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nollywood star Genevieve Nnaji drew a flood of praise from fans and colleagues after a rare public appearance at Lagos Fashion Week, wearing a dress straight from a designer's new collection.

Nollywood icon Genevieve Nnaji has set social media buzzing after a rare public appearance at Lagos Fashion Week.

What happened

Photos shared on Friday, 6 November 2026, showed the actress, who has kept a low profile in recent years, wearing a dress from the Nigerian label Orire's new spring/summer 2027 collection, taken straight from the runway. The pictures were shared by celebrity stylist and designer Veronica Odeka.

Stars react

Several Nollywood stars flooded the comments with praise. Actress Erica Nlewedim joked that the photos explained why everywhere suddenly felt so hot, while actor Steve Chuks also left a widely shared reaction. Fans also added their own tributes in the comments.

Background

Genevieve Nnaji rose to fame in the late 1990s and became one of Nollywood's biggest stars. In 2018 she directed 'Lionheart', which became the first Netflix original film from Nigeria. She has largely stepped away from the spotlight since then, which is why her occasional appearances attract so much attention. Lagos Fashion Week is one of Africa's biggest fashion events, and this year's edition has also featured Lagos governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu walking the runway.
