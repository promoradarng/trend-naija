Uzor Arukwe opens up on being sacked from telecom job before acting fame

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

Popular Nollywood actor Uzor Arukwe has shared the story of how he lost his job at a telecom company before he became one of the industry's most in-demand stars.

What he said

Speaking on the CreativiTea podcast on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Arukwe said he worked in sales at Etisalat, where he led a tech sales team. During a campaign that gave customers free promotional data with MiFi routers, members of his team allegedly charged customers for the data and kept the money. Arukwe said he was not involved, but his login was traced to some of the transactions. He said someone else had been using his login, insisting that was the truth.

How it ended

As head of the team, he said he was held responsible and was dismissed in 2018, along with one other colleague. He said the decision shook the company because he was well regarded and seen as someone with a future there. He added that he kept the sacking from his parents and dealt with it privately.

From sales to the screen

Arukwe went on to build a successful acting career and is now one of the most familiar faces in Nollywood films and series. In another part of the interview he also spoke about producers repeatedly offering him the same type of character and said he wants more varied roles.

How people reacted

Fans praised his honesty, with many saying the story showed that losing a job can sometimes open a new door.

Peter Obi says Nigerians will 'feel the difference from day one' if he and Kwankwaso win

20 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi, speaking in Anambra alongside former Kano governor Rabiu Kwankwaso, has promised to fight corruption and use public money for the public good.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has told supporters in Anambra State that Nigerians will feel a difference from the very first day if he and Rabiu Kwankwaso win the 2027 election.

What he said

Speaking in Anambra alongside the former Kano governor, Obi reportedly said their administration would prioritise accountability, transparency and the responsible management of public funds.

He said the two of them would fight corruption and make sure public money is used for the public good, adding that Nigerians would know from day one that a new team was in charge.

The context

Obi's comments came a day after Anambra Governor Charles Soludo hosted an APC rally in Awka for President Bola Tinubu and said the election was already decided. ADC chieftain Dele Momodu responded that Obi would win Anambra comfortably.

The race

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027, with Tinubu (APC), Atiku Abubakar (ADC), Obi (NDC) and other candidates in the race.

Edo police summon council chairman over alleged attack on NDC office in Afuze

20 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

The Edo State Police Command has summoned the chairman of Owan East Local Government over an alleged attack on the NDC secretariat in Afuze. Police say investigations have begun.

Read more

The Edo State Police Command has summoned the chairman of Owan East Local Government Area, Ernest Oyaole Aitalegbe, over his alleged link to an attack on the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) secretariat in Afuze.

What police said

Police spokesperson ASP Eno Ikoedem said in a statement on Friday, 20 November 2026, that investigations had begun into the incident. The attack reportedly involved armed men suspected to be operatives of the Edo State Security Network.

Police Commissioner Patrick Daaor warned that anyone found responsible for political violence would be arrested and prosecuted.

The other side

The council chairman's response to the allegation was not included in the reports. He has not been charged with any offence.

Background

Concerns about political violence have grown ahead of the 2027 elections, with the Senate recently urging police to investigate attacks on candidates and party supporters.
