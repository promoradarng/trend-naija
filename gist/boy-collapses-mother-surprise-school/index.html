Emotional moment boy collapses after his mother surprises him at school following four years abroad

28 Oct 2026, 10:42 GMT

An emotional reunion between a mother and her son has captured attention on social media after the young boy reportedly collapsed upon seeing his mother for the first time in four years.

According to the story accompanying the circulating video, the woman had been living abroad for four years, leaving her son behind while she pursued a better future for her family.

After years of separation, she decided to return home and surprise her son at his school without telling him she was coming.

What was supposed to be a joyful reunion quickly became an overwhelming moment for the young boy.

Upon seeing his mother unexpectedly, the boy reportedly became so overwhelmed that he collapsed, leaving those around him concerned.

The touching scene has sparked emotional reactions online, with many social media users reflecting on the sacrifices parents make when they travel abroad in search of better opportunities for their children.

For many families, living abroad comes with a painful price. Parents sometimes spend years away from their children, missing birthdays, school activities, family celebrations and important childhood moments.

Although financial support can help provide a better life, the emotional impact of prolonged separation can be difficult for both parents and children.

The circumstances surrounding the boy's reported collapse, including whether he needed medical attention, have not been independently confirmed.

Nevertheless, the reunion has reminded many people of the importance of family, parental presence and the emotional bond between mothers and their children.

Four years without seeing his mother, and suddenly she appeared right before his eyes. Imagine the emotions running through that little boy's heart.

A video of a campaign bus belonging to the All Progressives Congress (APC) stuck on a badly damaged road in Lagos has spread widely online.

What the video shows

The clip, shared on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, shows a minibus bearing APC logos and candidates' portraits trapped on a waterlogged, broken stretch of road at Alaba Market. People on board try to push the vehicle free, while bystanders jeer and refuse to help, using the moment to complain about the state of local infrastructure and criticise the ruling party.

Reactions

The video drew many comments online. Economist and politician Professor Pat Utomi joked that the bus driver needed only 'renewed hope' to endure the road, a reference to the slogan of President Bola Tinubu's government.

Background

A similar video of an APC campaign bus stuck on a bad road in Ebonyi State circulated about a month ago.

Read more

A live television debate took an awkward turn on Tuesday night, 10 November 2026, when Nigeria's national emergency number failed to connect after a ruling party official insisted it worked.

What happened

Speaking on a Channels Television programme to highlight the government's achievements, Alwan Hassan, a member of the APC presidential campaign council, said the 112 emergency response system had been upgraded and worked reliably nationwide. To prove his point, he dared the anchor, Seun Okinbaloye, to call the number live on air. The anchor accepted, dialled 112 on his phone, but the call did not go through.

Background

The 112 toll-free number is meant to connect people to police, fire, ambulance and other emergency services across Nigeria. Emergency response became a talking point last week when actor Kunle Afod complained about the response to a crash on the Lagos–Ibadan Expressway.

Former Obidient coordinator Yunusa Tanko joins SDP, to lead Adebayo's campaign

11 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Yunusa Tanko, once Peter Obi's spokesman and national coordinator of the Obidient Movement, has been named national coordinator of the SDP presidential campaign council for candidate Adewole Adebayo. He says he informed Obi before accepting.

Read more

Yunusa Tanko, a former spokesman for Peter Obi and ex-national coordinator of the Obidient Movement, has joined the Social Democratic Party (SDP) campaign for the 2027 presidential election.

The new role

At the inauguration of the SDP Presidential Campaign Council in Abuja on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, Tanko was named its national coordinator. In his acceptance speech he thanked the party's leadership and pledged full commitment to its presidential candidate, Prince Adewole Adebayo. He said he had reached out to both Obi and Senator Seriake Dickson, national leader of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), before taking up the post, and described his relationship with Adebayo as dating back to the 2023 election cycle.

How he left Obi's camp

Tanko was removed as national coordinator of the Obidient Movement in October 2026 and briefly served as Obi's political adviser for Northern Nigeria. He resigned from that role on 30 October, saying he was returning to the National Consultative Front, which he co-chairs with Professor Pat Utomi. He took on that position in October after the death of former House of Representatives Speaker Ghali Na'Abba, who had co-chaired the group.

Background

Obi, the 2023 Labour Party candidate, is now the NDC presidential candidate with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. Adebayo was also the SDP's presidential candidate in 2023. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027. Obi's campaign had not publicly reacted to Tanko's new role at the time of writing.
