VeryDarkMan challenges Zlatan Ibile to deny sending celebrities to beg him

8 Nov 2026, 17:19 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has continued his public exchange with singer Zlatan Ibile, daring him to openly deny claims that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with VDM over their ongoing disagreement.

"Just come out and say it's a lie"

Speaking partly in Pidgin, VDM said, in effect: "It's been over 24 hours since I dared Zlatan to come out and say I'm lying that he sent people to beg me. He acts like a hard guy in public, but does something else behind the scenes."

"Just come out and say it's a lie, simple! Let me call out the names of the celebrities you sent. If they deny it too, then you'll see what my next post will be."

He added that he was only holding back out of respect for Zlatan's parents: "It's because your mother and father are involved. We still respect parents here. If not, everyone would already know that you have no class."

Tough in public, soft in private?

VDM claims Zlatan is acting tough in public while making moves behind the scenes to settle the matter, and has threatened to name the celebrities he says were sent if Zlatan stays silent.

The feud began after a video of Davido singing a line from Zlatan's song INFERNO at Zlatan's wedding welcome party in Morocco, a lyric VDM believes was aimed at him. VDM then warned Zlatan that he would "learn the hard way".

Allegations, not facts

For now, these remain VeryDarkMan's claims. Zlatan has not responded publicly, and his silence does not confirm them.

Makinde camp denies claim he will step down for Atiku

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde's campaign has dismissed as fake a claim that he plans to withdraw his presidential bid in favour of Atiku Abubakar on 30 November. The claim was made by a political commentator.

Read more

The campaign of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has dismissed as fake news a claim that he plans to drop his 2027 presidential bid in favour of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar.

The claim

Political commentator Lere Olayinka had written that Makinde, the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) candidate, would step down for Atiku on 30 November, the date of the opposition parties' summit in Abuja.

The denial

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, the verified X account linked to Makinde's campaign rejected the claim and accused the ruling APC of spreading falsehoods. The post drew both support and criticism from social media users.

Background

Makinde said this week that he remains open to working with Atiku and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu, but ruled out a formal merger of parties. Opposition parties are due to hold their second summit in Abuja on Monday. No response from the APC to the accusation was included in the reports.

President Bola Tinubu sent a delegation to Minna, Niger State, on Thursday, 26 November 2026, to visit former military Head of State General Ibrahim Babangida and former Head of State General Abdulsalami Abubakar.

NDC deputy governorship candidate in Ebonyi defects to APC

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) deputy governorship candidate in Ebonyi State, Ambassador Uka Chris Ndubuisi, has reportedly left the party and joined the All Progressives Congress (APC).

The announcement

Ndubuisi made the announcement on Thursday, 26 November 2026, at an APC governorship campaign rally in the state. He pledged his loyalty to President Bola Tinubu and Governor Francis Nwifuru, and said the decision was entirely his own.

The welcome

Governor Nwifuru, Minister of Works David Umahi, who is a former governor of the state, and APC supporters were present to welcome him into the party.

The other side

No response from the NDC to the defection was included in the reports. Defections between parties have been common as the 2027 campaigns gather pace.

Nathaniel Bassey builds apartments for his staff and musicians

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Gospel singer Nathaniel Bassey has unveiled a housing project, Love Song Apartments, built for his staff and musicians. He said he hopes they will move in with their families before Christmas.

Read more

Gospel singer Nathaniel Bassey has drawn praise after revealing that he built apartments to help members of his team become homeowners.

The project

In an Instagram post, Bassey said the housing project, named Love Song Apartments, was created for his staff and musicians. He said he was particularly excited at the prospect of the beneficiaries moving into their new homes with their families before Christmas.

Why he did it

The singer described the project as more than providing accommodation, saying it was a way of making sure the people who work with him share in the fruits of their joint efforts.

How people reacted

Pictures of the apartments have been widely shared online.
