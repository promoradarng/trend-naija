VeryDarkMan challenges Zlatan Ibile to deny sending celebrities to beg him

8 Nov 2026, 17:19 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has continued his public exchange with singer Zlatan Ibile, daring him to openly deny claims that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with VDM over their ongoing disagreement.

"Just come out and say it's a lie"

Speaking partly in Pidgin, VDM said, in effect: "It's been over 24 hours since I dared Zlatan to come out and say I'm lying that he sent people to beg me. He acts like a hard guy in public, but does something else behind the scenes."

"Just come out and say it's a lie, simple! Let me call out the names of the celebrities you sent. If they deny it too, then you'll see what my next post will be."

He added that he was only holding back out of respect for Zlatan's parents: "It's because your mother and father are involved. We still respect parents here. If not, everyone would already know that you have no class."

Tough in public, soft in private?

VDM claims Zlatan is acting tough in public while making moves behind the scenes to settle the matter, and has threatened to name the celebrities he says were sent if Zlatan stays silent.

The feud began after a video of Davido singing a line from Zlatan's song INFERNO at Zlatan's wedding welcome party in Morocco, a lyric VDM believes was aimed at him. VDM then warned Zlatan that he would "learn the hard way".

Allegations, not facts

For now, these remain VeryDarkMan's claims. Zlatan has not responded publicly, and his silence does not confirm them.

Carter Efe and Peller miss out as India's Ashswag wins international streamer award

Nigerian content creators Carter Efe and Peller have missed out on an international award at the 2026 Streamer Awards.

The result

The two were nominated for Best International Streamer after the organisers released the shortlist in October. At the ceremony, reported on Friday, 13 November 2026, the prize went to Indian streamer Ashswag, who beat the Nigerian pair and other nominees from around the world.

Why it mattered

The nominations put the Nigerian duo among creators from around the world competing for the category.

Peller and Phyna react

In a post on X on Thursday night, Peller thanked his fans, saying their loyalty meant more to him than any trophy and that the nomination itself was a blessing. He complained, however, that the result of his category was announced on the red carpet before the main ceremony began, which he called unfair. Reality star Phyna went further in a livestream, accusing the organisers of using the Nigerian nominees to attract Nigerian viewers and engagement with no real intention of giving them the award.

Background

Peller rose to prominence as one of Nigeria's biggest TikTok live streamers, while Carter Efe, a skit maker and musician, has also built a large streaming following.

Sweden returns 39 Benin Bronzes to Nigeria, 129 years after they were looted

13 Nov 2026, 15:50 GMT

Sweden has handed back 39 Benin artefacts taken during the British invasion of Benin in 1897, at a ceremony in Lagos. Six of the objects will stay in Sweden on a 10-year loan.

Read more

Sweden has returned 39 Benin Bronzes and other artefacts to Nigeria, 129 years after they were looted from the Benin Kingdom.

The handover

Representatives of Sweden's National Museums of World Culture formally handed over the objects at a ceremony in Lagos on Thursday, 12 November 2026. They will be received by Nigeria's National Commission for Museums and Monuments. The collection includes cultural objects known as Emwin Arre. Six of the 39 artefacts will remain in Sweden under a 10-year loan arrangement.

Why it matters

The objects were among thousands taken by British forces who attacked and burned Benin City in 1897 before bringing the kingdom under British rule. Michael Barrett, curator for Africa at Sweden's National Museums of World Culture, described the return as an important step in addressing colonial-era looting of cultural property.

Background

Nigeria has been campaigning for decades for the return of the Benin Bronzes, which are held in museums and private collections around the world. The Netherlands returned 119 objects in 2025, and Germany handed over 20 Benin Bronzes in 2022. Many more remain abroad.

ECOWAS names ex-Ghana president Akufo-Addo to lead observers for Nigeria's 2027 polls

13 Nov 2026, 15:50 GMT

ECOWAS has appointed former Ghanaian President Nana Akufo-Addo to head its election observation mission for Nigeria's presidential and National Assembly elections on 16 January 2027.

Read more

The Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) has appointed former Ghanaian President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo to lead its election observation mission to Nigeria.

The appointment

The appointment was announced in a statement from Akufo-Addo's office on Thursday, 12 November 2026. He will oversee the deployment of ECOWAS observers for Nigeria's presidential and National Assembly elections, scheduled for 16 January 2027. The mission will monitor the electoral process and report on how the polls are conducted.

Who is Akufo-Addo

Akufo-Addo served two terms as Ghana's president, from 2017 to January 2025.

Background

International attention on Nigeria's election is growing. The European Union has raised concerns about violence and hate speech, and a senior US State Department official told Congress this week that the vote could spur violence. INEC says preparations are on track, with non-sensitive materials already being moved to the states.
