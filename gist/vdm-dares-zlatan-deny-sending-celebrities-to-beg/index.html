VeryDarkMan challenges Zlatan Ibile to deny sending celebrities to beg him

8 Nov 2026, 17:19 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has continued his public exchange with singer Zlatan Ibile, daring him to openly deny claims that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with VDM over their ongoing disagreement.

"Just come out and say it's a lie"

Speaking partly in Pidgin, VDM said, in effect: "It's been over 24 hours since I dared Zlatan to come out and say I'm lying that he sent people to beg me. He acts like a hard guy in public, but does something else behind the scenes."

"Just come out and say it's a lie, simple! Let me call out the names of the celebrities you sent. If they deny it too, then you'll see what my next post will be."

He added that he was only holding back out of respect for Zlatan's parents: "It's because your mother and father are involved. We still respect parents here. If not, everyone would already know that you have no class."

Tough in public, soft in private?

VDM claims Zlatan is acting tough in public while making moves behind the scenes to settle the matter, and has threatened to name the celebrities he says were sent if Zlatan stays silent.

The feud began after a video of Davido singing a line from Zlatan's song INFERNO at Zlatan's wedding welcome party in Morocco, a lyric VDM believes was aimed at him. VDM then warned Zlatan that he would "learn the hard way".

Allegations, not facts

For now, these remain VeryDarkMan's claims. Zlatan has not responded publicly, and his silence does not confirm them.

VeryDarkMan urges Obi, Atiku and Makinde to form one party to face Tinubu

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has called on Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to put their ambitions aside and unite under one party against President Tinubu. The three are due at an opposition summit on Monday.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one political party ahead of the 2027 election.

What he said

In a post on Instagram, VDM said the three should sit down together and form one party to defeat President Bola Tinubu. He argued that they need to set aside their personal ambitions, and that a united front would make the contest against the ruling party more competitive.

Background

Obi of the NDC, Atiku of the ADC and Makinde of the APM are expected at an opposition summit in Abuja on Monday, 30 November, where parties are due to sign a cooperation pact. The organisers have said it will not be a formal merger, and Makinde's camp has denied reports that he plans to step down for Atiku.

VeryDarkMan gives Ogoja council chairman two weeks over water crisis

Activist VeryDarkMan has called out the chairman of Ogoja Local Government in Cross River State over a community's lack of clean water. He gave him two weeks to act.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has called out the chairman of Ogoja Local Government Area in Cross River State, Christopher Agbeh, over a community's lack of clean water.

What he said

In a video message, VDM said it was unfortunate that in 2026 a whole community was still crying out for something as basic as water. He described the situation as disappointing and told the chairman he had two weeks to make himself useful to his people.

NAFDAC shuts Abuja's Zuba International Market over fake products worth ₦300m

27 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

NAFDAC has shut Zuba International Market in Abuja after a joint operation with police and the DSS reportedly uncovered counterfeit and unwholesome goods worth more than ₦300 million. Over 160 shops were inspected.

Read more

The National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) has shut down Zuba International Market in the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja.

The operation

The closure followed a joint enforcement operation with the Nigeria Police Force and the Department of State Services, which reportedly uncovered counterfeit and unwholesome products worth more than ₦300 million. The exercise covered more than 160 shops in the market.

Why now

NAFDAC said the closure was part of a wider crackdown on substandard and fake goods ahead of the festive season.
