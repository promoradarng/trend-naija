VeryDarkMan challenges Zlatan Ibile to deny sending celebrities to beg him

8 Nov 2026, 17:19 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has continued his public exchange with singer Zlatan Ibile, daring him to openly deny claims that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with VDM over their ongoing disagreement.

"Just come out and say it's a lie"

Speaking partly in Pidgin, VDM said, in effect: "It's been over 24 hours since I dared Zlatan to come out and say I'm lying that he sent people to beg me. He acts like a hard guy in public, but does something else behind the scenes."

"Just come out and say it's a lie, simple! Let me call out the names of the celebrities you sent. If they deny it too, then you'll see what my next post will be."

He added that he was only holding back out of respect for Zlatan's parents: "It's because your mother and father are involved. We still respect parents here. If not, everyone would already know that you have no class."

Tough in public, soft in private?

VDM claims Zlatan is acting tough in public while making moves behind the scenes to settle the matter, and has threatened to name the celebrities he says were sent if Zlatan stays silent.

The feud began after a video of Davido singing a line from Zlatan's song INFERNO at Zlatan's wedding welcome party in Morocco, a lyric VDM believes was aimed at him. VDM then warned Zlatan that he would "learn the hard way".

Allegations, not facts

For now, these remain VeryDarkMan's claims. Zlatan has not responded publicly, and his silence does not confirm them.

Presidential election turnout has fallen from 69% to 27% since 2003

28 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Turnout in Nigeria's presidential elections has dropped from a Fourth Republic high of about 69% in 2003 to under 27% in 2023, according to figures compiled by a statistics platform. The decline has continued for five straight elections.

Read more

Voter turnout in Nigeria's presidential elections has fallen sharply over the past two decades, according to figures shared by a statistics platform this week.

The numbers

Measured as total votes cast as a share of registered voters, turnout reached a Fourth Republic high of 69.08% in 2003. It then fell to 57.49% in 2007 and 53.68% in 2011, and continued to decline in each election after that, reaching just 26.71% in 2023. That is a drop of more than 42 percentage points in 20 years.

Why it matters

The figures come as parties and INEC step up efforts to get Nigerians to collect their Permanent Voter Cards and vote in the 16 January 2027 elections. INEC is also running a nationwide mock accreditation exercise on Saturday to test its systems.

Background

Concerns about insecurity, logistics and confidence in the electoral process have often been cited as reasons for low turnout. INEC this week named nine states as hotspots for campaign violence.

Chelsea suffer third straight Premier League defeat with 3-2 loss at Forest

Chelsea lost 3-2 to Nottingham Forest at the City Ground on Friday, their third Premier League defeat in a row. Emmanuel Emegha scored twice but could not prevent the loss.

Chelsea's poor run continued on Friday, 27 November 2026, as they lost 3-2 to Nottingham Forest at the City Ground, their third Premier League defeat in a row.

The match

Igor Jesus gave Forest the lead inside five minutes, and the home side led at half-time. Early in the second half, Morgan Gibbs-White scored from the penalty spot after Wesley Fofana fouled Liam Delap. Emmanuel Emegha pulled a goal back for Chelsea, but Gibbs-White restored the two-goal lead soon after. Emegha scored again late on, but Chelsea ran out of time.

Alonso's verdict

Manager Xabi Alonso said after the game that his side were struggling with confidence.

Poor form

Chelsea have now lost four of their last five league games, with their only win in that run coming against Manchester United. Last weekend they were beaten 4-1 at home by Leeds, after which Cole Palmer and Joao Pedro were seen exchanging words on the touchline.
