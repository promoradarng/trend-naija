VeryDarkMan challenges Zlatan Ibile to deny sending celebrities to beg him

8 Nov 2026, 17:19 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has continued his public exchange with singer Zlatan Ibile, daring him to openly deny claims that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with VDM over their ongoing disagreement.

"Just come out and say it's a lie"

Speaking partly in Pidgin, VDM said, in effect: "It's been over 24 hours since I dared Zlatan to come out and say I'm lying that he sent people to beg me. He acts like a hard guy in public, but does something else behind the scenes."

"Just come out and say it's a lie, simple! Let me call out the names of the celebrities you sent. If they deny it too, then you'll see what my next post will be."

He added that he was only holding back out of respect for Zlatan's parents: "It's because your mother and father are involved. We still respect parents here. If not, everyone would already know that you have no class."

Tough in public, soft in private?

VDM claims Zlatan is acting tough in public while making moves behind the scenes to settle the matter, and has threatened to name the celebrities he says were sent if Zlatan stays silent.

The feud began after a video of Davido singing a line from Zlatan's song INFERNO at Zlatan's wedding welcome party in Morocco, a lyric VDM believes was aimed at him. VDM then warned Zlatan that he would "learn the hard way".

Allegations, not facts

For now, these remain VeryDarkMan's claims. Zlatan has not responded publicly, and his silence does not confirm them.

Aremu Afolayan reportedly clashes with Isbae U on Curiosity Made Me Ask

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actor Aremu Afolayan's appearance on Isbae U's show Curiosity Made Me Ask reportedly ended in a heated exchange. He took offence when the host compared their late fathers.

Read more

Nollywood actor Aremu Afolayan has become a talking point online after his appearance on comedian Isbae U's interview show, Curiosity Made Me Ask, reportedly ended in a heated exchange.

What happened

Isbae U, known for his sarcastic interview style, is said to have questioned whether Aremu was still active in Nollywood and made jokes about his body and how he earns a living given his few recent film roles.

Aremu reportedly explained that he has stayed away from film sets because of insecurity, saying travelling for shoots had become dangerous and he was not willing to risk his life for a role paying ₦2 million or ₦3 million.

Where it went wrong

The mood reportedly changed when Isbae U brought up his own late father, actor Sir K. Kamoru, and compared him with Aremu's late father, the veteran filmmaker Ade Love Afolayan. Aremu is said to have taken offence and warned the host against such comparisons, insisting his father's contribution to the film industry was not up for debate. He also reportedly questioned Isbae U's own achievements as an actor.

Sowore calls for legal cannabis, says Nigeria could earn up to ₦2.86 trillion a year

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has urged the federal government to legalise, regulate and tax cannabis. He pointed to revenues in Canada and US states and projected Nigeria could earn between ₦715 billion and ₦2.86 trillion yearly.

Read more

African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore has called on the federal government to legalise cannabis, arguing that Nigeria is missing out on billions of naira in revenue by keeping it illegal.

What he said

In a post on X on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, Sowore said the country should build a regulated cannabis industry instead of spending resources arresting people and destroying plantations. He said he has backed legalisation since 2018.

The figures

He pointed to regulated markets abroad, citing about CA$2.2 billion in government revenue from recreational cannabis in Canada in 2023-2024, around $1.067 billion in cannabis tax in California in 2024, and roughly $255.4 million in Colorado.

Using those examples, he projected that Nigeria could earn between $550 million and $2.2 billion a year, or about ₦715 billion to ₦2.86 trillion at an exchange rate of ₦1,300 to the dollar. He acknowledged that actual earnings would depend on how any such market is designed and run.

He also argued that legalisation could create jobs in farming, pharmaceutical production and industrial hemp, and support research and exports.

Background

Cannabis remains illegal in Nigeria, and the NDLEA regularly carries out raids on farms and arrests dealers. Sowore, a long-time activist, is running for president in 2027 on the AAC platform.

Crystal Palace appoint Thomas Frank after sacking Pierre Sage

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Crystal Palace have named former Brentford and Tottenham boss Thomas Frank as their new manager on a three-year deal. He replaces Pierre Sage, who was sacked after four months with the club bottom of the Premier League.

Read more

Crystal Palace have appointed Thomas Frank as their new manager, following the sacking of Pierre Sage.

The change

Sage joined the club in the summer but was dismissed after only four months, with Palace bottom of the Premier League after 10 matches. Frank, who previously managed Brentford and Tottenham, has signed a three-year contract.

What they said

Palace chairman Steve Parish welcomed the Dane, saying he had a proven record of building winning teams and improving players, and that his Premier League experience would help the club in the rest of the season.

Frank said it was an honour to take charge of what he called an iconic club, and that he was looking forward to meeting the players and staff.

What happens next

His first game in charge is this weekend, when Palace travel to Coventry hoping to climb off the bottom of the table.
