VeryDarkMan challenges Zlatan Ibile to deny sending celebrities to beg him

8 Nov 2026, 17:19 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has continued his public exchange with singer Zlatan Ibile, daring him to openly deny claims that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with VDM over their ongoing disagreement.

"Just come out and say it's a lie"

Speaking partly in Pidgin, VDM said, in effect: "It's been over 24 hours since I dared Zlatan to come out and say I'm lying that he sent people to beg me. He acts like a hard guy in public, but does something else behind the scenes."

"Just come out and say it's a lie, simple! Let me call out the names of the celebrities you sent. If they deny it too, then you'll see what my next post will be."

He added that he was only holding back out of respect for Zlatan's parents: "It's because your mother and father are involved. We still respect parents here. If not, everyone would already know that you have no class."

Tough in public, soft in private?

VDM claims Zlatan is acting tough in public while making moves behind the scenes to settle the matter, and has threatened to name the celebrities he says were sent if Zlatan stays silent.

The feud began after a video of Davido singing a line from Zlatan's song INFERNO at Zlatan's wedding welcome party in Morocco, a lyric VDM believes was aimed at him. VDM then warned Zlatan that he would "learn the hard way".

Allegations, not facts

For now, these remain VeryDarkMan's claims. Zlatan has not responded publicly, and his silence does not confirm them.

VeryDarkMan warns Tinubu over deadly attack on NDC campaign in Delta

24 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has issued a strong warning to President Tinubu after gunmen killed four people, including NDC youth leader Efemena Umukoro, at Omo-Agege's campaign event in Delta. He said political violence could spiral out of control.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has issued a fierce public warning to President Bola Tinubu following the deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North, Delta State.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram, VDM told the president that he would burn Nigeria down, arguing that targeting opposition figures with gunfire is a dangerous political strategy that could spiral far beyond anyone's control. He warned that even people close to the president would not be safe if the cycle of violence continued, and drew attention to the fact that the attack happened in Delta State.

The attack

Gunmen opened fire on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege's campaign team on Monday, 23 November 2026, while he was addressing supporters. Four people were killed, according to his campaign, including Efemena Umukoro, an Urhobo youth leader popularly known as General Akima. Omo-Agege was unhurt.

Other reactions

Other social media commentators also condemned the killings, and Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori called for everyone involved to be arrested regardless of their party. The Inspector-General of Police has ordered an investigation.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to VDM's remarks was included in the reports.

NDC says supporters were attacked in Benin as Obi arrived for campaign

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Nigeria Democratic Congress has alleged that gunmen attacked its supporters and campaign vehicles on Airport Road, Benin City, as Peter Obi arrived in Edo State on Wednesday. It said one supporter was critically wounded.

Read more

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has alleged that its supporters were attacked in Benin City, Edo State, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, as its presidential candidate Peter Obi arrived for a town hall meeting and other campaign events.

What the party says

According to the party, attackers who arrived in a pickup van opened fire on its campaign vehicles parked along Airport Road while supporters waited for Obi. A party spokesperson posted videos online showing damaged vehicles and bloodstains, and said one supporter was critically wounded and receiving hospital treatment.

In a statement, the NDC's National Publicity Secretary said the attackers also assaulted people in the crowd and destroyed buses, and described the attack as unprovoked. The party blamed thugs it said were linked to the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

The other side

No response from the APC or the police to the allegation was included in the reports.

Background

The incident comes two days after gunmen attacked an NDC campaign event in Delta State, killing several people, and on the same day the House of Representatives warned that a state of emergency might be needed in Delta if violence continues. The NDC has also boycotted a security meeting called by the police chief.

Tinubu holds rally in Maiduguri, orders fresh push against insurgents

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu visited Maiduguri on Wednesday, where he commissioned projects, held a campaign rally and directed security agencies to step up operations against insurgents. He promised to turn security gains in the North-East into economic recovery.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu visited Maiduguri, the Borno State capital, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, where he held a campaign rally ahead of the January 2027 election.

Projects commissioned

On arrival, the president commissioned two projects: a female lodge for National Youth Service Corps members and the Borno International Conference Centre.

Security push

Addressing the crowd, Tinubu directed security agencies to intensify operations against retreating insurgents and promised support for the families of troops. He pledged that the peace gains recorded in the North-East would be turned into full economic recovery.

Background

Borno has been at the centre of the insurgency in the North-East for more than a decade. Earlier this week, state officials revealed that eight soldiers and 13 construction workers were killed during the rebuilding of the Mayanti community, which Governor Babagana Zulum has now reopened.
