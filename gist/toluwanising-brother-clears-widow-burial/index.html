Toluwanising's younger brother defends widow as burial dispute rages

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

The bitter dispute over where gospel singer Toluwanising should be buried has taken a new turn, after his younger brother reportedly spoke out in defence of the singer's widow.

What the brother said

In a voice note said to be his, reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, the brother said he wanted to clear the air because people were blaming the wrong person. He insisted that the widow had no role in the dispute and should be left alone. He blamed the singer's maternal relatives for turning him against his father's side of the family from childhood, and said their father had supported the singer financially during his school years. He also described a disagreement in which the singer's grandmother asked him for a Sallah ram, which he declined because he is a Christian, leaving some relatives upset.

Others weigh in

Media personality Nancy Umeh reportedly criticised the singer's family and said the widow has every right to decide where her husband is buried. Actor Yomi Fabiyi, however, reportedly urged the widow to allow the family to decide. On social media, many fans praised the brother for speaking up and said the widow had been unfairly attacked.

Background

Toluwanising died suddenly on 14 October 2026. Since then, members of his family have disagreed publicly about his burial. A family spokesman said in a viral video this week that relatives would remove his body if he was buried in a cemetery, and insisted he be laid beside his mother in the family compound. His widow had reportedly objected to a burial at the house they shared in Ibadan.

Mohbad's father weighs in

On Friday, Joseph Aloba, the father of the late singer Mohbad, shared a video in which he reportedly blamed Toluwanising's wife for the rift between the singer and his father's family, and urged people not to judge family matters they do not fully understand. Many social media users strongly criticised his comments and defended the widow.

The maternal uncle speaks

In a video interview that surfaced on Friday, a maternal uncle of the singer reportedly disputed the paternal family's account of his upbringing. He said Toluwanising's mother died when he was nine months old and that her family raised him, and claimed his father was absent for many years. He credited the widow with supporting the singer financially when he had little, and blamed an online interviewer for inflaming tensions. These claims have not been independently verified, and the paternal family has given a different account.

What happens next

No burial date has been announced, and fans are calling on both sides to resolve the matter privately so the singer can be laid to rest.

  • Nancy Umeh says the widow has the right to decide on the burial.
  • Yomi Fabiyi urged the widow to let the family decide.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

More nominations

Tems also picked up nominations for Best African Music Performance for 'Big Daddy', and Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song for 'What You Need'. Ayra Starr is nominated for Best African Music Performance for 'Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)', featuring Angélique Kidjo.

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.

DSS says it found no evidence it planned to disrupt ADC campaign in Sokoto, asks Atiku to retract or provide proof

18 Nov 2026, 07:57 GMT

The Department of State Services says its internal investigation found no evidence that it planned to recruit protesters to disrupt the ADC's presidential campaign flag-off in Sokoto. It has asked Atiku Abubakar to retract or clarify his allegation, or provide evidence.

Read more

The Department of State Services (DSS) says its investigation found no evidence that it planned to recruit protesters to disrupt the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential campaign in Sokoto.

How it started

Earlier this month, former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar accused the leadership of the DSS of recruiting protesters to disrupt the ADC's presidential campaign flag-off rally in Sokoto.

The allegation drew wide attention, and the DSS responded by ordering an investigation into the claim.

The DSS findings

The agency now says its investigation found nothing to support the allegation that it planned to recruit protesters or disrupt the ADC campaign.

It has asked Atiku to either retract or clarify his claim, or to provide evidence that could support further investigation.

Not the final word

It is important to note that this is the DSS's own preliminary finding. It was not an independent investigation, which means the agency effectively investigated an allegation made against itself.

Atiku has not issued a fresh public response to the DSS findings.

Why it matters

With the 2027 elections approaching, allegations that security agencies are being used against opposition parties are likely to keep coming up. Public trust depends on such claims being properly examined, whether they turn out to be true or not.

Kwara suspends children's hospital chief after mothers and sick children were locked out

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Kwara State Government has suspended the chief medical director of the Children's Specialist Hospital in Ilorin, after a viral video showed mothers and sick children allegedly locked out for arriving by 7am. The board says a shortage of doctors caused a backlog.

Read more

The Kwara State Government has reportedly suspended the Chief Medical Director of the Children's Specialist Hospital, Centre Igboro, Ilorin, Dr Mosunmola Folorunsho, after mothers and their sick children were allegedly locked out of the hospital.

What happened

A video that went viral showed mothers with sick children who said they had been shut out for arriving by 7am, reportedly because they had not arrived by 5:30am. Following the outrage, the Executive Secretary of the Kwara State Hospitals Management Board, Dr Abdulraheem Malik, and members of the management team made an emergency visit to the hospital.

The government's action

In a statement on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the board said the CMD had been suspended and given 48 hours to explain her role in the incident. She was directed to hand over to the most senior doctor at the hospital pending further directives.

The board's explanation

The board said its findings showed the hospital had a temporary shortage of doctors, after one of the doctors scheduled for the morning shift was away at a malaria training programme. This created a backlog of patients waiting to be seen.
