Toluwanising's younger brother defends widow as burial dispute rages

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

The bitter dispute over where gospel singer Toluwanising should be buried has taken a new turn, after his younger brother reportedly spoke out in defence of the singer's widow.

What the brother said

In a voice note said to be his, reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, the brother said he wanted to clear the air because people were blaming the wrong person. He insisted that the widow had no role in the dispute and should be left alone. He blamed the singer's maternal relatives for turning him against his father's side of the family from childhood, and said their father had supported the singer financially during his school years. He also described a disagreement in which the singer's grandmother asked him for a Sallah ram, which he declined because he is a Christian, leaving some relatives upset.

Others weigh in

Media personality Nancy Umeh reportedly criticised the singer's family and said the widow has every right to decide where her husband is buried. Actor Yomi Fabiyi, however, reportedly urged the widow to allow the family to decide. On social media, many fans praised the brother for speaking up and said the widow had been unfairly attacked.

Background

Toluwanising died suddenly on 14 October 2026. Since then, members of his family have disagreed publicly about his burial. A family spokesman said in a viral video this week that relatives would remove his body if he was buried in a cemetery, and insisted he be laid beside his mother in the family compound. His widow had reportedly objected to a burial at the house they shared in Ibadan.

Mohbad's father weighs in

On Friday, Joseph Aloba, the father of the late singer Mohbad, shared a video in which he reportedly blamed Toluwanising's wife for the rift between the singer and his father's family, and urged people not to judge family matters they do not fully understand. Many social media users strongly criticised his comments and defended the widow.

The maternal uncle speaks

In a video interview that surfaced on Friday, a maternal uncle of the singer reportedly disputed the paternal family's account of his upbringing. He said Toluwanising's mother died when he was nine months old and that her family raised him, and claimed his father was absent for many years. He credited the widow with supporting the singer financially when he had little, and blamed an online interviewer for inflaming tensions. These claims have not been independently verified, and the paternal family has given a different account.

What happens next

No burial date has been announced, and fans are calling on both sides to resolve the matter privately so the singer can be laid to rest.

  • The brother says the widow had no role in the dispute.
  • Nancy Umeh says the widow has the right to decide on the burial.
  • Yomi Fabiyi urged the widow to let the family decide.

Usher and Chris Brown reportedly argued backstage before delayed Atlanta show

7 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Usher and Chris Brown reportedly argued backstage before their joint concert in Atlanta on Thursday, contributing to a delay of nearly three hours. The dispute was said to be over whether to start while fans were still arriving.

Read more

R&B stars Usher and Chris Brown reportedly had an argument backstage before their concert in Atlanta on Thursday night, 5 November 2026, which contributed to a delay of almost three hours.

What happened

According to reports citing people close to the tour, the disagreement was about whether to start the show while many fans were still stuck in heavy traffic around the venue. Usher reportedly wanted to go ahead, while Brown preferred to wait until more concertgoers had reached their seats. The two are said to have exchanged words, with one source describing them as highly competitive throughout their joint tour and blaming the clash on egos.

Tour to continue

The sources reportedly said the two had not settled their differences, but did not believe the row threatened the rest of the tour. The pair were scheduled to perform in Atlanta again on Friday night. Some fans complained online about the long wait before the show began.

Background

Usher and Chris Brown are two of the biggest names in R&B and have collaborated in the past. The two reportedly had a confrontation at Brown's birthday party in 2023, when Usher was said to have stepped in after Brown began shouting at singer Teyana Taylor. Neither singer had publicly commented on the latest reports at the time of writing.

Why it matters

Both artists have large fan bases in Nigeria, and Chris Brown has strong links with Nigerian stars, having worked with Davido and other Afrobeats acts.

A video of Burna Boy in conversation with veteran singer Timaya has gone viral, with fans focusing on VeryDarkMan's friend Kokopee, who was standing nearby.

What the video shows

The clip, which began circulating on Thursday, 5 November 2026, shows Burna Boy and Timaya running into each other at an event and chatting. Kokopee, widely known as the best friend of activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), is seen standing a short distance away with his arms folded, watching the exchange. Burna Boy appears not to acknowledge him. At one point Kokopee seemed to move towards Burna Boy, but a bouncer reportedly stepped in before he could get close.

Burna Boy has reportedly supported VDM's charity projects, and the two have been seen as friendly, so many fans asked whether the singer had deliberately ignored Kokopee. How people reacted

Opinions were divided. Some fans said Burna Boy had clearly snubbed Kokopee, while others said the moment was being over-analysed and that the singer may simply not have noticed him in a busy event. Neither Burna Boy nor Kokopee had publicly commented at the time of writing.

Davido has drawn attention online after a video showed him praying for Zlatan Ibile and his wife at their white wedding reception in Morocco.

What happened

In footage shared on Friday, 6 November 2026, the singer is seen standing in a grand ballroom decorated with chandeliers and draped fabric, addressing the couple and their guests. Instead of giving a conventional speech, he said prayer was his priority. He began by mentioning the couple's traditional wedding, which had already taken place in Lagos, and said it had gone smoothly despite the large crowd, before praying for the newlyweds and their marriage.

Background

Zlatan, one of Nigeria's best-known rappers, held his white wedding in Morocco after a traditional ceremony in Lagos. Videos from the celebrations have been widely shared, including one of Zlatan in tears and another of Davido singing along to Zlatan's song 'Inferno'. The weddings came during a public feud between Davido and social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), who has also taken swipes at Zlatan and said this week he would be turning his attention to the rapper. Davido's aide Israel DMW has also denied claims linking the singer to VDM's past arrest.

How people reacted

Many fans praised Davido's sincerity and said the clip showed a spiritual side of him that people rarely see. Some asked why others were surprised, pointing out that he often speaks about his faith, while VDM's supporters continued to debate the feud in the comments.

Magixx says he struggled to pay bills after signing to Mavin Records

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Mavin singer Magixx says it took two to three months before he started earning after signing his record deal, and that people assumed he was rich. He says Nigerian artistes need songs with international appeal to build real wealth.

Read more

Mavin Records singer Magixx has spoken about the financial realities of the music business, saying a record deal does not automatically make an artiste rich.

What he said

Speaking on an episode of a music podcast, reported on Friday, 6 November 2026, the singer said he did not start earning from music immediately after signing to Don Jazzy's label. It took about two to three months before money began to come in, he said, and during that time he struggled to pay bills and meet family responsibilities. He added that asking people for help became difficult because they assumed his record deal meant he was already wealthy, while he was also expected to keep up the image of a star, including wearing expensive designer clothes.

Building wealth

Magixx said Nigerian musicians need songs with international appeal to make serious money, and that releasing hits consistently is key to staying financially stable.

Background

Magixx signed with Mavin Records in 2021 and is known for songs such as 'Love Don't Cost a Dime'. His comments come in the same week that fellow Mavin artiste Crayon posted, and later deleted, emotional messages about the label and his friendship with Rema.

How people reacted

Fans thanked him for being honest about the pressures young artistes face, while some said his story showed why upcoming musicians should manage expectations after signing a deal.
