Toluwanising's younger brother defends widow as burial dispute rages

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

The bitter dispute over where gospel singer Toluwanising should be buried has taken a new turn, after his younger brother reportedly spoke out in defence of the singer's widow.

What the brother said

In a voice note said to be his, reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, the brother said he wanted to clear the air because people were blaming the wrong person. He insisted that the widow had no role in the dispute and should be left alone. He blamed the singer's maternal relatives for turning him against his father's side of the family from childhood, and said their father had supported the singer financially during his school years. He also described a disagreement in which the singer's grandmother asked him for a Sallah ram, which he declined because he is a Christian, leaving some relatives upset.

Others weigh in

Media personality Nancy Umeh reportedly criticised the singer's family and said the widow has every right to decide where her husband is buried. Actor Yomi Fabiyi, however, reportedly urged the widow to allow the family to decide. On social media, many fans praised the brother for speaking up and said the widow had been unfairly attacked.

Background

Toluwanising died suddenly on 14 October 2026. Since then, members of his family have disagreed publicly about his burial. A family spokesman said in a viral video this week that relatives would remove his body if he was buried in a cemetery, and insisted he be laid beside his mother in the family compound. His widow had reportedly objected to a burial at the house they shared in Ibadan.

Mohbad's father weighs in

On Friday, Joseph Aloba, the father of the late singer Mohbad, shared a video in which he reportedly blamed Toluwanising's wife for the rift between the singer and his father's family, and urged people not to judge family matters they do not fully understand. Many social media users strongly criticised his comments and defended the widow.

The maternal uncle speaks

In a video interview that surfaced on Friday, a maternal uncle of the singer reportedly disputed the paternal family's account of his upbringing. He said Toluwanising's mother died when he was nine months old and that her family raised him, and claimed his father was absent for many years. He credited the widow with supporting the singer financially when he had little, and blamed an online interviewer for inflaming tensions. These claims have not been independently verified, and the paternal family has given a different account.

What happens next

No burial date has been announced, and fans are calling on both sides to resolve the matter privately so the singer can be laid to rest.

  • Nancy Umeh says the widow has the right to decide on the burial.

Burna Boy's 'For Everybody' campaign film wins 15 awards

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

'For Everybody', a sports and music campaign film starring Burna Boy, Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, has reportedly won 15 prizes at the 2026 w3 Awards, including a gold award. The film was directed by Dave Meyers and released in January.

Read more

A campaign film featuring Burna Boy and several of Africa's best-known footballers has picked up a string of international awards.

What happened

'For Everybody', made by Sporty Group's production arm Sporty Studios together with RadicalMedia, reportedly won 15 awards at the 2026 w3 Awards, according to reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026. It won a gold award in the creator and brand collaboration category, as well as 14 silver awards in categories including music video, cinematography, brand storytelling, branded content, and sports and entertainment.

About the film

The film was directed by Dave Meyers, an American director best known for his music videos with major global stars, and was released in January 2026. It brings together music and sport to celebrate African talent and culture. Alongside Burna Boy, it features Nigerian football stars Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, as well as Spain's Nico Williams, France's Eduardo Camavinga, Ghana legend Michael Essien, Kenya's Victor Wanyama and South African rugby star Cheslin Kolbe.

Background

The w3 Awards recognise creative work in digital media, including websites, videos and online campaigns. Burna Boy, a Grammy winner, has increasingly worked on projects linking music and sport, and recently presented the Socrates Award at this year's Ballon d'Or ceremony. Oshoala is one of Africa's most decorated female footballers, having won the African Women's Footballer of the Year award a record number of times.

How fans reacted

Fans celebrated the recognition online, saying the film showed how African music and sport can work together to reach global audiences.

Banky W returns to music on Victor Thompson's 'Super Power'

30 Oct 2026, 20:49 GMT

Singer and pastor Banky W is back on a new song, featuring on Victor Thompson's upcoming single 'Super Power', due on 6 November. He said he had accepted that his recording career was over and described the song as an act of obedience.

Read more

Banky W, the R&B singer turned pastor and former boss of Wizkid's old label, is making a return to music.

What he announced

On Friday, 30 October 2026, Banky W revealed on Instagram that he features on gospel singer Victor Thompson's upcoming single, 'Super Power', and shared a preview of the music video. The song is due to be released on Friday, 6 November. A clip of the two singers enjoying the song together was also shared online.

Why he returned

Banky W reportedly said he had accepted that his recording career had ended, and described the song as an act of obedience rather than a career move. He said he had prayed that if a return was meant to happen, the songs would come. About three weeks later, Thompson sent him a message saying they were meant to make music together.

Background

Banky W, whose real name is Olubankole Wellington, was one of Nigeria's leading R&B stars in the 2000s and early 2010s and founded the Empire Mates Entertainment label, which launched Wizkid's career. His last full project was the 2017 album 'Songs About U'. He has since focused on ministry and public life, and is married to actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington. Victor Thompson is known for the gospel hit 'This Year (Blessings)'.

How fans reacted

Fans welcomed the news with excitement, with many saying they had missed his voice. Some said they hoped the song would lead to a full gospel album.

Zlatan's diss track 'Inferno' tops Apple Music Nigeria as VeryDarkMan responds

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Zlatan Ibile's new track 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday and reportedly aimed at VeryDarkMan, has topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart. VDM has mocked its success on his Instagram story, after earlier daring it to stay at number one for a month.

Read more

Rapper Zlatan Ibile has scored a chart-topping hit with a song widely seen as a response to social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), and VDM has not stayed quiet.

The song

'Inferno', which features Peruzzi and Shoday, reportedly went to number one on the Apple Music Nigeria top songs chart and the Nigerian iTunes chart, according to reports on Friday, 30 October 2026. It also reportedly topped the chart in Sierra Leone. Fans have described it as a diss track aimed at VDM.

VDM's response

VDM reacted on his Instagram story with mockery, brushing off the chart success. Before the song reached number one, he had sarcastically challenged it to stay at the top for a full month and described the buzz around it as forced publicity.

Background

The feud between the two goes back to the period after the death of singer Mohbad in 2023, when VDM publicly pressured Zlatan and other music figures to speak out. More recently, VDM criticised Zlatan's high-profile wedding to Davita, claiming it was used to promote an album that did not do well. Zlatan has rarely responded directly, which is why many fans see 'Inferno' as his long-awaited reply.

How people reacted

Many users mocked VDM's response, with some calling it damage control, while his supporters said the song's success would not last. VDM has been involved in several online disputes this week, including with businessman Blord, former BBNaija winner Phyna and Davido's camp.

South-East governors urge region to back Tinubu at APC women's rally in Enugu

1 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

The governors of Enugu, Imo and Ebonyi, and a representative of Anambra's governor, have reportedly urged South-East voters to support President Tinubu's re-election at an APC women's rally in Enugu. The call sets up a direct contest with Peter Obi in his home region.

Read more

Four South-East state governments have thrown their weight behind President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid, in a show of support in a region widely seen as Peter Obi's stronghold.

What happened

At an APC Women Mega Rally and Empowerment Programme for the South-East at Okpara Square, Enugu, on Saturday, 31 October 2026, the governors reportedly urged residents to back the President and APC candidates at all levels in the January 2027 elections.

What the governors said

Enugu Governor Peter Mbah said the region should build on the government's economic reforms rather than change course, comparing it to a sailor who does not abandon his ship mid-sea. He also described an empowerment programme for 5,000 women across the five South-East states. Imo Governor Hope Uzodimma said Igbo people were now seeing real inclusion and development under Tinubu, and that improved federal allocations had helped his state pay salaries and pensions regularly. Ebonyi Governor Francis Nwifuru praised the APC's skills programmes and denied links to any coalition outside the party. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo was represented by his deputy, Gilbert Ibezim, who said the region should repay the administration with votes. Deputy Speaker Benjamin Kalu also spoke, noting that no Igbo person had been president in six decades and praising the South East Development Commission.

Background

Peter Obi, the NDC presidential candidate and a former Anambra governor, won most of the South East in the 2023 presidential election. He has said he would serve only one term if elected, while some APC figures argue the region should aim for a longer stay in power through alliances. Obi had not responded to the rally at the time of the report.

What happens next

Both the APC and the NDC are expected to intensify their campaigns in the South East, which is likely to be one of the most closely watched regions in the election.
