Davido becomes face of PUBG Mobile's new 'Flash' game across Africa

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Davido has signed on as the face of PUBG Mobile's new campaign across Africa, taking his brand into the continent's fast-growing gaming market.

The deal

The singer announced the partnership on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, sharing a promotional video in which he tells fans he is joining the battle royale game and that they do not need an expensive phone to play. He also invited fans to unlock an exclusive in-game 'King Set' outfit inspired by him.

The game

PUBG Mobile Flash, which launched on Friday, 13 November, is a lighter edition of the popular shooting game with a smaller download size, designed to run on a wider range of phones while keeping the main maps and gameplay. The campaign is aimed at expanding the game's audience in Africa, where many players use budget smartphones.

Background

Davido is one of Africa's biggest music stars and has a long list of brand partnerships. He has been in Tanzania this week, where he shot a project with Tanzanian star Diamond Platnumz.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

More nominations

Tems also picked up nominations for Best African Music Performance for 'Big Daddy', and Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song for 'What You Need'. Ayra Starr is nominated for Best African Music Performance for 'Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)', featuring Angélique Kidjo.

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.

Peter Obi says his US trip was not to seek American endorsement

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has dismissed reports that he went to Washington to seek the backing of US lawmakers for 2027. He said his meetings focused on Nigeria's democracy and development.

Read more

Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) presidential candidate Peter Obi has dismissed reports that his recent trip to Washington, DC, was aimed at winning the backing of United States lawmakers for his 2027 bid.

What Obi said

In a statement on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, the former Anambra governor said media interpretations of his meetings with US lawmakers and policy figures had been inaccurate. He said he did not ask for an endorsement and did not present the meetings as formal support from the US Congress or any individual legislator.

He explained that the purpose of the visit was to exchange views with congressional leaders and other American stakeholders on Nigeria's development, the welfare of its citizens and, above all, the strengthening of its democracy. He said the talks focused on democratic institutions, accountability and making sure elections are free, fair and credible.

His advice to others

Obi encouraged other Nigerian politicians to hold similar international discussions on governance, and urged that public debate on Nigeria's democratic future should not be reduced to claims of foreign endorsement.

Background

Claims that the US Congress had endorsed Obi circulated online after the trip, and a US lobbyist also commented on the matter.
