Davido becomes face of PUBG Mobile's new 'Flash' game across Africa

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Davido has signed on as the face of PUBG Mobile's new campaign across Africa, taking his brand into the continent's fast-growing gaming market.

The deal

The singer announced the partnership on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, sharing a promotional video in which he tells fans he is joining the battle royale game and that they do not need an expensive phone to play. He also invited fans to unlock an exclusive in-game 'King Set' outfit inspired by him.

The game

PUBG Mobile Flash, which launched on Friday, 13 November, is a lighter edition of the popular shooting game with a smaller download size, designed to run on a wider range of phones while keeping the main maps and gameplay. The campaign is aimed at expanding the game's audience in Africa, where many players use budget smartphones.

Background

Davido is one of Africa's biggest music stars and has a long list of brand partnerships. He has been in Tanzania this week, where he shot a project with Tanzanian star Diamond Platnumz.

  • Davido is the face of PUBG Mobile Flash in Africa.
  • The lighter game works on budget phones.
  • Fans can unlock a Davido 'King Set'.

KWAM 1 backs Tinubu's fuel subsidy removal at APC campaign event

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Fuji star Wasiu Ayinde, KWAM 1, has defended President Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy, saying it mainly benefited a small group rather than ordinary Nigerians.

Read more

Veteran Fuji musician Wasiu Ayinde, popularly known as KWAM 1, has thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. He questioned who had really benefited from the subsidy, saying a small group of people had gained from it while ordinary Nigerians saw little benefit.

Background

The petrol subsidy was removed when Tinubu took office in 2023, leading to a sharp rise in fuel prices.

The campaign

His comments came during the APC's campaign tour of the South-West, which included a presidential rally in Ibadan.

Davido celebrates early birthday on private jet, announces startup fund for young entrepreneurs

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Davido's team threw him a surprise party aboard his father's private jet ahead of his birthday on 21 November. The singer also announced that his foundation will give startup funds to Nigerians with business ideas.

Read more

Afrobeats star Davido has kicked off his birthday celebrations early, with a surprise party on board his father's private jet and a new initiative to support young Nigerian entrepreneurs.

Party in the sky

On Friday, 20 November 2026, his team reportedly surprised him with a celebration on the jet. Videos shared online showed Davido seated beside his wife, Chioma, as members of his 30BG crew sang Happy Birthday. The singer, in a playful mood, licked icing off the cake before cutting it, as champagne and finger food were served.

Startup funding

In a post on Instagram the same day, Davido announced that his foundation would expand its usual birthday charity work by providing startup funds to aspiring entrepreneurs, giving them a chance to turn their ideas into businesses, ahead of his birthday on Saturday, 21 November.

Background

His birthday this year comes amid debate over his recent photo with Seyi Tinubu, which VeryDarkMan criticised.

Turning 34

On Saturday, 21 November, Davido turned 34. His wife Chioma marked the day by sharing a childhood photo of him on her Instagram story with a short, affectionate caption, which the singer reposted.

Chioma's tribute

Later on his birthday, Chioma posted a longer tribute on Instagram calling Davido her forever home, alongside never-before-seen photos from her pregnancy and birthday wishes from their twins.

Aisha Buhari seen in Tinubu-branded outfit, days after claim she backed Atiku

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari wearing attire printed with President Tinubu's campaign logo has sparked debate. It came days after ADC chieftain Solomon Dalung claimed she had told Atiku that Buhari's supporters would back him.

Read more

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari, widow of the late President Muhammadu Buhari, wearing clothing printed with President Bola Tinubu's campaign logo has stirred political debate ahead of the 2027 elections.

The video

The clip was posted on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by former presidential aide Bashir Ahmad, with a caption describing it as a message from Daura, Buhari's hometown.

Why it matters

The video came days after Solomon Dalung, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), claimed in a television interview that Aisha Buhari had visited ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar and told him that the late President's large voter base would be backing him in 2027.

Many social media users took the new video as a response to that claim, suggesting the former First Lady is supporting Tinubu.

No statement

Aisha Buhari herself had not made a public statement on her political preference at the time of the reports.
