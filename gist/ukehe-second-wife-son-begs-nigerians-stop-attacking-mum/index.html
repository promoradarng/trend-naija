"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Dangote Refinery considers bigger IPO after ₦6 trillion in subscriptions

18 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Dangote Petroleum Refinery is reportedly considering enlarging its share sale by up to 30% after investors put in nearly ₦6 trillion. That could take the offer to about $2.1 billion, one of Africa's largest.

Read more

Dangote Petroleum Refinery is reportedly considering expanding its initial public offering (IPO) by as much as 30%, after strong investor demand pushed subscriptions to nearly ₦6 trillion, or about $4.5 billion.

The numbers

The refinery, owned by billionaire Aliko Dangote, initially planned to sell 4.1 billion shares. Demand is said to have exceeded the shares on offer by nearly three times, prompting talks about increasing the allocation.

If the expansion goes ahead, the value of the share sale could rise to about $2.1 billion, potentially making it one of the biggest stock market offerings on the continent.

Why it matters

The refinery is the largest in Africa, and its pricing decisions have recently driven changes in pump prices across Nigeria.

Air Peace offers ₦80,000 domestic fares to serving and retired military personnel

Air Peace chairman Allen Onyema has announced a special ₦80,000 one-way domestic fare for serving and retired members of the armed forces. He said it recognises their sacrifices for the country.

Read more

Air Peace has announced a special one-way domestic fare of ₦80,000 for serving and retired military personnel.

The announcement

The airline's chairman, Allen Onyema, disclosed the initiative on Monday, 16 November 2026, while delivering a lecture at the Chief of Defence Intelligence Annual Conference in Abuja.

He said the discounted fare would apply across all of Air Peace's domestic routes, and would cover serving officers and veterans who have served Nigeria since independence in 1960.

Why the airline is doing it

Onyema said the initiative was meant to recognise the sacrifices made by members of the armed forces in protecting the country.

Lookman faces backlash over video with Burna Boy on night of Tanzania draw

18 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Fans have criticised Ademola Lookman after a video reportedly showed him with Burna Boy in Lagos on the evening the Super Eagles drew with Tanzania. He had left the squad with a knee injury.

Read more

Super Eagles forward Ademola Lookman has come under fire from Nigerian fans after a video reportedly showed him socialising with Burna Boy in Lagos on the same evening the national team drew 0-0 with Tanzania.

Why fans are angry

Lookman had been excused from the squad before the second leg in Zanzibar, with the team citing a knee injury he picked up in the first leg in Uyo on 13 November. He was said to have been sent back to his club, Atletico Madrid, so its medical staff could assess the problem.

Many fans questioned why he appeared to be in Lagos instead, and criticised him for being seen out while the team struggled.

The bigger picture

The draw left Nigeria on seven points, three behind Guinea-Bissau, putting their AFCON 2027 qualification hopes under serious pressure.

No response yet

Lookman, the 2024 CAF Men's Player of the Year, had not publicly responded to the criticism at the time of the reports.
