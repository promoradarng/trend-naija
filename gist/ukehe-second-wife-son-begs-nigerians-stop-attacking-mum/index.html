"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Suspected insurgents bomb power lines linking Kainji Dam to Jebba

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Suspected Boko Haram insurgents reportedly blew up two electricity transmission towers connecting the Kainji Dam power plant to Jebba in Niger State. No lives were lost, but the attack raised fears over the safety of power infrastructure.

Read more

Suspected Boko Haram insurgents have reportedly bombed two electricity transmission towers connecting the Kainji Dam power station to Jebba in Niger State.

What happened

The explosions reportedly happened at about 2pm on 17 November 2026, between Kanti community in Mashegu Local Government Area and Kanya community in Mokwa Local Government Area. The attackers are said to have targeted the transmission infrastructure, raising concerns that the Kainji Dam power plant itself could be at risk.

No lives were lost, according to the reports, but the blasts caused panic in nearby communities. Security monitoring has continued in the area.

Why it matters

Kainji and Jebba are hydropower stations that feed the national grid.

Background

The incident follows a series of attacks on and vandalism of electricity infrastructure across the country. This month, the House of Representatives resolved to investigate the destruction of 10 transmission towers within five months.

Nigeria begins building $70m passport factory, local production from May 2027

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

The federal government has started building a $70 million passport production plant in Abuja. Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo says it will start producing passport booklets locally from May 2027.

Read more

Nigeria has started building a $70 million passport production plant in Abuja, as the federal government moves to end its reliance on foreign suppliers for passport booklets.

The announcement

Minister of Interior Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo announced the project at a groundbreaking ceremony at the headquarters of the Nigeria Immigration Service. He described it as a major step towards a self-sufficient passport system and said local production should begin by May 2027.

The plant

The facility is being built in partnership with IRIS Smart Technologies and will initially produce six million electronic passport booklets a year, with capacity that could later rise to 10 million.

Why it matters

The plant is meant to end Nigeria's reliance on foreign suppliers for passport booklets.

Femi Branch opens up on two years out of work after on-set accident

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Actor Femi Branch says a stunt that went wrong on a film set in Ikorodu left him unable to work for two years, forcing him to sell his late mother's car. He said some colleagues abandoned him.

Read more

Nollywood actor Femi Branch has opened up about a difficult period in his life after a serious accident on a film set left him unable to work for two years.

The accident

In an interview that surfaced online on Friday, 20 November 2026, Branch said he was injured while shooting a stunt in Ikorodu, Lagos. He said he had to jump from a storey building while dressed as a security guard, and the impact when he landed was so severe it sounded like an explosion.

Hard times

He said the injury kept him out of work for two years, leaving him isolated and in financial difficulty. He revealed that he had to sell his mother's car during that period, and said some colleagues deserted him while he was recovering.
