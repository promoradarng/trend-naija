"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Popular social media personality GehGeh has declared strong support for Peter Obi ahead of the 2027 presidential election, saying Obi stands apart because of the genuine love and confidence his supporters have in him.

"Genuine love and belief"

According to GehGeh, many Nigerians backing Obi, the NDC presidential candidate, do so because they believe he is qualified, capable and able to lead the country in a better direction.

His declaration also raises a fair question. Is support for Obi genuinely different from that for other politicians, or are Nigerians simply becoming more emotionally attached to their preferred candidates? Supporters of every major candidate would argue that their backing is sincere.

Copyright Commission takes legal action against NetNaija, MovieBox over piracy

14 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Nigerian Copyright Commission says it has begun legal action against operators of platforms including NetNaija and MovieBox over alleged copyright violations, warning that sites distributing protected content without permission could be shut down.

Read more

The Nigerian Copyright Commission (NCC) says it has started legal action against the operators of several online platforms, including NetNaija and MovieBox, over alleged copyright infringement.

What the commission said

Speaking on Thursday, 12 November 2026, at a copyright sensitisation programme at the University of Ibadan, Barrister Kunle Olatunji, deputy director of the commission's Regulatory and Compliance Department, said the action was part of efforts to protect authors, publishers, filmmakers and other creators whose work is used without permission. He said the Copyright Act 2022 had widened the commission's powers to tackle online piracy, allowing it to seek the closure of entire websites rather than just the removal of individual pirated items.

Why it matters

Olatunji said the law protects the rights of creative professionals whose works are exploited without their consent.

Background

The allegations against the platforms have not been tested in court, and their operators had not publicly responded at the time of writing.
