"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Delta man presumed dead after accident reportedly wakes up in mortuary

15 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

A man presumed dead after a motorcycle accident near Evwreni in Delta State reportedly woke up in a mortuary, sparking celebrations by residents in a video that has gone viral.

Read more

A man who was presumed dead after an accident in Delta State has reportedly woken up in a mortuary.

What happened

According to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026, the man was involved in a motorcycle accident near Evwreni and was taken to a mortuary after being presumed dead. He is said to have later woken up there. A video shared online shows residents celebrating his survival. Voices in the video say that when he woke up, the first thing he asked for was a cigarette and a lighter.

Celebrations

Residents rubbed white powder on their faces and bodies, a traditional sign of celebration often used at a child's birth, and also applied it to the survivor. Others shared snacks with passers-by to mark the occasion, while some urged him to go for a proper medical check-up.

Background

It is not clear who declared the man dead or whether he was examined by a doctor before being taken to the mortuary. The authorities had not commented on the incident at the time of writing.

Labour Party factional candidate Kennedy Ahanotu quits presidential race, backs Tinubu

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Kennedy Ahanotu, presidential candidate of the Abure-led Labour Party faction, has reportedly withdrawn from the 2027 race and endorsed President Tinubu. He blamed the party's long-running internal crisis.

Read more

Kennedy Ahanotu, who was chosen as presidential candidate by the Julius Abure-led faction of the Labour Party, has reportedly withdrawn from the 2027 presidential race and thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu.

What happened

Ahanotu is said to have announced the decision at a press briefing in Abuja on Monday, 16 November 2026. He said he would work with the City Boy Movement, a pro-Tinubu group, to mobilise support for the president's re-election.

The 42-year-old former national youth leader of the Labour Party, described as an ally of the party's 2023 candidate Peter Obi, reportedly said the move followed consultations with youth leaders from across the country, who concluded that the party's continuing internal crisis made a new direction necessary.

Background

Ahanotu was adopted as the factional candidate at primaries held in Abuja in May 2026, when the Labour Party was divided between the Abure-led group and the leadership headed by Senator Nenadi Usman. Peter Obi has since moved on to the NDC, where he is running with Rabiu Kwankwaso.

Betrayal claims

Responding to suggestions that he had betrayed the opposition, Ahanotu reportedly argued that it was the party that had failed its long-standing members, noting that he had joined it in 2016.

No response from either Labour Party faction was included in the reports.

Sanwo-Olu gives Flamingos ₦10m each and iPhones after U-17 World Cup win

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has reportedly rewarded the Flamingos with ₦10 million each, iPhone 18 Pro Max phones and other gifts. It follows earlier rewards from President Tinubu and Senate President Akpabio.

Read more

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has reportedly rewarded the Flamingos, Nigeria's under-17 women's team, with ₦10 million each and other gifts after their historic win at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The reward

The governor announced the package at a dinner held in honour of the players and technical crew at Lagos House, Marina, on Sunday. It is said to include a total cash reward of ₦300 million, iPhone 18 Pro Max phones, headphones and customised outfits.

Other rewards

The gesture follows President Bola Tinubu's announcement of ₦100 million for each player and for head coach Akeem Busari, with other members of the technical crew promised ₦50 million each. The presidential reward was announced by First Lady Oluremi Tinubu when she hosted the team in Abuja. Senate President Godswill Akpabio also announced a ₦100 million donation to the team after their return.

The historic win

The Flamingos made history on 7 November by beating Japan 2-1 in Rabat, becoming the first African team to win the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.

Pope Leo hit in the eye by rosary thrown from crowd in Peru

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Pope Leo XIV was reportedly struck in the eye by a rosary thrown from the crowd as he rode through Lima in the Popemobile. He was on the last day of a 12-day South America tour.

Read more

Pope Leo XIV was reportedly hit in the eye by a rosary thrown by a worshipper as he toured Lima, Peru, on Monday, 16 November 2026.

What happened

The Pope was riding through the city in the Popemobile when the rosary, thrown from the crowd, struck him. Video of the moment shows him holding and rubbing his eye. There was no report of serious injury, and he continued to a farewell Mass at Las Palmas air base, which was said to have drawn around one million people.

A special visit

The trip marked the final day of a 12-day tour of South America, six days of which he spent in Peru. The 71-year-old has deep ties to the country. Born in Chicago, he arrived in Peru in 1985 as a 29-year-old Augustinian missionary and spent many years there, including a decade in the northern city of Chiclayo, where he was known as Padre Roberto.

He is the first American pope and the first to hold Peruvian citizenship. Tens of thousands of Peruvians lined the streets on Monday, many carrying banners in his honour.
