"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

VeryDarkMan accuses Davido of hypocrisy over photo with Seyi Tinubu

19 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan has called out Davido over a photo with Seyi Tinubu in which both appeared to raise their hands in a gesture he read as the APC's "4+4" slogan. He said it contradicts the singer's past stance against the APC.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has reportedly called out Afrobeats star Davido after a photo of the singer with Seyi Tinubu, son of President Bola Tinubu, circulated online.

The photo

The picture showed Davido and Seyi Tinubu raising their hands in a gesture VDM interpreted as the APC's "4+4" slogan, which supporters use to call for a second term for President Tinubu in 2027.

What VDM said

VDM said the photo was significant given Davido's status and his previous statements that he would never support the All Progressives Congress (APC). He accused the singer of contradicting himself and questioned why he would pose in a picture that could be read as a political statement.

He also said that his past claims always end up being proved right, dismissing what he called propaganda against him.

Background

Davido had not responded to VDM's comments at the time of the reports.

Tems opens up about living with ADHD

19 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Grammy-winning singer Tems has spoken about how ADHD affects her relationships, saying she finds it hard to keep in touch with people and sometimes switches topics mid-conversation.

Read more

Nigerian singer Temilade Openiyi, known as Tems, has opened up about living with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and how it affects her relationships with family and friends.

What she said

In a radio interview in Lagos, the singer said keeping in touch with people is something she finds difficult, even though she has many supportive people around her.

She explained that ADHD sometimes makes her switch topics suddenly during conversations, and that she often forgets to reach out to people. She admitted she has not always been consistent in communicating with people who matter to her, but said she is trying.

Background

Tems leads the Nigerian nominees for the 2027 Grammy Awards with four nominations.

Enugu businessman Festus Oshaba-Onuh reportedly dies in road crash

19 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Billionaire businessman Chief Festus Oshaba-Onuh, CEO of Ferotex Group, has reportedly died in a crash on the Enugu-Port Harcourt Expressway. The business community in Enugu is in mourning.

Read more

The business community in Enugu State has been thrown into mourning following reports that billionaire businessman Chief Festus Oshaba-Onuh has died in a road accident.

What happened

Oshaba-Onuh, the chief executive of Ferotex Group and popularly known as Ferotex, reportedly died on the morning of Thursday, 19 November 2026, in a crash on the Enugu-Port Harcourt Expressway. He was said to be travelling back to Enugu at the time.

How the news spread

News of his death emerged after an Enugu-based social media influencer shared a video of the crash scene, showing a damaged SUV said to have been involved. The influencer described the death as a major loss to Enugu State and beyond.

No family statement yet

His family had not issued a public statement at the time of the reports.

Former Big Brother Naija housemate Saskay has called out President Bola Tinubu after she said she missed her flight because of a road closure near the airport.

In a post on X, the reality TV star said security operatives blocked a section of the airport road because the President was attending a conference nearby. She said the restriction left many motorists stuck in traffic for hours, causing her and several other travellers to miss their flights.

Her complaint

Visibly frustrated, Saskay questioned why ordinary citizens going about their business should be inconvenienced in this way.

No response yet

There was no official response to her complaint in the reports.
