"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Kano court jails TikToker five months and orders 10 strokes for insulting Hisbah commander

19 Nov 2026, 06:41 GMT

A Shariah court in Kano has reportedly sentenced a 19-year-old TikTok user to five months in prison and 10 strokes of the cane after she pleaded guilty to insulting the Hisbah Board and its commander. Two other defendants pleaded not guilty.

Read more

A Shariah court in Kano State has reportedly sentenced a 19-year-old TikTok user, Zainab Abubakar, to five months in prison and 10 strokes of the cane for insulting the state Hisbah Board and its commander, Malam Aminu Ibrahim Daurawa.

The judgment

The court, sitting at the Hisbah Board headquarters in Sharada, delivered the judgment on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, after she pleaded guilty to the charge under Section 312 of the Penal Code. She was sentenced without the option of a fine.

The other defendants

She was arraigned alongside two young men who were also arrested by the Hisbah over allegations of using TikTok to insult the agency and its commander. Both pleaded not guilty to charges of conspiracy and insulting the board. The court remanded them in custody for two weeks while it considers their bail application and directed the prosecution to prepare its witnesses.

Background

The Kano Hisbah is the state's religious police, which enforces Islamic moral codes. Earlier this week, TikTokers were reported to have been arrested over videos said to have insulted its commander.

FG, states and councils share ₦2.44 trillion October revenue

19 Nov 2026, 06:41 GMT

The Federation Account Allocation Committee has shared ₦2.441 trillion from October 2026 revenue among the federal, state and local governments. Both oil-related revenue and VAT dipped slightly from September.

Read more

The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) has shared ₦2.441 trillion from October 2026 revenue among the federal government, states and local government councils.

The breakdown

According to a statement on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, from the Office of the Accountant-General of the Federation, the pool was made up of ₦1.688 trillion in statutory revenue and ₦752.488 billion from Value Added Tax (VAT).

The federal government received ₦856.065 billion, the states ₦809.909 billion and local government councils ₦568.701 billion. A further ₦206.384 billion made up the rest of the pool.

Revenue dips

Total gross revenue for October was ₦3.811 trillion. Gross statutory revenue fell by about ₦97 billion to ₦2.999 trillion, compared with September, while gross VAT revenue dipped slightly to ₦812.446 billion. Around ₦128.8 billion was deducted as cost of collection and ₦1.241 trillion for transfers, refunds and savings.

Content creator and actor Debo Adedayo, known as Mr Macaroni, has opened up about why he is scared of marriage.

What he said

Speaking on the In My Opinion podcast, he reportedly said he has been single for so long that he no longer knows how to be in a relationship, including the constant calls and being present for a partner.

He revealed that there is a friend he genuinely loves and admires, but he is unsure he could commit to her fully. He said part of him wants a relationship, but some days he just wants to be alone, and it would be unfair to profess love to someone if he could not commit all the way.

Honest admission

Background
