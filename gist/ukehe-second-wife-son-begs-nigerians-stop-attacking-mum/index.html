"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Emergency number 112 fails to connect live on TV after APC official says it works

11 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

A member of the APC presidential campaign council, Alwan Hassan, dared a TV anchor to call the national emergency number 112 live on air to prove it works, but the call failed to connect, sparking reactions online.

A live television debate took an awkward turn on Tuesday night, 10 November 2026, when Nigeria's national emergency number failed to connect after a ruling party official insisted it worked.

What happened

Speaking on a Channels Television programme to highlight the government's achievements, Alwan Hassan, a member of the APC presidential campaign council, said the 112 emergency response system had been upgraded and worked reliably nationwide. To prove his point, he dared the anchor, Seun Okinbaloye, to call the number live on air. The anchor accepted, dialled 112 on his phone, but the call did not go through.

Background

The 112 toll-free number is meant to connect people to police, fire, ambulance and other emergency services across Nigeria. Emergency response became a talking point last week when actor Kunle Afod complained about the response to a crash on the Lagos–Ibadan Expressway.

INEC asks court to cancel subpoena for Tinubu's 1999 and 2023 election records

11 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

INEC has asked the Federal High Court in Abuja to set aside a subpoena ordering its chairman to appear and produce President Tinubu's 1999 and 2023 nomination documents in the trial of activist Omoyele Sowore.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has asked the Federal High Court in Abuja to set aside a subpoena requiring its chairman to produce President Bola Tinubu's election documents.

The application

INEC's lawyer, Alex Izinyon, a Senior Advocate of Nigeria, filed the motion on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, the day before INEC chairman Professor Joash Amupitan was due to appear as a defence witness in the criminal trial of activist Omoyele Sowore. The subpoena, issued by Justice Mohammed Umar, had directed the INEC chairman to produce Tinubu's nomination forms from the 1999 Lagos governorship election, including an affidavit relating to lost certificates, and from the 2023 presidential election.

The case

Sowore is being prosecuted by the State Security Service over social media posts in which he described the President as a criminal. His defence team asked the court to call the INEC chairman and Joe Igbokwe, a former APC publicity secretary in Lagos, as witnesses.

Background

Questions about the President's academic records have been raised by critics for years and were the subject of legal challenges after the 2023 election, which the Supreme Court dismissed. The President and his supporters have consistently rejected claims of wrongdoing.

Suspended Reform UK councillor convicted over post saying Nigerians should be 'melted down'

11 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Glenn Gibbins, a suspended Reform UK councillor, has been found guilty of sending a malicious communication after a Facebook post saying Nigerians should be melted down and used to fill potholes.

Read more

A suspended Reform UK councillor in England has been convicted over a social media post about Nigerians.

The case

Glenn Gibbins, 66, was found guilty at South Tyneside Magistrates' Court of sending a malicious communication, according to reports on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. In a Facebook post, he had complained about the number of Nigerians in the town and said they should be melted down and used to fill potholes. He told the court on Monday that the post was 'naughty' but that he did not mean it literally. The prosecutor said the comment conjured up despicable images and implied that Nigerians were less than human.

How it came to light

The post was found by the anti-racism group Hope Not Hate when it reviewed Gibbins's social media after he was announced as a Reform UK candidate for Sunderland City Council. He was later suspended by the party.

Background

Reform UK is led by Nigel Farage. Nigerians make up one of the largest African communities in the UK.

Abia APC chairman urges Igbo voters to back Tinubu, says 2023 approach was an error

11 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Abia APC chairman Chijioke Chukwu says it is wrong to believe the Igbo are not supporting President Tinubu, and urged them not to repeat what he called the political error of 2023, when many voted for Peter Obi.

Read more

The chairman of the All Progressives Congress (APC) in Abia State, Chijioke Chukwu, has called on Igbo people to rally behind President Bola Tinubu in 2027.

What he said

In a Facebook post on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Chukwu said it was wrong to believe that the Igbo were not supporting Tinubu and were backing Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC). He argued that many Igbo voters acted out of political naivety in 2023 by believing that their numbers alone could produce a president of Igbo origin without building alliances with other regions, and warned against repeating that approach.

Background

The debate over how the Igbo should vote in 2027 has intensified this week. Senator Victor Umeh of the NDC said any Igbo person who works against Obi is an enemy of the Igbo, a remark criticised by actor Yul Edochie. Several South-East governors have declared support for Tinubu, including Ebonyi's Francis Nwifuru, who promised him 80 per cent of the state's votes, while Abia's governor, Alex Otti, was elected on the Labour Party platform.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
