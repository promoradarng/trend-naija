"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Omotola Jalade opens up on difficult bond with late mother and Nollywood blacklist

28 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Veteran actress Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde has spoken about her strained relationship with her late mother and her regrets since her death. She also said she was once blacklisted in Nollywood for demanding better standards.

Read more

Veteran Nollywood actress Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde has spoken openly about her complicated relationship with her late mother and about being blacklisted in the film industry early in her career.

A difficult bond

In an interview released on Friday, 27 November 2026, Omotola said she and her mother did not always get along. Her mother worried that the film industry would expose her daughter to immorality, and the tension grew when Omotola began dating her husband, Captain Matthew Ekeinde. When he visited to formally ask for her hand, her mother reportedly questioned whether her daughter was ready for marriage.

Omotola said the relationship later improved and the two became much closer. She admitted that she still has regrets about the care and support she never got to show her mother, and said she now finds herself giving that care to other people's mothers. She added that the experience shaped her decision to make her directorial debut.

'Troublesome Eight'

Turning to her career, the actress said she was blacklisted after pushing for better standards and professionalism in Nollywood. She said she was one of a group of actors labelled the 'Troublesome Eight' because they demanded improvements. She also said she had a manager as far back as the 1990s, when professional management was not widely understood in the industry.

Motherhood

The mother of four also spoke about her approach to parenting and the lessons motherhood has taught her.

Obi, Makinde and Melaye join hands at Dunamis prayer night for 2027 polls

28 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Peter Obi, Seyi Makinde and Dino Melaye reportedly attended an all-night service at Dunamis church in Abuja, where Pastor Paul Enenche led prayers for the 2027 elections. Representatives of the APC and PDP were also called to the altar.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi, Oyo State governor and APM candidate Seyi Makinde, and ADC chieftain Dino Melaye were reportedly among politicians at an all-night service at the Dunamis International Gospel Centre in Abuja on Friday, 27 November 2026.

Prayers at the altar

The service was part of the church's 30th anniversary celebrations. At about 1am on Saturday, founder Paul Enenche reportedly invited the three politicians to the altar for a prayer session focused on Nigeria and the 2027 general election. They were joined by an aide representing Delta State governor Sheriff Oborevwori, who stood in for the APC, and a church member affiliated with the PDP.

With representatives of five parties at the altar, Enenche asked them to hold hands as he led the congregation in prayer. The prayers reportedly asked for God's will to prevail in Nigeria and included calls directed at INEC ahead of the polls.

Background

The presidential and National Assembly elections are scheduled for 16 January 2027. The service came as opposition figures prepare for a summit in Abuja on 30 November.

Nollywood actress Rita Edochie has reportedly opened up about the toll that veteran actor Pete Edochie's death has taken on her family, especially her husband, Tony Edochie.

What she said

In an Instagram post, Rita thanked fans and well-wishers for their support, but said the period had been very difficult behind closed doors. She said Tony, Pete's younger brother, who was very close to him, is not finding it easy, but has chosen to accept the loss as the will of God.

Background

Pete Edochie, one of Nigeria's best-known actors and broadcasters, died on 25 November 2026 at the age of 79 in Enugu. Tributes have poured in from across Nollywood, including from his son Yul Edochie and actress Patience Ozokwo.
