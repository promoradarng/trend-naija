"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Giant Tinubu campaign balloon spotted floating in Lagos lagoon

13 Nov 2026, 15:50 GMT

A video of a large Tinubu 2027 campaign balloon drifting loose in the Lagos lagoon beneath the Third Mainland Bridge has gone viral, with comedian Carter Efe's reaction drawing laughs online.

A large inflatable balloon promoting President Bola Tinubu's 2027 re-election campaign has been spotted floating loose in the Lagos lagoon.

The video

A short clip shared on X on Friday, 13 November 2026, shows the oversized balloon, bearing Tinubu's image, the All Progressives Congress (APC) colours and campaign branding, drifting in the water beside the Third Mainland Bridge. Comedian and content creator Carter Efe, who filmed it from his car, reacted with surprise, and his response quickly became the highlight of the clip for many viewers.

Background

Large inflatable balloons and outdoor displays have been part of the APC's early campaign push in Lagos and other cities ahead of the 2027 elections. It is not clear how the balloon ended up in the lagoon. The APC had not commented at the time of writing.

Ronaldinho set for competitive comeback at 46 with Italian club Ravenna

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Brazilian legend Ronaldinho is set to start for Serie C club Ravenna against Forli on Sunday, his first competitive match in more than a decade, the club's manager has confirmed.

Read more

Former Barcelona, PSG and AC Milan star Ronaldinho is set to return to competitive football at the age of 46.

The comeback

Ravenna manager Andrea Mandorlini confirmed on Friday, 13 November 2026, that the Brazilian will start for the Italian Serie C club in a local derby against Forli on Sunday, 15 November, as one of three forwards. Mandorlini described him as one of the greatest players of the modern era, adding that the coaching staff would decide together how long he plays.

A long wait

Ronaldinho joined Ravenna as an investor in August. His last competitive appearance was for Brazilian club Fluminense in September 2015, making Sunday's match his first in more than ten years.

Background

Ronaldinho won the Ballon d'Or in 2005 and the World Cup with Brazil in 2002, and is remembered as one of the most gifted and entertaining players of his generation.

Portugal retires Ronaldo's number 7 shirt design after end of his international career

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Portugal's football federation has permanently changed the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt in honour of Cristiano Ronaldo, whose international career has ended, and players will all wear 7 during their walk-out against Denmark.

Read more

Portugal's football federation has honoured Cristiano Ronaldo by permanently changing the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt.

The tribute

The Portuguese Football Federation announced on X that the style of number 7 worn by Ronaldo will not be used by any other player, and a new version will appear on future shirts. According to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026, every Portugal player will wear a number 7 shirt during the walk-out before their Nations League match against Denmark as a collective farewell.

How it ended

Ronaldo's international career reportedly came to an end after a public disagreement with Jorge Jesus over a substitution during a match in September.

Background

Ronaldo is Portugal's all-time record goalscorer and most-capped player and led the country to the Euro 2016 title. The gesture mirrors the honour Argentina gave Lionel Messi after his international retirement.

Oyo governorship candidate Oriyomi Hamzat says he backs Tinubu for 2027

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Broadcaster Oriyomi Hamzat, the Accord Party's governorship candidate in Oyo State, says he will support President Tinubu in 2027 rather than Governor Seyi Makinde or Peter Obi.

Read more

Oriyomi Hamzat, the Accord Party's governorship candidate in Oyo State, has declared support for President Bola Tinubu's re-election in 2027.

What he said

Speaking to journalists on Saturday, 14 November 2026, on the sidelines of the University of Ibadan's convocation, where he received a master's degree in peace and conflict resolution, the Ibadan-based broadcaster said he would not act against the President, citing loyalty. His comments came as voters in the South-West weigh three prominent southern presidential candidates: Tinubu, Oyo governor Seyi Makinde of the APM and Peter Obi of the NDC.

Background

Hamzat is a popular radio presenter in Ibadan. He is running to succeed Makinde, who is in his second and final term and is now challenging Tinubu for the presidency. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
