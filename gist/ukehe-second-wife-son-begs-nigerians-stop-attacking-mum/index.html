"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Tinubu endorses APC governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos at Ibadan rally, promises better power

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

At an APC rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, President Tinubu raised the hands of the party's governorship candidates for Oyo, Ogun and Lagos. He also guaranteed improved electricity and promised a factory in Ibadan.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos States at a campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The endorsements

Telling the crowd he would show them who to vote for as governor, Tinubu raised the hands of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC candidate in Oyo; Senator Solomon Adeola, known as Yayi, the party's candidate in Ogun; and Lagos Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, the APC flagbearer in Lagos. He also raised the hand of Remi Oseni, the party's senatorial candidate for Oyo South.

Promise on power

During the visit, the President also assured Nigerians of better electricity supply, saying the Minister of Power and his team were working hard. He added that the government would build a factory in Ibadan.

Context

The rally is part of the APC's campaign ahead of the 16 January 2027 elections.

The other side

Reactions from the opposition parties to the rally were not included in the reports.

Jimmy Odukoya sues sister and Fountain of Life trustees over his removal

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court in Lagos against his sister, Tolu Odukoya, and members of the Fountain of Life Church board over his removal as senior pastor.

Read more

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has gone to court over his removal as senior pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, filing a suit against his sister and several members of the church's board of trustees.

The suit

The suit was filed at the Federal High Court in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. Eight people are named as respondents, including his sister Tolu Odukoya, interim leader Pastor Rotimi Okpaise and other trustees.

The filing marks a significant escalation in the leadership dispute at the Ilupeju church founded by his late father, Taiwo Odukoya.

Background

Earlier this week, the trustees announced that Jimmy had been removed because his appointment did not reflect his father's succession wishes, and released a 2021 video in which the late founder reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his successor. Jimmy has insisted his removal is invalid. A Lagos State High Court had already granted the trustees an order barring him from acting as senior pastor while their own suit is heard.

What happens next

The court is expected to fix a date to hear the case. The trustees had not responded publicly to the new suit in the reports.

NELFUND reviewing ₦20,000 monthly upkeep for students

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Nigerian Education Loan Fund says it is reviewing the ₦20,000 monthly upkeep allowance it pays to student beneficiaries, citing economic pressures. MD Akin Sawyer said any increase must be carefully considered.

Read more

The Nigerian Education Loan Fund (NELFUND) is considering an increase to the ₦20,000 monthly upkeep allowance it pays to student beneficiaries.

What was said

NELFUND's Managing Director, Akin Sawyer, said in a television interview on Friday, 20 November 2026, that the allowance was being reviewed in light of the economic pressures facing students.

He said the matter is under constant review, but stressed that any increase would need careful consideration.

Background

NELFUND, set up under President Bola Tinubu's administration, gives loans to students in tertiary institutions and pays a monthly upkeep allowance to beneficiaries.
