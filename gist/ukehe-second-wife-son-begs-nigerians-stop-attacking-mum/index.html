"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum": Son of second wife accused of mistreating Mrs Anulika in Ukehe speaks out

10 Nov 2026, 10:13 GMT

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

The OK Movement, a support group named after the initials of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso, has reportedly withdrawn its campaign and mobilisation support from the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

The announcement

In a statement on Friday, 27 November 2026, the movement's national director-general, John Ughulu, said the group had been informed that Obi and the NDC had decided to cut ties with it. VeryDarkMan urges Obi, Atiku and Makinde to form one party to face Tinubu

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has called on Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to put their ambitions aside and unite under one party against President Tinubu. The three are due at an opposition summit on Monday.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one political party ahead of the 2027 election.

What he said

In a post on Instagram, VDM said the three should sit down together and form one party to defeat President Bola Tinubu. He argued that they need to set aside their personal ambitions, and that a united front would make the contest against the ruling party more competitive.

Background

Obi of the NDC, Atiku of the ADC and Makinde of the APM are expected at an opposition summit in Abuja on Monday, 30 November, where parties are due to sign a cooperation pact. The organisers have said it will not be a formal merger, and Makinde's camp has denied reports that he plans to step down for Atiku.
