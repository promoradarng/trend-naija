"Pharaoh, let my people go!": Cubana Chief Priest replies Umahi, accuses him of giving Tinubu fake promises on South-East votes

9 Nov 2026, 11:14 GMT

Socialite and businessman Cubana Chief Priest has replied Minister of Works David Umahi, a day after the minister mocked his famous "money na water" catchphrase and asked how many people he has given scholarships.

"Pharaoh, let my people go"

Borrowing from the Bible story of Moses and Pharaoh, Cubana Chief Priest gave the minister a new nickname, and accused him of making promises to President Bola Tinubu that he cannot deliver.

"Pharaoh, let my people go. Stop giving Tinubu fake promises on votes. What project are you using to convince South-East people to vote for him?" he said.

The governorship claim

He went further, claiming that Umahi is preparing his son to become governor of Ebonyi State after the current governor, Francis Nwifuru.

Speaking partly in Pidgin, he said, in effect: "You're busy preparing your son to be governor after Nwifuru. God won't let that happen. You want to use Ebonyi State to settle your child. Let the young man come and meet us on the street so we can teach him how to make 'money na water'."

This is Cubana Chief Priest's claim, and Umahi has not yet responded to this latest attack.

How it started

The exchange began weeks ago when Cubana Chief Priest described Umahi as the second-worst minister from the South-East. Umahi hit back by telling him he was "becoming very noisy", comparing him unfavourably with Dangote and Otedola, and asking, "How many people have you given scholarships?"

Now the socialite has turned the argument back to politics, questioning what Umahi has done to win South-East votes for the President.

Over to you

Who is winning this war of words: the minister or the socialite? And is Cubana Chief Priest right to question what the South-East has to show for supporting Tinubu?

Court freezes $100m linked to Governors' Forum in Paris Club fees dispute

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

An Abuja High Court has ordered the preservation of up to $100 million allegedly held by the Nigeria Governors' Forum, pending a dispute over legal and consultancy fees tied to Paris Club refunds. The order does not decide who owns the money.

Read more

A High Court of the Federal Capital Territory in Abuja has ordered the preservation of up to $100 million allegedly held by the Nigeria Governors' Forum (NGF), in a dispute over unpaid legal and consultancy fees connected to the Paris Club refunds.

The order

Justice O.C. Agbaza granted the interim freezing order on 19 November 2026, following an application by a lawyer against the NGF's incorporated trustees and 29 commercial banks. The order covers a single total sum of $100 million, about ₦135.6 billion, across the affected accounts.

What it means

The order preserves the disputed money until the court hears an application for an injunction or makes a further order. It does not decide who owns the funds.

The claim

The lawyer is seeking payment under an indemnity he says the NGF issued in December 2018, after the federal government paid $100 million to the forum to hold pending a decision on liability for professional fees. No response from the NGF was included in the reports.

Tinubu asks Sunday Igboho to deploy his security network in the South-West

23 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu reportedly asked activist Sunday Igboho to use his Iru Ekun Security Network to help protect lives and property in the South-West. The two met briefly in the president's car after the Ibadan rally.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has reportedly asked Yoruba nation activist Sunday Adeyemo, popularly known as Sunday Igboho, to deploy his Iru Ekun Security Network to help protect lives and property across South-West Nigeria.

The meeting

According to a statement released on Monday, 23 November 2026, by Igboho's media office in Ibadan, the two met briefly inside the president's official car after the APC presidential campaign rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, on Saturday.

What was discussed

The meeting is said to have focused on the work of Iru Ekun, a community security outfit founded by Igboho, and how its members could help reduce insecurity in rural and urban areas of the region.

Background

Igboho is a well-known campaigner for Yoruba self-determination. The meeting took place on the day of Tinubu's campaign rally in Ibadan, where the president also met traditional rulers.

Fani-Kayode denies being recalled as Nigeria's envoy to South Africa

23 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, has rejected social media claims that he was recalled or reprimanded. He said he was in Abuja for routine consultations.

Read more

Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, has dismissed reports on social media that the federal government had recalled or reprimanded him, describing them as completely unfounded.

What he said

Fani-Kayode made the clarification during a visit to Abuja, where he said he had been meeting officials at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs as part of routine diplomatic consultations. He said the talks focused on recent developments in South Africa and the direction of Nigeria's foreign policy on the continent.

He insisted that his presence in the capital had nothing to do with any disciplinary action.

Background

Fani-Kayode, a former aviation minister, is a vocal supporter of President Bola Tinubu.

Shettima says he will keep firing back at Atiku as long as he attacks Tinubu

24 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Vice President Kashim Shettima has reportedly said he will not stop his verbal exchanges with ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar while Atiku keeps targeting President Tinubu. He said he has never insulted Obi, Makinde, Kwankwaso or Amaechi.

Read more

Vice President Kashim Shettima has reportedly said he will continue to trade words with Atiku Abubakar, the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate, for as long as Atiku keeps attacking President Bola Tinubu.

What he said

Speaking in Abuja, Shettima is said to have made a distinction between his clashes with Atiku and his relationships with other opposition figures. He pointed out that he has never publicly insulted Peter Obi, Governor Seyi Makinde, Rabiu Kwankwaso or Rotimi Amaechi, describing them all as personal friends.

He reportedly drew a line between political rivalry and personal hostility.

Jonathan link

Shettima also traced his past political tensions with former President Goodluck Jonathan back to his open support for Amaechi during the contest for chairmanship of the Nigeria Governors' Forum.

Background

The campaign for the January 2027 election is heating up. No response from Atiku's campaign was included in the reports.
