Annie Idibia's birthday has drawn warm messages from her husband's family, and one tribute in particular has fans talking.

The post

Ujah Idibia, brother of music star 2Baba, shared photos of the singer and Annie side by side on Instagram with a heartfelt caption.

"Massive birthday blessings Queen @annieidibia1. May the universe be on ur side always. Just know u are loved dearly. More love n light for u. Have a wonderful Bday," he wrote, adding heart emojis.

2Baba's like

Background

2Baba, whose real name is Innocent Idibia, and actress Annie Idibia were one of Nigeria's most talked-about celebrity couples and have children together. Their separation became public in early 2025, and 2Baba has since been linked with Edo lawmaker Natasha Osawaru. The birthday gestures from him and his family suggest goodwill remains between the two.

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has introduced his wife to the public for the first time, sharing loved-up photos and videos of the couple on Instagram.

How they met

In the post, the actor said they met almost five years ago in an airport lounge before a flight to Houston. He recalled asking if the seat next to her was free, and said she firmly told him it was taken, but he sat down anyway. They ignored each other for an hour, then burst out laughing when they realised they were on the same flight.

His tribute

Jim Iyke described their relationship as something sacred rather than hidden, and said they have built a family together and travelled to eight countries. He called her his best friend, his prayer warrior and his wife, and said they have never stopped laughing together.

Femi Branch opens up on two years out of work after on-set accident

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Actor Femi Branch says a stunt that went wrong on a film set in Ikorodu left him unable to work for two years, forcing him to sell his late mother's car. He said some colleagues abandoned him.

Nollywood actor Femi Branch has opened up about a difficult period in his life after a serious accident on a film set left him unable to work for two years.

The accident

In an interview that surfaced online on Friday, 20 November 2026, Branch said he was injured while shooting a stunt in Ikorodu, Lagos. He said he had to jump from a storey building while dressed as a security guard, and the impact when he landed was so severe it sounded like an explosion.

Hard times

He said the injury kept him out of work for two years, leaving him isolated and in financial difficulty. He revealed that he had to sell his mother's car during that period, and said some colleagues deserted him while he was recovering.
