Nine killed as Bahamas-bound private jet plunges into Florida roadside

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

A private jet carrying nine people crashed beside a road in Palm Beach County, Florida, on Sunday, 1 November 2026, killing everyone on board in a fireball that was caught on video by passing motorists.

What happened

US Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said the Learjet 60 went down at about 12.50pm local time and described the crash as a horrific tragedy. The aircraft had taken off from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas and was flying to the Bahamas. Florida governor Ron DeSantis said there were no immediate reports of anyone on the ground being hurt, and described the crash as cataclysmic, saying the plane looked like a missile coming down and left a large crater by the roadside. Reports said the site was a few miles from President Donald Trump's Mar-a-Lago estate.

The final minutes

According to air traffic control recordings and flight tracking data, the jet was cruising at about 35,000 feet when the pilots reported light ice, moderate turbulence and rain, and asked to climb a further 2,000 feet. Shortly afterwards the plane went into a steep dive. The pilot declared a mayday, reporting that the aircraft was descending at 10,000 and then 20,000 feet per minute in an overspeed condition, before saying the jet was going to crash. Tracking data showed the rate of descent at one point exceeded 32,000 feet per minute.

The investigation

The National Transportation Safety Board is leading the investigation, supported by a team from the Federal Aviation Administration. The airport confirmed it was a private flight but did not say who was on board. Records list the plane's owner as a company registered in Delaware. Heavy rain had hit the region on Sunday, and a flood watch was in place until Monday afternoon.

Co-pilot identified

On Monday, relatives identified the co-pilot as 20-year-old Nathalia Gadelha, who grew up in Palm Beach County. She earned her private pilot's licence at 17, in October 2023, before she could even drive, and had trained at a flight school with a branch in Pompano Beach. She had said she dreamed of becoming a commercial airline pilot. The other victims have not yet been publicly named.

Victims named

On Monday, relatives identified the passengers as a 44-year-old technology entrepreneur, his wife and their two children, aged two and four, together with two close friends of the family and the children's nanny. The two pilots, an experienced aviator and his 20-year-old co-pilot, also died. In a statement, the families thanked first responders and the Palm Beach County community and said their hearts were also with the pilots' families.

What happens next

Investigators are expected to examine the wreckage, the weather and the aircraft's maintenance history.

  • All nine people on board the Learjet 60 were killed.
  • The pilot reported ice and turbulence before a steep dive.
  • The co-pilot was a 20-year-old from Palm Beach County.

British comedian and actor Russell Brand has been sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison.

The sentence

Brand, 51, was sentenced on Monday, 9 November 2026, after a jury of eight men and four women found him guilty of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others, in offences dating back to 2004. He was cleared of two counts of rape and one count of attempted indecent assault. Brand had maintained his innocence, saying all his encounters were consensual.

The judge's remarks

Judge Joel Bennathan said Brand had shown a casual and arrogant disregard for women's right to say no, and placed the crimes in the context of his rise to fame about 20 years ago, when he was widely seen as clever, funny and charismatic.

Background

Brand became famous in the 2000s as a stand-up comedian, television and radio presenter and Hollywood actor, and was known for his outrageous on-air behaviour and his public battles with drug and alcohol addiction. The allegations against him first emerged in a joint media investigation in 2023, after which he was charged by British police. In recent years he had reinvented himself as an online commentator with a large following.

Seven dead and over 100 injured in Egypt train collision near Cairo

At least seven people were killed and about 125 injured when a passenger train crashed into the rear of another in Giza province, south of Cairo. Prosecutors have opened an investigation.

At least seven people have been killed and more than 100 injured in a collision between two passenger trains in Egypt.

What happened

The crash happened on Sunday, 8 November 2026, in the al-Ayyat district of Giza province, about 52 kilometres south of Cairo. According to Egypt's Transportation Ministry, a train travelling from Giza to Fayoum crashed into the back of another train heading from Cairo to Aswan, derailing several carriages and trapping passengers. The Health Ministry said 125 people were injured. Emergency services sent 72 ambulances, and local residents helped pull survivors from the wreckage.

Investigation

Egypt's Public Prosecution has opened an investigation into the cause of the crash.

Background

Egypt has one of Africa's oldest and busiest railway networks, but it has a long history of deadly accidents, often blamed on poor maintenance and human error.

Hackers claim leaked Russian records show up to 450,000 war deaths in Ukraine

9 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Two hacking groups say records taken from Russia's state statistics agency show between about 394,000 and 450,000 Russian deaths linked to the war in Ukraine. The figures have not been verified and the agency denies any data was leaked.

Read more

Two hacking groups say they have obtained records from Russia's state statistics agency pointing to hundreds of thousands of Russian deaths in the war in Ukraine.

The claims

According to reports on Monday, 9 November 2026, the pro-Ukrainian group Silent Crow and the Belarusian Cyber Partisans said they accessed an internal database at Rosstat containing information from death certificates from February 2022 to August 2026. Silent Crow, which published the data, put the death toll at about 450,100, while the Belarusian Cyber Partisans, using a narrower method that counted only records naming military operations as the cause of death, put it at 393,881. The figures have not been independently verified.

Russia's response

Rosstat acknowledged that its systems were targeted between 29 October and 1 November but said no databases were compromised. Russia has banned both groups as 'extremist organisations'. Moscow has rarely given official figures for its military losses.

Background

Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. Independent estimates of Russian military deaths have varied widely, and both sides have been accused of under-reporting their own losses. The Belarusian Cyber Partisans also oppose the government of Belarusian President Alexander Lukashenko, a close ally of Moscow.

Gunman in Krakow Main Square shooting that killed six dies in hospital

9 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The 24-year-old suspected gunman in a shooting in Krakow's Main Square that killed six people and injured about 16 has died in hospital, Polish police say. Three of his relatives were also found dead at his home.

Read more

The suspected gunman in a deadly shooting in the centre of Krakow, Poland, has died in hospital, police say.

What happened

The shooting took place in the city's Main Square on the afternoon of Saturday, 7 November 2026. Six people, four women and two men, were killed and about 16 others were injured, including visitors from the United Kingdom, Portugal, Russia, Germany and Austria. Police said the 24-year-old suspect was arrested on a nearby street after trying to escape in a car, and that two officers were injured in an exchange of fire. Three more bodies were found at his home in Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski, about 150 kilometres from Krakow, and he is believed to have also killed three members of his own family.

The suspect dies

In an update on Monday, 9 November, regional police confirmed that the suspect had died in hospital that morning. He is believed to have used an AR-15-style rifle, and ammunition was recovered from his car. Authorities have not given a clear motive, but said he was fascinated by far-right ideologies.

Background

Mass shootings are rare in Poland, which has strict gun laws.
