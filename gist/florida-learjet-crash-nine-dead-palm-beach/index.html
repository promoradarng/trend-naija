Nine killed as Bahamas-bound private jet plunges into Florida roadside

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

A private jet carrying nine people crashed beside a road in Palm Beach County, Florida, on Sunday, 1 November 2026, killing everyone on board in a fireball that was caught on video by passing motorists.

What happened

US Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said the Learjet 60 went down at about 12.50pm local time and described the crash as a horrific tragedy. The aircraft had taken off from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas and was flying to the Bahamas. Florida governor Ron DeSantis said there were no immediate reports of anyone on the ground being hurt, and described the crash as cataclysmic, saying the plane looked like a missile coming down and left a large crater by the roadside. Reports said the site was a few miles from President Donald Trump's Mar-a-Lago estate.

The final minutes

According to air traffic control recordings and flight tracking data, the jet was cruising at about 35,000 feet when the pilots reported light ice, moderate turbulence and rain, and asked to climb a further 2,000 feet. Shortly afterwards the plane went into a steep dive. The pilot declared a mayday, reporting that the aircraft was descending at 10,000 and then 20,000 feet per minute in an overspeed condition, before saying the jet was going to crash. Tracking data showed the rate of descent at one point exceeded 32,000 feet per minute.

The investigation

The National Transportation Safety Board is leading the investigation, supported by a team from the Federal Aviation Administration. The airport confirmed it was a private flight but did not say who was on board. Records list the plane's owner as a company registered in Delaware. Heavy rain had hit the region on Sunday, and a flood watch was in place until Monday afternoon.

Co-pilot identified

On Monday, relatives identified the co-pilot as 20-year-old Nathalia Gadelha, who grew up in Palm Beach County. She earned her private pilot's licence at 17, in October 2023, before she could even drive, and had trained at a flight school with a branch in Pompano Beach. She had said she dreamed of becoming a commercial airline pilot. The other victims have not yet been publicly named.

Victims named

On Monday, relatives identified the passengers as a 44-year-old technology entrepreneur, his wife and their two children, aged two and four, together with two close friends of the family and the children's nanny. The two pilots, an experienced aviator and his 20-year-old co-pilot, also died. In a statement, the families thanked first responders and the Palm Beach County community and said their hearts were also with the pilots' families.

What happens next

Investigators are expected to examine the wreckage, the weather and the aircraft's maintenance history.

  • All nine people on board the Learjet 60 were killed.
  • The pilot reported ice and turbulence before a steep dive.
  • The co-pilot was a 20-year-old from Palm Beach County.

Robert Pattinson reportedly named the new James Bond

Robert Pattinson has reportedly been confirmed as the next James Bond, ending years of speculation over who would follow Daniel Craig. He is said to star in the upcoming 007 film directed by Denis Villeneuve.

British actor Robert Pattinson has reportedly been named the next James Bond, ending years of guessing over who would take over the role of the famous secret agent.

The announcement

The franchise is said to have confirmed the casting on Monday, 16 November 2026, with a short video showing the 40-year-old actor in a black tuxedo, pointing a gun. Pattinson is best known for the Twilight films and for currently playing Bruce Wayne in DC's Batman films.

The race for the role

Several actors, including Callum Turner, Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor, were reportedly screen-tested before the casting was announced for the new film, which will be directed by Denis Villeneuve. In a recent magazine interview, Villeneuve spoke about the pressure of taking on the franchise and said casting was still in progress at the time.

Background

Daniel Craig stepped down as 007 after five films over 15 years, ending with No Time to Die in October 2021. He was the sixth actor to play the character, after Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton and Pierce Brosnan. If confirmed, Pattinson would become the seventh actor to play Bond in the official film series.

What happens next

No release date for the new Bond film was given in the reports.

Guinea deports 1,557 West Africans convicted of illegal gold mining

16 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Guinea has deported 1,557 West Africans, mostly from Burkina Faso and Mali, who had been jailed for illegal gold mining, as it steps up a crackdown on unauthorised mining.

Read more

Guinea has expelled 1,557 West African nationals who were arrested and convicted for illegal gold mining.

The deportation

According to judicial and security sources quoted in reports on Sunday, 15 November 2026, the deportees, mostly citizens of Burkina Faso and Mali, were arrested at mining sites during security operations. After serving prison sentences of 18 months to two years, they were taken in a convoy of 44 vehicles from the capital, Conakry, to the border town of Kouremale on the Mali border, arriving on Saturday.

The crackdown

Guinean authorities stepped up operations against illegal mining in early November, particularly in Siguiri, a major gold-producing region in the north-east. The government says the aim is to stop illegal exploitation of mineral resources and limit environmental damage from unregulated mining.

Background

The crackdown has also raised tensions between miners and the authorities.

Jordan sentences three men to death over deadly church attack

A Jordanian court has sentenced three men to death for a gun attack on a Greek Orthodox church funeral in October that killed nine people and wounded 25.

A court in Jordan has sentenced three men to death by hanging for a deadly attack on a church in the south of the country.

The verdict

The State Security Court on Sunday, 15 November 2026, found the men, two brothers and a relative by marriage, guilty of terrorist attacks resulting in death, weapons offences, conspiracy to commit terrorist acts and promoting the ideas of a terrorist organisation, according to a statement from the Jordanian military.

The attack

Gunmen killed nine people and wounded 25 when they stormed a funeral at a Greek Orthodox church in Hammoud village, in the Karak governorate, on 23 October 2026. The three suspects fled after the attack and were arrested three days later.

Background

The attack shocked Jordan, a Muslim-majority country where Christians make up around three per cent of the population.

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf confirms divorce from Kwankwaso's daughter, says he will marry again

Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso, daughter of his former political mentor Rabiu Kwankwaso, has ended. Speaking with humour at a pensioners' event, he said the dissolution papers had been issued and that he still intends to take another wife.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, the daughter of former Kano governor and NDC leader Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, has ended.

What the governor said

Yusuf is said to have spoken about the matter on Monday, 16 November 2026, while addressing pensioners at an event at the Government House in Kano. Speaking in Hausa and in a light-hearted tone, he compared going from two wives to one to a demotion in military rank, from corporal to constable.

He reportedly confirmed that he had agreed to the divorce and that the papers for the dissolution had been issued. He also told the gathering that he still plans to take another wife, and asked residents to pray that he finds a suitable partner. He said he was at peace with the decision.

Background

The confirmation came two days after reports that Zainab Kwankwaso had filed a divorce petition at an Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja. She was said to be the governor's second wife.

The separation has drawn wide attention because of the long political relationship between Yusuf and Kwankwaso, who was his political mentor.
