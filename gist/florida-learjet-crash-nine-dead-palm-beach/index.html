Nine killed as Bahamas-bound private jet plunges into Florida roadside

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

A private jet carrying nine people crashed beside a road in Palm Beach County, Florida, on Sunday, 1 November 2026, killing everyone on board in a fireball that was caught on video by passing motorists.

What happened

US Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said the Learjet 60 went down at about 12.50pm local time and described the crash as a horrific tragedy. The aircraft had taken off from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas and was flying to the Bahamas. Florida governor Ron DeSantis said there were no immediate reports of anyone on the ground being hurt, and described the crash as cataclysmic, saying the plane looked like a missile coming down and left a large crater by the roadside. Reports said the site was a few miles from President Donald Trump's Mar-a-Lago estate.

The final minutes

According to air traffic control recordings and flight tracking data, the jet was cruising at about 35,000 feet when the pilots reported light ice, moderate turbulence and rain, and asked to climb a further 2,000 feet. Shortly afterwards the plane went into a steep dive. The pilot declared a mayday, reporting that the aircraft was descending at 10,000 and then 20,000 feet per minute in an overspeed condition, before saying the jet was going to crash. Tracking data showed the rate of descent at one point exceeded 32,000 feet per minute.

The investigation

The National Transportation Safety Board is leading the investigation, supported by a team from the Federal Aviation Administration. The airport confirmed it was a private flight but did not say who was on board. Records list the plane's owner as a company registered in Delaware. Heavy rain had hit the region on Sunday, and a flood watch was in place until Monday afternoon.

Co-pilot identified

On Monday, relatives identified the co-pilot as 20-year-old Nathalia Gadelha, who grew up in Palm Beach County. She earned her private pilot's licence at 17, in October 2023, before she could even drive, and had trained at a flight school with a branch in Pompano Beach. She had said she dreamed of becoming a commercial airline pilot. The other victims have not yet been publicly named.

Victims named

On Monday, relatives identified the passengers as a 44-year-old technology entrepreneur, his wife and their two children, aged two and four, together with two close friends of the family and the children's nanny. The two pilots, an experienced aviator and his 20-year-old co-pilot, also died. In a statement, the families thanked first responders and the Palm Beach County community and said their hearts were also with the pilots' families.

What happens next

Investigators are expected to examine the wreckage, the weather and the aircraft's maintenance history.

  • The co-pilot was a 20-year-old from Palm Beach County.

Bavarian deputy leader Hubert Aiwanger found dead in Berlin hotel

6 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Hubert Aiwanger, Bavaria's deputy premier and leader of Germany's Free Voters party, has been found dead in a Berlin hotel room at the age of 55. Prosecutors say an autopsy found no sign that anyone else was involved.

Read more

Hubert Aiwanger, the deputy premier of the German state of Bavaria and leader of the Free Voters party, has died at the age of 55.

What happened

Aiwanger was found dead in his hotel room in central Berlin on the evening of Wednesday, 4 November 2026, police and prosecutors confirmed. On Thursday, prosecutors said an autopsy had found no evidence that anyone else was involved in his death. He had reportedly been due to speak at the opening of his party's new Berlin office on Wednesday afternoon, but pulled out, citing health concerns.

Who he was

Aiwanger became leader of the Free Voters in Bavaria in 2006 and national party chairman in 2010. Under him, the party entered the Bavarian state parliament in 2008, and since 2018 it has governed Bavaria as the junior coalition partner of the Christian Social Union (CSU), with Aiwanger serving as deputy premier and economy minister. Known for his blunt, folksy style, he presented his party as an alternative to the CSU, but it never won seats in the national parliament.

Tributes

Bavarian premier Markus Söder described his sudden death as an absolute tragedy. Aiwanger is survived by his partner, Free Voters politician Tanja Schweiger, and their two children.

What happens next

The Free Voters will need to choose a new leader, and the Bavarian government will appoint a new deputy premier and economy minister.

Bomb threats hit at least 25 passenger flights across Europe

6 Nov 2026, 09:45 GMT

At least 25 flights across Europe were targeted by bomb threats on Thursday, forcing diversions, emergency landings and fighter jet escorts. No explosives were found on the aircraft searched. It was the second wave of threats this week.

Read more

At least 25 passenger flights across Europe were hit by bomb threats on Thursday, 5 November 2026, causing diversions, emergency landings and security checks at several airports.

What happened

According to reports, an Aer Lingus flight from Dublin to Rome carrying 151 passengers and six crew was diverted to a French military air base at Évreux, where bomb disposal experts inspected it. Three Ryanair flights to Bulgaria from Copenhagen, Madrid and London were also targeted. Two landed in Sofia and the third was diverted to Plovdiv. Italian fighter jets escorted a Finnair flight from Helsinki to Rome after a bomb alert. Flights operated by Wizz Air, LOT Polish Airlines and Aegean Airlines were also affected, with incidents reported in countries including France, Spain, Poland, Denmark, Italy, the Czech Republic, Austria and Romania.

No explosives found

No explosives were found on any of the aircraft searched so far, and all the affected planes landed safely. The threats followed another wave on Tuesday, when at least seven flights across Europe were targeted.

Background

Hoax bomb threats against airlines cause major disruption and cost, as pilots and authorities must treat every threat as real. Investigators have not said publicly who is behind the latest threats or whether the incidents are linked. European security agencies have warned in recent years of attempts to disrupt transport and infrastructure.

What it means for travellers

Passengers flying in Europe, including Nigerians travelling for the holidays, may face extra security checks and delays while investigations continue.

UK says Putin will not be invited to 2027 G20 summit, breaking with Trump

6 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

British Prime Minister Andy Burnham has reportedly said Russian President Vladimir Putin will not attend the G20 summit the UK hosts in Manchester in November 2027, because of sanctions. President Trump has invited Putin to this year's G20 in Miami.

Read more

The United Kingdom says it will not invite Russian President Vladimir Putin to the G20 summit it is hosting in 2027, setting it apart from the United States.

What was said

In an interview reported on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Prime Minister Andy Burnham was reportedly asked whether Putin would be invited to the summit in Manchester in November 2027, and replied that he would not. He said the guest list was handled by the G20, but that Putin would not be coming because of an outstanding sanction.

A contrast with Washington

President Donald Trump has invited Putin to this year's G20 summit in Miami in December. The Kremlin has not confirmed whether the Russian leader will attend.

Background

Britain has avoided direct engagement with Russian ministers since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, and has imposed wide sanctions on Russian officials. Tensions over Russia's role in the G20 were on display at a meeting of foreign ministers in October, when a planned group photo was cancelled after European ministers refused to pose with Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov. The G20 includes the world's largest economies, and the African Union became a full member in 2023.

Why it matters

The disagreement highlights divisions among Western allies over how to deal with Russia as the war in Ukraine continues.

American rapper Nicki Minaj has doubled down on her support for President Donald Trump after his party's heavy losses in the US midterm elections.

What she posted

In a series of posts on X on Wednesday night, 4 November 2026, Minaj reportedly shared a numbered list of messages, declaring that Trump is still the president and, in a reference to herself, that 'the Queen' is still people's obsession. She also praised Vice President JD Vance, congratulated Republican winners including Florida's governor-elect Byron Donalds, described Elon Musk as a trillionaire and urged Republicans to wake up. She thanked her fans, known as the Barbz, and shared a video responding to criticism of her support for Trump.

Background

The Democrats are projected to have won control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate in the 3 November midterms, in a result widely seen as a rebuke of Trump's second-term agenda. Trump said he was not on the ballot and later admitted the Iran war had contributed to the losses. Minaj, who was born in Trinidad and Tobago, has in recent years become an outspoken supporter of Trump, which has divided her fan base.

How people reacted

Her posts drew mixed reactions, with some fans applauding her and others criticising her political views.
