Adamu Garba denies endorsing Peter Obi for 2027, says he has quit partisan politics

Former APC presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has dismissed reports that he endorsed Peter Obi for the 2027 presidential election, saying he has withdrawn from partisan politics to focus on his businesses and Africa's digital economy.

Former All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has denied reports that he endorsed Peter Obi ahead of Nigeria's 2027 presidential election.

"The claim is false"

In a public statement dated 18 November 2026, Garba described the claim circulating online as false.

He said he has withdrawn from partisan politics and is now focusing on his business ventures and on Africa's digital economy.

His new focus

Garba named Crowwe, Tablars and Justitia SI among the projects currently receiving his attention.

He also urged the public to rely only on his official channels for accurate information about his activities.

Where did the story come from?

The origin of the endorsement claim remains unclear. No post, interview or public statement showing Garba endorsing Obi has been found.

What is clear is that Garba has publicly disputed the claim.

A familiar pattern

With the 2027 elections approaching, false claims about endorsements are spreading quickly on social media. It is the second time this week that a story about backing for Obi has been corrected, after Obi himself clarified that his Washington visit was not an endorsement from the US Congress.

Former senator and Adamawa State governorship candidate Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, popularly known as Binani, has announced the death of her father.

The announcement

In a post on social media, Binani said her father died after a brief illness in Abuja. She opened her message with the Islamic expression of condolence and thanked God despite the loss.

Funeral arrangements

She said the funeral prayer, known as Jana'iza, would take place on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the Central Mosque after the afternoon Zuhr prayer.

Who she is

Binani is a former senator who contested the Adamawa governorship election in 2023. She is now a member of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

Six presidential candidates have reportedly been invited to a policy debate in Abuja on 10 January 2027, six days before the presidential election.

The announcement

The convener of the organising group, Lai Omotola, announced the plan at a press conference in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. He said the debate is designed to push the candidates beyond campaign slogans and into detailed discussions of their policies.

Who is invited

The six candidates invited are President Bola Tinubu of the APC, Atiku Abubakar of the ADC, Peter Obi of the NDC, Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde, former Cross River governor Donald Duke and the SDP's Adewole Adebayo.

Will they attend?

It was not clear from the reports whether all the candidates have confirmed they will take part.

The election

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
