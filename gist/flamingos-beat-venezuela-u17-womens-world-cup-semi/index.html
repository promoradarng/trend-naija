Flamingos beat Venezuela to reach U-17 Women's World Cup semi-finals, face Brazil next

2 Nov 2026, 09:44 GMT

Nigeria's under-17 women's team, the Flamingos, are into the semi-finals of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The match

The Flamingos beat Venezuela 1-0 in the quarter-final on Sunday, 1 November 2026, at the Mohammed VI Football Academy in Salé. Captain Azeezat Oduntan scored the winner from the penalty spot in the 89th minute after Oluebube Umejiaku was fouled in the box, a decision confirmed by a VAR review.

What comes next

Nigeria will face Brazil in the semi-final on Wednesday, 4 November, at the Moulay El Hassan Stadium in Rabat. They are the only African side left in the competition after Ghana lost 1-0 to Japan in their quarter-final.

Reactions

The Confederation of African Football congratulated the team on social media, celebrating their place in the last four. Nigerian fans also shared videos of the players dancing and celebrating after earlier victories in the tournament.

Background

Nigeria has a strong record in women's youth football and has reached the later stages of age-group World Cups several times. The senior team, the Super Falcons, are the most successful side in African women's football and won another Women's Africa Cup of Nations title this year. The Flamingos' run has raised hopes of Nigeria's first-ever U-17 women's world title.

  • Nigeria play Brazil in the semi-final on Wednesday in Rabat.
  • They are Africa's last team in the tournament.

Ronaldinho set for competitive comeback at 46 with Italian club Ravenna

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Brazilian legend Ronaldinho is set to start for Serie C club Ravenna against Forli on Sunday, his first competitive match in more than a decade, the club's manager has confirmed.

Read more

Former Barcelona, PSG and AC Milan star Ronaldinho is set to return to competitive football at the age of 46.

The comeback

Ravenna manager Andrea Mandorlini confirmed on Friday, 13 November 2026, that the Brazilian will start for the Italian Serie C club in a local derby against Forli on Sunday, 15 November, as one of three forwards. Mandorlini described him as one of the greatest players of the modern era, adding that the coaching staff would decide together how long he plays.

A long wait

Ronaldinho joined Ravenna as an investor in August. His last competitive appearance was for Brazilian club Fluminense in September 2015, making Sunday's match his first in more than ten years.

Background

Ronaldinho won the Ballon d'Or in 2005 and the World Cup with Brazil in 2002, and is remembered as one of the most gifted and entertaining players of his generation.

Portugal retires Ronaldo's number 7 shirt design after end of his international career

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Portugal's football federation has permanently changed the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt in honour of Cristiano Ronaldo, whose international career has ended, and players will all wear 7 during their walk-out against Denmark.

Read more

Portugal's football federation has honoured Cristiano Ronaldo by permanently changing the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt.

The tribute

The Portuguese Football Federation announced on X that the style of number 7 worn by Ronaldo will not be used by any other player, and a new version will appear on future shirts. According to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026, every Portugal player will wear a number 7 shirt during the walk-out before their Nations League match against Denmark as a collective farewell.

How it ended

Ronaldo's international career reportedly came to an end after a public disagreement with Jorge Jesus over a substitution during a match in September.

Background

Ronaldo is Portugal's all-time record goalscorer and most-capped player and led the country to the Euro 2016 title. The gesture mirrors the honour Argentina gave Lionel Messi after his international retirement.

Olympic Eagles lose 2-0 to DR Congo in U-23 AFCON qualifier first leg

14 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Nigeria's U-23 team lost 2-0 to DR Congo in Lubumbashi in the first leg of their U-23 AFCON qualifier, with a controversial opening goal and an injury to Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna.

Read more

Nigeria's Olympic Eagles suffered a 2-0 defeat to the Democratic Republic of Congo in the first leg of their second-round qualifier for the 2027 Under-23 Africa Cup of Nations.

The match

In Lubumbashi on Friday, 13 November 2026, Nigeria started brightly, with midfielder Victor Samuel shooting narrowly wide early on and missing a one-on-one chance soon after. The hosts took the lead inside 20 minutes with a goal that Nigeria felt should have been ruled out for a challenge on goalkeeper Samuel James. Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna was then forced off injured on the half-hour, and DR Congo doubled their lead five minutes before half-time.

Who was in charge

The team was led by Ikorodu City coach Bright Osehai Ozebagbe and former U-20 assistant coach Deniran Ortega, while Eric Chelle was with the Super Eagles, who beat Tanzania 3-1 on the same day.

What happens next

Nigeria will need to overturn the two-goal deficit in the return leg at home to reach the tournament.

Brighton forward Zadok Yohanna, who was making his first start for the U-23 side, has been ruled out of the return leg with the injury he picked up in Lubumbashi.

Arokodare scores twice as Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 in AFCON qualifier

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Substitute Tolu Arokodare scored twice as Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 in Uyo on matchday three of the 2027 AFCON qualifiers, moving the Super Eagles to second in Group L, a point behind Guinea-Bissau.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 at the Godswill Akpabio Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November 2026, in a crucial 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier.

How the match went

Alhassan Yusuf gave Nigeria a dream start with a goal in the second minute, but Tanzania equalised through Alphonce Masanga. Ademola Lookman was forced off injured late in the first half and replaced by Moses Simon. Coach Eric Chelle then brought on Tolu Arokodare and Moses Usor in the second half, and the change paid off immediately: Arokodare scored in the 52nd minute, and the two substitutes combined for Arokodare's second goal and Nigeria's third in the 60th minute.

Table

The win moved Nigeria to second in Group L, one point behind Guinea-Bissau, who were held 1-1 by Madagascar. Nigeria play Tanzania again in their next match, and a win there would take them to nine points, putting them in a strong position to qualify with two games left.

Arokodare delivers

Arokodare, who has been in strong form on loan at Ajax in the Netherlands, had promised to carry his club form into the national team in the absence of the injured Victor Osimhen. Chelle later sent him a message after his brace.

Lookman a doubt

After the match, Chelle said Ademola Lookman was a doubt for the return fixture against Tanzania. He said the Atletico Madrid forward had felt discomfort in his knee before a knock to the same area made it worse, and the coaching staff are not expected to rush him back. Tanzania are one of the co-hosts of AFCON 2027, along with Kenya and Uganda.

A scan later confirmed a knee ligament sprain, and the Super Eagles' medical team ruled Lookman out of the second leg in Zanzibar, where Nigeria will defend a two-goal advantage. Victor Osimhen is also missing the November games.
