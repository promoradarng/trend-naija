Odumodublvck claims some artists turn to spiritual means to get hit songs

3 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Rapper Odumodublvck, real name Tochukwu Ojogwu, has made a controversial claim about how some musicians chase success in Nigeria's competitive music industry.

What he said

Speaking on a podcast, in comments shared on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, the rapper said getting a hit song is not easy and that some people even turn to spiritual practices, popularly called 'jazz', to get one. He said this is why some artists appear to struggle badly when they cannot get a hit. He did not name any artist or provide evidence for the claim.

Praise for the big three

Odumodublvck praised Burna Boy, Wizkid and Davido, saying their ability to release successful songs consistently for years deserves respect. He noted that some artists never get a single hit in their entire careers.

Background

Odumodublvck rose to fame with his 2023 hit 'Declan Rice' and is known for bold statements. Earlier this week he drew mockery after claiming that Nigerians did not know Arsenal midfielder Declan Rice until he released the song. Claims about artists using spiritual help for success are a long-running topic of gossip in Nigerian entertainment, but they are rarely backed by evidence.

How people reacted

Fans were divided. Some said the claim reflected what many people already suspect, while others accused the rapper of spreading rumours and said hard work and talent explain most hits.

Frank Edwards says he turned down a huge deal that asked him to remove Jesus from his songs

Gospel singer Frank Edwards has reportedly opened up about a deal he rejected early in his career, which he believes could have made him wealthy enough to own three private jets.

The offer

Speaking during a concert ministration, in a clip that circulated online on 21 November 2026, Edwards is said to have explained that the offer came shortly after he travelled to South Africa to shoot the video for his song Beautiful.

Promoters reportedly offered to take his music to a global audience on one condition: that he remove the name of Jesus from his songs and replace it with God, arguing that this would make the music more widely acceptable.

His message

Edwards told his audience that the temptation came disguised as a genuine business opportunity, and warned that not every compromise looks obviously wrong at first. He said his mother's advice helped him turn the deal down.

Four killed as gunmen attack Omo-Agege's NDC campaign in Delta

23 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Gunmen opened fire on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege and his campaign team in Uduere, Ughelli North, Delta State, on Monday. His campaign says four people were killed and four others critically injured, and that the senator escaped unhurt.

Read more

Four people were killed and several others injured when gunmen attacked a Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North Local Government Area of Delta State, on Monday, 23 November 2026.

What happened

According to his campaign organisation, armed men opened fire at close range on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege, the NDC's Delta Central senatorial candidate, and his entourage while he was addressing a crowd that had gathered to welcome him. The campaign said the senator was not hit, although bullets passed close to him.

The victims

The Omo-Agege Campaign Organisation said four people died and four others were critically injured and receiving treatment. It named two of the dead as aides of the campaign.

'Assassination attempt'

In a statement by its media adviser, the campaign described the incident as a targeted attempt on the senator's life rather than a political clash, citing eyewitnesses who said the attackers made clear he was their target.

What happens next

There was no word in the reports on arrests or a police statement. The attack comes as campaigns for the 2027 elections gather pace.
