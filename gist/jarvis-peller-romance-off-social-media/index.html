"My romantic side is off social media!" Jarvis reacts to online drama involving Peller

30 Oct 2026, 15:08 GMT

Popular streamer Jarvis has finally spoken out after the recent controversy surrounding her relationship with Peller, and it appears she has had enough of people interfering in their private lives.

What Jarvis said

In a message shared on her WhatsApp channel, Jarvis made it clear that she will no longer display her romantic moments with Peller on social media.

According to her, the two have spent the past two to three years loving each other, playing, arguing and settling their differences like siblings. Now, she says, people have turned their usual playful behaviour into a story of disrespect, and she is clearly not happy about it.

"Nobody tells me how to live my life"

Jarvis questioned why people expect her relationship to look perfect all the time, and reminded critics that nobody has the right to dictate how she should live or love.

She also took a swipe at those who constantly judge other people's relationships while pretending their own marriages are perfect. "Mr and Mrs All Perfect, my foot! Nobody tells me how to live my life," she wrote.

Choosing privacy

For Jarvis, the decision is simple: she would rather enjoy her relationship privately than keep putting her happiness on display for strangers to analyse, criticise and argue about.

She ended her message by saying people could call her whatever names they liked, but their opinions would never break her.

Fans who enjoy watching Jarvis and Peller's romantic moments online may now have to get used to seeing much less of that side of their relationship.

But here is the question: when celebrities constantly share their relationships online, can they really expect people not to have opinions about what they see?

Cute Abiola says he sold one of his cars as he appeals to Tinubu over power and hardship

6 Nov 2026, 09:45 GMT

Comedian and skit maker The Cute Abiola has posted videos appealing to President Tinubu over poor electricity and the cost of living, saying his solar system had run down and that he recently sold one of his cars to settle debts.

Read more

Comedian and skit maker The Cute Abiola has joined Nigerians complaining about poor electricity supply and economic hardship, in videos addressed directly to President Bola Tinubu.

What he said

In one video shared online on Friday, 6 November 2026, the content creator said his solar power system had gone off because of heavy use during the long power outages in his area. He appealed to the President to help Nigerians, saying everywhere was hot, there was no light and the suffering was too much. In another video, he said he had just sold one of his cars to ease his financial situation, explaining that he owed people money and still needed funds to shoot new content. He added that he hoped things would not get to the point where he had to sell his house.

Background

Abiola, whose real name is Abdulgafar Ahmed, became popular for his comedy skits and is a former member of the Nigerian Navy. His complaints come as consumers in Lagos, Abuja, Ogun, Kwara and Oyo states report renewed power outages, which the federal government has blamed on gas shortages, maintenance problems and reduced hydropower generation. The government has also started paying a ₦50,000 monthly relief grant to selected households.

How people reacted

Many fans sympathised and said his videos reflected what millions of Nigerians are going through. Some critics said he had not praised the government when things were going well for him and was now blaming the President, while others said public figures should be free to speak about their struggles.

"If not for VeryDarkMan, I wouldn't be where I am today": Ratel Princess Jojooflele expresses gratitude from China

5 Nov 2026, 21:44 GMT

Social media personality Jojooflele, known as the Ratel Princess, has publicly thanked VeryDarkMan for changing her life, saying her following and opportunities grew after she met him. She spoke from China, a long way from the Nedu controversy that first brought her into the spotlight.

Read more

Popular social media personality Jojooflele, widely known as the Ratel Princess, has openly expressed her appreciation for VeryDarkMan (VDM), acknowledging the role he has played in changing her life.

"If not for VDM"

Speaking from China, Jojooflele reflected on how far she has come and how her association with VDM opened doors she probably never imagined would open so soon.

"If not for VDM, I wouldn't be where I am today," she said.

According to her, things took a completely different turn after she met VeryDarkMan. Her social media pages began attracting more attention, her followers increased, and opportunities started coming her way.

How it all started

What makes her story even more interesting is how it began. Many will remember the controversy involving Nedu, when Jojooflele found herself at the centre of public attention, with people discussing her personal life and questioning her actions.

For someone else, that kind of controversy could have been enough to damage their confidence or push them off social media completely.

Instead, Jojooflele turned the attention into something positive. Rather than letting the controversy define her future, she kept building her presence online and eventually became a familiar face among supporters of the Ratel Movement.

A lesson in gratitude

Today, she is in China, reflecting on her journey and publicly appreciating someone she believes contributed greatly to her success.

Jojooflele deserves credit for how she has handled her growing popularity. The right connections can open doors, but what you do with those opportunities matters just as much.

Her appreciation for VDM is also worth acknowledging. In a society where people sometimes forget those who supported them once they start to succeed, publicly recognising someone's contribution says a lot about gratitude.

Setbacks are not the end

Perhaps the biggest lesson here is never to be too quick to conclude that your present difficulties will decide your future. The situation people expect to destroy you can end up introducing you to opportunities you never knew existed.

What looks like a setback today may become part of the story you proudly tell tomorrow.

Tinubu asks how Atiku built a university while he was vice-president

7 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

At an APC rally in Port Harcourt, President Tinubu said he was cleaning up a mess created by people complaining about hunger, naming Atiku Abubakar, and questioned how the ADC candidate built a university while in office.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has launched one of his sharpest attacks yet on Atiku Abubakar, presidential candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC).

What he said

Speaking at an All Progressives Congress (APC) rally in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, on Saturday, 7 November 2026, Tinubu dismissed critics who point to economic hardship under his government, saying he was cleaning up the mess left by those who came before him and that Atiku was one of them. He also questioned how Atiku had built a university while he was vice-president, a remark widely seen as raising questions about how the former vice-president acquired his wealth while in public office.

Background

Atiku served as vice-president from 1999 to 2007 under Olusegun Obasanjo. He founded the American University of Nigeria in Yola, Adamawa State, and has long said his wealth comes from legitimate private business interests. He has repeatedly blamed Tinubu's policies, including the way fuel subsidy removal was handled, for rising prices. Tinubu's Rivers visit follows his trip to Akwa Ibom on Friday, where he said the naira would have become worthless without his reforms. FCT Minister Nyesom Wike, whose Rainbow Coalition backs the President, is a key ally in Rivers, and Governor Siminalayi Fubara has also declared support for Tinubu.

Atiku's position

Atiku's campaign had not publicly responded to the remark at the time of writing. Atiku has vowed to stay in the race despite a court ruling that voided his nomination, which his party has appealed.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027, and campaigns by all the major parties are expected to intensify in the coming weeks.

Thugs reportedly destroy vehicles of NDC supporters at Delta rally

7 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Videos from a Nigeria Democratic Congress rally in Delta State reportedly show suspected thugs smashing vehicles belonging to party supporters, with some supporters said to have fought back. Police have not commented.

Read more

Tension broke out at a rally of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) in Delta State on Saturday, 7 November 2026, after suspected thugs were reportedly filmed destroying vehicles belonging to party supporters.

What happened

Footage shared on social media appears to show a group of men vandalising vehicles at or near the rally venue. According to reports, some NDC supporters retaliated after the attack, raising tensions further. It is not clear who the attackers were, how many vehicles were damaged or whether anyone was hurt. The police had not publicly commented on the incident at the time of writing.

Background

The NDC is the party of presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate, Rabiu Kwankwaso. Delta is governed by Sheriff Oborevwori, who joined the APC and has backed President Bola Tinubu's re-election. Political violence has already been reported on several sides ahead of the January 2027 elections: Obi's convoy was attacked in Calabar earlier this week, stones were thrown at a stage during a governor's campaign rally in Nasarawa, and APC supporters were attacked and a campaign vehicle burnt ahead of a rally in Katsina State last week. INEC has also warned of a plot to threaten and kidnap election workers.

How people reacted

Many social media users condemned the violence and expressed concern for the safety of supporters at political events, while some warned that the same thugs could be used to disrupt the elections.
