Uzodimma vs Wike? Imo governor rejects Rainbow Coalition, threatens to take back vehicles from APC members who work against the party

28 Oct 2026, 17:26 GMT

Political tension appears to be brewing in Imo State as Governor Hope Uzodimma sends a strong warning to members of the All Progressives Congress (APC), declaring that there is no room for a Rainbow Coalition in the state.

In remarks attributed to the governor, Uzodimma warned APC members against working outside the party's political structure, insisting that loyalty to the APC must remain their priority.

The governor reportedly went further by threatening to withdraw vehicles donated by Niger Delta leader High Chief Government Ekpemupolo, popularly known as Tompolo, from beneficiaries found supporting political arrangements outside the APC.

"No Rainbow Coalition in Imo State. If I see you work outside the APC party, I'll take the vehicles from you," Uzodimma was quoted as saying.

The governor also expressed appreciation to Tompolo for his generosity and pledged his support whenever the Niger Delta leader needed assistance in Imo State.

"I am very grateful to High Chief Tompolo. Anytime you need us in Imo State, let us know and we'll be there for you," he reportedly added.

Is Uzodimma drawing a political battle line against Wike?

The governor's reported declaration has raised questions about the political influence of the Minister of the Federal Capital Territory, Nyesom Wike, whose name has become associated with cross-party political alliances.

Wike, although a member of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP), serves in President Bola Tinubu's APC-led administration and has maintained political relationships across party lines. His political approach has often involved cooperation between politicians belonging to different parties, particularly in Rivers State.

However, Uzodimma's reported warning suggests that he is unwilling to accommodate a similar arrangement within the Imo APC.

Although the remarks could be interpreted as a message to Wike's political allies, they do not, on their own, establish that the two politicians are in a direct confrontation.

Questions over Tompolo's donated vehicles

Another interesting aspect of the governor's reported statement is his warning about withdrawing vehicles donated by Tompolo.

The remarks raise questions about whether the vehicles were intended as unconditional gifts or whether their continued use is tied to political loyalty. It also remains unclear what authority would govern the withdrawal of the vehicles from beneficiaries.

What does this mean for Imo politics?

With political calculations already taking shape ahead of the 2027 general elections, Uzodimma's reported position could signal an attempt to strengthen party discipline and prevent competing political alliances from gaining influence within the state.

For now, the bigger question is whether the governor's declaration is simply an internal warning to APC members or the beginning of a wider struggle over political influence.

One thing is certain: if these remarks are accurately reported, Governor Hope Uzodimma has made his position clear. He expects APC members in Imo State to remain loyal to the party.

But should vehicles donated to politicians be withdrawn simply because the beneficiaries choose to support a different political alliance? And could this be the beginning of a political disagreement between Uzodimma and Wike ahead of 2027?

Former All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has denied reports that he endorsed Peter Obi ahead of Nigeria's 2027 presidential election.

"The claim is false"

In a public statement dated 18 November 2026, Garba described the claim circulating online as false.

He said he has withdrawn from partisan politics and is now focusing on his business ventures and on Africa's digital economy.

His new focus

Garba named Crowwe, Tablars and Justitia SI among the projects currently receiving his attention.

He also urged the public to rely only on his official channels for accurate information about his activities.

Where did the story come from?

The origin of the endorsement claim remains unclear. No post, interview or public statement showing Garba endorsing Obi has been found.

What is clear is that Garba has publicly disputed the claim.

A familiar pattern

With the 2027 elections approaching, false claims about endorsements are spreading quickly on social media. It is the second time this week that a story about backing for Obi has been corrected, after Obi himself clarified that his Washington visit was not an endorsement from the US Congress.

Many feared dead as boat carrying over 300 passengers capsizes in Bayelsa

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A boat reportedly carrying more than 300 passengers to a burial ceremony has capsized in Southern Ijaw, Bayelsa State. Police say marine units have been deployed for rescue, but casualty figures are not yet known.

Read more

A boat said to be carrying more than 300 passengers has capsized in Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, with many people feared dead or missing.

What happened

The boat was travelling from Ayama community to Ukubie for a burial ceremony when it capsized at about noon on Friday, 20 November 2026, shortly after passing Oweikorogha community.

The exact number of passengers on board and the number of casualties had not been established at the time of the reports, but residents fear several people may have lost their lives.

Rescue efforts

The Bayelsa State Police Command spokesperson, DSP Musa Muhammad, confirmed the incident and said the Commissioner of Police had deployed the Marine Police Unit and the local divisional police officer for rescue operations. He said further details on casualties and missing persons would be released.

A former state chairman of the Maritime Workers Union also confirmed the incident.

What happens next

Rescue and recovery efforts are continuing, and official casualty figures are expected once they are confirmed.

A video of Bishop David Oyedepo speaking about church succession has gone viral, drawing attention amid the leadership dispute at The Fountain of Life Church involving Pastor Jimmy Odukoya.

What he said

In the clip, posted on Instagram on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, the founder of Living Faith Church Worldwide (Winners' Chapel) said planning for who comes next is one of the most important responsibilities of church leadership.

He said leadership transition in ministry is a spiritual process and not a family arrangement, warning against simply handing churches over to children. He pointed to Joshua succeeding Moses in the Bible as the model.

Why it is trending

The video spread widely as Nigerians debated the Fountain of Life crisis, where trustees say the late founder Taiwo Odukoya wanted another pastor to succeed him, while his son Jimmy insists his removal as senior pastor is invalid.
