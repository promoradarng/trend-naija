Peller floods viral 'Baba Jehovah' pastor's TikTok Live with gifts reportedly worth ₦3 million

6 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

TikTok streamer Peller has got people talking after surprising an elderly pastor popularly known as Jehovah, or Baba Jehovah, with a large wave of virtual gifts during a livestream.

What happened

In a video that spread widely on X on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Peller and fellow content creator Matta joined the pastor's TikTok Live and sent him gift after gift, including roses and a high-value 'Universe' gift that set off one of the app's full-screen animations. The gifts were reportedly worth around ₦3 million. As the alerts piled up on screen, the pastor prayed loudly for Peller and for his unborn child.

The controversy

The moment drew criticism because of what happened next. Matta reportedly told the elderly pastor to stand up and show appreciation to Peller, which he did, and then told him to roll on the floor as a sign of gratitude. Many viewers felt the request was disrespectful to an older man.

Background

Peller, whose real name is Habeeb Hamzat, is one of Nigeria's most popular TikTok streamers. He and his wife, fellow streamer Jarvis, announced in early October 2026 that they were expecting a child and later said it would be a girl. Peller has also been in the news recently after rapper YCee used him as an example while talking about the popularity of light-hearted online content. TikTok gifts can be converted into real money by the person receiving them, after the platform takes its share.

How people reacted

Reactions were split. Many fans praised Peller's generosity and said the gifts would make a real difference to the pastor, while others said the way he was asked to show gratitude went too far and urged creators to treat older people with more respect.

  • The gifts were reportedly worth about ₦3 million.
  • The pastor prayed for Peller and his unborn daughter.
  • Viewers criticised a request for him to roll on the floor.

Carter Efe and Peller miss out as India's Ashswag wins international streamer award

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Nigerian streamers Carter Efe and Peller lost out on the Best International Streamer prize at the 2026 Streamer Awards, which went to Indian streamer Ashswag.

Nigerian content creators Carter Efe and Peller have missed out on an international award at the 2026 Streamer Awards.

The result

The two were nominated for Best International Streamer after the organisers released the shortlist in October. At the ceremony, reported on Friday, 13 November 2026, the prize went to Indian streamer Ashswag, who beat the Nigerian pair and other nominees from around the world.

Why it mattered

The nominations put the Nigerian duo among creators from around the world competing for the category.

Peller and Phyna react

In a post on X on Thursday night, Peller thanked his fans, saying their loyalty meant more to him than any trophy and that the nomination itself was a blessing. He complained, however, that the result of his category was announced on the red carpet before the main ceremony began, which he called unfair. Reality star Phyna went further in a livestream, accusing the organisers of using the Nigerian nominees to attract Nigerian viewers and engagement with no real intention of giving them the award.

Background

Peller rose to prominence as one of Nigeria's biggest TikTok live streamers, while Carter Efe, a skit maker and musician, has also built a large streaming following.

Social media personality and relationship coach Blessing CEO has admitted that she did not have breast cancer, months after telling the public she was battling the disease.

What she said

In an interview with activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), reported on Thursday, 12 November 2026, she said a medical check-up had found a lump in her breast, but that it was not cancerous. She admitted that she used the discovery to tell people it was cancer because she wanted financial support. She said she first gave the impression that she was thinking of selling her car and house, before fans encouraged her to share her account details so they could donate.

How the donations ended

She said money started coming in from members of the public until VeryDarkMan questioned the fundraising and demanded proof of her diagnosis, after which the donations stopped.

VeryDarkMan criticised

VeryDarkMan later asked Nigerians to forgive her, drawing criticism from some social media users. Commentator Japhman, in a video on 12 November, accused him of a double standard, saying the activist's 'Ratel' movement is built on demanding accountability from others.

Presidency denies Tinubu's convoy was attacked after Friday prayers in Abuja

14 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

The Presidency has dismissed videos shared by ADC chieftain Dino Melaye that purportedly showed youths throwing stones at President Tinubu's convoy after Friday prayers in Abuja, calling them misleading and releasing its own footage.

Read more

The Presidency has denied claims that President Bola Tinubu's convoy was attacked by stone-throwing youths after he attended Friday prayers in Abuja.

The claim

Dino Melaye, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), shared a video on Friday, 13 November 2026, which he said showed young men throwing stones at a convoy belonging to the President after prayers at the Shagari Central Mosque in Area 1. Voices in the video can be heard chanting a Hausa phrase meaning 'we don't want you'.

The Presidency's response

O'tega Ogra, the President's senior special assistant on digital engagement and new media, disputed the account on X, describing the videos as misleading and linking them to opposition figures. He released a separate video showing Tinubu leaving the mosque in white attire and a black cap, with supporters waving and taking photographs as he walked to his vehicle. It has not been independently established where or when Melaye's video was recorded.

Background

The dispute comes amid rising concern about political violence ahead of the 2027 elections. On the same day, the President issued a statement condemning violence at political gatherings, directing security agencies to act firmly against thuggery and insisting that every party must be free to campaign anywhere. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
