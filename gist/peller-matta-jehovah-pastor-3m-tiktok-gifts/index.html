Peller floods viral 'Baba Jehovah' pastor's TikTok Live with gifts reportedly worth ₦3 million

6 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

TikTok streamer Peller has got people talking after surprising an elderly pastor popularly known as Jehovah, or Baba Jehovah, with a large wave of virtual gifts during a livestream.

What happened

In a video that spread widely on X on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Peller and fellow content creator Matta joined the pastor's TikTok Live and sent him gift after gift, including roses and a high-value 'Universe' gift that set off one of the app's full-screen animations. The gifts were reportedly worth around ₦3 million. As the alerts piled up on screen, the pastor prayed loudly for Peller and for his unborn child.

The controversy

The moment drew criticism because of what happened next. Matta reportedly told the elderly pastor to stand up and show appreciation to Peller, which he did, and then told him to roll on the floor as a sign of gratitude. Many viewers felt the request was disrespectful to an older man.

Background

Peller, whose real name is Habeeb Hamzat, is one of Nigeria's most popular TikTok streamers. He and his wife, fellow streamer Jarvis, announced in early October 2026 that they were expecting a child and later said it would be a girl. Peller has also been in the news recently after rapper YCee used him as an example while talking about the popularity of light-hearted online content. TikTok gifts can be converted into real money by the person receiving them, after the platform takes its share.

How people reacted

Reactions were split. Many fans praised Peller's generosity and said the gifts would make a real difference to the pastor, while others said the way he was asked to show gratitude went too far and urged creators to treat older people with more respect.

  • The gifts were reportedly worth about ₦3 million.
  • The pastor prayed for Peller and his unborn daughter.
  • Viewers criticised a request for him to roll on the floor.

Labour Party factional candidate Kennedy Ahanotu quits presidential race, backs Tinubu

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Kennedy Ahanotu, presidential candidate of the Abure-led Labour Party faction, has reportedly withdrawn from the 2027 race and endorsed President Tinubu. He blamed the party's long-running internal crisis.

Read more

Kennedy Ahanotu, who was chosen as presidential candidate by the Julius Abure-led faction of the Labour Party, has reportedly withdrawn from the 2027 presidential race and thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu.

What happened

Ahanotu is said to have announced the decision at a press briefing in Abuja on Monday, 16 November 2026. He said he would work with the City Boy Movement, a pro-Tinubu group, to mobilise support for the president's re-election.

The 42-year-old former national youth leader of the Labour Party, described as an ally of the party's 2023 candidate Peter Obi, reportedly said the move followed consultations with youth leaders from across the country, who concluded that the party's continuing internal crisis made a new direction necessary.

Background

Ahanotu was adopted as the factional candidate at primaries held in Abuja in May 2026, when the Labour Party was divided between the Abure-led group and the leadership headed by Senator Nenadi Usman. Peter Obi has since moved on to the NDC, where he is running with Rabiu Kwankwaso.

Betrayal claims

Responding to suggestions that he had betrayed the opposition, Ahanotu reportedly argued that it was the party that had failed its long-standing members, noting that he had joined it in 2016.

No response from either Labour Party faction was included in the reports.

Sanwo-Olu gives Flamingos ₦10m each and iPhones after U-17 World Cup win

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has reportedly rewarded the Flamingos with ₦10 million each, iPhone 18 Pro Max phones and other gifts. It follows earlier rewards from President Tinubu and Senate President Akpabio.

Read more

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has reportedly rewarded the Flamingos, Nigeria's under-17 women's team, with ₦10 million each and other gifts after their historic win at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The reward

The governor announced the package at a dinner held in honour of the players and technical crew at Lagos House, Marina, on Sunday. It is said to include a total cash reward of ₦300 million, iPhone 18 Pro Max phones, headphones and customised outfits.

Other rewards

The gesture follows President Bola Tinubu's announcement of ₦100 million for each player and for head coach Akeem Busari, with other members of the technical crew promised ₦50 million each. The presidential reward was announced by First Lady Oluremi Tinubu when she hosted the team in Abuja. Senate President Godswill Akpabio also announced a ₦100 million donation to the team after their return.

The historic win

The Flamingos made history on 7 November by beating Japan 2-1 in Rabat, becoming the first African team to win the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.
