Peller floods viral 'Baba Jehovah' pastor's TikTok Live with gifts reportedly worth ₦3 million

6 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

TikTok streamer Peller has got people talking after surprising an elderly pastor popularly known as Jehovah, or Baba Jehovah, with a large wave of virtual gifts during a livestream.

What happened

In a video that spread widely on X on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Peller and fellow content creator Matta joined the pastor's TikTok Live and sent him gift after gift, including roses and a high-value 'Universe' gift that set off one of the app's full-screen animations. The gifts were reportedly worth around ₦3 million. As the alerts piled up on screen, the pastor prayed loudly for Peller and for his unborn child.

The controversy

The moment drew criticism because of what happened next. Matta reportedly told the elderly pastor to stand up and show appreciation to Peller, which he did, and then told him to roll on the floor as a sign of gratitude. Many viewers felt the request was disrespectful to an older man.

Background

Peller, whose real name is Habeeb Hamzat, is one of Nigeria's most popular TikTok streamers. He and his wife, fellow streamer Jarvis, announced in early October 2026 that they were expecting a child and later said it would be a girl. Peller has also been in the news recently after rapper YCee used him as an example while talking about the popularity of light-hearted online content. TikTok gifts can be converted into real money by the person receiving them, after the platform takes its share.

How people reacted

Reactions were split. Many fans praised Peller's generosity and said the gifts would make a real difference to the pastor, while others said the way he was asked to show gratitude went too far and urged creators to treat older people with more respect.

  • The gifts were reportedly worth about ₦3 million.
  • The pastor prayed for Peller and his unborn daughter.
  • Viewers criticised a request for him to roll on the floor.

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

VeryDarkMan accuses Davido of hypocrisy over photo with Seyi Tinubu

VeryDarkMan has called out Davido over a photo with Seyi Tinubu in which both appeared to raise their hands in a gesture he read as the APC's "4+4" slogan. He said it contradicts the singer's past stance against the APC.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has reportedly called out Afrobeats star Davido after a photo of the singer with Seyi Tinubu, son of President Bola Tinubu, circulated online.

The photo

The picture showed Davido and Seyi Tinubu raising their hands in a gesture VDM interpreted as the APC's "4+4" slogan, which supporters use to call for a second term for President Tinubu in 2027.

What VDM said

VDM said the photo was significant given Davido's status and his previous statements that he would never support the All Progressives Congress (APC). He accused the singer of contradicting himself and questioned why he would pose in a picture that could be read as a political statement.

He also said that his past claims always end up being proved right, dismissing what he called propaganda against him.

Background

Davido had not responded to VDM's comments at the time of the reports.

Isaac Fayose defends Davido

Footage also emerged of Davido and his wife Chioma sharing a meal with Seyi Tinubu at an amala restaurant in Abuja, which many took as a sign he was moving towards the Tinubu camp. Isaac Fayose, brother of former Ekiti governor Ayo Fayose, came to the singer's defence in a video, insisting Davido would still vote for Peter Obi. Davido reportedly reacted to Fayose's video.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.

Join us in wishing VDM a quick recovery. Have you ever tried to treat malaria yourself before going to hospital?

Former senator Aishatu Binani announces death of her father

21 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Former Adamawa governorship candidate and ex-senator Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, known as Binani, has announced the death of her father after a brief illness. His funeral prayer was fixed for Saturday at the Central Mosque.

Read more

Former senator and Adamawa State governorship candidate Aishatu Dahiru Ahmed, popularly known as Binani, has announced the death of her father.

The announcement

In a post on social media, Binani said her father died after a brief illness in Abuja. She opened her message with the Islamic expression of condolence and thanked God despite the loss.

Funeral arrangements

She said the funeral prayer, known as Jana'iza, would take place on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the Central Mosque after the afternoon Zuhr prayer.

Who she is

Binani is a former senator who contested the Adamawa governorship election in 2023. She is now a member of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).
