Peller floods viral 'Baba Jehovah' pastor's TikTok Live with gifts reportedly worth ₦3 million

6 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

TikTok streamer Peller has got people talking after surprising an elderly pastor popularly known as Jehovah, or Baba Jehovah, with a large wave of virtual gifts during a livestream.

What happened

In a video that spread widely on X on Thursday, 5 November 2026, Peller and fellow content creator Matta joined the pastor's TikTok Live and sent him gift after gift, including roses and a high-value 'Universe' gift that set off one of the app's full-screen animations. The gifts were reportedly worth around ₦3 million. As the alerts piled up on screen, the pastor prayed loudly for Peller and for his unborn child.

The controversy

The moment drew criticism because of what happened next. Matta reportedly told the elderly pastor to stand up and show appreciation to Peller, which he did, and then told him to roll on the floor as a sign of gratitude. Many viewers felt the request was disrespectful to an older man.

Background

Peller, whose real name is Habeeb Hamzat, is one of Nigeria's most popular TikTok streamers. He and his wife, fellow streamer Jarvis, announced in early October 2026 that they were expecting a child and later said it would be a girl. Peller has also been in the news recently after rapper YCee used him as an example while talking about the popularity of light-hearted online content. TikTok gifts can be converted into real money by the person receiving them, after the platform takes its share.

How people reacted

Reactions were split. Many fans praised Peller's generosity and said the gifts would make a real difference to the pastor, while others said the way he was asked to show gratitude went too far and urged creators to treat older people with more respect.

  • The gifts were reportedly worth about ₦3 million.
  • The pastor prayed for Peller and his unborn daughter.
  • Viewers criticised a request for him to roll on the floor.

VeryDarkMan challenges Zlatan Ibile to deny sending celebrities to beg him

8 Nov 2026, 17:19 GMT

VeryDarkMan has dared Zlatan Ibile to publicly deny that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with him over their ongoing feud, accusing the singer of acting tough in public while trying to settle the matter behind the scenes. He has threatened to name the celebrities.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has continued his public exchange with singer Zlatan Ibile, daring him to openly deny claims that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with VDM over their ongoing disagreement.

"Just come out and say it's a lie"

Speaking partly in Pidgin, VDM said, in effect: "It's been over 24 hours since I dared Zlatan to come out and say I'm lying that he sent people to beg me. He acts like a hard guy in public, but does something else behind the scenes."

"Just come out and say it's a lie, simple! Let me call out the names of the celebrities you sent. If they deny it too, then you'll see what my next post will be."

He added that he was only holding back out of respect for Zlatan's parents: "It's because your mother and father are involved. We still respect parents here. If not, everyone would already know that you have no class."

Tough in public, soft in private?

VDM claims Zlatan is acting tough in public while making moves behind the scenes to settle the matter, and has threatened to name the celebrities he says were sent if Zlatan stays silent.

The feud began after a video of Davido singing a line from Zlatan's song INFERNO at Zlatan's wedding welcome party in Morocco, a lyric VDM believes was aimed at him. VDM then warned Zlatan that he would "learn the hard way".

Allegations, not facts

For now, these remain VeryDarkMan's claims. Zlatan has not responded publicly, and his silence does not confirm them.

Over to you

Will Zlatan respond, or will he let VDM keep controlling the conversation?

Gunman in Krakow Main Square shooting that killed six dies in hospital

9 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The 24-year-old suspected gunman in a shooting in Krakow's Main Square that killed six people and injured about 16 has died in hospital, Polish police say. Three of his relatives were also found dead at his home.

Read more

The suspected gunman in a deadly shooting in the centre of Krakow, Poland, has died in hospital, police say.

What happened

The shooting took place in the city's Main Square on the afternoon of Saturday, 7 November 2026. Six people, four women and two men, were killed and about 16 others were injured, including visitors from the United Kingdom, Portugal, Russia, Germany and Austria. Police said the 24-year-old suspect was arrested on a nearby street after trying to escape in a car, and that two officers were injured in an exchange of fire. Three more bodies were found at his home in Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski, about 150 kilometres from Krakow, and he is believed to have also killed three members of his own family.

The suspect dies

In an update on Monday, 9 November, regional police confirmed that the suspect had died in hospital that morning. He is believed to have used an AR-15-style rifle, and ammunition was recovered from his car. Authorities have not given a clear motive, but said he was fascinated by far-right ideologies.

Background

Mass shootings are rare in Poland, which has strict gun laws.

Kano APC council chairman freed after 68 days in kidnappers' hands

9 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Kano police say they have rescued Abdullahi Alasan Beli, APC chairman in Rogo LGA, after 68 days in captivity. He says his abductors moved him through parts of Kaduna and Zamfara states.

Read more

Kano State police say they have rescued the All Progressives Congress (APC) chairman in Rogo Local Government Area, Abdullahi Alasan Beli, popularly known as Abdullahi Fanka, after 68 days in captivity.

The rescue

Commissioner of Police Adamu Bakori announced the rescue while briefing Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf at Government House, Kano, on Sunday, 8 November 2026, according to a statement by the state Commissioner for Information, Sanusi Bature Dawakin Tofa. The police chief said the rescue was achieved through coordinated tactical operations, and that the command would step up efforts against kidnapping.

His ordeal

Beli was abducted by armed men from his home in Beli community, Rogo LGA. After his release, he said his kidnappers moved him through several places, including Danja, Ikara and Giwa in Kaduna State, as well as parts of Zamfara State.

Reactions

Governor Yusuf, who belongs to the NNPP and is an opponent of the APC, commended the police for securing the release of the APC chieftain.

Background

Kidnapping for ransom has affected parts of Kano, especially areas bordering Katsina and Kaduna states. It was not disclosed whether any ransom was paid.

Makinde visits families of Jos Palm Sunday attack victims

9 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

APM presidential candidate Seyi Makinde has visited Angwan Rukuba in Jos North, Plateau State, to console families of the 33 people killed in an attack on 29 March, telling residents not to give up on Nigeria.

Read more

Oyo State governor Seyi Makinde, presidential candidate of the Allied Peoples Movement (APM), has visited Angwan Rukuba in Jos North, Plateau State, to console families of victims of a deadly attack earlier this year.

The visit

During the visit on Sunday, 8 November 2026, Makinde met residents at the home of the community head and described the killings as a national tragedy rather than a local matter. He urged the grieving community not to give up on Nigeria and assured them they were not alone. He also visited the family of Promise Gideon Ayuba, one of the victims, whose mother received him and prayed for him.

The attack

Armed men on motorcycles attacked the Gari Ya Waye area of the community on the evening of 29 March 2026, Palm Sunday, killing 33 people. The victim's mother, Favour Ayuba, drew national attention after a video showed her placing a cutlass in her son's coffin as a demand for justice. She was also among relatives who met former Archbishop of Canterbury Justin Welby when he visited Plateau in September.

Background

Plateau State has suffered repeated deadly attacks in recent years, with communities in Jos and surrounding areas affected. Makinde is campaigning across the country ahead of the 16 January 2027 presidential election, and his campaign this week also criticised President Bola Tinubu's claims about the naira.
