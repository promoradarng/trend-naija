Tinubu prays inside the Kaaba as Umrah trip ends, critics respond

2 Nov 2026, 14:56 GMT

President Bola Tinubu has wrapped up his visit to Saudi Arabia, saying he and members of his delegation were granted the rare privilege of entering the Holy Kaaba in Mecca to pray during their Umrah, the lesser pilgrimage.

What the President said

In a statement on his official social media account on Monday, 2 November 2026, Tinubu thanked King Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman for the invitation and for their hospitality. He said that inside the Kaaba he prayed for peace, unity and prosperity for Nigeria and all Nigerians, and described the gesture as a sign of the long-standing bonds of faith and friendship between the two countries.

In a further statement released through his adviser Bayo Onanuga, Tinubu said he had asked God for wisdom and resilience to keep serving the people. Referring to a verse of the Quran, he said all power belongs to God, who can give leadership to anyone and take it away, and described being chosen as president out of about 200 million people as a great favour.

Why it matters

The Kaaba, inside the Grand Mosque in Mecca, is Islam's most sacred site. Entry into the structure itself is rarely allowed and is usually granted only to heads of state and other dignitaries invited by the Saudi royal family.

How people reacted

Supporters praised the President and prayed for him to lead well. Critics questioned the timing of the trip so close to the 2027 elections. Former senator Dino Melaye, an opposition figure, challenged Tinubu on X to a Quran recitation contest when he returns. Last week, Oyo governor and APM presidential candidate Seyi Makinde asked the Presidency to be more open about the purpose of the trip, while activist Omoyele Sowore claimed it was for medical treatment. The Presidency rejected that claim, saying the President travelled for Umrah, as he did twice in 2022.

What happens next

The President is expected back in Abuja in the coming days as campaigning for the January 2027 elections intensifies.

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf says his wife was taken from his home before she went to court

22 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly given more details about the breakdown of his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso. In a radio interview he said her belongings were moved out of his house without his knowledge, and that he was shocked when she filed for divorce in Abuja.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly opened up further about the collapse of his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, a relative of NDC vice-presidential candidate Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso.

What the governor said

Speaking in a Hausa-language interview on Sunday, 22 November 2026, Yusuf is said to have described family matters as extremely difficult. He reportedly said he and Zainab married about 20 years ago and have children together.

According to the reports, the governor said her belongings were moved out of his house and she was taken away from his home without his knowledge, but that he stayed patient and said nothing in public. He said he had even assured their children that their mother would come back, and was shocked to learn that she had gone to court, in Abuja rather than in Kano.

Yusuf reportedly declined to give details of how she left, but said he had witnesses. Asked why he was speaking publicly, he said he was not the one who had made the matter political.

Background

Earlier this week, Zainab Kwankwaso reportedly filed for divorce at an Upper Area Court in Abuja. The governor later told pensioners at Government House in Kano that he had accepted the dissolution of the marriage and still planned to marry again.

The split has drawn wide attention because Kwankwaso was long the governor's political mentor before the two fell out.

The other side

No response from Zainab Kwankwaso or Rabiu Kwankwaso was included in the reports.

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari wearing attire printed with President Tinubu's campaign logo has sparked debate. It came days after ADC chieftain Solomon Dalung claimed she had told Atiku that Buhari's supporters would back him.

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari, widow of the late President Muhammadu Buhari, wearing clothing printed with President Bola Tinubu's campaign logo has stirred political debate ahead of the 2027 elections.

The video

The clip was posted on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by former presidential aide Bashir Ahmad, with a caption describing it as a message from Daura, Buhari's hometown.

Why it matters

The video came days after Solomon Dalung, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), claimed in a television interview that Aisha Buhari had visited ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar and told him that the late President's large voter base would be backing him in 2027.

Many social media users took the new video as a response to that claim, suggesting the former First Lady is supporting Tinubu.

No statement

Aisha Buhari herself had not made a public statement on her political preference at the time of the reports.

Peter Obi says insecurity stops him walking freely in Nigeria, unlike in the UK

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi says security warnings prevent him from taking simple walks in Nigeria, recalling how he walked and took trains freely while living in London.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has spoken about worsening insecurity in Nigeria, saying safety warnings now stop him from taking simple walks or jogging in his own neighbourhood.

What he said

Addressing the Global Peter Obi Supporters Summit in Abuja on Saturday, 21 November 2026, the former Anambra governor recalled living in the UK, on the same street as the Mayor of London. He said they would walk the streets and take the train without security escorts.

He contrasted that with Nigeria today, recalling that he used to walk freely to church in Lagos in the 1980s and 1990s, but said security concerns now prevent him from doing so.

The campaign

Insecurity is one of the major issues in the 2027 campaign, ahead of the 16 January election.

Tinubu arrives in Lagos for family prayer session after Ibadan rally

21 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

President Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday after his campaign rally in Ibadan, ahead of a special prayer session his family has organised for him on Sunday. He was received by Governors Sanwo-Olu and Abiodun.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday, 21 November 2026, after addressing an APC presidential campaign rally in Ibadan, Oyo State.

The arrival

His aircraft landed at the Presidential Wing of the Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja at about 3pm. He was received by Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, Ogun Governor Dapo Abiodun, his Chief of Staff Femi Gbajabiamila and former Ogun governor Segun Osoba, along with party leaders and supporters.

Family prayer

The visit is centred on a special prayer session organised in his honour by members of his family, scheduled for Sunday, 22 November. The President is expected to return to Abuja afterwards.

The campaign

The trip comes as the APC steps up its campaign across the South-West. At the Ibadan rally, Tinubu endorsed the party's governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos and promised improved electricity supply.
