Governor Alex Otti opens Abia State facilities to opposition parties, says politics should not deny anyone access

2 Nov 2026, 12:03 GMT

Abia State Governor, Dr Alex Otti, has made it clear that opposition political parties should be allowed to use government-owned facilities in the state, provided they follow the proper procedures and pay the required fees.

The directive

According to the governor, he has already given instructions that no political party should be prevented from using public facilities, including the stadium, simply because it belongs to the opposition.

A message of political tolerance

Otti's position sends an important message about tolerance and fairness. Government facilities are maintained with public resources, and access to them should not depend on which party someone supports.

This is one area where Nigerian politicians need to learn to separate governance from party politics. You can disagree with your opponents and campaign against them, and still respect their right to operate within the law.

Governor Otti appears to be making that distinction clear in Abia State.

The big question

How many Nigerian governors would willingly allow opposition parties to use government facilities for their political activities?

Politics should never be an excuse to deny citizens their rights.

  • Opposition parties can use Abia facilities, including the stadium.
  • They must follow due process and pay the required fees.
  • Otti says he has already given the directive.

President Tinubu seen kissing the sacred Black Stone during Umrah in Makkah

2 Nov 2026, 20:39 GMT

President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has been seen kissing the Black Stone, known as Al-Hajar Al-Aswad, while performing Umrah in Makkah, Saudi Arabia. Photos show him at the eastern corner of the Kaaba, surrounded by security officials.

President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has been seen kissing the sacred Black Stone, known as Al-Hajar Al-Aswad, while performing Umrah in the holy city of Makkah, Saudi Arabia.

Photos from the visit show the President, dressed in white, leaning into the silver frame that holds the stone as Saudi security officers stand by. Another photo shows him with members of his delegation in the courtyard of the Grand Mosque.

About the Black Stone

The Black Stone sits at the eastern corner of the Kaaba, inside the Grand Mosque, and holds deep religious significance for Muslims around the world.

Kissing the stone is a practice associated with Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), and many pilgrims seek the chance to do so whenever they visit the holy site.

A spiritual journey

For Muslims, Umrah is a spiritual journey marked by prayer, worship and personal reflection.

The moment has also drawn attention to the religious side of Nigeria's leader as he performs one of Islam's important acts of worship.

National Assembly considering 74 extra seats reserved for women, says Senate leader

2 Nov 2026, 18:01 GMT

Senate Leader Opeyemi Bamidele says lawmakers are considering a constitutional amendment to add 37 women-only seats each to the Senate and House of Representatives. The arrangement would last 16 years before a review.

Read more

The National Assembly is considering a constitutional amendment that would create 74 new legislative seats reserved for women, Senate Leader Opeyemi Bamidele has said.

The proposal

Speaking on Monday, 2 November 2026, at a Law Week event organised by the Ekiti State branch of the International Federation of Women Lawyers, Bamidele reportedly said the plan would add 37 seats in the Senate and 37 in the House of Representatives, giving each state and the Federal Capital Territory one extra women-only seat in each chamber. He said the constitution review committee has also proposed three extra seats for women in each state house of assembly, and stressed that the new seats would be added to the existing ones rather than replacing any.

How long it would last

Bamidele, who is vice-chairman of the Senate committee reviewing the 1999 Constitution, said the arrangement would run for 16 years, or four election cycles, before a full review. He described this as a sunset clause meant to allow a more inclusive political culture to develop.

What happens next

Constitutional amendments need the approval of two-thirds of members of both chambers of the National Assembly and of at least 24 of the 36 state houses of assembly. Bamidele said the National Assembly was engaging state lawmakers to secure their support.

Background

Women hold only a small share of seats in Nigeria's National Assembly, one of the lowest rates in Africa. A similar bill on special seats for women failed in 2022, which led to protests by women's groups. Supporters say reserved seats would give women a stronger voice in law-making, while critics argue they would add to the cost of governance.

Tinubu prays inside the Kaaba as Umrah trip ends, critics respond

2 Nov 2026, 14:56 GMT

President Bola Tinubu says he was allowed to enter the Holy Kaaba in Mecca with his delegation, where he prayed for peace, unity and prosperity for Nigeria. Supporters welcomed the rare honour, while Dino Melaye challenged him to a Quran recitation contest.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has wrapped up his visit to Saudi Arabia, saying he and members of his delegation were granted the rare privilege of entering the Holy Kaaba in Mecca to pray during their Umrah, the lesser pilgrimage.

What the President said

In a statement on his official social media account on Monday, 2 November 2026, Tinubu thanked King Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman for the invitation and for their hospitality. He said that inside the Kaaba he prayed for peace, unity and prosperity for Nigeria and all Nigerians, and described the gesture as a sign of the long-standing bonds of faith and friendship between the two countries.

In a further statement released through his adviser Bayo Onanuga, Tinubu said he had asked God for wisdom and resilience to keep serving the people. Referring to a verse of the Quran, he said all power belongs to God, who can give leadership to anyone and take it away, and described being chosen as president out of about 200 million people as a great favour.

Why it matters

The Kaaba, inside the Grand Mosque in Mecca, is Islam's most sacred site. Entry into the structure itself is rarely allowed and is usually granted only to heads of state and other dignitaries invited by the Saudi royal family.

How people reacted

Supporters praised the President and prayed for him to lead well. Critics questioned the timing of the trip so close to the 2027 elections. Former senator Dino Melaye, an opposition figure, challenged Tinubu on X to a Quran recitation contest when he returns. Last week, Oyo governor and APM presidential candidate Seyi Makinde asked the Presidency to be more open about the purpose of the trip, while activist Omoyele Sowore claimed it was for medical treatment. The Presidency rejected that claim, saying the President travelled for Umrah, as he did twice in 2022.

What happens next

The President is expected back in Abuja in the coming days as campaigning for the January 2027 elections intensifies.

Court orders arrest of Lagos NURTW chairman Sego over alleged contempt

2 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

The National Industrial Court in Lagos has ordered the arrest of Lagos NURTW chairman Mustapha Adekunle, known as Sego, and the union's secretary, after they failed to appear to explain why they should not be jailed for contempt. The case arises from a dispute with tricycle operators.

Read more

The National Industrial Court in Lagos has ordered the arrest and detention of the Lagos State chairman of the National Union of Road Transport Workers (NURTW), Mustapha Adekunle, popularly known as Sego, and the union's secretary, Usman Teslim.

What the court said

Justice Isaac Essien gave the order on Monday, 2 November 2026, after the two officials failed to appear in court to show why they should not be sent to prison for contempt. According to reports, the judge described their conduct as brazen disobedience of court orders and warned that the authority of the courts must not be undermined. He directed the Lagos Commissioner of Police, the state commandant of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps and other security agencies to arrest the two men and hand them over to the officer in charge of the Kirikiri Correctional Centre.

Bailiff obstruction

The court also raised concern over an affidavit filed by its bailiff, who said he was prevented from serving court papers on 5 October by a security officer linked to one of the parties in the case. Justice Essien reportedly described the alleged obstruction as a dangerous development.

Background

The case stems from a long-running dispute between commercial tricycle operators and the NURTW in Lagos over control of operations and levies. The union has been at the centre of several leadership and legal battles in the state in recent years. Sego and the union have not publicly responded to the order.

What happens next

The officials can still appear before the court or challenge the order on appeal. It is not yet clear whether security agencies have acted on the directive.
