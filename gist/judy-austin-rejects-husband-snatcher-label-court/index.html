Judy Austin tells court she is not a 'husband snatcher'

25 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Nollywood actress Judy Austin has rejected the 'husband snatcher' label that has followed her relationship with actor Yul Edochie, while giving evidence at an Abuja High Court.

In court

Austin appeared on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, in proceedings in her defamation cases against veteran actress Rita Edochie, Yul's aunt. Under cross-examination by Rita's lawyer, she denied snatching Yul from anyone and said she did not belong to any group of husband snatchers.

She refused to answer questions about her former marriage to Emmanuel Obasi or her current marriage to Yul, insisting that her marital affairs are private.

Background

Austin is suing Rita Edochie over comments she says were defamatory. Yul Edochie, who took Austin as his second wife, accompanied her to court. A video also showed Rita Edochie confronting people she believed were filming her at the court premises.

Sad timing

The court appearance came a day before the family of Yul's father, Nollywood legend Pete Edochie, announced his death at the age of 79.

The handover

The transition took place at the association's election, award and swearing-in ceremony, where veteran actor and filmmaker Akeem Alimi, popularly called Ajala Jalingo, was unveiled as the new national president.

Background

Makinde camp denies claim he will step down for Atiku

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde's campaign has dismissed as fake a claim that he plans to withdraw his presidential bid in favour of Atiku Abubakar on 30 November. The claim was made by a political commentator.

Read more

The campaign of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has dismissed as fake news a claim that he plans to drop his 2027 presidential bid in favour of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar.

The claim

Political commentator Lere Olayinka had written that Makinde, the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) candidate, would step down for Atiku on 30 November, the date of the opposition parties' summit in Abuja.

The denial

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, the verified X account linked to Makinde's campaign rejected the claim and accused the ruling APC of spreading falsehoods. The post drew both support and criticism from social media users.

Background

Makinde said this week that he remains open to working with Atiku and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu, but ruled out a formal merger of parties. Opposition parties are due to hold their second summit in Abuja on Monday. No response from the APC to the accusation was included in the reports.
