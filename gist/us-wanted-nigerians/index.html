Nigeria confirms an Ebola case in Abuja as health officials trace 44 contacts

25 Oct 2026, 03:33 GMT

The Nigeria Centre for Disease Control says a woman who arrived from Uganda has tested positive for Ebola in Abuja, the country's first case in about a decade. A toll-free line, 6232, has been opened for suspected cases, while some Nigerians question how long detection took.
