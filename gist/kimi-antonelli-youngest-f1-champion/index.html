Kimi Antonelli, 20, becomes youngest Formula 1 world champion

2 Nov 2026, 14:56 GMT

Italian driver Kimi Antonelli has become the youngest world champion in the history of Formula 1, according to reports, after sealing the title at the Mexico City Grand Prix on Sunday, 1 November 2026.

What happened

Antonelli, who drives for Mercedes, needed only a ninth-place finish to make sure of the championship. He finished third, behind race winner Max Verstappen of Red Bull and Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, which put the title out of reach of his rivals with four races of the season still left. Hamilton and his Mercedes team-mate George Russell had been his closest challengers.

Records broken

At 20 years and 68 days, Antonelli breaks the record set by Sebastian Vettel, who was 23 years and 134 days old when he won his first title with Red Bull in 2010. He is also the first Italian to win the drivers' championship since Alberto Ascari in 1953, ending a wait of 73 years for the country. Remarkably, he is only in his second season in Formula 1.

What he said

Celebrating with his family and the Mercedes team, Antonelli reportedly thanked both, saying he would not be there without them. Verstappen was among the first to congratulate him and said he was very proud of the young driver, adding that it must be an emotional day for his family.

Background

Antonelli was picked by Mercedes as a teenager to replace Hamilton, who moved to Ferrari in 2025. Many questioned whether he was ready for such a big seat, but he has now delivered the team's first drivers' title since Hamilton's last championship in 2020.

What happens next

The remaining four races will decide the other places in the drivers' standings and the constructors' championship.

  • Antonelli won the title at 20 years and 68 days.
  • He beats Sebastian Vettel's 2010 record.
  • He is Italy's first F1 champion since 1953.

Aubameyang crashes Ferrari on way to training in Spain, no serious injuries

Former Arsenal striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang was involved in a crash on his way to training with Deportivo La Coruña. His Ferrari collided with another car, but no one was seriously hurt.

Former Arsenal and Chelsea striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang was involved in a road accident on his way to training with his Spanish club, Deportivo La Coruña, on Thursday, 26 November 2026.

The crash

The 37-year-old Gabon international was driving a Ferrari, reportedly worth around €380,000, when it collided with a BMW on the AP-9 motorway in north-west Spain at about 9:30am local time. The BMW overturned in the impact and had to be towed away.

No serious injuries

Emergency services, including the Civil Guard, firefighters and paramedics, attended the scene. Despite the force of the collision, no one was seriously injured.

FIFA says hackers stole personal and business information in cyberattack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

FIFA has confirmed it was the victim of a cyberattack in which criminals gained access to a limited set of personal and business information. It is working with law enforcement.

Read more

World football's governing body FIFA has confirmed that it was hit by a cyberattack in which hackers gained unauthorised access to personal and business information.

What FIFA said

In a statement on Thursday, 26 November 2026, FIFA said it had been the victim of a criminal cyberattack that gave access to a limited set of personal and business information. It said it was working with the relevant authorities, including law enforcement, to investigate.

The organisation strongly condemned the unlawful taking and publication of the information, particularly personal data, describing it as a serious violation of privacy that could have significant consequences for the people affected.

Unanswered questions

FIFA did not say when the attack happened, what kind of information was accessed or how many people were affected.

The forward shared several congratulatory messages from friends on her Instagram Stories to mark the achievement.

Oshoala, who now plays for Al Hilal in the Saudi Women's Premier League, holds the record for the most CAF Women's Player of the Year titles, having won the award six times, in 2014, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2022 and 2023. She is one of the most decorated players in African women's football.
