Nkem Owoh denies saying Yoruba founded Nollywood, credits them with pioneering films

8 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Veteran Nollywood actor Nkem Owoh, popularly known as Osuofia, has addressed comments attributed to him about who founded Nigeria's film industry.

What he said

Speaking on the YouTube interview show 'Curiosity Made Me Ask', hosted by content creator Isbae U, in an episode uploaded on Saturday, 7 November 2026, Owoh rejected reports that he had said the Yoruba people started Nollywood rather than the Igbo. He explained that the word 'Nollywood' did not even exist when he and his colleagues first began making films. However, he said Yoruba filmmakers had indeed pioneered the home-video tradition in Nigeria, giving particular credit to the late theatre and film pioneer Hubert Ogunde. When the host jokingly suggested Ogunde might have been Igbo, Owoh insisted that he was Yoruba.

The wider debate

The question of who built Nollywood has been debated for years. In August 2026, the late actor Francis Duru argued that Igbo businesspeople were the real architects of the home-video boom of the 1990s and early 2000s, while accepting that the Yoruba film community had become more dominant through stronger organisation. Owoh was reported at the time to have pointed out that Yoruba filmmakers were active in theatre and film long before the video boom. Duru died in October 2026.

Background

Owoh is one of Nollywood's best-loved comic actors, famous for the 'Osuofia in London' films and the song 'I Go Chop Your Dollar'. 'Curiosity Made Me Ask' is one of Nigeria's most popular celebrity interview shows.

  • Owoh says he never claimed the Yoruba founded Nollywood.
  • He credited Hubert Ogunde as a pioneer.
  • He spoke on 'Curiosity Made Me Ask'.

Nollywood actress Anike Sinleke dies after battle with ovarian cancer

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Nollywood actress Adenike Abike Babajide, known as Anike Sinleke, has reportedly died after battling ovarian cancer. Her death was announced on Sunday by an Instagram show host she was due to appear with that day.

Nollywood is mourning the death of actress Adenike Abike Babajide, popularly known as Anike Sinleke, who has reportedly died after a battle with ovarian cancer.

How the news broke

The news emerged on Sunday, 22 November 2026, in a post by Bukola Fagbuyi, host of the Instagram live show Rubbin Minds With Bukky. Fagbuyi said the actress had been scheduled to appear as a guest on the programme that same day.

Her career

Anike Sinleke learned her craft under the mentorship of veteran actor Remmy Shitta and worked on film sets with filmmaker Deji Adenuga. At one point she stepped away from acting to complete her Higher National Diploma.

Beyond the screen, she was known in Lagos entertainment and cultural circles for her role with the Sisi Eko beauty pageant, which started around 2011.

Tributes

Fans and colleagues have taken to social media to mourn her, with many expressing shock at the news.

Aisha Lawal opens up on building collapse that wiped out her savings

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Actress Aisha Lawal has reportedly spoken about how a building she put her life savings and loans into collapsed in Maryland, Lagos. She shared the story in an interview with Chude Jideonwo.

Read more

Nollywood actress Aisha Lawal has reportedly opened up about one of the hardest periods of her life, when a building she invested in collapsed.

What she said

Speaking on the interview series WithChude, hosted by Chude Jideonwo, Lawal said she put her savings, investments and borrowed money into a property project in the Maryland area of Lagos, made up of four flats and a duplex. She said the building was finished down to the tiles and ceiling work.

The day it fell

She recalled returning from a stage rehearsal when she had a sudden thought to sell the property rather than move in. When she reached the estate, she reportedly found emergency agency vehicles and heavy machinery on the ground, and her phone was flooded with messages about the collapse.

Background

Lawal is a well-known actress and stage performer. She has recently spoken publicly about other personal matters, including the end of her marriage.

Jim Iyke finally shows off his wife after almost five years together

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has shared photos and videos of his wife for the first time, revealing how they met on a flight to Houston almost five years ago.

Read more

Nollywood actor Jim Iyke has introduced his wife to the public for the first time, sharing loved-up photos and videos of the couple on Instagram.

How they met

In the post, the actor said they met almost five years ago in an airport lounge before a flight to Houston. He recalled asking if the seat next to her was free, and said she firmly told him it was taken, but he sat down anyway. They ignored each other for an hour, then burst out laughing when they realised they were on the same flight.

His tribute

Jim Iyke described their relationship as something sacred rather than hidden, and said they have built a family together and travelled to eight countries. He called her his best friend, his prayer warrior and his wife, and said they have never stopped laughing together.
