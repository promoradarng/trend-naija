APC youth leaders receive Toyota Prado SUVs for election mobilisation

25 Oct 2026, 21:37 GMT

APC National Youth Leader Dayo Israel has shared a video saying he presented Toyota Prado SUVs to selected youth leaders to support the party's election mobilisation. At least seven vehicles appear in the footage, some branded with the APC logo. Israel also thanked First Lady Oluremi Tinubu for her continuing support.

  • The handover comes as parties step up campaigning ahead of the 2027 elections.
  • It follows earlier debate online over branded SUVs being used for the 2027 campaign.
  • The party has not said how many youth leaders will receive vehicles in total.

Jubilation in Brazil as Lula supporters pour onto the streets to celebrate election victory

26 Oct 2026, 14:45 GMT

Supporters of Brazil's Workers' Party (PT) flooded the streets and set off fireworks to celebrate President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva's narrow runoff win over Flávio Bolsonaro of the Liberal Party (PL). Lula took about 51.8% of the vote after the full count, winning a fourth term in one of the most fiercely contested races in the country's recent history.
