Social media personality Nkiru Blessing Okoro, known as Blessing CEO, has issued an emotional public apology, weeks after the death of her nine-year-old son.

The video

In a video posted on Instagram on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, the relationship influencer was seen crying inside a parked car. She said she had gone there because she did not want her mother to see her in that state. She described feeling confused, numb, broken and surrounded by darkness, and said she was so exhausted that she needed people to pray for her.

Her apology

Blessing CEO apologised to everyone she had hurt with her words and actions, saying she had spoken carelessly about pain she had never experienced and could now feel the pain she once mocked. She said she was only human and bound to make mistakes, and compared herself to the prodigal son returning home. She also thanked those who still love her and apologised to those who no longer do.

Background

Her son, Bryan, died from an asthma attack while she was being held in custody in Lagos, according to reports. She was later released on bail and is facing a court case, which is still ongoing. Since her release she has spoken about her ordeal and denied rumours about drug use. Before her troubles, she was known for strong and often controversial relationship advice online, which drew both followers and critics.

How people reacted

Many social media users expressed sympathy and prayed for her healing, while some said her apology showed growth. Others urged people to stop mocking her and to respect a grieving mother.

Ned Nwoko asks public to stop death rumours about Regina Daniels, threatens legal action

2 Nov 2026, 18:01 GMT

Senator Ned Nwoko has appealed to Nigerians to stop spreading false reports about the death and health of his estranged wife, actress Regina Daniels. He said she needs compassion to recover and that his team is documenting false posts for legal action.

Read more

Senator Ned Nwoko has made a public appeal for people to stop spreading false stories about his estranged wife, Nollywood actress Regina Daniels, including rumours that she has died.

What he said

In a statement on social media on Monday evening, 2 November 2026, Nwoko asked fans, bloggers and social media commentators to give Regina some breathing space. He said she had been through more than many people realise and that what she needs most now is love, support and the chance to recover. He said the appeal was also for the sake of their children, who he said are well cared for and deserve to see their mother supported. He added that whatever difficulties the marriage has faced, her well-being has always mattered to him, and that this was a time for compassion, not judgement.

Warning to rumour spreaders

Nwoko said he would not tolerate the deliberate spread of false information about Regina, especially made-up reports about her death, health or personal circumstances. He said his team was documenting such publications and that those responsible would be held to account through the proper legal channels.

Background

Regina Daniels and Nwoko married in 2019 and have two sons. Their marriage has been the subject of public disputes this year, and the two are now described as estranged. In a video shared on her recent birthday, the actress gave fans a glimpse of health challenges she has been facing, which Nwoko referred to in his statement. Since then, several unverified claims about her condition have spread online.

How people reacted

Many fans welcomed the statement and prayed for the actress's recovery, while others urged bloggers to be more careful with stories about people's health.
