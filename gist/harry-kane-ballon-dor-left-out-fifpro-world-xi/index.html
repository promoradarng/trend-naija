Harry Kane becomes first Ballon d'Or winner left out of FIFPRO World XI

11 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Harry Kane has become the first Ballon d'Or winner to be left out of the FIFPRO Men's World XI in the same year.

What happened

The team, voted for by professional players around the world and announced on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, did not include the Bayern Munich and England striker, who won the 2026 Ballon d'Or after scoring 73 goals in 65 appearances for club and country. Erling Haaland and Kylian Mbappé were among the forwards chosen ahead of him.

A first

It is the first time since the FIFPRO World XI was introduced in 2005 that the reigning Ballon d'Or winner has failed to make the team.

Background

The Ballon d'Or is voted for by journalists, while the FIFPRO World XI is chosen by fellow players, and the two awards do not always agree. Kane joined Bayern Munich from Tottenham Hotspur in 2023 and has been one of the most prolific goalscorers in Europe since.

  • Kane scored 73 goals in 65 games.
  • He won the 2026 Ballon d'Or.
  • He is the first winner omitted from the FIFPRO XI.

WWE star Bronson Reed dies at 38

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

WWE has announced the death of wrestler Bronson Reed, real name Jermaine Haley, at the age of 38 from medical complications. He had been seriously injured at a WWE event in Saudi Arabia three weeks earlier.

Read more

WWE has announced the death of wrestler Bronson Reed, whose real name was Jermaine Haley, at the age of 38.

The announcement

In a statement on its website on Friday, 27 November 2026, WWE said Reed had died earlier that day from medical complications, but did not give further details. The company described him as a larger-than-life figure, a devoted family man and a beloved colleague, and sent its condolences to his pregnant wife, Paige, and their daughter.

Earlier injury

His death came about three weeks after he was seriously injured at WWE's Crown Jewel event in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on 7 November. During a tag team title match, Nigerian-born wrestler Oba Femi threw him off the stage through a stack of tables. Reed reportedly suffered broken ribs and a lacerated kidney and spent more than a week in hospital in Saudi Arabia. WWE has not said whether his death was linked to the injury.

His career

Born in Adelaide, Australia, Reed joined WWE in 2019. He won the NXT North American Championship and the 2024 André the Giant Memorial Battle Royal. Wrestlers and fans around the world have paid tribute.

Chelsea suffer third straight Premier League defeat with 3-2 loss at Forest

28 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Chelsea lost 3-2 to Nottingham Forest at the City Ground on Friday, their third Premier League defeat in a row. Emmanuel Emegha scored twice but could not prevent the loss.

Read more

Chelsea's poor run continued on Friday, 27 November 2026, as they lost 3-2 to Nottingham Forest at the City Ground, their third Premier League defeat in a row.

The match

Igor Jesus gave Forest the lead inside five minutes, and the home side led at half-time. Early in the second half, Morgan Gibbs-White scored from the penalty spot after Wesley Fofana fouled Liam Delap. Emmanuel Emegha pulled a goal back for Chelsea, but Gibbs-White restored the two-goal lead soon after. Emegha scored again late on, but Chelsea ran out of time.

Alonso's verdict

Manager Xabi Alonso said after the game that his side were struggling with confidence.

Poor form

Chelsea have now lost four of their last five league games, with their only win in that run coming against Manchester United. Last weekend they were beaten 4-1 at home by Leeds, after which Cole Palmer and Joao Pedro were seen exchanging words on the touchline.

Aubameyang crashes Ferrari on way to training in Spain, no serious injuries

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Former Arsenal striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang was involved in a crash on his way to training with Deportivo La Coruña. His Ferrari collided with another car, but no one was seriously hurt.

Former Arsenal and Chelsea striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang was involved in a road accident on his way to training with his Spanish club, Deportivo La Coruña, on Thursday, 26 November 2026.

The crash

The 37-year-old Gabon international was driving a Ferrari, reportedly worth around €380,000, when it collided with a BMW on the AP-9 motorway in north-west Spain at about 9:30am local time. The BMW overturned in the impact and had to be towed away.

No serious injuries

Emergency services, including the Civil Guard, firefighters and paramedics, attended the scene. Despite the force of the collision, no one was seriously injured.

INEC holds nationwide mock accreditation in 17,656 polling units

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

INEC is conducting a mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise across 17,656 polling units in all 36 states and the FCT on Saturday. It is testing its upgraded BVAS machines and security arrangements.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) is conducting a nationwide mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise on Saturday, 28 November 2026.

The exercise

The test is taking place at 17,656 polling units across the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. It is designed to test INEC's upgraded Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS), the result transmission process and election security arrangements under simulated election-day conditions. Sample ballot papers are being used.

Why it matters

The exercise comes ahead of the presidential and National Assembly elections on 16 January 2027. INEC has faced criticism in past elections over delays in uploading results, and this week the commission named nine states as hotspots for campaign violence.

Lagos adjusts

Lagos State cancelled its monthly environmental sanitation exercise, which had been scheduled for the same morning, so that residents could take part.
