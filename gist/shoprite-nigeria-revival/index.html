President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has been seen kissing the sacred Black Stone, known as Al-Hajar Al-Aswad, while performing Umrah in the holy city of Makkah, Saudi Arabia.

Photos from the visit show the President, dressed in white, leaning into the silver frame that holds the stone as Saudi security officers stand by. Another photo shows him with members of his delegation in the courtyard of the Grand Mosque.

About the Black Stone

The Black Stone sits at the eastern corner of the Kaaba, inside the Grand Mosque, and holds deep religious significance for Muslims around the world.

Kissing the stone is a practice associated with Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), and many pilgrims seek the chance to do so whenever they visit the holy site.

A spiritual journey

For Muslims, Umrah is a spiritual journey marked by prayer, worship and personal reflection.

The moment has also drawn attention to the religious side of Nigeria's leader as he performs one of Islam's important acts of worship.

Senator Ned Nwoko has made a public appeal for people to stop spreading false stories about his estranged wife, Nollywood actress Regina Daniels, including rumours that she has died.

What he said

In a statement on social media on Monday evening, 2 November 2026, Nwoko asked fans, bloggers and social media commentators to give Regina some breathing space. He said she had been through more than many people realise and that what she needs most now is love, support and the chance to recover. He said the appeal was also for the sake of their children, who he said are well cared for and deserve to see their mother supported. He added that whatever difficulties the marriage has faced, her well-being has always mattered to him, and that this was a time for compassion, not judgement.

Warning to rumour spreaders

Nwoko said he would not tolerate the deliberate spread of false information about Regina, especially made-up reports about her death, health or personal circumstances. He said his team was documenting such publications and that those responsible would be held to account through the proper legal channels.

Background

Regina Daniels and Nwoko married in 2019 and have two sons. Their marriage has been the subject of public disputes this year, and the two are now described as estranged. In a video shared on her recent birthday, the actress gave fans a glimpse of health challenges she has been facing, which Nwoko referred to in his statement. Since then, several unverified claims about her condition have spread online.

How people reacted

Many fans welcomed the statement and prayed for the actress's recovery, while others urged bloggers to be more careful with stories about people's health.

Obi's camp denies viral report that he broke his neck in gym accident

2 Nov 2026, 18:01 GMT

Peter Obi's spokesman has reportedly dismissed as false a viral claim that the NDC presidential candidate was hospitalised after breaking his neck in a gym. He said Obi travelled abroad on Sunday for an international engagement.

Read more

The campaign of Peter Obi, presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has rejected a viral social media report claiming that he had been hospitalised after breaking his neck in a gym accident.

What the camp said

Obi's spokesman, Idris Zekeri, reportedly described the report and the photographs shared with it as completely false, in comments published on Monday, 2 November 2026. He said he was with the former Anambra governor on Sunday morning before Obi boarded a flight for an international engagement. Zekeri accused political opponents of making up the story to cause confusion among Obi's supporters ahead of the 2027 election.

Background

False health claims about political figures have become common in the run-up to the January 2027 elections. Last week, the Presidency rejected claims by activist Omoyele Sowore that President Bola Tinubu travelled to Saudi Arabia for medical treatment, saying he went for Umrah. Fact-checkers have also debunked a clip that claimed Obi said he could not defeat Tinubu. Obi, who is running with Rabiu Kwankwaso, has been touring states including Nasarawa and Ebonyi in recent days.

Why it matters

Observers say fake stories and doctored images can mislead voters, and INEC's chairman warned on Monday that cheap AI tools are making such material easier to produce. Voters are being urged to check claims with official sources before sharing them.

What happens next

Obi is expected to resume campaigning when he returns from his trip.
