Patoranking contrasts peaceful US midterms with Nigerian elections

5 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Reggae-dancehall singer Patoranking has compared the recent US midterm elections with elections in Nigeria.

What he said

In a post on X, reported on Thursday, 5 November 2026, the singer noted that nobody died during the American polls and there were no reports of ballot box snatching. He said Nigeria's problems have become so bad that citizens now accept bad behaviour as normal.

Background

The US midterm elections on 3 November saw the Democrats projected to take control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate, with several historic firsts, including the first Muslim US senator. Midterms are held halfway through a president's four-year term and decide all 435 House seats and about a third of the Senate, as well as many state governorships. Nigeria's past elections have often been marred by violence and allegations of rigging, and attacks on campaign events have already been reported ahead of the January 2027 polls, including on Peter Obi's convoy in Calabar this week.

How people reacted

Many fans agreed with the singer and called for peaceful and credible elections in 2027, while others pointed out that the US also has problems with its elections.

  • Patoranking praised the peaceful US midterms.
  • He said Nigerians have normalised bad electoral behaviour.
  • Attacks on campaign events have been reported in Nigeria.

Atiku challenges Tinubu to explain ₦1.44tn drop in oil revenue

25 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly asked President Tinubu to explain a ₦1.44 trillion fall in oil revenue in the first half of 2026, citing Budget Office figures. His campaign says collections were far below target.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly challenged President Bola Tinubu to publicly explain a ₦1.44 trillion drop in Nigeria's gross oil revenue in the first half of 2026.

The figures

In a statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, Atiku's campaign cited figures from the government's own Budget Office. It said the government collected ₦7.88 trillion in oil revenue between January and June 2026, well below its half-year target of ₦18.65 trillion, leaving a shortfall of about ₦10.78 trillion.

The campaign argued that the drop came even as international crude prices rose in the second quarter of the year.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC campaign was included in the reports.

Background

Atiku, who turned 80 on Wednesday, is the ADC's candidate in the January 2027 election. President Tinubu sent him a birthday message despite their rivalry.
