Aubameyang crashes Ferrari on way to training in Spain, no serious injuries

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Former Arsenal striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang was involved in a crash on his way to training with Deportivo La Coruña. His Ferrari collided with another car, but no one was seriously hurt.

Read more

Former Arsenal and Chelsea striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang was involved in a road accident on his way to training with his Spanish club, Deportivo La Coruña, on Thursday, 26 November 2026.

The crash

The 37-year-old Gabon international was driving a Ferrari, reportedly worth around €380,000, when it collided with a BMW on the AP-9 motorway in north-west Spain at about 9:30am local time. The BMW overturned in the impact and had to be towed away.

No serious injuries

Emergency services, including the Civil Guard, firefighters and paramedics, attended the scene. Despite the force of the collision, no one was seriously injured.

FIFA says hackers stole personal and business information in cyberattack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

FIFA has confirmed it was the victim of a cyberattack in which criminals gained access to a limited set of personal and business information. It is working with law enforcement.

Read more

World football's governing body FIFA has confirmed that it was hit by a cyberattack in which hackers gained unauthorised access to personal and business information.

What FIFA said

In a statement on Thursday, 26 November 2026, FIFA said it had been the victim of a criminal cyberattack that gave access to a limited set of personal and business information. It said it was working with the relevant authorities, including law enforcement, to investigate.

The organisation strongly condemned the unlawful taking and publication of the information, particularly personal data, describing it as a serious violation of privacy that could have significant consequences for the people affected.

Unanswered questions

FIFA did not say when the attack happened, what kind of information was accessed or how many people were affected.

OK Movement withdraws support from Obi and the NDC

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

The OK Movement, named after Peter Obi and Rabiu Kwankwaso, has reportedly pulled its campaign support from the NDC. Its director-general said the group had been told Obi and the party had chosen to cut ties with it.

Read more

The OK Movement, a support group named after the initials of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso, has reportedly withdrawn its campaign and mobilisation support from the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

The announcement

In a statement on Friday, 27 November 2026, the movement's national director-general, John Ughulu, said the group had been informed that Obi and the NDC had decided to cut ties with it. He directed the movement's ward, local government and state structures to stop all involvement in NDC activities immediately.

What next

Ughulu said the group had begun discussions with other political parties and presidential aspirants.

Background

The development comes days before Obi is due to attend an opposition summit in Abuja with Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde.

The other side

No response from Obi or the NDC was included in the reports.

Davido and cousins turn heads at Governor Adeleke's second-term inauguration

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Osun Governor Ademola Adeleke was sworn in for a second term on Friday in Osogbo. His nephew Davido and other family members drew attention with a coordinated appearance at the ceremony.

Read more

Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke was inaugurated for a second term on Friday, 27 November 2026, at the Osogbo Township Stadium.

Family affair

Afrobeats star Davido, the governor's nephew, drew almost as much attention as the ceremony itself. He arrived with his brother Adewale Adeleke and cousins Shina Adeleke, known as Shina Rambo, and Adebayo Adeleke, known as B-Red. The four men wore matching white traditional outfits, and photos of them spread quickly online.

Davido's father, businessman Adedeji Adeleke, also drew attention for showing respect to the Ooni of Ife at the event.

Background

Adeleke, a chieftain of the Accord Party, dissolved his executive council earlier this week ahead of the start of his second term.
