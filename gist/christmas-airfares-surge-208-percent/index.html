Christmas airfares to South-East and South-South jump by up to 208%

5 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Nigerians planning to fly home for Christmas and the New Year face sharply higher fares, according to booking data reported on Thursday, 5 November 2026.

The new prices

Economy tickets for flights departing on 20 and 21 December on routes linking Lagos and Abuja to cities in the South-East and South-South have reportedly risen to between ₦196,000 and ₦337,500, with some fares up by as much as 208 per cent. One airline was reportedly charging about ₦310,500 for a one-way Lagos–Benin flight, up from about ₦120,000. Air Peace, Aero Contractors and United Nigeria Airlines were among the carriers with the steepest increases.

Why fares rise

Demand for flights to the South-East and South-South surges every December as many people travel home for the holidays. Airlines say high costs of aviation fuel, foreign exchange for maintenance and leasing, and limited aircraft numbers push up fares. Senate President Godswill Akpabio said this week that even politicians are struggling to find aircraft to hire.

Background

The Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC) previously accused some domestic airlines of fixing prices during the 2025 festive season. Airlines denied wrongdoing and blamed operating costs. Many travellers choose road travel instead, despite security concerns on some highways.

What it means

Consumer groups are urging regulators to monitor fares, while travel experts advise booking early to avoid the highest prices.

  • Some fares have risen by up to 208%.
  • Lagos–Benin now costs about ₦310,500.
  • The FCCPC previously accused airlines of price-fixing.

Kano drug task force seals pharmaceutical company after major pregabalin seizure

14 Nov 2026, 15:45 GMT

The Kano State Multi-Agency Task Force on Drug Abuse has sealed a pharmaceutical company after recovering about 3.18 million capsules suspected to be pregabalin, worth more than ₦900 million. Allegations of regulatory failures are still under investigation.

Read more

The Kano State Multi-Agency Task Force on Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking has sealed a pharmaceutical company following the earlier recovery of about 3.18 million capsules suspected to be pregabalin.

A ₦900 million haul

The task force estimates the value of the seized stock at more than ₦900 million. Photos of the seizure show cartons packed with boxes and blister packs of red-and-white capsules.

Pregabalin is a prescription medicine used to treat nerve pain and some other conditions. It is widely misused in parts of northern Nigeria, where it is taken in large doses for its sedating effects, and is a growing concern for health and security authorities.

Questions for the regulators

The task force has also raised allegations of possible regulatory failures that may have allowed such a large quantity of the drug to circulate.

The Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN) and the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) had not responded to the allegations at the time of reporting.

What is confirmed

The sealing of the company is confirmed. The allegations against the company and against regulatory officials remain under investigation and should not be treated as established facts.

FG signs Port Harcourt airport concession deal, unions say no jobs will be lost

14 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

The federal government has signed a concession agreement for Port Harcourt International Airport with PH Airport Limited. Aviation unions backed the deal after assurances that no worker would lose their job.

Read more

The federal government has signed a concession agreement for the Port Harcourt International Airport with a private operator.

The deal

The agreement with PH Airport Limited was signed at the office of the Minister of Aviation and Aerospace Development, Festus Keyamo, according to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026. The concessionaire was represented by its group managing director, Eyo Asuquo. The heads of the Nigeria Civil Aviation Authority and the Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria, ministry officials and aviation union leaders attended the signing. The company reportedly plans to recruit more workers.

Unions back the deal

Ben Nnabue, president of the National Union of Air Transport Employees, said organised labour supported the agreement after receiving assurances that no aviation worker would lose their job because of the concession, adding that protecting jobs remained the unions' top priority.

Background

The government has been seeking private investors to run and upgrade major airports.

Copyright Commission takes legal action against NetNaija, MovieBox over piracy

14 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Nigerian Copyright Commission says it has begun legal action against operators of platforms including NetNaija and MovieBox over alleged copyright violations, warning that sites distributing protected content without permission could be shut down.

Read more

The Nigerian Copyright Commission (NCC) says it has started legal action against the operators of several online platforms, including NetNaija and MovieBox, over alleged copyright infringement.

What the commission said

Speaking on Thursday, 12 November 2026, at a copyright sensitisation programme at the University of Ibadan, Barrister Kunle Olatunji, deputy director of the commission's Regulatory and Compliance Department, said the action was part of efforts to protect authors, publishers, filmmakers and other creators whose work is used without permission. He said the Copyright Act 2022 had widened the commission's powers to tackle online piracy, allowing it to seek the closure of entire websites rather than just the removal of individual pirated items.

Why it matters

Olatunji said the law protects the rights of creative professionals whose works are exploited without their consent.

Background

The allegations against the platforms have not been tested in court, and their operators had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

Zulum promises ₦50,000 to each soldier deployed to Borno frontline communities

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Borno governor Babagana Zulum has announced a ₦50,000 cash grant for every soldier deployed to the frontline communities of Maiwa and Koshebe, where Boko Haram killed scores of rice farmers in 2020.

Read more

Borno State governor Babagana Zulum has pledged ₦50,000 to every soldier deployed to the frontline communities of Maiwa and Koshebe.

The announcement

Zulum made the pledge on Friday, 13 November 2026, during a visit to the headquarters of the Army's 24 Task Force Brigade in Dikwa Local Government Area, where he met the brigade commander. He also promised continuous logistics support for troops in the area, describing the package as a sign of the state's commitment to soldiers taking on dangerous assignments.

Background

The two communities have suffered badly in the Boko Haram insurgency. In November 2020, insurgents attacked rice farmers in Koshebe, in one of the deadliest attacks on civilians in the region. Atiku Abubakar this week cited data showing that the North-East accounted for most of the 638 insecurity-related deaths recorded in October.
