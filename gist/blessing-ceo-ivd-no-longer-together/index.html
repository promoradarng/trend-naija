"IVD and I are no longer together. I've left him to pick up the pieces of my life": Blessing CEO reveals

5 Nov 2026, 10:05 GMT

Controversial Nigerian relationship therapist and social media personality Blessing CEO has got people talking again after saying her relationship with businessman IVD has come to an end.

"We are no longer together"

According to Blessing, she and IVD are no longer together, and she has decided to walk away from the relationship to concentrate on rebuilding her life.

"IVD and I are no longer together. I've left him to pick up the pieces of my life," she said.

A difficult period

The revelation comes during a difficult time in Blessing CEO's personal life. Only days ago she shared an emotional apology, describing herself as broken and comparing her journey to the biblical story of the prodigal son.

For someone who has spent years advising people about love, relationships and marriage, her own romantic life has often drawn public attention. With this latest news, many people are wondering what went wrong between her and IVD.

Their relationship had already generated considerable discussion on social media, with supporters and critics holding very different opinions about it.

What we don't know

Blessing has not given a detailed explanation of what led to the decision, and IVD has yet to respond publicly to her statement.

What seems clear from her words is that she wants to focus on herself and find a way forward after everything she says she has been through.

The relationship adviser's own relationship

Blessing CEO has built much of her public identity on relationship advice, sometimes making controversial statements about how men and women should handle love. Now that her own relationship has ended, social media users are likely to revisit some of those statements.

Relationships can end for many reasons, and the public may never know the full story behind this one.

Hilda Baci says it is upsetting when people credit her success to her partner

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Celebrity chef and Guinness World Record holder Hilda Baci says it is sometimes upsetting when people attribute her achievements to her partner, saying it shows the mindset of many Nigerians.

Read more

Celebrity chef Hilda Baci has spoken out against people who credit her success to her romantic partner rather than her own efforts.

What she said

In a video shared on Monday, 9 November 2026, the chef said such comments are sometimes upsetting and reveal the mindset of many people. Her remarks have sparked conversation about the tendency to link successful women's achievements to the men in their lives.

Background

Hilda Baci became a household name in 2023 when she set a Guinness World Record for the longest cooking marathon by an individual, cooking for about 93 hours in Lagos. She has since built a successful food and media business. Her relationship has often been discussed on social media.

How people reacted

Reactions were mixed. Some fans backed her, saying women deserve credit for their own work, while others joked online that her partner was better known because of her rather than the other way around.

Stars turned out for the launch of Priscilla Ojo's new store in Lagos, but a brief exchange between Funke Akindele and Toyosi Adesanya, who stood with her husband to greet her, got social media talking.

The opening of a new store by Priscilla Ojo, daughter of actress Iyabo Ojo, drew a crowd of celebrities in Lagos and some unexpected online debate.

The event

Destiny Etiko appointed Senior Special Assistant by Enugu governor

9 Nov 2026, 06:44 GMT

Actress Destiny Etiko has been appointed Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy to Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. She is the second Nollywood star to receive a role from his administration this month, after Rachael Okonkwo.

Read more

Nollywood actress Destiny Etiko has been given a government appointment in Enugu State.

The appointment

According to reports on Sunday, 8 November 2026, Etiko has been appointed Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy to Governor Peter Mbah. The actress, who is from Udi Local Government Area of the state, confirmed the news when she responded to a congratulatory post from fellow actress Sarah Martins.

Second Nollywood appointee

Etiko is the second prominent Nollywood figure to be appointed by the Mbah administration in a short period. Actress Rachael Okonkwo confirmed her own appointment as Senior Special Assistant on Films and Creative Industries earlier in November.

Background

Etiko publicly supported Mbah during his campaign ahead of the 2023 governorship election. She rose to prominence after the 2014 film 'Idemili' and has since built a successful career as an actress and producer. Mbah, who was elected on the PDP platform, has since moved to the APC and backs President Bola Tinubu's re-election.

How people reacted

Many fans and colleagues congratulated her while others had mixed reactions.

Bobrisky says leaving Nigeria was one of the best decisions of their life

7 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Crossdresser and socialite Bobrisky says relocating from Nigeria two years ago was one of the best decisions ever made, describing the country as toxic and full of daily drama, and saying life abroad is peaceful.

Read more

Socialite Idris Okuneye, known as Bobrisky, says leaving Nigeria was one of the best decisions of their life.

What Bobrisky said

In an Instagram post on Saturday, 7 November 2026, Bobrisky described Nigeria as a toxic environment where a new drama, piece of gossip or fight springs up every day. The socialite said life since relocating had been peaceful and that they would not give up that peace.

Background

Bobrisky left Nigeria in November 2024 after a public row with the federal government over claims about the conditions under which a six-month jail sentence for abusing the naira had been served. The sentence was handed down in April 2024 after Bobrisky pleaded guilty to spraying and mutilating naira notes. Allegations that the socialite had served part of the sentence outside prison led to a government investigation and a dispute with activist VeryDarkMan, who first raised the claims. Bobrisky was stopped at the Seme border while trying to leave the country in October 2024 before eventually relocating.
