Peller vows to unfollow the Grammys after Ice King Ochacho misses out on nomination

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Popular streamer Peller has reportedly hit out at the Recording Academy after young singer Ice King Ochacho was left out of the 2027 Grammy nominations.

What Peller said

In a video that circulated online on Monday, 16 November 2026, Peller is said to have voiced his frustration at the snub and declared that he would unfollow the Grammys' account. He reportedly argued that the Academy should even have created a special category to recognise the young performer.

Who is Ice King Ochacho

Ice King Ochacho, a teenager, is the son of businessman King Ochacho. He released his debut album, The Future, in October 2026, featuring collaborations with established artists, and his single No Competition reportedly passed one million streams on Spotify.

Background

Peller has been one of the young singer's loudest supporters. In September, King Ochacho recalled that the streamer had earlier predicted his son would one day win a Grammy.

The 2027 Grammy nominations, announced this week, include Nigerian stars Tems, Davido and Ayra Starr.

VeryDarkMan says he will register his own Ratel party and fund it himself

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has announced plans to register the Ratel Political Party before 2032, saying he will pay the registration costs from his business earnings. He said the movement will start with grassroots offices.

Read more

Social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has announced plans to register his own political party, the Ratel Political Party, before 2032.

Paying his own way

In a video, VDM said he would fund the project with his own earnings from his businesses, rather than relying on supporters. He said he had heard the registration fee was over ₦50 million and that he would raise it. INEC's party registration guidelines set a non-refundable administrative fee of ₦50 million, and payment does not guarantee approval.

Grassroots first

VDM said the Ratel movement would begin by focusing on grassroots positions, such as local government chairmen and councillors, rather than seats in the National Assembly.

Background

'Ratel' is the name VDM uses for his followers. This week he also urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one party against President Tinubu.

VeryDarkMan urges Obi, Atiku and Makinde to form one party to face Tinubu

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has called on Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to put their ambitions aside and unite under one party against President Tinubu. The three are due at an opposition summit on Monday.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one political party ahead of the 2027 election.

What he said

In a post on Instagram, VDM said the three should sit down together and form one party to defeat President Bola Tinubu. He argued that they need to set aside their personal ambitions, and that a united front would make the contest against the ruling party more competitive.

Background

Obi of the NDC, Atiku of the ADC and Makinde of the APM are expected at an opposition summit in Abuja on Monday, 30 November, where parties are due to sign a cooperation pact. The organisers have said it will not be a formal merger, and Makinde's camp has denied reports that he plans to step down for Atiku.

VeryDarkMan gives Ogoja council chairman two weeks over water crisis

27 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has called out the chairman of Ogoja Local Government in Cross River State over a community's lack of clean water. He gave him two weeks to act.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has called out the chairman of Ogoja Local Government Area in Cross River State, Christopher Agbeh, over a community's lack of clean water.

What he said

In a video message, VDM said it was unfortunate that in 2026 a whole community was still crying out for something as basic as water. He described the situation as disappointing and told the chairman he had two weeks to make himself useful to his people.

Why it matters

His comments have drawn attention to the lack of basic amenities in many Nigerian communities and renewed debate about the responsibilities of local governments.

The other side

No response from the Ogoja council chairman was included in the reports.

Alex Otti joins South-East governors to receive Tinubu at Enugu APC rally

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Abia governor Alex Otti has reportedly arrived in Enugu to join other South-East governors in receiving President Tinubu for the APC's South-East campaign rally. His appearance comes days after Tinubu said Otti was with him.

Read more

Abia State Governor Alex Otti reportedly arrived in Enugu on Saturday, 28 November 2026, to join other South-East governors and political leaders in receiving President Bola Tinubu ahead of the APC's South-East presidential campaign rally.

The arrival

Otti, who came with his wife, was reportedly received at the Akanu Ibiam International Airport by Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. He was also said to be listed among the leaders present at the rally at Michael Okpara Square in Enugu.

'He's with me'

His appearance came days after President Tinubu, speaking at a meeting with Igbo leaders in Lagos, claimed that Otti was among the South-East governors backing his re-election, recalling that the two had worked together before Otti became governor.

Mixed reactions

Otti's presence has reportedly sparked debate on social media, as he was elected governor on the Labour Party platform. Some users questioned why he attended an APC campaign event, while others said what mattered most was his performance in Abia State.

Background

The rally is part of the APC's zonal campaign tour ahead of the 16 January 2027 presidential election, as the NDC, ADC and other opposition parties also step up their campaigns.
