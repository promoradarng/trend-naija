Armed man arrested near White House after allegedly saying voices told him to kill Trump

7 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The US Secret Service has arrested a man near the White House after he allegedly told agents that voices in his head had instructed him to travel to Washington and kill President Donald Trump.

What happened

According to US officials, agents spotted the man behaving suspiciously in a car parked beside the White House on Thursday, 5 November 2026. During questioning, he allegedly said voices had directed him to come to the capital and assassinate the President. Agents found that the car had been reported stolen, and a search turned up a rifle, two handguns and dozens of rounds of ammunition.

The suspect

The Department of Homeland Security said the man is a Venezuelan national who entered the United States illegally in 2024. He has been charged with several firearms offences, as well as unauthorised use of a vehicle and receiving stolen property, and is being held by the US Marshals Service. He has not yet been tried.

Secret Service comments

Secret Service Director Sean Curran said the arrest showed the kind of security environment his agents deal with every day. Officials said the White House has seen a significant rise in incidents linked to mental health problems, as the presidency often becomes a focus for people experiencing such conditions.

Background

Trump survived two assassination attempts during the 2024 election campaign. The arrest came in the same week that the President's party suffered heavy losses in the midterm elections and he named Stephen Miller as his new chief of staff.

US revokes more than 6,000 visas in crackdown on 'birth tourism'

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

The US State Department says its birth tourism task force has revoked more than 6,000 visas held by foreigners who travelled to give birth in the United States or helped others to do so. It did not say which nationalities were affected.

Read more

The United States has revoked more than 6,000 visas in a crackdown on so-called birth tourism.

What was announced

In a statement on X on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the State Department said its Birth Tourism Prevention Task Force had cancelled the visas of foreign nationals found to have taken part in or facilitated the practice. It said the crackdown targets not only people who travelled to give birth but also those who helped arrange such trips, and described the practice as illicit. The department did not release a breakdown of the nationalities affected.

What is birth tourism

Birth tourism refers to travelling to the United States specifically to give birth, so that the child acquires US citizenship under the birthright citizenship provision of the 14th Amendment to the US Constitution.

Why it matters to Nigerians

Giving birth in the US has been popular among some Nigerians who can afford it, and the US has previously warned that visitor visas may be refused to applicants suspected of travelling to give birth.

JD Vance reportedly reconsidering 2028 presidential run after midterm losses

13 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

US Vice-President JD Vance is reportedly rethinking whether to run for president in 2028, after the Republicans' heavy defeat in this month's midterm elections led allies and donors to question the party's chances.

Read more

US Vice-President JD Vance is reportedly reconsidering whether to run for president in 2028.

What is reported

According to reports on Friday, 13 November 2026, citing people close to him, Vance has not made a final decision about entering the race after the Republican Party's poor performance in the midterm elections earlier this month. Allies and donors are said to be questioning whether the party can hold on to the White House. People close to him say he remains committed to the political movement that returned Donald Trump to power, but is increasingly asking whether he should be the one to lead it.

A bigger question

One ally reportedly said Vance has been wrestling with whether any Republican can win the presidency in the current political climate. Another noted that at 42, he could afford to wait for a better opportunity.

Background

Vance had been widely seen as the leading contender for the 2028 Republican nomination. In the 3 November midterms, the Democrats were projected to win control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate, and Trump has since replaced his chief of staff. Vance has not publicly commented on the reports.

Microsoft outage disrupts Outlook, Teams and Copilot in 74 countries

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A global Microsoft 365 outage that began on Thursday has disrupted Outlook, Teams and Copilot for users in 74 countries and territories, with nearly 140,000 problem reports logged at its peak. Microsoft says it is working to restore services.

Read more

Microsoft is working to restore its services after a global outage disrupted Outlook email, Teams and its Copilot AI assistant for users around the world.

What happened

The outage began on Thursday, 12 November 2026, and affected Microsoft 365 services in 74 countries and territories, according to reports on Friday. Many users were unable to load their inboxes or send and receive emails, while others had trouble with Teams calls and Copilot. Reports of Outlook problems on an outage-tracking website peaked at nearly 140,000 worldwide.

Microsoft's explanation

Microsoft initially blamed the problem on unusually high traffic across parts of its infrastructure, which caused connection errors. The company said it had paused some deployments while it worked on a fix.

Why it matters

Outlook and Teams are widely used by businesses and organisations around the world, including in Nigeria.

Arokodare scores twice as Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 in AFCON qualifier

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Substitute Tolu Arokodare scored twice as Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 in Uyo on matchday three of the 2027 AFCON qualifiers, moving the Super Eagles to second in Group L, a point behind Guinea-Bissau.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 at the Godswill Akpabio Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November 2026, in a crucial 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier.

How the match went

Alhassan Yusuf gave Nigeria a dream start with a goal in the second minute, but Tanzania equalised through Alphonce Masanga. Ademola Lookman was forced off injured late in the first half and replaced by Moses Simon. Coach Eric Chelle then brought on Tolu Arokodare and Moses Usor in the second half, and the change paid off immediately: Arokodare scored in the 52nd minute, and the two substitutes combined for Arokodare's second goal and Nigeria's third in the 60th minute.

Table

The win moved Nigeria to second in Group L, one point behind Guinea-Bissau, who were held 1-1 by Madagascar. Nigeria play Tanzania again in their next match, and a win there would take them to nine points, putting them in a strong position to qualify with two games left.

Arokodare delivers

Arokodare, who has been in strong form on loan at Ajax in the Netherlands, had promised to carry his club form into the national team in the absence of the injured Victor Osimhen. Chelle later sent him a message after his brace.

Background

Tanzania are one of the co-hosts of AFCON 2027, along with Kenya and Uganda.
