Armed man arrested near White House after allegedly saying voices told him to kill Trump

7 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The US Secret Service has arrested a man near the White House after he allegedly told agents that voices in his head had instructed him to travel to Washington and kill President Donald Trump.

What happened

According to US officials, agents spotted the man behaving suspiciously in a car parked beside the White House on Thursday, 5 November 2026. During questioning, he allegedly said voices had directed him to come to the capital and assassinate the President. Agents found that the car had been reported stolen, and a search turned up a rifle, two handguns and dozens of rounds of ammunition.

The suspect

The Department of Homeland Security said the man is a Venezuelan national who entered the United States illegally in 2024. He has been charged with several firearms offences, as well as unauthorised use of a vehicle and receiving stolen property, and is being held by the US Marshals Service. He has not yet been tried.

Secret Service comments

Secret Service Director Sean Curran said the arrest showed the kind of security environment his agents deal with every day. Officials said the White House has seen a significant rise in incidents linked to mental health problems, as the presidency often becomes a focus for people experiencing such conditions.

Background

Trump survived two assassination attempts during the 2024 election campaign. The arrest came in the same week that the President's party suffered heavy losses in the midterm elections and he named Stephen Miller as his new chief of staff.

Elon Musk promises two SpaceX shares to every child in America

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Elon Musk has said every child in the United States will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from him personally. He did not explain how the plan would work.

Elon Musk has said every child in the United States will receive two shares in SpaceX, doubling a pledge he made last month.

The pledge

In a post on X on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, Musk said one share would come from the company and one from him personally. He said the company-funded share would be transferred next month, with his personal contribution following in June next year.

Questions remain

Musk did not explain how children would qualify, how the shares would be distributed or who would manage them on their behalf. He advised recipients to hold on to their shares, claiming SpaceX could one day be worth vastly more than today's global economy if it achieves its long-term goals.

Background

In October, Musk promised every American child one SpaceX share, describing it at the time as the first of many.

US appeals court gives Trump new route to challenge hush money conviction

26 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

A US federal appeals court has revived Donald Trump's bid to move his New York hush money case into federal court, where he hopes to have it dismissed on presidential immunity grounds.

Read more

A US federal appeals court has given President Donald Trump a fresh opening to try to overturn his New York hush money conviction.

The ruling

In a short order issued on Monday, 23 November 2026, a three-judge panel of the 2nd US Circuit Court of Appeals sent the dispute back to a federal district court in Manhattan, instructing a judge to rule quickly on Trump's request to transfer the case to federal court.

The panel found that the previous judge had been wrong to conclude that moving a case to federal court after conviction was never possible.

What Trump wants

If the case is transferred, Trump intends to seek its dismissal on presidential immunity grounds. Any decision could later return to the 2nd Circuit.

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie settle long legal battle over French winery

26 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled their years-long dispute over Château Miraval out of court. The terms were not disclosed.

Read more

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled their long-running legal battle over the sale of their French winery, Château Miraval.

The settlement

In a joint statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the former couple said they had resolved the case out of court in the best interests of their family and would not comment further. They added that any comments attributed to anonymous sources did not come from either of them or anyone close to them. The terms of the settlement were not disclosed.

The dispute

Pitt, 62, and Jolie, 51, bought the estate in the south of France in 2008 and had been in dispute over it since they split in 2016. In 2022, Pitt sued Jolie, claiming they had agreed that neither would sell their share without the other's consent, and accusing her of selling her stake to a Russian businessman without his permission. Jolie disputed his account in her response.

At least 30 killed as fuel tanker overturns and bursts into flames in Cairo

26 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

At least 30 people died and 15 were injured when a fuel tanker overturned and caught fire on the Greater Cairo Ring Road in Egypt on Wednesday. The blaze engulfed 27 vehicles.

Read more

At least 30 people have died and 15 others were injured after a fuel tanker overturned and burst into flames on a busy stretch of the Greater Cairo Ring Road in Egypt on Wednesday, 25 November 2026.

What happened

According to Egypt's Ministry of Health and Population, the fire broke out above Tunnel 10 in the densely populated Al-Warraq area of Imbaba, a suburb of Greater Cairo. Preliminary findings suggest the privately operated tanker swerved, hit a concrete road divider and toppled over, causing an explosion that spread to 27 nearby vehicles.

The response

Authorities sent 39 ambulances to the scene, taking survivors to three hospitals in the area. Firefighters brought the blaze under control, and heavy machinery was brought in to clear the burnt-out vehicles.

Condolences

Egyptian President Abdel-Fattah el-Sisi and Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly sent condolences to the families of the victims.
