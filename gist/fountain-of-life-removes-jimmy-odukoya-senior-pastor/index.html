Court bars Jimmy Odukoya from acting as Fountain of Life senior pastor

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

There is confusion at The Fountain of Life Church in Ilupeju, Lagos, after an announcement that Jimmy Odukoya had been removed as senior pastor was quickly rejected by the church and by Odukoya himself.

The removal announcement

On Tuesday, 17 November 2026, a statement presented as coming from the church's board of trustees said Jimmy's tenure did not reflect the succession wishes of his late father and the church's founder, Taiwo Odukoya, who died in 2023. It said the board had reviewed the church's constitution and recordings of the founder's wishes, and that Jimmy was no longer recognised as senior pastor with immediate effect. The statement described the decision as a governance matter and not a personal judgement.

The church's response

Within hours, a statement posted on the church's official Instagram page and on Jimmy Odukoya's page rejected the announcement as having no constitutional weight. It said the announcement did not come from a properly constituted meeting of the Board of Trustees, but from a faction calling itself "Concerned Members of the Board".

The church said the individuals behind the faction had been formally asked to step down from the board on 14 March 2026 over what it described as repeated violations of the church's constitution and founding principles, and that the letter had been delivered to and acknowledged by them.

The founder's video

Later on Tuesday, a video said to have been recorded by the late Taiwo Odukoya in December 2021 emerged, with a transcript released by the board of trustees. In it, he reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as the person who should lead the church if he died or was unable to continue.

He is said to have stated that Jimmy should serve as Areogun's immediate administrative assistant and lead the youth church. The video was reportedly recorded by Jimmy at his father's request and witnessed by his older brother, Pastor John Odukoya, and it has now become central to the dispute.

Interim leader named

Later on Tuesday, the board of trustees appointed Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as interim spiritual and administrative leader of the church. It said its resolution of 16 November removing Jimmy Odukoya remained in force and directed him to vacate the office and all its responsibilities. The trustees said Jimmy was present when his father's 2021 video was recorded.

Social media dispute

The trustees have also accused Jimmy Odukoya of taking control of the church's official social media accounts without approval and using them to share what they called misleading statements. They told members and the public to treat only statements signed on the board's behalf as authentic, and said they were taking steps to recover the accounts. Jimmy Odukoya had not responded to that claim in the reports.

Jimmy Odukoya speaks

On the evening of Wednesday, 18 November, Jimmy Odukoya addressed about 300 church workers at the church premises, giving his own account of the crisis for the first time. He said he had stayed quiet because he believed the matter could be settled privately, but felt he had to speak after the board released his late father's video to the media.

Court order

On Wednesday, 18 November, a Lagos State High Court presided over by Justice O.O. Adewunmi-Oshin granted an order barring Jimmy Odukoya from holding himself out as senior pastor while the case brought by the church's incorporated trustees is resolved. Further details of the suit were not immediately available.

In his address to church workers, Jimmy Odukoya reportedly rejected his removal as unconstitutional and accused some trustees and his sisters of working against his leadership, citing disagreements over church finances and his father's succession plans. The church has also postponed a planned thanksgiving service amid the crisis.

Church reportedly sealed

On Thursday, 19 November, there were reports that police had sealed the church's headquarters in Ilupeju. On Friday, the church denied this in a post on its official Instagram page, saying it had not been sealed and inviting members to Sunday service in person or online.

Background

Jimmy Odukoya, an actor and pastor, took over leadership of the church after his father's death. On 1 November he remarried and presented his new wife to the congregation. A video of the late founder said to show the role he intended for his son has also been circulating online amid the dispute.

What happens next

It is not yet clear how the leadership dispute will be resolved. The faction behind the removal announcement had not responded publicly to the church's statement in the reports.

  • A court has barred him from acting as senior pastor for now.
  • Trustees named Pastor Rotimi Okpaise interim leader.
  • Jimmy Odukoya says his removal was invalid.

NNPC says $2.04bn in crude oil proceeds is not missing

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

NNPC has dismissed reports that $2.04 billion in crude oil proceeds linked to its former trading arm Duke Oil is missing. It says the figure is the gross value of 29 cargoes and that all transactions were properly accounted for.

Read more

The Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPC) has dismissed reports that $2.04 billion in crude oil proceeds involving its trading subsidiary, formerly known as Duke Oil, is missing.

What NNPC said

In a statement on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, NNPC's chief corporate communications officer said the $2.04 billion represented the gross value of 29 crude oil cargoes, not missing or unpaid revenue. The statement was titled 'Duke Oil cargoes are fully accounted for'.

The company maintained that all transactions under the Direct Sale Direct Purchase (DSDP) programme were properly documented. It said the apparent gaps in dollar receipts were due to the way the programme's settlements were structured.

Background

Reports suggesting the money could not be traced had sparked concern online.

Air Peace to start London–Owerri flights on 15 December

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Air Peace says it will begin direct flights between London and Owerri on 15 December, from both Heathrow and Gatwick. The route is expected to ease travel for South-East Nigerians coming home for Christmas.

Read more

Air Peace has announced that it will begin flights between London and Owerri on 15 December 2026, in a move expected to make it easier for many Nigerians, especially those from the South-East, to travel home.

The route

The airline said in a statement on Monday that the new service would operate from both London Heathrow and London Gatwick airports, giving passengers more flexibility when planning their journeys.

Why it matters

The launch comes ahead of Christmas and the New Year, when many Nigerians living abroad return home to spend time with their families. A direct route to Owerri, the Imo State capital, is expected to spare travellers the long road journeys from Lagos or Abuja to the South-East.

Background

The announcement comes as domestic fares have surged ahead of the festive season, with some return tickets within Nigeria reported to have doubled in recent weeks.

Petrol falls to ₦1,055 at Lagos depots as MRS cuts pump price

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Five Lagos depots cut their petrol price to ₦1,055 per litre on Tuesday, the lowest among surveyed terminals. MRS stations in Abuja have reduced their pump price by ₦70 to ₦1,295.

Read more

Petrol prices have fallen further in Nigeria's wholesale market as competition among depot operators intensifies.

Depot prices

Five Lagos-based depots cut their ex-depot price of petrol to ₦1,055 per litre on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, the lowest rate among the terminals surveyed by a market tracker.

At the pump

The cuts are slowly reaching filling stations. MRS stations in Abuja reduced their pump price by ₦70 to ₦1,295 per litre, although that is still well above the depot rate.

Background

Prices have been falling for several weeks amid competition between Dangote Refinery and fuel importers, though motorists have complained that pump prices are slow to reflect lower wholesale rates.

Lagos offers 20% discount on food in 66 markets for the festive season

24 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Lagos State Government has relaunched its Ounje Eko food discount scheme, offering 20% off selected items in 66 markets. The markets will open on three Sundays: 29 November, 6 December and 20 December.

Read more

The Lagos State Government has relaunched its Ounje Eko food discount programme for the festive season, offering residents a 20% reduction on selected food items in 66 markets across the state.

When and where

The Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Systems, Abisola Olusanya, announced on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, that the scheme will run on three Sundays: 29 November, 6 December and 20 December. The markets will open from 9am to 4pm and cover all 57 local government and local council development areas, across the five IBILE divisions of Ikeja, Badagry, Ikorodu, Lagos Island and Epe.

Why it matters

The commissioner said the initiative was designed to ease pressure on household budgets as residents prepare for Christmas and the New Year. It comes amid concerns that many families have yet to feel real relief from high food prices, even though food inflation has slowed.
