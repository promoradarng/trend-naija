Court bars Jimmy Odukoya from acting as Fountain of Life senior pastor

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

There is confusion at The Fountain of Life Church in Ilupeju, Lagos, after an announcement that Jimmy Odukoya had been removed as senior pastor was quickly rejected by the church and by Odukoya himself.

The removal announcement

On Tuesday, 17 November 2026, a statement presented as coming from the church's board of trustees said Jimmy's tenure did not reflect the succession wishes of his late father and the church's founder, Taiwo Odukoya, who died in 2023. It said the board had reviewed the church's constitution and recordings of the founder's wishes, and that Jimmy was no longer recognised as senior pastor with immediate effect. The statement described the decision as a governance matter and not a personal judgement.

The church's response

Within hours, a statement posted on the church's official Instagram page and on Jimmy Odukoya's page rejected the announcement as having no constitutional weight. It said the announcement did not come from a properly constituted meeting of the Board of Trustees, but from a faction calling itself "Concerned Members of the Board".

The church said the individuals behind the faction had been formally asked to step down from the board on 14 March 2026 over what it described as repeated violations of the church's constitution and founding principles, and that the letter had been delivered to and acknowledged by them.

The founder's video

Later on Tuesday, a video said to have been recorded by the late Taiwo Odukoya in December 2021 emerged, with a transcript released by the board of trustees. In it, he reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as the person who should lead the church if he died or was unable to continue.

He is said to have stated that Jimmy should serve as Areogun's immediate administrative assistant and lead the youth church. The video was reportedly recorded by Jimmy at his father's request and witnessed by his older brother, Pastor John Odukoya, and it has now become central to the dispute.

Interim leader named

Later on Tuesday, the board of trustees appointed Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as interim spiritual and administrative leader of the church. It said its resolution of 16 November removing Jimmy Odukoya remained in force and directed him to vacate the office and all its responsibilities. The trustees said Jimmy was present when his father's 2021 video was recorded.

Social media dispute

The trustees have also accused Jimmy Odukoya of taking control of the church's official social media accounts without approval and using them to share what they called misleading statements. They told members and the public to treat only statements signed on the board's behalf as authentic, and said they were taking steps to recover the accounts. Jimmy Odukoya had not responded to that claim in the reports.

Jimmy Odukoya speaks

On the evening of Wednesday, 18 November, Jimmy Odukoya addressed about 300 church workers at the church premises, giving his own account of the crisis for the first time. He said he had stayed quiet because he believed the matter could be settled privately, but felt he had to speak after the board released his late father's video to the media.

Court order

On Wednesday, 18 November, a Lagos State High Court presided over by Justice O.O. Adewunmi-Oshin granted an order barring Jimmy Odukoya from holding himself out as senior pastor while the case brought by the church's incorporated trustees is resolved. Further details of the suit were not immediately available.

In his address to church workers, Jimmy Odukoya reportedly rejected his removal as unconstitutional and accused some trustees and his sisters of working against his leadership, citing disagreements over church finances and his father's succession plans. The church has also postponed a planned thanksgiving service amid the crisis.

Church reportedly sealed

On Thursday, 19 November, there were reports that police had sealed the church's headquarters in Ilupeju. On Friday, the church denied this in a post on its official Instagram page, saying it had not been sealed and inviting members to Sunday service in person or online.

Background

Jimmy Odukoya, an actor and pastor, took over leadership of the church after his father's death. On 1 November he remarried and presented his new wife to the congregation. A video of the late founder said to show the role he intended for his son has also been circulating online amid the dispute.

What happens next

It is not yet clear how the leadership dispute will be resolved. The faction behind the removal announcement had not responded publicly to the church's statement in the reports.

  • A court has barred him from acting as senior pastor for now.
  • Trustees named Pastor Rotimi Okpaise interim leader.
  • Jimmy Odukoya says his removal was invalid.

Eight soldiers and 13 workers were killed while rebuilding Borno community, officials say

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Borno officials have revealed that eight soldiers, including a major, and 13 construction workers were killed by insurgents during the 14-month rebuilding of Mayanti in Bama. Governor Babagana Zulum has now inaugurated 500 houses and a school there.

Read more

At least 21 people, including eight soldiers and 13 construction workers, were reportedly killed by insurgents during the reconstruction of the Mayanti community in Bama Local Government Area of Borno State.

How it came to light

The deaths were disclosed on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by Borno's Commissioner for Education, Abba Wakilbe, who heads the community's resettlement committee, during the inauguration of the rebuilt community. Among those killed was a major, Major Mairiga, along with seven other soldiers.

Wakilbe said work on the project began in August 2025 and was completed in October 2026. He paid tribute to those who died, saying their sacrifice made the return of the community possible.

What was built

Governor Babagana Zulum inaugurated 500 houses, 2,000 semi-permanent shelters and a school at Mayanti. He praised the committee for finishing the project despite repeated attacks, recalling that the community was attacked four or five times while construction was going on.

Background

Many communities in Borno State were destroyed during years of insurgency, and the state government has been rebuilding and resettling displaced residents in several areas.

NCC warns 'London-used' phones may be cut off if they fail device checks

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Imported used phones flagged by the Nigerian Communications Commission could lose network service after 30 days, under its device checker rules. The regulator has been sending text alerts to affected users since October.

Read more

Nigerians who buy imported used phones, popularly called 'London-used', could reportedly lose mobile service if their devices fail checks by the Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC).

How it works

Under the regulator's Device Checker and Type Approval Compliance framework, flagged devices may be disconnected from local networks after 30 cumulative days.

The NCC began sending text alerts under the sender name NCC-Checker on 10 October 2026. The messages advise affected users to contact the seller or check their device using the regulator's device checker. Alerts have reportedly gone to users of iPhones, Google Pixels, TECNO, Infinix and other brands.

Existing phones

About 231 million devices that were already active on Nigerian networks before the rollout were captured automatically.

Troops rescue 18 of 22 children abducted in Kaduna, search on for four

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Soldiers have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kachia/Kajuru, Kaduna State, early on Sunday. The army says the search for the remaining four continues.

Read more

The Nigerian Army says its troops have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kaduna State early on Sunday, 22 November 2026, and are searching for four others.

What happened

According to the army, armed men intercepted 22 members of the Boys' and Girls' Brigade in the Mother-Cat community, in the Kachia/Kajuru area of the state, while they were on an early-morning group road walk, and took them away.

The rescue

The army's acting director of public relations, Colonel Appolonia Anele, said troops of 1 Division operating under Operation FANSAN YAMMA moved immediately after receiving a distress call and pursued the abductors. Troops advanced towards a riverside area, where some of the children were found after the fleeing attackers abandoned them. In all, 18 were rescued.

What happens next

The army said operations were continuing to rescue the remaining four children. The attack comes days after troops foiled a suspected kidnap attempt at a school in another part of Kaduna State.

Correctional officers probed after two death-row inmates escape in Ilorin

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

About 20 correctional officers are reportedly under investigation after two death-row inmates escaped from the Oke-Kura custodial centre in Ilorin. The service has confirmed the probe but not the number of officers involved.

Read more

About 20 officers of the Nigerian Correctional Service are reportedly under investigation after two death-row inmates escaped from the Medium Security Custodial Centre at Oke-Kura, Ilorin, Kwara State, last Wednesday.

The probe

Those affected are said to include members of the armed squad and duty officers responsible for supervising inmates around the clock. Sources described the breach as a coordinated escape attempt involving seven inmates, two of whom got away.

What the service said

The Kwara State Command of the service confirmed on Sunday, 22 November 2026, that an investigation had begun on the orders of the Controller-General of Corrections. Its spokesperson said the probe would be thorough, but declined to confirm how many officers had been questioned, saying it would be premature while the investigation was ongoing.
