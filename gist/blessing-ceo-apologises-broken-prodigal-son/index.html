"I am broken, I now understand the pain I once mocked": Blessing CEO apologises, says she is returning like the prodigal son

3 Nov 2026, 12:08 GMT

Controversial relationship therapist Blessing CEO has shared an emotional message of regret, apologising to people she may have offended through her words and actions in the past.

"I now understand the pain I once mocked"

In her statement, Blessing admitted that she once spoke carelessly about people's struggles without fully understanding what they were going through. Now, according to her, life has placed her in a position where she can finally relate to some of that pain.

She described herself as emotionally broken, saying her present struggles have left her feeling lost and surrounded by darkness.

Like the prodigal son

Blessing also compared her situation to the biblical story of the prodigal son, who returned home after going through difficult experiences.

Her words suggest a period of deep personal reflection, as she acknowledges past mistakes and expresses a desire to make peace with those she may have hurt.

A change in tone

For someone who built a reputation on speaking boldly about relationships, personal struggles and other people's choices, this public apology marks a noticeable change in tone.

Sometimes, life has a way of teaching people lessons they once thought they already understood.

The announcement

OK Movement withdraws support from Obi and the NDC

27 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

The OK Movement, named after Peter Obi and Rabiu Kwankwaso, has reportedly pulled its campaign support from the NDC. Its director-general said the group had been told Obi and the party had chosen to cut ties with it.

Read more

The OK Movement, a support group named after the initials of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso, has reportedly withdrawn its campaign and mobilisation support from the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

The announcement

In a statement on Friday, 27 November 2026, the movement's national director-general, John Ughulu, said the group had been informed that Obi and the NDC had decided to cut ties with it. He directed the movement's ward, local government and state structures to stop all involvement in NDC activities immediately.

What next

Ughulu said the group had begun discussions with other political parties and presidential aspirants.

Background

The development comes days before Obi is due to attend an opposition summit in Abuja with Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde.

The other side

No response from Obi or the NDC was included in the reports.

Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke was inaugurated for a second term on Friday, 27 November 2026, at the Osogbo Township Stadium.

Family affair

Afrobeats star Davido, the governor's nephew, drew almost as much attention as the ceremony itself.
