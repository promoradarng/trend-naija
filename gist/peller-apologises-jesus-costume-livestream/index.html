Peller apologises after calling himself Jesus Christ on livestream

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

Popular streamer Habeeb Adelaja, known as Peller, has apologised after a clip from his livestream in which he referred to himself as Jesus Christ drew widespread criticism.

What happened

In the clip, which spread online on Sunday, 1 November 2026, Peller appeared in a white and red costume with a wig and greeted his guest as Jesus Christ. The guest refused to go along with the joke and told him not to disrespect any religion, pointing out that someone with about 17 million followers had a responsibility to the people watching. Peller argued that he was only wearing a costume and that nobody alive knows what Jesus looked like. During the exchange he also said he was a traditionalist and joked about dressing as an Imam the next day, which the people with him warned was only making things worse.

The apology

In a follow-up video posted the same day, Peller said he had not expected the outfit to cause such a reaction, describing it as just a white cloth. He described himself as both a Christian and a Muslim, apparently to show that he meant no offence to either faith, and asked his fans to forgive him.

How people reacted

Many social media users criticised the stunt, saying religion should not be used for content. Some said the apology fell short because he did not clearly admit he was wrong, while others defended him as a young entertainer who made a mistake.

Background

Peller, one of Nigeria's best-known TikTok streamers, is often in the news for his livestreams. Last week, rapper YCee used his name as an example of what he called brain-rot content and refused to apologise for it. Peller and fellow content creator Jarvis held a white wedding in Lekki, Lagos, in August.

IK Ogbonna and Ego Nwosu reveal viral proposal was a promo for a wedding-themed event

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actors IK Ogbonna and Ego Nwosu have reportedly confirmed they are not getting married, after their viral proposal video drew congratulations. They said it was to promote a wedding-themed event in Port Harcourt.

Nollywood actors IK Ogbonna and Ego Nwosu have reportedly cleared the air on their viral proposal video, confirming that they are not actually getting married.

The reveal

In a new video posted on Monday, 16 November 2026, the pair appeared together and explained that the proposal was part of a campaign for an event called Love at Julirose, billed for 15 December in Port Harcourt, Rivers State.

The event is described as a full wedding-themed experience, with a live band, bridesmaids and groomsmen, photographers and live performances. Tickets are on sale with different price levels and a dress code.

The playful banter

The two kept up the jokes in the new video, with IK saying he was still the groom and Ego replying that she was still the bride, making it clear the campaign was designed to create buzz.

How fans reacted

Many fans who had congratulated the pair earlier this month reacted with amusement and mock anger, with some jokingly asking for their congratulations back.

Yul Edochie sparks debate after criticising men who kneel to propose

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actor Yul Edochie has reportedly drawn mixed reactions after describing kneeling to propose as a foreign practice that lowers African men. He was reacting to a viral video of a rejected proposal.

Read more

Nollywood actor Yul Edochie has reportedly sparked a fresh online debate after criticising men who go down on one knee to propose marriage.

What happened

Yul was reacting to a viral video, said to have been edited, showing a man kneeling to propose to his partner at an event, only for her to turn him down.

In a post on X, he reportedly described kneeling to propose as a practice Africans copied from Western culture, arguing that it reduces the dignity of African men. He used strong language to dismiss men who do it.

How people reacted

His comments drew a wide range of responses. Some users backed his view on cultural identity, while many others pushed back, saying the way a man proposes has nothing to do with the quality of a marriage, and some criticised the actor directly.
