Peller apologises after calling himself Jesus Christ on livestream

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

Popular streamer Habeeb Adelaja, known as Peller, has apologised after a clip from his livestream in which he referred to himself as Jesus Christ drew widespread criticism.

What happened

In the clip, which spread online on Sunday, 1 November 2026, Peller appeared in a white and red costume with a wig and greeted his guest as Jesus Christ. The guest refused to go along with the joke and told him not to disrespect any religion, pointing out that someone with about 17 million followers had a responsibility to the people watching. Peller argued that he was only wearing a costume and that nobody alive knows what Jesus looked like. During the exchange he also said he was a traditionalist and joked about dressing as an Imam the next day, which the people with him warned was only making things worse.

The apology

In a follow-up video posted the same day, Peller said he had not expected the outfit to cause such a reaction, describing it as just a white cloth. He described himself as both a Christian and a Muslim, apparently to show that he meant no offence to either faith, and asked his fans to forgive him.

How people reacted

Many social media users criticised the stunt, saying religion should not be used for content. Some said the apology fell short because he did not clearly admit he was wrong, while others defended him as a young entertainer who made a mistake.

Background

Peller, one of Nigeria's best-known TikTok streamers, is often in the news for his livestreams. Last week, rapper YCee used his name as an example of what he called brain-rot content and refused to apologise for it. Peller and fellow content creator Jarvis held a white wedding in Lekki, Lagos, in August.

BBNaija's Faith builds filter game for Funke Akindele's new film The Four

11 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Reality star Dr Faith Adewale has created a Snapchat filter game inspired by Funke Akindele's upcoming film The Four, complete with a ticket-booking button. Funke Akindele reacted warmly to the fan-made promotion.

Read more

Big Brother Naija star Dr Faith Adewale has won praise online after creating a game to promote Funke Akindele's upcoming film, The Four.

The game

In a video shared on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, Faith showed how the Snapchat filter works. Players are paired with three of their real friends and must complete quick physical challenges, such as tapping the screen rapidly or raising their eyebrows, to 'rescue' each one. When all three are saved, the group of four is reunited, echoing the theme of the film. Faith set a time of 15.4 seconds and challenged her followers to beat it. The filter also includes a button that links to a cinema website where users can buy tickets.

Funke reacts

Funke Akindele, who directs and stars in the film, spotted the post and responded with delight, and the cinema chain also celebrated the idea online.

Background

The Four is due in Nigerian cinemas on 11 December 2026, in time for the festive season. Funke Akindele is one of Nollywood's most commercially successful filmmakers, with several of her previous December releases breaking box office records.

Bobrisky hits back at TBoss over criticism of men in feminine fashion

11 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Bobrisky has responded to reality star TBoss, who criticised Nigerian men adopting feminine dressing styles at Lagos fashion events, saying nobody has the right to dictate how adults dress.

Read more

Socialite Bobrisky has publicly responded to Big Brother Naija alumna TBoss over her comments about men who dress in feminine styles.

How it started

TBoss, whose real name is Tokunbo Idowu, had expressed disapproval of what she described as a growing trend of Nigerian men adopting feminine styles, following outfits seen at GTCO Fashion Week in Lagos, saying 'we're slowly losing our men'. Her remarks sparked debate, with influencer Izzy Ogbeide and model Onyx Godwin among those who disagreed.

Bobrisky's response

In a series of Instagram posts on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, Bobrisky said they held TBoss in some regard but found her comments out of line, insisting that nobody has the right to tell another adult how to dress or express themselves. Bobrisky drew a distinction between sharing a personal opinion and trying to impose it on others.

TBoss responds

TBoss later responded to the backlash on social media, saying her comment was never about anyone's sexual preference or what people do in private, but only about what was being shown in public. She complained that people could no longer share a different opinion without being insulted, and criticised commentator Lizzy Ogbeide for bringing up her upbringing. Skit maker Steve Chuks was also among those who criticised her original remarks.

Background

Bobrisky now lives abroad. Content creator Enioluwa also defended the men's outfits at the fashion week, saying it was a fashion show and nobody was losing anything. TBoss had not responded at the time of writing.

Controversial relationship therapist Blessing CEO has expressed shock and gratitude over an unexpected gesture from social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM).

"I expected him to drag me"

According to her, she had expected VDM to take advantage of her difficult moment and drag her publicly, given their long history of disagreements.

Instead, she says, he offered her a helping hand and assured her that he would do whatever it takes to help her get back on her feet.

Tears and gratitude

An emotional Blessing CEO admitted that the gesture humbled her and brought tears to her eyes. She promised to share the full story once she has regained her composure.

She also asked people to help her thank VDM, and declared that she is now officially a member of the Ratel Movement.

A difficult season

The gesture comes during a hard period for Blessing CEO. In recent days she has publicly apologised to people she may have offended, described herself as broken and compared her journey to the prodigal son. She also announced that her relationship with businessman IVD has ended.

Never say never

Who would have imagined Blessing CEO and VDM on the same side after everything that has happened between them? In this life, never say never.
