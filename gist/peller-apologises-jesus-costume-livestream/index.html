Peller apologises after calling himself Jesus Christ on livestream

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

Popular streamer Habeeb Adelaja, known as Peller, has apologised after a clip from his livestream in which he referred to himself as Jesus Christ drew widespread criticism.

What happened

In the clip, which spread online on Sunday, 1 November 2026, Peller appeared in a white and red costume with a wig and greeted his guest as Jesus Christ. The guest refused to go along with the joke and told him not to disrespect any religion, pointing out that someone with about 17 million followers had a responsibility to the people watching. Peller argued that he was only wearing a costume and that nobody alive knows what Jesus looked like. During the exchange he also said he was a traditionalist and joked about dressing as an Imam the next day, which the people with him warned was only making things worse.

The apology

In a follow-up video posted the same day, Peller said he had not expected the outfit to cause such a reaction, describing it as just a white cloth. He described himself as both a Christian and a Muslim, apparently to show that he meant no offence to either faith, and asked his fans to forgive him.

How people reacted

Many social media users criticised the stunt, saying religion should not be used for content. Some said the apology fell short because he did not clearly admit he was wrong, while others defended him as a young entertainer who made a mistake.

Background

Peller, one of Nigeria's best-known TikTok streamers, is often in the news for his livestreams. Last week, rapper YCee used his name as an example of what he called brain-rot content and refused to apologise for it. Peller and fellow content creator Jarvis held a white wedding in Lekki, Lagos, in August.

Seyi Law admits his finances were better before Tinubu, but backs reforms

25 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Comedian Seyi Law has reportedly admitted that living standards have dropped under President Tinubu and that his own finances were better before 2023. He still defended the government's reforms, saying good policies take years to stabilise.

Read more

Comedian Seyi Law, a known supporter of President Bola Tinubu, has reportedly admitted that living standards have fallen sharply under the current administration.

What he said

Speaking on a podcast hosted by Nedu, Seyi Law is said to have acknowledged that his personal finances were much better before Tinubu took office in 2023, and that it would be hypocritical to say Nigerians are not suffering.

Still backing the reforms

He nevertheless defended the government's economic reforms, arguing that Nigerians would benefit in the long run. Comparing Nigeria with China, he said major national transformations often come with heavy early sacrifices. When the host asked whether that meant Nigerians must suffer greatly first, he said people must feel the pain of a policy before reaping its benefits, adding that good policies usually stabilise after 10 to 12 years.

How people reacted

His remarks have sparked debate online.

Several killed as commercial bus catches fire on Lagos-Badagry Expressway

26 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Several passengers died and more than a dozen were injured when a commercial bus burst into flames at Magbon on the Lagos-Badagry Expressway on Thursday morning. Officials gave death tolls of six and eight.

Read more

Several people were killed and others injured when a commercial bus caught fire on the Lagos-Badagry Expressway on Thursday morning, 26 November 2026.

What happened

The incident happened near the Checkpoint area, inward Magbon, between 8am and 9am. The bus, travelling from Mile 2 towards Badagry, reportedly suffered brake failure, and the driver lost control. The vehicle somersaulted and burst into flames, trapping passengers inside.

The toll

Officials gave different figures. The Lagos State Fire and Rescue Service put the death toll at six adults, two women and four men, with 12 others injured. The Lagos State Traffic Management Authority (LASTMA) said eight passengers died and 13 suffered severe burns. The injured were taken to Badagry General Hospital.

Driver fled

The LASTMA general manager said the driver fled the scene after the crash, leaving passengers trapped, and offered condolences to the families of the victims. Traffic and road safety officers were deployed to the scene.
