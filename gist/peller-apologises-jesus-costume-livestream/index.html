Peller apologises after calling himself Jesus Christ on livestream

2 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT

Popular streamer Habeeb Adelaja, known as Peller, has apologised after a clip from his livestream in which he referred to himself as Jesus Christ drew widespread criticism.

What happened

In the clip, which spread online on Sunday, 1 November 2026, Peller appeared in a white and red costume with a wig and greeted his guest as Jesus Christ. The guest refused to go along with the joke and told him not to disrespect any religion, pointing out that someone with about 17 million followers had a responsibility to the people watching. Peller argued that he was only wearing a costume and that nobody alive knows what Jesus looked like. During the exchange he also said he was a traditionalist and joked about dressing as an Imam the next day, which the people with him warned was only making things worse.

The apology

In a follow-up video posted the same day, Peller said he had not expected the outfit to cause such a reaction, describing it as just a white cloth. He described himself as both a Christian and a Muslim, apparently to show that he meant no offence to either faith, and asked his fans to forgive him.

How people reacted

Many social media users criticised the stunt, saying religion should not be used for content. Some said the apology fell short because he did not clearly admit he was wrong, while others defended him as a young entertainer who made a mistake.

Background

Peller, one of Nigeria's best-known TikTok streamers, is often in the news for his livestreams. Last week, rapper YCee used his name as an example of what he called brain-rot content and refused to apologise for it. Peller and fellow content creator Jarvis held a white wedding in Lekki, Lagos, in August.

Genevieve Nnaji wows fans with rare appearance at Lagos Fashion Week

7 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nollywood star Genevieve Nnaji drew a flood of praise from fans and colleagues after a rare public appearance at Lagos Fashion Week, wearing a dress straight from a designer's new collection.

Read more

Nollywood icon Genevieve Nnaji has set social media buzzing after a rare public appearance at Lagos Fashion Week.

What happened

Photos shared on Friday, 6 November 2026, showed the actress, who has kept a low profile in recent years, wearing a dress from the Nigerian label Orire's new spring/summer 2027 collection, taken straight from the runway. The pictures were shared by celebrity stylist and designer Veronica Odeka.

Stars react

Several Nollywood stars flooded the comments with praise. Actress Erica Nlewedim joked that the photos explained why everywhere suddenly felt so hot, while actor Steve Chuks also left a widely shared reaction. Fans also added their own tributes in the comments.

Background

Genevieve Nnaji rose to fame in the late 1990s and became one of Nollywood's biggest stars. In 2018 she directed 'Lionheart', which became the first Netflix original film from Nigeria. She has largely stepped away from the spotlight since then, which is why her occasional appearances attract so much attention. Lagos Fashion Week is one of Africa's biggest fashion events, and this year's edition has also featured Lagos governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu walking the runway.

Veteran Nollywood actor Diewait Ikpechukwu dies at 71

6 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Veteran Nollywood actor and former footballer Diewait Ikpechukwu has died at the age of 71 after a long illness, according to family sources. He was known for roles in classic films including August Meeting and Royal Palace.

Read more

Veteran Nollywood actor Diewait Ikpechukwu has died at the age of 71.

What happened

According to family sources, the actor died on Thursday, 5 November 2026, after a prolonged illness. News of his death was reported on Friday. Further details about the circumstances of his death had not been made public at the time of writing, and the family had not announced funeral arrangements.

His career

Before becoming an actor, Ikpechukwu was a professional footballer. He went on to have a long career in Nollywood and became a familiar face in classic Nigerian films. His film credits include 'August Meeting', 'Royal Palace', 'The Guilty', 'Across the River', 'Python King' and 'Ìjé Love', among many others.

Background

His death adds to a run of losses for Nollywood. The family of the late actor Francis Duru also recently announced burial arrangements for him.

Doris Ogala reportedly taken to hospital after losing consciousness in detention

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Actress and Abia governorship candidate Doris Ogala has reportedly been taken to hospital after blacking out and bleeding while in detention, according to a video shared by a person close to her. Her bail ruling was due on Friday.

Read more

Nollywood actress Doris Ogala, the African Action Congress (AAC) governorship candidate in Abia State, has reportedly been taken to hospital from detention.

What was said

On Thursday, 5 November 2026, a person close to the actress shared a video online saying he had spoken to her by phone while she was receiving treatment in hospital. According to him, she said she had lost consciousness, was bleeding and could not remember how she had been taken to the hospital. In the same video, Ogala could reportedly be heard speaking about her condition and raising concerns about bleeding linked to an earlier surgical procedure. The authorities have not publicly commented on her health.

Background

Ogala was remanded in custody in Lagos in late October after being arraigned before a Federal High Court on three counts linked to a complaint by the cleric Pastor Chris Okafor. The charges relate to the alleged publication of photographs, alleged false electronic messages and alleged threats. She has not been convicted of anything, and has described the case as an attempt to stop her governorship bid. At a later sitting she apologised to the court for her conduct at an earlier hearing, and the judge fixed Friday, 6 November, for a ruling on her bail application. Earlier this week, a medical report shared online said she had been treated in June for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters have expressed concern and called for her release on health grounds, urging the authorities to make sure she receives proper medical care while the case continues.

What happens next

Attention is now on the court's decision on her bail application.

Scripted slap from Sola Sobowale reportedly leaves mark on Toyin Abraham's face

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

A behind-the-scenes clip shows Toyin Abraham being comforted on a film set after a scripted slap from veteran actress Sola Sobowale reportedly left a mark on her cheek. Toyin is said to have insisted on retaking the scene.

Read more

A behind-the-scenes video from a film set has got fans talking after a scripted slap from Sola Sobowale reportedly left a visible mark on Toyin Abraham's face.

What the video shows

The clip, which went viral on Friday, 6 November 2026, shows Toyin seated and visibly shaken, with people around her fanning her and a man standing close by, apparently trying to reassure her. The scene appears to have been filmed indoors, in a home setting. According to reports, the slap was part of the script, but the impact was strong enough to leave a mark on her cheek. Toyin reportedly insisted that the crew retake the scene rather than stop filming for the day. The title of the production and the location of the shoot were not revealed.

Background

Sola Sobowale is one of Nollywood's most celebrated veteran actresses, known for powerful performances over several decades, including her role in the 'King of Boys' films. This week she opened an elderly care home in Ibadan, which was commissioned by Governor Seyi Makinde. Toyin Abraham is a popular actress and producer who recently announced that veteran actor Pete Edochie will star in her December cinema film, 'Bestie'. She has also been in the news this week after sending a cease and desist letter to bloggers and announcing a change to her name.

How people reacted

Fans praised Toyin's dedication to her craft, while others expressed concern for her and debated whether real slaps should be used in films at all, with some urging producers to use safer stage-fighting techniques.
