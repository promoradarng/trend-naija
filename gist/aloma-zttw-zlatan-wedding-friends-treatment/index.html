Rivers politics: Fubara backs Wike's cross-party Rainbow Coalition and its 2027 candidates

Read more

Governor Siminalayi Fubara has publicly pledged support for President Tinubu, Nyesom Wike and the Rainbow Coalition's 2027 candidates in Rivers State. The seven-party alliance unveiled Kingsley Chinda and Major Jack, both of the APC, as its governorship and deputy governorship choices. The rest of its slate combines APC and PDP candidates.

Why it is trending

"Rainbow Coalition" entered Nigeria's trending topics on X. The unveiling confirms that Fubara and Wike are now openly campaigning within the same political bloc after their long-running disagreement.

What has changed

The reconciliation has moved from meetings and public statements to a jointly supported electoral slate. The coalition adopted:

Kingsley Chinda and Major Jack for governor and deputy governor.

Two APC and one PDP senatorial candidates.

Ten APC and three PDP House of Representatives candidates.

Twenty-one APC and eleven PDP State Assembly candidates.

Still uncertain

A court challenge to Chinda's eligibility remains unresolved, with a hearing reportedly scheduled for 4 November 2026. The September Supreme Court decision at the centre of the dispute did not specifically disqualify Chinda, and rival groups disagree over what it means. The coalition's endorsement therefore does not settle the pending legal question.
