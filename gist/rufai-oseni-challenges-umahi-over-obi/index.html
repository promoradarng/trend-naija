Rufai Oseni challenges Umahi to win six million votes before comparing himself to Obi

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Television presenter Rufai Oseni has responded sharply to Minister of Works Dave Umahi's recent comments about NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi.

What Umahi said

At an APC town hall in Abakaliki, Ebonyi State, on Monday, 2 November 2026, Umahi reportedly questioned Obi's political relevance, described him as a 'nobody' and called himself the 'Lion of the South East'. He has also asked what makes Obi better than past South East leaders such as Sam Egwu and Anyim Pius Anyim, and has argued that the region should back President Bola Tinubu so that an Igbo candidate can later get eight years rather than four.

Oseni's response

Speaking on his breakfast television programme on Tuesday, 3 November, Oseni reportedly said self-given titles mean little without results. He said that before Umahi can compare himself with Obi, he should obtain a presidential nomination form, contest a general election and win at least six million votes, roughly the number Obi received in the 2023 presidential election.

Background

Umahi, a former two-term governor of Ebonyi State, is a leading APC figure in the South East and has campaigned hard for Tinubu's re-election. Obi, a former Anambra governor, came third in the 2023 presidential election as the Labour Party candidate and is now running on the NDC ticket with Rabiu Kwankwaso. The South East is seen as an important battleground in 2027, and four governors from the zone recently declared support for Tinubu.

How people reacted

Obi's supporters cheered Oseni's comments, while APC supporters said Umahi's record as governor and minister speaks for itself.

Amaechi says Nigerians now miss Buhari because life was better under him

10 Nov 2026, 20:44 GMT

ADC vice-presidential candidate Rotimi Amaechi says Nigerians miss former President Buhari, urging them to compare the exchange rate and fuel prices under Buhari with today. The APC says the reforms were needed to save the economy.

Read more

Rotimi Amaechi, the vice-presidential candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), says Nigerians miss former President Muhammadu Buhari because life was better under his government.

What he said

Speaking on a television programme on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Amaechi recalled Buhari's prediction that people would one day call him back, and said Nigerians were now saying they miss him. He urged people to compare their lives under Buhari with their circumstances today, pointing to the exchange rate, fuel prices and what he described as corruption.

Background

Amaechi served as Buhari's Minister of Transportation and directed his 2015 campaign, before losing the APC presidential ticket to Bola Tinubu in 2022. He said last week that Buhari had promised in writing to back him at that primary. Buhari died in 2025. The naira traded at about ₦461 to the dollar when Tinubu took office in May 2023 and petrol was subsidised; both changed sharply after the President removed the subsidy and unified the exchange rate. Tinubu says those reforms saved Nigeria from economic collapse and that the naira has since recovered some value.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Imo Catholic priest apologises for singing Peter Obi campaign song in church

10 Nov 2026, 20:44 GMT

Rev. Fr. Finbarr Chukwu, a Catholic priest in Imo State, has apologised after a viral video showed him singing a campaign song associated with Peter Obi during a church service, saying he joined in spontaneously.

Read more

A Catholic priest in Imo State has apologised after a video of him singing a campaign song associated with Peter Obi during a church service went viral.

The apology

Rev. Fr. Finbarr Chukwu, who is attached to St. Dominic's Secondary School, Ugiri, in Isiala Mbano Local Government Area, explained himself in a letter dated 4 November 2026, addressed to the Catholic Bishop of Okigwe Diocese, Most Rev. Solomon Amatu, fellow priests, the faithful and the public. He said the incident happened during a thanksgiving service attended by a family and some people linked to a political campaign. When some of them began singing campaign songs, he said, he joined in spontaneously without considering how it would be seen.

Regret

He said he later recognised the implications of his action and had advised those present to respect the sanctity of church functions by keeping politics out of religious events.

Background

Obi, now the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), is a Catholic and has a strong following in the South-East. The video spread widely amid heated campaigning ahead of the 16 January 2027 election.

Otti orders arrest of anyone attacking opposition campaigns in Abia, including his own party members

10 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Abia governor Alex Otti has told supporters that nobody has his authority to attack opposition parties and directed security agencies to arrest anyone who disrupts lawful political activities, including members of his own Labour Party.

Read more

Abia State governor Alex Otti has warned against political violence and intimidation ahead of the 2027 elections.

What he said

Speaking to supporters on Monday, 9 November 2026, Otti said no person or group had his mandate to attack opposition parties or stop their members from campaigning. He directed security agencies to arrest and deal decisively with anyone who tries to disrupt lawful political activities in the state, regardless of party, including members of his own Labour Party.

Background

Concerns about political violence have grown as campaigns intensify. In the past week, one person died and five were injured in a clash at an NDC rally in Asaba, Delta State, and supporters of different parties have been attacked in other states. INEC has also condemned inflammatory slogans and warned of plots against election workers. Otti, a former banker, won the Abia governorship on the Labour Party platform in 2023, when Peter Obi was the party's presidential candidate. Obi has since moved to the NDC.

APC chairman says Tinubu's dancing and busy campaign schedule show he is fit

10 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

APC national chairman Nentawe Yilwatda has defended President Tinubu's fitness for office, pointing to the President dancing in Akwa Ibom and 48 hours of non-stop engagements in Akwa Ibom and Rivers.

Read more

The national chairman of the All Progressives Congress (APC), Nentawe Yilwatda, has defended President Bola Tinubu's physical fitness for a second term.

What he said

Speaking on a television politics programme on Monday, 9 November 2026, Yilwatda pointed to the President's busy weekend in Akwa Ibom and Rivers states as evidence of his health. He said he was there in person when Tinubu danced to local music in Akwa Ibom, and that the President attended a dinner and town hall meeting late on Friday before travelling to Rivers the next morning. He argued that someone who was not fit could not manage 48 hours of non-stop engagements and travel.

Background

Critics have raised questions about the President's health since before the 2023 election, and Obi has previously criticised the time Tinubu spends abroad. His supporters say he is fully fit and point to his campaign tour, which this week also includes Ebonyi and Jigawa states.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
