Gospel singer Toluwanisings buried in Ibadan after family dispute

9 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Gospel singer Olayemi Bolaji, popularly known as Toluwanisings, is being laid to rest in Ibadan, Oyo State, on Monday, 9 November 2026.

The arrangements

According to funeral details shared on the singer's official Instagram page, the lying-in-state was scheduled for 9am to 10am, followed by a burial service from 10am to 11am at an event centre on Hope Road, Alakia, Ibadan. The announcement did not say where he would be interred.

The dispute

Toluwanisings died in the early hours of 14 October 2026. His burial was delayed by weeks of disagreement between his widow and his family over the arrangements, reportedly including where he should be buried. The Oyo State Police Command stepped in after learning of the dispute on 28 October, with Commissioner of Police Olugbenga Abimbola helping the two sides reach an agreement.

The burial

Videos from Monday's ceremony showed pallbearers in black suits carrying the singer's white casket, led by trumpeters, before he was taken to his home, where a private burial was held. Some fans remarked on the absence of fellow gospel artistes at the ceremony.

Background

Toluwanisings was a Nigerian gospel singer. The public dispute over his burial attracted wide attention.

  • The burial is in Ibadan on 9 November.
  • He died on 14 October.
  • Police helped resolve a family dispute.

Ayra Starr confirmed for Coachella 2027 on SZA-headlined Sunday

11 Nov 2026, 17:46 GMT

Ayra Starr will perform at the 2027 Coachella festival in California, appearing on the Sunday bill of both weekends, which SZA headlines. The singer told fans: 'See you in the desert.'

Afrobeats star Ayra Starr has been announced as one of the performers at the 2027 Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival.

The line-up

The festival's organisers revealed the line-up on Instagram on Wednesday, 11 November 2026. Ayra Starr is on the Sunday bill for both weekends and is expected to perform on 11 and 18 April 2027. The Sunday shows are headlined by American R&B star SZA, with Don Toliver, Kygo, Jorja Smith, Victoria Monét and Tove Lo among the other acts. Dua Lipa is among the festival's other headliners. Coachella takes place at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, from 9 to 11 April and 16 to 18 April.

Her reaction

The singer shared the news on her own Instagram page with a short message telling fans she would see them in the desert.

Background

Ayra Starr, signed to Mavin Records, rose to international fame with hits such as 'Rush' and has become one of the most successful young Nigerian artists of her generation. Coachella is one of the world's biggest music festivals.

Usher and Chris Brown's Los Angeles concert reportedly cancelled amid feud

11 Nov 2026, 17:46 GMT

Usher and Chris Brown's 16 November show at SoFi Stadium has reportedly been marked as cancelled on Ticketmaster, with fans told they will be refunded. It is unclear whether it is linked to the singers' backstage row in Atlanta.

Read more

A Los Angeles concert by R&B stars Usher and Chris Brown has reportedly been cancelled.

What happened

According to reports on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, the pair's 16 November show at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, was marked as cancelled on Ticketmaster, and ticket holders were told they would receive refunds. No reason was given. Their 15 November show at the same venue, and their remaining Atlanta dates, were still listed as going ahead.

The feud

It is not clear whether the cancellation is linked to the reported argument between the two singers before their Atlanta concert last week. Usher reportedly wanted to start that show on time, while Brown wanted to wait for fans stuck in traffic, and the disagreement was said to have delayed the performance by almost three hours. Sources quoted in reports say the two have still not met to settle their differences and describe the relationship as frosty.

Background

The two stars are on a joint tour. Neither singer had publicly commented on the cancellation at the time of writing.

Rapper and record label boss Olamide has announced a reunion concert featuring some of the biggest names to come through his label, YBNL.

The line-up

In an Instagram video on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Olamide said the show will take place in Lagos on 18 December as part of a December festival. The line-up includes Adekunle Gold, Lil Kesh, Fireboy DML and Young Jonn, as well as producer Pheelz and DJ Enimoney.

Why it matters

Background

Olamide founded YBNL in 2012 and has helped launch several successful careers. He recently announced a headline show at London's 20,000-capacity O2 Arena on 25 March 2027, and released a new single in October.

Peter Obi and Rabiu Kwankwaso visit Yelewata community in Benue State, offer support to vulnerable children

11 Nov 2026, 19:53 GMT

Peter Obi and Rabiu Kwankwaso have visited Yelewata in Benue State, where Obi laid a wreath at the memorial to victims of the community's deadly 2025 attack. The two leaders donated ₦10 million for vulnerable children and promised a motorised borehole.

Read more

Peter Obi and Rabiu Kwankwaso have visited Yelewata community in Benue State, bringing some hope to residents who have endured years of hardship and neglect.

A moment of remembrance

Yelewata was the scene of a deadly attack in June 2025 that left many residents dead and shocked the nation. During the visit, Obi knelt to lay a wreath at the memorial bearing the names of the victims, as a large crowd looked on.

Donations and promises

The two political leaders promised to provide a motorised borehole to help address the community's water needs. They also donated ₦10 million to support vulnerable children, while Professor Shija contributed another ₦2 million towards the education of orphans.

The people speak

Beyond the donations, the people of Yelewata had their own stories to tell. They spoke about years of neglect, the difficulties they face every day and the lack of meaningful government support.

Imagine living for years without some of the basic things that should make life easier, while those elected to serve appear distant from your suffering. For residents, the visit was a chance to make their voices heard and draw attention to their situation.

Action, not just visits

Promises must be followed by action. The community needs lasting solutions, not just occasional visits.

Leadership should be about compassion, listening to the people and doing something practical to improve their lives.
