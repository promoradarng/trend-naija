Governor Peter Mbah claims he started the 'APC Ka Mma' campaign song

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Enugu State governor Peter Mbah has sparked reactions after claiming credit for a popular campaign song.

What he said

In comments shared on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, Mbah reportedly said he was the first person to start singing 'APC Ka Mma', an Igbo phrase meaning 'APC is better'. He added that he was not sure other political parties had been given copyright permission to sing it. The song has become popular at campaign events, and supporters of other parties have adapted it, including a recent viral version in which a Catholic priest led a congregation in singing that Peter Obi's party is better.

Background

Mbah was elected governor of Enugu State in 2023 on the platform of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) and later joined the All Progressives Congress (APC). He is one of the South East governors who have declared support for President Bola Tinubu's re-election, and Enugu has hosted major APC rallies. The South East is expected to be a key battleground between Tinubu and Obi, a former Anambra governor.

How people reacted

Many social media users criticised the comment, saying a campaign song should not be used to restrict political expression and that the governor should focus on economic and security problems. Others saw it as a joke and said the song's popularity showed the APC was gaining ground in the region.

  • Critics said he should focus on bigger issues.

Senate President Godswill Akpabio has reportedly said Akwa Ibom State will give overwhelming support to President Bola Tinubu in the 2027 elections, setting a target of two million votes.

The event

Akpabio spoke at Ibom Hall Ground in Uyo, where the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement inaugurated its Akwa Ibom executives. The movement's grand patron, former militant leader Government Ekpemupolo, known as Tompolo, donated 31 campaign vehicles and other campaign materials. What was said

Akpabio is said to have praised the movement's growing grassroots presence. Its national coordinator described the Akwa Ibom inauguration as another milestone in its nationwide expansion.

Background

Campaigns for the January 2027 elections are now under way across the country, with parties building grassroots support in the states.

Makinde says Tinubu has only a 30% chance of winning in 2027

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly put President Tinubu's chances of re-election at 30 per cent, citing economic hardship and insecurity. The figure is his own estimate, not from a poll.

Read more

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly said President Bola Tinubu has only a 30 per cent chance of winning the 2027 presidential election.

His argument

Makinde is said to have argued that poor governance has worn away the goodwill the ruling APC once enjoyed. He pointed to food insecurity, the rising cost of living and insecurity as the main reasons he believes many Nigerians have turned against the party.

He also reportedly accused the APC leadership of dismissing citizens' concerns instead of addressing them.

Not a poll

The 30 per cent figure is Makinde's own assessment and was not taken from any published opinion poll or independent survey.

Background

Makinde is himself among the challengers in the race. His comments come days after Tinubu's APC held a campaign rally in Ibadan, the Oyo State capital.

Defence Ministry deletes post promoting Tinubu's Ibadan rally after backlash

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Ministry of Defence has reportedly removed a post from its official X account that publicised the APC presidential rally in Ibadan. Critics said it breached rules on the use of state resources in campaigns.

Read more

Nigeria's Ministry of Defence has reportedly taken down a post from its official social media accounts that promoted President Bola Tinubu's APC campaign rally in Ibadan, after a public outcry over the neutrality of state institutions.

The post

The post, published on Saturday on the ministry's verified X account, announced the APC's re-election rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, and included photos showing campaign banners of Tinubu and Vice President Kashim Shettima. It said Defence Minister Christopher Musa had inspected the venue to check security and crowd-control arrangements ahead of the president's arrival.

The criticism

Civic groups and opposition figures are said to have condemned the post, arguing that it went against the Electoral Act's ban on using state resources and public media to favour or disadvantage any party, as well as guidelines that require public institutions to remain neutral.

Background

Tinubu held the rally in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026, where APC candidates in the South-West were endorsed. He later said he kept the event low-key out of respect for victims of the Bayelsa boat accident. The ministry was not reported to have commented on why the post was removed.

Atiku denies quitting 2027 race, says those spreading the claim are working for Tinubu

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly dismissed claims that he has withdrawn from the 2027 election. His campaign said no group calling itself his supporters can speak for him, and accused rivals of tactics that help the ruling party.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly rejected claims that he has pulled out of the 2027 presidential race, describing them as a deliberate falsehood.

What his campaign said

In a statement issued on Sunday, 22 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, the campaign is said to have stressed that no group presenting itself as Atiku's supporters had the authority to announce a withdrawal on his behalf.

The statement reportedly accused those behind the claim of borrowing tactics it associated with the ruling APC, such as presenting so-called supporters from a rival camp. It said any opposition party spreading such stories was effectively helping President Bola Tinubu, and urged parties that want to defeat the APC to make their case directly to voters.

Still campaigning

Atiku's campaign said his Affordable Life campaign was still active and focused on the election.

Background

The denial comes as campaigns for the January 2027 presidential election gather pace, with Tinubu of the APC, Atiku of the ADC and Peter Obi of the NDC among the leading candidates. Speculation about possible withdrawals and alliances has been circulating online in recent days.
