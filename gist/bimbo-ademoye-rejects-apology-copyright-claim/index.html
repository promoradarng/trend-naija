Bimbo Ademoye rejects apology from man whose copyright claim hit her YouTube film

10 Nov 2026, 17:47 GMT

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Bimbo Ademoye has rejected an apology from a man whose copyright claim affected her YouTube film.

What happened

According to reports on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, the man uploaded the soundtrack from Ademoye's film 'My 30th Wedding' to a music streaming platform, which triggered an automatic copyright claim on the film on her YouTube channel, cutting off its earnings. He later posted an apology on Instagram, accepting responsibility and saying he had taken the song down from all platforms and that the claim should clear within 24 to 48 hours.

Her response

Ademoye shared a screenshot of the apology and said she was not satisfied with his explanation, vowing to take action. She also questioned why he had chosen that particular song from the film rather than a more popular one, suggesting it was because the artist behind the other song would not have tolerated it.

Background

Ademoye is one of Nollywood's most popular actresses and has built a large audience for films released on YouTube. Automated copyright systems such as YouTube's Content ID have become a common source of disputes for Nigerian filmmakers, as false or mistaken claims can redirect earnings away from creators.

  • He apologised and removed the song.
  • Ademoye says she will still take action.

The visit

A video circulating on social media shows actresses Sola Sobowale, Ebele Okaro, Chinyere Wilfred and Gloria Young spending time with Nwosu and encouraging her. During the visit, the group appealed to members of the public to help her financially ahead of her next medical procedure.

Earlier appeal

How fans reacted

Chude Jideonwo recalls how Pete Edochie made him bring a cow

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Media personality Chude Jideonwo has paid tribute to the late Pete Edochie, recalling how the actor reportedly asked him for a cow after he arrived late and empty-handed for an interview. He said the cow was delivered.

Read more

Media personality Chude Jideonwo has paid tribute to the late Nollywood legend Pete Edochie with a memorable story about a visit to the actor's home in Enugu.

The cow

In an Instagram post, Jideonwo recalled arriving late at Edochie's residence to conduct a special interview. He said the veteran actor quickly pointed out that he had come to the home of a titled man empty-handed and asked for a cow. Jideonwo said the cow was indeed delivered.

His tribute

Jideonwo described Edochie as a proud man without arrogance, who commanded any room he entered and never felt threatened by other people. He said the actor's death had made him reflect on an older Nigerian identity rooted in culture and dignity.

Background

Pete Edochie, one of Nigeria's most celebrated actors and broadcasters, died on 25 November 2026 at the age of 79 in Enugu. Tributes have continued to pour in from across the entertainment industry, including from Patience Ozokwo and his family.

Omotola Jalade opens up on difficult bond with late mother and Nollywood blacklist

28 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Veteran actress Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde has spoken about her strained relationship with her late mother and her regrets since her death. She also said she was once blacklisted in Nollywood for demanding better standards.

Read more

Veteran Nollywood actress Omotola Jalade-Ekeinde has spoken openly about her complicated relationship with her late mother and about being blacklisted in the film industry early in her career.

A difficult bond

In an interview released on Friday, 27 November 2026, Omotola said she and her mother did not always get along. Her mother worried that the film industry would expose her daughter to immorality, and the tension grew when Omotola began dating her husband, Captain Matthew Ekeinde. When he visited to formally ask for her hand, her mother reportedly questioned whether her daughter was ready for marriage.

Omotola said the relationship later improved and the two became much closer. She admitted that she still has regrets about the care and support she never got to show her mother, and said she now finds herself giving that care to other people's mothers. She added that the experience shaped her decision to make her directorial debut.

'Troublesome Eight'

Turning to her career, the actress said she was blacklisted after pushing for better standards and professionalism in Nollywood. She said she was one of a group of actors labelled the 'Troublesome Eight' because they demanded improvements. She also said she had a manager as far back as the 1990s, when professional management was not widely understood in the industry.

Motherhood

The mother of four also spoke about her approach to parenting and the lessons motherhood has taught her.

Rita Edochie says husband Tony is struggling after brother Pete's death

Actress Rita Edochie has reportedly said her husband, Tony Edochie, is finding the death of his elder brother Pete Edochie hard to bear. She said the family had been looking forward to Pete's 80th birthday.

Nollywood actress Rita Edochie has reportedly opened up about the toll that veteran actor Pete Edochie's death has taken on her family, especially her husband, Tony Edochie.

What she said

In an Instagram post, Rita thanked fans and well-wishers for their support, but said the period had been very difficult behind closed doors. She said Tony, Pete's younger brother, who was very close to him, is not finding it easy, but has chosen to accept the loss as the will of God.

She also revealed that the family had been looking forward to celebrating Pete's 80th birthday, a milestone they had hoped to mark in style.

Background

Pete Edochie, one of Nigeria's best-known actors and broadcasters, died on 25 November 2026 at the age of 79 in Enugu. Tributes have poured in from across Nollywood, including from his son Yul Edochie and actress Patience Ozokwo.
