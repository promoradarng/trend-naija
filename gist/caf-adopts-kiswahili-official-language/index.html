CAF adopts Kiswahili as its fifth official language

23 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Confederation of African Football (CAF) has adopted Kiswahili as its fifth official language, alongside English, French, Arabic and Portuguese.

The decision

The change was approved at CAF's 48th Ordinary General Assembly, held in Abuja on Saturday, 21 November 2026. CAF described it as a historic decision that reflects its commitment to Africa's linguistic and cultural diversity, and said it would strengthen the place of African languages in football administration.

Who pushed for it

The proposal was championed by the Tanzania Football Federation, with backing from the football bodies of Kenya and Uganda. CAF President Patrice Motsepe congratulated the three East African countries.

About Kiswahili

Kiswahili is spoken by more than 200 million people, mainly in Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda, and is also widely used in other parts of East, Central and Southern Africa.

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has avoided an immediate prison term after a court handed him a 12-month sentence, suspended for 18 months, for dangerous driving.

The sentence

The sentence was handed down on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, at Basingstoke Magistrates' Court. The judge said the 31-year-old had shown a conscious disregard for the safety of other road users.

What happened

CAS restores Senegal as AFCON 2025 champions, overturning CAF ruling

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Court of Arbitration for Sport has confirmed Senegal as winners of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations. It overturned in full the CAF Appeal Board decision that had handed the title to Morocco.

Read more

Senegal are once again Africa's champions after the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) overturned the decision that had stripped them of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations title.

The verdict

CAS delivered its ruling on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, reversing in full the decision of CAF's Appeal Board, which had awarded the title to hosts Morocco. With the verdict, Senegal are officially recognised as the 2025 AFCON champions.

How it started

Senegal beat Morocco 1-0 after extra time in the final at the Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium in Rabat on 18 January 2026, with Pape Gueye scoring the only goal. During the match, Senegal's players walked off the pitch for about 15 minutes in protest at a late penalty awarded to Morocco, which was later saved.

The Royal Moroccan Football Federation then took legal action, and on 17 March CAF's Appeal Board ruled that Senegal had forfeited the match, awarding Morocco a 3-0 win and the trophy. The Senegalese Football Federation refused to accept the decision and appealed to CAS.

What it means

The ruling ends months of legal uncertainty over the continent's top football title. Before the verdict, CAF President Patrice Motsepe had said the body would accept whatever CAS decided.

Morocco to fight on

The Royal Moroccan Football Federation said it rejected the ruling and would challenge it through every legal channel available, citing what it described as procedural failures. Senegal star Sadio Mane welcomed the decision.

CAF responds

In a short statement, CAF acknowledged the ruling and pledged to act on it. The trophy, medals and prize money were never taken back from Senegal during the dispute and will remain with the team.

25 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly asked President Tinubu to explain a ₦1.44 trillion fall in oil revenue in the first half of 2026, citing Budget Office figures. His campaign says collections were far below target.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly challenged President Bola Tinubu to publicly explain a ₦1.44 trillion drop in Nigeria's gross oil revenue in the first half of 2026.

The figures

In a statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, Atiku's campaign cited figures from the government's own Budget Office. It said the government collected ₦7.88 trillion in oil revenue between January and June 2026, well below its half-year target of ₦18.65 trillion, leaving a shortfall of about ₦10.78 trillion.

The campaign argued that the drop came even as international crude prices rose in the second quarter of the year.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC campaign was included in the reports.

Background

Atiku, who turned 80 on Wednesday, is the ADC's candidate in the January 2027 election. President Tinubu sent him a birthday message despite their rivalry.
