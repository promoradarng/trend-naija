"Her own papa?!": Rudeboy reacts in disbelief as young lady blames mother for marrying a broke man

5 Nov 2026, 18:08 GMT

Nigerian singer Paul Okoye, popularly known as Rudeboy, has expressed shock over a viral video of a young lady who reportedly blamed her mother for marrying a man who was not financially successful.

Rudeboy's reaction

Reacting to the video on his Instagram story, Rudeboy could hardly believe what he was hearing. "Imagine a daughter blaming her own mother for marrying down, as in marrying a broke man! As in her father! Her own papa!" he wrote.

It is easy to see why the statement caught his attention. Imagine growing up, looking at your parents, and deciding your mother made a terrible mistake simply because your father was not rich.

Social media reacts

The young lady's remarks have stirred plenty of reactions, with many Nigerians asking how someone could speak so openly about her own father's financial situation.

Some believe her comments reflect a growing obsession with money and luxury among young people. Others argue that children who grow up in financial hardship may develop resentment towards their parents, especially when they see other families living more comfortably.

Some social media users have even joked that they will save the video and wait to see the kind of wealthy man she eventually marries. Since she believes her mother chose poorly, they say, the public will be interested to see whether she makes a better choice when her own time comes.

One thing about social media is that people rarely forget statements like this. Years from now, someone may dig up the same video and remind her of what she said about her parents.

The other side

Nobody chooses the circumstances they are born into, and wanting a better life is perfectly understandable. But does that justify publicly blaming a parent for not marrying someone wealthy?

Marriage itself is unpredictable. A man can be comfortable today and lose everything tomorrow, while another can start with almost nothing and become successful years later.

That is why Rudeboy's reaction has become part of a much bigger conversation about money, marriage, parental sacrifice and the expectations children place on their parents.

"Grow up, the office is too big for jokes": Sule Lamido tells Shettima after VP claims he backs Tinubu

19 Nov 2026, 17:21 GMT

Former Jigawa Governor Sule Lamido has told Vice-President Kashim Shettima to be more careful with his public comments, after Shettima claimed Lamido supports President Tinubu and the APC. Lamido firmly distanced himself from the ruling party.

Read more

Former Jigawa State Governor Sule Lamido has told Vice-President Kashim Shettima to be more careful with his public comments, saying the office of the Vice-President is "too big for jokes".

How it started

Lamido was reacting to a claim by Shettima that he now supports President Bola Tinubu and the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Lamido rejected the claim outright and distanced himself from the ruling party.

"Ikon Allah! I didn't join the APC in 2014, only to join now in 2026?" he said, using a Hausa expression of astonishment. "Even though it was a Kanuri man who said it, I'm not surprised. But in your local dialect, the answer is 'Kattuwunum!'"

"Grow up"

Lamido described Shettima as intelligent and eloquent, but said the office of the Vice-President demands seriousness and restraint.

He also criticised some past remarks attributed to Shettima about former Vice-President Yemi Osinbajo, former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar and former Senate President Ahmad Lawan.

Political background

Lamido, a veteran politician and two-term governor of Jigawa State, has long been a member of the opposition. His sharp response makes clear that, whatever claims are made, he is not switching sides ahead of 2027.

Adamu Garba denies endorsing Peter Obi for 2027, says he has quit partisan politics

Former APC presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has dismissed reports that he endorsed Peter Obi for the 2027 presidential election, saying he has withdrawn from partisan politics to focus on his businesses and Africa's digital economy.

Read more

Former All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has denied reports that he endorsed Peter Obi ahead of Nigeria's 2027 presidential election.

"The claim is false"

In a public statement dated 18 November 2026, Garba described the claim circulating online as false.

He said he has withdrawn from partisan politics and is now focusing on his business ventures and on Africa's digital economy.

His new focus

Garba named Crowwe, Tablars and Justitia SI among the projects currently receiving his attention.

He also urged the public to rely only on his official channels for accurate information about his activities.

Where did the story come from?

The origin of the endorsement claim remains unclear. No post, interview or public statement showing Garba endorsing Obi has been found.

What is clear is that Garba has publicly disputed the claim.

A familiar pattern

With the 2027 elections approaching, false claims about endorsements are spreading quickly on social media. It is the second time this week that a story about backing for Obi has been corrected, after Obi himself clarified that his Washington visit was not an endorsement from the US Congress.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.
