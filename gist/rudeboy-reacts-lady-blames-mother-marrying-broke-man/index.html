"Her own papa?!": Rudeboy reacts in disbelief as young lady blames mother for marrying a broke man

5 Nov 2026, 18:08 GMT

Nigerian singer Paul Okoye, popularly known as Rudeboy, has expressed shock over a viral video of a young lady who reportedly blamed her mother for marrying a man who was not financially successful.

Rudeboy's reaction

Reacting to the video on his Instagram story, Rudeboy could hardly believe what he was hearing. "Imagine a daughter blaming her own mother for marrying down, as in marrying a broke man! As in her father! Her own papa!" he wrote.

It is easy to see why the statement caught his attention. Imagine growing up, looking at your parents, and deciding your mother made a terrible mistake simply because your father was not rich.

Social media reacts

The young lady's remarks have stirred plenty of reactions, with many Nigerians asking how someone could speak so openly about her own father's financial situation.

Some believe her comments reflect a growing obsession with money and luxury among young people. Others argue that children who grow up in financial hardship may develop resentment towards their parents, especially when they see other families living more comfortably.

Some social media users have even joked that they will save the video and wait to see the kind of wealthy man she eventually marries. Since she believes her mother chose poorly, they say, the public will be interested to see whether she makes a better choice when her own time comes.

One thing about social media is that people rarely forget statements like this. Years from now, someone may dig up the same video and remind her of what she said about her parents.

The other side

Nobody chooses the circumstances they are born into, and wanting a better life is perfectly understandable. But does that justify publicly blaming a parent for not marrying someone wealthy?

Marriage itself is unpredictable. A man can be comfortable today and lose everything tomorrow, while another can start with almost nothing and become successful years later.

That is why Rudeboy's reaction has become part of a much bigger conversation about money, marriage, parental sacrifice and the expectations children place on their parents.

Over to you

Is the young lady wrong for blaming her mother, or is she simply expressing the frustration of growing up in a financially struggling home?

Nigerian singer Temilade Openiyi, known as Tems, has opened up about living with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and how it affects her relationships with family and friends.

What she said

In a radio interview in Lagos, the singer said keeping in touch with people is something she finds difficult, even though she has many supportive people around her.

She explained that ADHD sometimes makes her switch topics suddenly during conversations, and that she often forgets to reach out to people. She admitted she has not always been consistent in communicating with people who matter to her, but said she is trying.

Background

Tems leads the Nigerian nominees for the 2027 Grammy Awards with four nominations.

The photo

The picture showed Davido and Seyi Tinubu raising their hands in a gesture VDM interpreted as the APC's "4+4" slogan, which supporters use to call for a second term for President Tinubu in 2027.

What VDM said

VDM said the photo was significant given Davido's status and his previous statements that he would never support the All Progressives Congress (APC). He accused the singer of contradicting himself and questioned why he would pose in a picture that could be read as a political statement.

Davido had not responded to VDM's comments at the time of the reports.

Enugu businessman Festus Oshaba-Onuh reportedly dies in road crash

19 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Billionaire businessman Chief Festus Oshaba-Onuh, CEO of Ferotex Group, has reportedly died in a crash on the Enugu-Port Harcourt Expressway. The business community in Enugu is in mourning.

Read more

The business community in Enugu State has been thrown into mourning following reports that billionaire businessman Chief Festus Oshaba-Onuh has died in a road accident.

What happened

Oshaba-Onuh, the chief executive of Ferotex Group and popularly known as Ferotex, reportedly died on the morning of Thursday, 19 November 2026, in a crash on the Enugu-Port Harcourt Expressway. He was said to be travelling back to Enugu at the time.

How the news spread

News of his death emerged after an Enugu-based social media influencer shared a video of the crash scene, showing a damaged SUV said to have been involved. The influencer described the death as a major loss to Enugu State and beyond.

No family statement yet

His family had not issued a public statement at the time of the reports.
