Delta man presumed dead after accident reportedly wakes up in mortuary

15 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

A man who was presumed dead after an accident in Delta State has reportedly woken up in a mortuary.

What happened

According to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026, the man was involved in a motorcycle accident near Evwreni and was taken to a mortuary after being presumed dead. He is said to have later woken up there. A video shared online shows residents celebrating his survival. Voices in the video say that when he woke up, the first thing he asked for was a cigarette and a lighter.

Celebrations

Residents rubbed white powder on their faces and bodies, a traditional sign of celebration often used at a child's birth, and also applied it to the survivor. Others shared snacks with passers-by to mark the occasion, while some urged him to go for a proper medical check-up.

Background

It is not clear who declared the man dead or whether he was examined by a doctor before being taken to the mortuary. The authorities had not commented on the incident at the time of writing.

NAFDAC shuts Abuja's Zuba International Market over fake products worth ₦300m

27 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

NAFDAC has shut Zuba International Market in Abuja after a joint operation with police and the DSS reportedly uncovered counterfeit and unwholesome goods worth more than ₦300 million. Over 160 shops were inspected.

Read more

The National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) has shut down Zuba International Market in the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja.

The operation

The closure followed a joint enforcement operation with the Nigeria Police Force and the Department of State Services, which reportedly uncovered counterfeit and unwholesome products worth more than ₦300 million. The exercise covered more than 160 shops in the market.

Why now

NAFDAC said the closure was part of a wider crackdown on substandard and fake goods ahead of the festive season.

Evans, 41, announced the split in a statement on his Instagram Stories on Friday, 27 November 2026. He said that after seven years of love and commitment it was an incredibly difficult time, that the decision was made by Scherzinger, 48, and that he respected it and wished her happiness.
