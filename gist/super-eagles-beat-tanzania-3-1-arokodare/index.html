Arokodare scores twice as Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 in AFCON qualifier

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Nigeria's Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 at the Godswill Akpabio Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November 2026, in a crucial 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier.

How the match went

Alhassan Yusuf gave Nigeria a dream start with a goal in the second minute, but Tanzania equalised through Alphonce Masanga. Ademola Lookman was forced off injured late in the first half and replaced by Moses Simon. Coach Eric Chelle then brought on Tolu Arokodare and Moses Usor in the second half, and the change paid off immediately: Arokodare scored in the 52nd minute, and the two substitutes combined for Arokodare's second goal and Nigeria's third in the 60th minute.

Table

The win moved Nigeria to second in Group L, one point behind Guinea-Bissau, who were held 1-1 by Madagascar. Nigeria play Tanzania again in their next match, and a win there would take them to nine points, putting them in a strong position to qualify with two games left.

Arokodare delivers

Arokodare, who has been in strong form on loan at Ajax in the Netherlands, had promised to carry his club form into the national team in the absence of the injured Victor Osimhen. Chelle later sent him a message after his brace.

Lookman a doubt

After the match, Chelle said Ademola Lookman was a doubt for the return fixture against Tanzania. He said the Atletico Madrid forward had felt discomfort in his knee before a knock to the same area made it worse, and the coaching staff are not expected to rush him back. Tanzania are one of the co-hosts of AFCON 2027, along with Kenya and Uganda.

A scan later confirmed a knee ligament sprain, and the Super Eagles' medical team ruled Lookman out of the second leg in Zanzibar, where Nigeria will defend a two-goal advantage. Victor Osimhen is also missing the November games.

Lookman is now expected to be out for about three weeks, while Victor Osimhen is reportedly on course to return for Galatasaray against Aston Villa on 24 November after knee surgery.

Crystal Palace appoint Thomas Frank after sacking Pierre Sage

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Crystal Palace have named former Brentford and Tottenham boss Thomas Frank as their new manager on a three-year deal. He replaces Pierre Sage, who was sacked after four months with the club bottom of the Premier League.

Crystal Palace have appointed Thomas Frank as their new manager, following the sacking of Pierre Sage.

The change

Sage joined the club in the summer but was dismissed after only four months, with Palace bottom of the Premier League after 10 matches. Frank, who previously managed Brentford and Tottenham, has signed a three-year contract.

What they said

Palace chairman Steve Parish welcomed the Dane, saying he had a proven record of building winning teams and improving players, and that his Premier League experience would help the club in the rest of the season.

Frank said it was an honour to take charge of what he called an iconic club, and that he was looking forward to meeting the players and staff.

What happens next

His first game in charge is this weekend, when Palace travel to Coventry hoping to climb off the bottom of the table.

Fulham reportedly want £50m for Calvin Bassey as Manchester United show interest

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Fulham are reportedly asking for about £50 million for Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey amid interest from Manchester United. A sale at that price would be a club record for Fulham.

Read more

Fulham are reportedly ready to demand around £50 million for Nigerian international defender Calvin Bassey in the January transfer window, as Manchester United weigh a move.

The interest

Manchester United are said to be genuinely interested in the 26-year-old, who plays mainly at centre-back but can also play at left-back, as they look to strengthen their defence for the second half of the season.

United reportedly believe a bid above £40 million would be needed to start talks, but Fulham are said to be holding out for closer to £50 million. That would beat the club's record sale, Aleksandar Mitrović's move to Al-Hilal in 2023.

Contract situation

Bassey's contract runs until summer 2027, with Fulham holding an option to extend it by a year. Talks on a new long-term deal have reportedly moved slowly, which could leave the club open to a big offer if nothing is agreed before January.

Background

Bassey, a former Ajax and Rangers defender, is a regular for the Super Eagles. He is reportedly open to a move to Old Trafford.

Abia government dissolves Enyimba management and technical crew

17 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Abia State Government has dissolved the management and technical crew of Enyimba FC with immediate effect, citing poor results and administrative problems. The sports ministry will lead a restructuring of the club.

Read more

The Abia State Government has dissolved the management and technical crew of Enyimba International FC with immediate effect, citing the club's poor performances and administrative problems.

What the government said

In a statement on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the state Commissioner for Information, Okey Kanu, said the decision was taken to address the club's recent disappointing results and structural challenges identified after a review of its affairs.

He said the government intends to fully restructure the club, restore its competitive identity and position it for lasting success in Nigerian and African football.

What happens next

The state Ministry of Sports and Youth Development has been asked to lead the restructuring, assess the club's management set-up and carry out administrative, technical and operational reforms aimed at restoring stability and accountability.

Background

Enyimba, based in Aba, are one of Nigeria's most successful clubs and twice won the CAF Champions League. They have struggled for form this season.

Sanwo-Olu gives Flamingos ₦10m each and iPhones after U-17 World Cup win

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has reportedly rewarded the Flamingos with ₦10 million each, iPhone 18 Pro Max phones and other gifts. It follows earlier rewards from President Tinubu and Senate President Akpabio.

Read more

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has reportedly rewarded the Flamingos, Nigeria's under-17 women's team, with ₦10 million each and other gifts after their historic win at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The reward

The governor announced the package at a dinner held in honour of the players and technical crew at Lagos House, Marina, on Sunday. It is said to include a total cash reward of ₦300 million, iPhone 18 Pro Max phones, headphones and customised outfits.

Other rewards

The gesture follows President Bola Tinubu's announcement of ₦100 million for each player and for head coach Akeem Busari, with other members of the technical crew promised ₦50 million each. The presidential reward was announced by First Lady Oluremi Tinubu when she hosted the team in Abuja. Senate President Godswill Akpabio also announced a ₦100 million donation to the team after their return.

The historic win

The Flamingos made history on 7 November by beating Japan 2-1 in Rabat, becoming the first African team to win the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.
