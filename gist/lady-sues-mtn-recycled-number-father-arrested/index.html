Woman sues MTN after father is arrested twice over recycled phone number

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

A Nigerian woman has reportedly sued telecommunications company MTN after her father was arrested twice over a phone number he received.

What happened

According to reports on Thursday, 5 November 2026, MTN reassigned a recycled phone number to her father. Unknown to him, the number had allegedly been used by a previous owner for criminal activity. As a result, he was reportedly arrested on two separate occasions over matters he had no knowledge of. His daughter has now taken legal action, holding MTN responsible for the distress the family suffered. MTN has not publicly commented on the case.

Why it matters

Telecom operators in Nigeria, as in many countries, recycle numbers that have been inactive for a long period and reassign them to new customers. The case has raised questions about whether operators should check that numbers are not linked to criminal investigations before giving them out, and about how security agencies verify that the current holder of a number is the person they are looking for.

Background

Nigeria requires all SIM cards to be linked to the National Identification Number, a policy meant to make it easier to trace criminals. However, problems with outdated records and recycled numbers have led to complaints from people who receive calls, messages or even police visits meant for previous owners.

How people reacted

Many social media users shared similar experiences with recycled numbers and said they hoped the case would force telecom companies to change their practices.

FIFA appoints normalisation committee to run NFF, names Amokachi and Enakhena

19 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

FIFA has named lawyer Sampson Ebomhe to chair a normalisation committee for the Nigeria Football Federation ahead of its 2027 elections. Daniel Amokachi, Godwin Enakhena, Amina Yerima and Joe Abah are also members.

Read more

FIFA has set up a normalisation committee to run the Nigeria Football Federation (NFF), appointing lawyer Sampson Ebomhe as chairman with immediate effect.

The members

The world governing body named four other members: sports journalist and administrator Godwin Enakhena, former Super Eagles captain Daniel Amokachi, Amina Yerima and Dr Joe Abah, former Director-General of the Bureau of Public Service Reforms.

FIFA's Chief Member Associations Officer, Elkhan Mammadov, confirmed the appointments in a letter, noting that all members are still subject to an eligibility check by the FIFA Review Committee before they are formally confirmed.

Background

Amokachi, nicknamed The Bull, played at two World Cups for Nigeria and is one of the country's best-known football figures.

Nigeria's Super Eagles have moved up one place to 29th in the latest FIFA world rankings, ending their 2026 international calendar with a small boost.

The ranking

The Super Eagles climbed from 30th to 29th in the world and moved back into Africa's top five, after slipping out of that group earlier in the year.

The results behind it

Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 in Uyo before playing out a goalless draw in the return leg in Zanzibar. The draw left them on seven points, three behind group leaders Guinea-Bissau, making their path to AFCON 2027 more complicated.
