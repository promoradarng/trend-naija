Woman sues MTN after father is arrested twice over recycled phone number

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

A Nigerian woman has reportedly sued telecommunications company MTN after her father was arrested twice over a phone number he received.

What happened

According to reports on Thursday, 5 November 2026, MTN reassigned a recycled phone number to her father. Unknown to him, the number had allegedly been used by a previous owner for criminal activity. As a result, he was reportedly arrested on two separate occasions over matters he had no knowledge of. His daughter has now taken legal action, holding MTN responsible for the distress the family suffered. MTN has not publicly commented on the case.

Why it matters

Telecom operators in Nigeria, as in many countries, recycle numbers that have been inactive for a long period and reassign them to new customers. The case has raised questions about whether operators should check that numbers are not linked to criminal investigations before giving them out, and about how security agencies verify that the current holder of a number is the person they are looking for.

Background

Nigeria requires all SIM cards to be linked to the National Identification Number, a policy meant to make it easier to trace criminals. However, problems with outdated records and recycled numbers have led to complaints from people who receive calls, messages or even police visits meant for previous owners.

How people reacted

Many social media users shared similar experiences with recycled numbers and said they hoped the case would force telecom companies to change their practices.

NDC boycotts IGP's security meeting, says its members are not safe

24 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

The Nigeria Democratic Congress has reportedly refused to attend a security meeting called by the Inspector-General of Police, accusing security agencies of failing to protect its members from attacks. It blamed thugs it said were loyal to the APC.

Read more

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has announced that it will boycott a security meeting convened by the Inspector-General of Police, citing attacks on its members ahead of the 2027 elections.

The party's reasons

In a statement on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, signed by its National Publicity Secretary, the party said it no longer felt safe under the Nigeria Police Force and would not lend legitimacy to a security process that had failed to protect its members.

The NDC accused security agencies of failing to prevent repeated attacks it alleged were carried out by thugs loyal to the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC). It pointed to incidents in Benue, Edo, Cross River, Enugu and Delta states, including what it described as an attempt to stop its presidential candidate, Peter Obi, from entering Benue State.

The Delta attack

The boycott follows Monday's deadly attack on the campaign team of the party's Delta Central senatorial candidate, Ovie Omo-Agege, in Ughelli North. The party alleged that security personnel were complicit. Police have since invited Omo-Agege and others for questioning as part of their investigation.

The other side

No response from the APC or the police to the NDC's allegations was included in the reports.

Court voids APC's Bayelsa West senatorial candidacy

24 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

A Federal High Court in Abuja has reportedly voided Fidelis Agbiki's candidacy as the APC's Bayelsa West senatorial candidate. The court upheld claims that his name was missing from the party's membership register.

Read more

A Federal High Court in Abuja has reportedly nullified the candidacy of High Chief Fidelis Agbiki, the All Progressives Congress (APC) senatorial candidate for Bayelsa West in the 2027 elections.

The case

The petitioners raised two main objections to his candidacy. First, they argued that his name did not appear in the APC membership register submitted to INEC, meaning he was not a recognised member of the party. Second, they claimed he did not resign from a political appointment before taking part in the senatorial primary.

The ruling

Justice Mohammed Garba Umar upheld the petition, finding that the primary breached the Electoral Act.

Background

The judgment came on the same day a Federal High Court in Warri voided the NDC candidacy of former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege for Delta Central on similar grounds relating to membership registers. Pre-election court cases are common in Nigeria, and both rulings can be appealed.

Lagos offers 20% discount on food in 66 markets for the festive season

24 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Lagos State Government has relaunched its Ounje Eko food discount scheme, offering 20% off selected items in 66 markets. The markets will open on three Sundays: 29 November, 6 December and 20 December.

Read more

The Lagos State Government has relaunched its Ounje Eko food discount programme for the festive season, offering residents a 20% reduction on selected food items in 66 markets across the state.

When and where

The Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Systems, Abisola Olusanya, announced on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, that the scheme will run on three Sundays: 29 November, 6 December and 20 December. The markets will open from 9am to 4pm and cover all 57 local government and local council development areas, across the five IBILE divisions of Ikeja, Badagry, Ikorodu, Lagos Island and Epe.

Why it matters

The commissioner said the initiative was designed to ease pressure on household budgets as residents prepare for Christmas and the New Year. It comes amid concerns that many families have yet to feel real relief from high food prices, even though food inflation has slowed.
