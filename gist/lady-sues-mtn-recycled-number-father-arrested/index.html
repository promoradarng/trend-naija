Woman sues MTN after father is arrested twice over recycled phone number

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

A Nigerian woman has reportedly sued telecommunications company MTN after her father was arrested twice over a phone number he received.

What happened

According to reports on Thursday, 5 November 2026, MTN reassigned a recycled phone number to her father. Unknown to him, the number had allegedly been used by a previous owner for criminal activity. As a result, he was reportedly arrested on two separate occasions over matters he had no knowledge of. His daughter has now taken legal action, holding MTN responsible for the distress the family suffered. MTN has not publicly commented on the case.

Why it matters

Telecom operators in Nigeria, as in many countries, recycle numbers that have been inactive for a long period and reassign them to new customers. The case has raised questions about whether operators should check that numbers are not linked to criminal investigations before giving them out, and about how security agencies verify that the current holder of a number is the person they are looking for.

Background

Nigeria requires all SIM cards to be linked to the National Identification Number, a policy meant to make it easier to trace criminals. However, problems with outdated records and recycled numbers have led to complaints from people who receive calls, messages or even police visits meant for previous owners.

How people reacted

Many social media users shared similar experiences with recycled numbers and said they hoped the case would force telecom companies to change their practices.

Cooking gas prices rise; refilling 12.5kg now costs up to ₦22,500

19 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Cooking gas has reportedly risen from about ₦1,400 per kg to between ₦1,500 and ₦1,800 depending on location. Some households are cutting back or switching to other fuels ahead of Christmas.

Read more

Nigerian households are facing fresh pressure as cooking gas prices rise across major cities ahead of the Christmas season.

The new prices

The price of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) has reportedly risen from about ₦1,400 per kilogram to between ₦1,500 and ₦1,800, depending on location. That means refilling a 12.5kg cylinder can now cost as much as ₦22,500, up from about ₦17,500.

How households are coping

Market findings suggest some Nigerians are now buying smaller quantities, cutting down on how much gas they use or switching to other cooking fuels.

FG to release stored rice and maize to bring down food prices

Agriculture Minister Abubakar Kyari says the government will release rice, maize and other grains from its stocks into the market to cut food prices. It also plans to distribute three million bags of fertiliser within a month.

Read more

The federal government has announced plans to release stored rice, maize and other grains into the market in a bid to bring down food prices.

What was announced

Minister of Agriculture and Food Security Abubakar Kyari disclosed the plan on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, at the presentation of the 2026 Wet Season Agricultural Performance Survey Report in Abuja.

He said grains bought under the Bank of Agriculture's Guaranteed Minimum Price programme would soon be released to the market.

Fertiliser for farmers

The government also plans to distribute three million bags of fertiliser to farmers within the next month to boost production and improve food supply across the country.

Background

The Guaranteed Minimum Price scheme gives farmers an assured buyer for their produce.

Social Democratic Party (SDP) presidential candidate Prince Adewole Adebayo has reportedly promised to end the Boko Haram insurgency within his first three months in office if he wins the 2027 election.

The rally

Adebayo made the pledge on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, at the SDP presidential campaign rally in Yola, Adamawa State, according to a statement from the party's campaign spokesman.

He blamed poor governance for the insecurity in the North-East, recalling the military campaigns to retake towns such as Mubi, Madagali, Michika, Gwoza and Bama, and argued that no government had properly dealt with the humanitarian fallout.

Other promises

His plans reportedly include bringing petrol down to ₦200 per litre, expanding the use of CNG, affordable food, free education and better healthcare.

The race

Adebayo is one of several candidates in the 16 January 2027 presidential election, alongside President Bola Tinubu (APC), Atiku Abubakar (ADC), Peter Obi (NDC) and others.
