Eedris Abdulkareem releases new song asking Seyi Tinubu to plead with his father

3 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Veteran rapper Eedris Abdulkareem has released a new song with a pointed political message for President Bola Tinubu, this time delivered through the President's son, Seyi Tinubu.

The song

In a music video posted on Instagram on Monday, 2 November 2026, Eedris reportedly addresses Seyi directly, asking him to plead with his father on behalf of Nigerians. The lyrics contrast the President's role as a good father to his own family with what the rapper describes as hardship for ordinary Nigerians. According to reports, the song also takes aim at the National Assembly over the newly passed tax law and criticises the electoral system.

Background

Eedris, best known for his 2004 hit 'Jaga Jaga', has used music to criticise Nigerian governments for more than two decades. In 2025 he released a song critical of the Tinubu government, which the broadcasting regulator restricted on air. On Monday he also denied claims that he had joined the APC, accusing Isaac Fayose of hypocrisy for saying so. Seyi Tinubu has become a visible figure in his father's re-election campaign, and the Presidency has defended the government's reforms, saying they are needed to fix the economy.

How people reacted

The video drew strong reactions online. Many fans praised the rapper for speaking up about the cost of living, while supporters of the President accused him of playing politics ahead of the 2027 elections.

What happens next

Neither Seyi Tinubu nor the Presidency had responded publicly at the time of reporting.

  • Eedris addresses Seyi Tinubu directly in the lyrics.
  • The song also criticises the new tax law.
  • The Presidency has not responded.

Afrobeats star Davido has marked his 34th birthday with a series of surprises, according to reports.

On the jet

Before landing, the singer was reportedly given an early birthday treat by his wife, Chioma, and members of his 30BG crew, who surprised him on his private jet with a cake and a chorus of Happy Birthday.

The surprise party

Neither Davido nor Chioma is said to have known that a bigger celebration awaited them. On arrival, the couple walked into a surprise party arranged by family and close friends. Guests included socialite Cubana Chiefpriest and Davido's cousin Nikos Living, along with other well-known figures.

What Chioma said

Speaking to the press with Davido beside her after the event, Chioma said she thoroughly enjoyed the celebration. She had earlier shared rare pregnancy photos to celebrate her husband's birthday.

Power couple list

Davido and Chioma were also named on Rolling Stone Africa's first Power Couple 50 list, published on 20 November, which ranks African and diaspora couples on global influence, social impact, creativity and business.

Frank Edwards says he turned down a huge deal that asked him to remove Jesus from his songs

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Gospel singer Frank Edwards has reportedly said he rejected an offer that could have made him rich enough to own three private jets. The promoters wanted him to replace the name Jesus with God in his songs.

Read more

Gospel singer Frank Edwards has reportedly opened up about a deal he rejected early in his career, which he believes could have made him wealthy enough to own three private jets.

The offer

Speaking during a concert ministration, in a clip that circulated online on 21 November 2026, Edwards is said to have explained that the offer came shortly after he travelled to South Africa to shoot the video for his song Beautiful.

Promoters reportedly offered to take his music to a global audience on one condition: that he remove the name of Jesus from his songs and replace it with God, arguing that this would make the music more widely acceptable.

His message

Edwards told his audience that the temptation came disguised as a genuine business opportunity, and warned that not every compromise looks obviously wrong at first. He said his mother's advice helped him turn the deal down.

Olamide honoured with 'Amuludun' title by Nike Davies-Okundaye

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Rapper Olamide has reportedly been given the Yoruba title Amuludun by artist Nike Davies-Okundaye during a visit to her Lagos gallery. The honour came days after he released his album Time and Space.

Read more

Rapper and YBNL boss Olamide has reportedly received a traditional Yoruba honour from the celebrated artist Chief Nike Davies-Okundaye.

The honour

During a visit to the Nike Art Gallery in Lekki, Lagos, Olamide was given the title Amuludun, which roughly means the one who brings joy to the town. The veteran artist is also said to have presented him with a custom-made beaded portrait.

A show of respect

A video of the visit shared online showed the rapper chatting warmly with her, and at one point prostrating before her in the traditional Yoruba gesture of respect, a moment that drew attention from fans.

Background

The visit came just days after Olamide released his latest album, Time and Space, on 16 November 2026. He remains one of the most influential figures in Nigerian hip-hop and Afrobeats, and his YBNL label is home to artists including Fireboy DML and Asake.
