Taylor Jasmine, the girlfriend of popular Nigerian comedian and content creator Carter Efe, has stirred reactions on social media after expressing her sadness over her planned return to the United States.

Taylor, who has been spending time in Nigeria, appears to have developed a strong attachment to the country, making the thought of leaving particularly difficult for her.

In emotional posts on X, she wrote that she was up crying at 7:40 am because she was going back to her home in the US the next day. "I don't wanna leave Nigeria I swear. I'll be back soon," she wrote, adding in another post: "I don't wanna go back home. The fact I'm leaving tomorrow. I'm crying. I had some fun out here, man."

Her words have attracted attention online, with many Nigerians reacting to her apparent love for the country and the experiences she has enjoyed during her stay.

For someone who has experienced life in both America and Nigeria, her reluctance to leave has also sparked conversations about the things that make Nigeria special despite its numerous economic and social challenges.

Nigeria may be facing difficulties, but its vibrant entertainment industry, rich culture, lively social atmosphere and welcoming communities continue to attract visitors from different parts of the world.

Her relationship with Carter Efe has also placed her in the spotlight, with social media users following their activities and interactions.

While she has expressed her reluctance to leave, it remains unclear whether she intends to extend her stay or proceed with her planned journey to America.

The development has once again highlighted how Nigeria continues to leave lasting impressions on visitors, particularly those who develop personal relationships and meaningful connections during their stay.
