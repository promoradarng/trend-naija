Akwa Ibom announces 13th-month pension bonus and ₦10,000 payment for retirees

31 Oct 2026, 20:42 GMT

Pensioners in Akwa Ibom State are set to receive an extra month's pension, Governor Umo Eno has announced.

What was announced

Speaking at the Ibom Hall Grounds in Uyo, in remarks reported on Saturday, 31 October 2026, the governor said all pensioners in the state would receive a 13th-month pension bonus. He also announced a separate ₦10,000 welfare payment, which will be paid together with November 2026 pensions. The exact amount and payment date of the 13th-month bonus were not stated in reports.

Clearing the backlog

The governor reportedly said his government had paid ₦100 billion of a ₦111 billion gratuity backlog it inherited and planned to clear the remainder before the end of its first term. He also urged pensioners aged 65 and above to register for the state's free health insurance scheme for older people.

Background

At the event, the Nigeria Union of Pensioners presented the governor with a merit award. Unpaid pensions and gratuities are a long-standing problem in many Nigerian states, and retirees have often protested over delays. Some states have recently announced extra payments to help workers and pensioners cope with rising living costs, while the Federal Government this week unveiled a ₦50,000 monthly cash relief scheme for vulnerable households.

How people reacted

Pensioners welcomed the announcement, while some residents called on other states to follow the example.

  • All pensioners in the state will get a 13th-month bonus.
  • A ₦10,000 welfare payment comes with November pensions.
  • ₦100bn of a ₦111bn gratuity backlog has reportedly been paid.

Three inmates died during Ilorin jailbreak attempt, correctional service says

26 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Nigerian Correctional Service says three inmates died during last week's attempted jailbreak at the Medium Security Custodial Centre in Ilorin. Two death-row inmates escaped and five others were stopped.

Read more

The Nigerian Correctional Service (NCoS) has said three inmates died during the recent jailbreak attempt at the Medium Security Custodial Centre in Ilorin, Kwara State.

What happened

Speaking during a visit to Ilorin on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the Controller-General of Corrections, Sylvester Nwakuche, said seven inmates tried to escape on 18 November. Officers stopped five of them, but two death-row inmates succeeded after reportedly breaking through and scaling the wall of the facility.

He said the service had been informed that three of the five who failed to escape had died, describing it as an unfortunate incident.

Investigation

Nwakuche said the Minister of Interior, Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo, had ordered an investigation, and a team had been set up to look into the incident thoroughly. He said anyone found to be involved would face the law. About 20 officers were earlier reported to be under investigation over the escape.

Travellers raise privacy fears over 'no NIN, no flight' rule

26 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Concerns are growing among air travellers over the government's plan to require NIN verification for domestic flights from 1 January 2027. Critics worry about data privacy and passengers without NINs.

Read more

Anxiety is growing among Nigerian air travellers over the federal government's plan to make National Identification Number (NIN) verification compulsory for domestic flights from 1 January 2027.

The policy

The rule will be enforced through the Verified Passenger Access System (VPASS), a biometric facial recognition platform. Aviation Minister Festus Keyamo has directed domestic airlines to connect to the system before the January deadline. The government says it will help stop ticket fraud and ensure passenger lists are accurate.

The concerns

The policy has raised worries about data privacy, freedom of movement and the possible exclusion of passengers who do not yet have a NIN.

The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) has commissioned a new dialysis centre at the University of Benin Teaching Hospital (UBTH) in Benin City, Edo State.

The centre

The facility, named the Enoch and Folu Adeboye Dialysis Centre, was unveiled on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, by the church's social responsibility arm, His Love Foundation. The church donated three dialysis machines to boost kidney care at the hospital.

Why it matters

The centre is expected to increase the hospital's capacity to provide haemodialysis to patients with kidney disease.

Background

Spectranet to shut down services in Ibadan and Port Harcourt

26 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

Internet provider Spectranet says it will end its broadband services in Ibadan and Port Harcourt after a six-month notice period, citing rising operating costs. It will focus on Lagos and Abuja.

Read more

Internet service provider Spectranet Limited has announced that it will discontinue its broadband services in Ibadan and Port Harcourt after a six-month notice period.

What it means for users

The company said it would keep its network running and continue to provide customer support in both cities until the notice period ends, giving subscribers time to arrange alternative connections.

Why

Spectranet said higher operating costs had made it increasingly difficult to maintain its service standards in the two cities. It plans to concentrate its resources in Lagos and Abuja, where it says it will work on improving network performance.
