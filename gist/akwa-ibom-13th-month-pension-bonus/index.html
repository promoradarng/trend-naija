Akwa Ibom announces 13th-month pension bonus and ₦10,000 payment for retirees

31 Oct 2026, 20:42 GMT

Pensioners in Akwa Ibom State are set to receive an extra month's pension, Governor Umo Eno has announced.

What was announced

Speaking at the Ibom Hall Grounds in Uyo, in remarks reported on Saturday, 31 October 2026, the governor said all pensioners in the state would receive a 13th-month pension bonus. He also announced a separate ₦10,000 welfare payment, which will be paid together with November 2026 pensions. The exact amount and payment date of the 13th-month bonus were not stated in reports.

Clearing the backlog

The governor reportedly said his government had paid ₦100 billion of a ₦111 billion gratuity backlog it inherited and planned to clear the remainder before the end of its first term. He also urged pensioners aged 65 and above to register for the state's free health insurance scheme for older people.

Background

At the event, the Nigeria Union of Pensioners presented the governor with a merit award. Unpaid pensions and gratuities are a long-standing problem in many Nigerian states, and retirees have often protested over delays. Some states have recently announced extra payments to help workers and pensioners cope with rising living costs, while the Federal Government this week unveiled a ₦50,000 monthly cash relief scheme for vulnerable households.

How people reacted

Pensioners welcomed the announcement, while some residents called on other states to follow the example.

  • A ₦10,000 welfare payment comes with November pensions.
  • ₦100bn of a ₦111bn gratuity backlog has reportedly been paid.

Kwara suspends children's hospital chief after mothers and sick children were locked out

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Kwara State Government has suspended the chief medical director of the Children's Specialist Hospital in Ilorin, after a viral video showed mothers and sick children allegedly locked out for arriving by 7am. The board says a shortage of doctors caused a backlog.

Read more

The Kwara State Government has reportedly suspended the Chief Medical Director of the Children's Specialist Hospital, Centre Igboro, Ilorin, Dr Mosunmola Folorunsho, after mothers and their sick children were allegedly locked out of the hospital.

What happened

A video that went viral showed mothers with sick children who said they had been shut out for arriving by 7am, reportedly because they had not arrived by 5:30am. Following the outrage, the Executive Secretary of the Kwara State Hospitals Management Board, Dr Abdulraheem Malik, and members of the management team made an emergency visit to the hospital.

The government's action

In a statement on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the board said the CMD had been suspended and given 48 hours to explain her role in the incident. She was directed to hand over to the most senior doctor at the hospital pending further directives.

The board's explanation

The board said its findings showed the hospital had a temporary shortage of doctors, after one of the doctors scheduled for the morning shift was away at a malaria training programme. This created a backlog of patients waiting to be seen.

TRCN gives all teachers until end of 2027 to get licensed

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria says every teacher must be certified and licensed by December 2027. Licences will have to be renewed every three years.

Read more

The Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria (TRCN) has given teachers across the country until December 2027 to obtain professional certification and practising licences.

What the council said

The council's Registrar, Dr Ronke Soyombo, announced the deadline while speaking to journalists in Abuja on Tuesday, 17 November 2026. She said the council will no longer tolerate people teaching without the required credentials and is strengthening registration, licensing and qualifying examinations.

How it works

Soyombo explained that professional certification is obtained once, while practising licences must be renewed every three years, with evidence that the teacher has taken part in continuous professional development programmes.

Education students are now required to sit the TRCN examination before completing their training, while serving teachers must also meet the requirements.

Help for non-education graduates

The council is introducing a faster route to professional status for teachers who do not hold education degrees, so they can get the training they need before the deadline.

Tinubu asks Senate to decriminalise attempted suicide

17 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

President Tinubu has sent a bill to the Senate that would remove criminal penalties for attempted suicide and replace them with medical and psychological support. The National Mental Health Amendment Bill was read during plenary.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has reportedly asked the Senate to decriminalise attempted suicide and put in place medical and psychological support for survivors instead of punishment.

The bill

The proposal is contained in the National Mental Health Amendment Bill, 2026, which Senate President Godswill Akpabio read during plenary on Tuesday, 17 November 2026. It seeks to amend the National Mental Health Act of 2021 by removing criminal liability for attempted suicide, which currently attracts prison terms under Nigeria's Criminal and Penal Codes, and replacing it with a public health approach.

In his letter to the Senate, the President urged lawmakers to give the bill quick consideration, saying it was designed to decriminalise attempted suicide and support anyone who tries to take their own life.

Why it matters

If passed, the law would treat attempted suicide as a health issue requiring care and treatment rather than a crime.

Getting help

Anyone who is struggling or thinking about suicide is encouraged to speak to someone they trust or contact a health professional or local support service.

Tinubu announces home loans of up to ₦100m at 9.75% interest over 20 years

17 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

President Tinubu says eligible Nigerians can get home loans of up to ₦100 million at a fixed 9.75% interest rate, repayable over 20 years. Buyers must pay at least 10% of the property's value upfront.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has announced that eligible Nigerians can now access home loans of up to ₦100 million at a fixed interest rate of 9.75%, repayable over 20 years.

How it works

In a post on his X account on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the President reportedly said the loans are available through the ₦1 trillion MOFI Real Estate Investment Fund (MREIF). Applicants must pay a deposit of at least 10% of the property's value to qualify.

Using a ₦10 million home as an example, he said the buyer would pay ₦1 million upfront and then about ₦85,400 a month over the 20 years.

Extra options

He added that workers in the Contributory Pension Scheme can use up to 25% of their retirement savings towards the deposit, while married couples can apply jointly to qualify for bigger loans.

What happens next

Full eligibility rules and the application process were not detailed in the reports.
