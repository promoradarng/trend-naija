Democrats projected to win both House and Senate in blow to Trump

4 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Democratic Party is projected to have won control of both chambers of the United States Congress in the 2026 midterm elections held on Tuesday, 3 November, according to projections by US television networks reported early on Wednesday.

The results

According to the projections, the Democrats have taken the House of Representatives and the Senate. The Senate results were described as particularly striking, with Democrats reportedly winning seats in states that President Donald Trump carried by wide margins in 2024, including Texas, South Carolina, Ohio, Kansas and Iowa. Texas had reportedly not elected a Democrat to statewide office since 1994. Final results in some races may take days to confirm.

Why voters turned

Analysts have pointed to Trump's low approval ratings, voter anger over tariffs and inflation, and the long-running conflict with Iran as reasons for the swing. On the eve of the vote, US forces carried out limited strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island, while Trump told supporters that Iran might eventually have to be given 'the hammer'.

Reactions

Trump sought to distance himself from the results, writing on social media that he was not on the ballot and that Republicans would have won in a landslide if he had been. He had held rallies in 20 states in the final weeks of the campaign. In a later interview, he said he was willing to accept the results and would look to work with the Democrats. Former President Barack Obama said voters had sent a clear message that they want politics that brings people together, and praised candidates who won in areas once seen as long shots. Among the night's firsts, Abdul El-Sayed became the first Muslim US senator, Keisha Lance Bottoms became the first Black woman elected governor of a US state, and Gina Hinojosa defeated Texas governor Greg Abbott.

Trump on the Iran war

Speaking to reporters at the White House on Wednesday, Trump said it was largely true that the war with Iran had contributed to the Republican losses, as Vice President JD Vance had suggested, pointing to the rise in petrol prices it caused. He said he was not considering removing Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth, and that he was open to working with Democrats as long as they were not destructive, adding that he still had the power to veto their bills.

What it means

With control of both chambers, Democrats will be able to block much of the President's legislative agenda, launch investigations with the power to compel testimony, and hold up his nominees, including judges, during the last two years of his term. Trump remains president until January 2029.

Why it matters for Nigeria

US policy on trade, aid and immigration affects Nigeria directly. A Democratic Congress could push back on measures such as visa restrictions and changes to trade programmes, including this week's decision to remove South Africa and Rwanda from the AGOA duty-free scheme. The White House had not formally responded to the projections at the time of reporting.

Zambia and Congo pull out of Miss Universe over US travel rules; Nigeria's queen arrives

Zambia and the Republic of the Congo have withdrawn from Miss Universe 2026 in Puerto Rico after their contestants could not get US travel permission. Nigeria's Danielle Adewusi has arrived and joined preliminary events.

Two African countries have withdrawn from the 75th Miss Universe pageant because their contestants could not get permission to travel to Puerto Rico, a US territory.

Who is affected

According to reports on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Zambia and the Republic of the Congo have pulled out, while representatives from Zimbabwe and South Sudan were still awaiting decisions ahead of the final on 24 November. Miss Universe Zambia said its representative, a 25-year-old registered nurse who won the national title in October, could not secure the required travel authorisation, and blamed US travel restrictions rather than any failing by her or the organisers. The Republic of the Congo had been due to send its first ever Miss Universe contestant.

Nigeria's contestant

Nigeria is also affected by partial US entry restrictions, but its representative, Danielle Adewusi, arrived in San Juan on 9 November and has joined the preliminary activities.

Background

Because Puerto Rico is a US territory, contestants travelling there must meet US immigration requirements.

Brian Tracy, the motivational speaker and bestselling author whose books shaped how millions of people approach work and personal goals, has died at the age of 82.

His death

Tracy died on 12 November 2026, according to reports. No official cause of death has been made public. His grandson shared an emotional tribute online.

His work

Tracy built his career in sales, marketing, real estate and consulting before founding Brian Tracy International. His company says he gave more than 5,000 talks and seminars to over five million people and wrote more than 70 books, translated into dozens of languages. When Congo turned on Lumumba, Egypt did not abandon his family: how Nasser sent a colonel to save his children

14 Nov 2026, 14:31 GMT

As Patrice Lumumba's government collapsed and his life came under threat, Egypt's President Gamal Abdel Nasser ordered a secret mission led by Colonel Saad El-Din El-Shazly to get Lumumba's children out of Congo. They grew up in Cairo under Egypt's protection.

Read more

It is one of the lesser-known chapters of Africa's liberation story, and a remarkable act of solidarity between two African nations.

A leader in danger

Patrice Lumumba, Congo's first Prime Minister after independence from Belgium in 1960, was overthrown within months and placed under house arrest. In January 1961 he was handed over to his enemies and assassinated, one of the most shocking political killings in African history.

As Congo descended into chaos, Lumumba's family was in grave danger, and his wife Pauline and their children had nowhere safe to turn.

Nasser's secret mission

Egypt did not look away. On the direct orders of President Gamal Abdel Nasser, Colonel Saad El-Din El-Shazly, who later became one of Egypt's most celebrated military commanders, took on the task of getting Lumumba's children safely out of the country.

El-Shazly knew Congo well. He had commanded the Egyptian battalion sent there in 1960 as part of the international force during the independence crisis, at a time when Egypt firmly backed Lumumba.

The operation was carried out in complete secrecy. Lumumba's children, including his eldest son François, were smuggled out of Congo and flown to Cairo, where they arrived safely while their father was still under arrest.

A new home in Cairo

After Lumumba's death, Pauline joined the children in Egypt. Nasser offered the family protection and support, and the Egyptian government helped with their housing, schooling and other needs.

Lumumba's children grew up in Egypt, which became not just a place of refuge but a home that embraced their father's cause as a symbol of African liberation.

Pauline later spent time in Europe before returning to Congo in the late 1960s, after Lumumba began to be honoured as a national hero. She settled in Kinshasa.

Africa looking out for its own

The story is a reminder that Egypt was not just a witness to Africa's independence struggles, but an active partner in them. When Lumumba's family was in danger, an African nation stepped in to protect them.
