IK Ogbonna and Ego Nwosu reveal viral proposal was a promo for a wedding-themed event

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Nollywood actors IK Ogbonna and Ego Nwosu have reportedly cleared the air on their viral proposal video, confirming that they are not actually getting married.

The reveal

In a new video posted on Monday, 16 November 2026, the pair appeared together and explained that the proposal was part of a campaign for an event called Love at Julirose, billed for 15 December in Port Harcourt, Rivers State.

The event is described as a full wedding-themed experience, with a live band, bridesmaids and groomsmen, photographers and live performances. Tickets are on sale with different price levels and a dress code.

The playful banter

The two kept up the jokes in the new video, with IK saying he was still the groom and Ego replying that she was still the bride, making it clear the campaign was designed to create buzz.

How fans reacted

Many fans who had congratulated the pair earlier this month reacted with amusement and mock anger, with some jokingly asking for their congratulations back.

Biola Adebayo puts new salon up for sale weeks after opening it

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Actress Biola Adebayo says she is selling her newly opened salon because her sister, who was meant to run it, has been granted a Canadian visa. She said she cannot manage the business herself.

Read more

Nollywood actress Biola Adebayo has announced that she is putting her newly opened salon up for sale, only weeks after unveiling it.

The reason

In a video on her social media page, Biola explained that she set up the salon to give her sister, who had been going through a difficult time, a business of her own to run.

However, just days after the salon was unveiled, her sister received approval for a Canadian visa she had been waiting for for more than three years, and will now be relocating.

Mixed feelings

The actress described the visa as good news, but admitted it left her with the challenge of managing the salon herself, which led to her decision to sell it.

Adamu Garba denies endorsing Peter Obi for 2027, says he has quit partisan politics

19 Nov 2026, 17:04 GMT

Former APC presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has dismissed reports that he endorsed Peter Obi for the 2027 presidential election, saying he has withdrawn from partisan politics to focus on his businesses and Africa's digital economy.

Read more

Former All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has denied reports that he endorsed Peter Obi ahead of Nigeria's 2027 presidential election.

"The claim is false"

In a public statement dated 18 November 2026, Garba described the claim circulating online as false.

He said he has withdrawn from partisan politics and is now focusing on his business ventures and on Africa's digital economy.

His new focus

Garba named Crowwe, Tablars and Justitia SI among the projects currently receiving his attention.

He also urged the public to rely only on his official channels for accurate information about his activities.

Where did the story come from?

The origin of the endorsement claim remains unclear. No post, interview or public statement showing Garba endorsing Obi has been found.

What is clear is that Garba has publicly disputed the claim.

A familiar pattern

With the 2027 elections approaching, false claims about endorsements are spreading quickly on social media. It is the second time this week that a story about backing for Obi has been corrected, after Obi himself clarified that his Washington visit was not an endorsement from the US Congress.
