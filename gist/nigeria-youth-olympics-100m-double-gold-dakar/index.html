Nigeria wins both 100m golds at Youth Olympics as Rosemary Nwankwo and Gift Bright triumph

10 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Nigeria has made history at the 2026 Youth Olympic Games in Dakar, Senegal, winning gold in both the girls' and boys' 100 metres.

The races

Rosemary Chigozie Nwankwo, a 17-year-old secondary school student, won the girls' 100m final in a wind-assisted 11.16 seconds, according to reports on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. Arwa Ali of the United Arab Emirates took silver in 11.41 seconds and Dana Salem of Qatar won bronze in 11.48. Nwankwo had reached the final by winning her semi-final in 11.53 seconds. Her compatriot Gift Bright then won the boys' 100m in a personal best of 10.28 seconds.

A first

The double makes Nigeria the first country to win both 100m titles at the same edition of the Youth Olympic Games. Nwankwo's win was also Nigeria's first gold medal of the Dakar Games, and she becomes the third Nigerian woman to win the event, after Josephine Omaka in Singapore in 2010 and Rosemary Chukwuma in Buenos Aires in 2018.

Background

The Dakar Games are the first Olympic event ever held in Africa. The sprint golds come days after Nigeria's Flamingos won the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco, adding to a remarkable run for Nigerian youth sport.

  • Rosemary Nwankwo won the girls' 100m in 11.16 seconds.
  • Gift Bright won the boys' 100m in 10.28 seconds.
  • Nigeria is the first country to win both at one Games.

Super Eagles head coach Eric Chelle reportedly clashed with a journalist at a press conference in Tanzania on Monday, 16 November 2026, ahead of Nigeria's second-leg AFCON 2027 qualifier.

What happened

The session is said to have become tense when a reporter questioned Chelle's formation and player selection. The coach reportedly responded firmly, drawing a line between his job and that of the media, and said his decisions are shaped by the players he has available and the opponents, not outside opinion. A video of the exchange was shared on X.

It is not the first time Chelle has had a heated exchange with Nigerian journalists over his choices.

The team will be without Ademola Lookman, who has been ruled out for several weeks with an injury, while Victor Osimhen is also absent injured, leaving the attack short of two key players.

Romelu Lukaku retires from international football with Belgium

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Belgium's all-time top scorer Romelu Lukaku has retired from international football at 33. He scored 94 goals in 135 appearances over 16 years.

Read more

Romelu Lukaku, Belgium's all-time record goalscorer, has announced his retirement from international football at the age of 33.

The announcement

The former Chelsea and Manchester United striker made the announcement on Monday, 16 November 2026, a day after he was an unused substitute in Belgium's 4-1 Nations League win over Italy. He had missed the previous game, a 2-1 win over Turkey, with a hip injury. His last appearance for his country came in a 4-1 defeat to France last month.

What he said

In his farewell message, Lukaku thanked his teammates, coaches and Belgian fans, and said he was proud to have played at four World Cups and three European Championships. He said he was at peace with his decision.

His record

Lukaku, who now plays for Fenerbahce, made his Belgium debut in 2010 and finished with 94 goals in 135 matches, the most goals ever scored for the national team.

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf confirms divorce from Kwankwaso's daughter, says he will marry again

Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso, daughter of his former political mentor Rabiu Kwankwaso, has ended. Speaking with humour at a pensioners' event, he said the dissolution papers had been issued and that he still intends to take another wife.

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, the daughter of former Kano governor and NDC leader Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, has ended.

What the governor said

Yusuf is said to have spoken about the matter on Monday, 16 November 2026, while addressing pensioners at an event at the Government House in Kano. Speaking in Hausa and in a light-hearted tone, he compared going from two wives to one to a demotion in military rank, from corporal to constable.

He reportedly confirmed that he had agreed to the divorce and that the papers for the dissolution had been issued. He also told the gathering that he still plans to take another wife, and asked residents to pray that he finds a suitable partner. He said he was at peace with the decision.

Background

The confirmation came two days after reports that Zainab Kwankwaso had filed a divorce petition at an Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja. She was said to be the governor's second wife.

The separation has drawn wide attention because of the long political relationship between Yusuf and Kwankwaso, who was his political mentor.
