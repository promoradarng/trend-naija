Osun government demotes two traditional rulers; one monarch claims APC sympathy was the reason

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

The Osun State Government has reportedly stripped two traditional rulers of their Part II chieftaincy status, reducing their stools to minor chieftaincies in a decision approved by Governor Ademola Adeleke.

What the government did

A letter from the state's Ministry of Local Government and Chieftaincy Affairs is said to show that the governor approved the de-recognition of the Alagunbelewo of Agunbelewo, Oba Maruf Lawal, and the Olu of Irepodun, Oba Adewale Adeniyi Sikiru, under the state's Chiefs Law. Both stools now revert to Part III, which means the rights, privileges and allowances attached to their former status have been withdrawn. The government reportedly said the approval would be published in the state gazette.

The monarch's response

Oba Lawal reportedly said the affected rulers received letters without any official reason and questioned whether the decision had been ratified by the State Executive Council as the law requires. He alleged that the action may have been linked to perceived sympathy for the All Progressives Congress (APC).

He further claimed that the rulers had been expected to show loyalty to the governor's party in return for support they received. These claims have not been independently confirmed.

What happens next

No response from the Osun government to the monarchs' allegations was included in the reports. The affected rulers say they want answers on the reasons for their demotion.

  • The two stools were moved from Part II to Part III.
  • One monarch says no reason was given.
  • He alleges the move was linked to APC sympathy.

Davido and cousins turn heads at Governor Adeleke's second-term inauguration

Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke was inaugurated for a second term on Friday, 27 November 2026, at the Osogbo Township Stadium.

Family affair

Afrobeats star Davido, the governor's nephew, drew almost as much attention as the ceremony itself. He arrived with his brother Adewale Adeleke and cousins Shina Adeleke, known as Shina Rambo, and Adebayo Adeleke, known as B-Red. Background

Adeleke, a chieftain of the Accord Party, dissolved his executive council earlier this week ahead of the start of his second term.

Alex Otti joins South-East governors to receive Tinubu at Enugu APC rally

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Abia governor Alex Otti has reportedly arrived in Enugu to join other South-East governors in receiving President Tinubu for the APC's South-East campaign rally. His appearance comes days after Tinubu said Otti was with him.

Read more

Abia State Governor Alex Otti reportedly arrived in Enugu on Saturday, 28 November 2026, to join other South-East governors and political leaders in receiving President Bola Tinubu ahead of the APC's South-East presidential campaign rally.

The arrival

Otti, who came with his wife, was reportedly received at the Akanu Ibiam International Airport by Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. He was also said to be listed among the leaders present at the rally at Michael Okpara Square in Enugu.

'He's with me'

His appearance came days after President Tinubu, speaking at a meeting with Igbo leaders in Lagos, claimed that Otti was among the South-East governors backing his re-election, recalling that the two had worked together before Otti became governor.

Mixed reactions

Otti's presence has reportedly sparked debate on social media, as he was elected governor on the Labour Party platform. Some users questioned why he attended an APC campaign event, while others said what mattered most was his performance in Abia State.

Background

The rally is part of the APC's zonal campaign tour ahead of the 16 January 2027 presidential election, as the NDC, ADC and other opposition parties also step up their campaigns.

Sola Sobowale and Nollywood stars visit Ngozi Nwosu, appeal for help before surgery

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Veteran actresses Sola Sobowale, Ebele Okaro, Chinyere Wilfred and Gloria Young have visited Ngozi Nwosu as she prepares for another surgery. They urged Nigerians to support her financially.

Read more

Veteran Nollywood actress Ngozi Nwosu has received a visit from some of her well-known colleagues as she prepares for another surgery.

The visit

A video circulating on social media shows actresses Sola Sobowale, Ebele Okaro, Chinyere Wilfred and Gloria Young spending time with Nwosu and encouraging her. During the visit, the group appealed to members of the public to help her financially ahead of her next medical procedure.

Earlier appeal

The visit comes months after Nwosu made an emotional appeal for help in July 2026. At the time, she said she had spent three weeks in hospital and urgently needed money to cover the cost of three surgeries.

How fans reacted

Many fans responded with prayers for her recovery. Several said Nollywood had lost too many people recently and urged Nigerians to step in so the industry would not mourn another veteran, coming in the same week as the death of Pete Edochie.
