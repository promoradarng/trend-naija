Singer Brymo has sparked a heated online debate after calling on President Bola Tinubu to rename the National Theatre in Lagos in honour of the late Nollywood legend Pete Edochie.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, Brymo addressed the president directly, urging him to immortalise Edochie by attaching his name to the iconic landmark.

He also argued that Nobel laureate Wole Soyinka, whose name has been linked with the National Theatre, should not receive the honour, and spoke critically of the writer.

Background

Pete Edochie died in Enugu early on Wednesday at the age of 79, after a long battle with prostate cancer. Tributes have poured in from President Tinubu, state governors and colleagues across the film industry.

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled their long-running legal battle over the sale of their French winery, Château Miraval.

The settlement

In a joint statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the former couple said they had resolved the case out of court in the best interests of their family and would not comment further. They added that any comments attributed to anonymous sources did not come from either of them or anyone close to them. The terms of the settlement were not disclosed.

The dispute

Pitt, 62, and Jolie, 51, bought the estate in the south of France in 2008 and had been in dispute over it since they split in 2016. In 2022, Pitt sued Jolie, claiming they had agreed that neither would sell their share without the other's consent, and accusing her of selling her stake to a Russian businessman without his permission. Jolie disputed his account in her response.

Three inmates died during Ilorin jailbreak attempt, correctional service says

26 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Nigerian Correctional Service says three inmates died during last week's attempted jailbreak at the Medium Security Custodial Centre in Ilorin. Two death-row inmates escaped and five others were stopped.

Read more

The Nigerian Correctional Service (NCoS) has said three inmates died during the recent jailbreak attempt at the Medium Security Custodial Centre in Ilorin, Kwara State.

What happened

Speaking during a visit to Ilorin on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the Controller-General of Corrections, Sylvester Nwakuche, said seven inmates tried to escape on 18 November. Officers stopped five of them, but two death-row inmates succeeded after reportedly breaking through and scaling the wall of the facility.

He said the service had been informed that three of the five who failed to escape had died, describing it as an unfortunate incident.

Investigation

Nwakuche said the Minister of Interior, Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo, had ordered an investigation, and a team had been set up to look into the incident thoroughly. He said anyone found to be involved would face the law. About 20 officers were earlier reported to be under investigation over the escape.
