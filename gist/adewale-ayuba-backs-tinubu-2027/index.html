Fuji star Adewale Ayuba explains why he now backs Tinubu for 2027

12 Nov 2026, 20:43 GMT

Veteran Fuji musician Adewale Ayuba has declared his support for President Bola Tinubu ahead of the 2027 election, explaining why he changed his stance on campaigning for politicians.

What he said

In an interview reported on Thursday, 12 November 2026, Ayuba said he had previously stayed away from political campaigns because he wanted to judge the President's performance for himself. He said his decision was based on what he had personally experienced, not on politicians' promises. He pointed to five major roads leading into his hometown of Ikenne in Ogun State, which he said were now in good condition, and recalled how difficult it was to get fuel for his band's trucks early in his career, saying fuel is now easier to access. He also said parents can now be more confident their children will graduate on time because of fewer strikes by university lecturers.

His message

Ayuba urged Nigerians to acknowledge improvements when they happen instead of focusing only on the negatives.

Background

Celebrities have been increasingly vocal on both sides of the 2027 race.

  • Ayuba says he judged Tinubu's record himself.
  • He praised roads into Ikenne and easier fuel access.
  • He urged Nigerians to acknowledge improvements.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

More nominations

Tems also picked up nominations for Best African Music Performance for 'Big Daddy', and Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song for 'What You Need'. Ayra Starr is nominated for Best African Music Performance for 'Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)', featuring Angélique Kidjo.

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.

VDM praised as Oyo corps members finally receive delayed allowance

18 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Oyo State Batch 25C corps members have reportedly received their delayed final ₦77,000 allowance, two days after VeryDarkMan raised the alarm. He shared a video of a corps member celebrating the payment.

Read more

Corps members of the Oyo State Batch 25C have reportedly received their long-delayed final allowance, after social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), whose real name is Martins Vincent Otse, publicly called out the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).

What happened

On Wednesday, 18 November 2026, VDM shared a video on Instagram showing a corps member celebrating after receiving a credit alert and thanking him for taking up the matter.

How it started

VDM first raised the issue on 16 November, after receiving messages from more than 100 affected corps members. He said they had completed their service and attended their passing-out parade at the end of October, but had not been paid their final ₦77,000 allowance. He called on the NYSC Director-General to act.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised the activist for taking up the corps members' complaint.
