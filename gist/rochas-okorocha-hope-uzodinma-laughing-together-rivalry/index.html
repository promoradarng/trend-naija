Rochas Okorocha and Governor Hope Uzodinma seen laughing and singing together despite political rivalry. Who is fooling who?

4 Nov 2026, 23:06 GMT

Nigerian politics will teach you lessons no classroom ever can.

Just look at former Imo State Governor Rochas Okorocha and the current governor, Hope Uzodinma, smiling, joking and singing together like two old friends who have never had a political disagreement.

Rivals on paper

These are the same political figures whose camps have been involved in a legal dispute over a senatorial candidacy.

Yet here they are, laughing and enjoying themselves, while some of their supporters are outside exchanging insults, fighting imaginary battles and treating political disagreements like personal family wars.

The real problem

Some supporters have destroyed friendships, fought their neighbours and made enemies of people they have known for years, all because of politicians who can sit together tomorrow, shake hands and laugh over a bottle of expensive wine.

The painful part is that some supporters are even willing to risk their lives defending these politicians. Meanwhile, the politicians themselves understand that politics is a game of interests, and today's enemy can become tomorrow's ally.

Look at Rochas and Hope again. Do they look like people who cannot stand each other?

A lesson for supporters

This should be a serious lesson to Nigerians, especially those who insult, threaten and attack others over political differences.

Support whoever you want. Campaign for your preferred candidate. But never allow political loyalty to turn you into an enemy of your fellow citizens.

At the end of the day, the politicians you are fighting for may be laughing together while you are still carrying their quarrel on your head.

Nigerians, when will we learn that politicians are not worth dying for? Look at the picture and tell us: who is really fooling who?

"Your approach was inappropriate": South African presidency fires back at Fani-Kayode over livestream complaint

14 Nov 2026, 09:27 GMT

South Africa's presidency has accused Nigeria's High Commissioner, Femi Fani-Kayode, of misrepresenting what happened when his speech was cut off at the ceremony where he presented his credentials to President Cyril Ramaphosa, describing his public complaint as inappropriate.

Read more

The South African presidency has criticised Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, over his public complaint that his speech was cut off during the ceremony where he presented his letters of credence to President Cyril Ramaphosa.

Fani-Kayode's complaint

Fani-Kayode had raised concerns about the incident, suggesting that he was interrupted while addressing the gathering during the official diplomatic ceremony.

South Africa responds

The South African presidency has rejected his account. Responding to the controversy, President Ramaphosa's spokesperson, Vincent Magwenya, accused Fani-Kayode of misrepresenting the situation and of wrongly dragging the South African President into a matter that did not require his involvement.

Magwenya also described the Nigerian diplomat's handling of the issue as inappropriate.

A routine ceremony turned row

The disagreement has drawn attention to what should have been a routine diplomatic ceremony between two African countries.

Diplomatic engagements are usually guided by established protocols, and disagreements over procedure are generally expected to be handled through official channels rather than in public.

For a newly appointed high commissioner, the public exchange has raised questions about diplomatic conduct, and about how misunderstandings between representatives of friendly nations should be resolved.

Copyright Commission takes legal action against NetNaija, MovieBox over piracy

14 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Nigerian Copyright Commission says it has begun legal action against operators of platforms including NetNaija and MovieBox over alleged copyright violations, warning that sites distributing protected content without permission could be shut down.

Read more

The Nigerian Copyright Commission (NCC) says it has started legal action against the operators of several online platforms, including NetNaija and MovieBox, over alleged copyright infringement.

What the commission said

Speaking on Thursday, 12 November 2026, at a copyright sensitisation programme at the University of Ibadan, Barrister Kunle Olatunji, deputy director of the commission's Regulatory and Compliance Department, said the action was part of efforts to protect authors, publishers, filmmakers and other creators whose work is used without permission. He said the Copyright Act 2022 had widened the commission's powers to tackle online piracy, allowing it to seek the closure of entire websites rather than just the removal of individual pirated items.

Why it matters

Olatunji said the law protects the rights of creative professionals whose works are exploited without their consent.

Background

The allegations against the platforms have not been tested in court, and their operators had not publicly responded at the time of writing.
