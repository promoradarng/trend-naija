Rochas Okorocha and Governor Hope Uzodinma seen laughing and singing together despite political rivalry. Who is fooling who?

4 Nov 2026, 23:06 GMT

Nigerian politics will teach you lessons no classroom ever can.

Just look at former Imo State Governor Rochas Okorocha and the current governor, Hope Uzodinma, smiling, joking and singing together like two old friends who have never had a political disagreement.

Rivals on paper

These are the same political figures whose camps have been involved in a legal dispute over a senatorial candidacy.

Yet here they are, laughing and enjoying themselves, while some of their supporters are outside exchanging insults, fighting imaginary battles and treating political disagreements like personal family wars.

The real problem

Some supporters have destroyed friendships, fought their neighbours and made enemies of people they have known for years, all because of politicians who can sit together tomorrow, shake hands and laugh over a bottle of expensive wine.

The painful part is that some supporters are even willing to risk their lives defending these politicians. Meanwhile, the politicians themselves understand that politics is a game of interests, and today's enemy can become tomorrow's ally.

Look at Rochas and Hope again. Do they look like people who cannot stand each other?

A lesson for supporters

This should be a serious lesson to Nigerians, especially those who insult, threaten and attack others over political differences.

Support whoever you want. Campaign for your preferred candidate. But never allow political loyalty to turn you into an enemy of your fellow citizens.

At the end of the day, the politicians you are fighting for may be laughing together while you are still carrying their quarrel on your head.

Over to you

Nigerians, when will we learn that politicians are not worth dying for? Look at the picture and tell us: who is really fooling who?

  • Their camps have clashed in court over a senatorial ticket.
  • Supporters are urged not to make politics personal.

Aisha Buhari seen in Tinubu-branded outfit, days after claim she backed Atiku

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari wearing attire printed with President Tinubu's campaign logo has sparked debate. It came days after ADC chieftain Solomon Dalung claimed she had told Atiku that Buhari's supporters would back him.

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari, widow of the late President Muhammadu Buhari, wearing clothing printed with President Bola Tinubu's campaign logo has stirred political debate ahead of the 2027 elections.

The video

The clip was posted on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by former presidential aide Bashir Ahmad, with a caption describing it as a message from Daura, Buhari's hometown.

Why it matters

The video came days after Solomon Dalung, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), claimed in a television interview that Aisha Buhari had visited ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar and told him that the late President's large voter base would be backing him in 2027.

Many social media users took the new video as a response to that claim, suggesting the former First Lady is supporting Tinubu.

No statement

Aisha Buhari herself had not made a public statement on her political preference at the time of the reports.

Peter Obi says insecurity stops him walking freely in Nigeria, unlike in the UK

22 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi says security warnings prevent him from taking simple walks in Nigeria, recalling how he walked and took trains freely while living in London.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has spoken about worsening insecurity in Nigeria, saying safety warnings now stop him from taking simple walks or jogging in his own neighbourhood.

What he said

Addressing the Global Peter Obi Supporters Summit in Abuja on Saturday, 21 November 2026, the former Anambra governor recalled living in the UK, on the same street as the Mayor of London. He said they would walk the streets and take the train without security escorts.

He contrasted that with Nigeria today, recalling that he used to walk freely to church in Lagos in the 1980s and 1990s, but said security concerns now prevent him from doing so.

The campaign

Insecurity is one of the major issues in the 2027 campaign, ahead of the 16 January election.

Tinubu arrives in Lagos for family prayer session after Ibadan rally

President Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday after his campaign rally in Ibadan, ahead of a special prayer session his family has organised for him on Sunday. He was received by Governors Sanwo-Olu and Abiodun.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday, 21 November 2026, after addressing an APC presidential campaign rally in Ibadan, Oyo State.

The arrival

His aircraft landed at the Presidential Wing of the Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja at about 3pm. He was received by Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, Ogun Governor Dapo Abiodun, his Chief of Staff Femi Gbajabiamila and former Ogun governor Segun Osoba, along with party leaders and supporters.

Family prayer

The visit is centred on a special prayer session organised in his honour by members of his family, scheduled for Sunday, 22 November. The President is expected to return to Abuja afterwards.

The campaign

The trip comes as the APC steps up its campaign across the South-West. At the Ibadan rally, Tinubu endorsed the party's governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos and promised improved electricity supply.

Tinubu endorses APC governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos at Ibadan rally, promises better power

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

At an APC rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, President Tinubu raised the hands of the party's governorship candidates for Oyo, Ogun and Lagos. He also guaranteed improved electricity and promised a factory in Ibadan.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos States at a campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The endorsements

Telling the crowd he would show them who to vote for as governor, Tinubu raised the hands of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC candidate in Oyo; Senator Solomon Adeola, known as Yayi, the party's candidate in Ogun; and Lagos Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, the APC flagbearer in Lagos. He also raised the hand of Remi Oseni, the party's senatorial candidate for Oyo South.

Promise on power

During the visit, the President also assured Nigerians of better electricity supply, saying the Minister of Power and his team were working hard. He added that the government would build a factory in Ibadan.

Context

The rally is part of the APC's campaign ahead of the 16 January 2027 elections.

The other side

Reactions from the opposition parties to the rally were not included in the reports.
