Ronaldo's 2007-08 season voted greatest in Ballon d'Or history

30 Oct 2026, 06:44 GMT

Cristiano Ronaldo has received a special 'Ballon d'Or of All Time' honour after fans voted his 2007-08 season at Manchester United the greatest in the history of the award.

What happened

The prize was reportedly presented by France Football, the magazine that runs the Ballon d'Or, after a month-long public online vote. The result was reported on Thursday, 29 October 2026. Lionel Messi's 2012 season finished second, while Ronaldo's own 2017 season came third.

The winning season

In 2007-08, Ronaldo scored 42 goals in all competitions and helped Manchester United win both the Premier League and the Champions League. That season earned him his first Ballon d'Or, the start of a long rivalry with Messi that saw the two players dominate the award for more than a decade.

What Ronaldo said

The Portuguese star thanked fans around the world on social media, saying football belongs to the fans and that the award belongs to them too.

How fans reacted

The result reignited the endless debate between supporters of Ronaldo and Messi. Ronaldo's fans celebrated the win as proof of his greatness, while Messi supporters pointed out that the Argentine has won more Ballon d'Or titles in total. The honour comes days after England captain Harry Kane won this year's Ballon d'Or.

  • Fans voted his 2007-08 Manchester United season the best ever.
  • Messi's 2012 season finished second.
  • Ronaldo said football and the award belong to the fans.

Victor Boniface sends ₦1m to cancer patient during TikTok livestream

Super Eagles striker Victor Boniface has been praised after sending ₦1 million to a woman battling cancer who told him on his TikTok livestream that she needed about ₦4 million for treatment.

Super Eagles striker Victor Boniface has won praise online after donating ₦1 million to a woman with cancer during a TikTok livestream.

What happened

In a clip that spread widely on Friday, 13 November 2026, the woman joined Boniface's livestream and told him she needed about ₦4 million for her treatment. After listening to her, the Bayer Leverkusen forward asked for her account details and sent the money immediately, leaving her visibly emotional. Many Nigerians commended him for his generosity.

His career

Away from the gesture, Boniface is going through a difficult spell. Injuries have held back his progress at Leverkusen, and a loan spell at Werder Bremen last season did not revive his form. He has played only a few minutes of Bundesliga football this season and is reportedly keen to leave in the January transfer window. Leverkusen, where he is under contract until 2028, are said to be open to a sale or a loan.

Manchester United sack Michael Carrick; Pochettino reportedly agrees to take over

12 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Manchester United have sacked head coach Michael Carrick after just one Premier League win in 10 games left them in the relegation zone. Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly agreed to replace him.

Read more

Manchester United have sacked head coach Michael Carrick following a poor start to the Premier League season.

Why he was sacked

United have won only one of their first 10 league matches, against Ipswich Town in August, leaving them in the relegation zone. Their other wins this season came in the Champions League. According to reports on Thursday, 12 November 2026, club executives met co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe in Monaco to finalise the decision. Carrick and his backroom staff are reportedly set to receive about £5 million in compensation.

Pochettino to take charge

Reports say the club lined up a replacement before ending Carrick's tenure, approaching US Soccer about releasing Mauricio Pochettino from his contract as head coach of the United States men's team. The former Tottenham and Chelsea manager is said to have agreed to take charge at Old Trafford. Releasing him from his US contract is reported to cost about £7 million.

Background

Carrick,, a former United midfielder, first stepped in after the dismissal of Ruben Amorim last season and did well enough to earn a permanent two-year contract in May, having guided the club into the Champions League. His first full season in charge has fallen well short of that early promise.

What happens next

United have not yet officially confirmed Pochettino's appointment.

Tinubu rewards each Flamingos player with ₦100m after U-17 World Cup win

11 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

President Tinubu has rewarded each Flamingos player with a ₦100 million savings certificate, held in trust until they turn 18, after their FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup win. The head coach gets ₦100 million and each technical official ₦50 million. First Lady Remi Tinubu announced the rewards at the Villa.

Read more

The Flamingos, Nigeria's under-17 women's football team, have been welcomed at the Presidential Villa in Abuja after winning the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.

The reception

First Lady Oluremi Tinubu received the players and officials on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, on behalf of President Bola Tinubu, in what was described as a celebratory homecoming visit and the federal government's formal recognition of the team's achievement.

₦100 million each

During the visit, the First Lady announced that the President was giving each player a ₦100 million savings certificate. The money will be held in trust in designated bank accounts until each player turns 18, so the teenage champions will not have immediate access to it. The reward was extended to the coaching staff: the head coach will receive ₦100 million in cash and each member of the technical crew ₦50 million.

The win

The Flamingos beat Japan 2–1 in the final in Morocco on Saturday, 7 November, with goals from Awawu Bashiru and Oluebube Umejiaku, becoming the first African side to win the tournament. After losing their opening game to Poland, they won five matches in a row, including victories over defending champions North Korea, Mexico, Venezuela and Brazil. The win means Nigeria has now won the FIFA U-17 World Cup in both the men's and women's competitions.

Rewards

The Senate has also pledged ₦100 million to the team, and the players had earlier received $50,000 in cash. FIFA does not award prize money at this level, so rewards depend on governments and sponsors.

Pochettino reportedly close to deal to become Manchester United coach

11 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Manchester United are reportedly close to agreeing a deal with Mauricio Pochettino to become their next head coach if they decide to sack Michael Carrick.

Read more

Manchester United are reportedly close to agreeing terms with Mauricio Pochettino to take charge of the club.

What is reported

According to reports on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, the deal would only go ahead if United decide to sack their current head coach, Michael Carrick. No decision has been publicly announced, and the club has not commented.

Talks with US Soccer

According to further reports on Thursday, 12 November, United have contacted US Soccer about triggering a reported $10 million release clause in Pochettino's contract, and expect to spend about $2 million more to bring in his backroom staff. Senior executives reportedly met minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe in Monaco on Wednesday and scheduled a formal meeting with Carrick for Thursday, after which he is widely expected to leave. United are 18th in the Premier League with just one league win this season.

Who is Pochettino

The Argentine has previously managed Tottenham Hotspur, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea, and was appointed head coach of the United States men's national team in September 2024.

Background

Carrick has come under growing pressure after a run of poor results, and senior figures at the club are reported to have met to discuss his future. Several candidates have been linked with the job in recent days.
