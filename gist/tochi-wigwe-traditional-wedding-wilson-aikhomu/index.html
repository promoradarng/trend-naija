Late banker Herbert Wigwe's daughter Tochi marries Wilson Aikhomu

5 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Otutochi 'Tochi' Wigwe, the eldest daughter of the late banker Herbert Wigwe, has married her partner, Wilson Omogbale Aikhomu, in a traditional ceremony in Lagos.

The wedding

The private ceremony, reported on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, brought both families together for traditional rites, including payment of the bride price, signing of family documents and the formal handing over of the bride to her husband's family. A video from the event spread widely online.

The couple

Tochi, 28, now leads the HOW Foundation set up by her father. Wilson Aikhomu is a Lagos-based investment professional and managing partner of an investment firm. He is the son of the late Rear Admiral Joseph Aikhomu and a nephew of the late Admiral Augustus Aikhomu, who served as Chief of General Staff under the military government of Ibrahim Babangida. He was a close friend of Tochi's late brother, Chizi.

Background

Herbert Wigwe, then chief executive of Access Holdings, died alongside his wife, Chizoba, and their son, Chizi, in a helicopter crash in California on 9 February 2024. Their deaths shocked Nigeria's business community and led to tributes across Africa. Tochi has since taken on a public role in continuing her father's philanthropic work.

How people reacted

Many people congratulated the couple and said the wedding brought joy to a family that had suffered a great loss, with some expressing emotion that her parents and brother could not be there.

A video of Nollywood veteran Richard Mofe-Damijo, popularly known as RMD, appearing to ignore a man who tried to take a selfie with him has gone viral.

The footage, which began circulating on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, was recorded at a conference at the Muson Centre in Lagos. Inside the hall, RMD appeared relaxed, laughing and chatting warmly with the same man.

Biola Adebayo puts new salon up for sale weeks after opening it

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Actress Biola Adebayo says she is selling her newly opened salon because her sister, who was meant to run it, has been granted a Canadian visa. She said she cannot manage the business herself.

Read more

Nollywood actress Biola Adebayo has announced that she is putting her newly opened salon up for sale, only weeks after unveiling it.

The reason

In a video on her social media page, Biola explained that she set up the salon to give her sister, who had been going through a difficult time, a business of her own to run.

However, just days after the salon was unveiled, her sister received approval for a Canadian visa she had been waiting for for more than three years, and will now be relocating.

Mixed feelings

The actress described the visa as good news, but admitted it left her with the challenge of managing the salon herself, which led to her decision to sell it.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says he nearly died

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Social media critic VeryDarkMan says he has been admitted to hospital in China after being diagnosed with what he called life-threatening malaria. He said he nearly lost his life on Wednesday night before being rushed to hospital.

Read more

Social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has been hospitalised in China after being diagnosed with what he described as life-threatening malaria.

What he said

VDM, who is in China on a business trip, shared the health update in a video on Instagram on Thursday, 19 November 2026. He said his condition got worse despite his attempts to treat the illness himself.

According to him, things became so serious that he nearly lost his life on Wednesday night, before he was rushed to hospital the next morning.

First hospital stay

The 32-year-old said he had never spent a night in hospital before, but that his condition would now keep him under medical care for several days.

Background

VDM is one of Nigeria's most influential social media critics. Earlier this week, Oyo corps members credited him after their delayed allowances were paid following his intervention.
