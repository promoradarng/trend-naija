Late banker Herbert Wigwe's daughter Tochi marries Wilson Aikhomu

5 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Otutochi 'Tochi' Wigwe, the eldest daughter of the late banker Herbert Wigwe, has married her partner, Wilson Omogbale Aikhomu, in a traditional ceremony in Lagos.

The wedding

The private ceremony, reported on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, brought both families together for traditional rites, including payment of the bride price, signing of family documents and the formal handing over of the bride to her husband's family. A video from the event spread widely online.

The couple

Tochi, 28, now leads the HOW Foundation set up by her father. Wilson Aikhomu is a Lagos-based investment professional and managing partner of an investment firm. He is the son of the late Rear Admiral Joseph Aikhomu and a nephew of the late Admiral Augustus Aikhomu, who served as Chief of General Staff under the military government of Ibrahim Babangida. He was a close friend of Tochi's late brother, Chizi.

Background

Herbert Wigwe, then chief executive of Access Holdings, died alongside his wife, Chizoba, and their son, Chizi, in a helicopter crash in California on 9 February 2024. Their deaths shocked Nigeria's business community and led to tributes across Africa. Tochi has since taken on a public role in continuing her father's philanthropic work.

How people reacted

Many people congratulated the couple and said the wedding brought joy to a family that had suffered a great loss, with some expressing emotion that her parents and brother could not be there.

Relationship influencer Blessing CEO has said she and her fiancé, businessman IVD, are no longer together. Speaking on Instagram Live, she said she had listened to fans who urged her to leave him.

Social media personality Nkiru Blessing Okoro, known as Blessing CEO, has confirmed that her relationship with businessman Ikechukwu Ogbonna, known as IVD, is over.

What she said

During an Instagram Live session on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, Blessing CEO was asked about her relationship status. She said she and IVD had broken up, adding that many of her fans had told her to leave him and that she now respects her fans' views. She said she had picked up the pieces of her life and described herself as back to being a single mother.

Background

The pair went public with their relationship in January 2025, and IVD proposed in April 2025. Their relationship drew strong reactions online because IVD's late wife, Bimbo, had died in a fire months earlier, and Blessing CEO had first met him as his relationship counsellor. In recent weeks Blessing CEO has been mourning the death of her nine-year-old son, who died while she was in custody, and this week she issued a tearful public apology to people she had hurt with her words. IVD also posted an emotional plea online earlier this week. Some critics responded to her apology by referring to IVD's late wife.

How people reacted

Many fans welcomed the news and wished her peace and healing, while others said she should focus on her children and her recovery.

How it started

The exchange began on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, when Olunloyo posted a message on Instagram directed at Regina Daniels's fans, a day after the actress's estranged husband, Senator Ned Nwoko, asked the public to stop spreading false stories about her health and threatened legal action against those responsible.

VeryDarkMan has challenged Davido's associates to show receipts after a social media user claimed the singer once put him up in a Lagos hotel. He also warned Zlatan after Davido was filmed singing along to lyrics believed to target him at Zlatan's wedding party.

The feud between social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and singer Davido has flared up again.

The receipts challenge

On Wednesday, 4 November 2026, VDM shared on his Instagram stories a screenshot of a post by an X user who claimed Davido had once flown him to Lagos, put him up in a five-star hotel and helped him rise to fame. VDM reportedly dismissed the claim and dared Davido or his team to produce proof of any financial support.

Warning to Zlatan

VDM also reacted angrily to a video from Zlatan Ibile's wedding welcome party in Morocco, in which Davido was seen singing along to lyrics from Zlatan's song 'Inferno', which fans believe is aimed at VDM. In a video, VDM criticised Davido and warned Zlatan that he would learn the hard way, saying he would not take the matter lightly. He also stood by recent controversial comments about his private life that had drawn reactions online.

Background

VDM and Davido were once close, but their relationship broke down earlier this year. Davido recently unfollowed VDM on Instagram and publicly congratulated Zlatan on the success of 'Inferno', which topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart. Comedian GehGeh this week urged Carter Efe not to fight other people's battles amid the wider VDM controversy.

Diss track

Later on Wednesday, VDM posted a studio preview of a new diss track aimed at Davido, titled 'On My Knees', which uses Zlatan's signature taglines and which he said would soon be released on streaming platforms.

How people reacted

Fans were divided, with some backing VDM and others saying he should move on from the feud.
