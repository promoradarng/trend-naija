Thousands march in Haiti to praise El Salvador's President Bukele as his troops take on gangs

18 Nov 2026, 14:49 GMT

Thousands of people took to the streets of Port-au-Prince and other parts of Haiti on Tuesday, praising El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele and his troops as they take on the country's powerful gangs.

"Viv Bukele"

Photos from the marches show huge crowds filling the streets, waving Haitian flags and holding up posters of the Salvadoran president. Many carried banners reading "Viv Bukele", Haitian Creole for "Long live Bukele".

Why Haitians are cheering

For years, armed gangs have controlled large parts of Port-au-Prince and beyond, killing, kidnapping and forcing hundreds of thousands of people from their homes.

Salvadoran troops have been in Haiti since early 2025 as part of an international mission, and recently launched their first major offensive against the gangs, with reports of heavy fighting and deaths.

Many marchers said Bukele is doing what the United Nations and the United States failed to do for years, while ordinary Haitians continued to suffer.

The Bukele model

Bukele became popular at home by cracking down hard on El Salvador's gangs, sharply reducing violence in a country once known as one of the most dangerous in the world. Many Haitians hope his forces can bring the same results.

His methods have also faced criticism from human rights groups, who say the crackdown in El Salvador involved mass arrests and abuses. But for Haitians who have lived in fear for years, many say any hope of relief is welcome.

Puerto Rico's Jennifer Barreto becomes first mother to win Miss Universe

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico has won Miss Universe 2026, becoming the first mother and first married woman to take the crown. The final was held in San Juan.

Puerto Rico's Jennifer Barreto has made history as the first mother to win the Miss Universe crown.

The final

The 27-year-old was crowned Miss Universe 2026 on Tuesday, 24 November, at the grand finale of the pageant's 75th edition, held at the José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum in San Juan, Puerto Rico. She beat contestants from 115 other countries.

A historic win

Barreto is married and has a seven-year-old daughter, making her the first mother and the first married woman to win the title. Her victory also gives Puerto Rico its sixth Miss Universe crown, ending a 20-year wait.

The winning answer

In the final question round, she was asked what human value should never be lost as artificial intelligence grows. She stressed using AI responsibly and keeping human values alive through consistent actions.

Canada resumes Express Entry draws, invites 1,999 for permanent residence

24 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Canada has resumed Express Entry draws, inviting 1,999 provincial nominees to apply for permanent residence on 23 November. It was the first draw under new ranking rules that reward Canadian work experience and high-wage job offers.

Read more

Canada has resumed its Express Entry draws after a short pause, issuing 1,999 Invitations to Apply (ITAs) for permanent residence to provincial nominees on 23 November 2026.

The draw

According to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada, candidates needed a minimum Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score of 673 to be invited, and must have created their profile before 31 July 2026.

New rules

It was the first draw since major changes to the CRS took effect on 3 November 2026. Candidates can now earn extra points through a new Labour Market Integration Factor, which rewards Canadian work experience or a qualifying job offer in a high-wage occupation.

Why it matters

Express Entry is one of Canada's main routes to permanent residence for skilled workers.

Police probe reported break-in at William and Kate's Norfolk home

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Police are investigating a reported break-in at Anmer Hall, the Norfolk home of the Prince and Princess of Wales. The family were in Windsor at the time and are safe.

Read more

Police are investigating a reported break-in at Anmer Hall, the Norfolk country home of the Prince and Princess of Wales, on Monday night, 23 November 2026.

What happened

Intruders are said to have entered the property while Prince William, Catherine and their children, Prince George, Princess Charlotte and Prince Louis, were at their home in Windsor. The family were safe.

About Anmer Hall

The ten-bedroom Georgian house sits on the King's Sandringham Estate and was a wedding gift from the late Queen Elizabeth II. The couple regularly use it for weekends and school holidays.

Business as usual

The reported intrusion came ahead of William's visit to Downing Street on Tuesday, where he met the Prime Minister to discuss shared priorities, including homelessness.

Two men charged

Police said they were called to Anmer Hall shortly before 6pm on Monday after reports of suspicious activity and arrested two men nearby. The two Albanian nationals have been charged with entering a dwelling with intent to steal and trespassing on a protected site, and are due to appear before magistrates in Norwich.
