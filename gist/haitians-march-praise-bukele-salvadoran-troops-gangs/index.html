Thousands march in Haiti to praise El Salvador's President Bukele as his troops take on gangs

18 Nov 2026, 14:49 GMT

Thousands of people took to the streets of Port-au-Prince and other parts of Haiti on Tuesday, praising El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele and his troops as they take on the country's powerful gangs.

"Viv Bukele"

Photos from the marches show huge crowds filling the streets, waving Haitian flags and holding up posters of the Salvadoran president. Many carried banners reading "Viv Bukele", Haitian Creole for "Long live Bukele".

Why Haitians are cheering

For years, armed gangs have controlled large parts of Port-au-Prince and beyond, killing, kidnapping and forcing hundreds of thousands of people from their homes.

Salvadoran troops have been in Haiti since early 2025 as part of an international mission, and recently launched their first major offensive against the gangs, with reports of heavy fighting and deaths.

Many marchers said Bukele is doing what the United Nations and the United States failed to do for years, while ordinary Haitians continued to suffer.

The Bukele model

Bukele became popular at home by cracking down hard on El Salvador's gangs, sharply reducing violence in a country once known as one of the most dangerous in the world. Many Haitians hope his forces can bring the same results.

His methods have also faced criticism from human rights groups, who say the crackdown in El Salvador involved mass arrests and abuses. But for Haitians who have lived in fear for years, many say any hope of relief is welcome.

Trump posthumously pardons baseball legend Pete Rose

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

US President Donald Trump has granted a posthumous pardon to baseball great Pete Rose, who pleaded guilty to tax offences in 1990 and was banned from the sport for betting on games.

Read more

US President Donald Trump has granted a posthumous pardon to baseball legend Pete Rose, two years after his death.

The pardon

Trump announced the pardon on Truth Social on Thursday, 19 November 2026, calling Rose a true baseball superstar and legend and praising his record of 4,256 career hits.

His conviction

Rose, who died in 2024 aged 83, pleaded guilty in 1990 to two counts of filing false tax returns, admitting he failed to report $354,968 over four years. He was also given a lifetime ban from Major League Baseball after an investigation found he bet on games.

Reinstatement

Rose, who played 24 seasons, was posthumously reinstated by MLB last year, making him eligible for the Baseball Hall of Fame. He remains the sport's all-time leader in career hits.

US corrections officer accused of inviting another man home, then calling him an intruder and firing gun when boyfriend returned

20 Nov 2026, 09:20 GMT

Lasacia Gore, a 34-year-old corrections officer in Michigan, has been charged with six felonies. Prosecutors say she invited another man to her home, then reported him as an intruder and fired a gun when her boyfriend came back unexpectedly. She has pleaded not guilty.

Read more

A 34-year-old woman in Michigan, United States, has been charged with six felonies after police say she invited another man to her home while her boyfriend was away, then reported him as an intruder when her partner returned unexpectedly.

The 7am phone call

According to prosecutors, Lasacia Gore, a corrections officer with Wayne County, called police at around 7am on Monday, claiming an unknown man had broken into her home in Eastpointe and was fighting with her boyfriend.

But investigators say they found that she had invited the man over herself, and had even left the door unlocked for him.

What police say happened

According to police, things turned violent when her boyfriend came home unexpectedly, found the visitor in the house and confronted him, leading to a fight.

Eastpointe Police Chief Corey Haines said Gore fired a gun while both men were wrestling on the kitchen floor. The bullet missed them and struck the wall above them.

"You told me to leave the door open"

Macomb County Prosecutor Pete Lucido said the visitor's account contradicted hers. "She's making a call saying she doesn't know him and this and that, and he says, 'What are you talking about? You're the one who told me to leave the door open and I'm coming in!'" he said.

"It's crazy. This could have been very tragic and somebody could've died," Lucido added.

Chief Haines also expressed concern that a corrections officer was allegedly involved in making a false police report and firing a weapon.

The charges

Gore faces six felony charges, including assault with a dangerous weapon, discharging a firearm at a building, filing a false felony report, lying to investigators and two firearm-related counts.

She pleaded not guilty at her arraignment on Wednesday. Her bond was set at $30,000, with a 10 per cent option. If released, she must stay at home under GPS monitoring, surrender her weapons and avoid contact with the complainant.

She is due back in court on 2 December for a probable cause conference. The charges are allegations and have not been proven in court.

Leeds stun Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge

21 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Leeds United beat Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge in the Premier League. Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso admitted the loss was painful and that his side's confidence has taken a hit.

Read more

Leeds United inflicted a heavy 4-1 defeat on Chelsea at Stamford Bridge on matchday 11 of the Premier League on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The match

Emmanuel Emegha gave Chelsea an early lead on his first start for the club since joining in the summer. Leeds hit back quickly through Noah Okafor, and the sides went into half-time level at 1-1.

After the break, Leeds were awarded a penalty when Morgan Rogers was judged to have handled the ball in the area, a decision that sparked debate, and went on to complete a convincing win.

Alonso's reaction

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso said the result was painful and reflected a team struggling to keep up its earlier form. He admitted his players' confidence had taken a hit at a difficult time.

Tinubu arrives in Lagos for family prayer session after Ibadan rally

21 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

President Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday after his campaign rally in Ibadan, ahead of a special prayer session his family has organised for him on Sunday. He was received by Governors Sanwo-Olu and Abiodun.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday, 21 November 2026, after addressing an APC presidential campaign rally in Ibadan, Oyo State.

The arrival

His aircraft landed at the Presidential Wing of the Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja at about 3pm. He was received by Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, Ogun Governor Dapo Abiodun, his Chief of Staff Femi Gbajabiamila and former Ogun governor Segun Osoba, along with party leaders and supporters.

Family prayer

The visit is centred on a special prayer session organised in his honour by members of his family, scheduled for Sunday, 22 November. The President is expected to return to Abuja afterwards.

The campaign

The trip comes as the APC steps up its campaign across the South-West. At the Ibadan rally, Tinubu endorsed the party's governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos and promised improved electricity supply.
