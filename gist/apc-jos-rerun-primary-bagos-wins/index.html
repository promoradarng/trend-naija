Dachung Bagos wins APC rerun primary for Jos South/Jos East seat

29 Oct 2026, 20:44 GMT

Former House of Representatives member Dachung Bagos has won the All Progressives Congress (APC) rerun primary for the Jos South/Jos East Federal Constituency in Plateau State, according to results announced on Thursday, 29 October 2026.

The result

The chairman of the rerun electoral committee, Simon Elisha, reportedly announced at the party's state secretariat in Jos that Bagos polled 12,181 votes, while his rival, Esther Simi Dusu, received 2,495 votes, a margin of almost 10,000. The rerun was held on Wednesday in 11 wards, including Du, Shen, Turu, Federe and Shere.

Why there was a rerun

A High Court in Jos had nullified the original primary for the seat after finding alleged irregularities in the 11 affected wards and ordered fresh voting in those areas. With the rerun result, Bagos is set to be the APC candidate for the seat in the 2027 general election.

Background

Bagos represented Jos South/Jos East in the House of Representatives before, and is a well-known figure in Plateau politics. Party primaries across the country have produced many court cases this year, with several candidates replaced or ordered to face reruns. The Plateau constituency has seen strong contests between the major parties in recent elections.

What happens next

It is not yet known whether the losing candidate will challenge the result. INEC is expected to update its list of candidates once the party submits the name.

Presidency denies Tinubu's convoy was attacked after Friday prayers in Abuja

14 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

The Presidency has dismissed videos shared by ADC chieftain Dino Melaye that purportedly showed youths throwing stones at President Tinubu's convoy after Friday prayers in Abuja, calling them misleading and releasing its own footage.

Read more

The Presidency has denied claims that President Bola Tinubu's convoy was attacked by stone-throwing youths after he attended Friday prayers in Abuja.

The claim

Dino Melaye, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), shared a video on Friday, 13 November 2026, which he said showed young men throwing stones at a convoy belonging to the President after prayers at the Shagari Central Mosque in Area 1. Voices in the video can be heard chanting a Hausa phrase meaning 'we don't want you'.

The Presidency's response

O'tega Ogra, the President's senior special assistant on digital engagement and new media, disputed the account on X, describing the videos as misleading and linking them to opposition figures. He released a separate video showing Tinubu leaving the mosque in white attire and a black cap, with supporters waving and taking photographs as he walked to his vehicle. It has not been independently established where or when Melaye's video was recorded.

Background

The dispute comes amid rising concern about political violence ahead of the 2027 elections. On the same day, the President issued a statement condemning violence at political gatherings, directing security agencies to act firmly against thuggery and insisting that every party must be free to campaign anywhere. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Lamido rules out Tinubu, backs Sandy Onor, warns divided opposition helps President

14 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Former Jigawa governor Sule Lamido says he will not support President Tinubu and has named Professor Sandy Onor as his choice for 2027, while warning that rivalry between Atiku and Obi is handing Tinubu an advantage.

Read more

Former Jigawa State governor Sule Lamido has declared that he will not back President Bola Tinubu's re-election and has named Professor Sandy Onor as his preferred presidential candidate.

His choice

Speaking in Dutse, Lamido reaffirmed his loyalty to the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) and named Onor outright as his candidate for the 16 January 2027 election, making a clear public break from the incumbent. Earlier this week, he denied a claim by Vice-President Kashim Shettima that he supported the APC.

Warning to the opposition

In a television interview on Friday, 13 November 2026, Lamido said the rivalry between Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi was working in Tinubu's favour. He pointed to the large number of serving and former governors in the APC and the failure of the two main opposition figures to cooperate, arguing that a joint ticket, with Atiku as president and Obi as running mate, would have posed a much stronger challenge. He also warned that whoever wins, Nigeria would lose if campaigns ignore national issues.

Background

Atiku is the ADC candidate and Obi the NDC candidate. Efforts to agree a single opposition candidate have repeatedly failed, though PRP candidate Donald Duke and APM candidate Seyi Makinde have both said they are open to collaboration.

Lalong, Dariye and other Plateau leaders endorse Tinubu for 2027

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

A coalition of Plateau State political leaders, including former governors Simon Lalong and Joshua Dariye, has endorsed President Tinubu and other APC candidates for 2027 at a meeting in Jos.

Read more

Prominent political figures from Plateau State have endorsed President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid for 2027.

The meeting

The endorsement was made by voice vote at a meeting in Jos organised by Arewa for Asiwaju (A4A), a pro-Tinubu group, according to a statement released on Friday, 13 November 2026. Those present included former governors, ministers, senators, members of the House of Representatives, commissioners and council chairmen, who also adopted other All Progressives Congress (APC) candidates in the state.

What the leaders said

Former governor Simon Lalong, who chaired the meeting as deputy chairman of A4A's board of trustees, said those present believed the President was committed to Nigeria's development and that keeping him in office would speed up economic growth. Former governor Joshua Dariye credited the Tinubu administration with meaningful contributions to Plateau and the country.

Background

Plateau is a key battleground in the North Central. Peter Obi won the state in the 2023 presidential election. A Middle Belt youth group that backed Obi in 2023 also recently switched its support to Tinubu. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
