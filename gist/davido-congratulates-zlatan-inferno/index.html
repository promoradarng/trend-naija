Davido congratulates Zlatan as 'Inferno' tops charts amid VeryDarkMan row

30 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido has publicly backed Zlatan Ibile's new song 'Inferno', in a move that fans say has reignited his own cold war with social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM).

What happened

In an Instagram post reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, Davido congratulated Zlatan, Peruzzi and Shoday on the song, thanking God for its success. 'Inferno' reportedly reached the top of the Apple Music Nigeria chart within 12 hours of release, as well as the Nigerian iTunes chart and the chart in Sierra Leone. Listeners have linked one of its lines to VDM's first name and his past controversies.

VDM's response

VDM has continued to mock the song and Zlatan's recent wedding, suggesting the wedding was used to promote an album that he said did not perform well. He has also sarcastically challenged the song to stay at number one on Apple Music for a whole month.

Background

Davido and VDM were once close, but their friendship broke down earlier this year. This week, VDM's friend Kokopee said the activist felt betrayed by the singer. Zlatan's feud with VDM goes back to the period after the death of singer Mohbad in 2023, when VDM publicly pressed him and other industry figures to speak out.

How fans reacted

Many fans saw Davido's post as a clear signal of where he stands, while VDM's supporters dismissed the song's success as short-lived.

  • The song topped Apple Music Nigeria within 12 hours.

Tems to lead soundtrack for Hollywood film Children of Blood and Bone

13 Nov 2026, 15:50 GMT

Tems is executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood adaptation of Tomi Adeyemi's novel 'Children of Blood and Bone', featuring Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Fireboy DML, BNXN and others. The album is out on 20 November.

Read more

Grammy-winning singer Tems has been named executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone'.

The soundtrack

The announcement was made on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026. The album brings together several of Africa's biggest stars, including Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Fireboy DML, BNXN and Kold AF. Tems described the project as a profound honour and said she was excited for fans to hear what she had put together. The soundtrack is due for release on 20 November, ahead of the film's cinema debut.

The film

'Children of Blood and Bone' is directed by Gina Prince-Bythewood and adapted from the bestselling fantasy novel by Nigerian-American author Tomi Adeyemi.

Background

Tems has become one of the most successful Nigerian artists internationally, winning Grammy Awards and earning an Oscar nomination for co-writing 'Lift Me Up' for 'Black Panther: Wakanda Forever'.

Davido becomes face of PUBG Mobile's new 'Flash' game across Africa

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Davido has been unveiled as the face of PUBG Mobile's Flash campaign in Africa, promoting a lighter version of the popular game that works on less powerful phones and includes an exclusive Davido outfit set.

Read more

Davido has signed on as the face of PUBG Mobile's new campaign across Africa, taking his brand into the continent's fast-growing gaming market.

The deal

The singer announced the partnership on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, sharing a promotional video in which he tells fans he is joining the battle royale game and that they do not need an expensive phone to play. He also invited fans to unlock an exclusive in-game 'King Set' outfit inspired by him.

The game

PUBG Mobile Flash, which launched on Friday, 13 November, is a lighter edition of the popular shooting game with a smaller download size, designed to run on a wider range of phones while keeping the main maps and gameplay. The campaign is aimed at expanding the game's audience in Africa, where many players use budget smartphones.

Background

Davido is one of Africa's biggest music stars and has a long list of brand partnerships. He has been in Tanzania this week, where he shot a project with Tanzanian star Diamond Platnumz.

Speed Darlington apologises to Burna Boy, begs him to drop court charges

12 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Singer Speed Darlington has publicly apologised to Burna Boy and asked him to withdraw the legal charges against him, promising never to insult or mention him again.

Read more

Singer Speed Darlington has made a public apology to Burna Boy, appealing to the Grammy-winning star to withdraw the court charges filed against him.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, Speed Darlington, whose real name is Darlington Okoye, admitted that his words may have caused Burna Boy pain and embarrassment. He said he had wrongly assumed that other people shared his own temperament and tolerance, and pledged to stop all comments about the singer, direct or indirect, if the charges are withdrawn. He also promised to stop mentioning Burna Boy's name altogether once the matter is settled.

How the feud began

The dispute escalated in October 2024, when Speed Darlington posted videos questioning Burna Boy's Grammy win for his album 'Twice as Tall' and making disparaging remarks about him. The matter later became a legal case, with Speed Darlington facing charges filed after a complaint from Burna Boy's camp.

How people reacted

The apology drew a wave of reactions online, with many users surprised by Speed Darlington's change of tone. Burna Boy had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

2Baba likes brother's birthday tribute to Annie Idibia: "Massive birthday blessings, Queen"

14 Nov 2026, 08:55 GMT

2Baba's brother Ujah Idibia has celebrated Annie Idibia on her birthday, calling her a queen who is "loved dearly". The post was liked by 2Baba himself, adding to talk about the estranged couple's relationship.

Read more

Annie Idibia's birthday has drawn warm messages from her husband's family, and one tribute in particular has fans talking.

The post

Ujah Idibia, brother of music star 2Baba, shared photos of the singer and Annie side by side on Instagram with a heartfelt caption.

"Massive birthday blessings Queen @annieidibia1. May the universe be on ur side always. Just know u are loved dearly. More love n light for u. Have a wonderful Bday," he wrote, adding heart emojis.

2Baba's like

What caught many fans' attention was that the post was liked by 2Baba's official account. Earlier, the singer had also shared Annie's photo on his Instagram story with his song 'Celebrate'.

Background

2Baba, whose real name is Innocent Idibia, and actress Annie Idibia were one of Nigeria's most talked-about celebrity couples and have children together. Their separation became public in early 2025, and 2Baba has since been linked with Edo lawmaker Natasha Osawaru. The birthday gestures from him and his family suggest goodwill remains between the two.
