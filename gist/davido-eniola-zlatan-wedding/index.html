Court strikes out ₦6bn money-laundering charges against Mompha, socialite says

10 Nov 2026, 17:47 GMT

Socialite Ismaila Mustapha, known as Mompha, says a court has struck out the ₦6 billion money-laundering charges filed against him by the EFCC, calling it vindication after seven years. The EFCC had not commented.

Read more

Socialite Ismaila Mustapha, popularly known as Mompha, says a court has struck out the money-laundering charges brought against him by the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC).

What he said

In an Instagram post on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Mompha said the court struck out the ₦6 billion charges on the grounds that they amounted to an abuse of court process and forum shopping. He described the outcome as personal vindication after what he called years of harassment, called the prosecution a witch-hunt, praised the judge and thanked his supporters. He also mocked the anti-graft agency in the post.

Background

Mompha's legal battle with the EFCC began in 2019, when he was arrested after returning to Nigeria. He has always denied the allegations, saying he came home to invest. The case dragged on for about seven years. The EFCC had not publicly commented on the ruling at the time of writing, and it was not immediately clear whether the commission would appeal or file fresh charges.

What it means

Mompha says his legal ordeal is now over.

Bimbo Ademoye rejects apology from man whose copyright claim hit her YouTube film

10 Nov 2026, 17:47 GMT

Actress Bimbo Ademoye says she will take action against a man who uploaded the soundtrack of her film 'My 30th Wedding' to streaming platforms, triggering a copyright claim that demonetised the film on YouTube, despite his public apology.

Read more

Nollywood actress and filmmaker Bimbo Ademoye has rejected an apology from a man whose copyright claim affected her YouTube film.

What happened

According to reports on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, the man uploaded the soundtrack from Ademoye's film 'My 30th Wedding' to a music streaming platform, which triggered an automatic copyright claim on the film on her YouTube channel, cutting off its earnings. He later posted an apology on Instagram, accepting responsibility and saying he had taken the song down from all platforms and that the claim should clear within 24 to 48 hours.

Her response

Ademoye shared a screenshot of the apology and said she was not satisfied with his explanation, vowing to take action. She also questioned why he had chosen that particular song from the film rather than a more popular one, suggesting it was because the artist behind the other song would not have tolerated it.

Background

Ademoye is one of Nollywood's most popular actresses and has built a large audience for films released on YouTube. Automated copyright systems such as YouTube's Content ID have become a common source of disputes for Nigerian filmmakers, as false or mistaken claims can redirect earnings away from creators.

Miss Universe Nigeria Danielle Adewusi arrives in Puerto Rico for 75th pageant

10 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Danielle Adewusi, crowned Miss Universe Nigeria 2026 in September, has arrived in Puerto Rico ahead of the 75th Miss Universe pageant on 24 November, where up to 120 countries will compete.

Read more

Nigeria's representative at Miss Universe 2026, Danielle Adewusi, has arrived in Puerto Rico ahead of the competition.

The arrival

Her arrival was confirmed on the Miss Universe Nigeria Facebook page on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. Adewusi was crowned Miss Universe Nigeria on 27 September at the Federal Palace Hotel in Victoria Island, Lagos, beating 19 other contestants while representing Lagos State.

The pageant

The 75th Miss Universe pageant will hold on 24 November at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico José Miguel Agrelot in San Juan. The organisers say up to 120 countries and territories will take part, one of the largest fields in the competition's history.

How to support her

Nigerians can support Adewusi through the fan vote and 'Beyond the Crown' categories, which are currently open.

Background

Nigeria has never won Miss Universe. The country's best recent result came in 2024, when Chidimma Adetshina finished as first runner-up, the highest placing by a Nigerian in the competition's history.

Lateef Adedimeji's praise for Tinubu's skills programmes divides fans

10 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Actor Lateef Adedimeji has drawn mixed reactions after posting a video praising President Tinubu's 3 Million Technical Talent and Skills Up Artisans programmes, with some fans calling it political.

Read more

Nollywood actor and filmmaker Lateef Adedimeji has sparked debate after speaking favourably about two of President Bola Tinubu's skills programmes.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Monday, 9 November 2026, Adedimeji spoke about the 3 Million Technical Talent (3MTT) programme and the Skills Up Artisans (SUPA) initiative, describing them as meaningful steps towards transforming Nigeria's tech and creative sectors. He encouraged his followers to position themselves to benefit from them.

The reaction

Many followers focused on the timing of the video, with the 2027 elections approaching, and saw it as politically motivated. Some long-time fans expressed disappointment, while others said they still admired him and that he was entitled to share information about government programmes.

Background

Adedimeji is one of the most popular actors in the Yoruba film industry and is married to actress Mo Bimpe. Several entertainers have openly backed the President's re-election, including Saidi Balogun and Eniola Badmus, and have also faced backlash online.
