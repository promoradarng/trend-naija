Habibat Jinadu says discrimination over her dark skin pushed her to bleach

29 Oct 2026, 17:46 GMT

Veteran Yoruba Nollywood actress Habibat Jinadu has spoken openly about why she lightened her skin, saying years of remarks about her complexion on film sets wore her down.

What she said

In an interview with a BBC Yoruba programme, reported on Thursday, 29 October 2026, Jinadu reportedly said that directors repeatedly complained that she was too dark under the lights on set. She said she did not take the comments to heart at first, until someone compared her to a monkey. She said fair-skinned actors were often preferred for roles when she was building her career, and that people who were not light-skinned were rarely called for jobs. The constant criticism, she said, eventually led her to bleach her skin.

Background

Jinadu has appeared in many Yoruba-language films over the years and is a familiar face to fans of the genre. Skin bleaching is a long-running issue in Nigeria and across Africa, and health experts have repeatedly warned about the dangers of some lightening creams. Other entertainers have spoken in the past about pressure to look a certain way on screen.

How people reacted

Her comments started a wide conversation online. Many users said colourism remains a real problem in the entertainment industry, while others said no one should be made to feel ashamed of their natural skin. Some praised her for speaking honestly about a painful experience.

  • She says fair-skinned actors were favoured for roles.
  • Her comments sparked a debate about colourism.

Fans raise concern over Doris Ogala's health in detention after medical report surfaces

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

A medical report shared online says actress and governorship aspirant Doris Ogala was treated for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition earlier this year, raising concern among supporters about her health in detention. Her bail ruling is due on 6 November.

Read more

Supporters of actress and governorship aspirant Doris Ogala have raised concerns about her health while she remains in detention, after a medical report about her condition was shared online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026.

The medical report

According to reports, the document from the Federal Medical Centre in Umuahia, dated 5 June 2026, shows that she was treated for severe anaemia linked to a fibroid condition, following an earlier surgical procedure in 2025. The report reportedly says she was admitted, treated and discharged after significant improvement, and was advised to avoid strenuous activity for six months and attend regular follow-up appointments. Reports claim she has been experiencing renewed bleeding while in detention, but this has not been independently confirmed.

How people reacted

Fans and supporters took to social media to express worry and call for her release on health grounds. Some urged the authorities to ensure she receives proper medical care while her case continues.

Background

Doris Ogala, 30, is a Nollywood actress and a governorship candidate of the African Action Congress. She was remanded in custody last month in a case linked to her dispute with Pastor Chris Okafor, which arose from social media posts. The AAC's presidential candidate, Omoyele Sowore, and other supporters have condemned her detention. A court is due to rule on her bail application on 6 November.

What happens next

Her lawyers are expected to raise her health when the court considers her bail application.

Blessing CEO apologises in tearful video weeks after son's death

3 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Relationship influencer Blessing CEO has shared an emotional video apologising to everyone she has hurt with her words, saying she now feels the pain she once spoke about carelessly. She is mourning her nine-year-old son, who died while she was in custody.

Read more

Social media personality Nkiru Blessing Okoro, known as Blessing CEO, has issued an emotional public apology, weeks after the death of her nine-year-old son.

The video

In a video posted on Instagram on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, the relationship influencer was seen crying inside a parked car. She said she had gone there because she did not want her mother to see her in that state. She described feeling confused, numb, broken and surrounded by darkness, and said she was so exhausted that she needed people to pray for her.

Her apology

Blessing CEO apologised to everyone she had hurt with her words and actions, saying she had spoken carelessly about pain she had never experienced and could now feel the pain she once mocked. She said she was only human and bound to make mistakes, and compared herself to the prodigal son returning home. She also thanked those who still love her and apologised to those who no longer do.

Background

Her son, Bryan, died from an asthma attack while she was being held in custody in Lagos, according to reports. She was later released on bail and is facing a court case, which is still ongoing. Since her release she has spoken about her ordeal and denied rumours about drug use. Before her troubles, she was known for strong and often controversial relationship advice online, which drew both followers and critics.

How people reacted

Many social media users expressed sympathy and prayed for her healing, while some said her apology showed growth. Others urged people to stop mocking her and to respect a grieving mother.
