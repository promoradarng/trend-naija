US congressman introduces bill to stop lobbyists working for Nigeria

31 Oct 2026, 14:43 GMT

US Representative Barry Moore has introduced a bill that would bar American lobbyists from being paid to represent Nigeria while it remains designated a Country of Particular Concern over religious freedom. He accuses the Nigerian government of paying lobbyists to counter criticism over attacks on Christians.

Read more

A United States congressman has introduced legislation aimed at stopping American lobbying firms from working for the Nigerian government.

What the bill does

Representative Barry Moore introduced H.R. 10939, which would bar people and firms in the United States from accepting payment to lobby on Nigeria's behalf for as long as Nigeria is designated a Country of Particular Concern (CPC) for religious freedom violations. Firms that take such payments during that period could face sanctions. The bill was reported on Saturday, 31 October 2026, and has been referred to the House Committee on Foreign Affairs.

Why Moore introduced it

Moore reportedly accused the Nigerian government of paying expensive lobbyists in Washington to play down the persecution of Christians in the country. The allegations of persecution are disputed by the Nigerian government, which has said that insecurity affects people of all faiths and is driven by terrorism, banditry and communal conflicts.

Background

The United States designated Nigeria a Country of Particular Concern, a label for countries accused of severe violations of religious freedom. The designation has strained relations between the two countries, and the Nigerian government has hired lobbying firms in Washington to present its position. The issue gained new attention this week after a Christian woman was convicted in Borno State over converting an adult, a case criticised by Christian groups.

What happens next

The bill must pass through committee and both houses of Congress before it can become law. The Nigerian government had not responded publicly at the time of the report.

Pope Leo XIV casts mail-in ballot in US midterm elections

30 Oct 2026, 20:49 GMT

The Vatican says Pope Leo XIV has voted by mail in the US midterm elections in his home state of Illinois, believed to be the first time a pope has voted in an American election. The Vatican said he believes citizens have a right and duty to take part in democracy.

Read more

Pope Leo XIV, the first American pope, has voted in the United States midterm elections, the Vatican has confirmed.

What happened

The Holy See press office said on Friday, 30 October 2026, that the Pope had cast a mail-in ballot in Illinois, where he was born. It said he had exercised his right to vote as a US citizen whenever he was eligible, and that he believes citizens have both a right and a duty to take part in democratic processes. His ballot is expected to include races for the US Senate, the governorship of Illinois and the House of Representatives. He is believed to be the first pope ever to vote in an American election.

Background

The Pope was born Robert Francis Prevost in Chicago and holds both American and Peruvian citizenship, having spent many years as a missionary and bishop in Peru. He was elected pope in May 2025 after the death of Pope Francis. Public records show he voted regularly in Illinois before becoming pope, including in the 2024 presidential election. The Vatican did not say who he voted for.

Why it matters

The US midterm elections in November will decide control of Congress, and the Pope's decision to vote has drawn wide attention because of his role as a global religious leader. Supporters praised him for setting an example of civic duty, while others questioned whether a pope should take part in a national election.

What happens next

The Pope is expected to continue his public schedule, including a planned visit to South America.

VeryDarkMan's RATEL brand reportedly records ₦90 million in sales in one day

31 Oct 2026, 23:17 GMT

VeryDarkMan and his RATEL brand have reportedly made about ₦90 million in sales in a single day, after he shared bank alerts showing a stream of customer payments. If the figure holds, it would be one of the strongest signs yet that the social media critic is turning his influence into a serious business.

Read more

VeryDarkMan (VDM) and his RATEL brand have reportedly recorded about ₦90 million in sales in just one day, a figure that has set social media talking about how far the activist-turned-businessman could go.

The claim followed a post showing VDM in RATEL Power branded safety gear, holding up his phone, alongside a screenshot of bank alerts. The alerts, all dated 31 October 2026, show several customer payments for RATEL products, including a 600-watt power station, with the account balance climbing above ₦83 million.

From critic to businessman

VDM first became widely known for his social media videos and for publicly challenging powerful figures. Over time he has moved into importing products from China, building his own brand and steadily establishing himself in business. RATEL now sells items ranging from power stations to sensor lights.

A marketing style built on openness

What stands out most is how he markets the brand. Rather than relying on traditional adverts, he brings his audience along with him. He shows them the products, explains how they work and tests them publicly, so followers are watching the business grow in real time.

Whether people like him or not, few can deny the attention he has drawn to RATEL.

Sales are not profit

It is worth remembering that ₦90 million in sales is not ₦90 million in profit. Running a business comes with stock costs, shipping, staff, risk and plenty of other challenges, and the figure itself has not been independently confirmed.

Still, if RATEL keeps up this momentum, many observers believe it could grow into a multibillion-naira business group. Ten years from now, people may look back at these early videos and remember exactly how it all started.

Why it matters

Nigeria needs more young people who can turn online influence into productive businesses that create jobs and last. VDM's journey, with its struggles, criticism, mistakes and determination, is playing out in full public view.

So the question is simple: do you believe RATEL can become one of Nigeria's biggest indigenous brands in the next five years?

Obasanjo and daughter Iyabo trade jokes on stage at Abeokuta symposium

31 Oct 2026, 20:42 GMT

Former President Olusegun Obasanjo and his daughter, former senator Iyabo Obasanjo, drew laughter at a symposium in Abeokuta when she jokingly said she did not know him and he replied that it was already an abuse of AI. The event focused on using artificial intelligence for development.

Read more

Former President Olusegun Obasanjo and his daughter, former senator Iyabo Obasanjo, provided a light-hearted moment at a public event in Abeokuta, Ogun State, with a playful exchange that quickly spread online.

What happened

At the Ajibosin Platform symposium, held at the Olusegun Obasanjo Presidential Library on Friday, 30 October 2026, Iyabo reportedly greeted the dignitaries in her opening address and acknowledged her father last, joking that he was someone she did not know. When the former President took the microphone, he responded that the abuse of artificial intelligence had already started, since his own daughter now claimed not to know her father. The audience laughed at both remarks.

About the event

The symposium, part of the Odun Omo Olowu festival, focused on using artificial intelligence to solve grassroots problems and speed up national development. It was at the same event that Obasanjo criticised President Bola Tinubu's attitude to borrowing, saying debt destroys generations, and warned against relying too heavily on advice from foreign-trained economists.

Background

Iyabo Obasanjo is a public health specialist who served as Ogun State Commissioner for Health and later as a senator representing Ogun Central. She is currently the PDP candidate for Ogun Central in the 2027 elections. Father and daughter have had a well-publicised strained relationship in the past, which made their friendly exchange all the more talked about.

How people reacted

Many social media users enjoyed the moment, saying it showed a warmer side of the former President, while others joked about the role of AI in family relationships.
