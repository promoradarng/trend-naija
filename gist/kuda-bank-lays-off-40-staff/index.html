Kuda Bank to lay off about 40 staff in second round of cuts this year

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigerian digital bank Kuda is reportedly preparing to lay off about 40 employees across seven countries, its second round of job cuts in 2026.

The cuts

The restructuring will affect around 7.5% of the bank's workforce in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, South Africa, Canada, Tanzania, Uganda and Ghana.

The reason

The company is said to be cutting costs as it works to become profitable by 2027. Its chief executive is reported to have explained the decision to staff.

The move has raised fresh concerns about job security in Nigeria's fintech industry.

Crypto firms given 30 November deadline to register for tax

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Nigerian Revenue Service has told cryptocurrency exchanges and other virtual asset providers serving Nigerians to register on its new tax portal by 30 November or face sanctions.

Read more

The Nigerian Revenue Service (NRS) has given cryptocurrency exchanges and other virtual asset service providers until 30 November 2026 to register on its new tax portal or risk regulatory sanctions.

Who is affected

The directive covers both local and foreign crypto businesses serving Nigerian customers, as the federal government moves to bring digital asset transactions under formal tax supervision.

The deadlines

According to a public notice, operators must complete their initial registration by 30 November and connect their systems to the portal by 31 December 2026.

Why it matters

The move is part of wider efforts to bring digital asset transactions under formal tax supervision.

Fidelity Bank names Stanley Amuchie as new MD/CEO from January

20 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Fidelity Bank has confirmed Stanley Amuchie as its next managing director and chief executive, after CBN approval. He takes over on 1 January 2027 from Nneka Onyeali-Ikpe.

Read more

Fidelity Bank Plc has confirmed Stanley Chiedoziem Amuchie as its new managing director and chief executive officer, following approval from the Central Bank of Nigeria.

The change

Amuchie, currently an executive director and the bank's chief operations and information officer, will take over on 1 January 2027. He succeeds Nneka Onyeali-Ikpe, whose term as MD/CEO ends on 31 December 2026.

Board's backing

The bank's chairman, Amaka Onwughalu, said the board was confident in Amuchie's ability to lead, pointing to his deep knowledge of the bank's operations and wider industry experience.

Google offers Nigerian students free AI subscription worth ₦92,400

Google is offering eligible university students in Nigeria and 26 other African countries 12 months of free access to its Google AI Plus plan, normally ₦7,700 a month.

Read more

Google is offering eligible higher-education students in Nigeria 12 months of free access to its AI subscription, a package worth about ₦92,400.

The offer

The promotion covers students in 27 African countries, including Nigeria. Google AI Plus normally costs ₦7,700 a month in Nigeria.

The package includes enhanced access to the Gemini assistant and other AI tools, including research and live conversation features, as well as 400GB of cloud storage.

Who can apply

The offer is aimed at eligible students in higher education institutions, who must verify their student status to claim it.
