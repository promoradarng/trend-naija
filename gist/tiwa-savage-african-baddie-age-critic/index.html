Tiwa Savage hits back at critic who mocked her age over 'African Baddie' single

29 Oct 2026, 20:44 GMT

Afrobeats star Tiwa Savage has fired back at a man who mocked her age over the title of her new single, 'African Baddie', in an exchange that quickly went viral.

What happened

On Thursday, 29 October 2026, a social media user reportedly questioned why a woman of 40 would call herself a 'baddie' and suggested she was not serious. Replying in a single post, the singer corrected him, saying she is actually 46, listed her achievements and described herself as unbothered. She asked what exactly his problem was and used the same post to promote her new release.

The new single

'African Baddie' is scheduled for release on Friday, 30 October. The singer has long presented herself as Africa's 'bad girl', and the title builds on that image.

Background

Tiwa Savage is one of Africa's most successful female artists. She has won major awards, signed with Universal Music Group and performed at the coronation concert of King Charles III in 2023. Her career spans more than a decade of hit songs, and she has frequently spoken about ageism and the pressure on women in the music industry.

How people reacted

Fans rallied behind the singer, praising her confidence and saying age should not limit how women express themselves. Others joked that the critic had given her free promotion a day before the song's release.

  • Tiwa says she is 46 and 'unbothered'.
  • 'African Baddie' is due out on Friday, 30 October.
  • Fans praised her response to the critic.

Frank Edwards says he turned down a huge deal that asked him to remove Jesus from his songs

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Gospel singer Frank Edwards has reportedly said he rejected an offer that could have made him rich enough to own three private jets. The promoters wanted him to replace the name Jesus with God in his songs.

Read more

Gospel singer Frank Edwards has reportedly opened up about a deal he rejected early in his career, which he believes could have made him wealthy enough to own three private jets.

The offer

Speaking during a concert ministration, in a clip that circulated online on 21 November 2026, Edwards is said to have explained that the offer came shortly after he travelled to South Africa to shoot the video for his song Beautiful.

Promoters reportedly offered to take his music to a global audience on one condition: that he remove the name of Jesus from his songs and replace it with God, arguing that this would make the music more widely acceptable.

His message

Edwards told his audience that the temptation came disguised as a genuine business opportunity, and warned that not every compromise looks obviously wrong at first. He said his mother's advice helped him turn the deal down.

Olamide honoured with 'Amuludun' title by Nike Davies-Okundaye

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Rapper Olamide has reportedly been given the Yoruba title Amuludun by artist Nike Davies-Okundaye during a visit to her Lagos gallery. The honour came days after he released his album Time and Space.

Read more

Rapper and YBNL boss Olamide has reportedly received a traditional Yoruba honour from the celebrated artist Chief Nike Davies-Okundaye.

The honour

During a visit to the Nike Art Gallery in Lekki, Lagos, Olamide was given the title Amuludun, which roughly means the one who brings joy to the town. The veteran artist is also said to have presented him with a custom-made beaded portrait.

A show of respect

A video of the visit shared online showed the rapper chatting warmly with her, and at one point prostrating before her in the traditional Yoruba gesture of respect, a moment that drew attention from fans.

Background

The visit came just days after Olamide released his latest album, Time and Space, on 16 November 2026. He remains one of the most influential figures in Nigerian hip-hop and Afrobeats, and his YBNL label is home to artists including Fireboy DML and Asake.

KWAM 1 backs Tinubu's fuel subsidy removal at APC campaign event

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Fuji star Wasiu Ayinde, KWAM 1, has defended President Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy, saying it mainly benefited a small group rather than ordinary Nigerians.

Read more

Veteran Fuji musician Wasiu Ayinde, popularly known as KWAM 1, has thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. He questioned who had really benefited from the subsidy, saying a small group of people had gained from it while ordinary Nigerians saw little benefit.

Background

The petrol subsidy was removed when Tinubu took office in 2023, leading to a sharp rise in fuel prices.

The campaign

His comments came during the APC's campaign tour of the South-West, which included a presidential rally in Ibadan.
