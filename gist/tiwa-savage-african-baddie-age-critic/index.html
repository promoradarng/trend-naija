Tiwa Savage hits back at critic who mocked her age over 'African Baddie' single

29 Oct 2026, 20:44 GMT

Afrobeats star Tiwa Savage has fired back at a man who mocked her age over the title of her new single, 'African Baddie', in an exchange that quickly went viral.

What happened

On Thursday, 29 October 2026, a social media user reportedly questioned why a woman of 40 would call herself a 'baddie' and suggested she was not serious. Replying in a single post, the singer corrected him, saying she is actually 46, listed her achievements and described herself as unbothered. She asked what exactly his problem was and used the same post to promote her new release.

The new single

'African Baddie' is scheduled for release on Friday, 30 October. The singer has long presented herself as Africa's 'bad girl', and the title builds on that image.

Background

Tiwa Savage is one of Africa's most successful female artists. She has won major awards, signed with Universal Music Group and performed at the coronation concert of King Charles III in 2023. Her career spans more than a decade of hit songs, and she has frequently spoken about ageism and the pressure on women in the music industry.

How people reacted

Fans rallied behind the singer, praising her confidence and saying age should not limit how women express themselves. Others joked that the critic had given her free promotion a day before the song's release.

  • Tiwa says she is 46 and 'unbothered'.
  • 'African Baddie' is due out on Friday, 30 October.
  • Fans praised her response to the critic.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

More nominations

Tems also picked up nominations for Best African Music Performance for 'Big Daddy', and Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song for 'What You Need'. Ayra Starr is nominated for Best African Music Performance for 'Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)', featuring Angélique Kidjo.

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.

Petrol prices fall at filling stations after Dangote cuts rate by ₦51

18 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Major marketers including AA Rano, NIPCO and AP Ardova have reportedly reduced pump prices to between ₦1,350 and ₦1,385 per litre. It follows a ₦51 per litre cut by Dangote Refinery.

Read more

Motorists are beginning to see some relief at filling stations as major marketers reduce petrol prices following a ₦51 per litre cut by the Dangote Petroleum Refinery.

The new prices

A market survey on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, reportedly showed that AA Rano, the Nigerian Independent Petroleum Company (NIPCO) and AP Ardova had lowered their petrol prices to between ₦1,350 and ₦1,385 per litre. Several filling stations are said to have cut prices by between ₦20 and ₦60 per litre, with NNPC and other marketers also adjusting their rates.

Why prices are falling

The reductions come as the cost of importing petrol has dropped, with the landing cost of imported fuel reportedly falling to about ₦1,067 per litre, below Dangote Refinery's own rate. This has put pressure on local prices.

The survey suggested more stations could follow with price cuts in the coming days.
