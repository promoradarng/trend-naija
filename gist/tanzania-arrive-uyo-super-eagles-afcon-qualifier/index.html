Tanzania head to Uyo early for Super Eagles AFCON qualifier

3 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Tanzania's national team, the Taifa Stars, are set to arrive early in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, ahead of their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Nigeria's Super Eagles, according to reports.

The fixtures

Nigeria will host the matchday three game at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November 2026. The two teams will meet again four days later in Dar es Salaam for the matchday four fixture.

Tanzania's preparations

Head coach Miguel Gamondi reportedly wants extra time for his players to get used to conditions in Nigeria before the game. He has named a 24-man squad split evenly between 12 home-based players and 12 who play abroad.

Background

The 2027 Africa Cup of Nations will be co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda. The Super Eagles need good results in the double-header to stay on course for the tournament. Uyo has become the Super Eagles' main home ground in recent years and is known for its passionate crowds. The confederation recently confirmed Nigerian Samson Adamu as its General Secretary and voted to return the Nations Cup to a two-year cycle.

What happens next

The Super Eagles are expected to open camp in Uyo next week, with fans looking forward to two important results.

  • Nigeria host Tanzania in Uyo on Friday, 13 November.
  • The return game is in Dar es Salaam four days later.
  • Tanzania's coach has named a 24-man squad.

Newcastle defender Amar Dedic stopped by armed police over bow and arrows

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Police in Northumberland stopped Newcastle United defender Amar Dedic and took a bow and arrows from his car after a member of the public reported two men carrying the weapon near a reservoir. Officers concluded there was no threat.

Read more

Newcastle United defender Amar Dedic was stopped by police in north-east England after a member of the public reported seeing two men carrying a bow and arrows.

What happened

According to reports on Friday, 6 November 2026, the 24-year-old Bosnia-Herzegovina international was travelling in a car with a friend when the report was made about two men walking along a track near a reservoir off Military Road in the Harlow Hill area of Northumberland. Six patrol cars, including armed officers, were reportedly sent, and the car was stopped about seven miles from where the men had been seen. Northumbria Police said that after speaking to the men they concluded there was no threat to the public, and a bow and arrows were seized from the vehicle. The police statement did not name the player.

Teammates arrive

Dedic reportedly cooperated fully with officers and had no bad intentions. Newcastle goalkeeper Mark Gillespie and former defender Paul Dummett reportedly arrived while officers were still talking to him, having been in the area, and stopped to support him.

Background

Dedic is a full-back who has played for clubs including Red Bull Salzburg and Benfica before moving to Newcastle.

Trump names Stephen Miller White House chief of staff after midterm losses

7 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

US President Donald Trump has made Stephen Miller, the architect of his immigration crackdown, his new chief of staff, replacing Susie Wiles days after Republicans' heavy defeat in the midterm elections. Democrats strongly criticised the choice.

Read more

US President Donald Trump has appointed Stephen Miller as White House chief of staff, replacing Susie Wiles following the Republican Party's heavy losses in this week's midterm elections.

What happened

A White House official said Wiles resigned on Thursday night, and Trump announced the change on his Truth Social platform, describing Miller as one of his most trusted advisers. He said Wiles would move to his political operation and thanked her for her service. Asked by reporters on Friday, 6 November 2026, why he had made the change, Trump said he wanted to make a change. Before the elections, he had repeatedly said there was no chance he would replace Wiles.

Who is Stephen Miller

Miller, 41, has been one of the most influential figures in Trump's administrations, serving as deputy chief of staff for policy and homeland security adviser. He played a central role in designing the mass deportation programme that became a defining policy of Trump's second term, which has drawn protests and legal challenges, including over the conduct of immigration agents.

Democrats react

Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer described Miller as the architect of the administration's harshest policies, said Trump could not have made a worse choice, and promised that the new Democratic majorities in Congress would fight him every step of the way.

Background

In the midterm elections on 3 November, the Democrats were projected to win control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate, a result that is expected to make it much harder for Trump to pass legislation in the last two years of his term. Wiles, who ran Trump's 2024 campaign, was the first woman to serve as White House chief of staff.

Usher and Chris Brown reportedly argued backstage before delayed Atlanta show

7 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Usher and Chris Brown reportedly argued backstage before their joint concert in Atlanta on Thursday, contributing to a delay of nearly three hours. The dispute was said to be over whether to start while fans were still arriving.

Read more

R&B stars Usher and Chris Brown reportedly had an argument backstage before their concert in Atlanta on Thursday night, 5 November 2026, which contributed to a delay of almost three hours.

What happened

According to reports citing people close to the tour, the disagreement was about whether to start the show while many fans were still stuck in heavy traffic around the venue. Usher reportedly wanted to go ahead, while Brown preferred to wait until more concertgoers had reached their seats. The two are said to have exchanged words, with one source describing them as highly competitive throughout their joint tour and blaming the clash on egos.

Tour to continue

The sources reportedly said the two had not settled their differences, but did not believe the row threatened the rest of the tour. The pair were scheduled to perform in Atlanta again on Friday night. Some fans complained online about the long wait before the show began.

Background

Usher and Chris Brown are two of the biggest names in R&B and have collaborated in the past. The two reportedly had a confrontation at Brown's birthday party in 2023, when Usher was said to have stepped in after Brown began shouting at singer Teyana Taylor. Neither singer had publicly commented on the latest reports at the time of writing.

Why it matters

Both artists have large fan bases in Nigeria, and Chris Brown has strong links with Nigerian stars, having worked with Davido and other Afrobeats acts.

ADP suspends national chairman Yabagi Sani, accuses him of backing Atiku

7 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

The Action Democratic Party's national working committee has suspended its chairman, Yabagi Yusuf Sani, accusing him of forgery, financial misconduct and openly endorsing ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar. He has not publicly responded.

Read more

The national working committee (NWC) of the Action Democratic Party (ADP) has placed its national chairman, Yabagi Yusuf Sani, on interim suspension.

The allegations

In a notice to the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), signed by national secretary Victor Fingesi and reported on Friday, 6 November 2026, the party accused Sani of forging party documents, gaining unauthorised access to and manipulating its candidate nomination portal, and behaving in ways that could damage the party's image. The party said it had already complained to INEC on 19 October about suspected irregularities in the uploading of its governorship candidates for Ogun, Enugu and Niger states. It also alleged that the management of the party's accounts had not been transparent, including money from the sale of nomination forms. None of the allegations has been tested in court.

Endorsing Atiku

The party also pointed to a television appearance on 30 August 2026 in which, it said, Sani described the ADP's own presidential candidate, Aliyu Ibn Abbas, as unserious and openly endorsed Atiku Abubakar of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), saying Atiku was the only candidate who provided money for campaign activities.

How it happened

The decision was reportedly taken at an NWC meeting in Abuja. The motion was moved by the party's national legal adviser and passed by a majority of members present. Sani is barred from acting as chairman pending a full investigation and a review by the party's national executive committee.

Background

Sani has led the ADP for several years. He had not publicly responded to the suspension at the time of writing. The move comes as the ADC itself faces a court ruling that removed its national leadership and voided Atiku's nomination, which the party has appealed.
