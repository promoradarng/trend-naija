Tanzania head to Uyo early for Super Eagles AFCON qualifier

3 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Tanzania's national team, the Taifa Stars, are set to arrive early in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, ahead of their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Nigeria's Super Eagles, according to reports.

The fixtures

Nigeria will host the matchday three game at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November 2026. The two teams will meet again four days later in Dar es Salaam for the matchday four fixture.

Tanzania's preparations

Head coach Miguel Gamondi reportedly wants extra time for his players to get used to conditions in Nigeria before the game. He has named a 24-man squad split evenly between 12 home-based players and 12 who play abroad.

Background

The 2027 Africa Cup of Nations will be co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda. The Super Eagles need good results in the double-header to stay on course for the tournament. Uyo has become the Super Eagles' main home ground in recent years and is known for its passionate crowds. The confederation recently confirmed Nigerian Samson Adamu as its General Secretary and voted to return the Nations Cup to a two-year cycle.

What happens next

The Super Eagles are expected to open camp in Uyo next week, with fans looking forward to two important results.

  • Nigeria host Tanzania in Uyo on Friday, 13 November.
  • The return game is in Dar es Salaam four days later.
  • Tanzania's coach has named a 24-man squad.

No Nigerian on CAF Women's Player of the Year shortlist for the first time

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

For the first time since the award began in 2001, no Nigerian player is on CAF's shortlist for Women's Player of the Year. The Super Falcons are still nominated for National Team of the Year, and three Nigerians made other lists.

Read more

Nigeria has no player on the shortlist for the 2026 CAF Women's Player of the Year award, the first time the country has been left out since the prize was introduced in 2001, according to reports.

The nominees

The Confederation of African Football released the nominations this week. The list includes Malawian sisters Tabitha and Temwa Chawinga, Cameroon's Marie Ngah Manga, Michaely Bihina and Monique Ngock, Zambia's Barbra Banda, South Africa's Thembi Kgatlana, Morocco's Hanane Ait El Haj, Algeria's Marine Dafeur and Ghana's Portia Boakye.

Nigerians on other lists

Nigeria still features in other categories. The Super Falcons were shortlisted for Women's National Team of the Year, Janet Akekoromowei and Oluwakemi Adegbuyi were nominated for the young talent award, and Edo Queens goalkeeper Christiana Uzoma is among the contenders for Goalkeeper of the Year.

Background

Nigerian players have dominated the award over the years, with Asisat Oshoala winning it a record six times. The omission follows a disappointing 2026 Women's Africa Cup of Nations for the Super Falcons and their failure to qualify for the Women's World Cup for the first time. The team only this week received the cash rewards promised for winning the previous WAFCON.

What happens next

The 2026 CAF Awards will be held on 7 December at the Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Creative Arts in Lagos.

ADC names venue as Atiku kicks off presidential campaign in Sokoto

4 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The ADC says Atiku Abubakar's 2027 presidential campaign will be flagged off at its secretariat on Ilorin Road, Sokoto, on Wednesday. The party had kept the venue quiet after alleging intimidation, a convoy attack and the sealing of an office.

Read more

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) has named the venue for the official launch of Atiku Abubakar's 2027 presidential campaign in Sokoto.

The venue

According to reports, the campaign will be flagged off at the party's secretariat on Ilorin Road, Sokoto, on Wednesday, 4 November 2026. Senator Ibrahim Lamido, who coordinates the ADC presidential campaign in the state, said Atiku was expected to arrive at Sultan Abubakar III International Airport at about 9am, and invited supporters and members of the public to welcome him before moving to the venue. He urged party executives in all 23 local government areas of the state to mobilise supporters.

Why the venue was kept quiet

The party had earlier withheld details of the venue, citing alleged intimidation, an attack on the convoy of its governorship candidate and the sealing of one of its campaign offices. On Tuesday, Atiku accused the Department of State Services of recruiting protesters to disrupt the rally, a claim the DSS had not publicly answered. The Sokoto State Government said it sealed the office over questions about who owns the building and to prevent further disturbances.

Background

Atiku, a former vice-president, is the ADC's candidate after the party became the platform of an opposition coalition. Sokoto is in the North-West, a region with a large share of Nigeria's voters, and is governed by the APC. Other candidates, including President Bola Tinubu of the APC and Peter Obi of the NDC, have also been holding rallies and town halls across the country.

What happens next

Security agencies are expected to be deployed in Sokoto for the event, and the ADC plans further rallies in other states.

Education minister says Tinubu graduated with first class, calls him a genius

4 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Education Minister Tunji Alausa has reportedly said President Tinubu graduated with a first class in accounting from Chicago State University and that he has met the President's classmates. He urged Nigerians to ignore opposition doubts about the President's credentials.

Read more

The Minister of Education, Tunji Alausa, has defended President Bola Tinubu's academic record, describing him as a genius.

What he said

Speaking on a television politics programme on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, Alausa reportedly said Tinubu graduated with a first class degree in accounting from Chicago State University in the United States. He said he knew the university well, had visited it many times, and had met many of the President's former classmates in person. He urged Nigerians to disregard what he described as the opposition's claims about the President's credentials, and called Tinubu the first internationally exposed president.

Background

Questions about the President's academic records were raised by opponents before and after the 2023 election. Chicago State University released records confirming that Tinubu graduated in 1979, and the Supreme Court dismissed petitions challenging his election, including claims about his certificates. Opposition figures have continued to raise the issue in political debates. Supporters say the matter has been settled by the courts and the university.

Background on the minister

Alausa, a medical doctor who practised in the United States, was appointed Minister of Education in 2024. He has introduced several policy changes in the sector.

How people reacted

APC supporters welcomed the comments, while critics said the minister's remarks would not end the debate and that voters care more about the economy than academic records.

57 Nigerians at risk as Gambia orders banks to replace foreign staff

4 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Nigerians in Diaspora Commission says 57 Nigerian professionals in four Nigerian-owned banks in The Gambia could lose their jobs after the Gambian central bank ordered banks to replace foreign staff with Gambians by 31 December. The FG has urged a rethink.

Read more

The federal government has raised concern over a directive by The Gambia's central bank ordering commercial banks to replace foreign employees with Gambian nationals, a move that could cost dozens of Nigerians their jobs.

The directive

According to reports, the Central Bank of The Gambia issued a circular dated 16 September 2026 giving banks until 31 December 2026 to replace non-Gambian staff with qualified Gambians.

Nigeria's concern

The Nigerians in Diaspora Commission (NiDCOM) said 57 Nigerian professionals working in four Nigerian-owned banks in The Gambia could be affected. It urged the Gambian authorities to reconsider the directive and to engage with the banks and other stakeholders to reach a fair solution.

Background

Nigerian banks have expanded across West Africa over the past two decades, with several operating subsidiaries in The Gambia. Both countries are members of the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS), whose protocols promote the free movement of people and the right to work across member states. Many West African countries, however, have rules encouraging the employment of their own citizens in key sectors.

Why it matters

The move comes as several countries tighten rules on foreign workers. Nigerians abroad have recently faced stricter immigration policies in the UK, Portugal and elsewhere.

What happens next

It is not yet clear whether The Gambia will change or delay the policy.
