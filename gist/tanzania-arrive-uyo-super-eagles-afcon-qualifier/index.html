Tanzania head to Uyo early for Super Eagles AFCON qualifier

3 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Tanzania's national team, the Taifa Stars, are set to arrive early in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, ahead of their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Nigeria's Super Eagles, according to reports.

The fixtures

Nigeria will host the matchday three game at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November 2026. The two teams will meet again four days later in Dar es Salaam for the matchday four fixture.

Tanzania's preparations

Head coach Miguel Gamondi reportedly wants extra time for his players to get used to conditions in Nigeria before the game. He has named a 24-man squad split evenly between 12 home-based players and 12 who play abroad.

Background

The 2027 Africa Cup of Nations will be co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda. The Super Eagles need good results in the double-header to stay on course for the tournament. Uyo has become the Super Eagles' main home ground in recent years and is known for its passionate crowds. The confederation recently confirmed Nigerian Samson Adamu as its General Secretary and voted to return the Nations Cup to a two-year cycle.

What happens next

The Super Eagles are expected to open camp in Uyo next week, with fans looking forward to two important results.

  • Nigeria host Tanzania in Uyo on Friday, 13 November.
  • The return game is in Dar es Salaam four days later.
  • Tanzania's coach has named a 24-man squad.

Newcastle defender Amar Dedic stopped by armed police over bow and arrows

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Police in Northumberland stopped Newcastle United defender Amar Dedic and took a bow and arrows from his car after a member of the public reported two men carrying the weapon near a reservoir. Officers concluded there was no threat.

Read more

Newcastle United defender Amar Dedic was stopped by police in north-east England after a member of the public reported seeing two men carrying a bow and arrows.

What happened

According to reports on Friday, 6 November 2026, the 24-year-old Bosnia-Herzegovina international was travelling in a car with a friend when the report was made about two men walking along a track near a reservoir off Military Road in the Harlow Hill area of Northumberland. Six patrol cars, including armed officers, were reportedly sent, and the car was stopped about seven miles from where the men had been seen. Northumbria Police said that after speaking to the men they concluded there was no threat to the public, and a bow and arrows were seized from the vehicle. The police statement did not name the player.

Teammates arrive

Dedic reportedly cooperated fully with officers and had no bad intentions. Newcastle goalkeeper Mark Gillespie and former defender Paul Dummett reportedly arrived while officers were still talking to him, having been in the area, and stopped to support him.

Background

Dedic is a full-back who has played for clubs including Red Bull Salzburg and Benfica before moving to Newcastle.

Flamingos beat Brazil 3-1 to reach first U-17 Women's World Cup final

5 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nigeria's Flamingos have reached the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup for the first time after beating Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final. Awawu Bashiru scored twice and Queen Joseph once. They face Japan in the final.

Read more

Nigeria's under-17 women's team, the Flamingos, have made history by reaching the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup for the first time.

The match

The Flamingos beat Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final on Wednesday night, 4 November 2026, according to reports. Queen Joseph opened the scoring in the 56th minute, and Awawu Bashiru doubled the lead in the 71st minute before adding her second goal in the 80th minute to put the game beyond Brazil's reach.

A record run

The win beats the team's previous best performance, a bronze medal at the 2022 edition of the tournament. On the way to the semi-final, the Flamingos beat Venezuela in the quarter-finals, a result that earned praise from the Confederation of African Football.

The final

The Flamingos will now face Japan in the final as they chase Nigeria's first FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup title. Japan, one of the strongest sides in women's youth football, won the tournament in 2014.

Background

The result comes at a difficult time for Nigerian women's football at senior level. The Super Falcons failed to qualify for the Women's World Cup for the first time, and no Nigerian player was shortlisted for this year's CAF Women's Player of the Year award. The Flamingos' success has been welcomed as a sign that a new generation of players is coming through.

FIFA's message

FIFA congratulated the Flamingos on qualifying for the final, a first for Nigeria at this level of the women's game.

How people reacted

Nigerians celebrated the win across social media, praising the young players and their coaches and urging the government and the football federation to support them ahead of the final.

Shettima says Peter Obi has run himself out of the 2027 race

6 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Vice-President Kashim Shettima told an APC rally in Uyo that NDC candidate Peter Obi had destroyed his own chances in 2027, mocking his comparisons with Bangladesh. The rally followed President Tinubu's inauguration of projects in Akwa Ibom.

Read more

Vice-President Kashim Shettima has said that Peter Obi, presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has ruined his own chances in the 2027 election.

What he said

Speaking at an All Progressives Congress (APC) campaign rally in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, Shettima reportedly said Obi had run himself out of the race through what he called lies and bogus statistics. He suggested that Obi had recently travelled abroad quietly before returning to Nigeria, and that he had thrown away the goodwill he once enjoyed among voters. The vice-president also mocked Obi's frequent comparisons between Nigeria and Bangladesh, saying a shirt made for one country cannot fit another, and dismissed Obi's claim that he won the 2023 presidential election.

Tinubu in Akwa Ibom

Before the rally, President Bola Tinubu inaugurated several projects built by Governor Umo Eno's administration, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo. Tinubu described the visit as a homecoming, praised Eno as a visionary leader, and said the airport's international flight status was the result of the governor's commitment to the state. The President also took a swipe at Obi at the event over erosion in Anambra State.

Background

Obi, a former Anambra governor, came third in the 2023 presidential election and challenged the result in court, insisting he won. He is running again in 2027 with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027. Obi's campaign had not publicly responded to Shettima's remarks at the time of writing.

INEC warns of plot to threaten and kidnap election workers ahead of 2027

6 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

INEC chairman Joash Amupitan says intelligence operations with the DSS uncovered a plot to intimidate and abduct ad hoc election staff, and that suspects have been arrested and charged. He warned officials against bias and negligence.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has warned of an alleged plot to threaten, intimidate and abduct temporary election workers ahead of the 2027 general elections.

What INEC said

Speaking in Lokoja, Kogi State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, during a North Central zonal readiness assessment, INEC chairman Professor Joash Amupitan said intelligence-led operations carried out with the Department of State Services (DSS) had led to the arrest of key suspects, who have since been charged in court. He warned that anyone who interferes with or harms election workers will be prosecuted to the full extent of the law. He did not give details of the suspects or the charges.

Warning to staff

Amupitan also warned electoral officers against negligence and political bias, saying neutrality at local level would directly affect public trust in the elections. He said credible elections cannot be achieved through last-minute arrangements, and that INEC is working with security agencies through the Inter-Agency Consultative Committee on Election Security to tackle threats early.

Background

INEC expects to manage more than 103 million registered voters across 176,846 polling units in 2027. The commission relies on hundreds of thousands of ad hoc staff, many of them members of the National Youth Service Corps, to run polling units, and attacks on election workers and offices have been a concern in past elections. This week INEC has also faced questions about about 1.5 million questionable entries found in the voter register, and disowned a fake poster about the ADC's leadership.

What happens next

The presidential and National Assembly elections are scheduled for 16 January 2027.
