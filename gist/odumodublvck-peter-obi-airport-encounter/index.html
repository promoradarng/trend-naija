Odumodublvck hails Peter Obi as he rushes to catch flight at Abuja airport

6 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Rapper Odumodublvck has set social media buzzing with a video of his brief encounter with Peter Obi, presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), at the airport in Abuja.

What happened

The video, which surfaced on X and Instagram on Friday, 6 November 2026, shows the two crossing paths at Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport. Odumodublvck spotted Obi near what appeared to be the check-in or security area and greeted him energetically, calling him 'His Excellency' and praising him as a man who is always on the move. Obi barely slowed his stride before hurrying further into the terminal to catch his flight. The rapper later shared the moment on his own social media pages.

Background

Obi, a former Anambra State governor, came third in the 2023 presidential election and is running again in 2027 with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. He is known for travelling on commercial flights and for an image of being constantly busy. The two men are said to have had a warmer, more relaxed meeting at an airport in 2025. Odumodublvck, an Abuja-based rapper, rose to fame with the hit 'Declan Rice' and recently warned young artists that Lagos is a hard place to grow in the music industry.

How people reacted

Many social media users found the moment funny, with some joking about Obi's speed and others asking whether he had snubbed the rapper. His supporters said he was simply rushing to avoid missing his flight, while political rivals used the clip to tease him.

Davido becomes face of PUBG Mobile's new 'Flash' game across Africa

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Davido has been unveiled as the face of PUBG Mobile's Flash campaign in Africa, promoting a lighter version of the popular game that works on less powerful phones and includes an exclusive Davido outfit set.

Read more

Davido has signed on as the face of PUBG Mobile's new campaign across Africa, taking his brand into the continent's fast-growing gaming market.

The deal

The singer announced the partnership on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, sharing a promotional video in which he tells fans he is joining the battle royale game and that they do not need an expensive phone to play. He also invited fans to unlock an exclusive in-game 'King Set' outfit inspired by him.

The game

PUBG Mobile Flash, which launched on Friday, 13 November, is a lighter edition of the popular shooting game with a smaller download size, designed to run on a wider range of phones while keeping the main maps and gameplay. The campaign is aimed at expanding the game's audience in Africa, where many players use budget smartphones.

Background

Davido is one of Africa's biggest music stars and has a long list of brand partnerships. He has been in Tanzania this week, where he shot a project with Tanzanian star Diamond Platnumz.

Speed Darlington apologises to Burna Boy, begs him to drop court charges

12 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Singer Speed Darlington has publicly apologised to Burna Boy and asked him to withdraw the legal charges against him, promising never to insult or mention him again.

Read more

Singer Speed Darlington has made a public apology to Burna Boy, appealing to the Grammy-winning star to withdraw the court charges filed against him.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, Speed Darlington, whose real name is Darlington Okoye, admitted that his words may have caused Burna Boy pain and embarrassment. He said he had wrongly assumed that other people shared his own temperament and tolerance, and pledged to stop all comments about the singer, direct or indirect, if the charges are withdrawn. He also promised to stop mentioning Burna Boy's name altogether once the matter is settled.

How the feud began

The dispute escalated in October 2024, when Speed Darlington posted videos questioning Burna Boy's Grammy win for his album 'Twice as Tall' and making disparaging remarks about him. The matter later became a legal case, with Speed Darlington facing charges filed after a complaint from Burna Boy's camp.

How people reacted

The apology drew a wave of reactions online, with many users surprised by Speed Darlington's change of tone. Burna Boy had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

Ayra Starr confirmed for Coachella 2027 on SZA-headlined Sunday

11 Nov 2026, 17:46 GMT

Ayra Starr will perform at the 2027 Coachella festival in California, appearing on the Sunday bill of both weekends, which SZA headlines. The singer told fans: 'See you in the desert.'

Read more

Afrobeats star Ayra Starr has been announced as one of the performers at the 2027 Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival.

The line-up

The festival's organisers revealed the line-up on Instagram on Wednesday, 11 November 2026. Ayra Starr is on the Sunday bill for both weekends and is expected to perform on 11 and 18 April 2027. The Sunday shows are headlined by American R&B star SZA, with Don Toliver, Kygo, Jorja Smith, Victoria Monét and Tove Lo among the other acts. Dua Lipa is among the festival's other headliners. Coachella takes place at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, from 9 to 11 April and 16 to 18 April.

Her reaction

The singer shared the news on her own Instagram page with a short message telling fans she would see them in the desert.

Background

Ayra Starr, signed to Mavin Records, rose to international fame with hits such as 'Rush' and has become one of the most successful young Nigerian artists of her generation. Coachella is one of the world's biggest music festivals.

"Peter Obi is one in a million. Just imagine the kind of travelling we do together": Dr. Mo

13 Nov 2026, 12:29 GMT

Dr. Mo has described Peter Obi as "one in a million", marvelling at the NDC presidential candidate's punishing travel schedule, which he says can take them from Lagos to Abuja, London, several US cities and back again in one trip.

Read more

Dr. Mo has expressed admiration for former Anambra State Governor Peter Obi, now the NDC presidential candidate, describing his travel schedule as one that is very hard to keep up with.

"Just visualise what I'm saying"

According to Dr. Mo, travelling with Obi means moving from one city to another, sometimes across continents, with little time to settle before the next journey.

"We move from Lagos to Abuja, then take off to London. From there, we spend eight hours flying to Houston, hold a meeting and move to Phoenix, then California. We return to Texas, go to Dallas, fly back to the UK and eventually return to Nigeria," he said.

"Just imagine it. Just visualise what I'm saying."

"One in a million"

Dr. Mo described Obi as "one in a million", expressing admiration for the amount of travelling and the number of engagements he handles.

For anyone who has done long-distance travel, moving through several cities, attending meetings and catching yet another flight can be exhausting. Doing it repeatedly, while running a national campaign, is another level entirely.
