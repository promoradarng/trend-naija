Odumodublvck hails Peter Obi as he rushes to catch flight at Abuja airport

6 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Rapper Odumodublvck has set social media buzzing with a video of his brief encounter with Peter Obi, presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), at the airport in Abuja.

What happened

The video, which surfaced on X and Instagram on Friday, 6 November 2026, shows the two crossing paths at Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport. Odumodublvck spotted Obi near what appeared to be the check-in or security area and greeted him energetically, calling him 'His Excellency' and praising him as a man who is always on the move. Obi barely slowed his stride before hurrying further into the terminal to catch his flight. The rapper later shared the moment on his own social media pages.

Background

Obi, a former Anambra State governor, came third in the 2023 presidential election and is running again in 2027 with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. He is known for travelling on commercial flights and for an image of being constantly busy. The two men are said to have had a warmer, more relaxed meeting at an airport in 2025. Odumodublvck, an Abuja-based rapper, rose to fame with the hit 'Declan Rice' and recently warned young artists that Lagos is a hard place to grow in the music industry.

How people reacted

Many social media users found the moment funny, with some joking about Obi's speed and others asking whether he had snubbed the rapper. His supporters said he was simply rushing to avoid missing his flight, while political rivals used the clip to tease him.

Lagos says construction of Epe International Airport will start in 2027

15 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Lagos State says work on the proposed Epe International Airport will begin in 2027, with Governor Sanwo-Olu announcing the timeline at an APC campaign event in Epe and his deputy saying preliminary work is well advanced.

Read more

The Lagos State Government plans to begin construction of the proposed Epe International Airport in 2027.

The announcement

According to a statement by the Lagos chapter of the All Progressives Congress (APC), reported on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu announced the timeline at an APC governorship campaign event in Epe. His deputy, Dr Obafemi Hamzat, said significant progress had been made on the documentation and groundwork needed for the project.

What it could mean

Lagos APC spokesman Seye Oladejo described the airport as a major infrastructure investment that could turn Epe into an important aviation, industrial and commercial hub, and said it would create opportunities for residents and businesses along the Lekki–Epe corridor.

Background

Lagos is currently served by Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, which handles most of Nigeria's international air traffic. The Lekki–Epe corridor has seen rapid development in recent years, including the Dangote Refinery and the Lekki Deep Sea Port. No cost or funding details were given in the latest announcement.

Floyd Mayweather trains in Abuja as he launches Nigerian boxing programme

15 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Unbeaten boxing legend Floyd Mayweather has been training in Abuja after arriving to launch the TMT Frontrow Fight Series, a programme to develop young Nigerian boxers, ahead of his exhibition fight in Bangkok in December.

Read more

American boxing legend Floyd Mayweather has been spotted training in Abuja during a visit to Nigeria.

Why he is in Nigeria

Mayweather arrived in the capital on Thursday, 13 November 2026, ahead of a press conference the next day to launch the TMT Frontrow Fight Series, a programme designed to give young Nigerian boxers access to professional coaching, international exposure and competitive fights. Organisers say boxing academies will be set up in Lagos, Abuja, Enugu, Calabar and other cities, and the first event in the series is planned for 2027.

Training for Bangkok

Videos of the 49-year-old training in Abuja delighted fans online. He is preparing for an exhibition bout against Australian light-heavyweight Matt Floyd in Bangkok, Thailand, on 19 December 2026.

Background

Mayweather retired from professional boxing in 2017 with an unbeaten record of 50 wins, 27 of them by knockout.
