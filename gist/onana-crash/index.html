Ex-Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami survives attack at his Abeokuta home

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Former Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami says he fought off three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta in the early hours of Friday. Police say he has been treated and discharged from hospital.

Read more

Former Super Eagles captain and football administrator Segun Odegbami has survived an attack by three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta, Ogun State, in the early hours of Friday, 20 November 2026.

What happened

The attackers reportedly overpowered his domestic staff and driver before forcing their way into his bedroom at about 2:30am. The 74-year-old was injured in the attack.

Odegbami described the incident as an attempt on his life. He said he fought back with help from his driver and house help, and the attackers eventually fled. He said it was the closest he had ever come to death.

Police response

The Ogun State Police Command confirmed the attack and said the former international had been treated in hospital and discharged.

Who he is

Odegbami is a former captain of the Super Eagles and one of Nigeria's best-known football figures.

Manhunt ordered

Ogun State Commissioner of Police Bode Ojajuni visited Odegbami at his home and ordered an immediate manhunt for the attackers, promising they would be prosecuted once caught.

FIFA President Infantino arrives in Warri for stadium commissioning

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has arrived in Warri, Delta State, for the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium. He was received by Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former NFF president Amaju Pinnick.

Read more

FIFA President Gianni Infantino arrived in Warri, Delta State, on Friday, 20 November 2026, ahead of the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium.

The arrival

Infantino landed at Osubi Airport on a private jet and was received by Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former Nigeria Football Federation president Amaju Pinnick. He said the warm welcome showed the unifying power of football.

Third visit

It is Infantino's third visit to Nigeria. He first came in 2016 shortly after taking office, and returned in 2023 for the Aisha Buhari Cup.

Background

The visit comes days after FIFA appointed a normalisation committee to run the NFF, and as Infantino prepares to face challengers in the FIFA presidential election in March 2027.

Suspected insurgents bomb power lines linking Kainji Dam to Jebba

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Suspected Boko Haram insurgents reportedly blew up two electricity transmission towers connecting the Kainji Dam power plant to Jebba in Niger State. No lives were lost, but the attack raised fears over the safety of power infrastructure.

Read more

Suspected Boko Haram insurgents have reportedly bombed two electricity transmission towers connecting the Kainji Dam power station to Jebba in Niger State.

What happened

The explosions reportedly happened at about 2pm on 17 November 2026, between Kanti community in Mashegu Local Government Area and Kanya community in Mokwa Local Government Area. The attackers are said to have targeted the transmission infrastructure, raising concerns that the Kainji Dam power plant itself could be at risk.

No lives were lost, according to the reports, but the blasts caused panic in nearby communities. Security monitoring has continued in the area.

Why it matters

Kainji and Jebba are hydropower stations that feed the national grid.

Background

The incident follows a series of attacks on and vandalism of electricity infrastructure across the country. This month, the House of Representatives resolved to investigate the destruction of 10 transmission towers within five months.

Nigeria begins building $70m passport factory, local production from May 2027

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

The federal government has started building a $70 million passport production plant in Abuja. Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo says it will start producing passport booklets locally from May 2027.

Read more

Nigeria has started building a $70 million passport production plant in Abuja, as the federal government moves to end its reliance on foreign suppliers for passport booklets.

The announcement

Minister of Interior Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo announced the project at a groundbreaking ceremony at the headquarters of the Nigeria Immigration Service. He described it as a major step towards a self-sufficient passport system and said local production should begin by May 2027.

The plant

The facility is being built in partnership with IRIS Smart Technologies and will initially produce six million electronic passport booklets a year, with capacity that could later rise to 10 million.

Why it matters

The plant is meant to end Nigeria's reliance on foreign suppliers for passport booklets.
