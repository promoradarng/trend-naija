Peller and Jarvis block each other after live-stream fight, two months into marriage

23 Oct 2026, 17:15 BST

Streamer Peller and his wife Jarvis have blocked each other on social media after a heated argument on a live stream in the early hours of Friday. The row began when Peller said the couple were like a father and mother to Jarvis's younger sister, and should be the ones to guide her and choose who she marries. Jarvis disagreed, telling him that if her family had chosen a husband for her, they would not have picked him, and that he should leave her family alone and focus on his own. An angry Peller removed her from the live and blocked her entire family, repeatedly asking his fans whether it was all because his body is small. Jarvis then blocked him too. Peller later went on his WhatsApp channel, called her a fool for blocking him, and blocked her back. The couple married in August, and Jarvis is seven months pregnant.
