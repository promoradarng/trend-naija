Davido and Chioma star together in romantic 'Already Falling' music video

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

Afrobeats star Davido has released the music video for his song 'Already Falling', and the star of the show is his wife, Chioma Adeleke.

What happened

The singer shared the video on Instagram on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, with a short caption presenting it as a gift from the couple to their fans. The video opens with a large portrait of the pair, with Davido performing in front of it. Chioma, a chef and businesswoman known to fans as Chef Chi, then appears in a Victorian-style gown, with Davido in a matching outfit. In one scene she playfully strokes his beard. Later the mood changes as she drives a red convertible while he sings beside her.

How fans reacted

Fans flooded the comments with praise for the couple's chemistry, describing them as genuine and clearly in love. Many complimented Chioma's look and called the pair Nigerian music royalty, while others said the video showed that the couple did not need to try hard to stand out.

Background

Davido and Chioma, who married in a lavish ceremony in Lagos in 2024 and have twins, rarely appear together in his videos, which made this release all the more talked about. Davido has been in the news in recent weeks, including over the cooling of his friendship with activist VeryDarkMan.

What happens next

The video is already among the most-discussed Nigerian music releases of the week, and fans are hoping for more joint appearances from the couple.

  • Chioma appears in a Victorian-style gown and drives a red convertible.
  • Fans praised the couple's on-screen chemistry.
  • The couple rarely appear together in his videos.

Davido's Ghana rep says promoter owes money over short concert performance

Davido's Ghanaian representative has reportedly blamed an unresolved payment dispute for the singer's brief performance at an event organised by Great Osama. He claimed the balance due on arrival was never paid as agreed.

Read more

Davido's Ghanaian representative, Sabi Boi, has reportedly spoken out on the controversy over the Afrobeats star's short performance at an event organised by Great Osama in Ghana.

What he said

In a TikTok Live session on Monday, 23 November 2026, Sabi Boi claimed that the organiser owed Davido's team money, and dismissed suggestions that the promoter had paid huge sums for the show.

The alleged deal

According to him, the arrangement was for the organiser to pay a deposit before the show and the rest once Davido arrived at the venue, but the second payment was not made as agreed. He also claimed the promoter tried to settle the balance with gold instead of cash.

The other side

No response from Great Osama to the claims was included in the reports.

ADC tells Tinubu to resign or face impeachment over Kano governor's interview

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The African Democratic Congress has reportedly demanded that President Tinubu resign or face impeachment, citing Kano Governor Abba Yusuf's recent interview about the 2023 Supreme Court ruling. The ADC says it points to possible gross misconduct.

Read more

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) has reportedly issued an ultimatum to President Bola Tinubu, demanding that he either resign or face impeachment proceedings in the National Assembly.

Why the ADC made the demand

The demand follows Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf's recent Hausa-language interview, in which he spoke about a promise to rejoin the APC if the president did not interfere in his Supreme Court case over the 2023 governorship election. The ADC argued that the governor's remarks suggested a link between the court case and a political deal, and that this could amount to gross misconduct, a constitutional ground for removing a president.

Background

Yusuf defected from the NNPP to the APC in January 2026. In his interview, he claimed that his former mentor, Rabiu Kwankwaso, sent him to make the promise to Tinubu. The Kwankwasiyya Movement has rejected the claim as false and said the governor offered no evidence.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to the ADC's demand was included in the reports.

NNPC says $2.04bn in crude oil proceeds is not missing

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

NNPC has dismissed reports that $2.04 billion in crude oil proceeds linked to its former trading arm Duke Oil is missing. It says the figure is the gross value of 29 cargoes and that all transactions were properly accounted for.

Read more

The Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPC) has dismissed reports that $2.04 billion in crude oil proceeds involving its trading subsidiary, formerly known as Duke Oil, is missing.

What NNPC said

In a statement on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, NNPC's chief corporate communications officer said the $2.04 billion represented the gross value of 29 crude oil cargoes, not missing or unpaid revenue. The statement was titled 'Duke Oil cargoes are fully accounted for'.

The company maintained that all transactions under the Direct Sale Direct Purchase (DSDP) programme were properly documented. It said the apparent gaps in dollar receipts were due to the way the programme's settlements were structured.

Background

Reports suggesting the money could not be traced had sparked concern online.
