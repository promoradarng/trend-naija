Davido and Chioma star together in romantic 'Already Falling' music video

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

Afrobeats star Davido has released the music video for his song 'Already Falling', and the star of the show is his wife, Chioma Adeleke.

What happened

The singer shared the video on Instagram on Wednesday, 28 October 2026, with a short caption presenting it as a gift from the couple to their fans. The video opens with a large portrait of the pair, with Davido performing in front of it. Chioma, a chef and businesswoman known to fans as Chef Chi, then appears in a Victorian-style gown, with Davido in a matching outfit. In one scene she playfully strokes his beard. Later the mood changes as she drives a red convertible while he sings beside her.

How fans reacted

Fans flooded the comments with praise for the couple's chemistry, describing them as genuine and clearly in love. Many complimented Chioma's look and called the pair Nigerian music royalty, while others said the video showed that the couple did not need to try hard to stand out.

Background

Davido and Chioma, who married in a lavish ceremony in Lagos in 2024 and have twins, rarely appear together in his videos, which made this release all the more talked about. Davido has been in the news in recent weeks, including over the cooling of his friendship with activist VeryDarkMan.

What happens next

The video is already among the most-discussed Nigerian music releases of the week, and fans are hoping for more joint appearances from the couple.

  • Fans praised the couple's on-screen chemistry.

Tems to lead soundtrack for Hollywood film Children of Blood and Bone

13 Nov 2026, 15:50 GMT

Tems is executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood adaptation of Tomi Adeyemi's novel 'Children of Blood and Bone', featuring Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Fireboy DML, BNXN and others. The album is out on 20 November.

Read more

Grammy-winning singer Tems has been named executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone'.

The soundtrack

The announcement was made on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026. The album brings together several of Africa's biggest stars, including Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Fireboy DML, BNXN and Kold AF. Tems described the project as a profound honour and said she was excited for fans to hear what she had put together. The soundtrack is due for release on 20 November, ahead of the film's cinema debut.

The film

'Children of Blood and Bone' is directed by Gina Prince-Bythewood and adapted from the bestselling fantasy novel by Nigerian-American author Tomi Adeyemi.

Background

Tems has become one of the most successful Nigerian artists internationally, winning Grammy Awards and earning an Oscar nomination for co-writing 'Lift Me Up' for 'Black Panther: Wakanda Forever'.

Davido has signed on as the face of PUBG Mobile's new campaign across Africa, taking his brand into the continent's fast-growing gaming market.

The deal

The singer announced the partnership on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, sharing a promotional video in which he tells fans he is joining the battle royale game and that they do not need an expensive phone to play. Speed Darlington apologises to Burna Boy, begs him to drop court charges

Singer Speed Darlington has publicly apologised to Burna Boy and asked him to withdraw the legal charges against him, promising never to insult or mention him again.

Singer Speed Darlington has made a public apology to Burna Boy, appealing to the Grammy-winning star to withdraw the court charges filed against him.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, Speed Darlington, whose real name is Darlington Okoye, admitted that his words may have caused Burna Boy pain and embarrassment. He said he had wrongly assumed that other people shared his own temperament and tolerance, and pledged to stop all comments about the singer, direct or indirect, if the charges are withdrawn. He also promised to stop mentioning Burna Boy's name altogether once the matter is settled.

How the feud began

The dispute escalated in October 2024, when Speed Darlington posted videos questioning Burna Boy's Grammy win for his album 'Twice as Tall' and making disparaging remarks about him. The matter later became a legal case, with Speed Darlington facing charges filed after a complaint from Burna Boy's camp.

How people reacted

The apology drew a wave of reactions online, with many users surprised by Speed Darlington's change of tone. Burna Boy had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

US grounds all departing flights nationwide after telecom outage

14 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The US Federal Aviation Administration ordered a nationwide ground stop on all departing flights on Friday, citing safety. More than 2,000 flights were cancelled and over 10,000 delayed, and officials blamed a telecommunications outage.

Read more

The United States briefly halted all departing flights across the country on Friday, 13 November 2026, causing widespread disruption.

What happened

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) issued a sudden nationwide ground stop at 3.54pm Eastern time, citing extraordinary circumstances and safety, and said all departing traffic should remain on the ground until further notice. It said inbound international flights would be handled case by case. At the time, more than 4,000 flights bound for US destinations were in the air, according to flight-tracking data.

The impact

Within hours, more than 2,000 flights had been cancelled and more than 10,000 delayed, according to flight-tracking figures, and lawmakers demanded an explanation.

The cause

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was no indication so far of anything malicious, and that the problem appeared to be a telecommunications outage. He said President Donald Trump had been briefed.
