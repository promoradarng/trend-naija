"Your approach was inappropriate": South African presidency fires back at Fani-Kayode over livestream complaint

14 Nov 2026, 09:27 GMT

The South African presidency has criticised Nigeria's High Commissioner to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, over his public complaint that his speech was cut off during the ceremony where he presented his letters of credence to President Cyril Ramaphosa.

Fani-Kayode's complaint

Fani-Kayode had raised concerns about the incident, suggesting that he was interrupted while addressing the gathering during the official diplomatic ceremony.

South Africa responds

The South African presidency has rejected his account. Responding to the controversy, President Ramaphosa's spokesperson, Vincent Magwenya, accused Fani-Kayode of misrepresenting the situation and of wrongly dragging the South African President into a matter that did not require his involvement.

Magwenya also described the Nigerian diplomat's handling of the issue as inappropriate.

A routine ceremony turned row

The disagreement has drawn attention to what should have been a routine diplomatic ceremony between two African countries.

Diplomatic engagements are usually guided by established protocols, and disagreements over procedure are generally expected to be handled through official channels rather than in public.

For a newly appointed high commissioner, the public exchange has raised questions about diplomatic conduct, and about how misunderstandings between representatives of friendly nations should be resolved.

Tinubu says water supply went off in the Villa while he was shaving

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

President Tinubu has reportedly said the Presidential Villa also suffers water cuts, recalling that supply stopped while he was shaving last week. He promised to fix the country's infrastructure problems.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has reportedly said that the Presidential Villa in Abuja is not spared from the kind of utility problems many Nigerians face every day.

What he said

Speaking in Lagos on Sunday, 22 November 2026, the president said the water supply at the Villa was cut off while he was shaving last Wednesday, and joked about the inconvenience it caused.

He is said to have promised that his administration would fix the country's infrastructure problems, saying there can be no development without power and water. He insisted that Nigeria has the capacity to overcome its challenges and that the government had only just started.

Background

Poor water supply and unreliable electricity remain major complaints across Nigeria. The president's comments come as campaigns for the January 2027 elections get under way.

The setting

He spoke at a prayer session organised by his family at the Yoruba Tennis Club in Onikan, Lagos, a day after the APC rally in Ibadan, and said the water went off on Wednesday, 18 November. He also urged party supporters in Lagos to step up voter mobilisation and to reconcile.

Reactions

His comments drew mixed reactions online, with many critics mocking the promise to fix the problems, noting that the government has already been in office for several years.

Goodluck Jonathan says dishonest leaders have made Nigeria a laughing stock

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Former President Goodluck Jonathan has reportedly said broken promises by politicians have damaged Nigeria's global image. He called for credible elections and a political culture that rewards competence over money.

Read more

Former President Goodluck Jonathan has reportedly criticised Nigeria's political class, saying dishonest leadership and unkept campaign promises have hurt the country's standing in the world.

Where he spoke

Jonathan gave the keynote address on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the third Paul Alaje Colloquium, themed The Brand: Nigeria, at the NAF Conference Centre in Kado, Abuja.

What he said

He is said to have lamented that Nigeria has lost much of the respect it once enjoyed internationally, and accused office holders of misleading citizens and acting against their public promises. He warned that continued dishonesty in public life would hold back the country's progress.

Jonathan reportedly called for reforms to how leaders are chosen, credible elections and real democracy within parties. He said the country needs a political culture that rewards ideas, character and competence rather than money, patronage and sectional interests.

Background

Jonathan was president from 2010 to 2015. His remarks come as campaigns for the January 2027 elections get under way.

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf says his wife was taken from his home before she went to court

22 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly given more details about the breakdown of his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso. In a radio interview he said her belongings were moved out of his house without his knowledge, and that he was shocked when she filed for divorce in Abuja.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly opened up further about the collapse of his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, a relative of NDC vice-presidential candidate Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso.

What the governor said

Speaking in a Hausa-language interview on Sunday, 22 November 2026, Yusuf is said to have described family matters as extremely difficult. He reportedly said he and Zainab married about 20 years ago and have children together.

According to the reports, the governor said her belongings were moved out of his house and she was taken away from his home without his knowledge, but that he stayed patient and said nothing in public. He said he had even assured their children that their mother would come back, and was shocked to learn that she had gone to court, in Abuja rather than in Kano.

Yusuf reportedly declined to give details of how she left, but said he had witnesses. Asked why he was speaking publicly, he said he was not the one who had made the matter political.

Background

Earlier this week, Zainab Kwankwaso reportedly filed for divorce at an Upper Area Court in Abuja. The governor later told pensioners at Government House in Kano that he had accepted the dissolution of the marriage and still planned to marry again.

The split has drawn wide attention because Kwankwaso was long the governor's political mentor before the two fell out.

The other side

No response from Zainab Kwankwaso or Rabiu Kwankwaso was included in the reports.

Who Zainab is

Zainab is the daughter of Kwankwaso's late elder brother and grew up in Kwankwaso's household. She married Yusuf in 2006, and the couple have six children. The governor reportedly suggested that Kwankwaso was behind the events that led to her leaving.

Aisha Buhari seen in Tinubu-branded outfit, days after claim she backed Atiku

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari wearing attire printed with President Tinubu's campaign logo has sparked debate. It came days after ADC chieftain Solomon Dalung claimed she had told Atiku that Buhari's supporters would back him.

Read more

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari, widow of the late President Muhammadu Buhari, wearing clothing printed with President Bola Tinubu's campaign logo has stirred political debate ahead of the 2027 elections.

The video

The clip was posted on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by former presidential aide Bashir Ahmad, with a caption describing it as a message from Daura, Buhari's hometown.

Why it matters

The video came days after Solomon Dalung, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), claimed in a television interview that Aisha Buhari had visited ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar and told him that the late President's large voter base would be backing him in 2027.

Many social media users took the new video as a response to that claim, suggesting the former First Lady is supporting Tinubu.

No statement

Aisha Buhari herself had not made a public statement on her political preference at the time of the reports.
