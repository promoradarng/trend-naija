VeryDarkMan, Opeyemi Famakin and Peller defend Enioluwa after youth comments backlash

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have spoken in defence of influencer Enioluwa, who apologised after his comments about young Nigerians drew criticism.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

His mother speaks

Enioluwa's mother, Bola Adeoluwa, a university lecturer, has also addressed the backlash after some students on TikTok turned their criticism on her, alleging she was a harsh grader. She responded publicly to the claims and the criticism of her son.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.

Join us in wishing VDM a quick recovery. Have you ever tried to treat malaria yourself before going to hospital?

Suspected insurgents bomb power lines linking Kainji Dam to Jebba

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Suspected Boko Haram insurgents reportedly blew up two electricity transmission towers connecting the Kainji Dam power plant to Jebba in Niger State. No lives were lost, but the attack raised fears over the safety of power infrastructure.

Read more

Suspected Boko Haram insurgents have reportedly bombed two electricity transmission towers connecting the Kainji Dam power station to Jebba in Niger State.

What happened

The explosions reportedly happened at about 2pm on 17 November 2026, between Kanti community in Mashegu Local Government Area and Kanya community in Mokwa Local Government Area. The attackers are said to have targeted the transmission infrastructure, raising concerns that the Kainji Dam power plant itself could be at risk.

No lives were lost, according to the reports, but the blasts caused panic in nearby communities. Security monitoring has continued in the area.

Why it matters

Kainji and Jebba are hydropower stations that feed the national grid.

Background

The incident follows a series of attacks on and vandalism of electricity infrastructure across the country. This month, the House of Representatives resolved to investigate the destruction of 10 transmission towers within five months.

Nigeria begins building $70m passport factory, local production from May 2027

21 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

The federal government has started building a $70 million passport production plant in Abuja. Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo says it will start producing passport booklets locally from May 2027.

Read more

Nigeria has started building a $70 million passport production plant in Abuja, as the federal government moves to end its reliance on foreign suppliers for passport booklets.

The announcement

Minister of Interior Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo announced the project at a groundbreaking ceremony at the headquarters of the Nigeria Immigration Service. He described it as a major step towards a self-sufficient passport system and said local production should begin by May 2027.

The plant

The facility is being built in partnership with IRIS Smart Technologies and will initially produce six million electronic passport booklets a year, with capacity that could later rise to 10 million.

Why it matters

The plant is meant to end Nigeria's reliance on foreign suppliers for passport booklets.
