Toyin Abraham steps in to stop her police escorts roughing up area boys

15 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Nollywood actress Toyin Abraham has drawn attention after a video showed her stepping in to stop her police escorts from roughing up a group of young men on a Lagos street.

What happened

In the footage, which spread on X after the incident on Saturday, 14 November 2026, the actress walks into a crowded scene involving the young men and onlookers and calmly but firmly tells her escorts to back off. The situation quickly calmed down after her intervention.

The context

The encounter reportedly happened during a film production. Groups of area boys, often called agberos, are known to approach film crews shooting in public places in Lagos and demand payment before allowing work to continue, which can lead to tense confrontations.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised the actress for preventing the situation from escalating, while others debated the use of police escorts by celebrities. The police recently opened an investigation after a video showed officers escorting businessman Blord.

Brown Igboegwu welcomes son 16 years into marriage

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Actor Brown Igboegwu has announced the birth of his son, six years after the couple welcomed their daughter following a 10-year wait. He said only God gives children.

Read more

Nollywood actor Brown Igboegwu has announced the birth of a son, weeks after he and his wife marked 16 years of marriage.

The announcement

Sharing a photo of himself at the hospital on Instagram, the actor said the birth was proof that only God gives children, and said no prophet, pastor or man of God could take credit.

A long wait

He recalled that the couple waited 10 years for their first child, a daughter, and visited several churches and prayer houses during that time. He said some people took advantage of their situation, using their videos and testimonies to promote their churches.

Six years after their daughter's birth, they have now welcomed a son. He described the last nine months as the toughest of his 50 years.

His message

He urged his followers to hold on to God and trust that their prayers will be answered.

A video of Nollywood veteran Richard Mofe-Damijo, popularly known as RMD, appearing to ignore a man who tried to take a selfie with him has gone viral.

The footage, which began circulating on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, was recorded at a conference at the Muson Centre in Lagos. Inside the hall, RMD appeared relaxed, laughing and chatting warmly with the same man.

But once they stepped outside, the man reached out for a selfie and the actor turned away and busied himself with something else. Comedian Ali Baba, who was nearby, had stepped back to give the man space for the photo, which made the moment more noticeable.

How people reacted

The clip quickly spread online and sparked debate.

No comment yet

RMD had not publicly commented on the video at the time of the reports.
