Court sacks ADC's David Mark-led leadership, voids Atiku's presidential nomination

5 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

A Federal High Court in Abuja has removed the David Mark-led leadership of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) and voided its decisions, including the nomination of former vice-president Atiku Abubakar as the party's 2027 presidential candidate, according to reports on Thursday, 5 November 2026.

The judgment

Justice Peter Lifu reportedly delivered the judgment in a suit filed by Nafiu Bala Gombe, a former national deputy chairman of the ADC for the North-East. The court ruled against the caretaker committee led by former Senate President David Mark and set aside all decisions it took on behalf of the party, which would include the primary that produced Atiku as candidate. Full details of the court's reasoning and any orders to INEC were still emerging.

Timing

The ruling came less than 24 hours after Atiku formally launched his presidential campaign at a rally in Sokoto, which his campaign said drew a large crowd.

Background

The ADC became the platform of a coalition of opposition figures in 2025, with David Mark as interim national chairman. Its leadership has faced legal challenges from former officials who say they were wrongly pushed aside. Several court cases over party primaries are ongoing across all major parties, and courts have recently sacked candidates in Nasarawa and ordered changes elsewhere.

Atiku responds

In a statement issued from Birnin Kebbi on Thursday through his campaign spokesman, Phrank Shaibu, Atiku condemned the ruling and described it as a deliberate attempt to derail his campaign after the large turnout at his Sokoto launch. His campaign pointed to an earlier Federal High Court ruling that had dismissed a similar challenge to the David Mark-led leadership, and said the judgment would be challenged at the Court of Appeal. It said the campaign would continue, moving on to Kebbi State.

Party and government react

The ADC's national publicity secretary, Bolaji Abdullahi, described the judgment as legally indefensible and politically motivated, and said its timing, straight after the Sokoto rally, raised serious concerns. The party cited a ruling by another Federal High Court judge on 2 July 2026 that affirmed the David Mark-led leadership, and warned the APC against using the courts to eliminate competition. Aviation Minister Festus Keyamo welcomed the judgment, saying justice may be slow but is always sure, and rejected claims that it threatens democracy.

What the judge ruled

According to further reports, Justice Lifu held that Mark's emergence as ADC chairman violated the party's constitution, the Electoral Act and the 1999 Constitution. The court also struck down the appointment of former Interior Minister Rauf Aregbesola as national secretary, along with other national officers and candidates the party had presented for 2027. Nafiu Bala Gombe, who filed the suit and leads a rival faction, praised the court for handling a politically sensitive case without outside influence.

Atiku vows to stay in the race

On Friday, 6 November, Atiku declared that he would not step back from the 2027 presidential contest. He accused the Tinubu administration of using political pressure and court cases to weaken opposition parties, and alleged that there were attempts to compromise the voter register with AI-generated photographs and false identities, warning that Nigeria risked drifting towards a one-party system. He also criticised the government's handling of fuel subsidy removal, the fall of the naira and rising food prices. Separately, Shehu Sani, the APC senatorial candidate in Kaduna, advised the ADC to concentrate on appealing the ruling, saying the party was paying the price for taking over an existing, troubled party instead of registering a new one.

INEC disowns fake poster

Also on Friday, INEC said a poster circulating on social media, which claimed the commission had reaffirmed the David Mark-led leadership of the ADC, was fake and did not come from it. The commission condemned the unauthorised use of its logo and of a photograph of National Commissioner Mohammed Haruna, and urged the public to rely only on its official channels.

ADC files appeal

Later on Friday, the ADC said it had filed an appeal at the Court of Appeal against the judgment, together with an application for a stay of execution to halt any action based on it while the appeal is heard. The party said INEC was formally notified of both filings on 6 November. In a statement by Phrank Shaibu, the party urged the commission not to take any action on the judgment until the stay application is decided, and asked its members and supporters to remain calm.

What happens next

The ADC and Atiku are widely expected to appeal. Until the appeal is decided, it is not clear how the ruling will affect his candidacy or the party's campaign.

  • The court voided all decisions of the Mark-led caretaker committee.
  • That includes Atiku's nomination as presidential candidate.
  • An appeal is widely expected.

APGA endorses Tinubu for 2027 as Soludo targets half of Anambra votes

6 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

The All Progressives Grand Alliance has formally adopted President Tinubu as its preferred presidential candidate for 2027 and will not field its own. Governor Charles Soludo pledged to deliver half of Anambra's votes to the President.

Read more

The All Progressives Grand Alliance (APGA) has formally endorsed President Bola Tinubu of the All Progressives Congress (APC) as its preferred candidate for the 2027 presidential election.

What was announced

Anambra State governor Charles Soludo, the party's national leader, announced the decision in Awka on Friday, 6 November 2026, at the inauguration of APGA's national campaign for the 2027 elections, according to reports. He said the party would not field its own presidential candidate. Soludo argued that Tinubu had shown genuine care for the Igbo people, particularly people from Anambra, pointing out that the President appointed several Anambra indigenes to key positions in Lagos when he was governor of that state.

Target of 50%

Soludo acknowledged that Tinubu received only about 5,000 votes in Anambra in the 2023 presidential election, but said the President had still moved to address problems in the state and the wider South-East. He pledged to deliver half of Anambra's votes to Tinubu in 2027, describing the endorsement as a strategic move to strengthen the state's bargaining power with the federal government. He also said APGA had backed the ruling party's presidential candidate in past elections, so the decision continued that pattern.

Background

APGA is the governing party in Anambra, which is also the home state of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi, a former APGA governor who won the state by a wide margin in 2023. Soludo has previously described Obi as a close family friend while declaring support for Tinubu, and he has been named a state coordinator in the President's expanded campaign council. This week the NDC's Anambra chapter set a target of 98 per cent of the state's votes for Obi.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027, and APGA is expected to campaign for the President alongside its own candidates for other offices.

Tinubu approves Ibom Deep Seaport request during Akwa Ibom campaign, promises jobs and economic development

6 Nov 2026, 20:21 GMT

At his campaign rally in Uyo, President Bola Tinubu announced on the spot that he had approved a request from Akwa Ibom's leadership for international export status for the Ibom Deep Seaport. He also promised jobs, skills development and investment in the state's palm economy.

Read more

President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has approved a request linked to the long-awaited Ibom Deep Seaport, announcing the decision while addressing supporters at his presidential campaign rally in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State.

"I look, and I approve it"

Speaking from the podium, flanked by party leaders and security officials, Tinubu told the crowd that the state's leadership had brought a request to him.

"Now your leader makes a request, for international export status. I look, and I approve it," he said, to cheers from supporters.

The Ibom Deep Seaport has been one of Akwa Ibom's biggest ambitions for years, with successive state governments pushing for it as a way to open the state to international trade and create jobs.

Promises of jobs and skills

Tinubu also used the occasion to make several promises about the state's economic future.

"I promise you skill development. I promise you jobs," he said. "We are going to plant a lot of economic trees."

He pointed to the palm tree, a major part of the region's economy, as an opportunity. "There is no reason why we cannot use the value of palm trees and the palm here," he said, adding that there was no reason the country could not create skills development that is "everlasting".

"Development must be even"

The President said growth must reach every part of the country. "Development must be even, inclusive and reasonable. It must bring progress and create economic value," he said.

What it means

If the approval leads to real progress on the seaport, it could be a major boost for Akwa Ibom's economy, bringing shipping, logistics and related businesses to the state.

But campaign promises are easy to make at rallies. Nigerians will be watching to see whether the approval is followed by funding, construction and the jobs that have been promised.

Lagos Trade Fair erupts as Peter Obi attends funeral of popular market leader Chief Okey Ezibe (Omeluora)

6 Nov 2026, 18:48 GMT

Peter Obi drew a huge, cheering crowd at the Trade Fair International Market in Lagos as he came to pay his last respects to the late Chief Sir Okey Ezibe, popularly known as Omeluora, regarded as one of the founding fathers of Balogun Market.

Read more

It was an emotional scene at the Trade Fair International Market in Lagos as former Anambra State Governor Peter Obi, the NDC presidential candidate and Labour Party candidate in the 2023 election, arrived to pay his last respects to the late Chief Sir Okey Ezibe, popularly known as Omeluora.

Chief Ezibe, widely regarded as one of the founding fathers of the famous Balogun Market in Lagos, was honoured by traders, associates, friends and well-wishers who gathered to celebrate his life and his contribution to commerce.

"My leader! My leader!"

Something remarkable happened when Obi arrived. The atmosphere suddenly changed. Traders stepped away from their businesses, and a large crowd gathered around him, cheering and calling out, "My leader! My leader!"

Many raised their phones to capture the moment, while members of his team, wearing T-shirts printed with "Rest in Peace Omeluora", waved to the crowd from his car.

For those watching, the reception was another reminder of the strong support Obi continues to enjoy among sections of the trading community, particularly in Lagos. He had come for a solemn occasion, yet his presence drew the kind of attention usually seen at major political gatherings.

Who was Chief Okey Ezibe (Omeluora)?

To many traders, the late Chief Sir Okey Ezibe was more than a successful businessman. He was a respected figure whose contributions helped shape the business community around Balogun Market, one of Nigeria's busiest commercial centres.

For years, Balogun Market has provided opportunities for thousands of traders, importers, wholesalers and small business owners. People like Omeluora are remembered for building business networks, encouraging younger entrepreneurs and strengthening the trading community. His passing marks the loss of a respected figure in that community.

Obi's reception gets people talking

Despite the disagreements and controversies that surround Nigerian politics, Obi still draws enthusiastic crowds in parts of the country, and the scenes at Trade Fair appeared to reflect that connection.

Some will see the reception as a sign of his political popularity ahead of the 2027 general election. Others may simply see it as a warm welcome for a familiar public figure attending the funeral of a respected businessman.

Whatever the interpretation, the excitement around his arrival became one of the memorable moments of the day.

Remembering Omeluora

Beyond the cheers and the political talk, the gathering was ultimately about remembering Chief Okey Ezibe and the legacy he left behind.

A man can build wealth, businesses and influence in his lifetime, but what people say about him after his death often tells another story. For Omeluora, the respect shown by the trading community speaks to the place he held in their lives.

Rest in peace, Omeluora. A great iroko has fallen. His name will continue to be remembered among those who helped commerce grow in Lagos.

Shettima says Peter Obi has run himself out of the 2027 race

6 Nov 2026, 17:43 GMT

Vice-President Kashim Shettima told an APC rally in Uyo that NDC candidate Peter Obi had destroyed his own chances in 2027, mocking his comparisons with Bangladesh. The rally followed President Tinubu's inauguration of projects in Akwa Ibom.

Read more

Vice-President Kashim Shettima has said that Peter Obi, presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has ruined his own chances in the 2027 election.

What he said

Speaking at an All Progressives Congress (APC) campaign rally in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, Shettima reportedly said Obi had run himself out of the race through what he called lies and bogus statistics. He suggested that Obi had recently travelled abroad quietly before returning to Nigeria, and that he had thrown away the goodwill he once enjoyed among voters. The vice-president also mocked Obi's frequent comparisons between Nigeria and Bangladesh, saying a shirt made for one country cannot fit another, and dismissed Obi's claim that he won the 2023 presidential election.

Tinubu in Akwa Ibom

Before the rally, President Bola Tinubu inaugurated several projects built by Governor Umo Eno's administration, including the new terminal building at Victor Attah International Airport in Uyo. Tinubu described the visit as a homecoming, praised Eno as a visionary leader, and said the airport's international flight status was the result of the governor's commitment to the state. The President also took a swipe at Obi at the event over erosion in Anambra State.

Background

Obi, a former Anambra governor, came third in the 2023 presidential election and challenged the result in court, insisting he won. He is running again in 2027 with Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027. Obi's campaign had not publicly responded to Shettima's remarks at the time of writing.
