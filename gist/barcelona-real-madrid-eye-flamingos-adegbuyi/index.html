Barcelona and Real Madrid reportedly eye Flamingos star Oluwakemi Adegbuyi

12 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Spanish giants Barcelona and Real Madrid are reportedly interested in Nigerian teenager Oluwakemi Adegbuyi after her performances for the Flamingos at the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.

Her tournament

The 16-year-old forward played in all seven of Nigeria's matches in Morocco, scoring two goals and providing one assist as the Flamingos became the first African team to win the competition. Scouts from both clubs are said to see her as a candidate for an early move to European football, according to reports on Thursday, 12 November 2026. Neither club has commented.

Her rise

Adegbuyi plays for FC Robo Queens in the Nigeria Women Football League and was named the league's Player of the Season for 2025/26 after scoring 11 goals. She was also named Player of the Tournament at the WAFU B Girls' Cup and at the WAFU B Champions League qualifiers, where she played for Edo Queens.

Background

If a move to Barcelona happens, she would follow Super Falcons legend Asisat Oshoala. The Flamingos have been showered with rewards since their win, including ₦100 million savings certificates for each player from President Tinubu.

Ex-Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami survives attack at his Abeokuta home

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Former Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami says he fought off three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta in the early hours of Friday. Police say he has been treated and discharged from hospital.

Read more

Former Super Eagles captain and football administrator Segun Odegbami has survived an attack by three armed men who broke into his home in Abeokuta, Ogun State, in the early hours of Friday, 20 November 2026.

What happened

The attackers reportedly overpowered his domestic staff and driver before forcing their way into his bedroom at about 2:30am. The 74-year-old was injured in the attack.

Odegbami described the incident as an attempt on his life. He said he fought back with help from his driver and house help, and the attackers eventually fled. He said it was the closest he had ever come to death.

Police response

The Ogun State Police Command confirmed the attack and said the former international had been treated in hospital and discharged.

Who he is

Odegbami is a former captain of the Super Eagles and one of Nigeria's best-known football figures.

FIFA President Infantino arrives in Warri for stadium commissioning

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has arrived in Warri, Delta State, for the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium. He was received by Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former NFF president Amaju Pinnick.

Read more

FIFA President Gianni Infantino arrived in Warri, Delta State, on Friday, 20 November 2026, ahead of the commissioning of the rebuilt Warri City Stadium.

The arrival

Infantino landed at Osubi Airport on a private jet and was received by Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former Nigeria Football Federation president Amaju Pinnick. He said the warm welcome showed the unifying power of football.

Third visit

It is Infantino's third visit to Nigeria. He first came in 2016 shortly after taking office, and returned in 2023 for the Aisha Buhari Cup.

Background

The visit comes days after FIFA appointed a normalisation committee to run the NFF, and as Infantino prepares to face challengers in the FIFA presidential election in March 2027.

Many feared dead as boat carrying over 300 passengers capsizes in Bayelsa

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A boat reportedly carrying more than 300 passengers to a burial ceremony has capsized in Southern Ijaw, Bayelsa State. Police say marine units have been deployed for rescue, but casualty figures are not yet known.

Read more

A boat said to be carrying more than 300 passengers has capsized in Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, with many people feared dead or missing.

What happened

The boat was travelling from Ayama community to Ukubie for a burial ceremony when it capsized at about noon on Friday, 20 November 2026, shortly after passing Oweikorogha community.

The exact number of passengers on board and the number of casualties had not been established at the time of the reports, but residents fear several people may have lost their lives.

Rescue efforts

The Bayelsa State Police Command spokesperson, DSP Musa Muhammad, confirmed the incident and said the Commissioner of Police had deployed the Marine Police Unit and the local divisional police officer for rescue operations. He said further details on casualties and missing persons would be released.

A former state chairman of the Maritime Workers Union also confirmed the incident.

What happens next

Rescue and recovery efforts are continuing, and official casualty figures are expected once they are confirmed.

Oyedepo says church leadership should not be handed to children like a family business

20 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A video of Bishop David Oyedepo saying church succession should be a spiritual process, not a family arrangement, has gone viral amid the Fountain of Life leadership crisis.

Read more

A video of Bishop David Oyedepo speaking about church succession has gone viral, drawing attention amid the leadership dispute at The Fountain of Life Church involving Pastor Jimmy Odukoya.

What he said

In the clip, posted on Instagram on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, the founder of Living Faith Church Worldwide (Winners' Chapel) said planning for who comes next is one of the most important responsibilities of church leadership.

He said leadership transition in ministry is a spiritual process and not a family arrangement, warning against simply handing churches over to children. He pointed to Joshua succeeding Moses in the Bible as the model.

Why it is trending

The video spread widely as Nigerians debated the Fountain of Life crisis, where trustees say the late founder Taiwo Odukoya wanted another pastor to succeed him, while his son Jimmy insists his removal as senior pastor is invalid.
