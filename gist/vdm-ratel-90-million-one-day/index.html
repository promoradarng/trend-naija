VeryDarkMan's RATEL brand reportedly records ₦90 million in sales in one day

31 Oct 2026, 23:17 GMT

VeryDarkMan (VDM) and his RATEL brand have reportedly recorded about ₦90 million in sales in just one day, a figure that has set social media talking about how far the activist-turned-businessman could go.

The claim followed a post showing VDM in RATEL Power branded safety gear, holding up his phone, alongside a screenshot of bank alerts. The alerts, all dated 31 October 2026, show several customer payments for RATEL products, including a 600-watt power station, with the account balance climbing above ₦83 million.

From critic to businessman

VDM first became widely known for his social media videos and for publicly challenging powerful figures. Over time he has moved into importing products from China, building his own brand and steadily establishing himself in business. RATEL now sells items ranging from power stations to sensor lights.

A marketing style built on openness

What stands out most is how he markets the brand. Rather than relying on traditional adverts, he brings his audience along with him. He shows them the products, explains how they work and tests them publicly, so followers are watching the business grow in real time.

Whether people like him or not, few can deny the attention he has drawn to RATEL.

Sales are not profit

It is worth remembering that ₦90 million in sales is not ₦90 million in profit. Running a business comes with stock costs, shipping, staff, risk and plenty of other challenges, and the figure itself has not been independently confirmed.

Still, if RATEL keeps up this momentum, many observers believe it could grow into a multibillion-naira business group. Ten years from now, people may look back at these early videos and remember exactly how it all started.

Why it matters

Nigeria needs more young people who can turn online influence into productive businesses that create jobs and last. VDM's journey, with its struggles, criticism, mistakes and determination, is playing out in full public view.

So the question is simple: do you believe RATEL can become one of Nigeria's biggest indigenous brands in the next five years?

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has continued his public exchange with singer Zlatan Ibile, daring him to openly deny claims that he secretly sent celebrities to plead with VDM over their ongoing disagreement.

"Just come out and say it's a lie"

Speaking partly in Pidgin, VDM said, in effect: "It's been over 24 hours since I dared Zlatan to come out and say I'm lying that he sent people to beg me. He acts like a hard guy in public, but does something else behind the scenes."

"Just come out and say it's a lie, simple! Let me call out the names of the celebrities you sent. If they deny it too, then you'll see what my next post will be."

Tough in public, soft in private?

VDM claims Zlatan is acting tough in public while making moves behind the scenes to settle the matter, and has threatened to name the celebrities he says were sent if Zlatan stays silent.

The feud began after a video of Davido singing a line from Zlatan's song INFERNO at Zlatan's wedding welcome party in Morocco, a lyric VDM believes was aimed at him. VDM then warned Zlatan that he would "learn the hard way".

Allegations, not facts

For now, these remain VeryDarkMan's claims. Zlatan has not responded publicly, and his silence does not confirm them.

9 Nov 2026, 00:56 GMT

A group of suspected thieves in South Africa has become the talk of social media after a video showed them lying face down in a line by the roadside, each with his face pressed against the backside of the man in front. Reactions online are split between laughter and concern.

A group of suspected thieves in South Africa has become the talk of social media after they were reportedly caught and subjected to an unusual form of punishment.

The viral video

The suspects were made to lie face down on the ground in a single line by the side of a road, with each person's face pressed against the backside of the man in front of him. Their hands appear to be tied behind them.

Reports say they were caught by locals, although a uniformed officer can also be seen standing beside the men in the footage, so it is not clear whether the police or members of the public arranged them that way.

Laughter and concern

The incident has sparked mixed reactions online. Many people found the punishment hilarious, joking that South Africans seem to have invented a brand-new style of discipline.

Others were less amused, questioning whether suspects should be humiliated in public before any court has found them guilty, and whether members of the public should ever take the law into their own hands.

What we don't know

The circumstances of the incident, including where and when it happened and what the men are accused of stealing, have not been independently verified.

Amnesty International condemns Zamfara killings, says burning of 12 family members alive exposes failure of Nigerian authorities

8 Nov 2026, 21:28 GMT

Amnesty International has condemned the reported killing of 12 members of one family in Zamfara State, who were said to have been burned alive, calling it another sign of the authorities' failure to protect communities from armed attacks.

Read more

Amnesty International has strongly condemned the reported killing of 12 members of a family in Zamfara State, describing the horrifying incident as another painful reminder of the Nigerian authorities' failure to protect innocent citizens from violent attacks.

An entire family wiped out

Imagine an entire family wiped out in such a cruel way. Twelve human beings, reportedly burned alive. People who had their own dreams, responsibilities and hopes for tomorrow, only to have their lives cut short in circumstances no family should ever experience.

The tragedy has once again raised serious questions about the worsening security situation in parts of northern Nigeria, where many families live under the constant threat of armed attacks, kidnappings and killings.

A failure to protect

For Amnesty International, the incident reflects a much bigger problem: the continued inability of the authorities to guarantee the safety of communities that have suffered attack after attack.

For years, residents of Zamfara and neighbouring states have endured violence from armed groups. Villages have been raided, homes destroyed, residents abducted and families forced to abandon communities where they have lived for generations.

Despite repeated assurances from government officials and security agencies, reports of fresh atrocities keep emerging.

Every citizen deserves protection

The Nigerian government has a constitutional duty to protect lives and property. That duty extends to every citizen, whether they live in Abuja, Lagos, Zamfara or a remote village far from the attention of the national media.

The victims deserve justice, and their surviving relatives deserve more than another round of official condolences. There must be a thorough investigation, those responsible must be brought to justice, and the authorities must take meaningful steps to prevent similar tragedies.

A country cannot claim to be making progress when families are being burned alive in their homes and communities are left wondering who will be attacked next.

Over to you

How many more innocent lives must be lost before protecting ordinary citizens becomes the government's most urgent priority?

Okowa says he left Atiku for Tinubu because he has 'seen the light'

8 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Former Delta governor Ifeanyi Okowa, Atiku Abubakar's running mate in 2023, told an APC rally in Port Harcourt that he switched to backing President Tinubu because he has seen the light, crediting the government's fiscal reforms.

Read more

Former Delta State governor Ifeanyi Okowa has explained why he abandoned former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar to support President Bola Tinubu ahead of the 2027 election.

What he said

Speaking at an All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign rally in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, on Saturday, 7 November 2026, Okowa reportedly said he had 'seen the light'. He argued that Tinubu inherited a country with foundations weakened by decades of mismanagement, and that the current administration was making tough but necessary decisions to fix them. He credited the government's fiscal reforms with increasing the revenue shared with states and local governments, saying the results could be seen in better project delivery and salary payments. He also compared Tinubu's record as Lagos governor with his performance as President, saying the same transformation was now happening across the country.

Background

Okowa was Atiku's vice-presidential running mate on the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) ticket in the 2023 election, which Tinubu won. He later left the PDP for the APC, following his successor in Delta, Governor Sheriff Oborevwori. Atiku is now the candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), although a Federal High Court ruling last week voided his nomination, a decision the party has appealed. At the same rally, Tinubu questioned how Atiku built a university while he was vice-president, prompting a sharp reply from Atiku's campaign.

What happens next

Atiku's campaign had not publicly responded to Okowa's remarks at the time of writing. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
