VeryDarkMan's RATEL brand reportedly records ₦90 million in sales in one day

31 Oct 2026, 23:17 GMT

VeryDarkMan (VDM) and his RATEL brand have reportedly recorded about ₦90 million in sales in just one day, a figure that has set social media talking about how far the activist-turned-businessman could go.

The claim followed a post showing VDM in RATEL Power branded safety gear, holding up his phone, alongside a screenshot of bank alerts. The alerts, all dated 31 October 2026, show several customer payments for RATEL products, including a 600-watt power station, with the account balance climbing above ₦83 million.

From critic to businessman

VDM first became widely known for his social media videos and for publicly challenging powerful figures. Over time he has moved into importing products from China, building his own brand and steadily establishing himself in business. RATEL now sells items ranging from power stations to sensor lights.

A marketing style built on openness

What stands out most is how he markets the brand. Rather than relying on traditional adverts, he brings his audience along with him. He shows them the products, explains how they work and tests them publicly, so followers are watching the business grow in real time.

Whether people like him or not, few can deny the attention he has drawn to RATEL.

Sales are not profit

It is worth remembering that ₦90 million in sales is not ₦90 million in profit. Running a business comes with stock costs, shipping, staff, risk and plenty of other challenges, and the figure itself has not been independently confirmed.

Still, if RATEL keeps up this momentum, many observers believe it could grow into a multibillion-naira business group. Ten years from now, people may look back at these early videos and remember exactly how it all started.

Why it matters

Nigeria needs more young people who can turn online influence into productive businesses that create jobs and last. VDM's journey, with its struggles, criticism, mistakes and determination, is playing out in full public view.

So the question is simple: do you believe RATEL can become one of Nigeria's biggest indigenous brands in the next five years?

Fashion entrepreneur Veekee James is at the centre of an online debate after she reportedly withdrew a ₦4 million scholarship from one recipient and gave it to another during a live event.

What happened

The incident took place on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, at the graduation ceremony of fashion designer Prudent Gabriel's fashion school. James attended with her husband, Femi Atere. VeryDarkMan warns Tinubu over deadly attack on NDC campaign in Delta

24 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has issued a strong warning to President Tinubu after gunmen killed four people, including NDC youth leader Efemena Umukoro, at Omo-Agege's campaign event in Delta. He said political violence could spiral out of control.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has issued a fierce public warning to President Bola Tinubu following the deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North, Delta State.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram, VDM told the president that he would burn Nigeria down, arguing that targeting opposition figures with gunfire is a dangerous political strategy that could spiral far beyond anyone's control. He warned that even people close to the president would not be safe if the cycle of violence continued, and drew attention to the fact that the attack happened in Delta State.

The attack

Gunmen opened fire on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege's campaign team on Monday, 23 November 2026, while he was addressing supporters. Four people were killed, according to his campaign, including Efemena Umukoro, an Urhobo youth leader popularly known as General Akima. Omo-Agege was unhurt.

Other reactions

Other social media commentators also condemned the killings, and Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori called for everyone involved to be arrested regardless of their party. The Inspector-General of Police has ordered an investigation.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to VDM's remarks was included in the reports.

Seyi Law admits his finances were better before Tinubu, but backs reforms

25 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Comedian Seyi Law has reportedly admitted that living standards have dropped under President Tinubu and that his own finances were better before 2023. He still defended the government's reforms, saying good policies take years to stabilise.

Read more

Comedian Seyi Law, a known supporter of President Bola Tinubu, has reportedly admitted that living standards have fallen sharply under the current administration.

What he said

Speaking on a podcast hosted by Nedu, Seyi Law is said to have acknowledged that his personal finances were much better before Tinubu took office in 2023, and that it would be hypocritical to say Nigerians are not suffering.

Still backing the reforms

He nevertheless defended the government's economic reforms, arguing that Nigerians would benefit in the long run. Comparing Nigeria with China, he said major national transformations often come with heavy early sacrifices. When the host asked whether that meant Nigerians must suffer greatly first, he said people must feel the pain of a policy before reaping its benefits, adding that good policies usually stabilise after 10 to 12 years.

How people reacted

His remarks have sparked debate online.
