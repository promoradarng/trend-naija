Content creator De Peters questions how VeryDarkMan presents his power station business

31 Oct 2026, 17:44 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) is facing fresh criticism over his power station business, this time from content creator De Peters.

What De Peters said

In comments reported on Saturday, 31 October 2026, De Peters reportedly asked why VDM was acting as though he was giving Nigerians free power stations, when he was in fact selling them to make money. He argued that VDM often criticises other influencers for promoting cryptocurrency, betting platforms and other products, yet was now promoting his own goods. He also said other businesses already sell power stations in Nigeria, some with similar capacity and specifications at lower prices.

Background

VDM has spent recent weeks promoting his Ratel power stations, imported from China, with videos from a warehouse and demonstrations of the units. He has said buyers will be limited to two units each and has clashed with businessman Blord, whom he accuses of copying the idea. Other commentators have praised VDM for bringing more affordable solar options to Nigerians, with one claiming that prices of power stations have fallen since the Ratel brand launched. The debate comes as federal agencies warn celebrities they will be held responsible for promoting fraudulent products, although there is no suggestion that VDM's products are fraudulent.

How people reacted

The comments sparked a wider conversation about influencer marketing. Some users agreed that VDM should not present a business as charity, while his supporters said he was offering a useful product at a fair price. VDM had not publicly responded at the time of the report.

  • De Peters says VDM is selling, not giving away, power stations.
  • He accuses VDM of criticising others' promotions while advertising his own.
  • VDM had not responded at the time of reporting.

Ronaldinho set for competitive comeback at 46 with Italian club Ravenna

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Brazilian legend Ronaldinho is set to start for Serie C club Ravenna against Forli on Sunday, his first competitive match in more than a decade, the club's manager has confirmed.

Read more

Former Barcelona, PSG and AC Milan star Ronaldinho is set to return to competitive football at the age of 46.

The comeback

Ravenna manager Andrea Mandorlini confirmed on Friday, 13 November 2026, that the Brazilian will start for the Italian Serie C club in a local derby against Forli on Sunday, 15 November, as one of three forwards. Mandorlini described him as one of the greatest players of the modern era, adding that the coaching staff would decide together how long he plays.

A long wait

Ronaldinho joined Ravenna as an investor in August. His last competitive appearance was for Brazilian club Fluminense in September 2015, making Sunday's match his first in more than ten years.

Background

Ronaldinho won the Ballon d'Or in 2005 and the World Cup with Brazil in 2002, and is remembered as one of the most gifted and entertaining players of his generation.

Portugal retires Ronaldo's number 7 shirt design after end of his international career

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Portugal's football federation has permanently changed the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt in honour of Cristiano Ronaldo, whose international career has ended, and players will all wear 7 during their walk-out against Denmark.

Read more

Portugal's football federation has honoured Cristiano Ronaldo by permanently changing the design of the number 7 on the national team shirt.

The tribute

The Portuguese Football Federation announced on X that the style of number 7 worn by Ronaldo will not be used by any other player, and a new version will appear on future shirts. According to reports on Saturday, 14 November 2026, every Portugal player will wear a number 7 shirt during the walk-out before their Nations League match against Denmark as a collective farewell.

How it ended

Ronaldo's international career reportedly came to an end after a public disagreement with Jorge Jesus over a substitution during a match in September.

Background

Ronaldo is Portugal's all-time record goalscorer and most-capped player and led the country to the Euro 2016 title. The gesture mirrors the honour Argentina gave Lionel Messi after his international retirement.

Kano drug task force seals pharmaceutical company after major pregabalin seizure

14 Nov 2026, 15:45 GMT

The Kano State Multi-Agency Task Force on Drug Abuse has sealed a pharmaceutical company after recovering about 3.18 million capsules suspected to be pregabalin, worth more than ₦900 million. Allegations of regulatory failures are still under investigation.

Read more

The Kano State Multi-Agency Task Force on Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking has sealed a pharmaceutical company following the earlier recovery of about 3.18 million capsules suspected to be pregabalin.

A ₦900 million haul

The task force estimates the value of the seized stock at more than ₦900 million. Photos of the seizure show cartons packed with boxes and blister packs of red-and-white capsules.

Pregabalin is a prescription medicine used to treat nerve pain and some other conditions. It is widely misused in parts of northern Nigeria, where it is taken in large doses for its sedating effects, and is a growing concern for health and security authorities.

Questions for the regulators

The task force has also raised allegations of possible regulatory failures that may have allowed such a large quantity of the drug to circulate.

The Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN) and the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) had not responded to the allegations at the time of reporting.

What is confirmed

The sealing of the company is confirmed. The allegations against the company and against regulatory officials remain under investigation and should not be treated as established facts.
