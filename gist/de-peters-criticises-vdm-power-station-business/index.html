Content creator De Peters questions how VeryDarkMan presents his power station business

31 Oct 2026, 17:44 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) is facing fresh criticism over his power station business, this time from content creator De Peters.

What De Peters said

In comments reported on Saturday, 31 October 2026, De Peters reportedly asked why VDM was acting as though he was giving Nigerians free power stations, when he was in fact selling them to make money. He argued that VDM often criticises other influencers for promoting cryptocurrency, betting platforms and other products, yet was now promoting his own goods. He also said other businesses already sell power stations in Nigeria, some with similar capacity and specifications at lower prices.

Background

VDM has spent recent weeks promoting his Ratel power stations, imported from China, with videos from a warehouse and demonstrations of the units. He has said buyers will be limited to two units each and has clashed with businessman Blord, whom he accuses of copying the idea. Other commentators have praised VDM for bringing more affordable solar options to Nigerians, with one claiming that prices of power stations have fallen since the Ratel brand launched. The debate comes as federal agencies warn celebrities they will be held responsible for promoting fraudulent products, although there is no suggestion that VDM's products are fraudulent.

How people reacted

The comments sparked a wider conversation about influencer marketing. Some users agreed that VDM should not present a business as charity, while his supporters said he was offering a useful product at a fair price. VDM had not publicly responded at the time of the report.

  • De Peters says VDM is selling, not giving away, power stations.
  • He accuses VDM of criticising others' promotions while advertising his own.
  • VDM had not responded at the time of reporting.

Tinubu promises ₦100m and a house to each family of NAF plane crash victims

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu has pledged ₦100 million and a three-bedroom flat to every family that lost a loved one in the NAF 931 aircraft crash in Ondo. He spoke at a memorial ceremony in Abuja.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has announced a support package of ₦100 million and a three-bedroom flat for each family that lost a loved one in the crash of the Nigerian Air Force aircraft NAF 931 in Ondo State.

The pledge

The president made the announcement at a memorial and wreath-laying ceremony in Abuja held in honour of the victims. The package covers the families of both the military personnel and the civilians who died in the crash.

Military support

Tinubu also assured the armed forces that the government would provide better training and equipment to improve the performance and readiness of personnel.

Opposition parties confirm summit in Abuja on 30 November

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Coalition for Democracy says the second summit of Nigeria's opposition parties will go ahead on Monday, 30 November, at the NAF Conference Centre in Abuja. The meeting is expected to adopt three key documents.

Read more

The Coalition for Democracy has confirmed that the second summit of Nigeria's opposition political parties will hold on Monday, 30 November 2026, at the NAF Conference Centre in Abuja.

The details

Proceedings will begin at 10am, with accreditation opening at 9am. The confirmation followed the seventh meeting of the coalition's central leadership committee on Wednesday, 26 November. The statement was signed on behalf of the coalition parties by Salihu Lukman.

What to expect

The summit is expected to adopt three key documents and deliberate on democratic renewal, party unity and electoral integrity ahead of the 2027 elections.

Background

The summit was earlier moved to 30 November. Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde said this week that he was open to working with Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu.

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar, the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate, has reportedly criticised President Bola Tinubu's government over the rising cost of energy.

In a statement issued on Thursday, 26 November 2026, by his director of strategic communication, Atiku is said to have argued that the government had made Nigeria too costly to produce in, too costly to build in and too expensive for ordinary families to live in.

Atiku's campaign has issued a series of attacks on the government's economic record in recent days, including over a drop in oil revenue.

Benin photographer Sh0tbySav reportedly dies after 'one-chance' attack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A photographer known as Sh0tbySav has reportedly died in Benin City after being robbed and pushed out of a moving vehicle by suspected 'one-chance' criminals. Fellow photographers are mourning her.

Read more

The photography community in Benin City, Edo State, is mourning after reports that a photographer known as Sh0tbySav died following a suspected 'one-chance' robbery.

What reportedly happened

According to accounts shared on social media, she was returning home after a photoshoot for a client when she boarded a commercial vehicle believed to have been operated by criminals. She was reportedly robbed of her belongings and pushed out of the moving vehicle.

Details of the incident remain limited, and no police statement was included in the reports.

A warning

Her death has drawn fresh attention to the dangers posed by 'one-chance' gangs, criminals who pose as commercial drivers and passengers to rob unsuspecting commuters, a recurring problem in several Nigerian cities.

Tributes

Fellow photographers have shared tributes online.
