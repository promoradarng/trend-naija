Kate Henshaw slams power minister Joseph Tegbe over electricity crisis

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Veteran Nollywood actress Kate Henshaw has joined the growing number of Nigerians complaining about poor electricity supply, in a video aimed at the Minister of Power, Joseph Tegbe.

What she said

In the video, posted on Instagram and reported on Friday, 6 November 2026, Henshaw said electricity in her area comes on for only a few minutes before going off again. She was particularly angered by a reported statement from the minister suggesting that some Nigerians had asked for power supply to be reduced because their freezers were freezing food. She accused officials of not telling the truth, demanded accountability for the billions of naira spent on the power sector, and mocked the way households now have to combine generators, inverters and solar panels to get through the day. She also criticised the minister's predecessor over repeated tariff increases. Henshaw said she had deliberately avoided commenting on national issues for most of the year, but had reached her limit.

Background

Consumers in Lagos, Abuja, Ogun, Kwara and Oyo states have reported renewed outages this week. The minister has blamed the drop in supply on gas shortages, maintenance problems and reduced generation from hydropower plants. Comedian The Cute Abiola also posted videos this week appealing to President Bola Tinubu over the outages and economic hardship.

How people reacted

The video drew thousands of comments from Nigerians who said they shared her frustration. The minister had not publicly responded to her comments at the time of writing.

"My hair costs more than my clothes": Davido's elder sister, Sharon Adeleke, says she doesn't believe in spending big on outfits

10 Nov 2026, 01:10 GMT

Davido's elder sister, Sharon Adeleke, says she would rather spend good money on her hair than on expensive designer clothes. She also revealed that her own wedding dress cost just $2,000, and that her outfit for Davido and Chioma's white wedding was a last-minute rescue.

Read more

Davido's elder sister, Sharon Adeleke, has opened up about her fashion choices, revealing that she would rather spend good money on her hair than buy expensive designer clothes.

"My hair costs more than my clothes"

Speaking on a podcast, Sharon said she has never really believed in spending a fortune on clothing, no matter how much money a person has.

She enjoys looking good, especially when it comes to her hair, but she does not see the need to attach an expensive price tag to every outfit.

The wedding outfit drama

Sharon also spoke about her appearance at Davido and Chioma's white wedding, revealing that things did not go as smoothly as she had planned.

She said she had hired a stylist and a fashion designer to handle her outfit, but they failed to deliver as expected. Everything ended up as a last-minute arrangement, leaving her with little choice but to hope it worked out. At some point, she said, it was simply a case of "God abeg!"

A $2,000 wedding dress

She also shared an interesting detail about her own wedding. Despite coming from one of Nigeria's wealthiest and best-known families, she said her husband spent just $2,000 on her wedding dress.

She added that she personally paid for the dresses worn by all 13 of her bridesmaids.

Not what people expect

Her revelations are interesting because many people assume wealthy families spend outrageous amounts on everything they wear.

Now imagine Davido's own elder sister saying she doesn't believe in buying expensive clothes, while some people borrow money just to wear designer labels and impress strangers.

Over to you

Do you agree with Sharon that expensive clothes are unnecessary, even when you can comfortably afford them?

Funke Akindele says influencer rejected her paid offer to promote 'Everybody Loves Jenifa'

9 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Filmmaker Funke Akindele says an unnamed influencer turned down her paid offer to promote 'Everybody Loves Jenifa', and ignored her follow-up messages, while speaking at a creative industry panel.

Read more

Nollywood filmmaker Funke Akindele has revealed that a social media influencer turned down her paid offer to promote her blockbuster film 'Everybody Loves Jenifa'.

What she said

Speaking at a panel session at the Creative Africa Nexus Weekend, in a video shared online on 7 November 2026, Akindele said she had been reluctant to approach influencers but eventually decided to try. She proposed that the influencer post about the film several times a week during the promotional campaign, and offered to pay. However, the influencer's management turned her down, and when she followed up to make clear she was willing to pay, her message was ignored. She did not name the influencer.

Background

'Everybody Loves Jenifa' was one of the biggest Nollywood box-office hits. Akindele, one of the industry's most successful filmmakers, is now promoting her new film 'The Four', which recently sold out its London premiere. Influencer marketing has become a major part of how Nigerian films are promoted, with content creators often paid to make videos about new releases.

Hilda Baci says it is upsetting when people credit her success to her partner

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Celebrity chef and Guinness World Record holder Hilda Baci says it is sometimes upsetting when people attribute her achievements to her partner, saying it shows the mindset of many Nigerians.

Read more

Celebrity chef Hilda Baci has spoken out against people who credit her success to her romantic partner rather than her own efforts.

What she said

In a video shared on Monday, 9 November 2026, the chef said such comments are sometimes upsetting and reveal the mindset of many people. Her remarks have sparked conversation about the tendency to link successful women's achievements to the men in their lives.

Background

Hilda Baci became a household name in 2023 when she set a Guinness World Record for the longest cooking marathon by an individual, cooking for about 93 hours in Lagos. She has since built a successful food and media business. Her relationship has often been discussed on social media.

How people reacted

Reactions were mixed. Some fans backed her, saying women deserve credit for their own work, while others joked online that her partner was better known because of her rather than the other way around.

Funke Akindele and Toyosi Adesanya moment at Priscilla Ojo's store launch sparks debate

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Stars turned out for the launch of Priscilla Ojo's new store in Lagos, but a brief exchange between Funke Akindele and Toyosi Adesanya, who stood with her husband to greet her, got social media talking.

Read more

The opening of a new store by Priscilla Ojo, daughter of actress Iyabo Ojo, drew a crowd of celebrities in Lagos and some unexpected online debate.

The event

Guests at the launch, reported on Monday, 9 November 2026, included Priscilla's husband, Tanzanian singer Juma Jux, as well as Mercy Aigbe, content creator Enioluwa, fashion designer Veekee James and Funke Akindele. Videos showed Iyabo Ojo greeting Funke warmly when she arrived.

The moment that got people talking

A separate clip showed a brief interaction between Funke and fellow actress Toyosi Adesanya, who stood up with her husband to welcome her. Viewers were sharply divided over how the greeting played out, with the clip sparking wider conversation about friendships and etiquette among celebrities. Neither actress commented on the debate.

Background

Priscilla Ojo married Juma Jux in 2025 in widely publicised ceremonies in Lagos and Tanzania. Funke Akindele is one of Nollywood's most successful filmmakers, and her new film 'The Four' recently sold out its London premiere. Iyabo Ojo also recently spoke about how she fought to get Funke roles early in her career.
