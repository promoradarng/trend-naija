Pastor Jimmy Odukoya introduces new wife to Fountain of Life congregation

1 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya, senior pastor of Fountain of Life Church in Ilupeju, Lagos, has remarried and presented his new wife to his church.

What happened

During Sunday service on 1 November 2026, Odukoya introduced his wife, who will be known as Pastor Mo Odukoya, to the congregation. Days earlier, he had sparked engagement rumours by sharing a photo of two hands wearing rings, captioned as a new beginning. According to Pastor Tunde Bakare, General Overseer of The Citadel Global Community Church, the wedding took place in the United States and he blessed the marriage. Bakare said the bride is the daughter of Reverend Theophilus and Bolaji Daniel.

What the pastor said

Odukoya reportedly told the congregation that he had remained single since taking over the leadership of the church and felt called to remarry, referring to the biblical teaching that it is not good for a man to be alone.

Background

Jimmy Odukoya, an actor and pastor, took over as senior pastor of Fountain of Life Church after the death of his father, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, in 2023. He played the lead role in the 2023 film 'Orisa'. He was previously married to Kemi Odukoya, with whom he has children.

How people reacted

Many church members and fans congratulated the couple and shared videos from the service. Others used the moment to debate remarriage and divorce in the church, with some asking questions about his previous marriage. Odukoya has not publicly commented on those questions.

Biola Adebayo puts new salon up for sale weeks after opening it

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Actress Biola Adebayo says she is selling her newly opened salon because her sister, who was meant to run it, has been granted a Canadian visa. She said she cannot manage the business herself.

Read more

Nollywood actress Biola Adebayo has announced that she is putting her newly opened salon up for sale, only weeks after unveiling it.

The reason

In a video on her social media page, Biola explained that she set up the salon to give her sister, who had been going through a difficult time, a business of her own to run.

However, just days after the salon was unveiled, her sister received approval for a Canadian visa she had been waiting for for more than three years, and will now be relocating.

Mixed feelings

The actress described the visa as good news, but admitted it left her with the challenge of managing the salon herself, which led to her decision to sell it.

Pochettino takes over at Manchester United, faces Liverpool in first game

19 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly arrived in England to take charge of Manchester United after the club sacked Michael Carrick. His first match is away at league leaders Liverpool, with United in the relegation zone.

Read more

Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly taken over as Manchester United manager, following the club's decision to sack Michael Carrick.

His arrival

The Argentine flew into England on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, after his final game in charge of the United States men's national team.

A tough start

His first match is a daunting one: a trip to Anfield to face league leaders Liverpool just five days after arriving. United are currently in the Premier League relegation zone.

A statistical model reportedly gives Liverpool a 57.3% chance of winning the game, with United at 20.5% and a draw at 22.2%.

Transfer plans

Reports have also linked Pochettino with transfer targets for January, with Nigeria defender Calvin Bassey said to be on his shopping list. Fulham are reportedly asking for around £50 million for the Super Eagles star.

Background

Pochettino previously managed Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea before taking the United States job.
