Magixx says he struggled to pay bills after signing to Mavin Records

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Mavin Records singer Magixx has spoken about the financial realities of the music business, saying a record deal does not automatically make an artiste rich.

What he said

Speaking on an episode of a music podcast, reported on Friday, 6 November 2026, the singer said he did not start earning from music immediately after signing to Don Jazzy's label. It took about two to three months before money began to come in, he said, and during that time he struggled to pay bills and meet family responsibilities. He added that asking people for help became difficult because they assumed his record deal meant he was already wealthy, while he was also expected to keep up the image of a star, including wearing expensive designer clothes.

Building wealth

Magixx said Nigerian musicians need songs with international appeal to make serious money, and that releasing hits consistently is key to staying financially stable.

Background

Magixx signed with Mavin Records in 2021 and is known for songs such as 'Love Don't Cost a Dime'. His comments come in the same week that fellow Mavin artiste Crayon posted, and later deleted, emotional messages about the label and his friendship with Rema.

How people reacted

Fans thanked him for being honest about the pressures young artistes face, while some said his story showed why upcoming musicians should manage expectations after signing a deal.

  • Magixx says it took months before he started earning.
  • He says people assumed he was rich.
  • He wants Nigerian artistes to aim for global hits.

Nigerian Afrobeats stars Burna Boy and Asake are reportedly among the artists whose music features in Grand Theft Auto VI, one of the most anticipated video games ever, which launches on Thursday, 19 November 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says he nearly died

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Social media critic VeryDarkMan says he has been admitted to hospital in China after being diagnosed with what he called life-threatening malaria. He said he nearly lost his life on Wednesday night before being rushed to hospital.

Read more

Social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has been hospitalised in China after being diagnosed with what he described as life-threatening malaria.

What he said

VDM, who is in China on a business trip, shared the health update in a video on Instagram on Thursday, 19 November 2026. He said his condition got worse despite his attempts to treat the illness himself.

According to him, things became so serious that he nearly lost his life on Wednesday night, before he was rushed to hospital the next morning.

First hospital stay

The 32-year-old said he had never spent a night in hospital before, but that his condition would now keep him under medical care for several days.

Background

VDM is one of Nigeria's most influential social media critics. Earlier this week, Oyo corps members credited him after their delayed allowances were paid following his intervention.

Naira weakens to ₦1,363 per dollar in official market

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The naira lost ₦7.43 against the dollar on Wednesday to close at ₦1,363.23 in the official market, and fell to ₦1,430 on the parallel market. Analysts point to reduced dollar inflows and rising demand.

Read more

The naira weakened against the US dollar in both the official and parallel foreign exchange markets on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, amid reduced foreign exchange inflows and rising demand for dollars.

Official market

Data from the Nigerian Autonomous Foreign Exchange Market (NAFEM) showed the naira fell by ₦7.43, or 0.54%, to close at ₦1,363.23 to the dollar, from ₦1,355.80 the previous day. It also lost ground against the euro, closing at ₦1,495.60, but gained slightly against the British pound to ₦1,795.24.

Parallel market

On the parallel market, the naira slipped by ₦10 to trade at ₦1,430 to the dollar, while at one major bank's foreign exchange desk it closed at ₦1,368.

Why it matters

The fall comes a day after the naira lost about ₦25 against the dollar, and as the Central Bank of Nigeria and NNPC move to boost foreign exchange inflows.

But once they stepped outside, the man reached out for a selfie and the actor turned away and busied himself with something else. Comedian Ali Baba, who was nearby, had stepped back to give the man space for the photo, which made the moment more noticeable.

How people reacted

No comment yet
