Magixx says he struggled to pay bills after signing to Mavin Records

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Mavin Records singer Magixx has spoken about the financial realities of the music business, saying a record deal does not automatically make an artiste rich.

What he said

Speaking on an episode of a music podcast, reported on Friday, 6 November 2026, the singer said he did not start earning from music immediately after signing to Don Jazzy's label. It took about two to three months before money began to come in, he said, and during that time he struggled to pay bills and meet family responsibilities. He added that asking people for help became difficult because they assumed his record deal meant he was already wealthy, while he was also expected to keep up the image of a star, including wearing expensive designer clothes.

Building wealth

Magixx said Nigerian musicians need songs with international appeal to make serious money, and that releasing hits consistently is key to staying financially stable.

Background

Magixx signed with Mavin Records in 2021 and is known for songs such as 'Love Don't Cost a Dime'. His comments come in the same week that fellow Mavin artiste Crayon posted, and later deleted, emotional messages about the label and his friendship with Rema.

How people reacted

Fans thanked him for being honest about the pressures young artistes face, while some said his story showed why upcoming musicians should manage expectations after signing a deal.

  • Magixx says it took months before he started earning.
  • He says people assumed he was rich.
  • He wants Nigerian artistes to aim for global hits.

Portable says he received ₦100 million after campaigning for APC

14 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

Singer Portable has claimed he woke up to ₦100 million after taking part in campaigning for the APC, without saying who sent it. The claim has drawn criticism online amid economic hardship.

Read more

Street-pop singer Portable has stirred debate online after claiming he received ₦100 million following his involvement in campaigns for the All Progressives Congress (APC).

What he said

In a post shared on Friday, 13 November 2026, the singer, whose real name is Habeeb Okikiola, said he simply woke up and found ₦100 million had been paid to him. He did not say who sent the money or what it was for. Earlier reports had linked the payment to an APC governorship candidate in Ogun State, but this has not been confirmed.

How people reacted

Many social media users criticised the claim, contrasting it with the economic hardship facing ordinary Nigerians and questioning whether celebrity endorsements actually influence voters.

Background

Portable has openly supported President Bola Tinubu and the APC. The APC has not commented on his claim.

Tems to lead soundtrack for Hollywood film Children of Blood and Bone

13 Nov 2026, 15:50 GMT

Tems is executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood adaptation of Tomi Adeyemi's novel 'Children of Blood and Bone', featuring Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Fireboy DML, BNXN and others. The album is out on 20 November.

Read more

Grammy-winning singer Tems has been named executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone'.

The soundtrack

The announcement was made on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026. The album brings together several of Africa's biggest stars, including Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Fireboy DML, BNXN and Kold AF. Tems described the project as a profound honour and said she was excited for fans to hear what she had put together. The soundtrack is due for release on 20 November, ahead of the film's cinema debut.

The film

'Children of Blood and Bone' is directed by Gina Prince-Bythewood and adapted from the bestselling fantasy novel by Nigerian-American author Tomi Adeyemi.

Background

Tems has become one of the most successful Nigerian artists internationally, winning Grammy Awards and earning an Oscar nomination for co-writing 'Lift Me Up' for 'Black Panther: Wakanda Forever'.

Ronaldinho set for competitive comeback at 46 with Italian club Ravenna

14 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Brazilian legend Ronaldinho is set to start for Serie C club Ravenna against Forli on Sunday, his first competitive match in more than a decade, the club's manager has confirmed.

Read more

Former Barcelona, PSG and AC Milan star Ronaldinho is set to return to competitive football at the age of 46.

The comeback

Ravenna manager Andrea Mandorlini confirmed on Friday, 13 November 2026, that the Brazilian will start for the Italian Serie C club in a local derby against Forli on Sunday, 15 November, as one of three forwards. Mandorlini described him as one of the greatest players of the modern era, adding that the coaching staff would decide together how long he plays.

A long wait

Ronaldinho joined Ravenna as an investor in August. His last competitive appearance was for Brazilian club Fluminense in September 2015, making Sunday's match his first in more than ten years.

Background

Ronaldinho won the Ballon d'Or in 2005 and the World Cup with Brazil in 2002, and is remembered as one of the most gifted and entertaining players of his generation.
