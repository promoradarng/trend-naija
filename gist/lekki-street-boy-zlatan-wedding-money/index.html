VeryDarkMan, Opeyemi Famakin and Peller defend Enioluwa after youth comments backlash

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have spoken in defence of influencer Enioluwa, who apologised after his comments about young Nigerians drew criticism.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

His mother speaks

Enioluwa's mother, Bola Adeoluwa, a university lecturer, has also addressed the backlash after some students on TikTok turned their criticism on her, alleging she was a harsh grader. She responded publicly to the claims and the criticism of her son.

Leeds stun Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge

21 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Leeds United beat Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge in the Premier League. Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso admitted the loss was painful and that his side's confidence has taken a hit.

Read more

Leeds United inflicted a heavy 4-1 defeat on Chelsea at Stamford Bridge on matchday 11 of the Premier League on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The match

Emmanuel Emegha gave Chelsea an early lead on his first start for the club since joining in the summer. Leeds hit back quickly through Noah Okafor, and the sides went into half-time level at 1-1.

After the break, Leeds were awarded a penalty when Morgan Rogers was judged to have handled the ball in the area, a decision that sparked debate, and went on to complete a convincing win.

Alonso's reaction

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso said the result was painful and reflected a team struggling to keep up its earlier form. He admitted his players' confidence had taken a hit at a difficult time.

Tinubu arrives in Lagos for family prayer session after Ibadan rally

21 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

President Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday after his campaign rally in Ibadan, ahead of a special prayer session his family has organised for him on Sunday. He was received by Governors Sanwo-Olu and Abiodun.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu arrived in Lagos on Saturday, 21 November 2026, after addressing an APC presidential campaign rally in Ibadan, Oyo State.

The arrival

His aircraft landed at the Presidential Wing of the Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja at about 3pm. He was received by Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, Ogun Governor Dapo Abiodun, his Chief of Staff Femi Gbajabiamila and former Ogun governor Segun Osoba, along with party leaders and supporters.

Family prayer

The visit is centred on a special prayer session organised in his honour by members of his family, scheduled for Sunday, 22 November. The President is expected to return to Abuja afterwards.

The campaign

The trip comes as the APC steps up its campaign across the South-West. At the Ibadan rally, Tinubu endorsed the party's governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos and promised improved electricity supply.

Tinubu endorses APC governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos at Ibadan rally, promises better power

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

At an APC rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, President Tinubu raised the hands of the party's governorship candidates for Oyo, Ogun and Lagos. He also guaranteed improved electricity and promised a factory in Ibadan.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos States at a campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026.

The endorsements

Telling the crowd he would show them who to vote for as governor, Tinubu raised the hands of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC candidate in Oyo; Senator Solomon Adeola, known as Yayi, the party's candidate in Ogun; and Lagos Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, the APC flagbearer in Lagos. He also raised the hand of Remi Oseni, the party's senatorial candidate for Oyo South.

Promise on power

During the visit, the President also assured Nigerians of better electricity supply, saying the Minister of Power and his team were working hard. He added that the government would build a factory in Ibadan.

Context

The rally is part of the APC's campaign ahead of the 16 January 2027 elections.

The other side

Reactions from the opposition parties to the rally were not included in the reports.
