Car dealer Moses Auto says fire destroyed vehicles worth over ₦700 million

2 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

A Nigerian car dealer known online as Moses Auto Car Care has described the heavy losses he suffered after a fire swept through his car lot.

What he said

In comments shared on Monday, 2 November 2026, the dealer reportedly said that cars worth more than ₦500 million belonging to him were destroyed in the fire. He added that customers' vehicles worth over ₦200 million were also burnt, including cars that buyers had already paid for but had not yet collected. He did not say what caused the fire or when exactly it happened.

How people reacted

Many social media users expressed sympathy and prayed for him to recover from the loss, with some praising him for staying strong. Others said the incident showed the importance of insurance, which many Nigerian businesses do not take, and of basic fire safety equipment such as alarms and sprinklers. Some asked how the customers who had paid for cars would be compensated.

Background

Fires at markets, shops and car lots cause large losses in Nigeria every year, often linked to electrical faults or poor safety measures. Fire services frequently urge business owners to install extinguishers and alarms and to insure their goods.

What happens next

It is not yet clear whether the business was insured or how the dealer plans to settle with affected customers.

"Zlatan, I'm coming for you, you will learn the hard way": VeryDarkMan fires warning at Zlatan over Davido's viral wedding video

4 Nov 2026, 17:26 GMT

VeryDarkMan has reacted angrily to a viral video of Davido singing lyrics that appeared to mock him at Zlatan Ibile's wedding welcome party in Morocco. VDM accuses Zlatan of using his new song, INFERNO, to take a swipe at him, and has warned the singer he will "learn the hard way".

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has once again stirred controversy after reacting angrily to a viral video of Afrobeats superstar Davido singing lyrics that appeared to mock him at Zlatan Ibile's wedding welcome party in Morocco.

What was supposed to be a moment of celebration has become another talking point online, with VDM accusing Zlatan of using his newly released song, INFERNO, to throw indirect insults at him.

The lyric that started it

The controversy began after a video surfaced showing Davido enthusiastically singing along to a line that sounded like, "I no be Vincent, I no dey mast…"

For those familiar with VeryDarkMan, whose real name is Martins Vincent Otse, the reference to "Vincent" immediately raised eyebrows, particularly because of his earlier controversial comments about masturbation.

VDM responds

VDM did not find it funny. In a video shared online, he defended his earlier statements, insisting he saw nothing wrong with his position. According to him, he would rather satisfy himself than go around begging women for attention.

He also questioned why his personal comments had apparently become material for a song, suggesting Zlatan was deliberately taking a swipe at him.

What caught many people's attention was the warning he directed at the singer. Speaking in Pidgin, he said, in effect: "Zlatan, I'm coming for you now. You will learn the hard way."

The Davido angle

The episode has also brought fresh attention to VDM's relationship with Davido. The two were once publicly friendly, and their association drew plenty of attention online. Their relationship has since appeared strained, with fans often speculating about what went wrong.

Seeing Davido sing along to lyrics VDM believes were aimed at him has only added another chapter to that conversation.

Was it really about VDM?

There is no confirmed evidence that Zlatan wrote the lyrics to attack VeryDarkMan. That interpretation is VDM's, and the viral clip alone does not show what the singer intended.

Even so, social media users are divided. Some believe VDM is taking an ordinary lyric too personally, while others argue that using a name tied to someone already at the centre of a public controversy was always going to draw a reaction.

Knowing how outspoken VDM can be, many followers are now watching to see whether this ends with his warning or turns into another public confrontation.

Disagreements between public figures are nothing new in Nigeria. Celebrities exchange insults, make accusations and drag one another for days, and sometimes the whole thing turns into entertainment for their followers.

But bringing someone's mother into a personal disagreement, especially with words wishing her death or misfortune, is something many people consider completely unacceptable.

Whatever the disagreement between Carter Efe and VeryDarkMan may be, one question remains: what has VDM's mother got to do with it? These are parents who may know little or nothing about the arguments their children are having online.

Tinubu asks Senate to approve $7.7bn foreign borrowing for 2026 budget

4 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

President Tinubu has asked the Senate to approve $7.7 billion in new external borrowing, about ₦10.79 trillion, mainly through Eurobonds and loans from international lenders. He said it would fund the 2026 budget and support the naira.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has asked the Senate to approve fresh external borrowing of $7.7 billion to help finance the 2026 budget.

The request

The request was contained in a letter read by Senate President Godswill Akpabio during plenary on Wednesday, 4 November 2026. At the budget exchange rate of ₦1,400 to the dollar, the amount is equal to about ₦10.79 trillion. According to the letter, the money would be raised mainly through Eurobond issuance, as well as loans from multilateral and bilateral lenders, international financial institutions and syndicated loans. The President said the borrowing would help finance the budget, strengthen foreign reserves and support the stability of the naira. He also asked the Senate to authorise the Minister of Finance and the Debt Management Office to carry out the plan.

What the Senate did

Akpabio said the new request appeared to replace an earlier borrowing proposal and gave more detail on where the money would come from. He referred it to the Senate Committee on Local and Foreign Debts for consideration and a report.

Background

Nigeria's public debt has risen sharply in recent years, and borrowing has become a major political issue ahead of the 2027 elections. Last week, former President Olusegun Obasanjo described the government's borrowing as 'absolute nonsense', while opposition candidates have warned that debt servicing is taking up too much of government revenue. The government says borrowing is needed to fund infrastructure and that its reforms have improved Nigeria's credit rating and ability to raise money abroad.

What happens next

The Senate committee is expected to review the request before the full Senate votes on it.

Tinubu says he drove a taxi to pay for his education in the United States

4 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

President Tinubu says he drove a taxi and took other jobs in the US to fund his university education, and even delayed starting school to save money. He linked the experience to his government's student loan scheme.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has shared how he worked to pay for his university education in the United States, saying the experience shaped his government's education policies.

What he said

In a post on his official X account on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, the President said that when he arrived in the US as a young man, he drove a taxi to earn money for his education, and even had to postpone starting school for several months to save enough. He said he did several other jobs while studying.

The policy link

Tinubu connected his personal story to his administration's education programmes, pointing to the student loan scheme, investment in medical schools and increased enrolment in nursing training as evidence of efforts to widen access to education.

Background

The President studied accounting at Chicago State University, graduating in 1979. On Tuesday, Education Minister Tunji Alausa said Tinubu graduated with a first class and described him as a genius, urging Nigerians to ignore opposition questions about his academic record. The Supreme Court dismissed petitions challenging the President's 2023 election, including claims about his certificates. The Nigerian Education Loan Fund, set up in 2024, provides interest-free loans to students in public tertiary institutions.

How people reacted

Supporters said the story showed the President understood the struggles of young Nigerians, while critics said many students still face high fees and limited opportunities despite the loan scheme.
