Car dealer Moses Auto says fire destroyed vehicles worth over ₦700 million

2 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

A Nigerian car dealer known online as Moses Auto Car Care has described the heavy losses he suffered after a fire swept through his car lot.

What he said

In comments shared on Monday, 2 November 2026, the dealer reportedly said that cars worth more than ₦500 million belonging to him were destroyed in the fire. He added that customers' vehicles worth over ₦200 million were also burnt, including cars that buyers had already paid for but had not yet collected. He did not say what caused the fire or when exactly it happened.

How people reacted

Many social media users expressed sympathy and prayed for him to recover from the loss, with some praising him for staying strong. Others said the incident showed the importance of insurance, which many Nigerian businesses do not take, and of basic fire safety equipment such as alarms and sprinklers. Some asked how the customers who had paid for cars would be compensated.

Background

Fires at markets, shops and car lots cause large losses in Nigeria every year, often linked to electrical faults or poor safety measures. Fire services frequently urge business owners to install extinguishers and alarms and to insure their goods.

What happens next

It is not yet clear whether the business was insured or how the dealer plans to settle with affected customers.

  • His own cars worth over ₦500 million were destroyed.
  • Customers' cars worth over ₦200 million were also burnt.
  • People online urged businesses to take insurance seriously.

Eight soldiers and 13 workers were killed while rebuilding Borno community, officials say

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Borno officials have revealed that eight soldiers, including a major, and 13 construction workers were killed by insurgents during the 14-month rebuilding of Mayanti in Bama. Governor Babagana Zulum has now inaugurated 500 houses and a school there.

Read more

At least 21 people, including eight soldiers and 13 construction workers, were reportedly killed by insurgents during the reconstruction of the Mayanti community in Bama Local Government Area of Borno State.

How it came to light

The deaths were disclosed on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by Borno's Commissioner for Education, Abba Wakilbe, who heads the community's resettlement committee, during the inauguration of the rebuilt community. Among those killed was a major, Major Mairiga, along with seven other soldiers.

Wakilbe said work on the project began in August 2025 and was completed in October 2026. He paid tribute to those who died, saying their sacrifice made the return of the community possible.

What was built

Governor Babagana Zulum inaugurated 500 houses, 2,000 semi-permanent shelters and a school at Mayanti. He praised the committee for finishing the project despite repeated attacks, recalling that the community was attacked four or five times while construction was going on.

Background

Many communities in Borno State were destroyed during years of insurgency, and the state government has been rebuilding and resettling displaced residents in several areas.

The event

Akpabio spoke at Ibom Hall Ground in Uyo, where the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement inaugurated its Akwa Ibom executives. Makinde says Tinubu has only a 30% chance of winning in 2027

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly put President Tinubu's chances of re-election at 30 per cent, citing economic hardship and insecurity. The figure is his own estimate, not from a poll.

Read more

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly said President Bola Tinubu has only a 30 per cent chance of winning the 2027 presidential election.

His argument

Makinde is said to have argued that poor governance has worn away the goodwill the ruling APC once enjoyed. He pointed to food insecurity, the rising cost of living and insecurity as the main reasons he believes many Nigerians have turned against the party.

He also reportedly accused the APC leadership of dismissing citizens' concerns instead of addressing them.

Not a poll

The 30 per cent figure is Makinde's own assessment and was not taken from any published opinion poll or independent survey.

Background

Makinde is himself among the challengers in the race. His comments come days after Tinubu's APC held a campaign rally in Ibadan, the Oyo State capital.
