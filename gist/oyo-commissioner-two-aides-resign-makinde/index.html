Oyo commissioner and two other officials resign from Makinde government in one day

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Three officials serving in Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde's administration resigned on Monday, 2 November 2026, according to reports, in the latest of a series of departures from the state government.

Who resigned

Those who left were reportedly the Commissioner for Budget and Economic Planning, Ayobami Ojo; the Special Adviser on Budget and Economic Planning, Adegbola Oyeleke; and Sunday Falana, a member of the Oyo State Independent Electoral Commission. Ojo, who is from Ago-Are in Atisbo Local Government Area, submitted his letter through the Secretary to the State Government, said his exit was with immediate effect and thanked the governor for the opportunity to serve, without giving a reason. Oyeleke reportedly said he was no longer comfortable with the working environment in the administration.

Background

Makinde is serving his second and final term as governor and is now the presidential candidate of the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) for 2027. Several officials have left his government in recent months as politicians reposition ahead of the elections, with some moving to rival parties. The governor has not publicly commented on the latest resignations.

Why it matters

The departures come as Makinde campaigns nationally, including in the South East, and as parties compete for control of Oyo State, where the governorship seat will be open in 2027.

Microsoft outage disrupts Outlook, Teams and Copilot in 74 countries

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A global Microsoft 365 outage that began on Thursday has disrupted Outlook, Teams and Copilot for users in 74 countries and territories, with nearly 140,000 problem reports logged at its peak. Microsoft says it is working to restore services.

Read more

Microsoft is working to restore its services after a global outage disrupted Outlook email, Teams and its Copilot AI assistant for users around the world.

What happened

The outage began on Thursday, 12 November 2026, and affected Microsoft 365 services in 74 countries and territories, according to reports on Friday. Many users were unable to load their inboxes or send and receive emails, while others had trouble with Teams calls and Copilot. Reports of Outlook problems on an outage-tracking website peaked at nearly 140,000 worldwide.

Microsoft's explanation

Microsoft initially blamed the problem on unusually high traffic across parts of its infrastructure, which caused connection errors. The company said it had paused some deployments while it worked on a fix.

Why it matters

Outlook and Teams are widely used by businesses and organisations around the world, including in Nigeria.

Victor Boniface sends ₦1m to cancer patient during TikTok livestream

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Super Eagles striker Victor Boniface has been praised after sending ₦1 million to a woman battling cancer who told him on his TikTok livestream that she needed about ₦4 million for treatment.

Read more

Super Eagles striker Victor Boniface has won praise online after donating ₦1 million to a woman with cancer during a TikTok livestream.

What happened

In a clip that spread widely on Friday, 13 November 2026, the woman joined Boniface's livestream and told him she needed about ₦4 million for her treatment. After listening to her, the Bayer Leverkusen forward asked for her account details and sent the money immediately, leaving her visibly emotional. Many Nigerians commended him for his generosity.

His career

Away from the gesture, Boniface is going through a difficult spell. Injuries have held back his progress at Leverkusen, and a loan spell at Werder Bremen last season did not revive his form. He has played only a few minutes of Bundesliga football this season and is reportedly keen to leave in the January transfer window. Leverkusen, where he is under contract until 2028, are said to be open to a sale or a loan.
