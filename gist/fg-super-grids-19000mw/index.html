FEC approves East and West super grids to lift power transmission to 19,000MW

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

The Federal Government has approved the start of procurement for two new high-capacity power lines, known as the East and West super grids, in a bid to fix the weak transmission network that stops much of Nigeria's electricity from reaching homes and businesses.

What happened

The decision was taken at the Federal Executive Council meeting chaired by President Bola Tinubu in Abuja on Wednesday, 28 October 2026. Minister of Power Joseph Tegbe told State House correspondents that the goal is to raise transmission capacity from about 5,000MW to 19,000MW within two years. He said Nigeria already has more than 13,000MW of available generation, but much of it is stranded because the grid cannot carry it.

The routes

The corridors are planned to run from Sokoto through Abuja into parts of the South. The government will start with the Lagos–Ogun, Benin–Port Harcourt and Abuja–Kaduna–Kano corridors. Tegbe said Lagos and Ogun alone account for about half of national electricity demand. The council also approved new transformers for the Ikeja West substation, a new line at the Katampe substation in Abuja and the upgrade of the Itumola–Shagamu line. The cost of the projects was not disclosed.

Other measures

Tegbe said a four-week technical audit is under way to make sure money goes to projects with real impact. The government has paid ₦1.23 trillion of about ₦3.3 trillion owed to gas suppliers and other power-sector players. Tinubu also ordered the ports and customs to clear more than 300 containers of electricity equipment, some of which have been stuck for up to 20 years. Work has started on a new National Control Centre in Osogbo to replace a facility that is 64 years old.

What happens next

Nigerians, used to frequent grid collapses, will be watching for results. The minister said the Lagos and Ikeja projects would show quickly whether power reaches consumers.

  • The target is 19,000MW of transmission capacity in two years.
  • More than 300 containers of power equipment are to be cleared from the ports.
  • The cost of the super grids was not disclosed.

Moniepoint opens North Central regional headquarters in Abuja

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened a regional headquarters in Abuja where customers can get in-person help, in line with CBN rules requiring physical locations for national microfinance banks.

Read more

Digital lender Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened its North Central regional headquarters in Abuja, giving customers a place to get face-to-face support.

Why now

The move is in line with a Central Bank of Nigeria requirement for nationally licensed microfinance banks to keep physical locations alongside their digital services.

Wider presence

Moniepoint already runs offices and service centres in several states, including Borno, Bauchi, Kaduna, Kano and Sokoto, and its point-of-sale network serves businesses in all 774 local government areas.

Jimmy Odukoya sues sister and Fountain of Life trustees over his removal

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court in Lagos against his sister, Tolu Odukoya, and members of the Fountain of Life Church board over his removal as senior pastor.

Read more

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has gone to court over his removal as senior pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, filing a suit against his sister and several members of the church's board of trustees.

The suit

The suit was filed at the Federal High Court in Lagos on Friday, 20 November 2026. Eight people are named as respondents, including his sister Tolu Odukoya, interim leader Pastor Rotimi Okpaise and other trustees.

The filing marks a significant escalation in the leadership dispute at the Ilupeju church founded by his late father, Taiwo Odukoya.

Background

Earlier this week, the trustees announced that Jimmy had been removed because his appointment did not reflect his father's succession wishes, and released a 2021 video in which the late founder reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his successor. Jimmy has insisted his removal is invalid. A Lagos State High Court had already granted the trustees an order barring him from acting as senior pastor while their own suit is heard.

What happens next

The court is expected to fix a date to hear the case. The trustees had not responded publicly to the new suit in the reports.

NELFUND reviewing ₦20,000 monthly upkeep for students

21 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Nigerian Education Loan Fund says it is reviewing the ₦20,000 monthly upkeep allowance it pays to student beneficiaries, citing economic pressures. MD Akin Sawyer said any increase must be carefully considered.

Read more

The Nigerian Education Loan Fund (NELFUND) is considering an increase to the ₦20,000 monthly upkeep allowance it pays to student beneficiaries.

What was said

NELFUND's Managing Director, Akin Sawyer, said in a television interview on Friday, 20 November 2026, that the allowance was being reviewed in light of the economic pressures facing students.

He said the matter is under constant review, but stressed that any increase would need careful consideration.

Background

NELFUND, set up under President Bola Tinubu's administration, gives loans to students in tertiary institutions and pays a monthly upkeep allowance to beneficiaries.
