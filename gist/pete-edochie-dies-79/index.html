Nollywood legend Pete Edochie dies at 79

25 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Veteran Nollywood actor Pete Edochie has died at the age of 79, his family has announced.

The announcement

In a statement shared on Instagram by his son, Leo Pete Edochie, on behalf of the family, the family said the actor died at 12:30am on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, in Enugu, after a prolonged battle with prostate cancer. The statement, titled 'An Iroko Has Fallen', described him as a beloved husband, father, brother and kinsman.

He is survived by his wife, Josephine Edochie, his children, including actors Yul and Linc Edochie, his siblings and other relatives. The family said funeral arrangements would be announced in due course.

His life and career

Pete Edochie hailed from Akamnato, Ifite Nteje, in Anambra State. He began his career in broadcasting in 1967 before becoming one of Nigeria's best-known actors. His major breakthrough came in 1987, when he played Okonkwo in the Nigerian Television Authority's adaptation of Chinua Achebe's Things Fall Apart, a role that brought him recognition far beyond Nigeria.

Known for his commanding voice and powerful screen presence, he became famous for portraying traditional patriarchs and kings in countless Nollywood films. He was honoured with the national award of Member of the Order of the Niger (MON).

His final weeks

In October, during a visit to Ghana, he spoke openly about his declining health, saying walking had become difficult, and prayed that he would not become a burden to those around him. Just this week, actress Toyin Abraham and her crew knelt to welcome him on her film set, and he had spoken publicly about his son Yul days earlier.

Tributes

News of his death has drawn an outpouring of grief from fans, many of whom described it as the end of an era for Nigerian film.

Governor and colleagues pay tribute

Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah described Edochie as an institution and said his death was a personal loss for the state, which the actor considered his birthplace and first port of call. Actress Funke Akindele, who worked with him in the film Egg of Life, recalled growing up watching him play Okonkwo and said his words of encouragement had stayed with her.

Tinubu: 'larger than life'

President Bola Tinubu, in a condolence statement from the State House, described Edochie as one of Nigeria's greatest actors and a founding figure of Nollywood, graceful and larger than life both on screen and in person. Joke Silva, whose husband Olu Jacobs died earlier this year, Toyin Abraham, Omoni Oboli and his daughter-in-law May Edochie were among those who also paid tribute, while his son Uche shared the last photo he took with his father.

More tributes

Content creator Peller also mourned the actor, sharing a moment from their last meeting and expressing gratitude for the time they spent together.

Genevieve Nnaji

Genevieve Nnaji, who worked with Edochie on her directorial debut Lionheart, shared a photo of the two of them and an emotional tribute, describing him with the Igbo term of endearment 'nnanyi', meaning 'our father'.

'Irreplaceable'

Veteran actress Patience Ozokwo described his death as an irreplaceable loss, saying he was deeply committed to authenticity and always made sure cultural details were portrayed correctly on set.

Obi and family

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi described Edochie as an irreplaceable figure in Nigerian and African culture and a distinguished son of Anambra State. His sister-in-law, actress Rita Edochie, said she was struck by how swiftly he was taken, referring to an illness of barely five weeks.

Yul Edochie speaks

On Thursday, his son Yul Edochie posted a personal tribute on Instagram, saying words had failed him. Many fans set aside past controversies to console him. Ghanaian actress Jackie Appiah also mourned Edochie, sharing their last conversation weeks before his death.

Judy Austin

Judy Austin, Yul Edochie's second wife, later posted her own tribute after some fans criticised her silence.

On set

Veteran actor Nkem Owoh also paid an emotional tribute, recalling their friendship, while actor Frederick Leonard led cast and crew in a minute's silence for Edochie on a film set.

Final moments

In a long tribute on Friday, Yul Edochie said he would never forget his father asking him to hold his hand in hospital, and that his father always urged him to call every day. His brother Leo also shared what he described as their father's final wish.

Minister visits family

A federal minister representing President Tinubu visited Edochie's widow and family members at their residence in Enugu to condole with them.

Adesua Etomi celebrates good news after secretly battling health problems this year

27 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington has revealed she spent much of 2026 going through medical procedures away from the public eye. She shared a video of her family celebrating test results, and some reports say she has been declared cancer-free.

Nollywood actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington has opened up about one of the hardest periods of her life, revealing that she spent much of 2026 dealing with health challenges that she kept largely private.

The announcement

In a video posted on Instagram on Friday, 27 November 2026, the actress, who is married to singer Banky W, was seen on a video call with a group of women, believed to be family members or close friends, as she shared test results from her doctor. The women burst into celebration on hearing the news.

Her message

In her post, Adesua said she had been quieter than usual this year because she was going through some of the most difficult months of her life, describing one procedure after another, tests, scans and hospital visits. She said the year had thrown everything at her but that she was not easily broken, and thanked God for carrying her through. She said there was more to her testimony that she would share later.

Some reports said she had been declared cancer-free, though she did not give details of her diagnosis in the post.

How fans reacted

Fans and colleagues have flooded her page with congratulatory messages and prayers.

WWE star Bronson Reed dies at 38

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

WWE has announced the death of wrestler Bronson Reed, real name Jermaine Haley, at the age of 38 from medical complications. He had been seriously injured at a WWE event in Saudi Arabia three weeks earlier.

Read more

WWE has announced the death of wrestler Bronson Reed, whose real name was Jermaine Haley, at the age of 38.

The announcement

In a statement on its website on Friday, 27 November 2026, WWE said Reed had died earlier that day from medical complications, but did not give further details. The company described him as a larger-than-life figure, a devoted family man and a beloved colleague, and sent its condolences to his pregnant wife, Paige, and their daughter.

Earlier injury

His death came about three weeks after he was seriously injured at WWE's Crown Jewel event in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on 7 November. During a tag team title match, Nigerian-born wrestler Oba Femi threw him off the stage through a stack of tables. Reed reportedly suffered broken ribs and a lacerated kidney and spent more than a week in hospital in Saudi Arabia. WWE has not said whether his death was linked to the injury.

His career

Born in Adelaide, Australia, Reed joined WWE in 2019. He won the NXT North American Championship and the 2024 André the Giant Memorial Battle Royal. Wrestlers and fans around the world have paid tribute.

INEC holds nationwide mock accreditation in 17,656 polling units

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

INEC is conducting a mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise across 17,656 polling units in all 36 states and the FCT on Saturday. It is testing its upgraded BVAS machines and security arrangements.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) is conducting a nationwide mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise on Saturday, 28 November 2026.

The exercise

The test is taking place at 17,656 polling units across the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. It is designed to test INEC's upgraded Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS), the result transmission process and election security arrangements under simulated election-day conditions. Sample ballot papers are being used.

Why it matters

The exercise comes ahead of the presidential and National Assembly elections on 16 January 2027. INEC has faced criticism in past elections over delays in uploading results, and this week the commission named nine states as hotspots for campaign violence.

Lagos adjusts

Lagos State cancelled its monthly environmental sanitation exercise, which had been scheduled for the same morning, so that residents could take part.

VeryDarkMan says he will register his own Ratel party and fund it himself

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has announced plans to register the Ratel Political Party before 2032, saying he will pay the registration costs from his business earnings. He said the movement will start with grassroots offices.

Read more

Social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has announced plans to register his own political party, the Ratel Political Party, before 2032.

Paying his own way

In a video, VDM said he would fund the project with his own earnings from his businesses, rather than relying on supporters. He said he had heard the registration fee was over ₦50 million and that he would raise it. INEC's party registration guidelines set a non-refundable administrative fee of ₦50 million, and payment does not guarantee approval.

Grassroots first

VDM said the Ratel movement would begin by focusing on grassroots positions, such as local government chairmen and councillors, rather than seats in the National Assembly.

Background

'Ratel' is the name VDM uses for his followers. This week he also urged opposition leaders Peter Obi, Atiku Abubakar and Seyi Makinde to unite under one party against President Tinubu.
