Nollywood legend Pete Edochie dies at 79

25 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Veteran Nollywood actor Pete Edochie has died at the age of 79, his family has announced.

The announcement

In a statement shared on Instagram by his son, Leo Pete Edochie, on behalf of the family, the family said the actor died at 12:30am on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, in Enugu, after a prolonged battle with prostate cancer. The statement, titled 'An Iroko Has Fallen', described him as a beloved husband, father, brother and kinsman.

He is survived by his wife, Josephine Edochie, his children, including actors Yul and Linc Edochie, his siblings and other relatives. The family said funeral arrangements would be announced in due course.

His life and career

Pete Edochie hailed from Akamnato, Ifite Nteje, in Anambra State. He began his career in broadcasting in 1967 before becoming one of Nigeria's best-known actors. His major breakthrough came in 1987, when he played Okonkwo in the Nigerian Television Authority's adaptation of Chinua Achebe's Things Fall Apart, a role that brought him recognition far beyond Nigeria.

Known for his commanding voice and powerful screen presence, he became famous for portraying traditional patriarchs and kings in countless Nollywood films. He was honoured with the national award of Member of the Order of the Niger (MON).

His final weeks

In October, during a visit to Ghana, he spoke openly about his declining health, saying walking had become difficult, and prayed that he would not become a burden to those around him. Just this week, actress Toyin Abraham and her crew knelt to welcome him on her film set, and he had spoken publicly about his son Yul days earlier.

Tributes

News of his death has drawn an outpouring of grief from fans, many of whom described it as the end of an era for Nigerian film.

Governor and colleagues pay tribute

Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah described Edochie as an institution and said his death was a personal loss for the state, which the actor considered his birthplace and first port of call. Actress Funke Akindele, who worked with him in the film Egg of Life, recalled growing up watching him play Okonkwo and said his words of encouragement had stayed with her.

Tinubu: 'larger than life'

President Bola Tinubu, in a condolence statement from the State House, described Edochie as one of Nigeria's greatest actors and a founding figure of Nollywood, graceful and larger than life both on screen and in person. Joke Silva, whose husband Olu Jacobs died earlier this year, Toyin Abraham, Omoni Oboli and his daughter-in-law May Edochie were among those who also paid tribute, while his son Uche shared the last photo he took with his father.

More tributes

Content creator Peller also mourned the actor, sharing a moment from their last meeting and expressing gratitude for the time they spent together.

Genevieve Nnaji

Genevieve Nnaji, who worked with Edochie on her directorial debut Lionheart, shared a photo of the two of them and an emotional tribute, describing him with the Igbo term of endearment 'nnanyi', meaning 'our father'.

'Irreplaceable'

Veteran actress Patience Ozokwo described his death as an irreplaceable loss, saying he was deeply committed to authenticity and always made sure cultural details were portrayed correctly on set.

Obi and family

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi described Edochie as an irreplaceable figure in Nigerian and African culture and a distinguished son of Anambra State. His sister-in-law, actress Rita Edochie, said she was struck by how swiftly he was taken, referring to an illness of barely five weeks.

Yul Edochie speaks

On Thursday, his son Yul Edochie posted a personal tribute on Instagram, saying words had failed him. Many fans set aside past controversies to console him. Ghanaian actress Jackie Appiah also mourned Edochie, sharing their last conversation weeks before his death.

Judy Austin

Judy Austin, Yul Edochie's second wife, later posted her own tribute after some fans criticised her silence.

On set

Veteran actor Nkem Owoh also paid an emotional tribute, recalling their friendship, while actor Frederick Leonard led cast and crew in a minute's silence for Edochie on a film set.

Final moments

In a long tribute on Friday, Yul Edochie said he would never forget his father asking him to hold his hand in hospital, and that his father always urged him to call every day. His brother Leo also shared what he described as their father's final wish.

  • He died in Enugu early on Wednesday.
  • He was best known as Okonkwo in Things Fall Apart.
  • Funeral arrangements will be announced later.

Fashola says Tinubu stands out because he has kept his allies for 16 years

27 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former Lagos Governor Babatunde Fashola has argued that President Tinubu differs from Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi because he has kept the loyalty of his political allies for about 16 years. He said even former opponents now work with him.

Read more

Former Lagos State Governor Babatunde Fashola has made a case for President Bola Tinubu's re-election, arguing that he stands apart from his main rivals by keeping his political allies for about 16 years.

The comparison

Speaking in a television interview on Friday, 27 November 2026, Fashola noted that Tinubu, Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi all began their political rise as state governors, although Atiku was elected governor but did not serve. He framed his argument as a job reference question, saying that Tinubu's long-time allies have stayed with him.

He also said that even some of the president's fiercest former opponents now work with him.

Background

Fashola, who succeeded Tinubu as Lagos governor and later served as a federal minister, also condemned the 'rig and die' slogan in the same interview. Atiku and Obi are expected to sign an opposition cooperation pact at a summit in Abuja on Monday.

The other side

No response from the Atiku or Obi campaigns was included in the reports.
