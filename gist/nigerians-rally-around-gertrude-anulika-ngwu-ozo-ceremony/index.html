Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.

Over to you

Should a woman who has no biological children be treated differently in her husband's family, especially when he marries another wife who has children? What happened to compassion, fairness and respect in our traditional family system?

Pochettino's Man United reign starts with 4-0 thrashing at Liverpool

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United manager ended in a 4-0 defeat at Anfield after Lenny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute. United are 19th in the Premier League.

Mauricio Pochettino's time in charge of Manchester United got off to a painful start on Sunday, 22 November 2026, as Liverpool beat his side 4-0 at Anfield.

How the game went

United's plans fell apart early when defender Lenny Yoro was shown a red card in the eighth minute, leaving them with ten men for more than 80 minutes. Liverpool quickly took advantage. Dominik Szoboszlai opened the scoring in the 10th minute, and Florian Wirtz doubled the lead three minutes later.

Liverpool kept their two-goal lead until half-time and added two more after the break through Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.

Where it leaves the clubs

The result left United in 19th place, inside the Premier League relegation zone, while Liverpool moved into the top four.

Pochettino's reaction

Speaking after the match, Pochettino reportedly admitted that the early red card shaped the game, but urged his players to turn their frustration into better performances in the coming matches.

Background

Pochettino took over at Old Trafford only recently. The club has been linked with several players ahead of the January window, including Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey.

Nollywood actress Anike Sinleke dies after battle with ovarian cancer

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Nollywood actress Adenike Abike Babajide, known as Anike Sinleke, has reportedly died after battling ovarian cancer. Her death was announced on Sunday by an Instagram show host she was due to appear with that day.

Read more

Nollywood is mourning the death of actress Adenike Abike Babajide, popularly known as Anike Sinleke, who has reportedly died after a battle with ovarian cancer.

How the news broke

The news emerged on Sunday, 22 November 2026, in a post by Bukola Fagbuyi, host of the Instagram live show Rubbin Minds With Bukky. Fagbuyi said the actress had been scheduled to appear as a guest on the programme that same day.

Her career

Anike Sinleke learned her craft under the mentorship of veteran actor Remmy Shitta and worked on film sets with filmmaker Deji Adenuga. At one point she stepped away from acting to complete her Higher National Diploma.

Beyond the screen, she was known in Lagos entertainment and cultural circles for her role with the Sisi Eko beauty pageant, which started around 2011.

Tributes

Fans and colleagues have taken to social media to mourn her, with many expressing shock at the news.

Goodluck Jonathan says dishonest leaders have made Nigeria a laughing stock

22 Nov 2026, 20:41 GMT

Former President Goodluck Jonathan has reportedly said broken promises by politicians have damaged Nigeria's global image. He called for credible elections and a political culture that rewards competence over money.

Read more

Former President Goodluck Jonathan has reportedly criticised Nigeria's political class, saying dishonest leadership and unkept campaign promises have hurt the country's standing in the world.

Where he spoke

Jonathan gave the keynote address on Saturday, 21 November 2026, at the third Paul Alaje Colloquium, themed The Brand: Nigeria, at the NAF Conference Centre in Kado, Abuja.

What he said

He is said to have lamented that Nigeria has lost much of the respect it once enjoyed internationally, and accused office holders of misleading citizens and acting against their public promises. He warned that continued dishonesty in public life would hold back the country's progress.

Jonathan reportedly called for reforms to how leaders are chosen, credible elections and real democracy within parties. He said the country needs a political culture that rewards ideas, character and competence rather than money, patronage and sectional interests.

Background

Jonathan was president from 2010 to 2015. His remarks come as campaigns for the January 2027 elections get under way.

Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey has reportedly confirmed that he is in talks with Fulham over a new long-term contract.

What he said

Speaking after Fulham's 1-1 draw with Manchester City at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday, the 26-year-old said negotiations were ongoing and that he would like a deal done quickly. He described Fulham as an amazing place to be and said the manager had shown great faith in him.

Transfer interest

His comments come amid reports linking him with a January move to Manchester United under new head coach Mauricio Pochettino, as well as interest from Italian side Inter Milan.

His contract

Bassey's current deal runs until next summer, although Fulham hold an option to extend it by a year. He captains the club under manager Álvaro Arbeloa.

The result

Saturday's draw ended Fulham's run of 17 straight defeats against Manchester City.
