Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.

Over to you

Should a woman who has no biological children be treated differently in her husband's family, especially when he marries another wife who has children? What happened to compassion, fairness and respect in our traditional family system?

Troops rescue 18 of 22 children abducted in Kaduna, search on for four

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Soldiers have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kachia/Kajuru, Kaduna State, early on Sunday. The army says the search for the remaining four continues.

Read more

The Nigerian Army says its troops have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kaduna State early on Sunday, 22 November 2026, and are searching for four others.

What happened

According to the army, armed men intercepted 22 members of the Boys' and Girls' Brigade in the Mother-Cat community, in the Kachia/Kajuru area of the state, while they were on an early-morning group road walk, and took them away.

The rescue

The army's acting director of public relations, Colonel Appolonia Anele, said troops of 1 Division operating under Operation FANSAN YAMMA moved immediately after receiving a distress call and pursued the abductors. Troops advanced towards a riverside area, where some of the children were found after the fleeing attackers abandoned them. In all, 18 were rescued.

What happens next

The army said operations were continuing to rescue the remaining four children. The attack comes days after troops foiled a suspected kidnap attempt at a school in another part of Kaduna State.

Defence Ministry deletes post promoting Tinubu's Ibadan rally after backlash

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Ministry of Defence has reportedly removed a post from its official X account that publicised the APC presidential rally in Ibadan. Critics said it breached rules on the use of state resources in campaigns.

Read more

Nigeria's Ministry of Defence has reportedly taken down a post from its official social media accounts that promoted President Bola Tinubu's APC campaign rally in Ibadan, after a public outcry over the neutrality of state institutions.

The post

The post, published on Saturday on the ministry's verified X account, announced the APC's re-election rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, and included photos showing campaign banners of Tinubu and Vice President Kashim Shettima. It said Defence Minister Christopher Musa had inspected the venue to check security and crowd-control arrangements ahead of the president's arrival.

The criticism

Civic groups and opposition figures are said to have condemned the post, arguing that it went against the Electoral Act's ban on using state resources and public media to favour or disadvantage any party, as well as guidelines that require public institutions to remain neutral.

Background

Tinubu held the rally in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026, where APC candidates in the South-West were endorsed. He later said he kept the event low-key out of respect for victims of the Bayelsa boat accident. The ministry was not reported to have commented on why the post was removed.

Correctional officers probed after two death-row inmates escape in Ilorin

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

About 20 correctional officers are reportedly under investigation after two death-row inmates escaped from the Oke-Kura custodial centre in Ilorin. The service has confirmed the probe but not the number of officers involved.

Read more

About 20 officers of the Nigerian Correctional Service are reportedly under investigation after two death-row inmates escaped from the Medium Security Custodial Centre at Oke-Kura, Ilorin, Kwara State, last Wednesday.

The probe

Those affected are said to include members of the armed squad and duty officers responsible for supervising inmates around the clock. Sources described the breach as a coordinated escape attempt involving seven inmates, two of whom got away.

What the service said

The Kwara State Command of the service confirmed on Sunday, 22 November 2026, that an investigation had begun on the orders of the Controller-General of Corrections. Its spokesperson said the probe would be thorough, but declined to confirm how many officers had been questioned, saying it would be premature while the investigation was ongoing.
