Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.

Over to you

Should a woman who has no biological children be treated differently in her husband's family, especially when he marries another wife who has children? What happened to compassion, fairness and respect in our traditional family system?

Christmas return air tickets climb as high as ₦822,000 on some routes

10 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Return tickets on some domestic routes for the Christmas and New Year period have reached ₦822,000, with Air Peace listing about ₦676,000 for Lagos–Anambra return between 24 December and 2 January.

Read more

Nigerians planning to fly home for Christmas and the New Year are facing steep fares, with return tickets on some domestic routes reaching ₦822,000.

The fares

According to checks reported on Monday, 9 November 2026, major carriers including Air Peace, United Nigeria Airlines and ValueJet have listed much higher fares for December 2026 and early January 2027, especially on routes linking Lagos and Abuja to the South-East and South-South. Air Peace listed a Lagos–Anambra return ticket between 24 December and 2 January at about ₦676,000, with the outbound flight at ₦341,500 and the return at ₦335,500. An Abuja–Anambra return costs about ₦677,000, and Lagos–Owerri about ₦671,000. The highest return fares found reached ₦822,000.

Why fares rise

Demand for flights to the South-East and South-South surges every December as many Nigerians travel home for the holidays, weddings and other ceremonies. Airlines say the high cost of aviation fuel, foreign exchange for maintenance and leasing, and a shortage of aircraft push up prices.

Background

Last week, one-way economy fares for flights around 20 and 21 December were reported at up to ₦337,500, increases of as much as 208 per cent. The Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission previously accused some airlines of price-fixing during the 2025 festive season, which the airlines denied. Many travellers turn to road transport instead, despite security concerns on some highways.

What it means

Travellers are being advised to book early to avoid the highest prices.

Court orders INEC chairman to produce Tinubu's 1999 and 2023 nomination forms in Sowore trial

10 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

A Federal High Court in Abuja has subpoenaed INEC chairman Joash Amupitan to produce President Tinubu's nomination forms from 1999 and 2023 in the criminal trial of activist Omoyele Sowore, who is charged over social media posts about the President.

Read more

A Federal High Court in Abuja has ordered the chairman of the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), Professor Joash Amupitan, to produce President Bola Tinubu's nomination documents in the trial of activist Omoyele Sowore.

The order

The subpoena, signed by Justice Mohammed Umar, directs the INEC chairman to present Tinubu's Form CF001 from his 1999 Lagos governorship campaign, which includes an affidavit relating to lost certificates, as well as the equivalent form submitted before the 2023 presidential election. It followed a ruling on Monday, 9 November 2026, approving a defence request to call Amupitan and Joe Igbokwe, a former publicity secretary of the APC in Lagos, as witnesses.

The case

Sowore, the publisher of Sahara Reporters and a former presidential candidate, is being tried over social media posts in which he described the President as a criminal. His lawyers say they intend to call both men to testify. The trial is due to resume on 11 November.

Background

Questions about Tinubu's academic records and early life have been raised by critics for years, and were the subject of legal challenges after the 2023 election, which the Supreme Court dismissed. The President and his supporters have consistently rejected claims of wrongdoing. Sowore has also faced other legal cases, including a separate cyberbullying trial in which a court recently rejected his bid to subpoena a DSS lawyer.

Jigawa declares holiday as Tinubu visits for rally and project launches

10 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Jigawa State declared Tuesday a public holiday for President Tinubu's first official visit since 2023. He is due to hold an APC rally and commission projects including an agricultural mechanisation programme with 400 tractors.

Read more

Jigawa State has declared Tuesday, 10 November 2026, a public holiday for a visit by President Bola Tinubu.

The visit

Governor Umar Namadi told journalists in Dutse on Monday that the President would hold an All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign rally and inaugurate four projects. He said it would be Tinubu's first official visit to the state since he took office in May 2023. The projects include a technical services centre, a skills acquisition centre named after the President, an agricultural mechanisation programme and a dualised road in Dutse.

The farm equipment

The agricultural package includes 400 tractors, 60 combine harvesters, 150 boom sprayers and 150 seeders, along with other equipment intended to boost farm output in the state.

Background

The Jigawa visit continues the President's campaign tour, which in the past week has taken him to Akwa Ibom, Rivers and Ebonyi states. Namadi, elected on the APC platform in 2023, is a strong supporter of the President's re-election. The North-West, which includes Jigawa, has one of the largest voting populations in Nigeria.

White House briefly locked down after car crashes into barrier near Lafayette Park

10 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The White House was briefly locked down after a driver crashed a car into a security barrier at Lafayette Park and clashed with Secret Service agents and National Guard troops. Two people were arrested; President Trump was inside.

Read more

The White House was briefly placed on lockdown on Monday, 9 November 2026, after a car crashed into a security barrier near the complex.

What happened

Shortly before 10am, a silver sedan mounted the pavement and hit a sign at the northern end of Lafayette Park, directly opposite the White House, according to reports. Witnesses said the driver got out and became involved in a physical confrontation with Secret Service agents and National Guard troops. Two people were arrested. The press area was locked down, reporters were ordered indoors, and roads around the park were closed. President Donald Trump was in the White House at the time. Officials said the driver was combative but was quickly arrested.

Background

The incident came days after the Secret Service arrested an armed man near the White House who allegedly said voices had told him to kill the President. Officials have said the White House has seen a rise in security incidents, many linked to mental health problems. No motive for Monday's crash was given, and there were no reports of serious injuries.
