Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.

Over to you

Should a woman who has no biological children be treated differently in her husband's family, especially when he marries another wife who has children? What happened to compassion, fairness and respect in our traditional family system?

CAS restores Senegal as AFCON 2025 champions, overturning CAF ruling

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Court of Arbitration for Sport has confirmed Senegal as winners of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations. It overturned in full the CAF Appeal Board decision that had handed the title to Morocco.

Read more

Senegal are once again Africa's champions after the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) overturned the decision that had stripped them of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations title.

The verdict

CAS delivered its ruling on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, reversing in full the decision of CAF's Appeal Board, which had awarded the title to hosts Morocco. With the verdict, Senegal are officially recognised as the 2025 AFCON champions.

How it started

Senegal beat Morocco 1-0 after extra time in the final at the Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium in Rabat on 18 January 2026, with Pape Gueye scoring the only goal. During the match, Senegal's players walked off the pitch for about 15 minutes in protest at a late penalty awarded to Morocco, which was later saved.

The Royal Moroccan Football Federation then took legal action, and on 17 March CAF's Appeal Board ruled that Senegal had forfeited the match, awarding Morocco a 3-0 win and the trophy. The Senegalese Football Federation refused to accept the decision and appealed to CAS.

What it means

The ruling ends months of legal uncertainty over the continent's top football title. Before the verdict, CAF President Patrice Motsepe had said the body would accept whatever CAS decided.

Court sacks Omo-Agege as NDC senatorial candidate for Delta Central

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

A Federal High Court in Warri has voided Ovie Omo-Agege's nomination as the NDC candidate for Delta Central and ordered INEC to remove his name. The judgment came a day after gunmen attacked his campaign.

Read more

A Federal High Court in Warri, Delta State, has nullified the nomination of former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege as the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) candidate for Delta Central Senatorial District in the 2027 elections.

The judgment

Delivering judgment on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, Justice Hyeladzira Ajiya Nganjiwa ruled that the NDC failed to comply with mandatory provisions of the Electoral Act in nominating Omo-Agege. The court directed INEC to remove his name from the list of senatorial candidates and barred him from presenting himself as the party's candidate.

Why the court ruled against him

The suit was filed by Professor Alexander Osondu Akpodiete, who challenged Omo-Agege's nomination, his resignation from the APC and his eligibility to take part in the NDC primary. The court held that the NDC did not submit its membership register to INEC as required, and that Omo-Agege was still an APC member when he took part in the party's senatorial primary on 18 May 2026. The judge first dismissed five preliminary objections raised by Omo-Agege, the NDC and INEC.

Background

The ruling came a day after gunmen attacked Omo-Agege's campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North, killing several people. The senator escaped unhurt.

CBN keeps interest rate at 23% despite slowing inflation

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Central Bank of Nigeria has held its benchmark interest rate at 23% at the last policy meeting of the year. Analysts had warned that election-year spending could put fresh pressure on prices.

Read more

The Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) has kept its benchmark interest rate unchanged at 23%, maintaining a tight policy stance even though inflation has continued to slow.

The decision

Announcing the outcome in Abuja on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, after the Monetary Policy Committee's final two-day meeting of the year, CBN Governor Olayemi Cardoso said members voted unanimously to hold the Monetary Policy Rate at 23%.

The committee also left the corridor around the rate unchanged, and kept the cash reserve requirement for commercial banks at 45% and for merchant banks at 16%.

Why it held

The decision reflects caution amid renewed pressure on the naira and uncertainty at home and abroad. Analysts had said the recent drop in inflation might not be enough to justify a cut, warning that spending ahead of the 2027 elections could increase money in circulation and push prices up again.

What it means

Borrowing costs for businesses and households are expected to remain high for now.

FG disowns ₦100,000 family grant portal, warns Nigerians not to share personal details

24 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The Federal Ministry of Youth Development has said the widely shared NFSP ₦100,000 family grant website is fraudulent. It warned people not to submit bank details, passwords, PINs or OTPs.

Read more

The federal government has disowned a widely publicised ₦100,000 family support grant, warning Nigerians not to register on the website promoting it.

The warning

In a disclaimer on its official X account, the Federal Ministry of Youth Development said the website for the so-called Nigeria Family Strengthening and Development Programme (NFSP) was fraudulent and was neither authorised nor run by the ministry.

The ministry urged people to stop using the platform immediately and not to upload personal documents. It specifically warned against sharing bank details, passwords, PINs and one-time passwords (OTPs).

Why it matters

The purported grant had drawn wide attention online in recent weeks, with many Nigerians rushing to apply. There are now concerns that some applicants may have already submitted sensitive personal information.
