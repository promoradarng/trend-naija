Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.

Over to you

Should a woman who has no biological children be treated differently in her husband's family, especially when he marries another wife who has children? What happened to compassion, fairness and respect in our traditional family system?

Thousands march in Haiti to praise El Salvador's President Bukele as his troops take on gangs

18 Nov 2026, 14:49 GMT

Thousands of Haitians took to the streets of Port-au-Prince and other cities on Tuesday to praise El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele and his soldiers, who are leading a tough new offensive against the gangs that have terrorised Haiti for years.

Read more

Thousands of people took to the streets of Port-au-Prince and other parts of Haiti on Tuesday, praising El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele and his troops as they take on the country's powerful gangs.

"Viv Bukele"

Photos from the marches show huge crowds filling the streets, waving Haitian flags and holding up posters of the Salvadoran president. Many carried banners reading "Viv Bukele", Haitian Creole for "Long live Bukele".

Why Haitians are cheering

For years, armed gangs have controlled large parts of Port-au-Prince and beyond, killing, kidnapping and forcing hundreds of thousands of people from their homes.

Salvadoran troops have been in Haiti since early 2025 as part of an international mission, and recently launched their first major offensive against the gangs, with reports of heavy fighting and deaths.

Many marchers said Bukele is doing what the United Nations and the United States failed to do for years, while ordinary Haitians continued to suffer.

The Bukele model

Bukele became popular at home by cracking down hard on El Salvador's gangs, sharply reducing violence in a country once known as one of the most dangerous in the world. Many Haitians hope his forces can bring the same results.

His methods have also faced criticism from human rights groups, who say the crackdown in El Salvador involved mass arrests and abuses. But for Haitians who have lived in fear for years, many say any hope of relief is welcome.

Over to you

Can Bukele's tough approach finally free Haiti from the grip of gangs, and could other troubled countries learn from it?

Peter Obi says his US trip was not to seek American endorsement

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has dismissed reports that he went to Washington to seek the backing of US lawmakers for 2027. He said his meetings focused on Nigeria's democracy and development.

Read more

Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) presidential candidate Peter Obi has dismissed reports that his recent trip to Washington, DC, was aimed at winning the backing of United States lawmakers for his 2027 bid.

What Obi said

In a statement on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, the former Anambra governor said media interpretations of his meetings with US lawmakers and policy figures had been inaccurate. He said he did not ask for an endorsement and did not present the meetings as formal support from the US Congress or any individual legislator.

He explained that the purpose of the visit was to exchange views with congressional leaders and other American stakeholders on Nigeria's development, the welfare of its citizens and, above all, the strengthening of its democracy. He said the talks focused on democratic institutions, accountability and making sure elections are free, fair and credible.

His advice to others

Obi encouraged other Nigerian politicians to hold similar international discussions on governance, and urged that public debate on Nigeria's democratic future should not be reduced to claims of foreign endorsement.

Background

Claims that the US Congress had endorsed Obi circulated online after the trip, and a US lobbyist also commented on the matter.

Juan Mata retires from football at 38

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former Chelsea and Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement at the age of 38. He won the World Cup and Euro 2012 with Spain and the Champions League with Chelsea.

Read more

Former Spain, Chelsea and Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement from professional football at the age of 38.

The announcement

Mata confirmed the decision on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, with a farewell video on X in which he symbolically hung up his boots. He thanked the game, saying that sometimes loving something means letting it go.

A decorated career

Mata was part of the Spain squads that won the 2010 World Cup and Euro 2012. After coming through Real Madrid's youth system and breaking through at Valencia, he joined Chelsea in 2011, where he won the Champions League and FA Cup in 2012 and the Europa League in 2013.

He moved to Manchester United in 2014 and made 285 appearances over eight seasons, winning the FA Cup in 2016 and the League Cup and Europa League in 2017.

Later years

After leaving United in 2022, he won domestic titles with Galatasaray in Turkey and Vissel Kobe in Japan, before finishing his career in Australia with Western Sydney Wanderers and Melbourne Victory, where he also took an ownership stake in the club earlier this year.

LeBron James sues AI betting app over alleged fake endorsement

18 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

NBA star LeBron James has filed a lawsuit against AI sports betting app Bron AI, accusing it of using his identity to market a product he never endorsed.

Read more

NBA star LeBron James has sued the AI-powered sports betting app Bron AI, accusing the company of using his identity to promote a product he never endorsed.

The lawsuit

The suit, filed on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, in a New York federal court by James and his trademark company, targets PlayVision Inc., the app's parent company, and its two co-founders. It came 12 days after James sent the company a cease-and-desist letter over what he called the unauthorised use of his name, image and likeness.

The suit argues that his endorsement is uniquely valuable and accuses the company of deliberately exploiting it.

The app

Bron AI describes itself as a tool that gathers data and odds from sportsbooks and prediction markets to help users find bets and place them on other apps. A promotional video, since removed, reportedly featured a character resembling James wearing a jersey with his nickname, Bron, and his number 23.

What happens next

The app had removed the video after the cease-and-desist letter, but had not changed its name as of Tuesday evening. The company's response to the lawsuit was not included in the reports.
