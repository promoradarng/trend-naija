Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.

Over to you

Should a woman who has no biological children be treated differently in her husband's family, especially when he marries another wife who has children? What happened to compassion, fairness and respect in our traditional family system?

Kwara suspends children's hospital chief after mothers and sick children were locked out

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Kwara State Government has suspended the chief medical director of the Children's Specialist Hospital in Ilorin, after a viral video showed mothers and sick children allegedly locked out for arriving by 7am. The board says a shortage of doctors caused a backlog.

Read more

The Kwara State Government has reportedly suspended the Chief Medical Director of the Children's Specialist Hospital, Centre Igboro, Ilorin, Dr Mosunmola Folorunsho, after mothers and their sick children were allegedly locked out of the hospital.

What happened

A video that went viral showed mothers with sick children who said they had been shut out for arriving by 7am, reportedly because they had not arrived by 5:30am. Following the outrage, the Executive Secretary of the Kwara State Hospitals Management Board, Dr Abdulraheem Malik, and members of the management team made an emergency visit to the hospital.

The government's action

In a statement on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the board said the CMD had been suspended and given 48 hours to explain her role in the incident. She was directed to hand over to the most senior doctor at the hospital pending further directives.

The board's explanation

The board said its findings showed the hospital had a temporary shortage of doctors, after one of the doctors scheduled for the morning shift was away at a malaria training programme. This created a backlog of patients waiting to be seen.

Ukraine strikes Crimean bridge, part of crossing collapses

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Ukraine has struck the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea, collapsing part of the road-and-rail crossing and halting road traffic. Russia also hit two major bridges in Kyiv.

Read more

Ukraine has struck the bridge linking Russia to the annexed Crimean peninsula, destroying part of the road-and-rail crossing and halting road traffic, according to reports on Tuesday, 17 November 2026.

The strike

Ukraine's SBU security service said one of the bridge's metal arches and the road span beneath it had been completely destroyed. The defence ministry said the central arch and spans had collapsed and the rail section had suffered critical damage. Images released by the SBU showed rubble and a gap several dozen metres wide between sections of the bridge.

Zelensky's message

President Volodymyr Zelensky described the strike as unprecedented and said it was a response to Russian attacks on Ukraine's infrastructure. He said Ukraine had repeatedly offered steps towards de-escalation but had been rebuffed.

Russia's attacks

Russia also attacked two major bridges in Kyiv, disrupting travel and damaging infrastructure in the Ukrainian capital. Road traffic on the Crimean bridge was suspended, and Russian-installed officials in Crimea urged residents not to panic.

TRCN gives all teachers until end of 2027 to get licensed

18 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria says every teacher must be certified and licensed by December 2027. Licences will have to be renewed every three years.

Read more

The Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria (TRCN) has given teachers across the country until December 2027 to obtain professional certification and practising licences.

What the council said

The council's Registrar, Dr Ronke Soyombo, announced the deadline while speaking to journalists in Abuja on Tuesday, 17 November 2026. She said the council will no longer tolerate people teaching without the required credentials and is strengthening registration, licensing and qualifying examinations.

How it works

Soyombo explained that professional certification is obtained once, while practising licences must be renewed every three years, with evidence that the teacher has taken part in continuous professional development programmes.

Education students are now required to sit the TRCN examination before completing their training, while serving teachers must also meet the requirements.

Help for non-education graduates

The council is introducing a faster route to professional status for teachers who do not hold education degrees, so they can get the training they need before the deadline.
