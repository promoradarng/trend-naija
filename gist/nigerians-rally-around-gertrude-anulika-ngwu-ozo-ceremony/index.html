Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.

Should a woman who has no biological children be treated differently in her husband's family, especially when he marries another wife who has children? What happened to compassion, fairness and respect in our traditional family system?

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf says his wife was taken from his home before she went to court

22 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly given more details about the breakdown of his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso. In a radio interview he said her belongings were moved out of his house without his knowledge, and that he was shocked when she filed for divorce in Abuja.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly opened up further about the collapse of his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, a relative of NDC vice-presidential candidate Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso.

What the governor said

Speaking in a Hausa-language interview on Sunday, 22 November 2026, Yusuf is said to have described family matters as extremely difficult. He reportedly said he and Zainab married about 20 years ago and have children together.

According to the reports, the governor said her belongings were moved out of his house and she was taken away from his home without his knowledge, but that he stayed patient and said nothing in public. He said he had even assured their children that their mother would come back, and was shocked to learn that she had gone to court, in Abuja rather than in Kano.

Yusuf reportedly declined to give details of how she left, but said he had witnesses. Asked why he was speaking publicly, he said he was not the one who had made the matter political.

Background

Earlier this week, Zainab Kwankwaso reportedly filed for divorce at an Upper Area Court in Abuja. The governor later told pensioners at Government House in Kano that he had accepted the dissolution of the marriage and still planned to marry again.

The split has drawn wide attention because Kwankwaso was long the governor's political mentor before the two fell out.

The other side

No response from Zainab Kwankwaso or Rabiu Kwankwaso was included in the reports.

Argentine midfielder Leandro Paredes, who won the 2022 FIFA World Cup with Argentina, reportedly collapsed on live television on Saturday, 21 November 2026, shortly after Boca Juniors won the Copa Sudamericana.

What happened

The 32-year-old was giving a post-match interview when he lost consciousness and fell. The journalist beside him reportedly moved quickly to stop his fall before medical staff rushed to help him.

The heat

The final, at the Estadio Metropolitano in Barranquilla, Colombia, was played in punishing conditions, with temperatures of around 32°C and humidity of about 80 per cent forecast for the day. Paredes is said to have shown signs of distress late in the first half, and his coach later confirmed it. He was substituted in the 57th minute.

The match

Boca were already 2-0 up by then, and goals from Santiago Ascacibar and Miguel Merentiel gave them victory over Brazilian side Atletico Mineiro. The win ended Boca's 18-year wait for international silverware.

SERAP sues INEC and data commission over suspected AI-generated photos in voter register

22 Nov 2026, 11:58 GMT

SERAP has filed a Federal High Court lawsuit asking for an independent investigation into suspected AI-generated photographs and other questionable records in INEC's preliminary voter register, and into whether voters' personal data was unlawfully accessed or altered.

Read more

The Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project (SERAP) has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court seeking an independent investigation into suspected artificial intelligence-generated photographs and other questionable records in INEC's preliminary Register of Voters.

The lawsuit

The suit, numbered FHC/ABJ/CS/2863/2026, names the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) as defendants.

SERAP is asking the court to compel the data protection commission to examine the relevant records and electronic data held by INEC.

It also wants investigators to establish whether voters' personal information was unlawfully accessed or altered, and whether the Nigeria Data Protection Act was breached.

What INEC has said

INEC has already acknowledged that suspected AI-generated photographs appeared in the preliminary register. The commission said its Automated Biometric Identification System flagged 268 records involving suspected AI images or other irregularities.

INEC insists the published register is not final, and says verification and data-cleaning will be completed before the register is certified on 15 December.

What the court has not decided

No court has found INEC responsible for manipulating the register, and the case has not yet been given a public hearing date.

Why it matters

The voter register is the foundation of any credible election. With the 2027 general elections approaching, questions about its accuracy, and about how voters' personal data is protected, go to the heart of public trust in the process.

Petrol prices fall by up to ₦85 a litre as Dangote suspends diesel price cut

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Petrol pump prices have reportedly fallen by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre in a week as refiners and importers compete. Dangote Refinery has, however, withdrawn a planned ₦150 cut in diesel prices pending a review.

Read more

Motorists are seeing some relief at filling stations, with petrol prices reportedly falling by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre over the past seven days.

Petrol prices

The reductions follow fresh price cuts by Dangote Refinery, importers and major marketers, amid growing competition between locally refined and imported petrol. Dangote has cut its petrol price several times this month, most recently to ₦1,070 per litre on 19 November, its lowest quoted rate since July.

Diesel U-turn

However, Dangote Refinery has withdrawn an earlier announcement that it would reduce the gantry price of diesel from ₦1,720 to ₦1,570 per litre. In a new message to customers, the refinery said the proposed ₦150 cut, which was meant to take effect on Saturday, 21 November, was still under internal review.

Marketers and businesses that rely on diesel are now waiting for clarity on the new rate.

Depot prices

Depot prices of petrol and diesel fell between 16 and 20 November in Lagos, Warri, Port Harcourt and Calabar. Lagos saw the biggest cuts, with some depots reducing petrol by as much as ₦120 per litre.
