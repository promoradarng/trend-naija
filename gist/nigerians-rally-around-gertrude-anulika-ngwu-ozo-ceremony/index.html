Nigerians rally around Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu after humiliating incident at husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony

6 Nov 2026, 08:20 GMT

What was supposed to be a day of celebration has turned into a story of humiliation, public sympathy and unexpected kindness for Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, whose experience at her husband's Ozo title-taking ceremony has drawn wide attention on social media.

Kind-hearted Nigerians have continued to visit her, offering financial help, gifts and words of encouragement after a viral video showed her being treated in a way many viewers described as humiliating.

The viral video

The video captured an uncomfortable moment during the traditional ceremony when the children of her husband's second wife reportedly refused to spray money on her, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

According to accounts shared alongside the footage, the children had earlier sprayed money on their biological mother. When the MC encouraged them to do the same for Mrs Anulika, they declined.

For many Nigerians who watched, it was a painful scene: a woman attending an important ceremony in her husband's life, yet appearing to be left out of a celebration in which she should have felt included and respected.

Years of mockery

The story became even more emotional following claims that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over her inability to have children.

Reports online suggest she eventually moved back to her father's home after allegedly facing repeated ridicule from her co-wife and the woman's children. These allegations have not been independently verified.

Strangers show her love

Nigerians who were touched by her story have decided to show her some love. In recent days, well-wishers have visited her at her father's residence with gifts, financial support and encouragement.

Some supporters have donated hundreds of thousands of naira, while others bought her a new mobile phone. More visits are planned: one support group has announced a grand visit to her village in Ukeh, Enugu State, on Friday, 6 November 2026, with supporters from Anambra, Imo and Abia invited to meet at Ekochin Bus Stop, 9th Mile.

Perhaps that is the most touching part of this story. People who have never met Mrs Anulika are going out of their way to make her smile. Strangers who watched a few minutes of her experience online have become concerned about her welfare.

A conversation about childlessness

Her story has also reopened an important conversation about how women who struggle with childlessness are treated in some Nigerian families.

Why should a woman's value in marriage be measured by the number of children she has? Why should a woman who has spent years in a marriage be made to feel less important simply because she has not given birth?

Marriage comes with its own difficulties, but no woman deserves to be publicly ridiculed or denied basic respect because of her circumstances.

Compassion still lives

Whatever may have happened behind the scenes in this family, the incident has clearly touched a nerve. The growing support for Mrs Anulika shows that compassion still has a place in society. Even at a time when many people are struggling financially, some are willing to share what little they have to comfort someone they believe has been treated unfairly.

One can only hope the support continues beyond social media and that she finds lasting peace, dignity and happiness. To every Nigerian who has visited, donated or offered her a kind word, your gesture deserves recognition. Sometimes, all a person needs is to know that somebody cares.

Over to you

Should a woman who has no biological children be treated differently in her husband's family, especially when he marries another wife who has children? What happened to compassion, fairness and respect in our traditional family system?

Super Eagles held 0-0 by Tanzania, AFCON 2027 hopes take a hit

17 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigeria were held to a goalless draw by Tanzania in Zanzibar in their AFCON 2027 qualifier, missing the chance to move to nine points. Tolu Arokodare hit the post in the first half, and coach Eric Chelle admitted afterwards that the team is in trouble.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles were held to a 0-0 draw by Tanzania at the Amaan Stadium in Zanzibar on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, a result that reportedly dents their hopes in 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying Group L.

The match

Nigeria went into the game knowing a win would take them to nine points and put pressure on the group leaders, Guinea-Bissau. Despite having long spells of possession, they could not break down a well-organised Tanzanian side.

Their best chance came in the 33rd minute, when Moses Usor set up Tolu Arokodare, whose close-range shot hit the post. Calvin Bassey fired over five minutes later, while Tanzania also struck the woodwork in the 42nd minute. Coach Eric Chelle made changes just after the hour, but the breakthrough never came.

After the game

Chelle reportedly admitted after the match that the team is now in trouble in the qualifying race. Nigeria had won the first leg 3-1 in Uyo, but the draw means they missed the chance to move above Guinea-Bissau.

Background

The game came a day after Chelle clashed with a journalist over his tactics at the pre-match press conference. Nigeria were without injured forwards Ademola Lookman and Victor Osimhen.

Nigeria and South Africa hold talks after Fani-Kayode diplomatic row

17 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Nigeria and South Africa have held talks in Pretoria over tensions following a public dispute involving Nigeria's ambassador, Femi Fani-Kayode. South Africa had summoned the envoy over his conduct after he presented his credentials.

Read more

Nigeria and South Africa have held talks to ease tensions in their relationship, days after a public dispute involving Nigeria's ambassador to South Africa, Femi Fani-Kayode, and officials of the South African government.

The summons

South Africa's Department of International Relations and Cooperation (DIRCO) summoned the envoy over his conduct following the presentation of his credentials. The row followed Fani-Kayode's complaint about being left out of a presidential livestream, which drew a response from the South African presidency.

The meeting

Fani-Kayode shared the outcome of the meeting on his X account, posting a clip of himself at DIRCO's head office in Pretoria. He said both sides raised concerns about the state of relations and the events that sparked the controversy.

He said the two countries look forward to building on the meeting and strengthening their long-standing bilateral ties.

What happened

Monguno is said to have travelled to Kukawa with former governor and senator Maina Maaji Lawan and other APC figures to campaign when the insurgents tried to overrun the area. Troops of Operation Hadin Kai, acting on intelligence, reportedly moved quickly to repel the attackers.

Gombe APC chairman and campaign DG injured in road accident on way to rally

Gombe APC chairman Mohammed Ndus and campaign director-general Sa'adu Hassan were reportedly injured in a road accident on their way to a rally in Balanga. Ndus is said to have suffered serious injuries.

Read more

The chairman of the All Progressives Congress (APC) in Gombe State, Mohammed Ndus, and the director-general of the party's 2027 campaign council, Sa'adu Hassan, have reportedly been injured in a road accident.

What happened

The two officials were travelling from Gombe to join Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya's convoy for a governorship campaign rally in Balanga North when the crash happened on Tuesday, 17 November 2026.

Ndus reportedly suffered serious injuries, while Hassan had minor ones. Both were taken to the Federal Teaching Hospital in Gombe for treatment.

Official response

The Federal Road Safety Corps sector commander in Gombe, Samson Kaura, confirmed receiving information about the crash, but said he was still working to establish the full circumstances.
