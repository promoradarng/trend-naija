Ned Nwoko asks public to stop death rumours about Regina Daniels, threatens legal action

2 Nov 2026, 18:01 GMT

Senator Ned Nwoko has made a public appeal for people to stop spreading false stories about his estranged wife, Nollywood actress Regina Daniels, including rumours that she has died.

What he said

In a statement on social media on Monday evening, 2 November 2026, Nwoko asked fans, bloggers and social media commentators to give Regina some breathing space. He said she had been through more than many people realise and that what she needs most now is love, support and the chance to recover. He said the appeal was also for the sake of their children, who he said are well cared for and deserve to see their mother supported. He added that whatever difficulties the marriage has faced, her well-being has always mattered to him, and that this was a time for compassion, not judgement.

Warning to rumour spreaders

Nwoko said he would not tolerate the deliberate spread of false information about Regina, especially made-up reports about her death, health or personal circumstances. He said his team was documenting such publications and that those responsible would be held to account through the proper legal channels.

Background

Regina Daniels and Nwoko married in 2019 and have two sons. Their marriage has been the subject of public disputes this year, and the two are now described as estranged. In a video shared on her recent birthday, the actress gave fans a glimpse of health challenges she has been facing, which Nwoko referred to in his statement. Since then, several unverified claims about her condition have spread online.

How people reacted

Many fans welcomed the statement and prayed for the actress's recovery, while others urged bloggers to be more careful with stories about people's health.

  • Nwoko says Regina needs compassion and space to heal.
  • His team is documenting false reports for legal action.
  • He says their children are well cared for.

Osita Iheme and I Go Save tell politicians: show us results, not dance moves

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Actor Osita Iheme and comedian I Go Save have criticised politicians who dance and show off their fitness at campaign rallies, saying Nigerians want competence, manifestos and results instead.

Read more

Two well-known entertainers have hit out at politicians who turn campaign rallies into dance shows ahead of the 2027 elections.

Osita Iheme

In an Instagram post on Monday, 16 November 2026, Nollywood actor Osita Iheme, popularly known as Pawpaw, urged politicians to spend their energy demonstrating competence rather than choreography. He said Nigerians want results, vision and accountability from their leaders, not entertainment, and issued what read as a warning to politicians to take voters seriously.

I Go Save

Comedian I Go Save also questioned why candidates increasingly run, jump and dance at public events instead of addressing national issues. He wondered whether the displays were meant to prove physical fitness or had become a new form of competition between candidates, and said they should present credible manifestos and practical ideas instead.

Background

Other celebrities have recently used their platforms to criticise politicians over roads and governance. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Nollywood actor Yul Edochie and his second wife, Judy Austin, have announced that they are expecting their fourth child together.

The announcement

The couple revealed the pregnancy in a new skit shared on Sunday, 15 November 2026, confirming rumours that had been circulating online. They already have three children together, and Judy has children from previous relationships. The news drew a flood of comments online, ranging from humour to disapproval.

May's new song

On the same day, Yul's first wife, May Edochie, released a gospel single titled 'Sorom', recorded with an artist called Nwando, on major streaming platforms. Announcing it on Instagram, she said there was a good reason for releasing it that day and described God as always being with her and never letting her enemies triumph over her. The lyrics draw on biblical imagery about escaping a snare and finding joy.

Background

Yul's public relationship with Judy Austin and his separation from May, with whom he has children, have been among the most talked-about celebrity stories in Nigeria in recent years. The couple's daughter was recently in the news for buying her first car.

Temi Otedola and Mr Eazi welcome their first child

15 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Temi Otedola and singer Mr Eazi have welcomed their first child, with Temi sharing a photo of tiny knitted baby socks on Instagram. The couple married in Iceland in August 2025.

Read more

Actress and fashion entrepreneur Temi Otedola and her husband, singer Mr Eazi, have welcomed their first child.

The announcement

Temi shared the news on Instagram on Sunday, 15 November 2026, posting a photo of a pair of cream knitted baby socks with a small bow and a caption counting their blessings. The couple did not immediately reveal the baby's name or sex.

Their story

The couple announced in May that they were expecting their first child. Temi, the daughter of billionaire businessman Femi Otedola, and Mr Eazi, whose real name is Oluwatosin Ajibade, got engaged in April 2022 after dating for about four years. They married in August 2025 at Hallgrímskirkja church in Reykjavík, Iceland. Temi also recently shared a message marking their marriage milestone, which drew a sweet reaction from her husband.

Background

Mr Eazi is a singer and music entrepreneur, while Temi has acted in films including 'Citation'.
