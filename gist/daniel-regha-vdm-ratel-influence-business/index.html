Social media clout without financial growth is a wasted opportunity: what Daniel Regha can learn from VDM's RATEL

1 Nov 2026, 09:07 GMT

Daniel Regha has weighed in on VeryDarkMan's growing RATEL business, writing on X that VDM had gone "from activist, to NGO business, to CEO" and "from criticizing business owners to joining the same businesses to make profit."

His post drew mixed reactions. One user replied that anyone who has not yet understood VDM is to be pitied, while another asked what VDM is supposed to do with the money he makes from social media: give it all away and own nothing because he is an activist?

Influence without a business

Sometimes one wonders whether Daniel Regha sees the business opportunities sitting right in front of him. For someone who commands so much attention on X, sparks conversations almost daily and has built a considerable following, you would expect him to have turned that influence into something financially rewarding by now.

Perhaps the fear of what people will say is holding him back.

You are only a tenant

Many influencers forget that they do not own these platforms. They are simply tenants. Facebook, X, TikTok and Instagram can suspend an account, restrict its reach or change their policies overnight, and years of gathering followers could suddenly amount to very little.

What VDM seems to understand

VeryDarkMan appears to understand something many influencers have yet to figure out: popularity has an expiry date. The same people cheering you today may move on to another personality tomorrow.

Instead of depending entirely on public attention, he is gradually converting his influence into a business. He is introducing products, building a customer base and creating something that could keep generating income long after the social media excitement fades. That is smart thinking.

People will always talk

Imagine spending years building an audience of hundreds of thousands, or even millions, of people, only to have nothing financially sustainable to show for it.

If you have the audience, build a business. Sell products, offer services, create something valuable and earn legitimate money from the influence you have worked hard to establish. If you sell something, people will criticise you. If you remain broke, those same people will still have something to say. The people whose opinions you fear will not come together to pay your rent, feed your family or settle your bills.

The real question

The advice to Daniel Regha and other social media personalities is simple: stop treating online popularity as a lifetime achievement, and find a way to turn that attention into lasting value. VDM may have his critics, but his decision to venture into business deserves recognition.

Because when the followers stop cheering and the cameras stop rolling, the real question will be: what did you build with all that influence?

What do you think? Should influencers concentrate on entertaining their followers, or should they use their popularity to build businesses?

Troops rescue 18 of 22 children abducted in Kaduna, search on for four

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Soldiers have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kachia/Kajuru, Kaduna State, early on Sunday. The army says the search for the remaining four continues.

Read more

The Nigerian Army says its troops have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kaduna State early on Sunday, 22 November 2026, and are searching for four others.

What happened

According to the army, armed men intercepted 22 members of the Boys' and Girls' Brigade in the Mother-Cat community, in the Kachia/Kajuru area of the state, while they were on an early-morning group road walk, and took them away.

The rescue

The army's acting director of public relations, Colonel Appolonia Anele, said troops of 1 Division operating under Operation FANSAN YAMMA moved immediately after receiving a distress call and pursued the abductors. Troops advanced towards a riverside area, where some of the children were found after the fleeing attackers abandoned them. In all, 18 were rescued.

What happens next

The army said operations were continuing to rescue the remaining four children. The attack comes days after troops foiled a suspected kidnap attempt at a school in another part of Kaduna State.

Defence Ministry deletes post promoting Tinubu's Ibadan rally after backlash

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Ministry of Defence has reportedly removed a post from its official X account that publicised the APC presidential rally in Ibadan. Critics said it breached rules on the use of state resources in campaigns.

Read more

Nigeria's Ministry of Defence has reportedly taken down a post from its official social media accounts that promoted President Bola Tinubu's APC campaign rally in Ibadan, after a public outcry over the neutrality of state institutions.

The post

The post, published on Saturday on the ministry's verified X account, announced the APC's re-election rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, and included photos showing campaign banners of Tinubu and Vice President Kashim Shettima. It said Defence Minister Christopher Musa had inspected the venue to check security and crowd-control arrangements ahead of the president's arrival.

The criticism

Civic groups and opposition figures are said to have condemned the post, arguing that it went against the Electoral Act's ban on using state resources and public media to favour or disadvantage any party, as well as guidelines that require public institutions to remain neutral.

Background

Tinubu held the rally in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026, where APC candidates in the South-West were endorsed. He later said he kept the event low-key out of respect for victims of the Bayelsa boat accident. The ministry was not reported to have commented on why the post was removed.

Read more

About 20 officers of the Nigerian Correctional Service are reportedly under investigation after two death-row inmates escaped from the Medium Security Custodial Centre at Oke-Kura, Ilorin, Kwara State, last Wednesday.

The probe

Those affected are said to include members of the armed squad and duty officers responsible for supervising inmates around the clock. Sources described the breach as a coordinated escape attempt involving seven inmates, two of whom got away.

What the service said

The Kwara State Command of the service confirmed on Sunday, 22 November 2026, that an investigation had begun on the orders of the Controller-General of Corrections. Its spokesperson said the probe would be thorough, but declined to confirm how many officers had been questioned, saying it would be premature while the investigation was ongoing.
