Odumodublvck says he refused to settle with man suing him over alleged assault

Rapper Odumodublvck has spoken about a court case brought against him by a man he says repeatedly filmed him without permission.

His account

According to reports on Monday, 9 November 2026, the rapper said the man gained access to his home through a mutual friend and began filming him without his consent, continuing even after warnings from him and his bouncer. He said the disagreement continued at a party when the man brought out his camera again, and that he confronted him on stage, but that they later settled their differences, with the man even dancing with him while still filming. Odumodublvck said he was surprised when the man later took him to court, accusing him of assault.

Refusing to settle

The rapper said his lawyers advised him to settle the matter out of court, but he refused. The man's side of the story has not been reported, and the allegations have not been tested in court.

Background

Odumodublvck, is an Abuja-based rapper known for hits such as 'Declan Rice'. He went viral last week after a brief encounter with Peter Obi at Abuja airport.

Gospel singer Toluwanisings buried in Ibadan after family dispute

9 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Gospel singer Olayemi Bolaji, known as Toluwanisings, is being buried in Ibadan on Monday, almost four weeks after his death, following a dispute between his widow and his family that police helped resolve.

Read more

Gospel singer Olayemi Bolaji, popularly known as Toluwanisings, is being laid to rest in Ibadan, Oyo State, on Monday, 9 November 2026.

The arrangements

According to funeral details shared on the singer's official Instagram page, the lying-in-state was scheduled for 9am to 10am, followed by a burial service from 10am to 11am at an event centre on Hope Road, Alakia, Ibadan. The announcement did not say where he would be interred.

The dispute

Toluwanisings died in the early hours of 14 October 2026. His burial was delayed by weeks of disagreement between his widow and his family over the arrangements, reportedly including where he should be buried. The Oyo State Police Command stepped in after learning of the dispute on 28 October, with Commissioner of Police Olugbenga Abimbola helping the two sides reach an agreement.

The burial

Videos from Monday's ceremony showed pallbearers in black suits carrying the singer's white casket, led by trumpeters, before he was taken to his home, where a private burial was held. Some fans remarked on the absence of fellow gospel artistes at the ceremony.

Background

Toluwanisings was a Nigerian gospel singer. The public dispute over his burial attracted wide attention.

APC manifesto promises 50,000 police recruits, 200,000 homes and 10,000MW of power

10 Nov 2026, 09:45 GMT

The APC has released President Tinubu's 109-page 2027 manifesto, 'From Reform to Prosperity Agenda 2027–2031', pledging 50,000 new police officers, 28,000 military recruits, 6% annual growth, 200,000 affordable homes and power generation approaching 10,000 megawatts.

Read more

The All Progressives Congress (APC) has released President Bola Tinubu's manifesto for the 2027 presidential election, setting out his plans for a second term.

The document

The 109-page manifesto, titled 'From Reform to Prosperity Agenda 2027–2031', was due to be formally unveiled at the Shehu Musa Yar'Adua Centre in Abuja on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. It covers security, the economy, housing, health and education, and says a second term would focus on turning the economic foundations of the first term into concrete gains for Nigerians.

Security

The manifesto pledges to train and deploy 50,000 new police officers and work towards one officer for every 400 citizens, recruit 28,000 more military personnel, and complete four new Army divisions in Makurdi, Ilorin, Jalingo and Benin City. It proposes that the civil defence corps take over static guard duties from the police, the rollout of body cameras and forensic facilities, wider electronic surveillance of borders, and the deployment of forest guards, agro-rangers and mining marshals.

Economy, housing and power

It targets annual economic growth of 6 per cent by 2031, halving youth unemployment, and extending credit to half of economically active Nigerians. It promises 200,000 affordable homes under a mass housing scheme and aims to raise available power generation towards 10,000 megawatts.

Health and education

The plan aims to raise the share of functional primary health centres from 53 to 90 per cent, extend health insurance to 40 per cent of Nigerians, cut out-of-pocket health spending from 71 per cent to below 50 per cent, recruit 20,000 more teachers, halve the number of out-of-school children and expand the student loan scheme.

Background

Tinubu faces 17 other presidential candidates, including Atiku Abubakar of the ADC, Peter Obi of the NDC and Seyi Makinde of the APM, in the election on 16 January 2027. Makinde launched his 'Reset Agenda' last week. Opposition candidates have criticised the government's record on the economy and insecurity, while the President says his reforms saved the economy from collapse.

Orji Kalu says he once sold arms to anyone willing to pay, until his parents stopped him

10 Nov 2026, 09:45 GMT

Former Abia governor Orji Uzor Kalu has said in a television interview that he previously sold weapons to individuals and countries that could pay, and stopped only after his parents told him to.

Read more

Former Abia State governor and senator Orji Uzor Kalu has said he once sold weapons to individuals and countries.

What he said

Speaking in a television interview reported on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Kalu said he sold arms to anyone willing to pay, and that he was interested only in making money at the time, not in how the weapons would be used. He said he stopped after his mother and father told him to. Asked whether he had supplied the African National Congress during the struggle against apartheid in South Africa, he said the ANC was not a specific customer and that he sold to anybody who wanted to buy. He did not say when the business took place or name any buyers.

Background

Kalu, a businessman and politician, governed Abia State from 1999 to 2007 and later served as senator for Abia North. He was convicted of fraud in 2019, but the Supreme Court later set the conviction aside on technical grounds. He has recently been in the news over comments on the Senate presidency and predictions about the 2027 election.
