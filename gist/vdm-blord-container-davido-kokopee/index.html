VeryDarkMan takes a swipe at Blord over power station business as Kokopee speaks on his rift with Davido

29 Oct 2026, 15:04 GMT

Social media activist Martins Vincent Otse, better known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), is again at the centre of online debate, this time over his new power station business and his broken friendship with Davido.

The swipe at Blord

In a video shared on Thursday, 29 October 2026, VDM boasted about the container power stations he has been importing from China under his Ratel brand. He said people first claimed the project would never work but were now asking how he managed it, adding that his critics had been complaining since he unveiled the container. He aimed the comments at businessman Blord, claiming he was the first to talk about the idea and that Blord rushed into a similar venture afterwards. Blord had not publicly responded at the time of writing. VDM also said each buyer would be limited to two units, and that he would not sell in bulk.

Kokopee on the Davido rift

Separately, content creator Kokopee, a close friend of VDM, said in an Instagram video on Wednesday that he acted as a go-between when the relationship between VDM and Davido began to fall apart. He said VDM felt let down by the singer and that a sense of betrayal was at the heart of the quarrel, while Davido disagreed with VDM's view of events. Kokopee declined to share the private details of their conversations and said his support for VDM had always been private advice rather than public backing. He also promised to speak later on a separate matter involving the rapper Zlatan.

How people reacted

Reactions online were mixed. Some fans praised VDM for turning to business, while others questioned whether he could sustain it. On the Davido rift, many fans urged both men to settle their differences, while others shared unproven theories about what caused the split.

Background

VDM and Davido were once seen as allies, but their friendship cooled publicly earlier this year. The activist has spent recent weeks promoting the Ratel power stations, which he showed off at a warehouse and in demonstration videos.

Eight soldiers and 13 workers were killed while rebuilding Borno community, officials say

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Borno officials have revealed that eight soldiers, including a major, and 13 construction workers were killed by insurgents during the 14-month rebuilding of Mayanti in Bama. Governor Babagana Zulum has now inaugurated 500 houses and a school there.

Read more

At least 21 people, including eight soldiers and 13 construction workers, were reportedly killed by insurgents during the reconstruction of the Mayanti community in Bama Local Government Area of Borno State.

How it came to light

The deaths were disclosed on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by Borno's Commissioner for Education, Abba Wakilbe, who heads the community's resettlement committee, during the inauguration of the rebuilt community. Among those killed was a major, Major Mairiga, along with seven other soldiers.

Wakilbe said work on the project began in August 2025 and was completed in October 2026. He paid tribute to those who died, saying their sacrifice made the return of the community possible.

What was built

Governor Babagana Zulum inaugurated 500 houses, 2,000 semi-permanent shelters and a school at Mayanti. He praised the committee for finishing the project despite repeated attacks, recalling that the community was attacked four or five times while construction was going on.

Background

Many communities in Borno State were destroyed during years of insurgency, and the state government has been rebuilding and resettling displaced residents in several areas.

Akpabio targets two million Akwa Ibom votes for Tinubu as Tompolo donates 31 campaign vehicles

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Senate President Godswill Akpabio has reportedly said Akwa Ibom will give President Tinubu two million votes in 2027. At the same event in Uyo, a pro-Tinubu group backed by Tompolo received 31 campaign vehicles.

Read more

Senate President Godswill Akpabio has reportedly said Akwa Ibom State will give overwhelming support to President Bola Tinubu in the 2027 elections, setting a target of two million votes.

The event

Akpabio spoke at Ibom Hall Ground in Uyo, where the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement inaugurated its Akwa Ibom executives. The movement's grand patron, former militant leader Government Ekpemupolo, known as Tompolo, donated 31 campaign vehicles and other campaign materials. He was represented by an executive of his security firm.

The vehicles are meant to cover all 31 local government areas of the state and support the movement's grassroots work.

What was said

Akpabio is said to have praised the movement's growing grassroots presence. Its national coordinator described the Akwa Ibom inauguration as another milestone in its nationwide expansion.

Background

Campaigns for the January 2027 elections are now under way across the country, with parties building grassroots support in the states.

Makinde says Tinubu has only a 30% chance of winning in 2027

23 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly put President Tinubu's chances of re-election at 30 per cent, citing economic hardship and insecurity. The figure is his own estimate, not from a poll.

Read more

Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has reportedly said President Bola Tinubu has only a 30 per cent chance of winning the 2027 presidential election.

His argument

Makinde is said to have argued that poor governance has worn away the goodwill the ruling APC once enjoyed. He pointed to food insecurity, the rising cost of living and insecurity as the main reasons he believes many Nigerians have turned against the party.

He also reportedly accused the APC leadership of dismissing citizens' concerns instead of addressing them.

Not a poll

The 30 per cent figure is Makinde's own assessment and was not taken from any published opinion poll or independent survey.

Background

Makinde is himself among the challengers in the race. His comments come days after Tinubu's APC held a campaign rally in Ibadan, the Oyo State capital.
