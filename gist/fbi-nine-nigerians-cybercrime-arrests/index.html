FBI reportedly arrests nine Nigerians in cybercrime operation targeting Americans

27 Oct 2026, 06:36 GMT

US authorities have reportedly arrested nine Nigerians wanted over online fraud schemes that targeted American victims. Dozens of phones and computers were said to have been seized in the operation. Details of the charges and where the suspects will face court have not been fully released.

  • About 53 devices were reportedly seized during the arrests.
  • The FBI has in recent weeks published names and photos of wanted Nigerian fraud suspects.
  • The suspects are expected to answer to charges in the United States.

CBN gives JPMorgan approval in principle to open a merchant bank in Nigeria

27 Oct 2026, 06:36 GMT

The Central Bank of Nigeria has granted JPMorgan approval in principle to set up JPMorgan Merchant Bank Nigeria Limited. Adedapo Olagunju, who has led the bank's West African operations since 2018, has been cleared to run it. The approval is only a first step, as a final licence is still needed before the bank can begin business.
