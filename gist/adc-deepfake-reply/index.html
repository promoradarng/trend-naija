Atiku accuses DSS of plotting to disrupt ADC rally as Sokoto seals party office

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has alleged that the DSS is recruiting protesters to disrupt his party's rally in Sokoto. Separately, the Sokoto government sealed an ADC campaign office, saying the building's ownership was unclear and citing a disturbance.

Read more

Tension is rising in Sokoto State ahead of a planned campaign rally by the African Democratic Congress (ADC), with presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar accusing the Department of State Services (DSS) of plotting to disrupt it.

Atiku's allegation

In a statement on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, Atiku alleged that the leadership of the DSS was recruiting people to stage protests in Sokoto in order to create disorder. He claimed hired protesters were being gathered at an office on Koko Road and sent out in trucks to key locations in the city. He also said public facilities had been blocked and logistical support withheld to frustrate the ADC's campaign, and warned that security officials who help suppress political freedom would be held to account under the law. He said the rally would go ahead. The DSS had not responded publicly to the claims at the time of reporting.

Campaign office sealed

Separately, the Sokoto State Government sealed an ADC campaign office on Ahmadu Bello Way on Monday, 2 November. Commissioner for Land and Housing Nasiru Dantsoho said records showed the building, once used by the defunct Savannah Bank and previously by the PDP, still belonged to the bank, and that the state had written to the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation to clarify its ownership. He said the premises were sealed after a disturbance involving party supporters and others, to prevent further trouble. A media aide to the ADC governorship candidate confirmed the closure.

Background

Sokoto is governed by the APC. Opposition parties have complained of attacks and obstacles to their campaigns in several states, and an ADC candidate's convoy was attacked in Sokoto in recent weeks. The APC and state governments have generally denied using state agencies against opponents.

What happens next

Security agencies are expected to be on alert as the ADC presses ahead with its rally.

Rufai Oseni challenges Umahi to win six million votes before comparing himself to Obi

3 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

TV presenter Rufai Oseni has reportedly hit back at Works Minister Dave Umahi, who called Peter Obi a 'nobody' and described himself as the Lion of the South East. Oseni said Umahi should first run for president and win at least six million votes.

Read more

Television presenter Rufai Oseni has responded sharply to Minister of Works Dave Umahi's recent comments about NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi.

What Umahi said

At an APC town hall in Abakaliki, Ebonyi State, on Monday, 2 November 2026, Umahi reportedly questioned Obi's political relevance, described him as a 'nobody' and called himself the 'Lion of the South East'. He has also asked what makes Obi better than past South East leaders such as Sam Egwu and Anyim Pius Anyim, and has argued that the region should back President Bola Tinubu so that an Igbo candidate can later get eight years rather than four.

Oseni's response

Speaking on his breakfast television programme on Tuesday, 3 November, Oseni reportedly said self-given titles mean little without results. He said that before Umahi can compare himself with Obi, he should obtain a presidential nomination form, contest a general election and win at least six million votes, roughly the number Obi received in the 2023 presidential election.

Background

Umahi, a former two-term governor of Ebonyi State, is a leading APC figure in the South East and has campaigned hard for Tinubu's re-election. Obi, a former Anambra governor, came third in the 2023 presidential election as the Labour Party candidate and is now running on the NDC ticket with Rabiu Kwankwaso. The South East is seen as an important battleground in 2027, and four governors from the zone recently declared support for Tinubu.

How people reacted

Obi's supporters cheered Oseni's comments, while APC supporters said Umahi's record as governor and minister speaks for itself.

Atiku's campaign says Umrah will not win Tinubu a second term

3 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A spokesman for Atiku Abubakar's campaign has reportedly said President Tinubu's Umrah trip will not secure his re-election, arguing that voters will judge him on the cost of living. Tinubu said he went to give thanks, as he did before the 2023 election.

Read more

The campaign of African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar says President Bola Tinubu's recent Umrah in Saudi Arabia will not guarantee him a second term.

What was said

Speaking on a television politics programme on Monday night, 2 November 2026, Phrank Shaibu, director of strategic communication for the ADC presidential campaign council, reportedly said political mandates cannot be won through religious display. He argued that pilgrimage should be a personal matter, not something publicised as a national event by political supporters, and said Nigerians would make their choice in 2027 based on the cost of living rather than prayers abroad. Asked whether Atiku would have joined Tinubu if invited, he said the former vice-president had supported Tinubu's earlier trips to Mecca. He also claimed Atiku was in a strong position in the North.

The President's side

Tinubu left Abuja for Makkah on 29 October. He described the trip as a chance to seek divine guidance, wisdom and resilience, and said that before the 2023 election he had performed Umrah and promised to return to give thanks if his prayers were answered. During the visit he was granted the rare privilege of entering the Kaaba, where he said he prayed for peace, unity and prosperity for Nigeria. The Presidency has rejected claims that the trip was for medical reasons.

Background

The trip has drawn comment across the political spectrum. Former senator Dino Melaye challenged Tinubu to a Quran recitation contest, while Oyo governor Seyi Makinde asked the Presidency to explain the purpose of the journey. APC supporters have welcomed the visit and the honour extended by the Saudi leadership.

Video of Catholic priest leading campaign song for Obi and Kwankwaso goes viral

3 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A video showing a Catholic priest leading his congregation in an Igbo song praising Peter Obi and Rabiu Kwankwaso's party during a Sunday service has gone viral. It adds to debate over religious leaders taking sides ahead of 2027.

Read more

A video of a Catholic priest leading worshippers in a song backing NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso has spread widely on social media.

What the video shows

In the clip, reportedly filmed during a Sunday service, the priest leads the congregation in an Igbo song saying the party of Obi and Kwankwaso is better, and the worshippers sing along. The priest's name and the location of the church have not been confirmed. The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), which ratified Obi as its presidential candidate in May, shared the video.

Religious leaders and politics

The video comes as religious leaders across Nigeria increasingly declare support for or against candidates ahead of the 2027 elections. According to reports, a separate bishops' conference has endorsed President Bola Tinubu for a second term, while an Islamic cleric was reported to have urged Muslims to vote along religious lines. Last week, a Catholic bishop warned politicians against trying to manipulate the electoral system, and a priest urged Nigerians to collect their voter cards instead of complaining.

How people reacted

Obi's supporters celebrated the video, while critics said churches should not be used for partisan campaigns and warned that such displays could deepen religious and ethnic divisions. Others said priests, like all citizens, are entitled to their political views.

Background

Electoral rules prohibit the use of places of worship for political campaigns, although enforcement has historically been rare. The Catholic Church has not publicly commented on the video.
