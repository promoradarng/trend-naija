Spain's transport minister resigns after deadly train crashes

5 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Spain's Transport Minister Oscar Puente has announced his resignation after two major train crashes in less than a year, including an October collision south of Madrid that killed 69 people. He said staying on would be inconsistent with political dignity.

Read more

Spain's Transport Minister, Oscar Puente, has announced that he will resign following two serious train crashes in less than a year.

What he said

Puente told lawmakers on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, that a transport minister in a country like Spain could not stay in office after two accidents of such severity, regardless of whether he was directly responsible. He described the decision as a matter of political dignity, consistency and empathy, and said he had decided to step down within minutes of the latest crash but stayed on to manage the aftermath and introduce safety measures. He did not say exactly when he would leave office, and confirmed he would not stand in Spain's early general election on 29 November.

The latest crash

The resignation came six days after a crash on 28 October near Urda, south of Madrid, in which a high-speed train run by the state operator Renfe hit the back of a stationary train run by a private operator on the Madrid–Andalusia line. The crash reportedly killed 69 people and injured more than 200. Authorities have blamed a combination of human and computer failures, and Puente said a red signal should have stopped the train.

Background

Spain has one of Europe's largest high-speed rail networks. The two crashes have caused public anger over rail safety.

What happens next

Investigations into both crashes are continuing.

UK court rejects Ibori's appeal to cut £101.5m confiscation order

5 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

The Court of Appeal in London has dismissed former Delta governor James Ibori's bid to reduce a £101.5 million confiscation order made after his 2012 conviction for money laundering. Ibori says he is consulting his lawyers.

Read more

Britain's Court of Appeal has dismissed a challenge by former Delta State governor James Ibori to reduce a £101.5 million confiscation order against him, according to reports.

The ruling

The court rejected the appeal on Tuesday, 3 November 2026. Ibori had sought to reduce the amount by about £4.3 million. The order was issued by Southwark Crown Court in July 2023, directing him to pay or face an eight-year prison term in default. The appeal had delayed enforcement, which can now proceed unless he takes the case to the UK Supreme Court.

Ibori's response

Ibori reportedly said he was consulting his lawyers on his next steps and expressed frustration at how long the proceedings had taken.

Background

Ibori governed Delta State from 1999 to 2007. He was arrested in Dubai and extradited to Britain in 2011. In 2012 he pleaded guilty in a London court to laundering tens of millions of dollars of public funds through British banks and properties, and was sentenced to 13 years in prison. He was released in late 2016 after serving about half of the sentence and returned to Nigeria in 2017. The confiscation proceedings followed investigations by Nigerian and British anti-corruption authorities, and Nigeria has pushed for the recovered funds to be returned.

Why it matters

The case is one of the most significant examples of international cooperation to recover stolen public funds from Nigeria.

"Her own papa?!": Rudeboy reacts in disbelief as young lady blames mother for marrying a broke man

5 Nov 2026, 18:08 GMT

Singer Paul Okoye, popularly known as Rudeboy, has expressed shock over a viral video of a young lady who blamed her mother for marrying a man who was not financially successful: her own father. The clip has sparked a wider debate about money, marriage and what children expect of their parents.

Read more

Nigerian singer Paul Okoye, popularly known as Rudeboy, has expressed shock over a viral video of a young lady who reportedly blamed her mother for marrying a man who was not financially successful.

Rudeboy's reaction

Reacting to the video on his Instagram story, Rudeboy could hardly believe what he was hearing. "Imagine a daughter blaming her own mother for marrying down, as in marrying a broke man! As in her father! Her own papa!" he wrote.

It is easy to see why the statement caught his attention. Imagine growing up, looking at your parents, and deciding your mother made a terrible mistake simply because your father was not rich.

Social media reacts

The young lady's remarks have stirred plenty of reactions, with many Nigerians asking how someone could speak so openly about her own father's financial situation.

Some believe her comments reflect a growing obsession with money and luxury among young people. Others argue that children who grow up in financial hardship may develop resentment towards their parents, especially when they see other families living more comfortably.

Some social media users have even joked that they will save the video and wait to see the kind of wealthy man she eventually marries. Since she believes her mother chose poorly, they say, the public will be interested to see whether she makes a better choice when her own time comes.

One thing about social media is that people rarely forget statements like this. Years from now, someone may dig up the same video and remind her of what she said about her parents.

The other side

Nobody chooses the circumstances they are born into, and wanting a better life is perfectly understandable. But does that justify publicly blaming a parent for not marrying someone wealthy?

Marriage itself is unpredictable. A man can be comfortable today and lose everything tomorrow, while another can start with almost nothing and become successful years later.

That is why Rudeboy's reaction has become part of a much bigger conversation about money, marriage, parental sacrifice and the expectations children place on their parents.

Over to you

VDM says his friendship with Davido began and ended over the fight for Mohbad

5 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan says the campaign for justice for the late singer Mohbad brought him and Davido together, and later drove them apart. He said Davido followed him in early 2024 and they became friends before things changed.

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has given his account of how his friendship with Davido broke down.

What he said

In a video released on Thursday, 5 November 2026, VDM said the same issue that brought them together eventually tore them apart: the campaign for justice for the late singer Mohbad. He recalled that he became heavily involved in demanding answers after Mohbad's death in September 2023, when questions about the circumstances of the death and the singer's earlier complaints about his former record label drew huge public attention. VDM said Davido followed him on social media in early 2024, which excited him, and that they became friends, with VDM supporting and promoting some of the singer's music. He said their relationship later changed, with the Mohbad matter at the heart of the fallout.

The current feud

The explanation comes amid an escalating public feud. This week VDM dared Davido's camp to show proof that the singer ever helped him financially, warned Zlatan Ibile after Davido was filmed singing along to Zlatan's song 'Inferno', and released a preview of his own diss track aimed at Davido. Several celebrities, including Oyemykke and Roskey, have weighed in.

Background

Mohbad, whose real name was Ilerioluwa Aloba, died in Lagos in September 2023 at the age of 27. His death led to protests and a police investigation, and a coroner's inquest was held into the circumstances.

How people reacted

Fans were divided, with some saying VDM's account explained the bitterness, and others urging both men to end the public quarrel.
