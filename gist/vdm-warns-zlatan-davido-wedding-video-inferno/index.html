"Zlatan, I'm coming for you, you will learn the hard way": VeryDarkMan fires warning at Zlatan over Davido's viral wedding video

4 Nov 2026, 17:26 GMT

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has once again stirred controversy after reacting angrily to a viral video of Afrobeats superstar Davido singing lyrics that appeared to mock him at Zlatan Ibile's wedding welcome party in Morocco.

What was supposed to be a moment of celebration has become another talking point online, with VDM accusing Zlatan of using his newly released song, INFERNO, to throw indirect insults at him.

The lyric that started it

The controversy began after a video surfaced showing Davido enthusiastically singing along to a line that sounded like, "I no be Vincent, I no dey mast…"

For those familiar with VeryDarkMan, whose real name is Martins Vincent Otse, the reference to "Vincent" immediately raised eyebrows, particularly because of his earlier controversial comments about masturbation.

VDM responds

VDM did not find it funny. In a video shared online, he defended his earlier statements, insisting he saw nothing wrong with his position. According to him, he would rather satisfy himself than go around begging women for attention.

He also questioned why his personal comments had apparently become material for a song, suggesting Zlatan was deliberately taking a swipe at him.

What caught many people's attention was the warning he directed at the singer. Speaking in Pidgin, he said, in effect: "Zlatan, I'm coming for you now. You will learn the hard way."

The Davido angle

The episode has also brought fresh attention to VDM's relationship with Davido. The two were once publicly friendly, and their association drew plenty of attention online. Their relationship has since appeared strained, with fans often speculating about what went wrong.

Seeing Davido sing along to lyrics VDM believes were aimed at him has only added another chapter to that conversation.

Was it really about VDM?

There is no confirmed evidence that Zlatan wrote the lyrics to attack VeryDarkMan. That interpretation is VDM's, and the viral clip alone does not show what the singer intended.

Even so, social media users are divided. Some believe VDM is taking an ordinary lyric too personally, while others argue that using a name tied to someone already at the centre of a public controversy was always going to draw a reaction.

Knowing how outspoken VDM can be, many followers are now watching to see whether this ends with his warning or turns into another public confrontation.

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has reportedly called out Afrobeats star Davido after a photo of the singer with Seyi Tinubu, son of President Bola Tinubu, circulated online.

The photo

The picture showed Davido and Seyi Tinubu raising their hands in a gesture VDM interpreted as the APC's "4+4" slogan, which supporters use to call for a second term for President Tinubu in 2027.

What VDM said

VDM said the photo was significant given Davido's status and his previous statements that he would never support the All Progressives Congress (APC). He accused the singer of contradicting himself and questioned why he would pose in a picture that could be read as a political statement.

He also said that his past claims always end up being proved right, dismissing what he called propaganda against him.

Background

Davido had not responded to VDM's comments at the time of the reports.

Isaac Fayose defends Davido

Footage also emerged of Davido and his wife Chioma sharing a meal with Seyi Tinubu at an amala restaurant in Abuja, which many took as a sign he was moving towards the Tinubu camp. Isaac Fayose, brother of former Ekiti governor Ayo Fayose, came to the singer's defence in a video, insisting Davido would still vote for Peter Obi. Davido reportedly reacted to Fayose's video.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.

Tinubu's praise singer Rarara mobbed at Abuja National Mosque

21 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Hausa praise singer Dauda Kahutu Rarara, a well-known supporter of President Tinubu, was attacked by an angry crowd at the Abuja National Mosque during Friday prayers. Security operatives struggled to hold the crowd back.

Read more

Popular Hausa praise singer Dauda Kahutu, widely known as Rarara, was attacked by an angry crowd at the Abuja National Mosque during Friday prayers on 20 November 2026.

What happened

Rarara, a prominent supporter of President Bola Tinubu, was entering the mosque with plain-clothed and uniformed security personnel when a group of people rushed at him. Security operatives tried to hold the crowd back but could not fully contain them. At one point, the singer removed his cap, which carried Tinubu's insignia.

A video of the incident has spread widely online.

Context

The incident came a week after President Tinubu's convoy was pelted with stones after Friday prayers at a mosque in Garki, Abuja, an attack for which police arrested 78 people before the Presidency directed their release.

Reactions

Paul Ibe, media adviser to ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar, condemned the violence on X while also making a political point about the incident.
