AY Makun responds to backlash after missing Cardiff show, promises free return

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Comedian and filmmaker Ayo 'AY' Makun has spoken out after facing a wave of criticism for not turning up at his AY Live show in Cardiff, Wales.

What happened

Fans who bought tickets for the Cardiff show on Sunday were left angry when the comedian did not appear. Many criticised him on social media, and some called for him to be cancelled. AY had earlier apologised, saying fans' time, money and trust deserved respect, but the apology did not end the criticism.

His side of the story

In a long statement reported on Friday, 30 October 2026, AY said veterans in the industry had advised him to stay quiet, but he felt his audience deserved an explanation. He said he had spent years performing at events where payment promises were broken, sometimes paying fellow performers from his own pocket. He said a five-hour drive from Sheffield to Cardiff, after repeated bad experiences on the same tour, became his breaking point, but added that the fans who turned up had done nothing wrong. He also complained that negative stories about him get more attention than his achievements, and said calls for cancellation would not make him give up.

What he promised

AY said he would host a free return show in Cardiff, paid for by himself, once a venue and logistics are confirmed. He said this would not replace refunds owed to people who bought tickets for the original event.

Background

AY is one of Nigeria's best-known comedians and has run the AY Live brand for many years, as well as producing hit films. His account of the tour problems has not been confirmed by the organisers.

  • AY says repeated problems on the tour led to his no-show.
  • He has promised a free return show in Cardiff.
  • Ticket holders are still owed refunds.

Brown Igboegwu welcomes son 16 years into marriage

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Actor Brown Igboegwu has announced the birth of his son, six years after the couple welcomed their daughter following a 10-year wait. He said only God gives children.

Read more

Nollywood actor Brown Igboegwu has announced the birth of a son, weeks after he and his wife marked 16 years of marriage.

The announcement

Sharing a photo of himself at the hospital on Instagram, the actor said the birth was proof that only God gives children, and said no prophet, pastor or man of God could take credit.

A long wait

He recalled that the couple waited 10 years for their first child, a daughter, and visited several churches and prayer houses during that time. He said some people took advantage of their situation, using their videos and testimonies to promote their churches.

Six years after their daughter's birth, they have now welcomed a son. He described the last nine months as the toughest of his 50 years.

His message

He urged his followers to hold on to God and trust that their prayers will be answered.

A video of Nollywood veteran Richard Mofe-Damijo, popularly known as RMD, appearing to ignore a man who tried to take a selfie with him has gone viral.

What the video shows

The footage, which began circulating on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, was recorded at a conference at the Muson Centre in Lagos. Inside the hall, RMD appeared relaxed, laughing and chatting warmly with the same man.

But once they stepped outside, the man reached out for a selfie and the actor turned away and busied himself with something else. Comedian Ali Baba, who was nearby, had stepped back to give the man space for the photo, which made the moment more noticeable.

How people reacted

The clip quickly spread online and sparked debate.

No comment yet

RMD had not publicly commented on the video at the time of the reports.
