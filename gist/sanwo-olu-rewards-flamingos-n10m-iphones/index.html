Sanwo-Olu gives Flamingos ₦10m each and iPhones after U-17 World Cup win

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has reportedly rewarded the Flamingos, Nigeria's under-17 women's team, with ₦10 million each and other gifts after their historic win at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The reward

The governor announced the package at a dinner held in honour of the players and technical crew at Lagos House, Marina, on Sunday. It is said to include a total cash reward of ₦300 million, iPhone 18 Pro Max phones, headphones and customised outfits.

Other rewards

The gesture follows President Bola Tinubu's announcement of ₦100 million for each player and for head coach Akeem Busari, with other members of the technical crew promised ₦50 million each. The presidential reward was announced by First Lady Oluremi Tinubu when she hosted the team in Abuja. Senate President Godswill Akpabio also announced a ₦100 million donation to the team after their return.

The historic win

The Flamingos made history on 7 November by beating Japan 2-1 in Rabat, becoming the first African team to win the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.

UEFA backs France's Philippe Diallo to challenge Infantino for FIFA presidency

17 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

French federation president Philippe Diallo is set to run against Gianni Infantino in the March 2027 FIFA election after winning UEFA's backing. Infantino is seeking a fourth term.

Read more

French Football Federation president Philippe Diallo is reportedly set to challenge Gianni Infantino in the FIFA presidential election in March 2027, after winning the backing of European football's governing body, UEFA.

What happened

Diallo, 63, was invited to stand at a UEFA meeting chaired by its president, Aleksander Čeferin, on Monday, 16 November 2026. UEFA said the associations on its executive committee and the FIFA Council had unanimously pledged their support. The French federation's executive committee is expected to formally approve his candidacy on Tuesday, a day before nominations close.

He is said to have emerged as UEFA's choice after Arsène Wenger, now FIFA's chief of global football development, declined to run.

Why it matters

UEFA said its members had serious concerns about the direction of FIFA and called for better governance and closer work with member associations. Infantino, 56, is seeking a fourth term at the FIFA Congress in Rabat, Morocco, on 18 March 2027.

Background

Infantino came under pressure this year over plans to sell stakes in the World Cup to private equity investors. UEFA threatened a boycott and the proposal was withdrawn. The French federation withdrew its support for him in September, and England, Wales and Scotland later withdrew their letters endorsing his re-election.

Eric Chelle reportedly snaps at journalist over tactics ahead of Tanzania return leg

Super Eagles coach Eric Chelle reportedly pushed back sharply at a journalist who questioned his formation and team selection before Tuesday's AFCON 2027 qualifier in Tanzania. Nigeria lead 3-1 from the first leg.

Super Eagles head coach Eric Chelle reportedly clashed with a journalist at a press conference in Tanzania on Monday, 16 November 2026, ahead of Nigeria's second-leg AFCON 2027 qualifier.

What happened

The session is said to have become tense when a reporter questioned Chelle's formation and player selection. The coach reportedly responded firmly, drawing a line between his job and that of the media, and said his decisions are shaped by the players he has available and the opponents, not outside opinion. A video of the exchange was shared on X.

It is not the first time Chelle has had a heated exchange with Nigerian journalists over his choices.

Nigeria arrived in Tanzania on Sunday and held their final training session on Monday. The Super Eagles won the first leg 3-1 in Uyo, so a good result in the return leg in Zanzibar on Tuesday would put them firmly on course for the tournament.

Injury worries

The team will be without Ademola Lookman, who has been ruled out for several weeks with an injury, while Victor Osimhen is also absent injured, leaving the attack short of two key players.

Romelu Lukaku retires from international football with Belgium

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Belgium's all-time top scorer Romelu Lukaku has retired from international football at 33. He scored 94 goals in 135 appearances over 16 years.

Read more

Romelu Lukaku, Belgium's all-time record goalscorer, has announced his retirement from international football at the age of 33.

The announcement

The former Chelsea and Manchester United striker made the announcement on Monday, 16 November 2026, a day after he was an unused substitute in Belgium's 4-1 Nations League win over Italy. He had missed the previous game, a 2-1 win over Turkey, with a hip injury. His last appearance for his country came in a 4-1 defeat to France last month.

What he said

In his farewell message, Lukaku thanked his teammates, coaches and Belgian fans, and said he was proud to have played at four World Cups and three European Championships. He said he was at peace with his decision.

His record

Lukaku, who now plays for Fenerbahce, made his Belgium debut in 2010 and finished with 94 goals in 135 matches, the most goals ever scored for the national team.

Fountain of Life Church removes Jimmy Odukoya as senior pastor

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The board of trustees of The Fountain of Life Church in Lagos has reportedly removed Jimmy Odukoya as senior pastor with immediate effect. It said his appointment did not reflect the succession wishes of his late father and founder, Taiwo Odukoya.

Read more

The Fountain of Life Church in Ilupeju, Lagos, has reportedly removed Jimmy Odukoya as its senior pastor, with the board of trustees saying his appointment conflicted with the succession plans left by the church's late founder, Taiwo Odukoya.

What the board said

In a statement on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, the board is said to have explained that it reviewed the church's constitution alongside recordings of the founder's wishes made before his death in 2023. It concluded that Jimmy's tenure did not reflect those wishes and said he was no longer recognised as senior pastor, effective immediately.

The board reportedly stressed that the decision was not a personal judgement against him but a governance matter, describing it as necessary to uphold the church's constitution and preserve the succession arrangements set up by the founder. It said the decision was not taken lightly.

Background

Jimmy Odukoya, an actor and pastor, took over leadership of the church after his father's death. Earlier this month, on 1 November, he remarried and presented his new wife to the congregation, in a ceremony at which Pastor Tunde Bakare officiated the blessing.

How people reacted

The news quickly became a talking point online, with many people revisiting a cryptic post he shared days before the announcement, and a video of the late founder that is said to show the role he intended for his son.

What happens next

There was no public response from Jimmy Odukoya in the reports, and the board did not say who would lead the church.
