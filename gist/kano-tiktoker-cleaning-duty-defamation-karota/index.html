Kano court orders TikToker to clean traffic agency offices for two weeks over defamation

4 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

A magistrate court in Kano has handed an unusual sentence to a TikTok user over videos that ridiculed officials of the state's traffic agency.

The sentence

According to the Kano State Road Traffic Agency (KAROTA), the court found that the TikTok user published videos containing statements that damaged the image of its personnel and exposed them to public ridicule. Magistrate Halima Wali ordered him to clean the agency's headquarters on Club Road, Kano, for two weeks and to carry out any other task the agency considers appropriate. He was also ordered to write a formal letter of apology to KAROTA's management and promise not to repeat the conduct. The agency's spokesman, Abubakar Ibrahim Sharada, confirmed the sentence.

Background

Non-custodial sentences such as community service are increasingly used by Nigerian courts for minor offences, partly to reduce overcrowding in prisons. Content creators have faced a rising number of cases over online posts in recent years, often under defamation or cybercrime laws. Rights groups have warned that such laws can be used to silence critics, while authorities say people must be held responsible for false or damaging content.

How people reacted

Many social media users found the sentence amusing and said it was a fair alternative to jail, while others said it showed the risks content creators face when they criticise officials.

The ceremony

Anambra State Governor and APGA leader Charles Soludo presided over a ceremony on Thursday, 19 November 2026, at which he presented the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima, who received it on the President's behalf.

Anambra government says Peter Obi billboard was removed over unpaid signage fee

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Anambra State Government has confirmed it ordered the removal of a billboard featuring NDC candidate Peter Obi and Senator Victor Umeh. It said Umeh paid the fee but Obi did not.

Read more

The Anambra State Government has confirmed that it ordered the removal of a billboard showing Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC).

The government's explanation

The state government, led by Governor Charles Soludo, said the billboard was taken down because the required signage fee had not been paid for Obi's image.

The billboard belonged to Victor Umeh, the senator representing Anambra Central and the NDC candidate for the district, and featured photographs of both Umeh and Obi. According to the government, Umeh had paid the fee for his own image, but no fee had been paid for Obi's.

Context

The government's explanation came on the same day Governor Soludo's party, APGA, formally adopted President Bola Tinubu as its candidate for 2027. Obi is a former governor of Anambra State.

The other side

Reactions from Obi or the NDC to the government's explanation were not included in the reports.
