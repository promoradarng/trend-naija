Zlatan teaches father-in-law his famous legwork ahead of Marrakesh white wedding

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Street-pop star Zlatan Ibile has delighted fans with a video of him teaching his father-in-law his famous dance moves.

What happened

The clip, which spread online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, was reportedly filmed at a welcome party for family and friends in Marrakesh, Morocco, where Zlatan and his wife, Davita, are holding their white wedding this weekend. In the video, Zlatan takes to the dance floor with his wife's father and breaks into his trademark 'Gbeseh' legwork. The older man gamely copies the moves as guests cheer.

Background

Zlatan and Davita recently held a high-profile wedding ceremony in Nigeria. The singer's new song 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday, recently topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart and has been seen as a response to activist VeryDarkMan, who criticised the wedding. Davido publicly congratulated Zlatan on the song's success.

Davido on the dance floor

Davido was among the celebrities at the welcome party, and a video of him dancing between two female guests also went viral, drawing playful reactions from fans.

The wedding

The white wedding took place in Morocco on Wednesday, 4 November. Davido attended with his wife, Chioma, who wore a pleated cape gown, while the singer wore a black tuxedo.

Tears at the altar

A video from the white wedding in Marrakesh showed Zlatan in tears at the altar as the officiant opened the ceremony with a prayer, even before his bride walked in. The couple had already held a traditional wedding in Lagos on 24 October and a private civil ceremony. Guests in Morocco included Davido and Chioma, Carter Efe and Pretty Mike.

How fans reacted

Fans praised the father-in-law for his enthusiasm and described the moment as heartwarming. Many said it showed a good relationship between Zlatan and his in-laws, and looked forward to seeing pictures from the white wedding.

Frank Edwards says he turned down a huge deal that asked him to remove Jesus from his songs

Gospel singer Frank Edwards has reportedly said he rejected an offer that could have made him rich enough to own three private jets. The promoters wanted him to replace the name Jesus with God in his songs.

Read more

Gospel singer Frank Edwards has reportedly opened up about a deal he rejected early in his career, which he believes could have made him wealthy enough to own three private jets.

The offer

Speaking during a concert ministration, in a clip that circulated online on 21 November 2026, Edwards is said to have explained that the offer came shortly after he travelled to South Africa to shoot the video for his song Beautiful.

Promoters reportedly offered to take his music to a global audience on one condition: that he remove the name of Jesus from his songs and replace it with God, arguing that this would make the music more widely acceptable.

His message

Edwards told his audience that the temptation came disguised as a genuine business opportunity, and warned that not every compromise looks obviously wrong at first. He said his mother's advice helped him turn the deal down.

More than ten APC members killed in road crash after Zamfara campaign flag-off

24 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

More than ten APC members reportedly died in a road crash in Zamfara State while returning from the party's governorship campaign flag-off in Talata-Mafara. The state government has suspended a planned rally in their honour.

Read more

More than ten members of the All Progressives Congress (APC) have reportedly died in a road crash in Zamfara State, shortly after attending the party's governorship campaign flag-off in Talata-Mafara.

What happened

The victims were travelling back to Gummi Local Government Area after the rally when the accident occurred. Several other people were injured.

Government response

Zamfara's Commissioner for Information and Culture confirmed the deaths in a statement on Monday, 23 November 2026, and said the state government had sent condolences to the victims' families and the wider APC family. As a mark of respect, the government announced the suspension of a campaign rally.

Background

The tragedy came on the same day that four people were killed in an attack on an NDC campaign event in Delta State.
