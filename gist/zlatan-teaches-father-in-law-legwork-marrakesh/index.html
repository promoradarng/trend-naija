Zlatan teaches father-in-law his famous legwork ahead of Marrakesh white wedding

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Street-pop star Zlatan Ibile has delighted fans with a video of him teaching his father-in-law his famous dance moves.

What happened

The clip, which spread online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, was reportedly filmed at a welcome party for family and friends in Marrakesh, Morocco, where Zlatan and his wife, Davita, are holding their white wedding this weekend. In the video, Zlatan takes to the dance floor with his wife's father and breaks into his trademark 'Gbeseh' legwork. The older man gamely copies the moves as guests cheer.

Background

Zlatan and Davita recently held a high-profile wedding ceremony in Nigeria. The singer's new song 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday, recently topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart and has been seen as a response to activist VeryDarkMan, who criticised the wedding. Davido publicly congratulated Zlatan on the song's success.

Davido on the dance floor

Davido was among the celebrities at the welcome party, and a video of him dancing between two female guests also went viral, drawing playful reactions from fans.

The wedding

The white wedding took place in Morocco on Wednesday, 4 November. Davido attended with his wife, Chioma, who wore a pleated cape gown, while the singer wore a black tuxedo.

Tears at the altar

A video from the white wedding in Marrakesh showed Zlatan in tears at the altar as the officiant opened the ceremony with a prayer, even before his bride walked in. The couple had already held a traditional wedding in Lagos on 24 October and a private civil ceremony. Guests in Morocco included Davido and Chioma, Carter Efe and Pretty Mike.

How fans reacted

Fans praised the father-in-law for his enthusiasm and described the moment as heartwarming. Many said it showed a good relationship between Zlatan and his in-laws, and looked forward to seeing pictures from the white wedding.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.

FG seeks another $500 million World Bank loan to expand renewable energy programme

16 Nov 2026, 16:20 GMT

The Federal Government is reportedly seeking an additional $500 million loan from the World Bank to expand its existing $1.25 billion renewable energy programme. Many Nigerians are asking where the results of earlier spending can be seen.

Read more

The Federal Government is reportedly seeking an additional $500 million loan from the World Bank to expand its existing $1.25 billion renewable energy programme.

What the money is for

The programme is meant to expand access to electricity through renewable sources such as solar mini-grids and stand-alone solar systems, particularly for homes, businesses, schools and health centres in areas the national grid does not reach.

In 2023, the World Bank approved $750 million for Nigeria's Distributed Access through Renewable Energy Scale-up (DARES) project, which aims to bring clean electricity to millions of Nigerians. The new loan request would add to that effort.

"Where is the renewable energy?"

The news has been met with frustration by many Nigerians, who say they are yet to see the impact of the money already borrowed.

Millions of households and small businesses still depend on generators, while frequent grid collapses and rising electricity tariffs continue to bite. For many people, the question is simple: where is the renewable energy?

The debt question

The request also comes amid growing concern about Nigeria's rising debt. Critics argue that new loans should only be taken when there is clear evidence that earlier funds have been well spent and are delivering results.

Supporters of the programme argue that renewable energy is essential for closing Nigeria's huge electricity gap, and that such projects take time to deliver.

Over to you

Should the government borrow more for renewable energy, or show results from the money already borrowed first?

Osita Iheme and I Go Save tell politicians: show us results, not dance moves

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Actor Osita Iheme and comedian I Go Save have criticised politicians who dance and show off their fitness at campaign rallies, saying Nigerians want competence, manifestos and results instead.

Read more

Two well-known entertainers have hit out at politicians who turn campaign rallies into dance shows ahead of the 2027 elections.

Osita Iheme

In an Instagram post on Monday, 16 November 2026, Nollywood actor Osita Iheme, popularly known as Pawpaw, urged politicians to spend their energy demonstrating competence rather than choreography. He said Nigerians want results, vision and accountability from their leaders, not entertainment, and issued what read as a warning to politicians to take voters seriously.

I Go Save

Comedian I Go Save also questioned why candidates increasingly run, jump and dance at public events instead of addressing national issues. He wondered whether the displays were meant to prove physical fitness or had become a new form of competition between candidates, and said they should present credible manifestos and practical ideas instead.

Background

Other celebrities have recently used their platforms to criticise politicians over roads and governance. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
