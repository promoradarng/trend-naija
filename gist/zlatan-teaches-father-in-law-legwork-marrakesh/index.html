Zlatan teaches father-in-law his famous legwork ahead of Marrakesh white wedding

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Street-pop star Zlatan Ibile has delighted fans with a video of him teaching his father-in-law his famous dance moves.

What happened

The clip, which spread online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, was reportedly filmed at a welcome party for family and friends in Marrakesh, Morocco, where Zlatan and his wife, Davita, are holding their white wedding this weekend. In the video, Zlatan takes to the dance floor with his wife's father and breaks into his trademark 'Gbeseh' legwork. The older man gamely copies the moves as guests cheer.

Background

Zlatan and Davita recently held a high-profile wedding ceremony in Nigeria. The singer's new song 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday, recently topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart and has been seen as a response to activist VeryDarkMan, who criticised the wedding. Davido publicly congratulated Zlatan on the song's success.

Davido on the dance floor

Davido was among the celebrities at the welcome party, and a video of him dancing between two female guests also went viral, drawing playful reactions from fans.

The wedding

The white wedding took place in Morocco on Wednesday, 4 November. Davido attended with his wife, Chioma, who wore a pleated cape gown, while the singer wore a black tuxedo.

Tears at the altar

A video from the white wedding in Marrakesh showed Zlatan in tears at the altar as the officiant opened the ceremony with a prayer, even before his bride walked in. The couple had already held a traditional wedding in Lagos on 24 October and a private civil ceremony. Guests in Morocco included Davido and Chioma, Carter Efe and Pretty Mike.

How fans reacted

Fans praised the father-in-law for his enthusiasm and described the moment as heartwarming. Many said it showed a good relationship between Zlatan and his in-laws, and looked forward to seeing pictures from the white wedding.

Beyoncé reportedly becomes first artist with 100 Grammy nominations

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Beyoncé's nomination for Best R&B Solo Performance at the 2027 Grammys reportedly takes her career total to 100, the most in Grammy history. Tems is among the artists nominated in the same category.

Read more

Beyoncé has reportedly become the first artist in history to receive 100 Grammy nominations, after picking up a new nod in the nominations for the 69th Annual Grammy Awards announced this week.

The nomination

The singer is nominated for Best R&B Solo Performance for Morning Dew (Donk), a song she released as a surprise on 4 July 2026. A remix featuring her husband, Jay-Z, followed a month later. She faces Kehlani, Victoria Monét, Jill Scott and Nigeria's Tems in the category.

A record career

Beyoncé was already the most-decorated artist in Grammy history, with 35 wins. Jay-Z is said to be close behind her on the all-time nominations list, now with 90.

The Nigerian angle

Tems' place in the same category means Nigerian fans will be watching this race closely. She is one of several Nigerian stars recognised in this year's nominations, alongside Davido and Ayra Starr, who are nominated for Best Global Music Album.

What happens next

Winners will be announced at the 2027 Grammy Awards ceremony early next year.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

More nominations

Tems also picked up nominations for Best African Music Performance for 'Big Daddy', and Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song for 'What You Need'. Ayra Starr is nominated for Best African Music Performance for 'Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)', featuring Angélique Kidjo.

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.

Ghanaian promoter upset after Davido reportedly performs under 30 minutes

16 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

A Ghanaian hotel owner who hosted 'The Davido Experience' in Manso Akwasiso was filmed looking upset after the singer reportedly performed for less than 30 minutes, sparking debate about the booking.

Read more

Davido is at the centre of a debate in Ghana after reportedly performing for less than half an hour at a show there.

What happened

The singer was the headline act at 'The Davido Experience' on Sunday, 15 November 2026, an event marking the third anniversary of a hotel in Manso Akwasiso owned by businessman Great Osama, whose real name is Kwaku Abonee. A video shared online appeared to show the businessman visibly upset after the short set, and another clip showed him apparently urging that the gates be kept shut as Davido prepared to leave.

The fee

Some reports claimed the booking fee was around $600,000, but neither Davido, his management nor the organiser has confirmed the figure, and the terms of the agreement, including the agreed performance time, have not been made public.

How people reacted

Reactions online were mixed, with some questioning the deal and others defending the singer. Davido had not publicly commented at the time of writing. He was also filmed warmly greeting Flavour in Ghana, a moment that sparked its own online debate.

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf confirms divorce from Kwankwaso's daughter, says he will marry again

16 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso, daughter of his former political mentor Rabiu Kwankwaso, has ended. Speaking with humour at a pensioners' event, he said the dissolution papers had been issued and that he still intends to take another wife.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly confirmed that his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, the daughter of former Kano governor and NDC leader Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, has ended.

What the governor said

Yusuf is said to have spoken about the matter on Monday, 16 November 2026, while addressing pensioners at an event at the Government House in Kano. Speaking in Hausa and in a light-hearted tone, he compared going from two wives to one to a demotion in military rank, from corporal to constable.

He reportedly confirmed that he had agreed to the divorce and that the papers for the dissolution had been issued. He also told the gathering that he still plans to take another wife, and asked residents to pray that he finds a suitable partner. He said he was at peace with the decision.

Background

The confirmation came two days after reports that Zainab Kwankwaso had filed a divorce petition at an Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja. She was said to be the governor's second wife.

The separation has drawn wide attention because of the long political relationship between Yusuf and Kwankwaso, who was his political mentor.
