Zlatan teaches father-in-law his famous legwork ahead of Marrakesh white wedding

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Street-pop star Zlatan Ibile has delighted fans with a video of him teaching his father-in-law his famous dance moves.

What happened

The clip, which spread online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, was reportedly filmed at a welcome party for family and friends in Marrakesh, Morocco, where Zlatan and his wife, Davita, are holding their white wedding this weekend. In the video, Zlatan takes to the dance floor with his wife's father and breaks into his trademark 'Gbeseh' legwork. The older man gamely copies the moves as guests cheer.

Background

Zlatan and Davita recently held a high-profile wedding ceremony in Nigeria. The singer's new song 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday, recently topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart and has been seen as a response to activist VeryDarkMan, who criticised the wedding. Davido publicly congratulated Zlatan on the song's success.

Davido on the dance floor

Davido was among the celebrities at the welcome party, and a video of him dancing between two female guests also went viral, drawing playful reactions from fans.

The wedding

The white wedding took place in Morocco on Wednesday, 4 November. Davido attended with his wife, Chioma, who wore a pleated cape gown, while the singer wore a black tuxedo.

Tears at the altar

A video from the white wedding in Marrakesh showed Zlatan in tears at the altar as the officiant opened the ceremony with a prayer, even before his bride walked in. The couple had already held a traditional wedding in Lagos on 24 October and a private civil ceremony. Guests in Morocco included Davido and Chioma, Carter Efe and Pretty Mike.

How fans reacted

Fans praised the father-in-law for his enthusiasm and described the moment as heartwarming. Many said it showed a good relationship between Zlatan and his in-laws, and looked forward to seeing pictures from the white wedding.

  • The video was filmed at a welcome party in Marrakesh.
  • Zlatan's white wedding takes place this weekend.
  • Fans praised his father-in-law's enthusiasm.

Davido's Ghanaian representative, Sabi Boi, has reportedly spoken out on the controversy over the Afrobeats star's short performance at an event organised by Great Osama in Ghana.

What he said

In a TikTok Live session on Monday, 23 November 2026, Sabi Boi claimed that the organiser owed Davido's team money, and dismissed suggestions that the promoter had paid huge sums for the show.

The other side

No response from Great Osama to the claims was included in the reports.

Reps urge Tinubu to consider state of emergency in Delta if violence worsens

25 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

The House of Representatives has asked President Tinubu to closely monitor security in Delta State, warning that a state of emergency may become necessary if political violence continues. It also told the police to set up a special investigative team.

Read more

The House of Representatives has called on President Bola Tinubu to closely watch the security situation in Delta State, warning that a state of emergency may be needed if conditions keep getting worse.

The motion

The resolution on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, followed a motion by Representative Benedict Etanabene, who pointed to a series of politically motivated attacks in the state. Lawmakers expressed concern that the violence was spreading fear and threatening peace among communities.

Directive to the police

The House also directed the Inspector-General of Police to identify flashpoints across the state and set up a special team to investigate the attacks.

Background

The resolution comes two days after gunmen attacked an NDC campaign event led by former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege in Uduere, Ughelli North, killing several people. Police have invited Omo-Agege and others for questioning, and the Inspector-General has ordered an investigation. Delta Governor Sheriff Oborevwori has called for everyone involved to be arrested, whatever their party.

Tinubu congratulates rival Atiku on his 80th birthday

25 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

President Tinubu has congratulated ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar on turning 80, despite their rivalry in the 2027 race. He recalled backing Atiku's 2007 presidential bid.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has congratulated Atiku Abubakar, the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate and former Vice President, on his 80th birthday.

The message

In a statement released on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by his Special Adviser on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, the president said the milestone deserved recognition. He noted that despite their different political paths, he wanted to acknowledge the history they share and Atiku's contributions to Nigeria.

Tinubu also recalled supporting Atiku's presidential bid in 2007.

Other well-wishers

Businessman Tein Jack-Rich was among others who sent birthday messages to the former vice president, praising his leadership and resilience.

Background

The two men are now rivals in the January 2027 presidential election, and their campaigns have traded sharp words in recent weeks.
