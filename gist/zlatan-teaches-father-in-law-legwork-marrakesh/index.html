Zlatan teaches father-in-law his famous legwork ahead of Marrakesh white wedding

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Street-pop star Zlatan Ibile has delighted fans with a video of him teaching his father-in-law his famous dance moves.

What happened

The clip, which spread online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, was reportedly filmed at a welcome party for family and friends in Marrakesh, Morocco, where Zlatan and his wife, Davita, are holding their white wedding this weekend. In the video, Zlatan takes to the dance floor with his wife's father and breaks into his trademark 'Gbeseh' legwork. The older man gamely copies the moves as guests cheer.

Background

Zlatan and Davita recently held a high-profile wedding ceremony in Nigeria. The singer's new song 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday, recently topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart and has been seen as a response to activist VeryDarkMan, who criticised the wedding. Davido publicly congratulated Zlatan on the song's success.

Davido on the dance floor

Davido was among the celebrities at the welcome party, and a video of him dancing between two female guests also went viral, drawing playful reactions from fans.

The wedding

The white wedding took place in Morocco on Wednesday, 4 November. Davido attended with his wife, Chioma, who wore a pleated cape gown, while the singer wore a black tuxedo.

Tears at the altar

A video from the white wedding in Marrakesh showed Zlatan in tears at the altar as the officiant opened the ceremony with a prayer, even before his bride walked in. The couple had already held a traditional wedding in Lagos on 24 October and a private civil ceremony. Guests in Morocco included Davido and Chioma, Carter Efe and Pretty Mike.

How fans reacted

Fans praised the father-in-law for his enthusiasm and described the moment as heartwarming. Many said it showed a good relationship between Zlatan and his in-laws, and looked forward to seeing pictures from the white wedding.

Singer Brymo has called on President Tinubu to rename the National Theatre in Lagos after the late Pete Edochie, while saying Wole Soyinka does not deserve the honour. His remarks have sparked heated debate.

Singer Brymo has sparked a heated online debate after calling on President Bola Tinubu to rename the National Theatre in Lagos in honour of the late Nollywood legend Pete Edochie.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, Brymo addressed the president directly, urging him to immortalise Edochie by attaching his name to the iconic landmark.

He also argued that Nobel laureate Wole Soyinka, whose name has been linked with the National Theatre, should not receive the honour, and spoke critically of the writer.

Background

Pete Edochie died in Enugu early on Wednesday at the age of 79, after a long battle with prostate cancer. Tributes have poured in from President Tinubu, state governors and colleagues across the film industry.

How people reacted

Brymo's comments have sparked heated debate online.

Tinubu promises ₦100m and a house to each family of NAF plane crash victims

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu has pledged ₦100 million and a three-bedroom flat to every family that lost a loved one in the NAF 931 aircraft crash in Ondo. He spoke at a memorial ceremony in Abuja.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu has announced a support package of ₦100 million and a three-bedroom flat for each family that lost a loved one in the crash of the Nigerian Air Force aircraft NAF 931 in Ondo State.

The pledge

The president made the announcement at a memorial and wreath-laying ceremony in Abuja held in honour of the victims. The package covers the families of both the military personnel and the civilians who died in the crash.

Military support

Tinubu also assured the armed forces that the government would provide better training and equipment to improve the performance and readiness of personnel.

Opposition parties confirm summit in Abuja on 30 November

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Coalition for Democracy says the second summit of Nigeria's opposition parties will go ahead on Monday, 30 November, at the NAF Conference Centre in Abuja. The meeting is expected to adopt three key documents.

Read more

The Coalition for Democracy has confirmed that the second summit of Nigeria's opposition political parties will hold on Monday, 30 November 2026, at the NAF Conference Centre in Abuja.

The details

Proceedings will begin at 10am, with accreditation opening at 9am. The confirmation followed the seventh meeting of the coalition's central leadership committee on Wednesday, 26 November. The statement was signed on behalf of the coalition parties by Salihu Lukman.

What to expect

The summit is expected to adopt three key documents and deliberate on democratic renewal, party unity and electoral integrity ahead of the 2027 elections.

Background

The summit was earlier moved to 30 November. Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde said this week that he was open to working with Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu.

Atiku attacks Tinubu over ₦842bn energy bill paid by 11 manufacturers

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly criticised the Tinubu government over reports that just 11 listed manufacturers spent ₦842 billion on energy in nine months. He said Nigeria had become too costly to produce in.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar, the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate, has reportedly criticised President Bola Tinubu's government over the rising cost of energy.

What he said

In a statement issued on Thursday, 26 November 2026, by his director of strategic communication, Atiku is said to have argued that the government had made Nigeria too costly to produce in, too costly to build in and too expensive for ordinary families to live in.

His remarks followed reports that only 11 manufacturers listed on the stock exchange spent ₦842 billion on energy in the first nine months of 2026.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC was included in the reports.

Background

Atiku's campaign has issued a series of attacks on the government's economic record in recent days, including over a drop in oil revenue.
