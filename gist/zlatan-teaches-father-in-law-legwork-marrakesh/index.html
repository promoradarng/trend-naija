Zlatan teaches father-in-law his famous legwork ahead of Marrakesh white wedding

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Street-pop star Zlatan Ibile has delighted fans with a video of him teaching his father-in-law his famous dance moves.

What happened

The clip, which spread online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, was reportedly filmed at a welcome party for family and friends in Marrakesh, Morocco, where Zlatan and his wife, Davita, are holding their white wedding this weekend. In the video, Zlatan takes to the dance floor with his wife's father and breaks into his trademark 'Gbeseh' legwork. The older man gamely copies the moves as guests cheer.

Background

Zlatan and Davita recently held a high-profile wedding ceremony in Nigeria. The singer's new song 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday, recently topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart and has been seen as a response to activist VeryDarkMan, who criticised the wedding. Davido publicly congratulated Zlatan on the song's success.

Davido on the dance floor

Davido was among the celebrities at the welcome party, and a video of him dancing between two female guests also went viral, drawing playful reactions from fans.

The wedding

The white wedding took place in Morocco on Wednesday, 4 November. Davido attended with his wife, Chioma, who wore a pleated cape gown, while the singer wore a black tuxedo.

Tears at the altar

A video from the white wedding in Marrakesh showed Zlatan in tears at the altar as the officiant opened the ceremony with a prayer, even before his bride walked in. The couple had already held a traditional wedding in Lagos on 24 October and a private civil ceremony. Guests in Morocco included Davido and Chioma, Carter Efe and Pretty Mike.

How fans reacted

Fans praised the father-in-law for his enthusiasm and described the moment as heartwarming. Many said it showed a good relationship between Zlatan and his in-laws, and looked forward to seeing pictures from the white wedding.

  • The video was filmed at a welcome party in Marrakesh.
  • Zlatan's white wedding takes place this weekend.
  • Fans praised his father-in-law's enthusiasm.

KWAM 1 backs Tinubu's fuel subsidy removal at APC campaign event

22 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Fuji star Wasiu Ayinde, KWAM 1, has defended President Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy, saying it mainly benefited a small group rather than ordinary Nigerians.

Read more

Veteran Fuji musician Wasiu Ayinde, popularly known as KWAM 1, has thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. He questioned who had really benefited from the subsidy, saying a small group of people had gained from it while ordinary Nigerians saw little benefit.

Background

The petrol subsidy was removed when Tinubu took office in 2023, leading to a sharp rise in fuel prices.

The campaign

His comments came during the APC's campaign tour of the South-West, which included a presidential rally in Ibadan.

Davido celebrates early birthday on private jet, announces startup fund for young entrepreneurs

20 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Davido's team threw him a surprise party aboard his father's private jet ahead of his birthday on 21 November. The singer also announced that his foundation will give startup funds to Nigerians with business ideas.

Read more

Afrobeats star Davido has kicked off his birthday celebrations early, with a surprise party on board his father's private jet and a new initiative to support young Nigerian entrepreneurs.

Party in the sky

On Friday, 20 November 2026, his team reportedly surprised him with a celebration on the jet. Videos shared online showed Davido seated beside his wife, Chioma, as members of his 30BG crew sang Happy Birthday. The singer, in a playful mood, licked icing off the cake before cutting it, as champagne and finger food were served.

Startup funding

In a post on Instagram the same day, Davido announced that his foundation would expand its usual birthday charity work by providing startup funds to aspiring entrepreneurs, giving them a chance to turn their ideas into businesses, ahead of his birthday on Saturday, 21 November.

Background

His birthday this year comes amid debate over his recent photo with Seyi Tinubu, which VeryDarkMan criticised.

Turning 34

On Saturday, 21 November, Davido turned 34. His wife Chioma marked the day by sharing a childhood photo of him on her Instagram story with a short, affectionate caption, which the singer reposted.

Chioma's tribute

Later on his birthday, Chioma posted a longer tribute on Instagram calling Davido her forever home, alongside never-before-seen photos from her pregnancy and birthday wishes from their twins.

Kano Governor Abba Yusuf says his wife was taken from his home before she went to court

22 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly given more details about the breakdown of his marriage to Zainab Kwankwaso. In a radio interview he said her belongings were moved out of his house without his knowledge, and that he was shocked when she filed for divorce in Abuja.

Read more

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly opened up further about the collapse of his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, a relative of NDC vice-presidential candidate Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso.

What the governor said

Speaking in a Hausa-language interview on Sunday, 22 November 2026, Yusuf is said to have described family matters as extremely difficult. He reportedly said he and Zainab married about 20 years ago and have children together.

According to the reports, the governor said her belongings were moved out of his house and she was taken away from his home without his knowledge, but that he stayed patient and said nothing in public. He said he had even assured their children that their mother would come back, and was shocked to learn that she had gone to court, in Abuja rather than in Kano.

Yusuf reportedly declined to give details of how she left, but said he had witnesses. Asked why he was speaking publicly, he said he was not the one who had made the matter political.

Background

Earlier this week, Zainab Kwankwaso reportedly filed for divorce at an Upper Area Court in Abuja. The governor later told pensioners at Government House in Kano that he had accepted the dissolution of the marriage and still planned to marry again.

The split has drawn wide attention because Kwankwaso was long the governor's political mentor before the two fell out.

The other side

No response from Zainab Kwankwaso or Rabiu Kwankwaso was included in the reports.
