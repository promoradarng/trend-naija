Zlatan teaches father-in-law his famous legwork ahead of Marrakesh white wedding

4 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Street-pop star Zlatan Ibile has delighted fans with a video of him teaching his father-in-law his famous dance moves.

What happened

The clip, which spread online on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, was reportedly filmed at a welcome party for family and friends in Marrakesh, Morocco, where Zlatan and his wife, Davita, are holding their white wedding this weekend. In the video, Zlatan takes to the dance floor with his wife's father and breaks into his trademark 'Gbeseh' legwork. The older man gamely copies the moves as guests cheer.

Background

Zlatan and Davita recently held a high-profile wedding ceremony in Nigeria. The singer's new song 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday, recently topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart and has been seen as a response to activist VeryDarkMan, who criticised the wedding. Davido publicly congratulated Zlatan on the song's success.

Davido on the dance floor

Davido was among the celebrities at the welcome party, and a video of him dancing between two female guests also went viral, drawing playful reactions from fans.

The wedding

The white wedding took place in Morocco on Wednesday, 4 November. Davido attended with his wife, Chioma, who wore a pleated cape gown, while the singer wore a black tuxedo.

Tears at the altar

A video from the white wedding in Marrakesh showed Zlatan in tears at the altar as the officiant opened the ceremony with a prayer, even before his bride walked in. The couple had already held a traditional wedding in Lagos on 24 October and a private civil ceremony. Guests in Morocco included Davido and Chioma, Carter Efe and Pretty Mike.

How fans reacted

Fans praised the father-in-law for his enthusiasm and described the moment as heartwarming. Many said it showed a good relationship between Zlatan and his in-laws, and looked forward to seeing pictures from the white wedding.

Rapper Olamide has reportedly been given the Yoruba title Amuludun by artist Nike Davies-Okundaye during a visit to her Lagos gallery. The honour came days after he released his album Time and Space.

Rapper and YBNL boss Olamide has reportedly received a traditional Yoruba honour from the celebrated artist Chief Nike Davies-Okundaye.

The honour

During a visit to the Nike Art Gallery in Lekki, Lagos, Olamide was given the title Amuludun, which roughly means the one who brings joy to the town. The veteran artist is also said to have presented him with a custom-made beaded portrait.

A show of respect

A video of the visit shared online showed the rapper chatting warmly with her, and at one point prostrating before her in the traditional Yoruba gesture of respect, a moment that drew attention from fans.

Background

The visit came just days after Olamide released his latest album, Time and Space, on 16 November 2026. He remains one of the most influential figures in Nigerian hip-hop and Afrobeats, and his YBNL label is home to artists including Fireboy DML and Asake.

KWAM 1 backs Tinubu's fuel subsidy removal at APC campaign event

Fuji star Wasiu Ayinde, KWAM 1, has defended President Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy, saying it mainly benefited a small group rather than ordinary Nigerians.

Read more

Veteran Fuji musician Wasiu Ayinde, popularly known as KWAM 1, has thrown his support behind President Bola Tinubu's removal of the petrol subsidy.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. He questioned who had really benefited from the subsidy, saying a small group of people had gained from it while ordinary Nigerians saw little benefit.

Background

The campaign

His comments came during the APC's campaign tour of the South-West, which included a presidential rally in Ibadan.

Troops rescue 18 of 22 children abducted in Kaduna, search on for four

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Soldiers have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kachia/Kajuru, Kaduna State, early on Sunday. The army says the search for the remaining four continues.

Read more

The Nigerian Army says its troops have rescued 18 children who were abducted by suspected terrorists in Kaduna State early on Sunday, 22 November 2026, and are searching for four others.

What happened

According to the army, armed men intercepted 22 members of the Boys' and Girls' Brigade in the Mother-Cat community, in the Kachia/Kajuru area of the state, while they were on an early-morning group road walk, and took them away.

The rescue

The army's acting director of public relations, Colonel Appolonia Anele, said troops of 1 Division operating under Operation FANSAN YAMMA moved immediately after receiving a distress call and pursued the abductors. Troops advanced towards a riverside area, where some of the children were found after the fleeing attackers abandoned them. In all, 18 were rescued.

What happens next

The army said operations were continuing to rescue the remaining four children. The attack comes days after troops foiled a suspected kidnap attempt at a school in another part of Kaduna State.

Defence Ministry deletes post promoting Tinubu's Ibadan rally after backlash

23 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The Ministry of Defence has reportedly removed a post from its official X account that publicised the APC presidential rally in Ibadan. Critics said it breached rules on the use of state resources in campaigns.

Read more

Nigeria's Ministry of Defence has reportedly taken down a post from its official social media accounts that promoted President Bola Tinubu's APC campaign rally in Ibadan, after a public outcry over the neutrality of state institutions.

The post

The post, published on Saturday on the ministry's verified X account, announced the APC's re-election rally at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, and included photos showing campaign banners of Tinubu and Vice President Kashim Shettima. It said Defence Minister Christopher Musa had inspected the venue to check security and crowd-control arrangements ahead of the president's arrival.

The criticism

Civic groups and opposition figures are said to have condemned the post, arguing that it went against the Electoral Act's ban on using state resources and public media to favour or disadvantage any party, as well as guidelines that require public institutions to remain neutral.

Background

Tinubu held the rally in Ibadan on Saturday, 21 November 2026, where APC candidates in the South-West were endorsed. He later said he kept the event low-key out of respect for victims of the Bayelsa boat accident. The ministry was not reported to have commented on why the post was removed.
