Osimhen to have knee surgery, ruled out of Super Eagles' Tanzania qualifiers

7 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Super Eagles striker Victor Osimhen is set to undergo knee surgery, ruling him out of Nigeria's upcoming Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Tanzania.

What happened

Galatasaray announced on Friday, 6 November 2026, that the 27-year-old needs arthroscopic surgery after medical checks found a loose body in his left knee. The problem was discovered after he played the full 90 minutes of the Turkish club's 2-0 Champions League win over VfB Stuttgart on Tuesday. The club said its doctor, Yener İnce, would carry out the operation at Maslak Acıbadem Hospital in Istanbul on Saturday, 7 November.

How long he could be out

Arthroscopic surgery is a minimally invasive procedure that uses small cuts and a camera to treat problems inside a joint. According to reports, Osimhen is expected to be out for two to four weeks, which could mean a return in early to mid-December if there are no setbacks. Galatasaray have not given an official timeline and say they will assess his recovery in stages.

Blow for the Super Eagles

The injury is a major blow for Nigeria, who face Tanzania twice in AFCON qualifying, including the match in Uyo on 13 November. Osimhen is the Super Eagles' main striker and one of Africa's most prolific forwards and was African Player of the Year in 2023. He was recently left off the shortlist for this year's CAF Player of the Year award.

Squad named

On Saturday, Galatasaray said the operation had been successful and that Osimhen would be out for at least three weeks. Super Eagles coach Eric Chelle then named a 24-man squad for the two Tanzania matches without Osimhen. Goalkeeper Maduka Okoye was also left out by the coach's choice, with Stanley Nwabali, Arthur Okonkwo and the recalled Francis Uzoho named in goal. Midfielder Frank Onyeka was dropped, while Fisayo Dele-Bashiru, Tochukwu Nnadi and Yusuf Alhassan return. Wilfred Ndidi, Alex Iwobi, Ola Aina and Calvin Bassey are among those included.

Background

Osimhen joined Galatasaray from Napoli and has been in strong form, with reports this week suggesting that a top Italian club is interested in signing him. His absence means the Super Eagles coaching staff will have to rely on other forwards for the Tanzania double-header.

Floyd Mayweather trains in Abuja as he launches Nigerian boxing programme

15 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Unbeaten boxing legend Floyd Mayweather has been training in Abuja after arriving to launch the TMT Frontrow Fight Series, a programme to develop young Nigerian boxers, ahead of his exhibition fight in Bangkok in December.

Read more

American boxing legend Floyd Mayweather has been spotted training in Abuja during a visit to Nigeria.

Why he is in Nigeria

Mayweather arrived in the capital on Thursday, 13 November 2026, ahead of a press conference the next day to launch the TMT Frontrow Fight Series, a programme designed to give young Nigerian boxers access to professional coaching, international exposure and competitive fights. Organisers say boxing academies will be set up in Lagos, Abuja, Enugu, Calabar and other cities, and the first event in the series is planned for 2027.

Training for Bangkok

Videos of the 49-year-old training in Abuja delighted fans online. He is preparing for an exhibition bout against Australian light-heavyweight Matt Floyd in Bangkok, Thailand, on 19 December 2026.

Background

Mayweather retired from professional boxing in 2017 with an unbeaten record of 50 wins, 27 of them by knockout.

Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr all nominated for 2027 Grammy Best Global Music Album

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido's 'ORIADÉ', Tems' 'Love Is a Kingdom' and Ayra Starr's 'Starrgirl' are among the five nominees for Best Global Music Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside Anitta and Angélique Kidjo. The ceremony is on 7 February 2027.

Read more

Three Nigerian stars, Davido, Tems and Ayra Starr, have been nominated in the same category at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

The nominees

The Recording Academy announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, that the nominees for Best Global Music Album are 'EQUILIBRIVM' by Brazilian star Anitta, 'ORIADÉ' by Davido, 'HOPE!!' by Beninese legend Angélique Kidjo, 'STARRGIRL' by Ayra Starr and 'LOVE IS A KINGDOM' by Tems. The 69th Grammy Awards will be held on 7 February 2027.

Davido's album

'ORIADÉ', Davido's sixth studio album, was released on 31 July 2026 and marks his 15 years in music. The 13-track project was submitted in several categories, including Album of the Year, but received its nomination in the global music category. Davido has been nominated before, including for his album 'Timeless' and the songs 'Unavailable' and 'Feel' in 2024, but has yet to win.

A strong year for Nigeria

Tems, who has already won Grammys, is also executive music producer of the soundtrack for the Hollywood film 'Children of Blood and Bone', while Ayra Starr was recently announced for Coachella 2027. Kidjo is a five-time Grammy winner.

Osita Iheme and I Go Save tell politicians: show us results, not dance moves

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Actor Osita Iheme and comedian I Go Save have criticised politicians who dance and show off their fitness at campaign rallies, saying Nigerians want competence, manifestos and results instead.

Read more

Two well-known entertainers have hit out at politicians who turn campaign rallies into dance shows ahead of the 2027 elections.

Osita Iheme

In an Instagram post on Monday, 16 November 2026, Nollywood actor Osita Iheme, popularly known as Pawpaw, urged politicians to spend their energy demonstrating competence rather than choreography. He said Nigerians want results, vision and accountability from their leaders, not entertainment, and issued what read as a warning to politicians to take voters seriously.

I Go Save

Comedian I Go Save also questioned why candidates increasingly run, jump and dance at public events instead of addressing national issues. He wondered whether the displays were meant to prove physical fitness or had become a new form of competition between candidates, and said they should present credible manifestos and practical ideas instead.

Background

Other celebrities have recently used their platforms to criticise politicians over roads and governance. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Pastor Ibiyeomie accuses fellow preachers of staying silent on Tinubu government

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Pastor David Ibiyeomie has accused many Nigerian preachers of keeping quiet about the Tinubu administration out of tribal loyalty and financial ties, contrasting it with how vocal they were under Goodluck Jonathan.

Read more

Pastor David Ibiyeomie has criticised fellow preachers over what he described as their silence on the Tinubu administration.

What he said

In a sermon shared on Instagram on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Ibiyeomie argued that many clerics had gone quiet on pressing national issues, including poverty, the killing of Nigerians in South Africa and what he described as anti-Christian laws, because of tribal loyalty and financial compromise. He contrasted the outspokenness of many preachers during former President Goodluck Jonathan's government with their silence now.

No money from government

The pastor said he had never accepted money from any government official and challenged anyone to prove otherwise, arguing that financial dependence on politicians is what silences preachers.

Background

Ibiyeomie, based in Port Harcourt, recently dedicated a 120,000-seat cathedral attended by Bishop David Oyedepo, who has also warned politicians over insecurity. The sermon sparked significant debate online.
