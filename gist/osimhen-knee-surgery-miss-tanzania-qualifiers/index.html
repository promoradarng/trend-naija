Osimhen to have knee surgery, ruled out of Super Eagles' Tanzania qualifiers

7 Nov 2026, 11:43 GMT

Super Eagles striker Victor Osimhen is set to undergo knee surgery, ruling him out of Nigeria's upcoming Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Tanzania.

What happened

Galatasaray announced on Friday, 6 November 2026, that the 27-year-old needs arthroscopic surgery after medical checks found a loose body in his left knee. The problem was discovered after he played the full 90 minutes of the Turkish club's 2-0 Champions League win over VfB Stuttgart on Tuesday. The club said its doctor, Yener İnce, would carry out the operation at Maslak Acıbadem Hospital in Istanbul on Saturday, 7 November.

How long he could be out

Arthroscopic surgery is a minimally invasive procedure that uses small cuts and a camera to treat problems inside a joint. According to reports, Osimhen is expected to be out for two to four weeks, which could mean a return in early to mid-December if there are no setbacks. Galatasaray have not given an official timeline and say they will assess his recovery in stages.

Blow for the Super Eagles

The injury is a major blow for Nigeria, who face Tanzania twice in AFCON qualifying, including the match in Uyo on 13 November. Osimhen is the Super Eagles' main striker and one of Africa's most prolific forwards and was African Player of the Year in 2023. He was recently left off the shortlist for this year's CAF Player of the Year award.

Squad named

On Saturday, Galatasaray said the operation had been successful and that Osimhen would be out for at least three weeks. Super Eagles coach Eric Chelle then named a 24-man squad for the two Tanzania matches without Osimhen. Goalkeeper Maduka Okoye was also left out by the coach's choice, with Stanley Nwabali, Arthur Okonkwo and the recalled Francis Uzoho named in goal. Midfielder Frank Onyeka was dropped, while Fisayo Dele-Bashiru, Tochukwu Nnadi and Yusuf Alhassan return. Wilfred Ndidi, Alex Iwobi, Ola Aina and Calvin Bassey are among those included.

Background

Osimhen joined Galatasaray from Napoli and has been in strong form, with reports this week suggesting that a top Italian club is interested in signing him. His absence means the Super Eagles coaching staff will have to rely on other forwards for the Tanzania double-header.

  • A loose body was found in Osimhen's left knee.

  • He will miss the AFCON qualifiers against Tanzania.

Romelu Lukaku retires from international football with Belgium

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Belgium's all-time top scorer Romelu Lukaku has retired from international football at 33. He scored 94 goals in 135 appearances over 16 years.

Read more

Romelu Lukaku, Belgium's all-time record goalscorer, has announced his retirement from international football at the age of 33.

The announcement

The former Chelsea and Manchester United striker made the announcement on Monday, 16 November 2026, a day after he was an unused substitute in Belgium's 4-1 Nations League win over Italy. He had missed the previous game, a 2-1 win over Turkey, with a hip injury. His last appearance for his country came in a 4-1 defeat to France last month.

What he said

In his farewell message, Lukaku thanked his teammates, coaches and Belgian fans, and said he was proud to have played at four World Cups and three European Championships. He said he was at peace with his decision.

His record

Lukaku, who now plays for Fenerbahce, made his Belgium debut in 2010 and finished with 94 goals in 135 matches, the most goals ever scored for the national team.

American boxing legend Floyd Mayweather has been spotted training in Abuja during a visit to Nigeria.

Why he is in Nigeria

Mayweather arrived in the capital on Thursday, 13 November 2026, ahead of a press conference the next day to launch the TMT Frontrow Fight Series, a programme designed to give young Nigerian boxers access to professional coaching, international exposure and competitive fights. Police say 78 arrested over Tinubu convoy incident, now being released after presidential clemency

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

The FCT Police Command says it arrested 78 people after the president's motorcade was reportedly pelted with stones in Garki, Abuja, on Friday. It says the suspects are now being warned and released after the Presidency extended clemency to them.

Read more

The Federal Capital Territory Police Command has reportedly said that 78 people were arrested in connection with an incident involving President Bola Tinubu's motorcade in Abuja last Friday, and that all of them are now being released on the Presidency's directive.

What the police said

In a statement on Monday, 16 November 2026, the command's spokesperson, CSP Josephine Adeh, said the incident happened on Friday, 13 November, shortly after the President observed Friday prayers at a mosque in the Garki District. According to the police account, some people threw stones and other objects at the departing convoy, damaging several vehicles and injuring some security personnel.

The police said a joint security team responded at the scene and that later intelligence-led raids in the area brought the total number of people arrested to 78.

Clemency and release

The statement said the Presidency had extended clemency to those involved and directed that the suspects be warned and released. The command said they were going through the release process.

Earlier denial

The Presidency had earlier dismissed reports that the convoy was attacked, while some opposition figures, including former senator Dino Melaye, insisted the incident happened and shared videos online. The police statement is the first official account to describe arrests in connection with it.

Call for calm

The FCT Commissioner of Police, CP Ahmed Mohammed Sanusi, warned against violence and destruction of property as campaigns for the 2027 general elections intensify. He urged political parties, politicians and their supporters to avoid intimidation and inflammatory statements, and directed area commanders and tactical units to step up security operations across the territory.

Robert Pattinson reportedly named the new James Bond

16 Nov 2026, 17:45 GMT

Robert Pattinson has reportedly been confirmed as the next James Bond, ending years of speculation over who would follow Daniel Craig. He is said to star in the upcoming 007 film directed by Denis Villeneuve.

Read more

British actor Robert Pattinson has reportedly been named the next James Bond, ending years of guessing over who would take over the role of the famous secret agent.

The announcement

The franchise is said to have confirmed the casting on Monday, 16 November 2026, with a short video showing the 40-year-old actor in a black tuxedo, pointing a gun. Pattinson is best known for the Twilight films and for currently playing Bruce Wayne in DC's Batman films.

The race for the role

Several actors, including Callum Turner, Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor, were reportedly screen-tested before the casting was announced for the new film, which will be directed by Denis Villeneuve. In a recent magazine interview, Villeneuve spoke about the pressure of taking on the franchise and said casting was still in progress at the time.

Background

Daniel Craig stepped down as 007 after five films over 15 years, ending with No Time to Die in October 2021. He was the sixth actor to play the character, after Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton and Pierce Brosnan. If confirmed, Pattinson would become the seventh actor to play Bond in the official film series.

What happens next

No release date for the new Bond film was given in the reports.
