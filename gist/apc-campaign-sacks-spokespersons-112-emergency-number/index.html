APC campaign council reportedly sacks spokespersons after embarrassing 112 emergency number incident on live TV

11 Nov 2026, 20:22 GMT

The All Progressives Congress (APC) Presidential Campaign Council has reportedly removed some of its spokespersons following the controversy over a failed attempt to demonstrate Nigeria's 112 emergency number on live television.

Only three can now speak

According to reports, President Bola Tinubu's Chief of Staff, Femi Gbajabiamila, announced on Wednesday that the spokespersons earlier named by the campaign council had been removed.

He said only three officials are now authorised to speak on behalf of the council: Senator Abdulaziz Yari, the Director General; Minister of Solid Minerals Dele Alake; and Minister of Information Mohammed Idris.

What happened on live TV

It all started on Tuesday, when APC official Alwan Hassan appeared as a guest on a live television politics programme.

While discussing the achievements of the APC-led government, Hassan pointed to the 112 emergency number as an example of a working public service. He explained that Nigerians could dial it whenever they needed urgent help, whether in a medical emergency, a security threat or a road accident.

To prove his point, he challenged the host to dial 112 right there in the studio.

That was where things took an unexpected turn. The host tried the number, but the call failed to connect. He tried again, with the same result.

Hassan insisted the line was working and suggested network problems might have stopped the calls going through. His explanation did little to stop the criticism that followed.

The backlash

Clips of the interview spread quickly online, with Nigerians questioning the reliability of a service people are meant to depend on in life-threatening situations. Opposition figures also seized on the incident to challenge the government's claims about improved public services.

The real question

Is removing spokespersons really the answer, or should the government be more concerned about finding out why the emergency calls failed?

Someone calling 112 during an armed robbery, a serious accident or a medical emergency may not have the luxury of trying again and again until the network connects.

One failed demonstration does not prove the whole emergency system is broken. But it raises questions that deserve proper answers, and for many Nigerians, those answers matter far more than who is allowed to speak for a political campaign.

SERAP sues INEC and data commission over suspected AI-generated photos in voter register

22 Nov 2026, 11:58 GMT

SERAP has filed a Federal High Court lawsuit asking for an independent investigation into suspected AI-generated photographs and other questionable records in INEC's preliminary voter register, and into whether voters' personal data was unlawfully accessed or altered.

Read more

The Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project (SERAP) has filed a lawsuit at the Federal High Court seeking an independent investigation into suspected artificial intelligence-generated photographs and other questionable records in INEC's preliminary Register of Voters.

The lawsuit

The suit, numbered FHC/ABJ/CS/2863/2026, names the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) and the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) as defendants.

SERAP is asking the court to compel the data protection commission to examine the relevant records and electronic data held by INEC.

It also wants investigators to establish whether voters' personal information was unlawfully accessed or altered, and whether the Nigeria Data Protection Act was breached.

What INEC has said

INEC has already acknowledged that suspected AI-generated photographs appeared in the preliminary register. The commission said its Automated Biometric Identification System flagged 268 records involving suspected AI images or other irregularities.

INEC insists the published register is not final, and says verification and data-cleaning will be completed before the register is certified on 15 December.

What the court has not decided

No court has found INEC responsible for manipulating the register, and the case has not yet been given a public hearing date.

Why it matters

The voter register is the foundation of any credible election. With the 2027 general elections approaching, questions about its accuracy, and about how voters' personal data is protected, go to the heart of public trust in the process.

Petrol prices fall by up to ₦85 a litre as Dangote suspends diesel price cut

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Petrol pump prices have reportedly fallen by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre in a week as refiners and importers compete. Dangote Refinery has, however, withdrawn a planned ₦150 cut in diesel prices pending a review.

Read more

Motorists are seeing some relief at filling stations, with petrol prices reportedly falling by between ₦25 and ₦85 per litre over the past seven days.

Petrol prices

The reductions follow fresh price cuts by Dangote Refinery, importers and major marketers, amid growing competition between locally refined and imported petrol. Dangote has cut its petrol price several times this month, most recently to ₦1,105 per litre at its depot.

Diesel U-turn

However, Dangote Refinery has withdrawn an earlier announcement that it would reduce the gantry price of diesel from ₦1,720 to ₦1,570 per litre. In a new message to customers, the refinery said the proposed ₦150 cut, which was meant to take effect on Saturday, 21 November, was still under internal review.

Marketers and businesses that rely on diesel are now waiting for clarity on the new rate.

Troops foil suspected kidnap attempt at Kaduna school

22 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Soldiers reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at a college in Zangon Kataf, Kaduna State. A headcount showed no student was missing.

Read more

Troops have reportedly foiled a suspected kidnapping attempt at Tafawa Balewa Memorial Commercial College in Samaru, Zangon Kataf Local Government Area of Kaduna State.

What happened

The incident happened at about 11:56pm on 18 November 2026, after reports of suspicious movements by unidentified people around the school. Soldiers responded quickly and moved to the school, forcing the suspects to abandon their plan and flee.

All students safe

The troops searched the school and surrounding areas but did not make contact with the fleeing suspects. A headcount carried out with the school authorities showed that no student or other occupant was missing.

Security stepped up

The troops have continued patrols in the area to prevent further attacks.

A video of former First Lady Aisha Buhari, widow of the late President Muhammadu Buhari, wearing clothing printed with President Bola Tinubu's campaign logo has stirred political debate ahead of the 2027 elections.

The video

The clip was posted on Saturday, 21 November 2026, by former presidential aide Bashir Ahmad, with a caption describing it as a message from Daura, Buhari's hometown.

Why it matters

The video came days after Solomon Dalung, a chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), claimed in a television interview that Aisha Buhari had visited ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar and told him that the late President's large voter base would be backing him in 2027.

Many social media users took the new video as a response to that claim, suggesting the former First Lady is supporting Tinubu.

No statement

Aisha Buhari herself had not made a public statement on her political preference at the time of the reports.
