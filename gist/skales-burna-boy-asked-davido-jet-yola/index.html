Skales says Burna Boy once asked Davido for space on his private jet

4 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Singer Skales has shared a story from the early days of his career involving two of Nigeria's biggest stars, Burna Boy and Davido.

What he said

In comments that spread on Wednesday, 4 November 2026, Skales said he and Burna Boy were booked to perform their collaboration 'Temper' at a show in Yola. According to him, Burna Boy contacted Davido and asked if they could travel on his private jet, and Davido agreed. Because the plane was already full, Davido reportedly asked one of his team members to stay behind and fly the next day. Skales said the jet was still so crowded that one person had to sit on the toilet seat during the flight.

A lucky break

Skales reportedly said the Yola trip led to an important connection for him with the international act Major Lazer, which helped his career.

Background

'Temper', released in 2014, was one of Skales's biggest hits and featured Burna Boy. Burna Boy and Davido have had a complicated relationship over the years, with periods of rivalry among their fans, although they have also shown respect for each other in public. Both are now global stars, with Burna Boy winning a Grammy in 2021 and Davido receiving multiple Grammy nominations.

How people reacted

Fans enjoyed the throwback, with some joking about the toilet seat and others saying it showed that the stars once helped each other.

Singer Brymo has sparked a heated online debate after calling on President Bola Tinubu to rename the National Theatre in Lagos in honour of the late Nollywood legend Pete Edochie.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, Brymo addressed the president directly, urging him to immortalise Edochie by attaching his name to the iconic landmark.

Davido's Ghana rep says promoter owes money over short concert performance

Davido's Ghanaian representative has reportedly blamed an unresolved payment dispute for the singer's brief performance at an event organised by Great Osama. He claimed the balance due on arrival was never paid as agreed.

Read more

Davido's Ghanaian representative, Sabi Boi, has reportedly spoken out on the controversy over the Afrobeats star's short performance at an event organised by Great Osama in Ghana.

What he said

In a TikTok Live session on Monday, 23 November 2026, Sabi Boi claimed that the organiser owed Davido's team money, and dismissed suggestions that the promoter had paid huge sums for the show.

The alleged deal

According to him, the arrangement was for the organiser to pay a deposit before the show and the rest once Davido arrived at the venue, but the second payment was not made as agreed. He also claimed the promoter tried to settle the balance with gold instead of cash.

The other side

No response from Great Osama to the claims was included in the reports.

Atiku challenges Tinubu to explain ₦1.44tn drop in oil revenue

25 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly asked President Tinubu to explain a ₦1.44 trillion fall in oil revenue in the first half of 2026, citing Budget Office figures. His campaign says collections were far below target.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly challenged President Bola Tinubu to publicly explain a ₦1.44 trillion drop in Nigeria's gross oil revenue in the first half of 2026.

The figures

In a statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, Atiku's campaign cited figures from the government's own Budget Office. It said the government collected ₦7.88 trillion in oil revenue between January and June 2026, well below its half-year target of ₦18.65 trillion, leaving a shortfall of about ₦10.78 trillion.

The campaign argued that the drop came even as international crude prices rose in the second quarter of the year.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC campaign was included in the reports.

Background

Atiku, who turned 80 on Wednesday, is the ADC's candidate in the January 2027 election. President Tinubu sent him a birthday message despite their rivalry.
