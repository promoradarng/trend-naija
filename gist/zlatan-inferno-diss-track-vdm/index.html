Zlatan's diss track 'Inferno' tops Apple Music Nigeria as VeryDarkMan responds

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Rapper Zlatan Ibile has scored a chart-topping hit with a song widely seen as a response to social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), and VDM has not stayed quiet.

The song

'Inferno', which features Peruzzi and Shoday, reportedly went to number one on the Apple Music Nigeria top songs chart and the Nigerian iTunes chart, according to reports on Friday, 30 October 2026. It also reportedly topped the chart in Sierra Leone. Fans have described it as a diss track aimed at VDM.

VDM's response

VDM reacted on his Instagram story with mockery, brushing off the chart success. Before the song reached number one, he had sarcastically challenged it to stay at the top for a full month and described the buzz around it as forced publicity.

Background

The feud between the two goes back to the period after the death of singer Mohbad in 2023, when VDM publicly pressured Zlatan and other music figures to speak out. More recently, VDM criticised Zlatan's high-profile wedding to Davita, claiming it was used to promote an album that did not do well. Zlatan has rarely responded directly, which is why many fans see 'Inferno' as his long-awaited reply.

How people reacted

Many users mocked VDM's response, with some calling it damage control, while his supporters said the song's success would not last. VDM has been involved in several online disputes this week, including with businessman Blord, former BBNaija winner Phyna and Davido's camp.

  • The feud dates back to the period after Mohbad's death.

INEC asks court to cancel subpoena for Tinubu's 1999 and 2023 election records

11 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

INEC has asked the Federal High Court in Abuja to set aside a subpoena ordering its chairman to appear and produce President Tinubu's 1999 and 2023 nomination documents in the trial of activist Omoyele Sowore.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has asked the Federal High Court in Abuja to set aside a subpoena requiring its chairman to produce President Bola Tinubu's election documents.

The application

INEC's lawyer, Alex Izinyon, a Senior Advocate of Nigeria, filed the motion on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, the day before INEC chairman Professor Joash Amupitan was due to appear as a defence witness in the criminal trial of activist Omoyele Sowore. The subpoena, issued by Justice Mohammed Umar, had directed the INEC chairman to produce Tinubu's nomination forms from the 1999 Lagos governorship election, including an affidavit relating to lost certificates, and from the 2023 presidential election.

The case

Sowore is being prosecuted by the State Security Service over social media posts in which he described the President as a criminal. His defence team asked the court to call the INEC chairman and Joe Igbokwe, a former APC publicity secretary in Lagos, as witnesses.

Background

Questions about the President's academic records have been raised by critics for years and were the subject of legal challenges after the 2023 election, which the Supreme Court dismissed. The President and his supporters have consistently rejected claims of wrongdoing.
