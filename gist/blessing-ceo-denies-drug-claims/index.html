"I am not a drug addict, I don't smoke, drink or even party" – Blessing CEO breaks silence on drug claims

26 Oct 2026, 18:24 GMT

Popular Nigerian relationship therapist and social media personality Blessing CEO has strongly denied allegations of drug use, insisting that she has never been involved in smoking, drinking alcohol or taking illegal substances.

Blessing made the clarification during a recent interview following her release from custody, where she addressed several rumours and allegations that circulated on social media while she was detained.

During the interview, she was confronted with claims suggesting that she had been struggling with substance use and needed rehabilitation or professional help rather than business opportunities.

However, the controversial relationship therapist dismissed the allegations, describing them as false and misleading.

"I am not a drug addict. I do not smoke, drink, and I do not even party," she stated.

According to Blessing, many of the stories circulated about her on social media have little or no connection to her actual lifestyle.

She expressed frustration over what she described as the growing habit of internet users creating and spreading damaging narratives about public figures without verifying their claims.

Blessing maintained that being controversial or frequently discussed online should not give people the freedom to make unfounded accusations about her personal life.

Her remarks have once again drawn attention to the challenges celebrities and internet personalities face when rumours about their private lives spread across social media.

While Blessing has firmly rejected the allegations, the claims remain unsubstantiated, and no verified evidence of illegal drug use was presented in the account of the interview.

VeryDarkMan warns Tinubu over deadly attack on NDC campaign in Delta

24 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has issued a strong warning to President Tinubu after gunmen killed four people, including NDC youth leader Efemena Umukoro, at Omo-Agege's campaign event in Delta. He said political violence could spiral out of control.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has issued a fierce public warning to President Bola Tinubu following the deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North, Delta State.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram, VDM told the president that he would burn Nigeria down, arguing that targeting opposition figures with gunfire is a dangerous political strategy that could spiral far beyond anyone's control. He warned that even people close to the president would not be safe if the cycle of violence continued, and drew attention to the fact that the attack happened in Delta State.

The attack

Gunmen opened fire on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege's campaign team on Monday, 23 November 2026, while he was addressing supporters. Four people were killed, according to his campaign, including Efemena Umukoro, an Urhobo youth leader popularly known as General Akima. Omo-Agege was unhurt.

Other reactions

Other social media commentators also condemned the killings, and Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori called for everyone involved to be arrested regardless of their party. The Inspector-General of Police has ordered an investigation.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to VDM's remarks was included in the reports.

Shettima says he will keep firing back at Atiku as long as he attacks Tinubu

Vice President Kashim Shettima has reportedly said he will not stop his verbal exchanges with ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar while Atiku keeps targeting President Tinubu. He said he has never insulted Obi, Makinde, Kwankwaso or Amaechi.

Read more

Vice President Kashim Shettima has reportedly said he will continue to trade words with Atiku Abubakar, the African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate, for as long as Atiku keeps attacking President Bola Tinubu.

What he said

Speaking in Abuja, Shettima is said to have made a distinction between his clashes with Atiku and his relationships with other opposition figures. He pointed out that he has never publicly insulted Peter Obi, Governor Seyi Makinde, Rabiu Kwankwaso or Rotimi Amaechi, describing them all as personal friends.

He reportedly drew a line between political rivalry and personal hostility.

Jonathan link

Shettima also traced his past political tensions with former President Goodluck Jonathan back to his open support for Amaechi during the contest for chairmanship of the Nigeria Governors' Forum.

Background

The campaign for the January 2027 election is heating up. No response from Atiku's campaign was included in the reports.

Lagos offers 20% discount on food in 66 markets for the festive season

24 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Lagos State Government has relaunched its Ounje Eko food discount scheme, offering 20% off selected items in 66 markets. The markets will open on three Sundays: 29 November, 6 December and 20 December.

Read more

The Lagos State Government has relaunched its Ounje Eko food discount programme for the festive season, offering residents a 20% reduction on selected food items in 66 markets across the state.

When and where

The Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Systems, Abisola Olusanya, announced on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, that the scheme will run on three Sundays: 29 November, 6 December and 20 December. The markets will open from 9am to 4pm and cover all 57 local government and local council development areas, across the five IBILE divisions of Ikeja, Badagry, Ikorodu, Lagos Island and Epe.

Why it matters

The commissioner said the initiative was designed to ease pressure on household budgets as residents prepare for Christmas and the New Year. It comes amid concerns that many families have yet to feel real relief from high food prices, even though food inflation has slowed.

Davido's Ghana rep says promoter owes money over short concert performance

Davido's Ghanaian representative has reportedly blamed an unresolved payment dispute for the singer's brief performance at an event organised by Great Osama. He claimed the balance due on arrival was never paid as agreed.

Read more

Davido's Ghanaian representative, Sabi Boi, has reportedly spoken out on the controversy over the Afrobeats star's short performance at an event organised by Great Osama in Ghana.

What he said

In a TikTok Live session on Monday, 23 November 2026, Sabi Boi claimed that the organiser owed Davido's team money, and dismissed suggestions that the promoter had paid huge sums for the show.

The alleged deal

According to him, the arrangement was for the organiser to pay a deposit before the show and the rest once Davido arrived at the venue, but the second payment was not made as agreed. He also claimed the promoter tried to settle the balance with gold instead of cash.

The other side

No response from Great Osama to the claims was included in the reports.
