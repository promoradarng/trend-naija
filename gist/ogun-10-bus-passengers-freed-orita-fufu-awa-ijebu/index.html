All 10 passengers abducted from commercial bus in Ogun regain freedom, one suspected kidnapper killed

8 Nov 2026, 18:52 GMT

There is good news from Ogun State: all 10 passengers abducted from a commercial bus at Orita Fufu, Awa-Ijebu, have regained their freedom.

Released unharmed

The passengers were taken on Tuesday, 3 November 2026. Police say they were released unharmed on the night of Saturday, 7 November, following sustained security pressure and intelligence-led efforts.

Suspect killed, weapons and cash recovered

During a follow-up operation, police say one suspected kidnapper was killed. An AK-47 rifle, ammunition, ₦1.37 million in cash and a mobile phone were reportedly recovered.

Photos of the recovered items show bundles of naira notes, an AK-47 magazine and a mobile phone laid out on the ground in the bush.

Questions still unanswered

Police have not said whether any ransom was paid for the passengers' release.

Reports also differ on whether any of the passengers was a serving or recently discharged member of the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).

Safe at last

The most important update is that all 10 passengers are alive and safely out of captivity, after five days that must have been terrifying for them and their families.

The incident is another reminder of the danger travellers face on Nigeria's highways, and of the need for security agencies to keep up pressure on kidnapping gangs.

Dangote cuts cooking gas price to ₦1,000 per kg, but retail prices stay high

27 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Dangote Refinery has reportedly cut its cooking gas gantry price to ₦1,000 per kg, with its marketers selling at ₦1,100. Consumers, however, are still paying between ₦1,600 and ₦1,800 per kg at retail outlets.

Read more

Dangote Petroleum Refinery has reportedly reduced the wholesale price of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), commonly known as cooking gas, to ₦1,000 per kilogram.

The new prices

Marketers linked to the refinery have lowered their selling price to ₦1,100 per kilogram, according to a market survey.

The retail gap

Despite the cuts, consumers are still paying between ₦1,600 and ₦1,800 per kilogram at retail outlets, raising concerns about the gap between wholesale and retail prices. Earlier this week, cooking gas was reported to be selling for up to ₦2,000 per kg in parts of Abuja.

Background

The refinery has also cut its petrol and diesel prices in recent weeks, most recently reducing diesel by ₦125 to ₦1,595 per litre.

NNPC cuts petrol price by ₦60 to ₦1,240 per litre in Abuja

27 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

NNPC has reportedly reduced its petrol pump price from ₦1,300 to ₦1,240 per litre at outlets in Abuja, undercutting rivals. The cut comes amid intense competition among marketers.

Read more

The Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPCL) has reduced its petrol pump price again, as competition among marketers intensifies.

The new price

A market survey on Thursday, 26 November 2026, showed that NNPC cut its price from ₦1,300 to ₦1,240 per litre, a reduction of ₦60. The new price was seen at several NNPC stations across the Federal Capital Territory, including Gwarinpa, the Kubwa Expressway, Airport Junction and Wuse Zone 6.

Competition

The cut undercuts other major marketers such as MRS and AA Rano, which have also reduced prices in recent days. Wholesale prices have fallen sharply, with some Lagos depots selling at about ₦1,055 per litre.

Background

Prices have been falling for weeks amid competition between Dangote Refinery and importers.

President Bola Tinubu has announced a support package of ₦100 million and a three-bedroom flat for each family that lost a loved one in the crash of the Nigerian Air Force aircraft NAF 931 in Ondo State.

The pledge

The president made the announcement at a memorial and wreath-laying ceremony in Abuja held in honour of the victims. The package covers the families of both the military personnel and the civilians who died in the crash.

Military support

Tinubu also assured the armed forces that the government would provide better training and equipment to improve the performance and readiness of personnel.

Benin photographer Sh0tbySav reportedly dies after 'one-chance' attack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

A photographer known as Sh0tbySav has reportedly died in Benin City after being robbed and pushed out of a moving vehicle by suspected 'one-chance' criminals. Fellow photographers are mourning her.

Read more

The photography community in Benin City, Edo State, is mourning after reports that a photographer known as Sh0tbySav died following a suspected 'one-chance' robbery.

What reportedly happened

According to accounts shared on social media, she was returning home after a photoshoot for a client when she boarded a commercial vehicle believed to have been operated by criminals. She was reportedly robbed of her belongings and pushed out of the moving vehicle.

Details of the incident remain limited, and no police statement was included in the reports.

A warning

Her death has drawn fresh attention to the dangers posed by 'one-chance' gangs, criminals who pose as commercial drivers and passengers to rob unsuspecting commuters, a recurring problem in several Nigerian cities.

Tributes

Fellow photographers have shared tributes online.
