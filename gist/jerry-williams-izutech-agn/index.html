Concern grows over Nollywood actor Jerry Williams as producer Izutech calls out Actors Guild of Nigeria over alleged neglect

27 Oct 2026, 15:44 GMT

Fresh concerns have emerged over the welfare of popular Nollywood actor Jerry Williams after filmmaker and producer Izutech publicly questioned the Actors Guild of Nigeria (AGN) over its alleged failure to provide sustained support for the troubled actor.

According to Izutech, the Actors Guild of Nigeria had previously promised to take responsibility for Jerry Williams' situation, but he is now questioning what became of that commitment.

The producer alleged that the once-popular Nollywood star is facing serious personal challenges, including suspected substance-related problems and concerns about his mental health.

Izutech further claimed that Jerry Williams has allegedly been sending private messages to people on Instagram, appealing for financial assistance to buy food and meet his basic needs.

The claims have raised concerns about the actor's welfare, particularly among those who remember his performances in several Nollywood productions.

Expressing his disappointment, Izutech questioned why an actor who contributed to Nigeria's entertainment industry should allegedly be left struggling without adequate support.

He also disclosed his willingness to intervene again by arranging a rehabilitation programme lasting up to two years, which he believes could help the actor recover and rebuild his life.

However, the producer admitted that such an intervention would require considerable financial resources and appealed for support from colleagues, industry stakeholders and well-meaning Nigerians.

The development has once again brought attention to the welfare of entertainers in Nollywood, especially actors facing health challenges, financial difficulties or personal crises after years in the spotlight.

It also raises questions about the responsibility of professional associations to support members experiencing serious difficulties.

While Izutech's allegations have attracted concern, the actor's current medical condition and the extent of any assistance provided by the Actors Guild of Nigeria have not been independently verified.

The bigger question remains: what support systems are available to Nollywood actors when they can no longer cope with life's challenges?

For many fans, the hope is that Jerry Williams receives appropriate professional care, genuine support and an opportunity to regain his health and stability.

Should the Actors Guild of Nigeria establish a dedicated welfare and rehabilitation fund to support actors facing serious personal and health challenges?

Miss Universe Nigeria Danielle Adewusi arrives in Puerto Rico for 75th pageant

10 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Danielle Adewusi, crowned Miss Universe Nigeria 2026 in September, has arrived in Puerto Rico ahead of the 75th Miss Universe pageant on 24 November, where up to 120 countries will compete.

Read more

Nigeria's representative at Miss Universe 2026, Danielle Adewusi, has arrived in Puerto Rico ahead of the competition.

The arrival

Her arrival was confirmed on the Miss Universe Nigeria Facebook page on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. Adewusi was crowned Miss Universe Nigeria on 27 September at the Federal Palace Hotel in Victoria Island, Lagos, beating 19 other contestants while representing Lagos State.

The pageant

The 75th Miss Universe pageant will hold on 24 November at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico José Miguel Agrelot in San Juan. The organisers say up to 120 countries and territories will take part, one of the largest fields in the competition's history.

How to support her

Nigerians can support Adewusi through the fan vote and 'Beyond the Crown' categories, which are currently open.

Background

Nigeria has never won Miss Universe. The country's best recent result came in 2024, when Chidimma Adetshina finished as first runner-up, the highest placing by a Nigerian in the competition's history.

Lateef Adedimeji's praise for Tinubu's skills programmes divides fans

10 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Actor Lateef Adedimeji has drawn mixed reactions after posting a video praising President Tinubu's 3 Million Technical Talent and Skills Up Artisans programmes, with some fans calling it political.

Read more

Nollywood actor and filmmaker Lateef Adedimeji has sparked debate after speaking favourably about two of President Bola Tinubu's skills programmes.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram on Monday, 9 November 2026, Adedimeji spoke about the 3 Million Technical Talent (3MTT) programme and the Skills Up Artisans (SUPA) initiative, describing them as meaningful steps towards transforming Nigeria's tech and creative sectors. He encouraged his followers to position themselves to benefit from them.

The reaction

Many followers focused on the timing of the video, with the 2027 elections approaching, and saw it as politically motivated. Some long-time fans expressed disappointment, while others said they still admired him and that he was entitled to share information about government programmes.

Background

Adedimeji is one of the most popular actors in the Yoruba film industry and is married to actress Mo Bimpe. Several entertainers have openly backed the President's re-election, including Saidi Balogun and Eniola Badmus, and have also faced backlash online.

"My hair costs more than my clothes": Davido's elder sister, Sharon Adeleke, says she doesn't believe in spending big on outfits

10 Nov 2026, 01:10 GMT

Davido's elder sister, Sharon Adeleke, says she would rather spend good money on her hair than on expensive designer clothes. She also revealed that her own wedding dress cost just $2,000, and that her outfit for Davido and Chioma's white wedding was a last-minute rescue.

Read more

Davido's elder sister, Sharon Adeleke, has opened up about her fashion choices, revealing that she would rather spend good money on her hair than buy expensive designer clothes.

"My hair costs more than my clothes"

Speaking on a podcast, Sharon said she has never really believed in spending a fortune on clothing, no matter how much money a person has.

She enjoys looking good, especially when it comes to her hair, but she does not see the need to attach an expensive price tag to every outfit.

The wedding outfit drama

Sharon also spoke about her appearance at Davido and Chioma's white wedding, revealing that things did not go as smoothly as she had planned.

She said she had hired a stylist and a fashion designer to handle her outfit, but they failed to deliver as expected. Everything ended up as a last-minute arrangement, leaving her with little choice but to hope it worked out. At some point, she said, it was simply a case of "God abeg!"

A $2,000 wedding dress

She also shared an interesting detail about her own wedding. Despite coming from one of Nigeria's wealthiest and best-known families, she said her husband spent just $2,000 on her wedding dress.

She added that she personally paid for the dresses worn by all 13 of her bridesmaids.

Not what people expect

Her revelations are interesting because many people assume wealthy families spend outrageous amounts on everything they wear.

Now imagine Davido's own elder sister saying she doesn't believe in buying expensive clothes, while some people borrow money just to wear designer labels and impress strangers.
