Concern grows over Nollywood actor Jerry Williams as producer Izutech calls out Actors Guild of Nigeria over alleged neglect

27 Oct 2026, 15:44 GMT

Fresh concerns have emerged over the welfare of popular Nollywood actor Jerry Williams after filmmaker and producer Izutech publicly questioned the Actors Guild of Nigeria (AGN) over its alleged failure to provide sustained support for the troubled actor.

According to Izutech, the Actors Guild of Nigeria had previously promised to take responsibility for Jerry Williams' situation, but he is now questioning what became of that commitment.

The producer alleged that the once-popular Nollywood star is facing serious personal challenges, including suspected substance-related problems and concerns about his mental health.

Izutech further claimed that Jerry Williams has allegedly been sending private messages to people on Instagram, appealing for financial assistance to buy food and meet his basic needs.

The claims have raised concerns about the actor's welfare, particularly among those who remember his performances in several Nollywood productions.

Expressing his disappointment, Izutech questioned why an actor who contributed to Nigeria's entertainment industry should allegedly be left struggling without adequate support.

He also disclosed his willingness to intervene again by arranging a rehabilitation programme lasting up to two years, which he believes could help the actor recover and rebuild his life.

However, the producer admitted that such an intervention would require considerable financial resources and appealed for support from colleagues, industry stakeholders and well-meaning Nigerians.

The development has once again brought attention to the welfare of entertainers in Nollywood, especially actors facing health challenges, financial difficulties or personal crises after years in the spotlight.

It also raises questions about the responsibility of professional associations to support members experiencing serious difficulties.

While Izutech's allegations have attracted concern, the actor's current medical condition and the extent of any assistance provided by the Actors Guild of Nigeria have not been independently verified.

The bigger question remains: what support systems are available to Nollywood actors when they can no longer cope with life's challenges?

For many fans, the hope is that Jerry Williams receives appropriate professional care, genuine support and an opportunity to regain his health and stability.

Should the Actors Guild of Nigeria establish a dedicated welfare and rehabilitation fund to support actors facing serious personal and health challenges?

Funke Akindele says influencer rejected her paid offer to promote 'Everybody Loves Jenifa'

9 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Filmmaker Funke Akindele says an unnamed influencer turned down her paid offer to promote 'Everybody Loves Jenifa', and ignored her follow-up messages, while speaking at a creative industry panel.

Read more

Nollywood filmmaker Funke Akindele has revealed that a social media influencer turned down her paid offer to promote her blockbuster film 'Everybody Loves Jenifa'.

What she said

Speaking at a panel session at the Creative Africa Nexus Weekend, in a video shared online on 7 November 2026, Akindele said she had been reluctant to approach influencers but eventually decided to try. She proposed that the influencer post about the film several times a week during the promotional campaign, and offered to pay. However, the influencer's management turned her down, and when she followed up to make clear she was willing to pay, her message was ignored. She did not name the influencer.

Background

'Everybody Loves Jenifa' was one of the biggest Nollywood box-office hits. Akindele, one of the industry's most successful filmmakers, is now promoting her new film 'The Four', which recently sold out its London premiere. Influencer marketing has become a major part of how Nigerian films are promoted, with content creators often paid to make videos about new releases.

Hilda Baci says it is upsetting when people credit her success to her partner

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Celebrity chef and Guinness World Record holder Hilda Baci says it is sometimes upsetting when people attribute her achievements to her partner, saying it shows the mindset of many Nigerians.

Read more

Celebrity chef Hilda Baci has spoken out against people who credit her success to her romantic partner rather than her own efforts.

What she said

In a video shared on Monday, 9 November 2026, the chef said such comments are sometimes upsetting and reveal the mindset of many people. Her remarks have sparked conversation about the tendency to link successful women's achievements to the men in their lives.

Background

Hilda Baci became a household name in 2023 when she set a Guinness World Record for the longest cooking marathon by an individual, cooking for about 93 hours in Lagos. She has since built a successful food and media business. Her relationship has often been discussed on social media.

How people reacted

Reactions were mixed. Some fans backed her, saying women deserve credit for their own work, while others joked online that her partner was better known because of her rather than the other way around.

Destiny Etiko appointed Senior Special Assistant by Enugu governor

9 Nov 2026, 06:44 GMT

Actress Destiny Etiko has been appointed Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy to Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. She is the second Nollywood star to receive a role from his administration this month, after Rachael Okonkwo.

Read more

Nollywood actress Destiny Etiko has been given a government appointment in Enugu State.

The appointment

According to reports on Sunday, 8 November 2026, Etiko has been appointed Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy to Governor Peter Mbah. The actress, who is from Udi Local Government Area of the state, confirmed the news when she responded to a congratulatory post from fellow actress Sarah Martins.

Second Nollywood appointee

Etiko is the second prominent Nollywood figure to be appointed by the Mbah administration in a short period. Actress Rachael Okonkwo confirmed her own appointment as Senior Special Assistant on Films and Creative Industries earlier in November.

Background

Etiko publicly supported Mbah during his campaign ahead of the 2023 governorship election. She rose to prominence after the 2014 film 'Idemili' and has since built a successful career as an actress and producer. Mbah, who was elected on the PDP platform, has since moved to the APC and backs President Bola Tinubu's re-election.

How people reacted

Many fans and colleagues congratulated her while others had mixed reactions.
