Funke Akindele's 'The Four' sells out London premiere at Odeon Leicester Square

6 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Funke Akindele's new film, 'The Four', has sold out its London premiere ahead of its release in Nigerian cinemas.

The premiere

The film's distributor, FilmOne Entertainment, announced on Thursday, 5 November 2026, that tickets for the premiere at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square, one of London's most famous cinemas, were no longer available. The premiere is scheduled for 29 November. Akindele had earlier described the event as a historic moment for Nigerian cinema.

Background

Akindele is Nollywood's most commercially successful filmmaker, with several of her films topping the Nigerian box office, including 'Battle on Buka Street', 'A Tribe Called Judah' and 'Everybody Loves Jenifa'. 'A Tribe Called Judah' became the first Nollywood film to earn more than ₦1 billion at the box office. 'The Four' features several well-known actors, including Wumi Toriola, and is expected in Nigerian cinemas in December, traditionally the busiest period for local releases.

Why it matters

A sold-out premiere at a major London venue shows the growing international audience for Nollywood films, particularly among the Nigerian diaspora in the UK.

How fans reacted

Fans congratulated the actress and said they could not wait for the film's release in Nigeria.

  • The premiere at Odeon Luxe Leicester Square is on 29 November.
  • Tickets have sold out.
  • The film opens in Nigerian cinemas in December.

Brown Igboegwu welcomes son 16 years into marriage

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Actor Brown Igboegwu has announced the birth of his son, six years after the couple welcomed their daughter following a 10-year wait. He said only God gives children.

Read more

Nollywood actor Brown Igboegwu has announced the birth of a son, weeks after he and his wife marked 16 years of marriage.

The announcement

Sharing a photo of himself at the hospital on Instagram, the actor said the birth was proof that only God gives children, and said no prophet, pastor or man of God could take credit.

A long wait

He recalled that the couple waited 10 years for their first child, a daughter, and visited several churches and prayer houses during that time. He said some people took advantage of their situation, using their videos and testimonies to promote their churches.

Six years after their daughter's birth, they have now welcomed a son. He described the last nine months as the toughest of his 50 years.

His message

He urged his followers to hold on to God and trust that their prayers will be answered.

But once they stepped outside, the man reached out for a selfie and the actor turned away and busied himself with something else. Comedian Ali Baba, who was nearby, had stepped back to give the man space for the photo, which made the moment more noticeable.

How people reacted

The clip quickly spread online and sparked debate.

No comment yet

RMD had not publicly commented on the video at the time of the reports.
