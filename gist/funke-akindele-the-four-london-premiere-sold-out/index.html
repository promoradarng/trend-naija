Funke Akindele's 'The Four' sells out London premiere at Odeon Leicester Square

6 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Funke Akindele's new film, 'The Four', has sold out its London premiere ahead of its release in Nigerian cinemas.

The premiere

The film's distributor, FilmOne Entertainment, announced on Thursday, 5 November 2026, that tickets for the premiere at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square, one of London's most famous cinemas, were no longer available. The premiere is scheduled for 29 November. Akindele had earlier described the event as a historic moment for Nigerian cinema.

Background

Akindele is Nollywood's most commercially successful filmmaker, with several of her films topping the Nigerian box office, including 'Battle on Buka Street', 'A Tribe Called Judah' and 'Everybody Loves Jenifa'. 'A Tribe Called Judah' became the first Nollywood film to earn more than ₦1 billion at the box office. 'The Four' features several well-known actors, including Wumi Toriola, and is expected in Nigerian cinemas in December, traditionally the busiest period for local releases.

Why it matters

A sold-out premiere at a major London venue shows the growing international audience for Nollywood films, particularly among the Nigerian diaspora in the UK.

How fans reacted

Fans congratulated the actress and said they could not wait for the film's release in Nigeria.

  • The premiere at Odeon Luxe Leicester Square is on 29 November.
  • Tickets have sold out.
  • The film opens in Nigerian cinemas in December.

Osita Iheme and I Go Save tell politicians: show us results, not dance moves

16 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Actor Osita Iheme and comedian I Go Save have criticised politicians who dance and show off their fitness at campaign rallies, saying Nigerians want competence, manifestos and results instead.

Read more

Two well-known entertainers have hit out at politicians who turn campaign rallies into dance shows ahead of the 2027 elections.

Osita Iheme

In an Instagram post on Monday, 16 November 2026, Nollywood actor Osita Iheme, popularly known as Pawpaw, urged politicians to spend their energy demonstrating competence rather than choreography. He said Nigerians want results, vision and accountability from their leaders, not entertainment, and issued what read as a warning to politicians to take voters seriously.

I Go Save

Comedian I Go Save also questioned why candidates increasingly run, jump and dance at public events instead of addressing national issues. He wondered whether the displays were meant to prove physical fitness or had become a new form of competition between candidates, and said they should present credible manifestos and practical ideas instead.

Background

Other celebrities have recently used their platforms to criticise politicians over roads and governance. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Yul Edochie and Judy Austin announce fourth child as May Edochie releases gospel song

15 Nov 2026, 20:43 GMT

Actor Yul Edochie and his second wife Judy Austin have revealed they are expecting their fourth child together, on the same day his first wife May Edochie released a gospel single, 'Sorom', about overcoming her enemies.

Read more

Nollywood actor Yul Edochie and his second wife, Judy Austin, have announced that they are expecting their fourth child together.

The announcement

The couple revealed the pregnancy in a new skit shared on Sunday, 15 November 2026, confirming rumours that had been circulating online. They already have three children together, and Judy has children from previous relationships. The news drew a flood of comments online, ranging from humour to disapproval.

May's new song

On the same day, Yul's first wife, May Edochie, released a gospel single titled 'Sorom', recorded with an artist called Nwando, on major streaming platforms. Announcing it on Instagram, she said there was a good reason for releasing it that day and described God as always being with her and never letting her enemies triumph over her. The lyrics draw on biblical imagery about escaping a snare and finding joy.

Background

Yul's public relationship with Judy Austin and his separation from May, with whom he has children, have been among the most talked-about celebrity stories in Nigeria in recent years. The couple's daughter was recently in the news for buying her first car.

Late banker Herbert Wigwe's daughter Tochi weds in star-studded Lagos ceremony

15 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Tochi Wigwe, eldest daughter of the late Access Holdings chief Herbert Wigwe, has married investment professional Wilson Aikhomu in a traditional ceremony in Lagos attended by Osinbajo, Sanwo-Olu, Dangote and the Emir of Kano, with performances by Davido and Flavour.

Read more

Otutochi 'Tochi' Wigwe, the eldest daughter of the late banker Herbert Wigwe, has married her partner, Wilson Omogbale Aikhomu, in a traditional ceremony in Lagos.

The ceremony

The wedding on Saturday, 14 November 2026, drew prominent guests including former Vice-President Yemi Osinbajo, Lagos governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, businessman Aliko Dangote, the Emir of Kano Muhammadu Sanusi II, banker Aigboje Aig-Imoukhuede and Seyi Tinubu. Davido and Flavour performed for guests.

The couple

The groom is a Lagos-based investment professional and managing partner of an investment firm. He is the son of the late Rear Admiral Joseph Aikhomu and a nephew of former Chief of General Staff Admiral Augustus Aikhomu, and was a close friend of Tochi's late brother, Chizi. Tochi, 28, leads her late father's non-profit organisation.

Background

Herbert Wigwe, chief executive of Access Holdings, died in a helicopter crash in California in February 2024 alongside his wife, Chizoba, and son, Chizi.
