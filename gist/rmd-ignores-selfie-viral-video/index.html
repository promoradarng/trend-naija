Video of RMD appearing to ignore man's selfie request sparks debate

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

A video of Nollywood veteran Richard Mofe-Damijo, popularly known as RMD, appearing to ignore a man who tried to take a selfie with him has gone viral.

What the video shows

The footage, which began circulating on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, was recorded at a conference at the Muson Centre in Lagos. Inside the hall, RMD appeared relaxed, laughing and chatting warmly with the same man.

But once they stepped outside, the man reached out for a selfie and the actor turned away and busied himself with something else. Comedian Ali Baba, who was nearby, had stepped back to give the man space for the photo, which made the moment more noticeable.

How people reacted

The clip quickly spread online and sparked debate.

No comment yet

RMD had not publicly commented on the video at the time of the reports.

Brown Igboegwu welcomes son 16 years into marriage

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Actor Brown Igboegwu has announced the birth of his son, six years after the couple welcomed their daughter following a 10-year wait. He said only God gives children.

Read more

Nollywood actor Brown Igboegwu has announced the birth of a son, weeks after he and his wife marked 16 years of marriage.

The announcement

Sharing a photo of himself at the hospital on Instagram, the actor said the birth was proof that only God gives children, and said no prophet, pastor or man of God could take credit.

A long wait

He recalled that the couple waited 10 years for their first child, a daughter, and visited several churches and prayer houses during that time. He said some people took advantage of their situation, using their videos and testimonies to promote their churches.

Six years after their daughter's birth, they have now welcomed a son. He described the last nine months as the toughest of his 50 years.

His message

He urged his followers to hold on to God and trust that their prayers will be answered.

Biola Adebayo puts new salon up for sale weeks after opening it

19 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Actress Biola Adebayo says she is selling her newly opened salon because her sister, who was meant to run it, has been granted a Canadian visa. She said she cannot manage the business herself.

Read more

Nollywood actress Biola Adebayo has announced that she is putting her newly opened salon up for sale, only weeks after unveiling it.

The reason

In a video on her social media page, Biola explained that she set up the salon to give her sister, who had been going through a difficult time, a business of her own to run.

However, just days after the salon was unveiled, her sister received approval for a Canadian visa she had been waiting for for more than three years, and will now be relocating.

Mixed feelings

The actress described the visa as good news, but admitted it left her with the challenge of managing the salon herself, which led to her decision to sell it.

APGA formally adopts Tinubu as its 2027 presidential candidate

19 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The All Progressives Grand Alliance has formally adopted President Tinubu as its candidate for 2027. Anambra Governor Charles Soludo handed the party flag to Vice President Shettima.

Read more

The All Progressives Grand Alliance (APGA) has formally adopted President Bola Tinubu as its presidential candidate for the 2027 general elections.

The ceremony

Anambra State Governor and APGA leader Charles Soludo presided over a ceremony on Thursday, 19 November 2026, at which he presented the party's flag to Vice President Kashim Shettima, who received it on the President's behalf.

What it means

Tinubu is already the presidential candidate of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC). APGA's adoption means the party will back him rather than field its own candidate, marking a significant alliance between the party and the federal government.

Background

APGA controls Anambra State, the home state of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi.

The other side

No reaction from the NDC or the ADC to the endorsement was included in the reports.
