Four killed as gunmen attack Omo-Agege's NDC campaign in Delta

23 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Four people were killed and several others injured when gunmen attacked a Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North Local Government Area of Delta State, on Monday, 23 November 2026.

What happened

According to his campaign organisation, armed men opened fire at close range on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege, the NDC's Delta Central senatorial candidate, and his entourage while he was addressing a crowd that had gathered to welcome him. The campaign said the senator was not hit, although bullets passed close to him.

The victims

The Omo-Agege Campaign Organisation said four people died and four others were critically injured and receiving treatment. It named two of the dead as aides of the campaign.

'Assassination attempt'

In a statement by its media adviser, the campaign described the incident as a targeted attempt on the senator's life rather than a political clash, citing eyewitnesses who said the attackers made clear he was their target.

What happens next

There was no word in the reports on arrests or a police statement. The attack comes as campaigns for the 2027 elections gather pace.

Omo-Agege speaks

In a television interview on Monday night, Omo-Agege said the gunmen started shooting less than five minutes after he began addressing supporters, and that they shouted his name. He said a chance movement, stepping to the side of his vehicle to keep eye contact with the crowd, saved his life.

Reactions

NDC chieftain Pat Utomi condemned the attack, saying violence would not cow Delta residents, and accused the ruling party of being behind political violence in the state. No response from the APC to that claim was included in the reports.

IGP orders probe

On Tuesday, the Inspector-General of Police, Olatunji Disu, ordered the Delta State Police Command to begin an impartial investigation. Preliminary police reports put the death toll at six. The police said the probe would also examine the conduct of officers deployed to the venue, and that anyone found culpable would be prosecuted.

Governor demands arrests

Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori described the attack as tragic and said everyone involved must be arrested, whatever party they belong to.

Who was killed

Among the dead was Efemena Umukoro, popularly known as General Akima, an Urhobo youth leader and one of the campaign's aides.

Police invite Omo-Agege, others

The Delta State Police Command has invited Omo-Agege, the Ughelli North Local Government chairman and five others for questioning over the attack, as part of its investigation. Omo-Agege and the chairman are due at the State CID in Asaba on Thursday.

Obi mourns

Speaking at a town hall meeting in Taraba State on Tuesday, NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi led a minute of silence for the party members killed in the attack.

  • Four people were killed and four critically injured.
  • Omo-Agege escaped unhurt, his campaign says.
  • The campaign calls it an assassination attempt.

The figures

In a statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, Atiku's campaign cited figures from the government's own Budget Office. The campaign argued that the drop came even as international crude prices rose in the second quarter of the year.

No response from the presidency or the APC campaign was included in the reports.

Background

Atiku, who turned 80 on Wednesday, is the ADC's candidate in the January 2027 election. President Tinubu sent him a birthday message despite their rivalry.

NDC says supporters were attacked in Benin as Obi arrived for campaign

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

The Nigeria Democratic Congress has alleged that gunmen attacked its supporters and campaign vehicles on Airport Road, Benin City, as Peter Obi arrived in Edo State on Wednesday. It said one supporter was critically wounded.

Read more

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has alleged that its supporters were attacked in Benin City, Edo State, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, as its presidential candidate Peter Obi arrived for a town hall meeting and other campaign events.

What the party says

According to the party, attackers who arrived in a pickup van opened fire on its campaign vehicles parked along Airport Road while supporters waited for Obi. A party spokesperson posted videos online showing damaged vehicles and bloodstains, and said one supporter was critically wounded and receiving hospital treatment.

In a statement, the NDC's National Publicity Secretary said the attackers also assaulted people in the crowd and destroyed buses, and described the attack as unprovoked. The party blamed thugs it said were linked to the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

The other side

No response from the APC or the police to the allegation was included in the reports.

Background

The incident comes two days after gunmen attacked an NDC campaign event in Delta State, killing several people, and on the same day the House of Representatives warned that a state of emergency might be needed in Delta if violence continues. The NDC has also boycotted a security meeting called by the police chief.

Tinubu holds rally in Maiduguri, orders fresh push against insurgents

25 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

President Tinubu visited Maiduguri on Wednesday, where he commissioned projects, held a campaign rally and directed security agencies to step up operations against insurgents. He promised to turn security gains in the North-East into economic recovery.

Read more

President Bola Tinubu visited Maiduguri, the Borno State capital, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, where he held a campaign rally ahead of the January 2027 election.

Projects commissioned

On arrival, the president commissioned two projects: a female lodge for National Youth Service Corps members and the Borno International Conference Centre.

Security push

Addressing the crowd, Tinubu directed security agencies to intensify operations against retreating insurgents and promised support for the families of troops. He pledged that the peace gains recorded in the North-East would be turned into full economic recovery.

Background

Borno has been at the centre of the insurgency in the North-East for more than a decade. Earlier this week, state officials revealed that eight soldiers and 13 construction workers were killed during the rebuilding of the Mayanti community, which Governor Babagana Zulum has now reopened.
