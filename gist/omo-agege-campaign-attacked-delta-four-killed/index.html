Four killed as gunmen attack Omo-Agege's NDC campaign in Delta

23 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Four people were killed and several others injured when gunmen attacked a Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North Local Government Area of Delta State, on Monday, 23 November 2026.

What happened

According to his campaign organisation, armed men opened fire at close range on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege, the NDC's Delta Central senatorial candidate, and his entourage while he was addressing a crowd that had gathered to welcome him. The campaign said the senator was not hit, although bullets passed close to him.

The victims

The Omo-Agege Campaign Organisation said four people died and four others were critically injured and receiving treatment. It named two of the dead as aides of the campaign.

'Assassination attempt'

In a statement by its media adviser, the campaign described the incident as a targeted attempt on the senator's life rather than a political clash, citing eyewitnesses who said the attackers made clear he was their target.

What happens next

There was no word in the reports on arrests or a police statement. The attack comes as campaigns for the 2027 elections gather pace.

Omo-Agege speaks

In a television interview on Monday night, Omo-Agege said the gunmen started shooting less than five minutes after he began addressing supporters, and that they shouted his name. He said a chance movement, stepping to the side of his vehicle to keep eye contact with the crowd, saved his life.

Reactions

NDC chieftain Pat Utomi condemned the attack, saying violence would not cow Delta residents, and accused the ruling party of being behind political violence in the state. No response from the APC to that claim was included in the reports.

IGP orders probe

On Tuesday, the Inspector-General of Police, Olatunji Disu, ordered the Delta State Police Command to begin an impartial investigation. Preliminary police reports put the death toll at six. The police said the probe would also examine the conduct of officers deployed to the venue, and that anyone found culpable would be prosecuted.

Governor demands arrests

Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori described the attack as tragic and said everyone involved must be arrested, whatever party they belong to.

Who was killed

Among the dead was Efemena Umukoro, popularly known as General Akima, an Urhobo youth leader and one of the campaign's aides.

Police invite Omo-Agege, others

The Delta State Police Command has invited Omo-Agege, the Ughelli North Local Government chairman and five others for questioning over the attack, as part of its investigation. Omo-Agege and the chairman are due at the State CID in Asaba on Thursday.

Obi mourns

Speaking at a town hall meeting in Taraba State on Tuesday, NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi led a minute of silence for the party members killed in the attack.

  • Four people were killed and four critically injured.
  • Omo-Agege escaped unhurt, his campaign says.
  • The campaign calls it an assassination attempt.

Report records 124 cases of electoral violence and 22 deaths in four months

26 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

A civic group says it has documented 124 incidents of election-related violence and 22 deaths across Nigeria since July. It warned the trend could worsen as the January 2027 elections approach.

Read more

A non-partisan civic organisation has warned of rising political violence in Nigeria, saying it recorded 124 cases of electoral violence and 22 deaths in the past four months.

The figures

In a statement in Abuja on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, the Kimpact Development Initiative (KDI) said the incidents were documented between 1 July and 23 November 2026 through its election violence monitoring project. Incidents rose from three in July to 48 in October, with another 40 recorded in the first half of November alone.

The warning

The group warned that the trend could worsen as the 16 January 2027 elections draw closer unless political parties, security agencies and other stakeholders take urgent action. It pointed to growing political intolerance and inflammatory rhetoric among politicians and their supporters as key triggers.

KDI stressed that the problem cuts across parties and should not be blamed on any single group, and urged candidates to restrain their supporters.

Background

The report comes days after deadly attacks on NDC campaign events in Delta and Edo states, and after INEC warned that the popular 'rig and die' slogan could endanger polling staff and voters. The House of Representatives has also warned that a state of emergency may be needed in Delta if violence continues.

Atiku challenges Tinubu to explain ₦1.44tn drop in oil revenue

25 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

ADC candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly asked President Tinubu to explain a ₦1.44 trillion fall in oil revenue in the first half of 2026, citing Budget Office figures. His campaign says collections were far below target.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly challenged President Bola Tinubu to publicly explain a ₦1.44 trillion drop in Nigeria's gross oil revenue in the first half of 2026.

The figures

In a statement on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, Atiku's campaign cited figures from the government's own Budget Office. It said the government collected ₦7.88 trillion in oil revenue between January and June 2026, well below its half-year target of ₦18.65 trillion, leaving a shortfall of about ₦10.78 trillion.

The campaign argued that the drop came even as international crude prices rose in the second quarter of the year.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC campaign was included in the reports.

Background

Atiku, who turned 80 on Wednesday, is the ADC's candidate in the January 2027 election. President Tinubu sent him a birthday message despite their rivalry.
