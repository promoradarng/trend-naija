Francis Duru's family announces burial for 11 December in Imo

2 Nov 2026, 18:01 GMT

The family of veteran Nollywood actor Francis Duru has announced his burial arrangements, weeks after he died at the age of 59.

The arrangements

In a statement titled 'Forever in our hearts' posted on the actor's official Instagram page on Monday, 2 November 2026, the family said funeral events will begin with a service of songs and tributes in Abuja on Wednesday, 25 November, at 5pm at the Family Worship Center in Wuye. A second service of songs will be held on Thursday, 10 December, at his late father's family compound in Okai, Eziama, Isiala Mbano, Imo State. The funeral and burial will follow on Friday, 11 December, in the same community. The family added that branches and associations in various states are also organising their own services of songs.

Background

Duru died on 22 October 2026 after an illness the family did not disclose. He was one of Nollywood's best-known faces from the 1990s and 2000s, starring in many popular films. His death led to tributes from colleagues across the industry. Last week, the family warned the public against fake burial flyers circulating online and said only official announcements should be trusted.

How people reacted

Fans and colleagues shared tributes under the post and promised to attend the services.

Hilda Baci says it is upsetting when people credit her success to her partner

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Celebrity chef and Guinness World Record holder Hilda Baci says it is sometimes upsetting when people attribute her achievements to her partner, saying it shows the mindset of many Nigerians.

Read more

Celebrity chef Hilda Baci has spoken out against people who credit her success to her romantic partner rather than her own efforts.

What she said

In a video shared on Monday, 9 November 2026, the chef said such comments are sometimes upsetting and reveal the mindset of many people. Her remarks have sparked conversation about the tendency to link successful women's achievements to the men in their lives.

Background

Hilda Baci became a household name in 2023 when she set a Guinness World Record for the longest cooking marathon by an individual, cooking for about 93 hours in Lagos. She has since built a successful food and media business. Her relationship has often been discussed on social media.

How people reacted

Reactions were mixed. Some fans backed her, saying women deserve credit for their own work, while others joked online that her partner was better known because of her rather than the other way around.

Funke Akindele and Toyosi Adesanya moment at Priscilla Ojo's store launch sparks debate

9 Nov 2026, 09:43 GMT

Stars turned out for the launch of Priscilla Ojo's new store in Lagos, but a brief exchange between Funke Akindele and Toyosi Adesanya, who stood with her husband to greet her, got social media talking.

Read more

The opening of a new store by Priscilla Ojo, daughter of actress Iyabo Ojo, drew a crowd of celebrities in Lagos and some unexpected online debate.

The event

Guests at the launch, reported on Monday, 9 November 2026, included Priscilla's husband, Tanzanian singer Juma Jux, as well as Mercy Aigbe, content creator Enioluwa, fashion designer Veekee James and Funke Akindele. Videos showed Iyabo Ojo greeting Funke warmly when she arrived.

Funke Akindele is one of Nollywood's most successful filmmakers, and her new film 'The Four' recently sold out its London premiere. Iyabo Ojo also recently spoke about how she fought to get Funke roles early in her career.

Destiny Etiko appointed Senior Special Assistant by Enugu governor

9 Nov 2026, 06:44 GMT

Actress Destiny Etiko has been appointed Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy to Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. She is the second Nollywood star to receive a role from his administration this month, after Rachael Okonkwo.

Read more

Nollywood actress Destiny Etiko has been given a government appointment in Enugu State.

The appointment

According to reports on Sunday, 8 November 2026, Etiko has been appointed Senior Special Assistant on Youth Engagement and Creative Economy to Governor Peter Mbah. The actress, who is from Udi Local Government Area of the state, confirmed the news when she responded to a congratulatory post from fellow actress Sarah Martins.

Second Nollywood appointee

Etiko is the second prominent Nollywood figure to be appointed by the Mbah administration in a short period. Actress Rachael Okonkwo confirmed her own appointment as Senior Special Assistant on Films and Creative Industries earlier in November.

Background

Etiko publicly supported Mbah during his campaign ahead of the 2023 governorship election. She rose to prominence after the 2014 film 'Idemili' and has since built a successful career as an actress and producer. Mbah, who was elected on the PDP platform, has since moved to the APC and backs President Bola Tinubu's re-election.

How people reacted

Many fans and colleagues congratulated her while others had mixed reactions.

Saidi Balogun hits back at critics over his campaigning for Tinubu

8 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Nollywood actor Saidi Balogun has responded to online attacks over videos of him hosting campaign rallies for President Tinubu, urging people to respect others' political choices.

Read more

Nollywood actor Saidi Balogun has responded to criticism over his open support for President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid.

What happened

The actor had shared videos on social media showing him hosting campaign rallies for the President in different parts of the country, which drew strong criticism from some social media users. In a response reported on Sunday, 8 November 2026, Balogun refused to back down, urging critics to allow people to hold different political views without being attacked.

Background

Balogun is a veteran actor, producer and director in the Yoruba film industry. He is one of several entertainers who have appeared at Tinubu's campaign events in recent weeks, alongside others such as actress Eniola Badmus, who also posted a video this week explaining why she believes the President deserves a second term.

The wider debate

The involvement of entertainers in the 2027 campaigns has divided opinion online. Some argue that public figures have every right to back the candidates they choose, while others say supporting the government amounts to endorsing economic hardship. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.
