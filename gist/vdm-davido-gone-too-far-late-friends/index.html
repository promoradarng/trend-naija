All is fair in war, but has VeryDarkMan gone too far with Davido?

9 Nov 2026, 17:57 GMT

VeryDarkMan (VDM) does not seem to believe in fighting fairly. When he has a disagreement with someone, he appears willing to go to any length to make his point, even if it means bringing up some of the most painful moments in that person's life.

A painful chapter

Over the years, Davido has lost several close friends and associates in tragic circumstances. Their deaths set off all kinds of rumours and conspiracy theories online.

Some people even claimed the deaths were connected to rituals or sacrifices for fame and success. None of those claims was ever proven, and there is no credible evidence linking Davido to any of their deaths.

Imagine losing people who were very close to you, only to have strangers turn their deaths into accusations against you. Davido has lived with those memories for years. He has carried on with his music career, built his family and moved forward with his life, but moving forward does not mean the pain has disappeared.

Reopening old wounds

Now, amid his ongoing disagreement with Davido, VDM has reportedly brought those painful memories back into public discussion. This is where things appear to be getting out of hand.

You can criticise Davido's music, lifestyle, public statements or behaviour. You can disagree with him and challenge his opinions. That is perfectly normal. But bringing up the deaths of his close friends because of a disagreement raises serious questions.

What exactly is the intention? Is it to expose wrongdoing, to make a legitimate point, or simply to hurt someone where he is most vulnerable?

Where are the limits?

VDM has built a reputation for confronting people without holding back, and his supporters may argue he is only giving his opponents the same treatment they give others. But surely there should be limits.

Davido is not above criticism, and neither is VeryDarkMan. Both are public figures who should be held accountable for their actions. Using someone's personal tragedies as ammunition in a public quarrel, however, is something else entirely.

There are some experiences people never fully recover from. They may laugh and smile in public, but that does not mean they have forgotten. If winning an argument requires reopening another person's deepest wounds, perhaps the argument has already gone too far.

Over to you

People say all is fair in love and war, but does that mean we should abandon all compassion when we disagree with someone? Has VDM crossed the line with Davido, or is this just two public figures fighting without limits?

Police open probe after video shows officers escorting Blord

11 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The Nigeria Police Force says it has opened an investigation after a video showed police officers escorting businessman and influencer Blord, describing the arrangement as unlawful. Some social media users have defended him.

Read more

The Nigeria Police Force has launched an investigation into officers seen in a video providing an escort for businessman and social media personality Linus Williams, popularly known as Blord.

What the police said

In a post on X on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, the Force Public Relations Officer described the situation as unlawful and illegal, added the hashtag #NoToImpunity, and confirmed that a probe had begun. The statement did not make clear exactly which part of the incident is being investigated.

Background

The video drew attention after social media users pointed out that Blord had recently been seen in caps associated with Rabiu Kwankwaso, running mate to Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), fuelling speculation about his political leanings. Blord was recently released from detention and publicly thanked activist Omoyele Sowore, now a presidential candidate himself, for his intervention. In late 2025, President Bola Tinubu ordered the withdrawal of police officers attached to VIPs.

How people reacted

The police announcement drew strong reactions online, with some users defending Blord. Blord had not publicly responded at the time of writing.

Armed thugs storm NDC town hall in Lagos; supporters fight back

12 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Suspected thugs armed with cutlasses, cudgels and stones stormed a Nigeria Democratic Congress town hall meeting at Oregun, Lagos, on Thursday morning, scattering attendees before party supporters regrouped and drove them out.

Read more

A town hall meeting of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) in Lagos was disrupted on Thursday morning, 12 November 2026, when suspected thugs stormed the venue.

What happened

The attackers, armed with cutlasses, cudgels and stones, arrived at an event centre in Oregun, Ikeja, where supporters of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi and his running mate Rabiu Kwankwaso had gathered. The atmosphere had been calm, with supporters singing and dancing before the meeting began. According to a journalist at the scene, the group was heard saying the NDC supporters were few and could be overpowered, and some members were heard asking for clearance to enter with guns.

Supporters fight back

Many people inside the hall fled when the attackers forced their way in, while motorists on Kudirat Abiola Way turned back to avoid the area. NDC supporters then regrouped, reportedly chased the attackers out of the hall and caught one of them in the car park. It is not clear how many people were injured, who sent the attackers, or whether anyone has been arrested. The police had not publicly commented at the time of writing.

Background

Obi won Lagos in the 2023 presidential election as the Labour Party candidate, with more than 580,000 votes, in a state seen as a stronghold of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC). Campaign-related violence has been reported against several parties in recent weeks, and on Wednesday the Inspector-General of Police warned officers not to aid thuggery, while the European Union raised concerns about violence ahead of the 16 January 2027 election.

IK Ogbonna calls out Governor Okpebholo over 'dangerous' Edo roads

12 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Actor IK Ogbonna has criticised Edo State governor Monday Okpebholo over the poor state of roads he saw on a road trip through the state, saying Edo people deserve better.

Read more

Nollywood actor IK Ogbonna has publicly criticised Edo State governor Monday Okpebholo over the condition of roads in the state.

What he said

In posts on his Instagram Stories on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the actor said he had just travelled by road through Edo and was shocked by what he saw. He described the roads as dangerously bad and a real safety risk for travellers, and questioned how a state with such a proud cultural and historical identity could still have crumbling roads. He tagged the governor and said the people of Edo deserve better.

Background

On the same day, comedian Sabinus criticised the Rivers State government over the state of roads in Rivers. Okpebholo, of the All Progressives Congress (APC), became Edo governor in November 2024. His government had not publicly responded to the actor's comments at the time of writing.

Sabinus criticises Rivers government over bad roads and refuse

12 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Comedian Sabinus has criticised Rivers State governor Siminalayi Fubara and other officials over bad roads, refuse dumps and a poorly maintained stadium in the state, questioning how public funds are being used.

Read more

Comedian and skit maker Sabinus, also known as Mr Funny, has criticised the Rivers State government over the condition of roads and public facilities in the state.

What he said

In a video shared on Instagram on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the comedian said his home state of Rivers had some of the worst roads he had seen, with refuse dumps everywhere. He said the state has a governor, council chairmen and lawmakers who drive past the problems with sirens every day, yet nothing changes. He also asked officials in charge of sports how money meant for the state stadium was being spent, given its condition.

Background

Siminalayi Fubara has governed Rivers since 2023 and has declared support for President Bola Tinubu's re-election. Sabinus's comments came on the same day actor IK Ogbonna criticised Edo State's governor over bad roads. The Rivers State government had not publicly responded at the time of writing.
