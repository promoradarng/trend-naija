All is fair in war, but has VeryDarkMan gone too far with Davido?

9 Nov 2026, 17:57 GMT

VeryDarkMan (VDM) does not seem to believe in fighting fairly. When he has a disagreement with someone, he appears willing to go to any length to make his point, even if it means bringing up some of the most painful moments in that person's life.

A painful chapter

Over the years, Davido has lost several close friends and associates in tragic circumstances. Their deaths set off all kinds of rumours and conspiracy theories online.

Some people even claimed the deaths were connected to rituals or sacrifices for fame and success. None of those claims was ever proven, and there is no credible evidence linking Davido to any of their deaths.

Imagine losing people who were very close to you, only to have strangers turn their deaths into accusations against you. Davido has lived with those memories for years. He has carried on with his music career, built his family and moved forward with his life, but moving forward does not mean the pain has disappeared.

Reopening old wounds

Now, amid his ongoing disagreement with Davido, VDM has reportedly brought those painful memories back into public discussion. This is where things appear to be getting out of hand.

You can criticise Davido's music, lifestyle, public statements or behaviour. You can disagree with him and challenge his opinions. That is perfectly normal. But bringing up the deaths of his close friends because of a disagreement raises serious questions.

What exactly is the intention? Is it to expose wrongdoing, to make a legitimate point, or simply to hurt someone where he is most vulnerable?

Where are the limits?

VDM has built a reputation for confronting people without holding back, and his supporters may argue he is only giving his opponents the same treatment they give others. But surely there should be limits.

Davido is not above criticism, and neither is VeryDarkMan. Both are public figures who should be held accountable for their actions. Using someone's personal tragedies as ammunition in a public quarrel, however, is something else entirely.

There are some experiences people never fully recover from. They may laugh and smile in public, but that does not mean they have forgotten. If winning an argument requires reopening another person's deepest wounds, perhaps the argument has already gone too far.

Over to you

People say all is fair in love and war, but does that mean we should abandon all compassion when we disagree with someone? Has VDM crossed the line with Davido, or is this just two public figures fighting without limits?

VeryDarkMan, Opeyemi Famakin and Peller defend Enioluwa after youth comments backlash

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

VeryDarkMan and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have spoken in defence of influencer Enioluwa, who apologised after his comments about young Nigerians drew criticism.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) and food critic Opeyemi Famakin have reportedly come to the defence of influencer Enioluwa Adeoluwa, after his comments about young Nigerians sparked a fierce online debate.

The controversy

Enioluwa had urged young people to take personal responsibility, seek mentors and keep going rather than expect quick results. Some critics said his remarks ignored the economic struggles many young Nigerians face.

On 17 November 2026, he apologised on X, saying he never meant to suggest Nigerian youths are lazy and acknowledging that his privilege may have shaped his view.

The defence

VDM and Famakin both spoke up for Enioluwa following the apology, adding their voices to the debate over his comments.

Peller joins in

Streamer Peller has also appealed to Nigerians to forgive Enioluwa. He said the influencer was wrong, but argued that people make mistakes and should be given the chance to learn rather than be condemned indefinitely.

His mother speaks

Enioluwa's mother, Bola Adeoluwa, a university lecturer, has also addressed the backlash after some students on TikTok turned their criticism on her, alleging she was a harsh grader. She responded publicly to the claims and the criticism of her son.

Okanlawon weighs in

Actor Deyemi Okanlawon also criticised social media users who attacked Enioluwa in harsh terms. Enioluwa had told a conference that many young people expect success to come easily and asked those in their twenties who say they are tired of life whether they had really experienced life.

What he said

Speaking at an APC campaign event in Oyo State on Saturday, 21 November 2026, KWAM 1 argued that the policy was necessary to secure Nigeria's future. The campaign

Moniepoint opens North Central regional headquarters in Abuja

Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened a regional headquarters in Abuja where customers can get in-person help, in line with CBN rules requiring physical locations for national microfinance banks.

Read more

Digital lender Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has opened its North Central regional headquarters in Abuja, giving customers a place to get face-to-face support.

Why now

The move is in line with a Central Bank of Nigeria requirement for nationally licensed microfinance banks to keep physical locations alongside their digital services.

Wider presence

Moniepoint already runs offices and service centres in several states, including Borno, Bauchi, Kaduna, Kano and Sokoto, and its point-of-sale network serves businesses in all 774 local government areas.
