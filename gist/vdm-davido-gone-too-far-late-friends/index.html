All is fair in war, but has VeryDarkMan gone too far with Davido?

9 Nov 2026, 17:57 GMT

VeryDarkMan (VDM) does not seem to believe in fighting fairly. When he has a disagreement with someone, he appears willing to go to any length to make his point, even if it means bringing up some of the most painful moments in that person's life.

A painful chapter

Over the years, Davido has lost several close friends and associates in tragic circumstances. Their deaths set off all kinds of rumours and conspiracy theories online.

Some people even claimed the deaths were connected to rituals or sacrifices for fame and success. None of those claims was ever proven, and there is no credible evidence linking Davido to any of their deaths.

Imagine losing people who were very close to you, only to have strangers turn their deaths into accusations against you. Davido has lived with those memories for years. He has carried on with his music career, built his family and moved forward with his life, but moving forward does not mean the pain has disappeared.

Reopening old wounds

Now, amid his ongoing disagreement with Davido, VDM has reportedly brought those painful memories back into public discussion. This is where things appear to be getting out of hand.

You can criticise Davido's music, lifestyle, public statements or behaviour. You can disagree with him and challenge his opinions. That is perfectly normal. But bringing up the deaths of his close friends because of a disagreement raises serious questions.

What exactly is the intention? Is it to expose wrongdoing, to make a legitimate point, or simply to hurt someone where he is most vulnerable?

Where are the limits?

VDM has built a reputation for confronting people without holding back, and his supporters may argue he is only giving his opponents the same treatment they give others. But surely there should be limits.

Davido is not above criticism, and neither is VeryDarkMan. Both are public figures who should be held accountable for their actions. Using someone's personal tragedies as ammunition in a public quarrel, however, is something else entirely.

There are some experiences people never fully recover from. They may laugh and smile in public, but that does not mean they have forgotten. If winning an argument requires reopening another person's deepest wounds, perhaps the argument has already gone too far.

Over to you

People say all is fair in love and war, but does that mean we should abandon all compassion when we disagree with someone? Has VDM crossed the line with Davido, or is this just two public figures fighting without limits?

VeryDarkMan warns Tinubu over deadly attack on NDC campaign in Delta

24 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Activist VeryDarkMan has issued a strong warning to President Tinubu after gunmen killed four people, including NDC youth leader Efemena Umukoro, at Omo-Agege's campaign event in Delta. He said political violence could spiral out of control.

Read more

Social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has issued a fierce public warning to President Bola Tinubu following the deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Uduere, Ughelli North, Delta State.

What he said

In a video posted on Instagram, VDM told the president that he would burn Nigeria down, arguing that targeting opposition figures with gunfire is a dangerous political strategy that could spiral far beyond anyone's control. He warned that even people close to the president would not be safe if the cycle of violence continued, and drew attention to the fact that the attack happened in Delta State.

The attack

Gunmen opened fire on former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege's campaign team on Monday, 23 November 2026, while he was addressing supporters. Four people were killed, according to his campaign, including Efemena Umukoro, an Urhobo youth leader popularly known as General Akima. Omo-Agege was unhurt.

Other reactions

Other social media commentators also condemned the killings, and Delta State Governor Sheriff Oborevwori called for everyone involved to be arrested regardless of their party. The Inspector-General of Police has ordered an investigation.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to VDM's remarks was included in the reports.

ADC tells Tinubu to resign or face impeachment over Kano governor's interview

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

The African Democratic Congress has reportedly demanded that President Tinubu resign or face impeachment, citing Kano Governor Abba Yusuf's recent interview about the 2023 Supreme Court ruling. The ADC says it points to possible gross misconduct.

Read more

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) has reportedly issued an ultimatum to President Bola Tinubu, demanding that he either resign or face impeachment proceedings in the National Assembly.

Why the ADC made the demand

The demand follows Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf's recent Hausa-language interview, in which he spoke about a promise to rejoin the APC if the president did not interfere in his Supreme Court case over the 2023 governorship election. The ADC argued that the governor's remarks suggested a link between the court case and a political deal, and that this could amount to gross misconduct, a constitutional ground for removing a president.

Background

Yusuf defected from the NNPP to the APC in January 2026. In his interview, he claimed that his former mentor, Rabiu Kwankwaso, sent him to make the promise to Tinubu. The Kwankwasiyya Movement has rejected the claim as false and said the governor offered no evidence.

The other side

No response from the presidency or the APC to the ADC's demand was included in the reports.

NNPC says $2.04bn in crude oil proceeds is not missing

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

NNPC has dismissed reports that $2.04 billion in crude oil proceeds linked to its former trading arm Duke Oil is missing. It says the figure is the gross value of 29 cargoes and that all transactions were properly accounted for.

Read more

The Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPC) has dismissed reports that $2.04 billion in crude oil proceeds involving its trading subsidiary, formerly known as Duke Oil, is missing.

What NNPC said

In a statement on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, NNPC's chief corporate communications officer said the $2.04 billion represented the gross value of 29 crude oil cargoes, not missing or unpaid revenue. The statement was titled 'Duke Oil cargoes are fully accounted for'.

The company maintained that all transactions under the Direct Sale Direct Purchase (DSDP) programme were properly documented. It said the apparent gaps in dollar receipts were due to the way the programme's settlements were structured.

Background

Reports suggesting the money could not be traced had sparked concern online.

Air Peace to start London–Owerri flights on 15 December

25 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Air Peace says it will begin direct flights between London and Owerri on 15 December, from both Heathrow and Gatwick. The route is expected to ease travel for South-East Nigerians coming home for Christmas.

Read more

Air Peace has announced that it will begin flights between London and Owerri on 15 December 2026, in a move expected to make it easier for many Nigerians, especially those from the South-East, to travel home.

The route

The airline said in a statement on Monday that the new service would operate from both London Heathrow and London Gatwick airports, giving passengers more flexibility when planning their journeys.

Why it matters

The launch comes ahead of Christmas and the New Year, when many Nigerians living abroad return home to spend time with their families. A direct route to Owerri, the Imo State capital, is expected to spare travellers the long road journeys from Lagos or Abuja to the South-East.

Background

The announcement comes as domestic fares have surged ahead of the festive season, with some return tickets within Nigeria reported to have doubled in recent weeks.
