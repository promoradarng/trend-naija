Carter Efe sparks outrage with harsh words against VeryDarkMan, drags his mother into their feud

4 Nov 2026, 16:25 GMT

Popular Nigerian comedian and content creator Carter Efe has stirred fresh controversy on social media after a disturbing statement directed at activist VeryDarkMan (VDM).

What he said

In a video circulating online, Carter Efe made a harsh declaration involving VeryDarkMan's mother, leaving many people asking how far social media disagreements should be allowed to go.

Speaking in Pidgin, he said, in effect: "VeryDarkMan, if you don't bring me down, your mother will die. Whatever your mother is looking for in this life, she will not find it."

The statement, which amounts to wishing death and misfortune on VDM's mother, has raised concerns about the kind of language being used in online confrontations.

When feuds go too far

Disagreements between public figures are nothing new in Nigeria. Celebrities exchange insults, make accusations and drag one another for days, and sometimes the whole thing turns into entertainment for their followers.

But bringing someone's mother into a personal disagreement, especially with words wishing her death or misfortune, is something many people consider completely unacceptable.

Whatever the disagreement between Carter Efe and VeryDarkMan may be, one question remains: what has VDM's mother got to do with it? These are parents who may know little or nothing about the arguments their children are having online.

No explanation yet

At the time of writing, Carter Efe has not publicly explained what led him to make the statement.

Carter Efe and Peller miss out as India's Ashswag wins international streamer award

13 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Nigerian streamers Carter Efe and Peller lost out on the Best International Streamer prize at the 2026 Streamer Awards, which went to Indian streamer Ashswag.

Nigerian content creators Carter Efe and Peller have missed out on an international award at the 2026 Streamer Awards.

The result

The two were nominated for Best International Streamer after the organisers released the shortlist in October. At the ceremony, reported on Friday, 13 November 2026, the prize went to Indian streamer Ashswag, who beat the Nigerian pair and other nominees from around the world.

Why it mattered

The nominations put the Nigerian duo among creators from around the world competing for the category.

Peller and Phyna react

In a post on X on Thursday night, Peller thanked his fans, saying their loyalty meant more to him than any trophy and that the nomination itself was a blessing. He complained, however, that the result of his category was announced on the red carpet before the main ceremony began, which he called unfair. Reality star Phyna went further in a livestream, accusing the organisers of using the Nigerian nominees to attract Nigerian viewers and engagement with no real intention of giving them the award.

Background

Peller rose to prominence as one of Nigeria's biggest TikTok live streamers, while Carter Efe, a skit maker and musician, has also built a large streaming following.

Social media personality and relationship coach Blessing CEO has admitted that she did not have breast cancer, months after telling the public she was battling the disease.

What she said

In an interview with activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), reported on Thursday, 12 November 2026, she said a medical check-up had found a lump in her breast, but that it was not cancerous. She admitted that she used the discovery to tell people it was cancer because she wanted financial support. She said she first gave the impression that she was thinking of selling her car and house, before fans encouraged her to share her account details so they could donate.

How the donations ended

She said money started coming in from members of the public until VeryDarkMan questioned the fundraising and demanded proof of her diagnosis, after which the donations stopped.

VeryDarkMan criticised

VeryDarkMan later asked Nigerians to forgive her, drawing criticism from some social media users. Commentator Japhman, in a video on 12 November, accused him of a double standard, saying the activist's 'Ratel' movement is built on demanding accountability from others.

US grounds all departing flights nationwide after telecom outage

14 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

The US Federal Aviation Administration ordered a nationwide ground stop on all departing flights on Friday, citing safety. More than 2,000 flights were cancelled and over 10,000 delayed, and officials blamed a telecommunications outage.

Read more

The United States briefly halted all departing flights across the country on Friday, 13 November 2026, causing widespread disruption.

What happened

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) issued a sudden nationwide ground stop at 3.54pm Eastern time, citing extraordinary circumstances and safety, and said all departing traffic should remain on the ground until further notice. It said inbound international flights would be handled case by case. At the time, more than 4,000 flights bound for US destinations were in the air, according to flight-tracking data.

The impact

Within hours, more than 2,000 flights had been cancelled and more than 10,000 delayed, according to flight-tracking figures, and lawmakers demanded an explanation.

The cause

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was no indication so far of anything malicious, and that the problem appeared to be a telecommunications outage. He said President Donald Trump had been briefed.
