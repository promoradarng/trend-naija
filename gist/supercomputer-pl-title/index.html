FIFA says hackers stole personal and business information in cyberattack

26 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

FIFA has confirmed it was the victim of a cyberattack in which criminals gained access to a limited set of personal and business information. It is working with law enforcement.

Read more

World football's governing body FIFA has confirmed that it was hit by a cyberattack in which hackers gained unauthorised access to personal and business information.

What FIFA said

In a statement on Thursday, 26 November 2026, FIFA said it had been the victim of a criminal cyberattack that gave access to a limited set of personal and business information. It said it was working with the relevant authorities, including law enforcement, to investigate.

The organisation strongly condemned the unlawful taking and publication of the information, particularly personal data, describing it as a serious violation of privacy that could have significant consequences for the people affected.

Unanswered questions

FIFA did not say when the attack happened, what kind of information was accessed or how many people were affected.

Raheem Sterling given suspended jail term for dangerous driving

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has been handed a 12-month prison sentence, suspended for 18 months, after pleading guilty to dangerous driving. The court heard he was inhaling from a balloon while driving his Lamborghini.

Read more

Former England winger Raheem Sterling has avoided an immediate prison term after a court handed him a 12-month sentence, suspended for 18 months, for dangerous driving.

The sentence

The sentence was handed down on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, at Basingstoke Magistrates' Court. The judge said the 31-year-old had shown a conscious disregard for the safety of other road users.

What happened

The court heard that Sterling drove his Lamborghini on the M25 and M3 motorways during the morning rush hour on 28 May. Witnesses told police they saw him weaving between lanes while inhaling from a balloon. Officers who attended his later crash found him in the passenger seat with two canisters of nitrous oxide.

Background

Sterling, who most recently played for Dutch club Feyenoord, pleaded guilty to dangerous driving in September.
