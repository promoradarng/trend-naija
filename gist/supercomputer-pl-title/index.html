Newcastle defender Amar Dedic stopped by armed police over bow and arrows

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Police in Northumberland stopped Newcastle United defender Amar Dedic and took a bow and arrows from his car after a member of the public reported two men carrying the weapon near a reservoir. Officers concluded there was no threat.

Read more

Newcastle United defender Amar Dedic was stopped by police in north-east England after a member of the public reported seeing two men carrying a bow and arrows.

What happened

According to reports on Friday, 6 November 2026, the 24-year-old Bosnia-Herzegovina international was travelling in a car with a friend when the report was made about two men walking along a track near a reservoir off Military Road in the Harlow Hill area of Northumberland. Six patrol cars, including armed officers, were reportedly sent, and the car was stopped about seven miles from where the men had been seen. Northumbria Police said that after speaking to the men they concluded there was no threat to the public, and a bow and arrows were seized from the vehicle. The police statement did not name the player.

Teammates arrive

Dedic reportedly cooperated fully with officers and had no bad intentions. Newcastle goalkeeper Mark Gillespie and former defender Paul Dummett reportedly arrived while officers were still talking to him, having been in the area, and stopped to support him.

Background

Dedic is a full-back who has played for clubs including Red Bull Salzburg and Benfica before moving to Newcastle.

Flamingos beat Brazil 3-1 to reach first U-17 Women's World Cup final

5 Nov 2026, 06:43 GMT

Nigeria's Flamingos have reached the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup for the first time after beating Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final. Awawu Bashiru scored twice and Queen Joseph once. They face Japan in the final.

Read more

Nigeria's under-17 women's team, the Flamingos, have made history by reaching the final of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup for the first time.

The match

The Flamingos beat Brazil 3-1 in the semi-final on Wednesday night, 4 November 2026, according to reports. Queen Joseph opened the scoring in the 56th minute, and Awawu Bashiru doubled the lead in the 71st minute before adding her second goal in the 80th minute to put the game beyond Brazil's reach.

A record run

The win beats the team's previous best performance, a bronze medal at the 2022 edition of the tournament. On the way to the semi-final, the Flamingos beat Venezuela in the quarter-finals, a result that earned praise from the Confederation of African Football.

The final

The Flamingos will now face Japan in the final as they chase Nigeria's first FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup title. Japan, one of the strongest sides in women's youth football, won the tournament in 2014.

Background

The result comes at a difficult time for Nigerian women's football at senior level. The Super Falcons failed to qualify for the Women's World Cup for the first time, and no Nigerian player was shortlisted for this year's CAF Women's Player of the Year award. The Flamingos' success has been welcomed as a sign that a new generation of players is coming through.

FIFA's message

FIFA congratulated the Flamingos on qualifying for the final, a first for Nigeria at this level of the women's game.

How people reacted

Nigerians celebrated the win across social media, praising the young players and their coaches and urging the government and the football federation to support them ahead of the final.

Enugu governor's wife visits Ukehe woman, gives her financial support, promises new house and business

6 Nov 2026, 23:49 GMT

Mrs Nkechinyere Mbah, wife of the Enugu State governor, has reportedly visited a woman in Ukehe with an envelope of cash, and promised to build her a house and help her set up a business so she can support herself.

Read more

A woman from Ukehe in Enugu State has reportedly received a visit from the wife of the state governor, Mrs Nkechinyere Mbah, who came with financial assistance and promises that could significantly improve her living conditions.

Cash, a house and a business

During the visit, the governor's wife reportedly handed the woman an envelope containing cash. But the help did not end there.

She also promised to build a house for the woman and to help her set up a business that would give her a steady income, so she can take care of herself without depending on others to survive.

Photos from the visit show the woman, dressed in lime green, raising her hands in joy as she walks with a group of women, and later receiving an envelope as people gather around.

Support that lasts

For someone facing difficult living conditions, a decent roof and a business that can put food on the table could make a lasting difference.

What makes the gesture stand out is the promise of long-term support. Money solves an immediate problem, but helping someone build a business can give them a livelihood for years to come.

Remembering the vulnerable

The visit has also drawn attention to the importance of identifying vulnerable people in our communities and offering help that goes beyond temporary relief.

Many Nigerians are struggling quietly, especially elderly women, widows and people without a reliable income. Sometimes what they need most is a chance to become financially independent.

For the Ukehe woman, the hope now is that the promises are fulfilled and that she soon has a comfortable home and a business of her own.

APGA endorses Tinubu for 2027 as Soludo targets half of Anambra votes

6 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

The All Progressives Grand Alliance has formally adopted President Tinubu as its preferred presidential candidate for 2027 and will not field its own. Governor Charles Soludo pledged to deliver half of Anambra's votes to the President.

Read more

The All Progressives Grand Alliance (APGA) has formally endorsed President Bola Tinubu of the All Progressives Congress (APC) as its preferred candidate for the 2027 presidential election.

What was announced

Anambra State governor Charles Soludo, the party's national leader, announced the decision in Awka on Friday, 6 November 2026, at the inauguration of APGA's national campaign for the 2027 elections, according to reports. He said the party would not field its own presidential candidate. Soludo argued that Tinubu had shown genuine care for the Igbo people, particularly people from Anambra, pointing out that the President appointed several Anambra indigenes to key positions in Lagos when he was governor of that state.

Target of 50%

Soludo acknowledged that Tinubu received only about 5,000 votes in Anambra in the 2023 presidential election, but said the President had still moved to address problems in the state and the wider South-East. He pledged to deliver half of Anambra's votes to Tinubu in 2027, describing the endorsement as a strategic move to strengthen the state's bargaining power with the federal government. He also said APGA had backed the ruling party's presidential candidate in past elections, so the decision continued that pattern.

Background

APGA is the governing party in Anambra, which is also the home state of NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi, a former APGA governor who won the state by a wide margin in 2023. Soludo has previously described Obi as a close family friend while declaring support for Tinubu, and he has been named a state coordinator in the President's expanded campaign council. This week the NDC's Anambra chapter set a target of 98 per cent of the state's votes for Obi.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027, and APGA is expected to campaign for the President alongside its own candidates for other offices.
