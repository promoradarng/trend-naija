What happened

The governor modelled a native outfit from the collection of designer Ugo Monye, walking the catwalk to loud cheers from the audience. At the end of the show, he returned to the runway with the designer, and the two shook hands and walked out together to applause.

What he said

Sanwo-Olu shared footage of the moment on his social media pages, describing it as a welcome break from his usual work and calling himself a part-time model for the night. Havertz goal gives Arsenal first league win at Anfield in 14 years, sends them top

1 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Arsenal beat Liverpool 1-0 at Anfield on Sunday through Kai Havertz's early goal, moving to the top of the Premier League with 24 points. It was the Gunners' first league win at Anfield in 14 years.

Read more

Arsenal have moved to the top of the Premier League after ending a long wait for a league win at Anfield.

The match

Kai Havertz scored the only goal in the 16th minute on Sunday, 1 November 2026, turning in a cross from Declan Rice at the back post. Liverpool controlled long spells of the game and pushed hard for an equaliser, with two penalty appeals turned down and Florian Wirtz hitting the crossbar from a free-kick. Arsenal goalkeeper David Raya denied Alexander Isak, while at the other end Alisson made a late save to stop substitute Christos Tzolis from doubling the lead.

The table

The result takes Arsenal to 24 points, three ahead of Manchester City, who reportedly lost to Brighton on the same day. It was Arsenal's first league win at Anfield in 14 years, ending a run of 13 league visits without victory.

What Arteta said

Manager Mikel Arteta told the BBC that the win was for everyone connected to the club and that 14 years without a victory at Anfield was a long time. He praised his players' performance but said it was too early to talk about the title race, adding that the team would now prepare for a big European match in Prague.

Why it matters

Arsenal have finished as runners-up in recent seasons and are hoping to win their first league title since 2004. The win at one of the hardest grounds in English football will boost their belief, while Liverpool will be disappointed not to have taken anything from a game they dominated for long periods.

Yahaya Bello vows to spend two weeks in Kano campaigning for Tinubu

1 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Former Kogi governor Yahaya Bello has reportedly pledged to move to Kano for two weeks to campaign for President Tinubu and Governor Abba Yusuf. He urged non-indigenous communities in the state to deliver more votes than in 2023.

Read more

Former Kogi State Governor Yahaya Bello has promised to personally campaign in Kano for President Bola Tinubu's re-election.

What he said

Speaking at a political mobilisation and empowerment programme for Yoruba and other non-indigenous communities in Kano, organised by the foundation of Rep. Leke Abejide and reported on Sunday, 1 November 2026, Bello said he would move to Kano for two weeks to campaign for the President and for Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf. He urged non-indigenous communities in the state to beat the nearly 500,000 votes they reportedly gave Tinubu in 2023, and said Abejide's network could deliver at least two million votes. He also pointed to work on the Kano–Abuja highway as evidence of the President's commitment to the North.

Support for communities

Abejide, who represents Yagba Federal Constituency in Kogi State and holds a Yoruba community title in Kano, announced ₦500 million in interest-free loans for women's cooperatives, along with support for widows. He said programmes for young people and men are being planned.

Background

Kano has the second-highest number of registered voters in Nigeria after Lagos, and the APC is investing heavily there after Governor Yusuf moved from the NNPP to the APC. The state is also the home of Rabiu Kwankwaso, Peter Obi's running mate on the NDC ticket. Bello, who governed Kogi from 2016 to 2024, is facing a money-laundering case brought by the EFCC, which he denies. Deputy Senate President Barau Jibrin also predicted a Tinubu victory at an APC event in Kano on Sunday.

What happens next

The campaign in Kano is expected to intensify as the APC and NDC compete for the state's large vote.
