Former All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential aspirant Adamu Garba has denied reports that he endorsed Peter Obi ahead of Nigeria's 2027 presidential election.

"The claim is false"

In a public statement dated 18 November 2026, Garba described the claim circulating online as false.

He said he has withdrawn from partisan politics and is now focusing on his business ventures and on Africa's digital economy.

His new focus

Garba named Crowwe, Tablars and Justitia SI among the projects currently receiving his attention.

He also urged the public to rely only on his official channels for accurate information about his activities.

Where did the story come from?

The origin of the endorsement claim remains unclear. No post, interview or public statement showing Garba endorsing Obi has been found.

What is clear is that Garba has publicly disputed the claim.

A familiar pattern

With the 2027 elections approaching, false claims about endorsements are spreading quickly on social media. It is the second time this week that a story about backing for Obi has been corrected, after Obi himself clarified that his Washington visit was not an endorsement from the US Congress.

VeryDarkMan hospitalised in China with severe malaria, says it is his first hospital admission in 32 years

19 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT

Social media commentator VeryDarkMan says he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria during a business trip. He said doctors told him he may stay for four to seven days.

Read more

Nigerian social media commentator Martins Vincent Otse, popularly known as VeryDarkMan (VDM), has disclosed that he is receiving treatment in a hospital in China after being diagnosed with severe malaria.

The hospital video

VDM shared a video from a hospital bed on Thursday, 19 November 2026, lying in a hospital gown beside medical equipment.

He said he fell ill while on a business trip to China and at first tried to treat himself.

"First admission in 32 years"

According to him, his condition got worse on Wednesday night, and he was taken to hospital the following morning. He said doctors told him he could remain under medical care for between four and seven days.

VDM added that this was the first time in 32 years that he had been admitted to hospital overnight.

What we know

The video confirms that he was in a medical facility when it was recorded. However, no hospital document or statement from his doctors has been made public, so details of his diagnosis and treatment are based on his own account.

He did not name the hospital or the city where he is being treated.

A reminder about self-medication

His experience is a reminder that malaria can become serious quickly, and that treating it at home without proper testing can be risky. Health experts advise anyone with symptoms to get tested and treated early.

Cooking gas prices rise; refilling 12.5kg now costs up to ₦22,500

19 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Cooking gas has reportedly risen from about ₦1,400 per kg to between ₦1,500 and ₦1,800 depending on location. Some households are cutting back or switching to other fuels ahead of Christmas.

Read more

Nigerian households are facing fresh pressure as cooking gas prices rise across major cities ahead of the Christmas season.

The new prices

The price of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) has reportedly risen from about ₦1,400 per kilogram to between ₦1,500 and ₦1,800, depending on location. That means refilling a 12.5kg cylinder can now cost as much as ₦22,500, up from about ₦17,500.

How households are coping

Market findings suggest some Nigerians are now buying smaller quantities, cutting down on how much gas they use or switching to other cooking fuels.
