Burna Boy's 'For Everybody' campaign film wins 15 awards

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

'For Everybody', a sports and music campaign film starring Burna Boy, Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, has reportedly won 15 prizes at the 2026 w3 Awards, including a gold award. The film was directed by Dave Meyers and released in January.

Read more

A campaign film featuring Burna Boy and several of Africa's best-known footballers has picked up a string of international awards.

What happened

'For Everybody', made by Sporty Group's production arm Sporty Studios together with RadicalMedia, reportedly won 15 awards at the 2026 w3 Awards, according to reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026. It won a gold award in the creator and brand collaboration category, as well as 14 silver awards in categories including music video, cinematography, brand storytelling, branded content, and sports and entertainment.

About the film

The film was directed by Dave Meyers, an American director best known for his music videos with major global stars, and was released in January 2026. It brings together music and sport to celebrate African talent and culture. Alongside Burna Boy, it features Nigerian football stars Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, as well as Spain's Nico Williams, France's Eduardo Camavinga, Ghana legend Michael Essien, Kenya's Victor Wanyama and South African rugby star Cheslin Kolbe.

Background

The w3 Awards recognise creative work in digital media, including websites, videos and online campaigns. Burna Boy, a Grammy winner, has increasingly worked on projects linking music and sport, and recently presented the Socrates Award at this year's Ballon d'Or ceremony. Oshoala is one of Africa's most decorated female footballers, having won the African Women's Footballer of the Year award a record number of times.

How fans reacted

Fans celebrated the recognition online, saying the film showed how African music and sport can work together to reach global audiences.

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya introduces new wife to Fountain of Life congregation

1 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya of Fountain of Life Church has introduced his new wife, Mo Odukoya, to his congregation during Sunday service in Lagos. Pastor Tunde Bakare says the couple married in the United States and that he blessed the union.

Read more

Pastor Jimmy Odukoya, senior pastor of Fountain of Life Church in Ilupeju, Lagos, has remarried and presented his new wife to his church.

What happened

During Sunday service on 1 November 2026, Odukoya introduced his wife, who will be known as Pastor Mo Odukoya, to the congregation. Days earlier, he had sparked engagement rumours by sharing a photo of two hands wearing rings, captioned as a new beginning. According to Pastor Tunde Bakare, General Overseer of The Citadel Global Community Church, the wedding took place in the United States and he blessed the marriage. Bakare said the bride is the daughter of Reverend Theophilus and Bolaji Daniel.

What the pastor said

Odukoya reportedly told the congregation that he had remained single since taking over the leadership of the church and felt called to remarry, referring to the biblical teaching that it is not good for a man to be alone.

Background

Jimmy Odukoya, an actor and pastor, took over as senior pastor of Fountain of Life Church after the death of his father, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, in 2023. He played the lead role in the 2023 film 'Orisa'. He was previously married to Kemi Odukoya, with whom he has children.

How people reacted

Many church members and fans congratulated the couple and shared videos from the service. Others used the moment to debate remarriage and divorce in the church, with some asking questions about his previous marriage. Odukoya has not publicly commented on those questions.

Adeboye says he never carried a placard against Jonathan, calls it a march against corruption

1 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

RCCG General Overseer Pastor Enoch Adeboye has denied carrying a placard against former President Goodluck Jonathan, saying the event was a prayer walk against corruption. He said he has never protested against any government.

Read more

The General Overseer of the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG), Pastor Enoch Adeboye, has responded to claims that he once protested against former President Goodluck Jonathan's government.

What he said

Speaking at the church's monthly Holy Ghost Service, in comments reported on Sunday, 1 November 2026, Adeboye said he did not carry any placard against Jonathan. He explained that the event was a prayer walk and a march against corruption, aimed at drawing attention to the country's problems. He added that he has never protested against any government and that his role is to pray for those in authority.

Why it came up

Clips and posts claiming that the cleric had carried a placard against the Jonathan administration have circulated online in recent weeks, as supporters of different candidates debate the role of religious leaders in politics ahead of the 2027 elections. Some critics argued that church leaders who spoke out under Jonathan have been quieter under the current government.

Background

Adeboye leads one of Nigeria's largest churches, with branches around the world, and his statements are closely followed. Religious leaders have played a prominent part in national debates on insecurity and the economy. Several pastors and Islamic clerics have also spoken out this week about the elections and religious freedom.

How people reacted

His explanation drew mixed reactions. Some members welcomed the clarification, while others insisted the march was widely seen as a protest at the time.

APC supporters reportedly attacked and vehicle burnt before Katsina governor's rally

1 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Suspected hoodlums reportedly attacked APC supporters travelling to Governor Dikko Radda's campaign rally in Faskari, Katsina State, on Saturday and set a campaign vehicle on fire. Several people were injured, and police had not commented at the time of the report.

Read more

Violence has again marred campaigning in Katsina State, with supporters of the All Progressives Congress (APC) reportedly attacked on their way to a rally by Governor Dikko Umaru Radda.

What happened

According to reports on Sunday, 1 November 2026, suspected hoodlums ambushed APC supporters in Yankara town, Faskari Local Government Area, on Saturday as they travelled to a rally for President Bola Tinubu and the governor's 2027 campaigns. The attackers reportedly blocked a major road used by the campaign convoy and set fire to a campaign vehicle belonging to a support group, the Musa Adamu Network. The violence reportedly followed a religious procession in the area, which the attackers are said to have exploited. Several people were injured. A witness claimed there were deaths, but this has not been independently confirmed.

Reactions

The governor went ahead with the rally. The secretary of the Musa Adamu Network condemned the attack and called on security agencies to identify and prosecute those responsible, while the state APC chairman, Bishir Saulawa, pledged a peaceful, issue-based campaign. The police had not commented at the time of the report.

Background

It is the second reported attack on an APC campaign in Katsina in less than a week, after suspected thugs disrupted a rally in Malumfashi. Opposition figures have also reported attacks, including on AAC presidential candidate Omoyele Sowore in Ekiti and the ADC governorship candidate in Sokoto. Atiku Abubakar and other leaders have called for investigations and warned against political violence.

What happens next

Security agencies are under pressure to ensure safe campaigning as parties step up their activities ahead of the January 2027 elections.
