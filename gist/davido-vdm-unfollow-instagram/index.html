Davido and VeryDarkMan unfollow each other on Instagram as feud deepens

1 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

The rift between Davido and social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has moved to a new stage, with the two reportedly unfollowing each other on Instagram.

What happened

Checks on Saturday, 31 October 2026, reportedly showed that Davido, who has more than 31 million followers, had removed VDM from his following list, and that VDM, who has more than 3 million followers, had done the same. Neither appears to have blocked the other, and neither has publicly explained the move.

The build-up

The two were once close allies, but their friendship broke down earlier this year. This week, VDM's friend Kokopee said he had tried to bring them back together and that VDM felt betrayed by the singer. Davido responded with a 'cap' emoji, suggesting he disputed the account. He also congratulated rapper Zlatan Ibile on 'Inferno', a chart-topping song widely seen as a jab at VDM.

VDM's court case

Separately, VDM announced that a criminal case brought against him by the police had been struck out, and he shared his excitement about the ruling.

How people reacted

Fans were divided. Some praised Davido for cutting ties, while others criticised him and urged him to support VDM's charity work instead. Many said the unfollow confirmed that a reconciliation was unlikely for now.

Nollywood actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington has opened up about one of the hardest periods of her life, revealing that she spent much of 2026 dealing with health challenges that she kept largely private.

In a video posted on Instagram on Friday, 27 November 2026, the actress, who is married to singer Banky W, was seen on a video call with a group of women, believed to be family members or close friends, as she shared test results from her doctor. Fashola says Tinubu stands out because he has kept his allies for 16 years

27 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Former Lagos Governor Babatunde Fashola has argued that President Tinubu differs from Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi because he has kept the loyalty of his political allies for about 16 years. He said even former opponents now work with him.

Read more

Former Lagos State Governor Babatunde Fashola has made a case for President Bola Tinubu's re-election, arguing that he stands apart from his main rivals by keeping his political allies for about 16 years.

The comparison

Speaking in a television interview on Friday, 27 November 2026, Fashola noted that Tinubu, Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi all began their political rise as state governors, although Atiku was elected governor but did not serve. He framed his argument as a job reference question, saying that Tinubu's long-time allies have stayed with him.

He also said that even some of the president's fiercest former opponents now work with him.

Background

Fashola, who succeeded Tinubu as Lagos governor and later served as a federal minister, also condemned the 'rig and die' slogan in the same interview. Atiku and Obi are expected to sign an opposition cooperation pact at a summit in Abuja on Monday.

The other side

No response from the Atiku or Obi campaigns was included in the reports.
