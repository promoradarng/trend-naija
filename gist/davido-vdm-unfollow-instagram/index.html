Davido and VeryDarkMan unfollow each other on Instagram as feud deepens

1 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

The rift between Davido and social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has moved to a new stage, with the two reportedly unfollowing each other on Instagram.

What happened

Checks on Saturday, 31 October 2026, reportedly showed that Davido, who has more than 31 million followers, had removed VDM from his following list, and that VDM, who has more than 3 million followers, had done the same. Neither appears to have blocked the other, and neither has publicly explained the move.

The build-up

The two were once close allies, but their friendship broke down earlier this year. This week, VDM's friend Kokopee said he had tried to bring them back together and that VDM felt betrayed by the singer. Davido responded with a 'cap' emoji, suggesting he disputed the account. He also congratulated rapper Zlatan Ibile on 'Inferno', a chart-topping song widely seen as a jab at VDM.

VDM's court case

Separately, VDM announced that a criminal case brought against him by the police had been struck out, and he shared his excitement about the ruling.

How people reacted

Fans were divided. Some praised Davido for cutting ties, while others criticised him and urged him to support VDM's charity work instead. Many said the unfollow confirmed that a reconciliation was unlikely for now.

Kano court jails TikToker five months and orders 10 strokes for insulting Hisbah commander

19 Nov 2026, 06:41 GMT

A Shariah court in Kano has reportedly sentenced a 19-year-old TikTok user to five months in prison and 10 strokes of the cane after she pleaded guilty to insulting the Hisbah Board and its commander. Two other defendants pleaded not guilty.

Read more

A Shariah court in Kano State has reportedly sentenced a 19-year-old TikTok user, Zainab Abubakar, to five months in prison and 10 strokes of the cane for insulting the state Hisbah Board and its commander, Malam Aminu Ibrahim Daurawa.

The judgment

The court, sitting at the Hisbah Board headquarters in Sharada, delivered the judgment on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, after she pleaded guilty to the charge under Section 312 of the Penal Code. She was sentenced without the option of a fine.

The other defendants

She was arraigned alongside two young men who were also arrested by the Hisbah over allegations of using TikTok to insult the agency and its commander. Both pleaded not guilty to charges of conspiracy and insulting the board. The court remanded them in custody for two weeks while it considers their bail application and directed the prosecution to prepare its witnesses.

Background

The Kano Hisbah is the state's religious police, which enforces Islamic moral codes. Earlier this week, TikTokers were reported to have been arrested over videos said to have insulted its commander.

Mr Macaroni says he is scared of marriage after being single for too long

19 Nov 2026, 06:41 GMT

Skit maker Mr Macaroni has reportedly said he is afraid of settling down because he has been single for so long. He admitted he cares for a friend but fears he cannot commit fully.

Read more

Content creator and actor Debo Adedayo, known as Mr Macaroni, has opened up about why he is scared of marriage.

What he said

Speaking on the In My Opinion podcast, he reportedly said he has been single for so long that he no longer knows how to be in a relationship, including the constant calls and being present for a partner.

He revealed that there is a friend he genuinely loves and admires, but he is unsure he could commit to her fully. He said part of him wants a relationship, but some days he just wants to be alone, and it would be unfair to profess love to someone if he could not commit all the way.

Honest admission

He jokingly described himself as a terrible partner, admitting he can go weeks without communicating. He gave the example of promising to call his love interest, only to play video games when he got home instead.

Background

Mr Macaroni recently completed a master's degree in Theatre Arts.

VDM praised as Oyo corps members finally receive delayed allowance

18 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Oyo State Batch 25C corps members have reportedly received their delayed final ₦77,000 allowance, two days after VeryDarkMan raised the alarm. He shared a video of a corps member celebrating the payment.

Read more

Corps members of the Oyo State Batch 25C have reportedly received their long-delayed final allowance, after social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), whose real name is Martins Vincent Otse, publicly called out the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).

What happened

On Wednesday, 18 November 2026, VDM shared a video on Instagram showing a corps member celebrating after receiving a credit alert and thanking him for taking up the matter.

How it started

VDM first raised the issue on 16 November, after receiving messages from more than 100 affected corps members. He said they had completed their service and attended their passing-out parade at the end of October, but had not been paid their final ₦77,000 allowance. He called on the NYSC Director-General to act.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised the activist for taking up the corps members' complaint.
