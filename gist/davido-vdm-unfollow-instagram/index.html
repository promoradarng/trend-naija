Davido and VeryDarkMan unfollow each other on Instagram as feud deepens

1 Nov 2026, 09:41 GMT

The rift between Davido and social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has moved to a new stage, with the two reportedly unfollowing each other on Instagram.

What happened

Checks on Saturday, 31 October 2026, reportedly showed that Davido, who has more than 31 million followers, had removed VDM from his following list, and that VDM, who has more than 3 million followers, had done the same. Neither appears to have blocked the other, and neither has publicly explained the move.

The build-up

The two were once close allies, but their friendship broke down earlier this year. This week, VDM's friend Kokopee said he had tried to bring them back together and that VDM felt betrayed by the singer. Davido responded with a 'cap' emoji, suggesting he disputed the account. He also congratulated rapper Zlatan Ibile on 'Inferno', a chart-topping song widely seen as a jab at VDM.

VDM's court case

Separately, VDM announced that a criminal case brought against him by the police had been struck out, and he shared his excitement about the ruling.

How people reacted

Fans were divided. Some praised Davido for cutting ties, while others criticised him and urged him to support VDM's charity work instead. Many said the unfollow confirmed that a reconciliation was unlikely for now.

Atiku denies quitting 2027 race, says those spreading the claim are working for Tinubu

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has reportedly dismissed claims that he has withdrawn from the 2027 election. His campaign said no group calling itself his supporters can speak for him, and accused rivals of tactics that help the ruling party.

Read more

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has reportedly rejected claims that he has pulled out of the 2027 presidential race, describing them as a deliberate falsehood.

What his campaign said

In a statement issued on Sunday, 22 November 2026, by the director of strategic communication for the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, the campaign is said to have stressed that no group presenting itself as Atiku's supporters had the authority to announce a withdrawal on his behalf.

The statement reportedly accused those behind the claim of borrowing tactics it associated with the ruling APC, such as presenting so-called supporters from a rival camp. It said any opposition party spreading such stories was effectively helping President Bola Tinubu, and urged parties that want to defeat the APC to make their case directly to voters.

Still campaigning

Atiku's campaign said his Affordable Life campaign was still active and focused on the election.

Background

The denial comes as campaigns for the January 2027 presidential election gather pace, with Tinubu of the APC, Atiku of the ADC and Peter Obi of the NDC among the leading candidates. Speculation about possible withdrawals and alliances has been circulating online in recent days.

Wike says rejecting Tinubu in 2027 would leave reforms unfinished

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

FCT Minister Nyesom Wike has reportedly said voting President Tinubu out in 2027 would leave his economic reforms incomplete. He spoke at a church service in Rivers State that dedicated his Rainbow Coalition campaign council.

Read more

FCT Minister Nyesom Wike has reportedly warned that rejecting President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid in 2027 would leave the government's economic reforms unfinished and could undo gains already made.

Where he spoke

Wike made the remarks on Sunday, 22 November 2026, at a thanksgiving and dedication service at St Peter's Anglican Church in Rumueprikom, his hometown in Obio/Akpor Local Government Area of Rivers State. The service formally dedicated the Rainbow Coalition Campaign Council ahead of the elections, and Wike said campaigning would begin on 23 November.

His argument

He reportedly described Tinubu as a leader who took unpopular but necessary decisions to stop the country from sliding into crisis. As an example, he pointed to the university system, saying the repeated cycle of lecturers' strikes over unpaid entitlements had largely been broken.

Background

Wike, a former PDP governor of Rivers State, is one of Tinubu's most vocal backers and leads a coalition of PDP members supporting the president. Earlier this month, he told his FCT campaign team not to let opposition parties reach 25 per cent of the vote in Abuja.

Miley Cyrus marries drummer Maxx Morando in Palm Springs

Singer Miley Cyrus has married musician Maxx Morando in a poolside ceremony in Palm Springs, California. The wedding came about a year after the couple got engaged.

Read more

American singer Miley Cyrus has married drummer Maxx Morando, according to reports.

The wedding

The 33-year-old pop star and Morando, 28, who plays drums for the band Liily, wed on Saturday, 21 November 2026, in Palm Springs, California. Photos released after the event reportedly showed the couple standing by a swimming pool under an arch decorated with white flowers.

The guests

Miley's mother, Tish Cyrus, her father, Billy Ray Cyrus, and her sister Noah Cyrus were among the guests. Billy Ray's partner, actress Elizabeth Hurley, and her son Damian also attended.

The couple hosted a rehearsal dinner with loved ones the evening before, with the menu reportedly printed with the couple's initials.

Background

Miley and Morando got engaged about a year ago. The singer was previously married to Australian actor Liam Hemsworth.

Olamide honoured with 'Amuludun' title by Nike Davies-Okundaye

22 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

Rapper Olamide has reportedly been given the Yoruba title Amuludun by artist Nike Davies-Okundaye during a visit to her Lagos gallery. The honour came days after he released his album Time and Space.

Read more

Rapper and YBNL boss Olamide has reportedly received a traditional Yoruba honour from the celebrated artist Chief Nike Davies-Okundaye.

The honour

During a visit to the Nike Art Gallery in Lekki, Lagos, Olamide was given the title Amuludun, which roughly means the one who brings joy to the town. The veteran artist is also said to have presented him with a custom-made beaded portrait.

A show of respect

A video of the visit shared online showed the rapper chatting warmly with her, and at one point prostrating before her in the traditional Yoruba gesture of respect, a moment that drew attention from fans.

Background

The visit came just days after Olamide released his latest album, Time and Space, on 16 November 2026. He remains one of the most influential figures in Nigerian hip-hop and Afrobeats, and his YBNL label is home to artists including Fireboy DML and Asake.
