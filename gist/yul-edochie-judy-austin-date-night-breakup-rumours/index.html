Yul Edochie shares date-night video with Judy Austin amid breakup rumours

2 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Nollywood actor Yul Edochie appears to have answered rumours about the state of his marriage to actress Judy Austin with a romantic video.

What they posted

On Sunday, 1 November 2026, Yul shared an Instagram video of himself and Judy relaxing together on a night out, wishing fans a happy new month. Judy also shared footage from the date night, describing him as her partner for life. Neither addressed the rumours directly.

The rumours

The couple had been unusually quiet on social media in recent weeks, which led some fans to speculate that they had split. Some even linked Yul to another actress after he attended her birthday party, claims that were never confirmed.

How people reacted

Reactions were mixed. Some fans welcomed the video and said it put the rumours to rest, while others questioned whether the couple were truly happy and described the post as damage control. Yul also drew criticism this week for reposting a fan-made video about his life that included footage of his first wife, May Edochie.

Background

Yul Edochie, the son of veteran actor Pete Edochie, announced in 2022 that he had taken Judy Austin as a second wife, sparking a long public dispute with May Edochie, his first wife. May later went to court over the marriage, and the matter has remained a talking point among Nollywood fans. Yul also defended polygamy this week in a discussion about a US pastor's second marriage.

Nollywood actress Iheme Nancy has opened up about returning to church after four years away, crediting reality TV star Mercy Eke for bringing her back.

What she shared

In a post shared on Sunday, 15 November 2026, Nancy said a painful experience within the church in 2022, which she described as a betrayal by fellow Christians, made her walk away from her faith completely after the death of her mother.

She said Mercy Eke contacted her on Saturday and insisted that she would be attending Harvesters Church with her the next morning, promising she would not regret it. Mercy then checked on her at around 9am on Sunday to make sure she was getting ready.

At the service

Nancy admitted she was reluctant and in a bad mood, but went anyway. She said she was flustered when Mercy walked straight to the front pews, close to the pulpit, at the church led by Pastor Bolaji Idowu.

Actors IK Ogbonna and Ego Nwosu have reportedly confirmed they are not getting married, after their viral proposal video drew congratulations. They said it was to promote a wedding-themed event in Port Harcourt.

Nollywood actors IK Ogbonna and Ego Nwosu have reportedly cleared the air on their viral proposal video, confirming that they are not actually getting married.

The reveal

In a new video posted on Monday, 16 November 2026, the pair appeared together and explained that the proposal was part of a campaign for an event called Love at Julirose, billed for 15 December in Port Harcourt, Rivers State.

The event is described as a full wedding-themed experience, with a live band, bridesmaids and groomsmen, photographers and live performances. Tickets are on sale with different price levels and a dress code.

The playful banter

The two kept up the jokes in the new video, with IK saying he was still the groom and Ego replying that she was still the bride, making it clear the campaign was designed to create buzz.

How fans reacted

Many fans who had congratulated the pair earlier this month reacted with amusement and mock anger, with some jokingly asking for their congratulations back.

Yul Edochie sparks debate after criticising men who kneel to propose

17 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

Actor Yul Edochie has reportedly drawn mixed reactions after describing kneeling to propose as a foreign practice that lowers African men. He was reacting to a viral video of a rejected proposal.

Read more

Nollywood actor Yul Edochie has reportedly sparked a fresh online debate after criticising men who go down on one knee to propose marriage.

What happened

Yul was reacting to a viral video, said to have been edited, showing a man kneeling to propose to his partner at an event, only for her to turn him down.

In a post on X, he reportedly described kneeling to propose as a practice Africans copied from Western culture, arguing that it reduces the dignity of African men. He used strong language to dismiss men who do it.

How people reacted

His comments drew a wide range of responses. Some users backed his view on cultural identity, while many others pushed back, saying the way a man proposes has nothing to do with the quality of a marriage, and some criticised the actor directly.
