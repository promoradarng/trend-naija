Burna Boy's 'For Everybody' campaign film wins 15 awards

31 Oct 2026, 11:42 GMT

'For Everybody', a sports and music campaign film starring Burna Boy, Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, has reportedly won 15 prizes at the 2026 w3 Awards, including a gold award. The film was directed by Dave Meyers and released in January.

Read more

A campaign film featuring Burna Boy and several of Africa's best-known footballers has picked up a string of international awards.

What happened

'For Everybody', made by Sporty Group's production arm Sporty Studios together with RadicalMedia, reportedly won 15 awards at the 2026 w3 Awards, according to reports on Saturday, 31 October 2026. It won a gold award in the creator and brand collaboration category, as well as 14 silver awards in categories including music video, cinematography, brand storytelling, branded content, and sports and entertainment.

About the film

The film was directed by Dave Meyers, an American director best known for his music videos with major global stars, and was released in January 2026. It brings together music and sport to celebrate African talent and culture. Alongside Burna Boy, it features Nigerian football stars Asisat Oshoala and Kenneth Omeruo, as well as Spain's Nico Williams, France's Eduardo Camavinga, Ghana legend Michael Essien, Kenya's Victor Wanyama and South African rugby star Cheslin Kolbe.

Background

The w3 Awards recognise creative work in digital media, including websites, videos and online campaigns. Burna Boy, a Grammy winner, has increasingly worked on projects linking music and sport, and recently presented the Socrates Award at this year's Ballon d'Or ceremony. Oshoala is one of Africa's most decorated female footballers, having won the African Women's Footballer of the Year award a record number of times.

How fans reacted

Fans celebrated the recognition online, saying the film showed how African music and sport can work together to reach global audiences.

Banky W returns to music on Victor Thompson's 'Super Power'

30 Oct 2026, 20:49 GMT

Singer and pastor Banky W is back on a new song, featuring on Victor Thompson's upcoming single 'Super Power', due on 6 November. He said he had accepted that his recording career was over and described the song as an act of obedience.

Read more

Banky W, the R&B singer turned pastor and former boss of Wizkid's old label, is making a return to music.

What he announced

On Friday, 30 October 2026, Banky W revealed on Instagram that he features on gospel singer Victor Thompson's upcoming single, 'Super Power', and shared a preview of the music video. The song is due to be released on Friday, 6 November. A clip of the two singers enjoying the song together was also shared online.

Why he returned

Banky W reportedly said he had accepted that his recording career had ended, and described the song as an act of obedience rather than a career move. He said he had prayed that if a return was meant to happen, the songs would come. About three weeks later, Thompson sent him a message saying they were meant to make music together.

Background

Banky W, whose real name is Olubankole Wellington, was one of Nigeria's leading R&B stars in the 2000s and early 2010s and founded the Empire Mates Entertainment label, which launched Wizkid's career. His last full project was the 2017 album 'Songs About U'. He has since focused on ministry and public life, and is married to actress Adesua Etomi-Wellington. Victor Thompson is known for the gospel hit 'This Year (Blessings)'.

How fans reacted

Fans welcomed the news with excitement, with many saying they had missed his voice. Some said they hoped the song would lead to a full gospel album.

Zlatan's diss track 'Inferno' tops Apple Music Nigeria as VeryDarkMan responds

30 Oct 2026, 11:49 GMT

Zlatan Ibile's new track 'Inferno', featuring Peruzzi and Shoday and reportedly aimed at VeryDarkMan, has topped the Apple Music Nigeria chart. VDM has mocked its success on his Instagram story, after earlier daring it to stay at number one for a month.

Read more

Rapper Zlatan Ibile has scored a chart-topping hit with a song widely seen as a response to social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), and VDM has not stayed quiet.

The song

'Inferno', which features Peruzzi and Shoday, reportedly went to number one on the Apple Music Nigeria top songs chart and the Nigerian iTunes chart, according to reports on Friday, 30 October 2026. It also reportedly topped the chart in Sierra Leone. Fans have described it as a diss track aimed at VDM.

VDM's response

VDM reacted on his Instagram story with mockery, brushing off the chart success. Before the song reached number one, he had sarcastically challenged it to stay at the top for a full month and described the buzz around it as forced publicity.

Background

The feud between the two goes back to the period after the death of singer Mohbad in 2023, when VDM publicly pressured Zlatan and other music figures to speak out. More recently, VDM criticised Zlatan's high-profile wedding to Davita, claiming it was used to promote an album that did not do well. Zlatan has rarely responded directly, which is why many fans see 'Inferno' as his long-awaited reply.

How people reacted

Many users mocked VDM's response, with some calling it damage control, while his supporters said the song's success would not last. VDM has been involved in several online disputes this week, including with businessman Blord, former BBNaija winner Phyna and Davido's camp.

Sanwo-Olu becomes 'part-time model' on Lagos Fashion Week runway

1 Nov 2026, 11:44 GMT

Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu walked the runway at Lagos Fashion Week on Saturday in an outfit from designer Ugo Monye's collection, drawing cheers from the crowd. He shared the video online, calling himself a model for one night.

Read more

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu surprised fashion lovers by taking to the runway at Lagos Fashion Week on Saturday night, 31 October 2026.

What happened

The governor modelled a native outfit from the collection of designer Ugo Monye, walking the catwalk to loud cheers from the audience. At the end of the show, he returned to the runway with the designer, and the two shook hands and walked out together to applause.

What he said

Sanwo-Olu shared footage of the moment on his social media pages, describing it as a welcome break from his usual work and calling himself a part-time model for the night. He said he had a really good time and congratulated the designer and everyone working to make Lagos a hub of creativity, fashion and talent.

How people reacted

The video drew warm and humorous reactions. Actress Funke Akindele described it as dope, while broadcaster Do2dtun praised the governor for supporting the entertainment industry. Other users joked that the governor had found a side hustle.

Background

Lagos Fashion Week is one of Africa's biggest fashion events and showcases designers from Nigeria and across the continent. Sanwo-Olu, who is serving his second and final term, often appears at cultural and entertainment events in the state and has promoted Lagos as a centre for the creative economy. Earlier this week, a video of him giving flowers to his wife also went viral.
