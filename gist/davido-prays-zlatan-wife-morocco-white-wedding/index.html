Davido prays for Zlatan and his wife at their white wedding in Morocco

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Davido has drawn attention online after a video showed him praying for Zlatan Ibile and his wife at their white wedding reception in Morocco.

What happened

In footage shared on Friday, 6 November 2026, the singer is seen standing in a grand ballroom decorated with chandeliers and draped fabric, addressing the couple and their guests. Instead of giving a conventional speech, he said prayer was his priority. He began by mentioning the couple's traditional wedding, which had already taken place in Lagos, and said it had gone smoothly despite the large crowd, before praying for the newlyweds and their marriage.

Background

Zlatan, one of Nigeria's best-known rappers, held his white wedding in Morocco after a traditional ceremony in Lagos. Videos from the celebrations have been widely shared, including one of Zlatan in tears and another of Davido singing along to Zlatan's song 'Inferno'. The weddings came during a public feud between Davido and social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM), who has also taken swipes at Zlatan and said this week he would be turning his attention to the rapper. Davido's aide Israel DMW has also denied claims linking the singer to VDM's past arrest.

How people reacted

Many fans praised Davido's sincerity and said the clip showed a spiritual side of him that people rarely see. Some asked why others were surprised, pointing out that he often speaks about his faith, while VDM's supporters continued to debate the feud in the comments.

  • Davido prayed for the couple at the Morocco reception.
  • Their traditional wedding took place in Lagos.
  • The video comes amid VDM's feud with both singers.

Olamide announces YBNL reunion concert with Adekunle Gold, Fireboy and Lil Kesh

11 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

Olamide is bringing past and present YBNL stars, including Adekunle Gold, Fireboy DML, Lil Kesh, Young Jonn and Pheelz, together for a reunion show in Lagos on 18 December.

Read more

Rapper and record label boss Olamide has announced a reunion concert featuring some of the biggest names to come through his label, YBNL.

The line-up

In an Instagram video on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Olamide said the show will take place in Lagos on 18 December as part of a December festival. The line-up includes Adekunle Gold, Lil Kesh, Fireboy DML and Young Jonn, as well as producer Pheelz and DJ Enimoney.

Why it matters

The artists represent different eras of the label. Adekunle Gold and Lil Kesh rose to fame in YBNL's early years, while Fireboy DML and Young Jonn became key names later on. Pheelz produced many of the label's hits and helped shape its sound.

Background

Olamide founded YBNL in 2012 and has helped launch several successful careers. He recently announced a headline show at London's 20,000-capacity O2 Arena on 25 March 2027, and released a new single in October.

INEC says its staff in about 10 states uploaded AI photos to voters' register

11 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

INEC says it has identified its own officials in about 10 states who uploaded AI-generated photographs to the voters' register. The images have been removed and the commission says the staff involved will face disciplinary action.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) says it has identified officials in about 10 states who uploaded photographs generated by artificial intelligence to Nigeria's voters' register.

What INEC said

Speaking on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, at a webinar on AI, misinformation and the 2027 elections, INEC National Commissioner Mohammed Haruna, who chairs the commission's Information and Voter Education Committee, said a large number of AI-generated pictures had been posted onto the register and had since been taken down. He said the commission had the names of the staff involved across about 10 states and would take disciplinary action against them.

Limits on what INEC can do

Haruna said it could be difficult for the commission to stop members of the public from digitally altering their own photographs before submitting them, but that INEC could act directly where its own staff were responsible.

Background

The issue of AI-generated images on the register had already drawn public attention, raising concerns about the accuracy of the voter roll ahead of the general elections. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

First Lady Remi Tinubu receives U-17 World Cup champions Flamingos at the Villa

11 Nov 2026, 14:42 GMT

First Lady Oluremi Tinubu, standing in for President Tinubu, received the Flamingos at the Presidential Villa in Abuja after their historic FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup win, the first by an African team.

Read more

The Flamingos, Nigeria's under-17 women's football team, have been welcomed at the Presidential Villa in Abuja after winning the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.

The reception

First Lady Oluremi Tinubu received the players and officials on Wednesday, 11 November 2026, on behalf of President Bola Tinubu, in what was described as a celebratory homecoming visit and the federal government's formal recognition of the team's achievement.

The win

The Flamingos beat Japan 2–1 in the final in Morocco on Saturday, 7 November, with goals from Awawu Bashiru and Oluebube Umejiaku, becoming the first African side to win the tournament. After losing their opening game to Poland, they won five matches in a row, including victories over defending champions North Korea, Mexico, Venezuela and Brazil. The win means Nigeria has now won the FIFA U-17 World Cup in both the men's and women's competitions.

Rewards

The Senate has pledged ₦100 million to the team. The players had earlier received $50,000 in cash.
