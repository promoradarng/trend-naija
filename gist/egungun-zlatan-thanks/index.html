Nigerian politics will teach you lessons that no university can offer!

Nollywood veteran and political activist Hilda Dokubo, who has previously been associated with strong opposition to FCT Minister Nyesom Wike, has reportedly visited him at his Port Harcourt residence.

This is the same Hilda Dokubo whom critics have accused of making hostile remarks about Wike and of supporting groups that displayed his obituary on a coffin during political protests. Those allegations remain unverified.

Now, reports of a meeting between the two have raised eyebrows, especially because of the presence of Kingsley Chinda, a prominent Rivers politician widely regarded as a Wike ally.

The big question is: what could these three political figures be discussing behind closed doors?

Is this political reconciliation? A fresh alliance ahead of the 2027 elections? Or another example of the famous Nigerian political principle, "scratch my back, and I will scratch yours"?

Of course, nobody outside that meeting can say for certain what was discussed. But considering the bitter political disagreements that have characterised Rivers State politics, the reported gathering is bound to generate questions.

It is a reminder for Nigerians to be careful about fighting, insulting and destroying relationships because of politicians.

Today, two politicians are sworn enemies. Tomorrow, they are sitting in the same living room, smiling, shaking hands and possibly negotiating their next political move.

Meanwhile, their supporters are still fighting yesterday's battles on Facebook!

Never gamble your peace, friendships or future on the political quarrels of Nigerian politicians and their celebrity supporters. In Nigerian politics, today's enemy can become tomorrow's political partner.

What do you think brought Hilda Dokubo, Wike and Kingsley Chinda together? Reconciliation, political calculation or something else?

Emotional moment boy collapses after his mother surprises him at school following four years abroad

28 Oct 2026, 10:42 GMT

A young boy reportedly collapsed with emotion when his mother, who had been living abroad for four years, turned up unannounced at his school. The video of their reunion has touched many online and sparked a conversation about the price families pay when parents travel abroad for a better future.

Read more

An emotional reunion between a mother and her son has captured attention on social media after the young boy reportedly collapsed upon seeing his mother for the first time in four years.

According to the story accompanying the circulating video, the woman had been living abroad for four years, leaving her son behind while she pursued a better future for her family.

After years of separation, she decided to return home and surprise her son at his school without telling him she was coming.

What was supposed to be a joyful reunion quickly became an overwhelming moment for the young boy.

Upon seeing his mother unexpectedly, the boy reportedly became so overwhelmed that he collapsed, leaving those around him concerned.

The touching scene has sparked emotional reactions online, with many social media users reflecting on the sacrifices parents make when they travel abroad in search of better opportunities for their children.

For many families, living abroad comes with a painful price. Parents sometimes spend years away from their children, missing birthdays, school activities, family celebrations and important childhood moments.

Although financial support can help provide a better life, the emotional impact of prolonged separation can be difficult for both parents and children.

The circumstances surrounding the boy's reported collapse, including whether he needed medical attention, have not been independently confirmed.

Nevertheless, the reunion has reminded many people of the importance of family, parental presence and the emotional bond between mothers and their children.

Four years without seeing his mother, and suddenly she appeared right before his eyes. Imagine the emotions running through that little boy's heart.

What do you think about parents leaving their young children behind for several years to work abroad in search of a better future? Is the sacrifice worth it?
