Detained El-Rufai tags Trump, Macron and UN in appeal for a fair trial

31 Oct 2026, 06:42 GMT

Former Kaduna State Governor Nasir El-Rufai has taken his legal battle to the international stage, appealing for a fair trial in a social media post that tagged several world leaders and international bodies.

What he posted

In a post on X on Friday, 30 October 2026, under the hashtag #FairTrial, El-Rufai reportedly said that access to justice and a fair hearing are basic human rights, not favours to be granted at will. He tagged US President Donald Trump, French President Emmanuel Macron, the United Nations and its Human Rights Council, Amnesty International, the European Union, the African Union, the UK government and major media organisations. He did not make a specific formal request, but observers said the post was aimed at drawing international attention to his case.

His legal situation

El-Rufai has been in the custody of the Independent Corrupt Practices and Other Related Offences Commission (ICPC) since February 2026, after being transferred from the EFCC. He faces corruption and money-laundering charges in cases at the Federal High Court and the Kaduna State High Court. He was briefly released in March to attend his mother's burial. On 9 October, the Court of Appeal in Kaduna dismissed his challenge to a refusal of bail, and on 26 October a Federal High Court in Abuja rejected his no-case submission in a separate case brought by the DSS, ordering him to open his defence on 25 November.

The other side

El-Rufai and his supporters say his detention is politically motivated. The ICPC rejects this, saying it has followed due process.

Background

El-Rufai governed Kaduna from 2015 to 2023 and was once a close ally of President Bola Tinubu before falling out with the ruling party. He has since aligned with the opposition.

Makinde camp denies claim he will step down for Atiku

26 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Oyo Governor Seyi Makinde's campaign has dismissed as fake a claim that he plans to withdraw his presidential bid in favour of Atiku Abubakar on 30 November. The claim was made by a political commentator.

Read more

The campaign of Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde has dismissed as fake news a claim that he plans to drop his 2027 presidential bid in favour of former Vice President Atiku Abubakar.

The claim

Political commentator Lere Olayinka had written that Makinde, the Allied Peoples Movement (APM) candidate, would step down for Atiku on 30 November, the date of the opposition parties' summit in Abuja.

The denial

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, the verified X account linked to Makinde's campaign rejected the claim and accused the ruling APC of spreading falsehoods. The post drew both support and criticism from social media users.

Background

Makinde said this week that he remains open to working with Atiku and Peter Obi to unseat President Tinubu, but ruled out a formal merger of parties. Opposition parties are due to hold their second summit in Abuja on Monday. No response from the APC to the accusation was included in the reports.

NDC deputy governorship candidate in Ebonyi defects to APC

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) deputy governorship candidate in Ebonyi State, Ambassador Uka Chris Ndubuisi, has reportedly left the party and joined the All Progressives Congress (APC).

The announcement

Ndubuisi made the announcement on Thursday, 26 November 2026, at an APC governorship campaign rally in the state. He pledged his loyalty to President Bola Tinubu and Governor Francis Nwifuru, and said the decision was entirely his own.

The other side

No response from the NDC to the defection was included in the reports. Defections between parties have been common as the 2027 campaigns gather pace.
