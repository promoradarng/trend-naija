MC Oluomo says he is taking adult English lessons, jokes with Lege Miami

4 Nov 2026, 17:42 GMT

National Union of Road Transport Workers president Musiliu Akinsanya, popularly known as MC Oluomo, has spoken openly about his efforts to improve his English.

What he said

Speaking at a rally in support of President Bola Tinubu's re-election on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, MC Oluomo reportedly said he was attending adult lessons to sharpen his spoken English. Addressing Nollywood actor Lege Miami, who organised the event, he joked about their shared struggles with the language. He also pointed to his children, who he said work in the United States as a nurse, a doctor and an engineer, as proof of his success.

Background

MC Oluomo rose from Lagos motor parks to become the national president of the NURTW and is a close ally of the President. He has often been mocked online over his English. At the same rally, Lege Miami broke down in tears while thanking MC Oluomo and others for supporting the event. Separately, a Lagos court this week ordered the arrest of the union's Lagos State chairman over alleged contempt.

How people reacted

Many people praised MC Oluomo for being honest and willing to keep learning, while others joked about the exchange between him and Lege Miami.

  • MC Oluomo says he is taking adult English lessons.

Senator Seriake Dickson dives into River Forcados, urges youths to learn to swim

Former Bayelsa governor Seriake Dickson has shared a video of himself diving into the River Forcados near the community where he grew up, encouraging young people in riverine areas to learn to swim as a life skill.

Senator Henry Seriake Dickson has drawn reactions online after sharing a video of himself swimming in the River Forcados in Bayelsa State.

The video

In the post, shared on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the former Bayelsa governor said he had returned to the river near the community where he grew up until the age of 20, and that swimming there is something he does from time to time. He said his children also know how to swim.

His message

Dickson urged young people, especially young men in riverine communities, to learn to swim, describing it as an essential life skill rather than just a pastime.

How people reacted

Many social media users praised the senator, with some calling him a lifesaver for promoting the skill.

Background

Dickson represents Bayelsa West in the Senate and is the national leader of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), the party of presidential candidate Peter Obi.

APC-branded ₦200 recharge cards reportedly handed out at Tinubu's Rivers rally spark debate

12 Nov 2026, 06:42 GMT

Photos of ₦200 recharge cards bearing APC branding, reportedly distributed at President Tinubu's campaign rally in Rivers State, have drawn mockery and debate online about campaign giveaways.

Read more

Images of recharge cards bearing the logo of the All Progressives Congress (APC), reportedly handed out at President Bola Tinubu's campaign rally in Rivers State, have sparked debate on social media.

What happened

A man who came across pictures of the ₦200 airtime cards questioned online what anyone could achieve with such a small amount, and his comment, shared on Thursday, 12 November 2026, quickly drew reactions. The cards were said to have circulated among people who attended the rally.

How people reacted

Some users mocked the giveaway and said the money would be better spent on fixing the country, while others joked about how the airtime might be used. Some warned that small gifts like this could still influence some voters.

Background

Campaign giveaways have become a talking point ahead of the 2027 elections. The African Democratic Congress (ADC) recently accused the APC of handing out cash and gifts at campaign events and called on the anti-graft agency and INEC to investigate. The APC had not publicly responded to the recharge card images at the time of writing. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Obi and NDC supporters recite old national anthem instead of new one at Lagos town hall

13 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

A video from the NDC town hall in Lagos shows Peter Obi and Lagos governorship candidate Funso Doherty leading supporters in reciting the old national anthem, 'Arise O Compatriots', instead of the new one adopted in 2024.

Read more

Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), has drawn attention after reportedly leading supporters in reciting Nigeria's former national anthem rather than the current one.

What happened

A video from the party's town hall meeting in Oregun, Lagos, on Thursday, 12 November 2026, which went viral afterwards, shows Obi and the NDC's Lagos governorship candidate, Funso Doherty, choosing not to recite the current anthem when attendees were asked to stand for it. Instead, they and the crowd recited the old anthem, 'Arise O Compatriots', together.

The anthem change

Nigeria replaced 'Arise O Compatriots', which had been in use since 1978, with its original post-independence anthem, 'Nigeria We Hail Thee', in May 2024. President Bola Tinubu signed the change into law after the bill passed quickly through the National Assembly. The speed of the change drew criticism at the time.

Response

Neither Obi's campaign nor the APC had commented on the video at the time of writing.

Background

The same town hall meeting was disrupted earlier in the day by suspected thugs, before it went ahead. The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Bruno Fernandes reportedly rejects second Man United contract offer

13 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has reportedly turned down a second contract offer, with his current deal running out at the end of the season, adding to the turmoil after Michael Carrick's sacking.

Read more

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has reportedly rejected a second contract offer from the club.

The talks

According to reports on Friday, 13 November 2026, United first offered Fernandes a new deal in the summer, which he turned down, and talks between his representatives and the club have continued since then without agreement. His current contract expires at the end of the season.

His record

The Portugal international had an outstanding 2025/26 season, helping United finish third and return to the Champions League after two years away. He set a new Premier League record of 21 assists in a season, breaking the mark previously shared by Thierry Henry and Kevin De Bruyne, and won both the FWA Footballer of the Year and PFA Player of the Year awards.

Background

The news adds to a difficult period at Old Trafford. The club sacked head coach Michael Carrick this week after one league win in 10 games left them in the relegation zone, and Mauricio Pochettino has reportedly agreed to take over.
