ADC names venue as Atiku kicks off presidential campaign in Sokoto

4 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) has named the venue for the official launch of Atiku Abubakar's 2027 presidential campaign in Sokoto.

The venue

According to reports, the campaign will be flagged off at the party's secretariat on Ilorin Road, Sokoto, on Wednesday, 4 November 2026. Senator Ibrahim Lamido, who coordinates the ADC presidential campaign in the state, said Atiku was expected to arrive at Sultan Abubakar III International Airport at about 9am, and invited supporters and members of the public to welcome him before moving to the venue. He urged party executives in all 23 local government areas of the state to mobilise supporters.

Why the venue was kept quiet

The party had earlier withheld details of the venue, citing alleged intimidation, an attack on the convoy of its governorship candidate and the sealing of one of its campaign offices. On Tuesday, Atiku accused the Department of State Services of recruiting protesters to disrupt the rally, a claim the DSS had not publicly answered. The Sokoto State Government said it sealed the office over questions about who owns the building and to prevent further disturbances.

Background

Atiku, a former vice-president, is the ADC's candidate after the party became the platform of an opposition coalition. Sokoto is in the North-West, a region with a large share of Nigeria's voters, and is governed by the APC. Other candidates, including President Bola Tinubu of the APC and Peter Obi of the NDC, have also been holding rallies and town halls across the country.

What happens next

Security agencies are expected to be deployed in Sokoto for the event, and the ADC plans further rallies in other states.

  • The launch holds at the ADC secretariat on Ilorin Road, Sokoto.
  • Atiku is expected to arrive at 9am.
  • The party cited intimidation for keeping the venue quiet.

Alex Otti joins South-East governors to receive Tinubu at Enugu APC rally

28 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

Abia governor Alex Otti has reportedly arrived in Enugu to join other South-East governors in receiving President Tinubu for the APC's South-East campaign rally. His appearance comes days after Tinubu said Otti was with him.

Read more

Abia State Governor Alex Otti reportedly arrived in Enugu on Saturday, 28 November 2026, to join other South-East governors and political leaders in receiving President Bola Tinubu ahead of the APC's South-East presidential campaign rally.

The arrival

Otti, who came with his wife, was reportedly received at the Akanu Ibiam International Airport by Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah. He was also said to be listed among the leaders present at the rally at Michael Okpara Square in Enugu.

'He's with me'

His appearance came days after President Tinubu, speaking at a meeting with Igbo leaders in Lagos, claimed that Otti was among the South-East governors backing his re-election, recalling that the two had worked together before Otti became governor.

Mixed reactions

Otti's presence has reportedly sparked debate on social media, as he was elected governor on the Labour Party platform. Some users questioned why he attended an APC campaign event, while others said what mattered most was his performance in Abia State.

Background

The rally is part of the APC's zonal campaign tour ahead of the 16 January 2027 presidential election, as the NDC, ADC and other opposition parties also step up their campaigns.

Obi, Makinde and Melaye join hands at Dunamis prayer night for 2027 polls

28 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Peter Obi, Seyi Makinde and Dino Melaye reportedly attended an all-night service at Dunamis church in Abuja, where Pastor Paul Enenche led prayers for the 2027 elections. Representatives of the APC and PDP were also called to the altar.

Read more

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi, Oyo State governor and APM candidate Seyi Makinde, and ADC chieftain Dino Melaye were reportedly among politicians at an all-night service at the Dunamis International Gospel Centre in Abuja on Friday, 27 November 2026.

Prayers at the altar

The service was part of the church's 30th anniversary celebrations. At about 1am on Saturday, founder Paul Enenche reportedly invited the three politicians to the altar for a prayer session focused on Nigeria and the 2027 general election. They were joined by an aide representing Delta State governor Sheriff Oborevwori, who stood in for the APC, and a church member affiliated with the PDP.

With representatives of five parties at the altar, Enenche asked them to hold hands as he led the congregation in prayer. The prayers reportedly asked for God's will to prevail in Nigeria and included calls directed at INEC ahead of the polls.

Background

The presidential and National Assembly elections are scheduled for 16 January 2027. The service came as opposition figures prepare for a summit in Abuja on 30 November.

INEC holds nationwide mock accreditation in 17,656 polling units

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

INEC is conducting a mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise across 17,656 polling units in all 36 states and the FCT on Saturday. It is testing its upgraded BVAS machines and security arrangements.

Read more

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) is conducting a nationwide mock voter accreditation and result transmission exercise on Saturday, 28 November 2026.

The exercise

The test is taking place at 17,656 polling units across the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. It is designed to test INEC's upgraded Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS), the result transmission process and election security arrangements under simulated election-day conditions. Sample ballot papers are being used.

Why it matters

The exercise comes ahead of the presidential and National Assembly elections on 16 January 2027. INEC has faced criticism in past elections over delays in uploading results, and this week the commission named nine states as hotspots for campaign violence.

Lagos adjusts

Lagos State cancelled its monthly environmental sanitation exercise, which had been scheduled for the same morning, so that residents could take part.

Remi Tinubu says EFCC can investigate her empowerment funds

28 Nov 2026, 09:42 GMT

First Lady Remi Tinubu has said she does not use government money for her empowerment programmes and invited the EFCC to investigate. She spoke at an APC women's rally in Warri.

Read more

First Lady Oluremi Tinubu has challenged the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) to investigate the funds she uses for her empowerment programmes, insisting she does not use government money.

What she said

Speaking on Friday, 27 November 2026, at the South-South APC women's presidential campaign rally in Warri, Delta State, the First Lady said wealthy Nigerians willingly donate to her charitable work because they trust her not to misuse their money. She said she had been involved in charity for years, had served three terms as a senator, and did not need to beg anyone for money.

Peace message

She also urged APC supporters not to fight anyone during the campaign, saying the party was confident of victory.

Background

The rally took place in Delta State days after a deadly attack on an NDC campaign event in Ughelli North, and amid growing concern about political violence ahead of the 2027 elections.
