Read more

The son of the second wife at the centre of the viral Ukehe controversy has spoken out, begging Nigerians to stop attacking his mother.

"Is it not enough already?"

"Nigerians, please stop attacking my mum. Is it not enough already? That woman does not deserve all this hate," he said.

"Do you want her to die? If anything happens to my mum, you people won't like it. Please, this is enough."

How it started

His mother has faced a wave of criticism online since a video from his father's Ozo title-taking ceremony went viral. In the video, the second wife's children reportedly refused to spray money on the first wife, Mrs Gertrude Anulika Ngwu, despite repeated appeals from the master of ceremonies.

Claims then spread online that Mrs Anulika had endured years of mockery over being childless and had moved back to her father's home. Those claims have not been independently verified.

Since then, well-wishers have visited Mrs Anulika with gifts and money, and the Enugu governor's wife sent a representative with cash and promises of a house and a business.

The other side of the story

The son's plea is a reminder that there are always two sides to a family story, and that online anger can quickly turn into harassment.

Sympathy for Mrs Anulika is understandable. But insults and threats against another woman, whose side of the story has not been heard, help no one. Families caught up in viral moments are still real people.

Senate sets aside ₦100 million reward for World Cup-winning Flamingos

10 Nov 2026, 20:44 GMT

Senate President Godswill Akpabio has announced a ₦100 million reward for the Flamingos after their FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup win, following a motion by Senator Abdul Ningi, who said the team had received too little recognition.

Read more

The Senate has set aside ₦100 million for the Flamingos, Nigeria's under-17 women's football team, after their victory at the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup.

What happened

Senate President Godswill Akpabio announced the reward during plenary on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, as the chamber considered a congratulatory motion moved by Senator Abdul Ningi of the ADC, who represents Bauchi Central. Ningi described the team's win as historic, saying the young players had achieved what many senior national teams had not, despite going into the tournament with little public attention. He said the players deserved far more publicity and a proper reception on their return.

Background

The Flamingos beat Japan 2-1 in the final in Rabat, Morocco, on 7 November to become the first African team to win the tournament, and Nigeria's first FIFA world champions in women's football at any level. FIFA does not pay prize money for the competition, but a sponsor of the National Sports Commission gave the team $50,000 in Rabat. NDC candidate Peter Obi said NFF officials stopped him from greeting the team at Abuja airport, a claim the Presidency said politicised the national teams.

Amaechi says Nigerians now miss Buhari because life was better under him

10 Nov 2026, 20:44 GMT

ADC vice-presidential candidate Rotimi Amaechi says Nigerians miss former President Buhari, urging them to compare the exchange rate and fuel prices under Buhari with today. The APC says the reforms were needed to save the economy.

Read more

Rotimi Amaechi, the vice-presidential candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), says Nigerians miss former President Muhammadu Buhari because life was better under his government.

What he said

Speaking on a television programme on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, Amaechi recalled Buhari's prediction that people would one day call him back, and said Nigerians were now saying they miss him. He urged people to compare their lives under Buhari with their circumstances today, pointing to the exchange rate, fuel prices and what he described as corruption.

Background

Amaechi served as Buhari's Minister of Transportation and directed his 2015 campaign, before losing the APC presidential ticket to Bola Tinubu in 2022. He said last week that Buhari had promised in writing to back him at that primary. Buhari died in 2025. The naira traded at about ₦461 to the dollar when Tinubu took office in May 2023 and petrol was subsidised; both changed sharply after the President removed the subsidy and unified the exchange rate. Tinubu says those reforms saved Nigeria from economic collapse and that the naira has since recovered some value.

What happens next

The presidential election is scheduled for 16 January 2027.

Mele Kyari denies ₦210 trillion went missing at NNPC under his watch

10 Nov 2026, 20:44 GMT

Former NNPC boss Mele Kyari has told a Senate committee that no money was lost under his leadership, saying the disputed ₦210 trillion in the company's accounts came from a misreading of its financial records.

Read more

Former Group Managing Director of the Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPCL), Mele Kyari, has denied that ₦210 trillion went unaccounted for while he led the company.

What happened

Kyari appeared before the Senate Committee on Public Accounts, which is examining queries raised by the Auditor-General of the Federation over NNPCL's audited financial statements for 2017 to 2023, according to reports on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. The disputed figure is made up of ₦103 trillion recorded as accrued expenses and ₦107 trillion listed as sundry receivables. Kyari told the committee that no money was lost under his watch and that the figures arose from a misreading of the company's records.

The committee's position

The committee began examining the figures in June 2025 and has repeatedly rejected the company's explanations, insisting that NNPCL properly account for the entries.

Background

Kyari led NNPC from 2019 until 2025, overseeing its conversion into a limited liability company under the Petroleum Industry Act.
