Abba Yusuf claims Kwankwaso once sent him to promise Tinubu they would join APC

23 Nov 2026, 11:42 GMT

Kano State Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf has reportedly claimed that his former political mentor, Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, sent him to President Bola Tinubu after the 2023 governorship election to promise that they would return to the All Progressives Congress (APC).

The claim

Speaking in a Hausa-language interview on Sunday, 22 November 2026, Yusuf is said to have explained the background to his defection from the NNPP to the APC in January 2026. He claimed Kwankwaso asked him to tell Tinubu that they would join the ruling party if the Supreme Court upheld his victory over the APC's Nasir Yusuf Gawuna, and was even willing for him to swear on the Qur'an to show the promise was serious.

Yusuf also reportedly alleged that Kwankwaso held several meetings with Tinubu and sent delegations to discuss the arrangement.

Background

Yusuf and Kwankwaso, once close allies, have since split. Kwankwaso is now the running mate to Peter Obi on the NDC presidential ticket, while Yusuf is in the APC. In the same interview, the governor spoke about the end of his marriage to Kwankwaso's niece, Zainab.

The other side

No response from Kwankwaso or the NDC to the claim was included in the reports.

The timeline

According to the governor, the visit to Tinubu took place while his 2023 victory was being challenged in court by the APC's candidate, and the aim was to assure the president that they would join the APC if he let the case run its course without interference. Yusuf defected to the APC on 26 January 2026.

Kwankwasiyya denies claim

The Kwankwasiyya Movement has dismissed the governor's claim as false, describing it as another attempt to shift responsibility for his own political choices onto Kwankwaso. In a statement on Monday, its spokesperson said Yusuf had every right to explain his defection but should not make allegations against others without evidence, and that major decisions in the movement have always required wide consultation.

  • Yusuf says Kwankwaso offered to rejoin the APC.
  • He claims Kwankwaso met Tinubu several times.
  • No response from Kwankwaso was reported.

El-Rufai tells court he never tapped Ribadu's phone

25 Nov 2026, 11:41 GMT

Former Kaduna Governor Nasir El-Rufai has reportedly told a Federal High Court in Abuja that he never intercepted the phone calls of National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu. He opened his defence on Wednesday.

Read more

Former Kaduna State Governor Nasir El-Rufai has reportedly told a Federal High Court in Abuja that he never intercepted the telephone conversations of National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu, and never asked anyone to do so for him.

In court

El-Rufai opened his defence on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, before Justice Joyce Abdulmalik, after his no-case submission was dismissed in October. Led in evidence by his lawyer, he told the court that he had never owned equipment capable of intercepting calls and had no technical knowledge of how such interception works.

He also denied having any access to the NSA's communication networks or personal devices.

How the case began

The case arises from remarks El-Rufai made in a television interview on 13 February 2026. He denies the charges.

What happens next

The trial continues as El-Rufai presents his defence.

'Political rhetoric'

El-Rufai told the court that his 'tapped' remark was political rhetoric. The court later adjourned the trial to February to allow his defence to continue.

Several killed as commercial bus catches fire on Lagos-Badagry Expressway

26 Nov 2026, 14:41 GMT

Several passengers died and more than a dozen were injured when a commercial bus burst into flames at Magbon on the Lagos-Badagry Expressway on Thursday morning. Officials gave death tolls of six and eight.

Read more

Several people were killed and others injured when a commercial bus caught fire on the Lagos-Badagry Expressway on Thursday morning, 26 November 2026.

What happened

The incident happened near the Checkpoint area, inward Magbon, between 8am and 9am. The bus, travelling from Mile 2 towards Badagry, reportedly suffered brake failure, and the driver lost control. The vehicle somersaulted and burst into flames, trapping passengers inside.

The toll

Officials gave different figures. The Lagos State Fire and Rescue Service put the death toll at six adults, two women and four men, with 12 others injured. The Lagos State Traffic Management Authority (LASTMA) said eight passengers died and 13 suffered severe burns. The injured were taken to Badagry General Hospital.

Driver fled

The LASTMA general manager said the driver fled the scene after the crash, leaving passengers trapped, and offered condolences to the families of the victims. Traffic and road safety officers were deployed to the scene.

Gunmen have killed at least 16 people, including women and children, in an attack on a community in Bassa Local Government Area of Plateau State, according to a local association. Four others were injured.

At least 16 people, including women, young people and children, have reportedly been killed in an attack on a community in Bassa Local Government Area of Plateau State.

The attack

According to the Irigwe Development Association, gunmen armed with assault rifles stormed the Tafi Gana community near Miango at about 11:30pm on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, and opened fire. By dawn on Thursday, 16 people were dead and four others had been taken to hospital with injuries.

Background

The attack is the latest in a series of deadly incidents in Plateau State, including in Bokkos, where gunmen set a secondary school on fire earlier this week.

Response

No statement from the police or the state government on the attack was included in the initial reports.
