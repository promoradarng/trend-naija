Scripted slap from Sola Sobowale reportedly leaves mark on Toyin Abraham's face

6 Nov 2026, 14:43 GMT

A behind-the-scenes video from a film set has got fans talking after a scripted slap from Sola Sobowale reportedly left a visible mark on Toyin Abraham's face.

What the video shows

The clip, which went viral on Friday, 6 November 2026, shows Toyin seated and visibly shaken, with people around her fanning her and a man standing close by, apparently trying to reassure her. The scene appears to have been filmed indoors, in a home setting. According to reports, the slap was part of the script, but the impact was strong enough to leave a mark on her cheek. Toyin reportedly insisted that the crew retake the scene rather than stop filming for the day. The title of the production and the location of the shoot were not revealed.

Background

Sola Sobowale is one of Nollywood's most celebrated veteran actresses, known for powerful performances over several decades, including her role in the 'King of Boys' films. This week she opened an elderly care home in Ibadan, which was commissioned by Governor Seyi Makinde. Toyin Abraham is a popular actress and producer who recently announced that veteran actor Pete Edochie will star in her December cinema film, 'Bestie'. She has also been in the news this week after sending a cease and desist letter to bloggers and announcing a change to her name.

How people reacted

Fans praised Toyin's dedication to her craft, while others expressed concern for her and debated whether real slaps should be used in films at all, with some urging producers to use safer stage-fighting techniques.

  • The slap was reportedly scripted.
  • It reportedly left a mark on Toyin's cheek.
  • Toyin is said to have insisted on a retake.

In posts on his Instagram Stories on Thursday, 12 November 2026, the actor said he had just travelled by road through Edo and was shocked by what he saw. Arokodare scores twice as Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 in AFCON qualifier

13 Nov 2026, 20:42 GMT

Substitute Tolu Arokodare scored twice as Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 in Uyo on matchday three of the 2027 AFCON qualifiers, moving the Super Eagles to second in Group L, a point behind Guinea-Bissau.

Read more

Nigeria's Super Eagles beat Tanzania 3-1 at the Godswill Akpabio Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November 2026, in a crucial 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier.

How the match went

Alhassan Yusuf gave Nigeria a dream start with a goal in the second minute, but Tanzania equalised through Alphonce Masanga. Ademola Lookman was forced off injured late in the first half and replaced by Moses Simon. Coach Eric Chelle then brought on Tolu Arokodare and Moses Usor in the second half, and the change paid off immediately: Arokodare scored in the 52nd minute, and the two substitutes combined for Arokodare's second goal and Nigeria's third in the 60th minute.

Table

The win moved Nigeria to second in Group L, one point behind Guinea-Bissau, who were held 1-1 by Madagascar. Nigeria play Tanzania again in their next match, and a win there would take them to nine points, putting them in a strong position to qualify with two games left.

Arokodare delivers

Arokodare, who has been in strong form on loan at Ajax in the Netherlands, had promised to carry his club form into the national team in the absence of the injured Victor Osimhen. Chelle later sent him a message after his brace.

Background

Tanzania are one of the co-hosts of AFCON 2027, along with Kenya and Uganda.
